## History of the notice: the shift of 29 October 2026

On **29 October 2026** the copyright of Exponential moved from **7x alone** to the **Exponential Foundation and 7x
together**, and every notice of the project was replaced by one notice in two forms:

| Form | Text | Used in |
|---|---|---|
| Visible notice | `Exponential Copyright © 1998-2026 Exponential Foundation and 7x` | every footer, sign-in page, about and copyright page, the help, the setup wizard, error pages, `./console --version` |
| Header notice | `Copyright (C) 1998 - 2026 Exponential Foundation and 7x. All rights reserved.` | file headers, `ezinfo.php`, `extension.xml`, `LICENSE`, this file |

Before that date the project's own notice was written in several ways, among them "7x and others",
"7x / Exponential Foundation", "7x & Exponential Foundation", "7x and the Exponential Foundation" and "7x" alone.
On 2 October 2026 the project's line had been put above the eZ Systems AS line in some 3,000 files; the change of
29 October 2026 made all of them one notice, with the Foundation first, in about 3,800 files. The notice is never
translated. The text of the license did not change.

The full story, the reasons and the rules for new files and extensions are in the documentation:
[Copyright and copyleft](doc/eng-us/help/copyright-and-copyleft.md)
([German](doc/ger-de/hilfe/urheberrecht-und-copyleft.md)).
