# What changes for editors when the site is static

Your organization publishes its public website as a **static copy**. For you, almost everything stays the same. This
page explains the few things that change.

## What you'll have at the end

You will know when your changes reach visitors, how to see whether a page is out of date online, and what to tell
visitors who ask about forms or comments.

## What stays the same

- You create and edit content in Exponential with the same editor, images, relations and languages.
- Drafts, versions, review and scheduling work as before.
- What you see on the Exponential site is what the copy will look like.

## What changes

**Your change goes online when the site is published.** Pressing **Publish** on an article publishes it in
Exponential. Visitors of the public site see it after the static copy is built and published again. Depending on how
your site is set up, that happens:

- automatically, a few minutes after you publish content;
- on a schedule, for example every night;
- or when someone with the right permission presses **Publish** under Setup > Static publishing.

Ask your administrator which applies to you.

**Stale pages.** A page you changed after the last static publish is called *stale*. The website sidebar's
**Static** panel shows it, and if you are allowed to, **Publish this page** sends just that page online. See
[The website sidebar panel](static-publishing/the-website-sidebar-panel.md).

## What visitors cannot do on the static site

- Sign in, see member pages or use a basket.
- Post comments. They may see a link to the live site instead.
- Send forms, unless your site sends them to the Exponential installation.

Search still works. Details: [Search, forms and comments on a static site](static-publishing/search-forms-and-comments-on-a-static-site.md).

## Privacy

The copy is made as an anonymous visitor sees the site. Drafts, unpublished versions and pages for members only are
never copied. See [Keep private content private](static-publishing/keep-private-content-private.md).
