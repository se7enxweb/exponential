# Getting support: free help and paid help

Exponential is free software, and its developers are not obliged to give free support; the project's
[support page](https://exponential.earth/support) says so plainly, and adds that they like to see people use the
software and go the extra mile when they can. So there are two kinds of support: **free help from the community**,
and **paid help from 7x** when you need it faster or need someone to do the work.

## First: look it up

Most questions have been answered before. Look in this order:

1. **This help.** Search it with the search box of the help dashboard, or press the "?" button on any page of the
   administration for help about that page.
2. **The documentation website**, [exponential.doc.exponential.earth](https://exponential.doc.exponential.earth/),
   with the installation manual for Exponential 6 and the technical manual.
3. **eZpedia**, [ezpedia.exponential.earth](https://ezpedia.exponential.earth/), the community wiki.
4. **The articles on Share Exponential**, [share.exponential.earth/learn](https://share.exponential.earth/learn),
   on key subjects.

More places are listed in [Documentation sources](documentation-sources.md).

## Free help from the community

If the answer is not documented, ask. These are the channels the support page names, all free:

| Channel | Good for | Address |
|---|---|---|
| **Share Exponential forums** | Questions that need a longer answer, that others will search for later | <https://share.exponential.earth/forums> |
| **Telegram** | Quick questions in real time; the support page describes it as a chat with members of the development team and the community | <https://t.me/exponentialcms> |
| **Discord** | Chat with the community, development talk, feedback | <https://discord.gg/zXzGMgfzej> |
| **Slack** | Chat, especially about development | <https://exponentialcms.slack.com/> |

Details and the other places the community meets are on [Community channels](community-channels.md).

### How to ask so you get an answer

- **Say what you run**: the Exponential version, the PHP version, the web server (Velocity, Apache or nginx with
  PHP-FPM, FrankenPHP), the database. **Setup > System information** gives you a summary to copy, without passwords,
  keys or server paths.
- **Say what you did, what you expected and what happened**, with the exact message.
- **Show what you tried** and what you already read.
- **Never post** passwords, keys, session cookies, database addresses, or personal data of your users or customers.
- **Report back** when you solved it, so the next person finds the answer.

## When it is a bug, not a question

If the software does something wrong, report it where it can be fixed for everyone:
[When something goes wrong](../when-something-goes-wrong.md#filing-a-bug-in-exponential) explains which issue
tracker to use, and the **Report an error** page of this help prepares the issue for you. Security problems are
never posted in public: send them privately as
[When something goes wrong](../when-something-goes-wrong.md#security-problems) explains.

## Paid help from 7x

When you cannot wait, or you need someone to do the work, the support page recommends hiring **7x**, the company that
maintains Exponential: [se7enx.com/services](https://se7enx.com/services), or contact them at
[se7enx.com/contact](https://se7enx.com/contact). The support page notes that developers both in and outside the
Exponential team can be hired; 7x is the one it names. More about 7x: [Who is 7x?](../who-is-7x.md).

Companies that offer services around Exponential can also become partners of the project, and partners have their own
support levels ([Partners: support levels and response times](../partners/support-levels-and-response-times.md)).

## What free support cannot promise

Free help depends on the time people give. Nobody promises an answer time in the forums or chats, and nobody will
log in to your server for free. For a guaranteed answer time, or work on your site, use paid support. If the free
help saved you time or money, consider giving something back: an answer to someone else's question, a fix, or a
donation ([Give and donate](give-and-donate.md)).
