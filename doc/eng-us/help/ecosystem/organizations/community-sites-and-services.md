## What the roadmap plans for them

The roadmap names a redesigned documentation portal, a possible redesign of Projects with package management web
services, more eZpedia content and a shared sign-in with Share and Projects, and a Share redesign with "share a site"
announcements ([Roadmap](../../project/roadmap.md)). These are plans, not features; check before you announce them.
