## What it is

"a 501(c)(3) Non-Profit Corporation founded in Seattle, Washington, USA", founded on 19 March 2026, with a mission
"dedicated directly to the continued Funding, Adoption, Support, Direction, Education, Development, Promotion and
Documentation for the Exponential Enterprise Open Source Project" ([In short](../../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#in-short)).

## Its role

| Role | Source |
|---|---|
| "This project is operated by the Exponential Foundation a Public Non-Profit Corporation" | exponential.earth/about/project |
| It shares the copyright of the kernel with 7x | `COPYRIGHT.md` |
| It decides on conduct reports and their consequences | `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md` |
| It names three products: "Exponential, Exponential Platform and Exponential Basic" | [The product family at a glance](../../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#the-product-family-at-a-glance) |
| It funds open source work through sponsorship | [What the Foundation funds](../../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#what-the-foundation-funds) |

## What is not published

No public source says "who sits on the Foundation's board, how it is governed, whether it has members besides its
supporters, and what its budget is" ([What the sources do not say](../../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#what-the-sources-do-not-say)).
Do not fill these gaps with guesses.
