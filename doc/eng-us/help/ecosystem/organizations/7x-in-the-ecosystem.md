## Who 7x is

"7x is the North American corporation driving The Continued General Use, Support, Development, Hosting, Design of
Exponential" and "took over leadership of the project and it's development, support, adoption and community growth
in 2023". "Previously before 2022, 7x was called Brookins Consulting" (`README.md`, "Who is 7x"). The full story:
[Who is 7x?](../../who-is-7x.md).

## What 7x does in the ecosystem

| Area | What 7x does | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Code | Maintains the kernel, the extensions and the platform forks under the se7enxweb organization | [Who is 7x?](../../who-is-7x.md) |
| Releases | Publishes the 6.0.x releases since 6.0.0 on 1 January 2024 | [Who is 7x?](../../who-is-7x.md#the-60x-releases) |
| Copyright | Receives the copyright in kernel contributions through the CLA | `CONTRIBUTING.md` |
| Partner program | Runs it: "7x reserves full rights to change or administer this program" | [Governance and decisions](../../partners/governance-and-decisions.md) |
| Funding | "the current leader in contributions both financial, code, extension development, new features, infrastructure, maintenance and much much more" (the Foundation's words) | [7x, the first sponsor](../../sponsors/7x-the-first-sponsor.md) |
| Community sites | Share is "Proudly hosted by se7enx.com" | [Who is 7x?](../../who-is-7x.md#the-community) |

## Services

7x sells services around the open source software, while "the software itself stays free": web design and
development, e-commerce and payment integration, training, upgrades from the old product line to Exponential 6,
support and maintenance contracts, and managed hosting ([Services and hosting](../../who-is-7x.md#services-and-hosting)).
Prices and terms are on the 7x website.
