## Levels

Eight levels, Student, Now, Starter, Basic, Professional, Silver, Gold and Platinum, from $25 to $2,600 a month as
published on 18 October 2026 ([Partner levels](../../partners/partner-levels.md),
[Partner plans and prices](../../partners/partner-plans-and-prices.md)).

## How partners fit the ecosystem

- Partners are inside the community: they use, contribute and promote.
- They fund the work that keeps the ecosystem current.
- They carry Exponential to organizations that would not find it alone: customers, public bodies, schools.
- They follow the code of conduct's "Partners and commercial conduct" and the
  [partner code of conduct](../../partners/partner-code-of-conduct.md).
