## Before Exponential

- The code base began as eZ Publish. Its creator discontinued it after almost eighteen years of service
  ([The products one by one](../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#the-products-one-by-one)).
- The copyright years of eZ Systems AS in the code ran from 1999 to 2014 (`COPYRIGHT.md`).
- eZpedia, the community encyclopedia, started in 2006.
- "The platform originally known as eZ Publish reached end-of-life in 2021. We kept going." (se7enxweb profile
  README, quoted in the [Timeline](../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#timeline)).
- The Symfony-based successor, eZ Platform, was later discontinued as well in favor of Ibexa's product line
  (`README.md`, "What was eZ Platform?").

## Stewardship and renaming

| When | What happened |
|---|---|
| 2014 | 7x's initial idea: the se7enx.com domain was registered and the fork of the ecosystem prepared |
| Before 2022 | 7x was called Brookins Consulting, "the outspoken leader in the active Exponential Community" (`README.md`) |
| 2022 | 7x's project started |
| 2023 | 7x "took over leadership of the project and it's development, support, adoption and community growth" (`README.md`); in December the first outside change was merged |
| 1 January 2024 | Exponential 6.0.0, then still under the old name |
| 2024 to 2026 | The Platform, Legacy, Nexus and DXP repositories were created |
| 11 and 12 August 2025 | The product became Exponential |
| 19 March 2026 | The Exponential Foundation was founded in Seattle |
| 7 June 2026 | Exponential 6.0.14 |
| September 2026 | Exponential Velocity joined Exponential |
| October 2026 | Exponential Intelligence, the LoveStack features and the help system in the 6.0.15 line |

Source for the table: the [Timeline](../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#timeline).

## The ecosystem today

- A maintained 6.0 line, with security fixes published in the next 6.0.x release (`SECURITY.md`).
- The Platform line in nine distributions for Symfony users (`README.md`, "What is Exponential Platform?").
- Hundreds of packages on Packagist and a history of 6556 ledger changes in 53 repositories since December 2023
  ([The Exponential platform ecosystem](../../history/ecosystem.md)).
- A help system that ships with the product, under the GNU FDL.

## Writing about the history

Use the old name only for history, in the past tense, and do not suggest that Exponential is associated with the
original vendor: "This project is not associated with the original eZ Publish software or its original developer,
eZ Systems or Ibexa" (`README.md`). Guidance: [Writing about the history correctly](../advocates/writing-about-the-history-correctly.md).
