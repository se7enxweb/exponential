# Exponential Intelligence in the ecosystem

**"A writing assistant built into the administration interface"** that "writes drafts, as the signed-in editor and
with that editor's own permissions", never publishes on its own, and works with hosted providers in the US or the EU
or with a local open-weight model (`README.md`, "Exponential Intelligence").

## Its place in the ecosystem

- Package `se7enxweb/expai`, required in `composer.json` since 6.0.15
  ([Exponential Intelligence](../../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#exponential-intelligence)).
- For "Editors, project managers and content writers" (same).
- It connects the Exponential world with external AI services, or with a model running on your own server.

## Defaults

"A new installation activates it by default" and it "sends nothing anywhere until you add a provider key" (`README.md`,
quoted on [Exponential Intelligence](../../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#exponential-intelligence)).

## Providers

The README's section "Providers: hosted, in the EU, or on your own server" lists the choice: a hosted provider, a
provider in the EU, or a local open-weight model on the same server. Requirements: "an API key of a hosted AI provider,
or a local open-weight model on the same server" (`README.md`, "Requirements").
