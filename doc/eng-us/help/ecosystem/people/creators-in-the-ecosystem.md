## What creators share

Creators decide what they share. Code they keep for themselves or their customers stays theirs; the GPL's conditions
apply when they distribute it ([Licenses in the community](../../community/how-it-works/licenses-in-the-community.md)).
What they publish becomes part of what others can build on
([Publishing into the ecosystem](publishing-into-the-ecosystem.md)).

## Creators and the community

A creator who publishes, answers questions or contributes back is also a community member; a creator company that
works directly with 7x and the Foundation can become a partner
([Partners, community and ecosystem](../../community/partners-community-and-ecosystem.md)).
