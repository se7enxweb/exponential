# The ecosystem for decision makers

A buyer of a content management system "buys the assurance that the software will still be maintained in five years,
that they can find help when they are stuck, that they can hire people or companies to work on it, and that they are
not locked to one supplier" ([The community and ecosystem as a selling point](../../business/community-and-ecosystem.md)).

## Questions and proof

| Question | What the ecosystem offers | Where the proof is |
|---|---|---|
| Will it still exist? | A code base kept alive after its original vendor's end of life in 2021; a maintained 6.0 line since 1 January 2024 | [A short history of the ecosystem](../a-short-history-of-the-ecosystem.md) |
| Who is responsible? | A company (7x) and a nonprofit (the Foundation) | [Organizations](../organizations/README.md) |
| Where do we get help? | Public forums, chats, documentation, trackers; paid support from 7x and partners | [Community sites and services](../organizations/community-sites-and-services.md) |
| Can we extend it without the vendor? | Every package is a public repository and a Composer package under the GPL | [Packages](../packages/README.md) |
| Are we locked in? | Six databases, many servers and operating systems, free software | [The PHP stack](../php-stack/README.md) |
| Can we hire people? | Partners and contributors whose work is public | [Partners in the ecosystem](../organizations/partners-in-the-ecosystem.md) |

## What the sources do not answer

The Foundation's board, governance and budget are not published; numbers of installations or customers are not
given. If they matter to your decision, ask the Foundation or 7x directly.
