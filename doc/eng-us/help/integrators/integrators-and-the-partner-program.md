# Integrators and the partner program

This page is for integration companies and independent integration developers who consider becoming an
**Exponential Partner**. It explains how integration work fits into the program. The partner pages are authoritative:
prices, levels and terms are quoted from them and should be checked there
([Partners: start here](../partners/partners-start-here.md)).

## Which profile fits an integrator

The program groups partners into profiles; integrators usually fit the **IT** profile, and those who build and publish
extensions also the **Development** partners listed among the classes
([Partner profiles](../partners/partner-profiles.md), [Implementation partners](../partners/implementation-partners.md),
[Extension and theme partners](../partners/extension-and-theme-partners.md)).

## Levels and costs

The levels are **Student, Now, Starter, Basic, Professional, Silver, Gold and Platinum**. Prices per month in US
dollars, as documented on [Partner plans and prices](../partners/partner-plans-and-prices.md) from
<https://exponential.earth/partner/benefits> (read 18 October 2026):

| Level | Price per month | Revenue requirement per quarter |
|---|---|---|
| Student | $25 | None |
| Now | $45 | None |
| Starter | $175 | None |
| Basic | $300 | None |
| Professional | $500 | $1,800 |
| Silver | $800 | $3,000 |
| Gold | $1,000 | $4,500 |
| Platinum | $2,600 | $7,500 |

Tickets, response times, discounts and lead priority per level are on the plans page. The live benefits page and the
7x store are authoritative.

## Referrals and leads

Partners who pass a lead to 7x receive a commission of **10%** of the total contract value for a **cold** lead and
**20%** for a **hot** lead (a customer ready to purchase from 7x)
([Partner plans and prices, partner terms](../partners/partner-plans-and-prices.md#partner-terms)). An integrator often
meets customers who need hosting, support or development beyond the integration; the referral rules, qualification
and hand-off are on [Referrals and leads](../partners/referrals-and-leads.md) and [Lead hand-offs](../partners/lead-handoffs.md).

## Joining

1. Read the program pages and choose a level ([How to apply](../partners/how-to-apply.md)).
2. **Pay first, then sign**: buy the partner subscription in the 7x store, then sign the partnership agreement and send
   it to the partner team.
3. Contact: <partners@exponential.earth>, or the form at <https://exponential.earth/partner/contact-us>.

A partner website and dashboard are planned for mid-2027; until then, log your activity yourself
([Logging partner activity](../partners/logging-partner-activity.md)).

## What is not published

How integration projects or published connectors count toward points or levels beyond what the partner pages say,
and any integrator-specific terms, are not published; ask the partner team. Contributions in general count as
community work ([Earning points through community work](../partners/earning-points-through-community-work.md)).
