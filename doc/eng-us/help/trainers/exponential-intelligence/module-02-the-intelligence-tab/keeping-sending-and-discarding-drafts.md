# Lesson 2.2: Keeping, sending and discarding drafts

The assistant's drafts are ordinary drafts of the editor. This lesson teaches the three decisions: keep a draft to
work on it, send it for publishing (through approval where it applies), or throw it away.

## At a glance

## Learning objectives

After this lesson, learners can:

- review each draft with its view and edit links before deciding;
- keep drafts with **Save as drafts** and find them under **My drafts**;
- edit a draft in the normal edit form;
- discard a draft and confirm;
- send a draft with **Send for approval** and read whether it waits for an approver or is published at once.

## Trainer's notes

### How to present it

Sources: the panel's own explanation and the approval page under **EI: Help** (`extension/expai/doc/approval-workflow.md`).

1. **Ordinary drafts** (3 minutes). A new item is version 1 with status draft, owned by the editor; a change is a new
   draft version, the published one stays as it is. They are in **My drafts**, open in the normal edit form (with
   autosave) and can be discarded there.
2. **The panel's buttons** (4 minutes). Read the hint under them aloud: *"Save as drafts" keeps the selected drafts;
   you find them under My drafts and can edit them in the normal edit form. "Send for approval" publishes them the
   normal way: where an approval workflow applies they wait for the approver, otherwise they are published at once.
   Each draft says which applies.*
3. **Before sending** (3 minutes). Each draft says what will happen, for example *Published at once when sent (no
   approval applies)*. Required fields must be filled; otherwise the panel says which are empty and to open the draft
   in the edit form.
4. **Discard** (2 minutes). **Discard** asks *Discard the draft "<name>"? This cannot be undone.* It removes the draft;
   for a new item, the item is gone.
5. **The label** (2 minutes). Versions written by the assistant carry a label in the version views and in approval
   items, so reviewers know.

### Common questions

**Is Send for approval the same as Send for publishing?** It runs the same publish operation as the editor; where a
workflow holds it, it waits for approval like any other version.

**Can I edit an AI draft before sending?** Yes, always: open it in the edit form, change it, then publish there or
send it from the panel.

**Who is the author of the draft?** The editor. The label records that the assistant wrote it.

### Pitfalls

- **Sending without reading.** Module 6 covers reviewing; insist on reading every draft first.
- **Discarding the wrong draft.** Read the name in the question.
- **Approval surprises.** On a site with approval, sent drafts wait under **My pending items**.

## Hands-on exercise

Use the training installation and the two drafts from lesson 2.1.

1. Open your course folder's **Intelligence** tab. Find **Drafts written with Exponential Intelligence**.
2. Read each draft with its view link. Note one thing you would change in each.
3. Select both drafts and press **Save as drafts**.
4. Open **My drafts** on the dashboard: both are there.
5. Open the first draft with **Edit**, make your change in the edit form, and press **Store draft and exit**.
6. Back in the Intelligence tab, read what each draft says will happen when it is sent.
7. Select the second draft and press **Discard**; read the question and confirm.
8. Select the first draft and press **Send for approval**. Read the panel's message: published, or waiting for an
   approver.
9. Check your course folder (and **My pending items** if an approval applies).

## Expected result

- One article (edited by the learner) is published or pending; the other draft is gone.
- **My drafts** no longer lists either.
- The trainer checks by opening one learner's article and its versions; the version view shows the assistant's label.

## Quiz

1. Where do you find drafts kept with **Save as drafts**?
2. What does **Send for approval** do on a site without an approval workflow?
3. What happens to a new item whose only draft you discard?
   A. It is published B. It goes to the trash C. It is removed D. It is hidden
4. True or false: you must send an AI draft from the panel; it cannot be edited in the normal form.
5. How do reviewers know a version was written by the assistant?

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. Under **My drafts**.
2. It publishes the draft at once (the panel says so before you send).
3. **C.**
4. **False.** It is an ordinary draft; edit it in the normal edit form.
5. The version carries the assistant's label.

</details>
