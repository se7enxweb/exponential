# Lesson 6.2: Previewing a draft

Before publishing, a careful editor looks at the draft as visitors will see it, on a phone as well as on a desktop.
The preview does exactly that, without publishing anything. This short lesson teaches the preview page and its
buttons.

## Learning objectives

After this lesson, learners can:

- open the preview of a draft from the edit form and from the versions page;
- view the draft at the widths of a phone, a tablet, a desktop and a large desktop, and turn the device to landscape;
- go back to the form with **Back to edit** or publish with **Publish**;
- explain that a preview never changes the site.

## Trainer's notes

### How to present it

Source: [Preview](../../../admin-tour.md#5-preview).

1. **Opening it** (2 minutes). In the edit form, **Preview** in the left column. On the versions page, **View** on a
   draft. On an item's page, the round **Preview** button shows the published version.
2. **The buttons** (5 minutes). At the top of the preview page: **iPhone**, **iPad**, **Desktop**, **Large Desktop**
   and **Landscape** set the width; **Back to edit** returns to the form; **Publish** publishes from there; **Manage
   versions** opens the versions page; **Back** leaves. Some installations show further settings with **Update
   view** (for example the siteaccess or language the preview uses).
3. **Why it matters** (3 minutes). The site's design arranges content differently on a phone: long titles wrap,
   tables scroll, images shrink. Check what visitors on phones will see.

### Common questions

**Is the preview exactly the site?** It is the draft rendered with the site's design. Things that depend on other
published items (for example a list of the latest news) show what is published now.

**Can someone else see my preview?** Only people who may read your draft; normally only you.

**Does Publish in the preview do the same as Send for publishing?** Yes: the draft becomes the published version (or
pending, where an approval applies).

### Pitfalls

- **Publishing from the preview by accident.** **Publish** sits next to **Back to edit**. Read before you click.
- **Judging the phone view on a small projector.** Ask learners to check on their own screens too.
- **Previewing the published version.** The round **Preview** button on the item's page shows the published
  version, not the draft. Use **Preview** in the form's left column for the draft.

## Hands-on exercise

Use the training installation and your article *News two (updated)* with its image.

1. Open *News two (updated)* and press **Edit**.
2. Add a long sentence to **Intro**, so that the text wraps on a narrow screen.
3. In the left column of the form, press **Preview**.
4. Press **iPhone**. Look at how the title, the image and the intro are arranged.
5. Press **Landscape**, then **iPad**, then **Desktop**, then **Large Desktop**. Write down one difference you see.
6. Press **Back to edit**. The form opens with your draft.
7. Shorten the long sentence, then press **Preview** again.
8. Press **Publish** in the preview.
9. Open the article on the site with **View on site** and compare it with what the preview showed.

## Expected result

- The learner saw the draft at four widths and in landscape, and wrote down one difference (for example the image
  moves above the text on a phone).
- **Back to edit** returned to the same draft with the long sentence still there.
- After **Publish**, the site shows the shortened intro.
- The trainer checks by asking two learners what differed between **iPhone** and **Desktop**.

## Quiz

1. Where do you open the preview of the draft you are editing?
2. Which buttons set the width of the preview? Name three.
3. True or false: opening the preview publishes the draft.
4. What does **Back to edit** do?
   A. Discards the draft B. Returns to the form with the draft C. Publishes D. Opens the site
5. The round **Preview** button on an item's page shows which version?

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. With **Preview** in the left column of the edit form ([A tour of the administration](../../../admin-tour.md#5-preview)).
2. Any three of **iPhone**, **iPad**, **Desktop**, **Large Desktop** (and **Landscape** turns the device).
3. **False.** The preview never changes the site; only **Publish** does.
4. **B.**
5. The published version.

</details>
