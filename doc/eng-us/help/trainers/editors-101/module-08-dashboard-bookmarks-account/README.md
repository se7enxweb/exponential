# Module 8: Dashboard, bookmarks and your account

The everyday comfort module. After this, editors start their day on the dashboard, reach their items through
bookmarks, and know how to keep their account safe and how the site tells them about changes.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Course | [Editors 101](../README.md) |
| Duration | 2 hours 10 minutes (with a 10-minute break) |
| Lessons | 3 |
| Level | Beginner |

## Lessons

| Lesson | Duration | What learners do |
|---|---|---|
| [Lesson 8.1: The dashboard as a daily start](the-dashboard.md) | 30 minutes | Read every block of the dashboard and write a daily routine |
| [Lesson 8.2: Bookmarks](bookmarks.md) | 45 minutes | Add bookmarks, sort them into a folder, find them, use them in the link dialog |
| [Lesson 8.3: Your account and notifications](your-account-and-notifications.md) | 45 minutes | Explore **My account**, the password page, two-step sign-in, follow an item with a digest |

## Preparing this module

- Learners need the content from earlier modules, so the dashboard's lists are not empty.
- Decide whether learners may change their training passwords in lesson 8.3. If not, they press **Cancel**.
- Make sure the training installation does not send real mail (training accounts at `example.invalid`).
- After the course, reset the training accounts: passwords, two-step sign-in, bookmarks and notification settings.
