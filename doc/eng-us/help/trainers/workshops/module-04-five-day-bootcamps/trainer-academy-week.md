# Bootcamp 4.3: The trainer academy week

New trainers need two things: the material and practice. This week condenses [Train the trainer](../../train-the-trainer/README.md)
into four days and spends the fifth on teaching practice, where every trainer teaches a real lesson to the group.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Course | [Workshops and bootcamps](../README.md) |
| Module | [Module 4: Five-day bootcamps](README.md) |
| Duration | 5 days, 6 hours of sessions each |
| Level | Intermediate |
| Audience | New trainers, 4 to 10 learners |
| Prerequisites | Experience with the subject they will teach (editing, administration or development) |

## Learning objectives

At the end of the week, each trainer can:

- build and reset a training lab;
- design, time and adapt a course from the material in the help;
- deliver a lesson safely and accessibly;
- assess learners honestly and collect feedback.

## The agenda

| Day | Morning (3 h) | Afternoon (3 h) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Module 1](../../train-the-trainer/module-01-preparing-to-teach/README.md): preparing to teach | [Module 2](../../train-the-trainer/module-02-building-the-lab/README.md), lessons 2.1 to 2.4 |
| 2 | Module 2, lessons 2.5 to 2.8 | [Module 3](../../train-the-trainer/module-03-designing-a-course/README.md): designing a course |
| 3 | [Module 4](../../train-the-trainer/module-04-delivering/README.md): delivering | [Module 5](../../train-the-trainer/module-05-accessibility-and-inclusion/README.md): accessibility and inclusion |
| 4 | [Module 6](../../train-the-trainer/module-06-assessment-and-follow-up/README.md): assessment and follow-up | Preparing the teaching practice: each trainer picks a lesson and rehearses it |
| 5 | Teaching practice: each trainer teaches a 20-minute part of a lesson; 10 minutes of feedback each | Teaching practice continued; individual development plans |

Breaks: 15 minutes mid-morning and mid-afternoon, 45 minutes lunch, each day.

## Trainer's notes

- **Teaching practice** uses a rubric: objectives met, accuracy, timing, safety (nothing private on screen),
  accessibility, and handling of questions
  ([Quizzes and practical tasks](../../train-the-trainer/module-06-assessment-and-follow-up/quizzes-and-practical-tasks.md)).
- **Feedback** in the order: what worked, one thing to change, one question.
- **No official status**: the week's certificate, if any, is from the organizer
  ([Certificates of attendance in your own name](../../train-the-trainer/module-06-assessment-and-follow-up/certificates-of-attendance.md)).

## Hands-on exercise: the teaching practice

1. Choose one lesson from [Editors 101](../../editors-101/README.md) or [Train the trainer](../../train-the-trainer/README.md).
2. Rehearse it on your own lab; prepare its handout.
3. Teach a 20-minute part to the group, including at least part of the hands-on exercise.
4. Receive feedback against the rubric and write a three-line development plan.

## Expected result

- Every trainer taught once and received rubric-based feedback.
- Every trainer has a development plan with one strength and two things to practise.

## Quiz (end of week)

1. Name the six rubric criteria of the teaching practice.
2. In which order is feedback given?
3. Who issues a certificate for this week?

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. Objectives met, accuracy, timing, safety, accessibility, handling of questions.
2. What worked, one thing to change, one question.
3. The organizer or trainer, in their own name; no official status is claimed.

</details>
