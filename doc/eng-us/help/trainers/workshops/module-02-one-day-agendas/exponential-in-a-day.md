# Agenda 2.5: Exponential in a day (mixed group)

Project kick-offs often bring the whole team at once: editors, an administrator, developers. This agenda keeps them
together in the morning, where the topics are shared, and splits them in the afternoon.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Course | [Workshops and bootcamps](../README.md) |
| Module | [Module 2: One-day agendas](README.md) |
| Duration | 6 hours of sessions (9:00 to 16:30 with breaks and lunch) |
| Level | Beginner |
| Audience | A project team of mixed roles, 6 to 18 learners |
| Prerequisites | One installation per learner, or a shared site plus one installation per administrator and developer; a trainer or helper per afternoon track |

## Learning objectives

At the end of the day, every learner can:

- explain the core concepts (object, node, version, role, siteaccess);
- create, publish and version an article;
- name the next step for their role;

and, by track: editors place an image and a link; administrators build a role; developers make and undo an override.

## Trainer's notes

- **One trainer per afternoon track**, or stations that learners rotate through with a helper each.
- **Show-and-tell** at 15:15 builds understanding across roles: editors see why developers need templates, developers
  see what editors do every day.
- **Next steps**: give each role its course from the catalogue.

## Hands-on exercise

The morning capstone (everyone): create the article "Team kick-off", publish it, edit and publish a second version,
and open **Manage versions**. The afternoon tracks use the capstones of their one-day agendas, shortened.

## Expected result

- Every learner has a published article at version 2.
- Each track presents one result: an article with an image, a role page, an override.

## Quiz (end of day)

1. Name two concepts every role needs.
2. Which course follows for an editor? For an administrator? For a developer?
3. What did the other tracks build?

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. For example object and node, versions, roles ([Teaching the core concepts](../../teaching-the-core-concepts.md)).
2. Editors 101; Site administration; Developers ([Course catalogue](../../course-catalogue.md)).
3. An article with an image and a link; a role assigned to a group; an override shown and removed.

</details>
