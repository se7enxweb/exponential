# Agenda 2.6: The decision makers' half day

Decision makers need facts, not clicks: what Exponential is, what it costs to run, who stands behind it, and what the
risks are. This half day combines a guided demo with a sourced fact session and a structured discussion.

| Level | Beginner |
| Audience | Managers, boards, project sponsors, 3 to 12 people |
| Prerequisites | A demo installation the trainer controls; no laptops needed for the audience |

## Learning objectives

At the end of the session, participants can:

- describe what Exponential is and who stands behind it, with sources;
- name its licenses (GNU GPL for the code, GNU FDL for the documentation);
- list the hosting and database options;
- decide on next steps with a list of open questions.

## Trainer's notes

- **Facts with sources only.** Do not quote figures that no source gives
  ([Claims you must not make](../../../advocates/claims-you-must-not-make.md)).
- **No sales pressure.** If you also sell services, say so
  ([Disclosure and conflicts of interest](../../../advocates/disclosure-and-conflicts-of-interest.md)).
- **The demo** runs on your own demo installation with invented content.

## Hands-on exercise: the decision worksheet

Participants fill in together:

1. Our needs (content types, languages, users, integrations).
2. What we saw today that meets each need (with the help page).
3. Open questions and who will answer them.
4. Next step: a pilot, a one-day workshop for the team, or no further action.

## Expected result

- A one-page decision worksheet with needs, matches, open questions and a next step.

## Quiz

1. Who leads Exponential, and what is the Exponential Foundation?
2. Under which license is the code? The documentation?
3. Name three supported databases.

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. 7x leads it; the Exponential Foundation is the nonprofit that operates and funds the project
   ([Who is the Exponential Foundation?](../../../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md)).
2. The code: the GNU GPL; the documentation: the GNU FDL 1.3 or later ([Free documentation](../../../free-documentation.md)).
3. Any three of SQLite, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, MariaDB, Oracle, MySQL ([Database support](../../../database-support.md)).

</details>
