# Workshops and bootcamps: course overview

Not every group can take a full course. This part of the trainer material packs the courses into ready programs: one
day, three days and five days, plus a half day for decision makers. Each program names the lessons and help pages it
uses, the checkpoint of each block and what to cut first, and ends with a capstone.

## Who it is for

- Trainers who must deliver in a fixed, short time.
- Organizers who plan training for a team or an event.
- Agencies onboarding a whole team.

**Outcome:** the trainer can choose a format, plan the logistics and run a workshop or bootcamp from a ready agenda,
ending with a capstone and a fair check of the result.

## Format and duration

| | |
|---|---|
| Programs | 6 one-day (including a half day), 4 three-day, 3 five-day; 3 capstone briefs |
| Planning module | 4 lessons, 2 hours 5 minutes |
| Level | Beginner to advanced, depending on the program |
| Prerequisites | For the trainer: [Train the trainer](../train-the-trainer/README.md), at least Modules 2 and 4 |

## Assessing learners

Each program ends with a capstone and an end-of-day or end-of-bootcamp quiz; the capstone briefs of Module 5 carry
rubrics. All of it is the trainer's own material, not an official certification
([Certifications](../../partners/certifications.md) describes what the partner program publishes about its own).
