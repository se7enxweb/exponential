# Lesson 6.5: After the course

Learning continues after the course, or it fades. A short follow-up with the right links, a place to ask questions
and a next step makes the difference. This lesson closes the train-the-trainer course with the trainer's
after-course routine.

## Learning objectives

After this lesson, learners can:

- write a follow-up message with the answers to parked questions and their sources;
- point learners to the community channels and the next course;
- describe the path from user to contributor;
- complete the trainer's clean-up and improvement list.

## Trainer's notes

### How to present it

1. The follow-up message (4 minutes): thanks; answers to the question board with links; the help pages of the course;
   the next course; where to ask.
2. Where to ask (3 minutes): [The community channels](../../../advocates/community-channels.md) lists the forums at
   share.exponential.earth and the Telegram chat, and the issue trackers.
3. From user to contributor (3 minutes): [Develop with Git](../../../develop-with-git.md) and the extension
   development book ([Extension development](../../../extension-development/README.md)).

### Common questions

**Should I add learners to a mailing list?** Only with their consent.

**What is the next course after Editors 101?** Editors 201; after Site administration, Hosting and operations or
Multisite; after Developers, Theme design or Layouts (see the [Course catalogue](../../course-catalogue.md)).

### Pitfalls

- **Answers without sources.** Every answer gets its link.
- **Forgotten labs.** Clean-up is part of the course.

## Hands-on exercise

1. Write a follow-up message for an imagined Editors 101 group: thanks, two answered questions with links, the help
   pages of the course, the next course, and where to ask.
2. Check the message: no personal data of other learners, no attachments with passwords.
3. Write your clean-up and improvement list: stop servers, remove accounts, delete lab directories, one change to the
   course from the feedback, one help fix or report.
4. Read [The advocacy ladder](../../../advocates/the-advocacy-ladder.md) and note one thing a learner could do next
   to help others.

## Expected result

- A follow-up message with sourced answers and the next steps.
- A clean-up and improvement list of at least five items.
