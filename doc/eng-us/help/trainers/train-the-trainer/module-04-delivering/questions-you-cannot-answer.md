# Lesson 4.3: Questions you cannot answer

Every trainer meets questions they cannot answer. Learners respect "I don't know, let's find out" far more than a
confident guess that turns out wrong. This lesson gives a routine for those questions and shows the places where the
answers usually are.

## Learning objectives

After this lesson, learners can:

- answer "I don't know" without losing the room;
- find an answer in the Help center, the guides or the feature pages within five minutes;
- keep a question board and close it after the course with sources;
- point learners to the community channels for questions beyond the course.

## Trainer's notes

### How to present it

1. The routine (5 minutes): say you do not know; decide whether to look it up now (if it takes under five minutes and
   helps everyone) or park it on the board; always come back to it.
2. Where answers are (5 minutes): the Help center search; [Where the facts live](../../../advocates/where-the-facts-live.md);
   the guides, feature pages and install chapters; for settings, the commented `.ini` files under `settings/`; the
   community channels ([The community channels](../../../advocates/community-channels.md)).

### Common questions

**Should I guess and correct later?** No. A wrong answer gets written down and repeated.

**What about roadmap questions?** Do not promise features. Point to the changelogs and the issue trackers.

### Pitfalls

- **Lost questions.** Write every parked question down with the asker's first name only.
- **Answering by memory after the course.** Each answer gets a link to its source.

## Hands-on exercise

1. Collect five questions from a partner about Exponential that you cannot answer right now (for example: "Which
   databases are supported?", "How long are log files kept?").
2. For each, set a five-minute timer and search the Help center.
3. Write the answer in one or two sentences with the link to the page that proves it.
4. For the questions you could not answer in five minutes, write where you would ask next.
5. Present one answer to the group with its source.

## Expected result

- At least three of the five questions answered with a source link.
- For the rest, a named next place to ask (a forum, an issue tracker, the code).

## Quiz

1. What is the first thing to say when you do not know an answer?
2. How long should you look something up during class before parking it?
3. Where are log files and their rotation described?
4. What goes with every answer you send after the course?

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. That you do not know, and how you will find out.
2. About five minutes, and only if it helps everyone.
3. In [Troubleshooting, 12.2](../../../../install/12-troubleshooting.md#122-where-the-logs-are).
4. A link to the source page.

</details>
