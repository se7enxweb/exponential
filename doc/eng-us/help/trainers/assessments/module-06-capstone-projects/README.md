# Module 6: Capstone projects

The capstones are the largest assessments of this course: realistic jobs for an invented organization that need the
skills of several courses at once. Lesson 6.1 shows how to plan and run one; lessons 6.2 to 6.6 are ready briefs with
rubrics; lesson 6.7 keeps results consistent across scorers and runs.

> **Note**
> Capstones are the trainer's own assessments, not an official certification. They run on training installations,
> with mail written to files and test accounts at `example.invalid`.

## Preparing this module

- [ ] The capstone chosen for the group's courses ([Lesson 6.1](planning-capstone-projects.md)), its scenario
      rewritten with an invented organization.
- [ ] One training installation per learner or team, prepared as the capstone's trainer's notes say; mail transports
      `file`, no crontab, accounts at `example.invalid`.
- [ ] For lesson 6.3: two reversible incidents written down with how to undo them.
- [ ] For lesson 6.5: the sample site or export, made for class.
- [ ] A second scorer, or a plan for moderation ([Lesson 6.7](reviewing-and-moderating-results.md)).
- [ ] Rooms or online rooms for the presentations, with a projector or screen sharing.

## Timing plan

For a group that takes one capstone, for example 6.2:

| Time | Part |
|---|---|
| Day before, 0:00 to 0:45 | Lesson 6.1: the trainer adapts the brief (preparation, not with learners) |
| Day 1, 0:00 to 0:15 | Briefing |
| Day 1, 0:15 to 5:15 | Work, with checkpoints after 2 and 4 hours |
| Day 1, 5:15 to 6:00 | Presentations (10 minutes per team, for four teams plus changeover) |
| Day 1, 6:00 to 6:30 | Scoring |
| After the day, 0:00 to 0:40 | Lesson 6.7: moderation of borderline results |
| Total | 6 hours 30 minutes with the learners, plus 1 hour 25 minutes of trainer work |
