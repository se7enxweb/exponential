# Lesson 2.1: Editors, written test

This test closes the Editors 101 course. It checks what an editor must understand to work safely: what a draft is,
what publishing does, how to go back, where removed items go, how files and translations work and how to find
things. Use it as it is, or as a model for your own questions.

> **Note**
> This is the trainer's own assessment, not an official certification. Check every button name against your
> training installation before the course; the test describes the Admin 4 design.

## Learning objectives

Learners who pass this test have shown that they can:

- explain drafts, versions and the four version states;
- tell what **Send for publishing**, **Store draft and exit** and **Discard draft** do;
- explain how to restore an earlier version and a removed item;
- describe how files are uploaded and why images need an alternative text;
- translate an item and choose the language before editing;
- find an item by search, by number or by address.

## Trainer's notes

### How to run it

1. Hand out the questions without the key. Read the issuer line and the rules: 30 minutes, the help may be open or
   not as you announced, no talking.
2. Collect the sheets. Go through the answers with the group, each with its cited page on the projector.
3. Score with the rule under **Scoring**.

### Common questions

**May learners use the help?** Decide before the course. The questions work both ways; with the help open, shorten
the time to 20 minutes.

**My site has other fields on Article.** Question 4 names the fields of a standard installation. Change it to your
site's form.

### Pitfalls

- **Role differences.** If learners may not empty the trash on your site, question 9 is still answerable from the
  page; say so.
- **Approval workflows.** On a site with approval, publishing makes a version pending. Question 2 covers it.

## The test

1. You press **Edit** on a published article and change the title, but have not published yet. What do visitors of
   the site see?
2. Which version state means "sent for publishing, but a workflow holds it"? A. Draft B. Pending C. Published D.
   Archived
3. You want to stop working on an article for today and continue tomorrow. Which button do you press?
4. On a standard installation, which two fields of an article are required?
5. What happens to the version that was published before, when you publish a new one?
6. True or false, and why: **Discard draft** on an existing article removes the article.
7. You opened an article and the page **Possible edit conflict** appears. Name two things you may do.
8. How do you bring back the text of an article as it was three versions ago?
9. You removed a folder by mistake. Where is it, and how do you get it back?
10. An item has two locations. You remove one of them. Is the item in the trash?
11. Why should every image have an alternative text?
12. You upload twenty photos and want to check their titles before anyone sees them. Which upload option helps?
13. An item exists in English and German. What do you do before pressing **Edit** to change the German text?
14. You know that an item has the node number 1234. What is the fastest way to open it?

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. The published version; the draft is visible only to its creator until it is published
   ([Publishing, versions and the trash](../../../publishing-and-versions.md#drafts-and-versions)).
2. **B.** Pending: sent for publishing, but a workflow, usually an approval, holds it
   ([Publishing, versions and the trash](../../../publishing-and-versions.md#drafts-and-versions)).
3. **Store draft and exit**; the draft waits under **My drafts**
   ([Publishing, versions and the trash](../../../publishing-and-versions.md#publish)).
4. **Title** and **Intro**, on a standard installation; a red `*` marks a required field in the form
   ([Creating your first article](../../editors-101/module-03-creating-and-editing/creating-your-first-article.md),
   [Creating and editing content](../../../editing-content.md#the-edit-form)).
5. It becomes archived and is kept, so you can go back to it
   ([Publishing, versions and the trash](../../../publishing-and-versions.md#drafts-and-versions)).
6. **False.** It throws the draft away; the published version stays. Only for a new item that was never published
   does discarding mean the item is not created
   ([Creating and editing content](../../../editing-content.md#create-a-new-item)).
7. Continue one of your own drafts (**Edit selected**), start a **New draft** from the published version, or
   **Cancel** ([Creating and editing content](../../../editing-content.md#when-someone-else-is-editing)).
8. **Manage versions**, choose the old version's translation under **New draft from this version, in**, press
   **Make a new draft**, check it and publish it
   ([Publishing, versions and the trash](../../../publishing-and-versions.md#go-back-to-an-earlier-version)).
9. In the **Trash**, with everything below it; **Restore** puts it back in its original place if that place still
   exists ([Publishing, versions and the trash](../../../publishing-and-versions.md#the-trash)).
10. No. It stays in its other location; it goes to the trash only when its last location is removed
    ([Publishing, versions and the trash](../../../publishing-and-versions.md#remove-and-restore)).
11. For people who use a screen reader, and for when the image cannot load
    ([Media and uploads](../../../media-and-uploads.md#good-practice)).
12. **Keep as drafts**: the new items wait in **My drafts** until they are edited and published
    ([Media and uploads](../../../media-and-uploads.md#options)).
13. Choose German in the language list next to **Edit**, then press **Edit**
    ([Languages](../../../languages.md#translate-an-item)).
14. Type the number into the **Content finder** below the tree and press **Find**
    ([Searching](../../../searching.md#open-an-item-by-its-number-or-address)).

</details>

## Scoring

- One point per question; questions 7 and 8 need both parts for the point (half a point for one part).
- Pass: 10 of 14 points or more, and question 6 answered correctly, because confusing "discard" with "remove" costs
  content in real work.
- Write the result as "passed" or "not yet" and give feedback as in
  [Lesson 1.6](../module-01-designing-assessments/giving-feedback-on-results.md).
