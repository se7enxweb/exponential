# Lesson 2.4: Exponential Layouts, practical task

In this task each learner builds a small landing page layout, works with drafts the way a careful editor does, and
reads the mappings to explain where a layout is used. The result is visible in the editor and on the **Layouts**
list, so it can be scored quickly.

> **Note**
> This is the trainer's own assessment, not an official certification. Use a training installation, never a live
> site. Learners create and publish layouts of their own; they do not change existing mappings.

## Learning objectives

Learners who pass this task have shown that they can:

- create a layout of a chosen type and name it;
- add blocks of different types to different zones and arrange them;
- change a block's **Content** and **Design** properties;
- keep a draft and publish deliberately;
- read the mappings and explain which layout a given page uses.

## Trainer's notes

### How to run it

1. **Before**: check that every learner's account shows the **Layouts** tab, that the layout type *2 columns* exists
   on the installation (pick another two-zone type if not, and change step 2), and that at least one enabled mapping
   exists that learners can read.
2. **Briefing**: hand out the steps and the rubric. Layout names start with the learner's account name, so the trainer
   finds them in the list.
3. **Scoring**: open each learner's layout from **Layouts** with **Open** and read the blocks; check the dates on the
   list. Leave with **Cancel**, never **Discard**, so the learner's draft survives.

### Common questions

**The editor asks to clear caches after publishing.** Choose **Clear caches** on a training installation, as the help
says.

**May learners map their layout to a page?** Only if your course taught it and the installation is theirs alone.
Changing mappings decides what whole parts of the site look like.

### Pitfalls

- **Shared installation, shared mappings.** A learner who edits an existing mapping changes everyone's pages. The task
  only reads mappings.
- **Dragging problems.** Learners who cannot drag reliably (touchpad, accessibility) may need more time; see
  [Lesson 1.4](../module-01-designing-assessments/accommodations-in-assessment.md).

## Hands-on exercise

The task, as handed to learners. Use your training installation and your own account.

1. Open the **Layouts** tab. Note the numbers of layouts and mappings on the **Layouts Dashboard**.
2. Press **New layout**. Choose the layout type *2 columns*, name it `<your account> landing page` and press
   **Create layout**.
3. Press **Add a block** and drag a **Title** block into the first zone. In its **Content** tab, type
   `Welcome to our training`.
4. Add a **Rich text** (or **Text**) block below the title in the same zone, with two sentences.
5. Add an **Image** block to the second zone and choose any image of the media library.
6. Open the menu with three dots at the top and choose **Save draft and close**.
7. Open your layout again from **Layouts** with **Open**. Check that your three blocks are there.
8. Select the title block and change something in its **Design** tab (for example the view type or style).
9. Use **Duplicate block** on the rich text block, change the copy's text, then **Delete block** the copy again.
10. Press **Publish layout**. If the editor asks about caches, choose **Clear caches**.
11. Open **Rules** and find one enabled mapping. Write down its layout, its **Targets** and its **Conditions**, and in
    one sentence which pages it applies to.

## Expected result

- On **Layouts**, `<account> landing page` is listed with the type *2 columns* and a current date.
- Opened in the editor, it shows the title, the rich text in the first zone and the image in the second; the title's
  design differs from the default; there is no duplicated rich text block.
- The learner's note names a real mapping, its targets and conditions and a correct sentence about where it applies.
- The trainer checks the layout with **Open** and **Cancel**, and the note against **Rules**.

## Scoring rubric

| Criterion | 0: Not shown | 1: Partly | 2: Mostly | 3: Fully | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Layout created | None | Wrong type or unnamed | Right type, name not as asked | Right type and name | **Layouts** list |
| Blocks in zones | No blocks | One block | All blocks, one in the wrong zone | Title and text in zone 1, image in zone 2 | The editor |
| Content and design | Defaults only | Content set, design unchanged | Both set, text incomplete | Content and design changed as asked, no leftover copy | Block properties |
| Draft and publish (must) | Layout lost or discarded | Never published | Published, but the draft step skipped | Draft kept in step 6, published in step 10 | Dates on the list, learner's account of steps |
| Reading mappings | No note | Layout named only | Targets or conditions missing | Layout, targets, conditions and a correct sentence | The note and **Rules** |

**Pass**: 10 of 15 points or more and **Draft and publish** at 2 or 3.

## Quiz

Debrief questions to ask after scoring.

1. In step 7, what did visitors see while your layout was only a draft?
2. Why did the task ask you to read a mapping but not to change one?
3. What would **Discard** have done in step 6?
4. Your block shows no change on the site after publishing. What do you check?

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. Nothing of it: an unpublished layout is not on the site
   ([The layouts editor](../../../layouts-editor.md#the-editor)).
2. Changing mappings decides what whole parts of the site look like
   ([The layouts editor](../../../layouts-editor.md#which-pages-use-a-layout)).
3. Thrown the draft away after a confirmation and closed the editor
   ([The layouts editor](../../../layouts-editor.md#publish-keep-or-throw-away)).
4. That the layout was published, that a mapping uses it for that page, and whether the caches were cleared
   ([The layouts editor](../../../layouts-editor.md#publish-keep-or-throw-away)).

</details>
