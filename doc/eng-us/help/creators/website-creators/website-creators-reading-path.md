# The website creator's reading path

This page is for website creators who want to learn in order. It follows a site from first look to maintenance in six
stages. Each stage ends with what you should be able to do; when you can, move on.

## Stage 1: look around (a day)

1. [What you can build with Exponential](what-you-can-build.md)
2. [Your first site, step by step](your-first-site-step-by-step.md)
3. [The admin tour](../../admin-tour.md)
4. [Editing content](../../editing-content.md)

You can: install a demo site, sign in, create and publish an article.

## Stage 2: plan (a few days)

1. [Planning your website](planning-your-website.md)
2. [Site map and content inventory](site-map-and-content-inventory.md)
3. [Designing your content model](designing-your-content-model.md)
4. [Choosing an install method for a new site](choosing-an-install-method.md)
5. [Hosting requirements for a website](hosting-for-a-website.md)

You can: hand someone a plan with the classes, the tree, the languages and the hosting.

## Stage 3: build the structure

1. [Content classes for websites](content-classes-for-websites.md)
2. [Choosing datatypes for your fields](choosing-datatypes.md)
3. [Structuring the content tree](structuring-the-content-tree.md)
4. [Sections and object states for site builders](sections-and-object-states.md)
5. [Relations and content reuse](relations-and-reuse.md)
6. [Tagging content with eztags](tagging-with-eztags.md)
7. [Setting up several sites in one installation](multisite-setup.md) and [Building a multilingual site](multilingual-sites.md), when needed

You can: create the classes and folders of your site and fill them with real content.

## Stage 4: design

1. [Choosing a design or theme](choosing-a-design-or-theme.md)
2. [Designs, siteaccesses and the design fallback chain](designs-and-the-fallback-chain.md)
3. [The pagelayout: your site's frame](the-pagelayout.md)
4. [Template overrides for site builders](template-overrides.md)
5. [Stylesheets and scripts for your site](stylesheets-and-scripts.md)
6. [Menus and navigation](menus-and-navigation.md)
7. [Branding](branding-your-site.md)
8. [Images and image variations](images-and-image-variations.md)
9. [Landing pages with the layouts editor](landing-pages-with-layouts.md), then [Layout types, zones and blocks](layout-types-zones-and-blocks.md), [Layout rules](layout-rules.md) and [Shared layouts](shared-header-and-footer.md)
10. [Accessibility for site builders](accessibility-for-site-builders.md)

You can: show every page type in your own design, with landing pages editors can change.

## Stage 5: features

Pick what your site needs:

- Forms: [Forms with the information collector](forms-with-the-information-collector.md), [Building a contact form](building-a-contact-form.md), [Spam protection](spam-protection-for-forms.md)
- Newsletter: [Newsletters with cjw_newsletter](newsletters-with-cjw-newsletter.md), [Sending newsletters safely](sending-newsletters.md)
- Shop: [Running a shop](shop-overview.md), [Products, prices and VAT](products-prices-and-vat.md), [Basket, checkout and orders](basket-checkout-and-orders.md), [Payment gateways](payment-gateways.md)
- Members: [Member registration](member-registration-and-areas.md), [Visitor permissions](visitor-permissions.md)
- Search: [Site search](site-search.md), [Search with ezfind and Solr](search-with-ezfind-and-solr.md)
- Findability: [SEO basics](seo-basics.md), [Page metadata](metadata-and-social-sharing.md), [XML sitemaps](sitemaps.md), [URL aliases](url-aliases-and-nice-urls.md)

You can: run every feature end to end, logged out, with test data.

## Stage 6: launch and run

1. [Performance and caching for websites](performance-and-caching.md)
2. [Testing your site's accessibility](testing-accessibility.md)
3. [The launch checklist](launch-checklist.md)
4. [Going live: hosts, HTTPS and siteaccesses](going-live-hosts-https.md)
5. [Handing the site over to editors](handing-over-to-editors.md)
6. [Maintaining your website](maintaining-your-website.md), [Backups and restores](backups-and-restores.md), [Updates and upgrades](updates-and-upgrades.md)

You can: launch, hand over and keep the site healthy.

modules, fetch functions, datatypes, workflows and APIs. Project managers plan the same journey in
