# Feature requests and sponsored features

This page is for partners whose customers need something Exponential does not yet do.

## Five ways a feature gets built

| Way | Cost | Speed | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Public feature request | None | Unpredictable | <https://github.com/se7enxweb/exponential/issues> |
| Your own pull request | Your developers' time | You control it | CONTRIBUTING.md, [Contributing code back](contributing-code-back.md) |
| Paid development by 7x | A quote from 7x | Agreed | <https://exponential.earth/support> ("Hire 7x!") |
| A bounty through GitHub Sponsors | One-time tiers as listed | Agreed | "One bug or medium sized bounty" ($1,000 one time) on both sponsor pages |
| A sponsor tier with backlog influence | Monthly | Priority, not a promise | 7x tier "$750 a month": "a bounty for the development of a feature ... tailored to your needs" |

Sponsor tiers were read from the GitHub Sponsors pages <https://github.com/sponsors/se7enxweb> and
<https://github.com/sponsors/exponentialcms> on 18 October 2026; the live pages are authoritative. Details:
[Sponsoring 7x on GitHub](../sponsors/sponsoring-7x.md) and
[Sponsoring the Exponential Foundation](../sponsors/sponsoring-the-foundation.md).

The sponsors page of the project says "Sponsors take advantage of inside access to 7x Team and get their features
considered" (<https://exponential.earth/about/project/sponsors>). "Considered" is the right word to use with
customers: funding raises priority; it does not buy a guarantee unless a written contract says so.

## Keeping it open

A feature paid by one customer and published for all is the best outcome for everyone: the customer gets
maintenance by the community, the community gets the feature. Agree in the customer contract that generic features
may be published under the GPL ([Open source values in daily partner work](open-source-values-for-partners.md)).

## Decision guide

| The feature is | Then |
|---|---|
| Small, general, and you have developers | Send a pull request |
| General but large | Discuss in an issue first, then fund or build it with others |
| Specific to one customer | Build it as a customer extension; do not push it into the kernel |
| Urgent and critical | Paid development with a written agreement |
