# Your first year as a partner

This page is for partnership owners planning twelve months. A partnership "lasts one year, with renewals and
upgrades based on proven activity", and "Your activity over the previous 12 months will determine your new level"
(<https://exponential.earth/partner/requirements>, <https://exponential.earth/partner/why>, read 18 October 2026).
Plan the year so the review has something to show.

## The year in four quarters

| Quarter | Theme | Goals | Pages |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Foundations | Onboarding done; partner page live; team on current Exponential; first contribution | [Onboarding checklist](onboarding-checklist.md), [First 30 days](your-first-30-days.md) |
| Q2 | Deliver | One project delivered on a current 6.0.x release, with the customer's permission to name it | [Implementation project playbook](implementation-project-playbook.md) |
| Q3 | Show | Case study, one tutorial or article, conference talk or local meetup if possible | [Writing a case study](writing-a-case-study.md), [Earning points through community work](earning-points-through-community-work.md) |
| Q4 | Review | Points and revenue counted; certifications planned; renewal decision | [Renewals, upgrades and downgrades](renewals-upgrades-and-downgrades.md) |

## Measures that matter

| Measure | Why | Where to get it |
|---|---|---|
| Points earned | Decides your level | Your own log and the Partner Dashboard once the partner website is online, planned for mid-2027 ([Logging your partner activity](logging-partner-activity.md)) |
| Revenue counted by the program | Required from Professional | The partner team |
| Customer sites on a supported release | Shows you keep customers safe | Your own inventory ([Keeping customer sites current](keeping-customers-current.md)) |
| Contributions merged | Shows you give back | GitHub |
| Tickets used and response times met | Shows the level fits | Your ticket history |

## Quarterly review template

```text
Quarter review <YYYY-Qn> — <Company>

Points this quarter: ____   Year to date: ____   Target level threshold: ____
Revenue counted (if applicable): ____
Projects delivered: ______________________
Contributions: PRs ___  bug reports ___  docs/translations ___  forum ___
Tickets opened: ___   met response time: ___ of ___
What worked: ______________________
What to change next quarter: ______________________
Help we need from 7x / the community: ______________________
```

## Common first-year mistakes

- Choosing a level for its logo, then not using its tickets or meetings.
- Leaving customer sites on old releases because nobody owns upgrades.
- Writing the case study at the end of the year, when the customer contact has moved on.
- Not logging activity monthly, then reconstructing it from memory before the review.
