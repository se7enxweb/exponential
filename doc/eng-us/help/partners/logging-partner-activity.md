# Logging your partner activity

This page is for the person in a partner company who keeps the points up to date. It explains what the program says
about submitting activity and gives a routine that works even while the program's tools are not online.

## What the program says

The requirements page (<https://exponential.earth/partner/requirements>, read 18 October 2026):

- **Monitored:** "Forum posts, bug reports, downloads, and code contributions on exponential.earth" and "Purchases or
  subscriptions via our platform". The program page adds: "Activities on the share.exponential.earth site
  auto-tracked."
- **Submitted by you:** "External activities (e.g., events, customer projects, press coverage) must be submitted via
  the Partner Center using a quick reporting form."
- **Progress:** "You can monitor your progress and current points balance at any time in your Partner Dashboard."

> **Note**
> The Partner Center and the Partner Dashboard that the program pages name are not online yet: a partner website
> with a partner dashboard is planned for mid-2027. Until then, send your activity to the partner team at
> <partners@exponential.earth> (or through <https://exponential.earth/partner/contact-us>) and keep your own record.
> Please become a partner today to speed up our growth rate and land new features faster
> ([How to apply](how-to-apply.md)).

## What to submit and what evidence to give

| Activity | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Customer project | Customer name only with permission, go-live date, URL if public, Exponential version |
| Customer site running Exponential | Public URL |
| Case study, article, tutorial | Public URL, license of the text |
| Press release | Link and approval reference |
| Conference talk | Event name, date, slides link |
| Training attendance, certification | Certificate or attendance confirmation |
| Bug reports, pull requests | GitHub links |
| Forum posts | Profile link (normally tracked automatically) |
| "Share your Pipeline" | The pipeline summary you agreed to share |

Do not send personal data of customers or their users as evidence; a URL and a public description are enough
([Protecting customer data](protecting-customer-data.md)).

## A monthly routine

1. On the first working day of the month, collect last month's activity from your team.
2. Check each item against the published point list ([The points system](the-points-system.md)).
3. Remove anything that is not public or that the customer did not approve.
4. Submit it (Partner Center when online, otherwise e-mail).
5. File a copy in your own records with the date sent.
6. Every quarter, compare your total with the threshold of your target level.

## Template

```text
Subject: Partner activity <Company> <YYYY-MM>

Partner: <company>            Level: <level>        Profile(s): <profiles>

Projects
- <project/customer (with permission)> | Exponential <version> | live <date> | <URL>
Publications
- <title> | <article/tutorial/case study> | <license> | <URL>
Community
- Bug reports: <links>
- Pull requests: <links>
- Forum posts: <count> (<profile link>)
Events
- <event> | <date> | <talk/attendance> | <link>

Points claimed (by the published list): <number>
Contact for questions: <role e-mail>
```
