# Co-marketing with 7x and the project

This page is for marketing staff in partner companies. It explains the co-marketing benefits and how to use them
well.

## What the program offers

| Benefit | Levels | As published (read 18 October 2026) |
|---|---|---|
| Dedicated page on share.exponential.earth | All | "Get your own dedicated page to promote your services" (<https://exponential.earth/partner/benefits>) |
| Extra visibility on share.exponential.earth | Basic and higher (benefits page); Gold and Platinum (program page) | benefits page; <https://exponential.earth/partner/program> |
| Joint marketing activities | Silver and higher (benefits page); Professional and higher (program page) | same |
| Co-marketing budget (up to 5%) | Gold and Platinum | benefits page; "Co-marketing investment from 7x" on the program page |
| Marketing placement | Silver: Medium, Gold: High, Platinum: Priority | benefits page |
| Sales tools, marketing exposure | Program-wide | "Access to sales tools, marketing exposure, and licenses strengthens business performance" (<https://exponential.earth/partner>) |

What the 5% refers to (your revenue, your subscription, the campaign cost) is not stated. Ask before you plan a
budget around it.

## Campaign ideas

| Idea | Effort | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| Joint case study | Medium | Every level; earns points ([Writing a case study](writing-a-case-study.md)) |
| Webinar: "What's new in the latest release" | Medium | Implementation and hosting partners |
| Local meetup or user group | High | Building a regional community |
| Migration offer for sites on old eZ Publish era installs | Medium | Implementation partners |
| Tutorial series | Medium | Developers' trust; earns points |
| Newsletter feature | Low | Announcing partnerships and releases |

## Rules for joint messages

- Every claim has a source; no invented numbers ([Sales and marketing: start here](../business/sales-and-marketing-start-here.md#the-ground-rules)).
- Name everyone correctly ([Naming and brand rules for partners](naming-and-brand-for-partners.md)).
- Joint material is approved in writing by both sides before publication.
- Customers are named only with their written permission.
- Use only the logos your level allows ([Using partner logos and the certified mark](partner-logos-and-marks.md)).

## Measuring

Agree before the campaign what success means: sign-ups, leads, downloads, attendees. Use numbers you can measure
yourself without collecting more personal data than needed ([Protecting customer data](protecting-customer-data.md)).
