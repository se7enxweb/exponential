# Your first 30 days as a partner

This page is for the partnership owner. It turns the [Onboarding checklist](onboarding-checklist.md) into four
weeks, so that by day 30 the partnership is visible, usable and planned.

## Week 1: set up

| Task | Outcome |
|---|---|
| File the agreement; set the renewal reminders | Nothing administrative is forgotten in a year |
| Create or check GitHub and share.exponential.earth accounts | Your team can open tickets and post |
| Join Telegram, the forums and the mailing lists | You hear about releases and meetings |
| Agree who may open partner tickets | Tickets are not wasted |

## Week 2: become visible

| Task | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Write your partner page ([Your partner page](your-partner-page.md)) | Customers can find you |
| Add the right logo for your level to your site ([Partner logos](partner-logos-and-marks.md)) | Your level is shown correctly |
| Announce the partnership on your own channels, without speaking for 7x or the Foundation ([Press releases](press-releases-and-announcements.md)) | Your network knows |

## Week 3: learn the routines

| Task | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Open a first, real support ticket and note the response time | You know how support works in practice |
| Install the current Exponential from Git in a test environment ([Develop with Git](../develop-with-git.md)) | Your developers are on the current code |
| Read the latest changelog and decide which customers need an upgrade | A first upgrade list ([Keeping customer sites current](keeping-customers-current.md)) |

## Week 4: give back and plan

| Task | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Send one bug report or pull request ([Contributing code back](contributing-code-back.md)) | Your first community points |
| Write down three goals for the year (projects, points, certifications) | A plan for [your first year](your-first-year.md) |
| Book or request your first guidance meeting (all levels have at least one a quarter) | You discuss the plan with 7x |

## The day-30 check

- [ ] The partner page is live and correct.
- [ ] Every person who talks to customers knows the naming rules.
- [ ] One ticket opened, one contribution sent.
- [ ] The first-year plan exists and is shared with 7x.
- [ ] The first activity log is sent ([Logging your partner activity](logging-partner-activity.md)).
