# Handing leads over and back

This page is for partner sales staff. The program shares leads ([Referrals and leads](referrals-and-leads.md)) but
does not publish a hand-off procedure. This page proposes one that keeps the customer's experience first and avoids
disputes. Agree its details with 7x and with partners you work with.

> **Note**
> The process below is a recommended practice, not a published program rule.

## Principles

1. **The customer chooses.** A lead is a person or organization with a need, not property.
2. **One owner at a time.** Whoever has the lead says so, and answers the customer.
3. **Fast or not at all.** If you cannot respond quickly, hand the lead back.
4. **Written.** Every hand-off has a note and a date.

## The hand-off note

```text
Lead hand-off
From: <company/role>   To: <company/role>   Date: <date>
Customer: <organization> (contact details shared with consent: yes/no)
Need: <one paragraph>
What was said or promised so far: <...>
Cold or hot (as agreed): <...>
Commission / referral terms: <reference to written agreement>
Next step and by when: <...>
```

## Response times

Agree them with your partners. A useful default: acknowledge the lead within one business day, contact the customer
within two.

## Returning a lead

If the project does not fit your skills, size or region, hand it back or on with a short reason. The program's
value **Sharing** ("work together as a team, both internally and with customers, partners and the Exponential
community", <https://exponential.earth/partner/program>) applies here. Returning a lead you cannot serve is good
conduct, not failure.

## Conflicts between partners

- If two partners already talk to the same customer, the one with the earlier documented contact continues, unless
  the customer decides otherwise.
- Never disparage another partner to win a customer ([Partner code of conduct](partner-code-of-conduct.md)).
- Unresolved conflicts go to the partner team at 7x.

## Closing the loop

Tell the referring party the outcome (won, lost, no decision) without sharing confidential details. It improves the
next referral.
