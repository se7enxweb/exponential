# Partner plans and prices

This page is for whoever budgets the partnership. Prices are in US dollars per month. The levels and the tables
follow the current partner benefits page, <https://exponential.earth/partner/benefits> (read 18 October 2026);
where an older page of the program shows a different table, the benefits page applies. The store and the live
pages are authoritative: "7x reserves full rights to change or administer this program"
(<https://exponential.earth/partner/program>).

## The plans

| Plan | Price per month | GitHub issue tickets | Response time | Guidance meetings | Revenue requirement per quarter |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Student | $25 | 2 a week | ≤ 36 h | 1 a quarter | None |
| Now | $45 | 2 a week | ≤ 36 h | 1 a quarter | None |
| Starter | $175 | 15 a month | ≤ 36 h | 1 a quarter | None |
| Basic | $300 | 15 a month | ≤ 24 h | 3 a quarter | None |
| Professional | $500 | 5 a week | ≤ 12 h | 2 a month | $1,800 |
| Silver | $800 | 7 a week | ≤ 12 h | 3 a month | $3,000 |
| Gold | $1,000 | 10 a week | ≤ 4 h | 6 a quarter | $4,500 |
| Platinum | $2,600 | 15 a week | ≤ 3 h | 12 a quarter | $7,500 |

Each plan has its own store page under <https://my.se7enx.com/store/exponential-partners>, for example
`.../exponential-student-partner-plan` and `.../exponential-platinum-partner-plan` (linked from the benefits page).

Whether response hours mean business hours or clock hours, and in which time zone, is not stated on the benefits
page. Ask for it in writing before you promise anything to your own customers that depends on it
([Support levels and response times](support-levels-and-response-times.md)).

The Kick-Off Package for Basic "includes program fee, book, online training, certification, and 8h support for
$3,200 (30% discount)" (<https://exponential.earth/partner/benefits>).

## How to budget

1. **Pick the level by need, not prestige.** Count the tickets and meetings you will really use in a quarter.
2. **Add the revenue requirement.** From Professional on, you must generate the published revenue per quarter; see
   [Partner requirements](partner-requirements.md) for what counts.
3. **Add certification costs from Silver.** Prices for certifications are not published; see
   [Certifications](certifications.md).
4. **Plan a year.** "Partnership lasts one year" (requirements page); budget twelve months.
5. **Subtract the discounts you will use.** The discounts by level are in the table above and on
   [Partner benefits](partner-benefits.md).

```text
Partnership budget, year 1
  Level ......................  ________
  Monthly price x 12 .........  $________
  Kick-Off Package (Basic) ...  $3,200 (if taken)
  Certifications (if any) ....  $________
  Staff time (hours x rate) ..  $________
  Expected discounts .........- $________
  Expected referral income ...- $________   (do not count on it in year 1)
  Net cost ...................  $________
```

## Invoices and taxes

Invoices for the partner subscription come from the store's seller, 7x. Tax treatment of a business subscription
is not described in the sources; ask your accountant. The partner subscription is a purchase of services, not a
donation; for donations see [Sponsors: start here](../sponsors/sponsors-start-here.md).
