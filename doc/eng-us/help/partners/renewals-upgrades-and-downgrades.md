# Renewals, upgrades and downgrades

This page is for partnership owners at review time. It collects what the program pages say about the yearly cycle
(read 18 October 2026).

## The yearly cycle

- "Partnership is valid for one year. We'll evaluate your standing 30 days before expiration. Your activity over the
  previous 12 months will determine your new level" (<https://exponential.earth/partner/why>).
- "Renewing an existing partnership: Valid for 1 year" (<https://exponential.earth/partner/program>).
- Partners "who do not reach the Professional level annually must pay this fee again each year"
  (<https://exponential.earth/partner/requirements>).

## Upgrades

- "Partner tiers are reviewed monthly. You will always be placed in the highest tier for which you meet the
  requirements" (requirements page).
- "If you surpass your current tier during the year, you'll be upgraded immediately, unlocking even more benefits"
  (why page).

How an upgrade changes the subscription price in the store is not described. Ask before you accept an upgrade, and
budget for the higher price ([Partner plans and prices](partner-plans-and-prices.md)).

## Downgrades

- "If a partner downgrades (e.g., from Gold to Silver), associated benefits adjust accordingly. Partners are required
  to update their use of the Exponential Partner logo within 30 days to reflect their new level"
  (<https://exponential.earth/partner/benefits>).
- The contract: "Benefits reflect current tier. Changes to logo/branding must occur within 30 days" (program page).

Downgrade checklist:

- [ ] Replace the logo on your website, proposals, e-mail signatures and printed material within 30 days
- [ ] Update your partner page and any directory listing
- [ ] Tell your sales team which benefits (referrals, pre-sales, white label) no longer apply
- [ ] Check open commitments to customers that relied on the higher level's response times

## Renewal checklist

Start 45 days before the end date.

- [ ] Count points and revenue for the last 12 months ([Logging your partner activity](logging-partner-activity.md))
- [ ] Compare with the thresholds ([Partner requirements](partner-requirements.md))
- [ ] Decide: renew at the same level, apply for a higher one, or step down
- [ ] Check certifications that need renewing ([Certifications](certifications.md))
- [ ] Confirm the subscription in the store and the invoice details
- [ ] Update the first-year plan into a second-year plan ([Your first year](your-first-year.md))

## Ending a partnership

The contract: "30-day termination notice", "All commissions still due", "Material must be returned after breach"
(program page). To end cleanly:

1. Give notice in writing, 30 days ahead.
2. Settle open referrals and commissions.
3. Remove partner logos and the partner page reference within the notice period.
4. Keep your customers informed about who supports their sites from now on.

Ending a partnership changes nothing in your right to work with Exponential under the GPL.
