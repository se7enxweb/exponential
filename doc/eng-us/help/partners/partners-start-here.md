# Partners: start here

This section is for **Exponential Partners**: organizations, companies and individual professionals who support
Exponential directly, as sustaining partners in the shared success of the community on the internet. It is also for
everyone who is thinking about becoming one: agencies, developers, designers, hosting companies, resellers, trainers,
schools and freelancers.

It answers four questions: what an Exponential Partner is, how the partner program works as it is published today,
what a partner does in practice (projects, hosting, extensions, marketing, referrals, contributions), and where the
published sources stop and the Exponential Foundation still has to decide.

## Exponential partnership in one minute

- **What the program is.** "The official partner program is designed for companies who deliver professional
  products and services based on the Exponential open source Content Management System"
  (<https://exponential.earth/partner/apply>, read 18 October 2026).
- **Who runs it today.** The published program is run by **7x**: "7x categorizes partners into the following
  levels", and the partnership contract is signed "On behalf of 7x" (<https://exponential.earth/partner/program>).
  The role of the **Exponential Foundation** in the partner program is not described in the published sources:
  to be defined by the Foundation. See [Who runs the partner program](who-runs-the-partner-program.md).
- **Levels.** Student, Now, Starter, Basic, Professional, Silver, Gold and Platinum
  ([Partner levels](partner-levels.md)).
- **Profiles.** IT, Creative, Hosting and Distribution partners, plus Development partners listed among the classes
  ([Partner profiles](partner-profiles.md)).
- **How you get in.** Apply at <https://exponential.earth/partner/apply>, sign the agreement, buy the partner
  subscription in the 7x store; or qualify through the points system ([How to apply](how-to-apply.md),
  [The points system](the-points-system.md)).
- **What it gives.** Revenue opportunities, visibility, partner meetings, support tickets, discounts and, at higher
  levels, referrals, co-marketing and roadmap requests ([Partner benefits](partner-benefits.md)).
- **What it asks.** A commitment to open source values and quality, yearly renewal, and at higher levels revenue,
  points and certifications ([Partner requirements](partner-requirements.md)).

> **Note**
> Two different offers carry the word "partner". The **Exponential Partner Program** at
> <https://exponential.earth/partner> is the program this section describes. The **7x Partner Subscription** at
> <https://se7enx.com/partner> is a managed service of 7x (hosting, support and development for your own site).
> [The partner program at a glance](partner-program-at-a-glance.md) compares them.

## The ground rules of this section

1. **Every fact names its source.** Prices, levels and benefits are quoted from the published program pages, with
   the date they were read. The live pages are authoritative; "7x reserves full rights to change or administer this
   program" (<https://exponential.earth/partner/program>).
2. **The benefits page is current.** The levels, prices, discounts, lead priority and benefits follow
   <https://exponential.earth/partner/benefits>; where an older program page differs, the benefits page applies.
   The partner terms (commission, Kick-Off Package, paying and signing, the partner address) are on
   [Partner plans and prices](partner-plans-and-prices.md#partner-terms).
3. **Where the sources are silent, the page says so.** "To be defined by the Foundation" marks every open question.
4. **Ideas are labeled as proposals.** Certification ideas and similar suggestions are marked "Proposal, not a
   program".
5. **No invented names.** No page names partner companies or members; the published directory requires sign-in
   ([Partner directories](partner-directories.md)).
