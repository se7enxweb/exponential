# Certifications

This page is for partners aiming at Silver or higher, and for anyone who wants to know what "Exponential Certified"
means. It separates what is published from what is not.

## What is published

From <https://exponential.earth/partner/requirements> (read 18 October 2026):

- "For Silver tier and above, partners must hold valid certifications."
- Profile certifications: "Exponential Developer Certification (for technical teams)", "Exponential Design
  Certification (for design/ux teams)", "Exponential Solution Certification (for content-focused teams)".
- Sales certifications: "Each solution includes an associated Sales Certification, complete with: Product training;
  Certification in a single-day session".

From <https://exponential.earth/partner/program>:

- "Certifications: Required for Silver and higher. Types: Developer, WCMS, Solution, Sales. Sales certification
  includes training and materials. All certifications can be earned in 1 day."
- "Silver, Gold and Platinum partners are certified and can use the 'Exponential certified' logo on their website
  and in their marketing material."

| Level | Certifications required (requirements page) | (program page) |
|---|---|---|
| Silver | 1 profile + 2 sales | 1 profile + 1 sales |
| Gold | 2 profile + 6 sales | 2 profile + 2 sales |
| Platinum | 3 profile + 10 sales | 3 profile + 3 sales |

A certification earns 1,000 points per person ([The points system](the-points-system.md)).

## What is not published

The following are not described on the program pages and are **to be defined by the Foundation** (or by 7x, which
runs the program):

- the syllabus and learning objectives of each certification;
- the exam format, the pass mark and the language(s);
- the price, outside the Kick-Off Package, which "includes program fee, book, online training, certification, and 8h
  support" (its own price is [to be confirmed](partner-plans-and-prices.md#values-still-to-be-confirmed));
- the dates and places, online or in person;
- how long a certification stays valid and how it is renewed;
- who may examine, and whether the Foundation endorses the certificates.

Ask the partner team (<https://exponential.earth/partner/contact-us>) before you plan a team's certification schedule.
