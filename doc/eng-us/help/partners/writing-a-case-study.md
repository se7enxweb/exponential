# Writing a case study

This page is for partners who want to tell the story of a project. The sales team's detailed process is in
[Writing case studies and content marketing](../business/case-studies-and-content-marketing.md); this page is the
partner's version, with the program's rules.

## Why it matters

A case study is proof that Exponential works for real organizations, and proof of your skill. The program rewards it:
"Case study published on exponential.earth" earns 1,000 points (<https://exponential.earth/partner/requirements>,
read 18 October 2026; the program page names <https://7xwebsites.com> as the place), and "Customer reference
published" 500.

## The steps

| Step | Do | Do not |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Choose | A project live for a few months, with a result the customer is proud of | A project still in trouble |
| 2. Consent | Ask the customer in writing before you write anything ([Case studies, step 2](../business/case-studies-and-content-marketing.md#step-2-ask-for-consent-before-you-write)) | Write first and ask later |
| 3. Interview | Ask about the problem, the decision, the result, in their words | Put words in their mouth |
| 4. Facts | Collect versions, features used, numbers the customer can confirm | Invent or round up figures |
| 5. Draft | Use the [Case study template](case-study-template.md) | Turn it into an advertisement |
| 6. Check | Every fact against a source or the customer's confirmation | Leave unchecked claims |
| 7. Approve | Written approval of the final text by the customer | Publish an unapproved version |
| 8. Publish | On your site, and submit it to the program | Publish in 7x's or the Foundation's name |
| 9. Maintain | Update or withdraw it if facts change or the customer asks | Leave outdated claims online |

## What makes it credible

- Real problem, real constraints, real result.
- The Exponential version and the features used (multilingual, layouts, shop, workflows ...).
- What did not work at first and how it was solved.
- No personal data beyond the names and roles the customer approved.
- Correct names: Exponential, 7x, the Exponential Foundation ([Naming and brand rules for partners](naming-and-brand-for-partners.md)).

## License

If you want others (7x, the Foundation, other partners) to reuse your case study, publish it under a free license
and say so. The program asks for "FLOSS-licensed" articles to earn article points.
