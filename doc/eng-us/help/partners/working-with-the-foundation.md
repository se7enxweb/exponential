# Working with the Exponential Foundation

This page is for partners who want to understand and support the nonprofit behind the open source project.

## What the Foundation is

- "a 501(c)(3) Non-Profit Corporation founded in Seattle, Washington, USA", founded on 19 March 2026
  (<https://foundation.exponential.earth/>).
- "The mission of the Exponential Foundation is dedicated directly to the continued Funding, Adoption, Support,
  Direction, Education, Development, Promotion and Documentation for the Exponential Enterprise Open Source Project"
  (same page).
- "This project is operated by the Exponential Foundation" (<https://exponential.earth/about/project>).
- It names 7x "the current leader in contributions both financial, code, extension development, new features,
  infrastructure, maintenance and much much more back to the project" (<https://foundation.exponential.earth/supporters>).

The whole picture: [Who is the Exponential Foundation?](../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md).

## The Foundation and the partner program

The published partner program is run by 7x. No published page describes the Foundation's role in it: **to be
defined by the Foundation** ([Who runs the partner program](who-runs-the-partner-program.md)).

The Foundation states on its site: "Funds donated are tax free and tax deductible on the donner's taxes in the United
States." Check with your own tax adviser how this applies to your company; a partner subscription to 7x is a purchase
of services and is not a donation to the Foundation.

## Two organizations, one project

When you talk about the project, keep them apart:

- **7x** is a company that sells services, runs the partner program and leads development.
- **The Foundation** is a nonprofit that operates the project and funds its open source work.

Say "led by 7x and operated by the Exponential Foundation" when you need one sentence
([Naming and brand rules for partners](naming-and-brand-for-partners.md)).
