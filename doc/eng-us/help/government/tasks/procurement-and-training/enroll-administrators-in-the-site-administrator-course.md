# Enroll administrators in the site administration course

**Who:** the training coordinator and the IT manager. **When:** during the build, before go-live, so the people who
will run the site can take over. Course: [Site administration](../../../trainers/site-administrators/README.md),
48 lessons, about 30 hours ([Course catalogue](../../../trainers/course-catalogue.md)).

## Contents

1. [Before you start](#before-you-start)
2. [Steps](#steps)
3. [Result](#result)
4. [Pitfalls](#pitfalls)

## Before you start

- Name the people: at least two, so there is cover.
- Prerequisites from the catalogue: Editors 101 (modules 1 to 3 are enough for an administrator) and basic command
  line skills.

## Steps

1. **Check prerequisites**: run them through Editors 101 modules 1 to 3 first if needed
   ([Editors 101](../../../trainers/editors-101/README.md)).
2. **Choose a schedule**: the full course (about five days) or a shorter schedule from the course overview; the
   catalogue also mentions an intensive four-day schedule for an intranet administrator
   ([Course catalogue](../../../trainers/course-catalogue.md#combinations-for-common-groups)).
3. **Make sure the security modules are included**: [Module 7: logs and the audit trail](../../../trainers/site-administrators/module-07-logs-and-the-audit-trail/README.md)
   and [Module 8: security](../../../trainers/site-administrators/module-08-security/README.md), and
   [Module 9: backups and maintenance](../../../trainers/site-administrators/module-09-backups-and-maintenance/README.md).
4. **Provide a lab** on the same Exponential version as your site ([Lab quick card](../../../trainers/lab-quick-card.md)).
5. **Book the trainer**: your own trainer, a partner or 7x ([Buying training from a partner](../../procurement/training/buying-training-from-a-partner.md)).
6. **Assess**: the site administrators' written test and practical task
   ([Site administrators practical task](../../../trainers/assessments/module-03-administration-courses/site-administrators-practical-task.md)).
7. **Hand over the role**: after the course, the administrator role with two-step sign-in where your site uses it
   ([Two-step sign-in](../../../../guides/two-factor-login.md)); walk through the operating routine together
   ([Operating a site](../../../../guides/operating-a-site.md)).
8. **Plan the follow-up course** if they run servers too ([Hosting and operations](../../../trainers/hosting-operations/README.md)).

## Result

Two or more trained administrators who hold the administrator role in your organization, not only at the supplier.

## Pitfalls

- Only one trained person: no cover during leave.
- Training on a different version than production.
- The supplier keeps the only administrator accounts.
