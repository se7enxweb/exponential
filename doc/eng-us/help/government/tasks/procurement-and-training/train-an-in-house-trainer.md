# Train an in-house trainer

**Who:** the training coordinator. **When:** once the site is running and onboarding is a recurring need. Background:
[Building an in-house training team](../../procurement/training/building-an-in-house-training-team.md).

## Contents

1. [Before you start](#before-you-start)
2. [Steps](#steps)
3. [Result](#result)
4. [Pitfalls](#pitfalls)

## Before you start

- A candidate who uses the site every week and wants to teach.
- Agreement from their manager on time for preparation and teaching.

## Steps

1. **Train the trainer**: 34 lessons, about 21 hours, six modules: preparing to teach, building the lab, designing a
   course, delivering, accessibility and inclusion, assessment and follow-up
   ([Train the trainer](../../../trainers/train-the-trainer/README.md)).
2. **The subject course** as a participant, for example Editors 101.
3. **Build their lab** and practice a reset ([Building the lab](../../../trainers/train-the-trainer/module-02-building-the-lab/README.md),
   [Lab quick card](../../../trainers/lab-quick-card.md)).
4. **Adapt one course** to your site: content classes, sections, editorial rules; keep the GFDL notices
   ([Free documentation](../../../free-documentation.md)).
5. **Rehearse every exercise** on your production version
   ([Writing exercises that work](../../../trainers/train-the-trainer/module-03-designing-a-course/writing-exercises-that-work.md)).
6. **Teach back**: a pilot session for colleagues, observed by an experienced trainer if possible.
7. **Collect feedback** and improve ([Collecting feedback](../../../trainers/train-the-trainer/module-06-assessment-and-follow-up/collecting-feedback.md)).
8. **Put them on the onboarding rota** ([Onboarding new staff](../../procurement/training/onboarding-new-staff.md)).
9. **Plan upkeep**: review the material after each upgrade.

## Result

A trainer in your organization who can onboard new editors at any time with up-to-date material.

## Pitfalls

- Calling the result "official certification": what makes training official is not yet defined by the project
  ([Training partners](../../../partners/training-partners.md)).
- No time budget for preparation.
