# Request a support quote from a partner

**Who:** the project manager or contract manager. **When:** before or after the build, for the running phase. The
background: [Getting support](../../../project/getting-support.md),
[Evaluating a support partner](../../procurement/evaluation/evaluating-a-support-partner.md).

> **Note** This page describes common practice; it is not legal advice. Follow your organization's procurement rules,
> which may require a formal tender above a threshold.

## Before you start

- Know what you run: versions, extensions, engine, database, from **Setup > System information**, whose summary
  leaves out passwords and keys ([System information](../../../../guides/system-information.md)).
- Know who could quote: 7x ([Who is 7x?](../../../who-is-7x.md)) and partners ([Partners: start here](../../../partners/partners-start-here.md)).
- Know what the project itself does not offer: no SLA ([SLA options for Exponential hosting](../../../hosting/business/sla-options.md)).

## Steps

1. **Define the scope**: application support only, or hosting too; your own extensions included or not.
2. **Define severities** in your words (site down, security, major function broken, minor), and ask for a response and
   a resolution approach per severity; choose the times you need.
3. **Ask for security updates**: how they watch releases and how fast they apply them.
4. **Ask for routine updates**: included or per hour, and hours per update for your site.
5. **Ask for staffing**: who, how many people know Exponential, cover outside office hours.
6. **Ask for escalation**: how they reach the project for upstream problems; partners have their own route
   ([Escalations and security issues](../../../partners/escalations-and-security-issues.md)).
7. **Ask for reporting and exit**: monthly report contents; cooperation at handover.
8. **Send the same request** to every supplier and compare on the same basis.

## A request template

```text
Request for a support quote - <body>                         reply by <date>
Site: Exponential <version>, extensions <list>, engine <...>, database <...>, hosted by <...>
Scope: <application support | application + hosting>; custom extensions: <list>
Severities and the response we need: S1 <time>, S2 <time>, S3 <time>, S4 <time>, hours <...>
Security updates: applied within <time> of release
Routine updates: <included | hours per update>
Please state: staff and cover, escalation to the project, monthly report, price per <period>,
              hourly rate for extra work, notice period, exit cooperation.
```

## Result

Comparable quotes that answer the same questions.

## Pitfalls

- Sending passwords, database addresses or personal data in the request ([Getting support](../../../project/getting-support.md#how-to-ask-so-you-get-an-answer)).
- Treating a partner's level in the program as a promise to you; the partner's own contract is what counts
  ([Support levels and response times](../../../partners/support-levels-and-response-times.md)).
