# Build an evaluation team

**Who:** the procurement officer or project manager. **When:** before the tender is published, so the team helps set
the criteria. The method is in [Building an evaluation panel](../../procurement/evaluation/building-an-evaluation-panel.md).

> **Note** This page describes common practice; it is not legal advice. Follow your organization's procurement rules.

## Contents

1. [Before you start](#before-you-start)
2. [Steps](#steps)
3. [Result](#result)
4. [Pitfalls](#pitfalls)

## Before you start

- The requirements are drafted ([Requirements](../../procurement/requirements/README.md)).
- Your procurement rules on panels (size, independence, records) are known.

## Steps

1. **Cover the expertise** the requirements need:

   | Role | Evaluates |
   |---|---|
   | Editorial lead | editing, workflow, usability for editors |
   | Accessibility lead | accessibility evidence and the demo |
   | Security officer | security answers and hardening plans |
   | Data protection officer | processing, third parties, retention |
   | IT or hosting specialist | hosting, operations, upgrades |
   | Project manager | plan, staffing, cost model, exit |
   | Procurement officer | process, records, fairness (often non-scoring) |

2. **Collect conflict-of-interest declarations** from every member.
3. **Prepare the team**: each member installs or uses a test installation before bids arrive, so they know what the
   product does without a vendor's guidance. One command on SQLite is enough
   ([Run a proof of concept on SQLite in one command](run-a-proof-of-concept-on-sqlite.md)). A half-day introduction
   exists for decision makers ([Decision makers' half day](../../../trainers/workshops/module-02-one-day-agendas/decision-makers-half-day.md)).
4. **Share the facts** the panel needs: the standards pages for accessibility, security and privacy, and
   [What Exponential can and cannot evidence](../../procurement/requirements/what-exponential-can-and-cannot-evidence.md).
5. **Assign criteria** to members; each criterion has one lead scorer and one second scorer.
6. **Agree the scoring rules** before reading bids ([Write the evaluation scoring sheet](write-the-evaluation-scoring-sheet.md)).
7. **Plan the calendar**: reading, demos, proof of concept, reference checks, moderation meeting.

## Result

A named team with declared interests, a shared understanding of the product and its documented gaps, and a division of
criteria.

## Pitfalls

- A panel without accessibility or security expertise, which then scores those criteria on the bidder's words.
- Members who first see the product in a vendor's demo.
- Scoring rules agreed after reading the bids.
