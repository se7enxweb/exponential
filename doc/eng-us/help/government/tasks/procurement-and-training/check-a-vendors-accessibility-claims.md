# Check a vendor's accessibility claims

**Who:** the accessibility lead on the evaluation team. **When:** while scoring bids. The reference is
[Accessibility standards](../../../../standards/accessibility.md); the method is in
[Evaluating accessibility evidence](../../procurement/evaluation/evaluating-accessibility-evidence.md).

## Before you start

Know the project's own status words, which a bidder should not exceed for the product: WCAG 2.2 AA **Partly complies**
for admin4 and admin4l; EN 301 549 and Section 508 **Unverified**; public designs not audited; no VPAT or ACR.

## Steps

1. **List every accessibility claim** in the bid, word for word.
2. **Separate claims about the product** (Exponential) from **claims about the site** the bidder will build.
3. **Check product claims** against the standards page. A claim stronger than "Partly complies" or "Unverified" needs
   a document you can read: a named evaluation, method, scope and date.
4. **Check site claims** for a method and a scope: which pages, which standard, which tools, who tests.
5. **Ask for reports** of earlier work if the bidder cites them; check they are real reports, not marketing pages.
6. **Test in the demo**: a keyboard-only task in the proposed administration design
   ([Prepare a demo for the evaluation panel](prepare-a-demo-for-the-evaluation-panel.md)).
7. **Record** each claim with your finding and score.

## Claims and what to check

| Claim in a bid | What to ask |
|---|---|
| "Exponential is WCAG 2.2 AA compliant" | which evaluation shows it? The project says Partly complies, for admin4 only |
| "We provide a VPAT" | a VPAT for what, made by whom, on which version? None exists from the project |
| "The site will be accessible" | which standard and level, which method, which pages, who fixes failures, at whose cost? |
| "The editor is accessible" | the online editor is Unverified; how will staff who use assistive technology work? |
| "Accessibility statement included" | who drafts it and keeps it current? |

## Result

A list of claims, each marked verified, unverified or contradicted, and requirements tightened for the contract
([Write an accessibility requirement](write-an-accessibility-requirement.md)).

## Pitfalls

- Scoring the confidence of a claim rather than its evidence.
- Accepting an automated scan as proof of conformance; automated tools find only part of the problems.
