# Plan editor training for a department

**Who:** the training coordinator, with the department's editorial lead. **When:** two to four weeks before the
department starts publishing. Courses: [Editors 101](../../../trainers/editors-101/README.md) and the
[Editors' day](../../../trainers/workshops/module-02-one-day-agendas/editors-day.md).

## Contents

1. [Before you start](#before-you-start)
2. [Steps](#steps)
3. [Result](#result)
4. [Pitfalls](#pitfalls)

## Before you start

- The department's area, section and roles exist ([Onboarding a department](../../checklists/onboarding-a-department.md)).
- A training installation ([Lab quick card](../../../trainers/lab-quick-card.md)).

## Steps

1. **List the participants** and their future role: editor, approver, translator ([Roles to courses](../../procurement/training/roles-to-courses.md)).
2. **Ask about needs** privately: accessibility accommodations, language ([Accessible and inclusive training](../../procurement/training/accessible-and-inclusive-training.md)).
3. **Choose the format**:
   - occasional editors: the one-day editors' agenda, or Editors 101 modules 1 to 6;
   - regular editors: Editors 101 in full (27 lessons, about 19.5 hours);
   - approvers: add Editors 201, module 5, approvals and collaboration.
4. **Prepare the lab** with the department's content classes and a copy of its section, without personal data.
5. **Adapt the exercises** to the department's real content types (for example a service page or a notice).
6. **Schedule** close to the first publication; book rooms or an online session; send pre-work
   ([Trainer toolkit](../../../trainers/trainer-toolkit.md)).
7. **Run the course** and the capstone task ([Module 9: the Editor siteaccess](../../../trainers/editors-101/module-09-the-editor-siteaccess/README.md)).
8. **Sign off** each participant on one supervised publication; then assign the live role
   ([Onboard a new editor](onboard-a-new-editor.md)).
9. **Collect feedback** and adjust ([Collecting feedback](../../../trainers/train-the-trainer/module-06-assessment-and-follow-up/collecting-feedback.md)).

## Result

Trained editors who publish in their own area from the first day, with a record of who was trained and when.

## Pitfalls

- Training months before the first publication.
- Generic exercises that do not use the department's content types.
- Giving the live role before the supervised publication.
