# Publish a press release

This page is for press officers who publish releases on the website. It works with a Press release class (as in
[Press releases](../../building/news-and-alerts/press-releases.md)) or with the shipped Article class. For a release
that must appear at a set time, see [Schedule a news item](schedule-a-news-item.md).

## Contents

1. [Before you start](#before-you-start)
2. [Steps](#steps)
3. [Check the result](#check-the-result)
4. [Pitfalls](#pitfalls)

## Before you start

- The text is approved by whoever approves releases in your organization.
- Images: you have the right to use them, the credit line, and an alternative text for each.
- You know whether your release goes through an approval workflow on the site.

## Steps

1. Open the press releases folder (for example **News > Press releases > <year>**), **Create new**, choose
   **Press release** (or **Article**).
2. Fill the title, the lead (the first paragraph: who, what, when, where), the body and the notes to editors.
3. **Press contact**: the press office's line and shared mailbox, not a personal mobile number.
4. **Attachments** or **Image**: **Upload new**, choose the photo, fill its **alternative text** and put the credit in
   the caption or description ([Add alternative text to images](add-alternative-text-to-images.md)).
5. **Topics**: add the tags of the policy areas ([Tag content with eztags](tag-content-with-eztags.md)).
6. **Preview**, then **Send for publishing**.

## Check the result

- [ ] The release appears at the top of the press releases list, signed out.
- [ ] The media RSS feed lists it when the feed is next written ([RSS feeds](../../building/news-and-alerts/rss-feeds.md)).
- [ ] Images show with alternative text (check with the browser's accessibility inspector or a screen reader).
- [ ] If it went for approval: **My pending items** shows it until it is approved.

## Pitfalls

| Pitfall | Avoid it |
|---|---|
| Publishing before an embargo | use the scheduled method, or wait |
| A personal phone number as press contact | use role contacts; staff change |
| Images of people without consent where your rules require it | check before upload |
| The whole release only as a PDF | the text belongs on the page; a PDF can be an extra |
| Correcting a release silently | correct openly ([Correct a published notice and keep the history](correct-a-published-notice-and-keep-the-history.md)) |
