# Publish a tender notice

This page is for editors in procurement or communications who announce calls for tenders on the website. In many
jurisdictions the legally required publication happens on an official procurement platform or journal; the website
announces the opportunity and points there. Exponential has no procurement or e-tendering module.

> **Note** This page describes features and common practice; it is not legal advice. Check the law that applies to
> your organization with your legal counsel.

## Before you start

- The procurement officer confirms the notice is published (or will be) on the official platform, and gives you its
  address and reference.
- You have the short description, the deadline for tenders and the deadline for questions.
- You know whether documents are published on the website too or only on the platform.

## Steps

1. Open **Business > Tenders** (or your procurement folder), **Create new**, choose the notice class your site uses (a
   Public notice, or an Article).
2. **Title**: "Call for tenders: <subject>".
3. Text: what is bought, the reference, who may tender, the deadlines (with time and time zone), and the link to the
   official notice on the procurement platform as the place to tender.
4. **Documents**: only if they are also published on the website; otherwise link to the platform.
5. **Hide after** (if the class has it): the tender deadline, so the notice leaves the list automatically
   ([Publish a public notice with an expiry date](publish-a-public-notice-with-an-expiry-date.md)).
6. **Send for publishing**.

## Check the result

- [ ] Signed out, the notice is in the tenders list; the platform link opens the official notice.
- [ ] Deadlines on the website match the official notice exactly.

## During and after the procedure

| Event | Do |
|---|---|
| Clarifications or changed deadlines | follow the platform; update the website notice with a dated note ([Correct a published notice and keep the history](correct-a-published-notice-and-keep-the-history.md)) |
| Deadline passed | the notice is hidden (automatically or by hand) |
| Contract awarded | publish the award information as your rules require, as a new item or an update |

## Pitfalls

| Pitfall | Avoid it |
|---|---|
| Different deadlines on the website and the platform | copy them from the official notice; point to it as the reference |
| Accepting tenders by e-mail from the website | tenders go through the procedure's channel |
| Publishing documents only on the website | equal access: the official platform is where bidders look |
