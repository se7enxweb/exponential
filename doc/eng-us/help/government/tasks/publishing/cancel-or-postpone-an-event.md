# Cancel or postpone an event

This page is for editors who must cancel or postpone an event that is already published. Keep the page: people have
the link. Make the change obvious, then tell those who signed up. Background:
[Cancellations and changes](../../building/events/cancellations-and-changes.md).

## Contents

1. [Before you start](#before-you-start)
2. [Steps to cancel](#steps-to-cancel)
3. [Steps to postpone](#steps-to-postpone)
4. [Check the result](#check-the-result)

## Before you start

- The cancellation or new date is confirmed by the organizer.
- You know whether people signed up (a sign-up form lists them in **Setup > Collected information**).

## Steps to cancel

1. Open the event, **Edit**.
2. **Short title**: start it with "Cancelled:" so calendars and lists show it.
3. At the top of **Text**: "Cancelled on <date>. <Reason if appropriate.> <What people can do instead.>"
4. If your class has a booking status, set it to *cancelled*.
5. **Send for publishing** (not **Publish without notification**: followers should know).

## Steps to postpone

1. **Edit** the event.
2. New date known: change **From Time** and **To Time**; add at the top "Postponed from <old date> to <new date>."
3. New date not known: keep the dates; start the short title with "Postponed:"; say a new date will follow.
4. **Send for publishing**.

## Check the result

- [ ] Signed out, the calendar shows "Cancelled:" or the new date on the right day.
- [ ] The event page starts with the change.
- [ ] People who signed up are told: export their contact data from **Setup > Collected information** and write to
  them from your normal mail ([Export form responses to CSV](export-form-responses-to-csv.md)).
- [ ] **Manage versions** keeps the earlier wording.

## Pitfalls

| Pitfall | Avoid it |
|---|---|
| Removing the event at once | people looking for it find nothing; hide it after the original date |
| Changing only the text, not the dates | the calendar still shows the old day |
| Front page lists still show the old title | they refresh with their cache expiry; ask the web team if it is urgent ([Refresh a page that shows old content](refresh-a-page-that-shows-old-content.md)) |
| Keeping sign-up data after telling people | remove it ([Remove form responses that are no longer needed](remove-form-responses-that-are-no-longer-needed.md)) |
