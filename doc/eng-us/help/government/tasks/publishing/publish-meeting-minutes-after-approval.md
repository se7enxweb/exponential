# Publish meeting minutes after approval

This page is for editors who publish minutes after the council or committee has approved them, usually at its next
meeting. Minutes are part of the official record: publish the approved text, keep a trace of any change, and never
replace the record silently.

## Contents

1. [Before you start](#before-you-start)
2. [Steps](#steps)
3. [Check the result](#check-the-result)
4. [Pitfalls](#pitfalls)

## Before you start

- The minutes are approved, and you have the final file (an accessible PDF, or text for the page).
- You know whether your rules also publish draft minutes before approval.
- You can edit the meeting page.

## Steps

1. Upload the minutes: open the meeting's **Papers** folder (or the media library's minutes folder), **Upload**, choose
   the file, give the title "Minutes of <body>, <date> (approved)" and press **Upload all**
   ([Media and uploads](../../../media-and-uploads.md#upload-files)).
2. Open the meeting, **Edit**.
3. In **Minutes**, press **Add existing** and choose the new File object (or **Upload new** to do step 1 here).
4. Set **Status** to *minutes approved* (or *record complete*).
5. Optionally add one line to the agenda field: "Minutes approved on <date>."
6. **Send for publishing**.

If draft minutes were published earlier as a separate file, either hide the draft file
([Hide a page temporarily](hide-a-page-temporarily.md)) or keep it, clearly titled "draft", as your rules require.

## Check the result

- [ ] Signed out, the meeting page shows the minutes link and the status.
- [ ] The minutes download and open.
- [ ] **Manage versions** on the meeting shows the new version; the earlier one is archived.

## Pitfalls

| Pitfall | Avoid it |
|---|---|
| Publishing minutes before approval when your rules forbid it | check the status with the secretariat |
| Replacing approved minutes with another file | corrections go through the body that approved them; publish the corrected file as a new version with a note ([Replace a document with a new version](replace-a-document-with-a-new-version.md)) |
| Names of members of the public in minutes | follow your rules on what is published; check before upload |
| Minutes only as a scan | ask for an accessible version; the page should state the main decisions in text |
