# Publish a profile of an official

This page is for editors who publish profiles of office holders: council members, board members, heads of department.
Profiles concern a real person, so publish only what their public role requires. The default site package installs a
**Person** class (`ng_person`: Name, CV, Image, Copyright, social media links, Meta Data); otherwise use an Article.

> **Note** This page describes features and common practice; it is not legal advice. Check the law that applies to
> your organization with your legal counsel.

## Before you start

- Agree with the person (and your data protection officer's rules) what is published: name, role, party or group where
  relevant, committees, official contact, a short biography of the public role.
- Have their consent for the photo, and the photographer's credit.
- Official contact details only: an official mailbox, not a private phone or address.

## Steps

1. Open the folder of office holders (for example **Council and democracy > Members**), **Create new**, choose
   **Person**.
2. **Name**: as the person uses it officially.
3. **CV**: the public role first (role, since when, committees), then a short professional background if agreed.
4. **Image**: **Upload new**, with alternative text ("<name>, <role>") and the credit in **Copyright**.
5. Social media links: only official accounts, if your policy allows.
6. **Meta Data**: a description such as "<role> of <body>".
7. **Preview**, then **Send for publishing**.

## Check the result

- [ ] Signed out, the profile shows the role information and the photo with alternative text.
- [ ] No private contact details appear anywhere on the page.
- [ ] Lists of members include the person (meetings, committees) where your templates relate them.

## When the person leaves office

| Need | Do |
|---|---|
| Their past decisions and votes refer to them | keep the profile with an end date or "Former member", hidden from the current members list |
| No ongoing need | hide or remove the profile as your rules say |
| Their photo | remove it when there is no longer a reason to show it |

## Pitfalls

| Pitfall | Avoid it |
|---|---|
| Copying a private CV | the role profile only |
| Photos of family or at private events | official photos only |
| Outdated roles | review profiles after every election or appointment |
| Social media links to private accounts | official accounts only, if at all |
