# Publish a service page

This page is for editors who write service pages: how to get a parking permit, register a birth, apply for a grant.
It uses a Service class as built in [Modeling services](../../building/service-catalog/modeling-services.md); if your
site uses Articles for services, follow the same order with headings in the body.

## Contents

1. [Before you start](#before-you-start)
2. [Steps](#steps)
3. [Check the result](#check-the-result)
4. [Pitfalls](#pitfalls)

## Before you start

- The service owner has confirmed the facts: who can use it, cost, documents needed, processing time.
- You have the address of the online application, if there is one, and the contact point that handles questions.
- You know the topic and life event tags it belongs to.

## Steps

1. Open the service area folder (for example **Waste and recycling**), **Create new**, choose **Service**.
2. **Service name**: start with the thing, in residents' words: "Garden waste collection".
3. **In short**: two or three sentences: what it is and who it is for ([Write a plain-language summary](write-a-plain-language-summary.md)).
4. **Who can use it**, **What you need**, **What it costs**: short lists.
5. **How to apply**: numbered steps.
6. **Apply online**: the address of the application system ([Add a link to an external service](add-a-link-to-an-external-service.md)).
7. **How long it takes**, **What happens next**.
8. **Contact**: **Add existing**, choose the contact point (not a person).
9. **Related documents**, **Topics and life events**, **Next review** (a date in the future).
10. **Preview**, then **Send for publishing**.

## Check the result

- [ ] Read it as a resident who knows nothing about your organization: can you tell if it is for you and what to do?
- [ ] The online link opens the right application.
- [ ] The service appears on its topic page and in the A to Z list (after their cache renews).
- [ ] The site search finds it with the word residents would type.

## Pitfalls

| Pitfall | Avoid it |
|---|---|
| Writing the department's name for the service | use the residents' words |
| Copying fees into many pages | fees in one place, related ([Update opening hours or contact details](update-opening-hours-or-contact-details.md) shows the same idea for contacts) |
| No review date | stale pages; the field is required for a reason |
| All information in a PDF form | the page explains; the form is an attachment |
