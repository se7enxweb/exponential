# Publish a frequently asked questions page

This page is for editors who publish a page of questions and answers, for example during a change of service or a
consultation. A good FAQ answers the questions people actually ask, briefly, and sends them to the full page; a bad
one becomes a second, outdated copy of the site.

## Contents

1. [Before you start](#before-you-start)
2. [Steps](#steps)
3. [Check the result](#check-the-result)
4. [Pitfalls](#pitfalls)

## Before you start

- Collect real questions: from the call center or front desk, from **Setup > Search statistics** (phrases people
  search, if logging is on), from form messages.
- Ask whether a question is better answered by improving the service page itself; often it is.
- Know the owner who confirms the answers.

## Steps

1. Create the page where the topic lives: **Create new**, **Article** (or a Folder with a description).
2. Intro: one sentence on what the page covers and when it was last checked.
3. In the body, each question as a heading (use the editor's heading style, not bold text), in the words people use.
4. Under each heading, a short answer (two or three sentences) and a link to the full page.
5. Order questions by how often they are asked, or group them under subheadings.
6. If your site has an accordion block or class (for example the `ng_accordion_item` class of the default package's
   demo classes, or a layouts block), use it only if it works with a keyboard and a screen reader; plain headings
   always work.
7. **Send for publishing**.

## Check the result

- [ ] Headings form a list that a screen reader can jump through.
- [ ] Every answer links to a page with the full information.
- [ ] Search finds the FAQ by the words of its questions.

## Pitfalls

| Pitfall | Avoid it |
|---|---|
| Invented questions ("Why is our service so great?") | real questions only |
| Long answers duplicating service pages | link instead; one place for each fact |
| No review | add a review date and check after every change to the service |
| Questions as bold text | use real headings |
