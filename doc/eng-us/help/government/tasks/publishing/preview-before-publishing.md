# Preview before publishing

This page is for editors who want to see a change before it goes live. The edit form's **Preview** opens the draft in
the version preview, rendered with the site's design. The labels below are those of the administration's version
preview page (`content/versionview`).

## Contents

1. [Before you start](#before-you-start)
2. [Steps](#steps)
3. [What a preview cannot show](#what-a-preview-cannot-show)
4. [Pitfalls](#pitfalls)

## Before you start

- You are editing a draft (**Edit** was pressed); the draft is saved automatically while you work, and with
  **Store draft** you save it yourself.

## Steps

1. In the edit form, press **Preview** (in the left column: *Preview the draft that is being edited.*).
2. The preview page shows the draft. Choose:
   - the **siteaccess** to render it with (for example the public site, or a language siteaccess), then
     **Update view**;
   - the device size: **iPhone**, **iPad**, **Desktop**, and **Landscape**.
3. Read it as a visitor: headings, links, images, the summary.
4. Then:
   - **Back to edit** to change it;
   - **Publish** to publish from the preview (it follows the same rules as publishing from the form, including
     approval);
   - **Manage versions** to compare with earlier versions.

## What a preview cannot show

| Not shown | Why |
|---|---|
| Lists on other pages (front page, menus) | they show published content only |
| Notifications and feeds | made on publish |
| Exactly what a visitor without rights sees | the preview runs as you; restricted parts may show |
| Cached pages | the published page may need a cache refresh |

Check those after publishing, signed out.

## Pitfalls

| Pitfall | Avoid it |
|---|---|
| Previewing in the admin design only | choose the public siteaccess |
| Assuming a phone view is enough for accessibility | also try the keyboard and zoom |
| Leaving the draft unpublished after previewing | it waits in **My drafts** ([Recover a lost draft](recover-a-lost-draft.md)) |
