# Share a page between two sites

This task is for editors in a multisite installation. Background:
[Shared content across sites](../../building/multisite/shared-content-across-sites.md).

## Before you start

- You may create content in both sites' folders.
- The page's section is readable by visitors of both sites.

## Steps

1. Open the page and its **Locations** tab.
2. Press **Add locations** and choose, in the content browser, the folder of the second site.
3. Confirm the choice.

The item's **Locations** tab now shows two locations; one is the main location.

## Check

Open the page on both sites, signed out. Edit it once; both show the change after publishing.

## Problems

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| The second site shows "not found" | the page has no translation in that site's languages and is not always available |
| Links on the page point into the wrong site | see "Link from one department site to another" |
| You wanted an independent copy | use **Copy** instead; then the two copies change separately |
