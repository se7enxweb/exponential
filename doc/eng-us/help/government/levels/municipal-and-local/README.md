# Municipal and local government

This folder is for project managers, administrators and website creators at a city, town, council, borough or
district. Local government is where most people meet the state: waste collection, parking, permits, local taxes,
libraries, schools, social services, the council. Its website is a service desk first. The pages answer what a
municipality publishes, how to build it in Exponential, which requirements weigh most, how to run the first 90 days,
and how to publish council meetings and local services.

Product facts come from the documentation of this repository; descriptions of local government are general
public-sector practice and responsibilities differ between countries.

## What sets local government apart

| Topic | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Tasks, not information | residents come to do something: report, apply, pay, book, find out when |
| Small teams | often one or two people run the site beside other work |
| Many topics | from waste to weddings, each owned by a different department |
| The council | meetings, agendas and minutes on a fixed cycle, with deadlines set by law |
| Everyone is a user | residents of all ages, languages and abilities |

What helps most in Exponential: a small set of classes, a role per department with a Section limitation, an approval
only where it is needed, and the editor siteaccess for occasional editors
([The editor siteaccess](../../../logging-into-the-editor.md)).
