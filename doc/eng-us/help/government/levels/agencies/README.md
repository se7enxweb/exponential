# Agencies

This folder is for project managers, administrators and website creators at a public agency: a regulator, an
inspectorate, an executive or arm's-length body that runs a program, or a specialist authority. Agencies publish for
two audiences at once: the public, and the organizations or professionals they regulate or serve. Their guidance is
relied on, so it must be correct, dated and traceable. The pages answer what agencies publish, how to build it in
Exponential, which requirements weigh most, how to run the first 90 days, and how to publish guidance for regulated
parties.

Product facts come from the documentation of this repository; descriptions of agencies are general public-sector
practice.

## What sets agencies apart

| Topic | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Reliance | regulated parties act on guidance; a wrong or undated page has consequences |
| Traceability | "what did the guidance say on that date?" must be answerable |
| Two audiences | the public and professionals need different entry points |
| Decisions about named parties | enforcement decisions may name organizations, and sometimes people |
| Data | registers and statistics are often expected as open data |

The product features that answer these: versions with compare and the audit trail
([Versions of an object](../../../../guides/content-history.md), [Audit trail](../../../../features/6.0/audit-trail.md)),
object states for a document's status ([Object states](../../../../guides/object-states.md)), notifications for
subscribers ([Notifications](../../../../guides/notifications-administrator.md)) and RSS
([RSS feeds](../../../../guides/rss-feeds.md)).
