# Government glossary: M to R

This page is for readers in a public body who meet a term they do not know. Each entry has one to three sentences and
a link to the page that explains it. The full product glossary is [the Exponential glossary](../../../glossary.md).

> **Note** Definitions of legal terms are plain-language descriptions, not legal definitions. Check the law that
> applies to your organization with your legal counsel.

## M and N

**Maintenance mode.** Takes a site offline with a notice while work is done; administrators can still sign in
([Maintenance mode](../../../guides/maintenance-mode.md)).

**Multisite.** Several websites run from one Exponential installation, each with its own siteaccess, design and
settings ([Multisite setup](../../creators/website-creators/multisite-setup.md)).

**Node.** One place of a content object in the content tree, with a parent and a visibility
([The content model and editing content](../../../guides/content-model-and-editing.md)).

## O

**Object state.** A stage of your own for content (for example "in review" or "archived"), used in permissions
([Object states](../../../guides/object-states.md)).

**Open data.** Data published so anyone can use and share it, under an open license; in the EU see the
[Open Data Directive](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2019/1024/oj) and the [Open Definition](https://opendefinition.org/od/2.1/en/).

**Open source.** Software whose license meets the [Open Source Definition](https://opensource.org/osd): free
redistribution, source code, derived works allowed. Exponential's GPL license is one.

**Owner (content).** The user a content object belongs to; roles can limit what someone may do to their own content
([Changing the owner of content](../../../guides/changing-owners.md)).

## P

**Package.** A bundle of content classes, content, extensions or design that Exponential can export and install
(`.ezpkg`), used by the setup wizard to build sites ([Packages](../../../guides/packages.md)).

**Penetration test.** A test in which an assessor tries to break into a system. No report of one is published in the
Exponential repository; commission one for your own site if your rules require it.

**Plain language.** Writing that readers understand the first time; in the United States the Plain Writing Act of 2010
applies to federal agencies ([plainlanguage.gov](https://www.plainlanguage.gov/)).

**Policy.** One permission inside a role: a module, a function and optional limitations
([Roles, policies and unactivated users](../../../guides/roles-and-policies.md)).

**Processing agreement.** The contract between a data controller and a processor that sets how personal data is
handled (Article 28 of the [GDPR](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj)).

**Proof of concept.** A short test installation that shows a product can meet key requirements before a decision.
Exponential can be installed on SQLite with one command ([Installing Exponential in one command](../../../features/6.0/install-in-one-command.md)).

**Pseudonymization.** Replacing names and identifiers with values that need extra information to link back to a person.
Exponential's audit trail pseudonymizes records after a set number of days ([Audit trail](../../../features/6.0/audit-trail.md)).

## R

**Records management.** Managing records from creation to disposal under a retention schedule; the international
standard is ISO 15489-1 ([iso.org](https://www.iso.org/standard/62542.html)). Exponential keeps content versions and
an audit trail but is not a records management system by itself ([Versions of an object](../../../guides/content-history.md)).

**Redirect.** An instruction that sends a visitor from an old address to a new one; URL wildcards and aliases do this in
Exponential ([Links, URL aliases, wildcards](../../../guides/urls-and-aliases.md)).

**Request for proposal (RFP).** A tender document asking suppliers for proposals against stated requirements.

**request-shield.** A small web application firewall by the CJW Network, a separate MIT-licensed project, that runs
before the Exponential kernel: it refuses scanners, slows floods and can limit the administration to a network. The
hardening guide recommends it for every site reachable from the internet ([Security hardening](../../security-hardening.md#a-shield-in-front-request-shield)).

**Retention schedule.** The rule for how long each kind of record or data is kept before deletion or archiving.

**Right-to-left (RTL).** Languages written from right to left, such as Arabic and Hebrew; Exponential sets the page
direction for them ([Right-to-left languages](../../../features/6.0/right-to-left.md)).

**Role.** A named set of policies given to users or groups ([Roles, policies and unactivated users](../../../guides/roles-and-policies.md)).

**RPO and RTO.** Recovery point objective (how much data may be lost) and recovery time objective (how long a restore
may take) ([SLA options for Exponential hosting](../../hosting/business/sla-options.md#recovery-point-and-recovery-time)).
