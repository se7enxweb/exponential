# Government glossary: S to Z

This page is for readers in a public body who meet a term they do not know. Each entry has one to three sentences and
a link to the page that explains it. The full product glossary is [the Exponential glossary](../../../glossary.md).

> **Note** Definitions of legal terms are plain-language descriptions, not legal definitions. Check the law that
> applies to your organization with your legal counsel.

## S

**Section.** A label on content that selects its design and permissions; in a public body often one per department
([Sections](../../../guides/sections.md)).

**Service level agreement (SLA).** The part of a contract that sets measurable service promises such as availability
and response times. The Exponential project publishes none; suppliers and hosts do
([SLA options for Exponential hosting](../../hosting/business/sla-options.md)).

**Session.** The signed-in state of a browser. **Setup > Sessions** shows who is signed in and can sign browsers out
([Sessions](../../../guides/sessions.md)).

**Siteaccess.** One way of reaching an installation (by address, host or port) with its own settings and design, such as
the public site and the administration ([the Exponential glossary](../../../glossary.md#s)).

**Source code.** The form of a program that people read and change. The GPL gives everyone who receives Exponential its
source; the code is public on GitHub ([Licensing questions](../../project/licensing-faq.md)).

**Staging.** A copy of the site where changes and upgrades are tested before they reach the live site
([Deploying](../../../guides/deploying.md)).

**Supported version.** A version that still receives security fixes. For Exponential that is the 6.0.x line
(`SECURITY.md`; [Supported versions](../procurement/lifecycle/supported-versions.md)).

## T

**Tender.** A formal invitation to suppliers to bid for a contract under procurement rules.

**Total cost of ownership (TCO).** All costs of a system over its life: implementation, hosting, support, staff,
training, upgrades and exit, not only the purchase price. Exponential has no license fee.

**Translation.** A language version of a content object, or a language file of the interface
([Translations and languages](../../../features/6.0/translations-and-languages.md)).

**Trash.** Where removed content waits before it is purged or restored ([Trash](../../../features/6.0/trash-who-and-where.md)).

**Two-step sign-in.** Sign-in with a second factor after the password, such as a code from an authenticator app;
in Exponential it comes with the extension `sevenx_authentication_2fa` and is opt-in
([Two-step sign-in](../../../guides/two-factor-login.md)).

## U and V

**URL alias.** The readable address of a page, mapped to a node ([Links, URL aliases, wildcards](../../../guides/urls-and-aliases.md)).

**User group.** A group of user accounts that roles are assigned to; in a public body often one per department or
function ([Roles, policies and unactivated users](../../../guides/roles-and-policies.md)).

**Velocity (Exponential Velocity).** The PHP application server that ships with Exponential and can serve a site in
place of Apache or nginx with PHP-FPM ([Velocity web server](../../../features/6.0/velocity-web-server.md)).

**Version.** One saved state of a content object: draft, published or archived. Older versions can be compared and
restored ([Versions of an object](../../../guides/content-history.md)). For the software itself, see release policy
([Release policy and version numbers](../procurement/lifecycle/release-policy-and-version-numbers.md)).

**VPAT.** Voluntary Product Accessibility Template, the form vendors use to write an Accessibility Conformance Report,
mainly for Section 508. There is none for Exponential ([Accessibility standards](../../../standards/accessibility.md#section-508)).

**Vulnerability disclosure.** The process for reporting a security weakness privately so it can be fixed before it is
public. Exponential's is in `SECURITY.md` ([Security standards](../../../standards/security.md#vulnerability-disclosure)).

## W to Z

**WCAG.** The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines of the W3C; level AA of version 2.2 is the usual target
([WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/); [Accessibility standards](../../../standards/accessibility.md)).

**Wildcard (URL).** A rule that redirects a whole pattern of old addresses to new ones
([URL wildcards](../../creators/website-creators/url-wildcards.md)).

**Workflow.** A process that runs when something happens to content, for example holding a version for approval
([Workflows](../../../guides/workflows.md)).

**Zone.** An area of a page layout that holds blocks, in Exponential Layouts ([Layouts editor](../../layouts-editor.md)).
