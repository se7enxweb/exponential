# Government glossary

This glossary is for everyone in a public body who reads the government section of this help: procurement officers,
managers, editors and administrators. It explains public-sector terms (tender, data controller, accessibility
statement) and the Exponential terms they meet (siteaccess, section, role), each in one to three sentences with a link
to the page that explains it. For the complete list of product terms, use [the Exponential glossary](../../../glossary.md);
this glossary links to it rather than copying it.

## Reading order

Start with [Exponential terms for government readers](exponential-terms-for-government-readers.md) if you are new to the
product, and keep [Acronyms](acronyms.md) open while you read a standard or a tender. Use the A to Z pages to look up
single terms.

## How entries are written

- One to three sentences, in plain words, with a link to the page that explains the term.
- Exponential terms are checked against the documentation.
- Legal terms are plain-language descriptions, not legal definitions; check the law that applies to you with your
  legal counsel.
- Official sources are linked only where the help names them (standards bodies, EU and US government portals).
