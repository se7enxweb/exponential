# Tender template: training clause

This page is for procurement officers and training coordinators who buy training with a CMS. The Exponential help
contains complete trainer courses under the GNU FDL ([Course catalogue](../../../trainers/course-catalogue.md)), so
a supplier does not need to invent materials: the clause can require that the delivered training follows them,
adapted to your site, and that the materials are yours to reuse.

**Template** - adapt to your procurement rules; not legal advice

## The clause

```text
T. TRAINING

T.1 Audiences. The Supplier trains the following groups: <number> editors,
    <number> approvers, <number> site administrators, <number> technical staff,
    [<number> internal trainers].

T.2 Programme. For each group the Supplier proposes a programme with learning
    objectives, duration, method (classroom, online, hands-on) and the course
    modules it is based on, delivered <before go-live / within <period>>.

T.3 Site-specific content. Exercises use the Customer's own content types, roles
    and workflows on a training environment, not the production site.

T.4 Materials. The Supplier delivers all training materials in editable form.
    Materials based on free documentation remain under its license; materials
    created for this contract are licensed to the Customer under <license> and
    may be reused and changed by the Customer.

T.5 Accessibility of training. Training and materials are accessible to
    participants with disabilities; the Supplier provides <captions / accessible
    documents / alternative formats> on request.

T.6 Train the trainer. The Supplier prepares <number> internal trainers to teach
    the editor courses to new staff.

T.7 Evaluation. Participants' learning is checked with practical exercises and
    the results are reported anonymously by group. Training is accepted when
    <criterion>.

T.8 Refreshers. After each major change of the system the Supplier provides
    <hours> of update training.
```

## Materials and licensing

- The courses are part of the documentation, which is free documentation under the GNU FDL 1.3 or later
  ([Free documentation](../../../free-documentation.md)). Your organization may copy and adapt them; adapted versions
  stay under the same license.
- Accessibility and inclusion in teaching are covered in Train the trainer, module 5
  ([Accessibility and inclusion](../../../trainers/train-the-trainer/module-05-accessibility-and-inclusion/README.md)).

## Notes for the evaluator

- Ask which trainers will teach and what they have taught before; ask to see one lesson plan adapted to your site.
- A bidder who builds on the published courses should cost less for materials; score the adaptation, not the volume
  of new slides.
