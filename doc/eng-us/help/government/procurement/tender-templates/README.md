# Tender templates

This section is for procurement officers, legal advisers and project managers who draft the tender and contract for
a website or intranet built on Exponential, or on any open source CMS. Each page holds clause text in a block you can
copy, with `<placeholders>` and blanks for every figure: this help never suggests response times, percentages or
prices. Each page then explains what an Exponential-based bid can show for the clause, with links to the evidence,
and gives notes for the evaluator.

**Template** - adapt to your procurement rules; not legal advice

> **Note** This page describes features and common practice; it is not legal advice. Check the law that applies to
> your organization with your legal counsel.

## Reading order

1. Start with the [RFP outline](rfp-outline.md) to see where each clause goes.
2. Write the requirements first ([Requirements for a public-sector CMS](../requirements/README.md)); the clauses refer
   to them.
3. Add the quality clauses (accessibility, security, patching, privacy), then the service clauses (hosting, service
   levels, support, training, documentation, languages).
4. Finish with the clauses that protect the long term: open source, custom work, acceptance, exit and no lock-in.
5. Have legal counsel review the result against your procurement rules.

## Conventions on these pages

- Clause numbers (`X.1`, `Y.2` ...) are placeholders; renumber them in your contract.
- Square brackets mark options: keep one, delete the rest.
- Status words for Exponential (Complies, Partly complies, Unverified, Does not comply) come from the
  [standards pages](../../../../standards/README.md) and are not upgraded here.
