# Training costs for an Exponential site

This page is for project managers who must budget training for editors, administrators and technical staff. The
help ships a complete set of trainer courses with modules, timing plans and exercises, as free documentation. So the
material costs nothing; trainer time, participants' time and the training lab still do. Course lengths below are
those the [Course catalogue](../../../trainers/course-catalogue.md) states.

## Who needs which course

| Group | Course | Length stated in the catalogue |
|---|---|---|
| New editors | [Editors 101](../../../trainers/editors-101/README.md) | 27 lessons, about 19.5 hours (about 3 days) |
| Editors who publish every week | [Editors 201](../../../trainers/editors-201/README.md) | 23 lessons, about 16 hours (about 3 days) |
| Page builders | [Exponential Layouts](../../../trainers/layouts/README.md) | 23 lessons, about 17.5 hours (about 3 days) |
| Site administrators | [Site administration](../../../trainers/site-administrators/README.md) | 48 lessons, about 30 hours (about 5 days) |
| System administrators | [Hosting and operations](../../../trainers/hosting-operations/README.md) | 28 lessons, about 20 hours (3 to 4 days) |
| Newsletter senders | [Newsletters](../../../trainers/newsletter/README.md) | 20 lessons, about 16 hours |
| Developers | [Developers](../../../trainers/developers/README.md) | 52 lessons, about 44 hours (about 7 days) |
| In-house trainers | [Train the trainer](../../../trainers/train-the-trainer/README.md) | 34 lessons, about 21 hours (3 to 4 days) |
| Compact programs | [Workshops and bootcamps](../../../trainers/workshops/README.md) | half-day, 1-, 3- and 5-day programs |

Durations include exercises but not breaks. Each course overview lists shorter schedules built from its modules; an
intranet administrator, for example, may need only part of Editors 101 before Site administration (catalogue,
"Combinations for common groups").

## What is free and what costs

| Free | Costs |
|---|---|
| course material, timing plans, exercises, quizzes | trainer time (internal or bought) |
| the right to adapt and translate the material (GNU FDL) | participants' time away from their work |
| the software for the training lab | a training machine or lab environment |
| the help in the administration | translating material into your languages, if needed |
| | refresher and onboarding sessions over the years |

Training partners of the program may offer courses; the program's own official training is still being defined
([Training partners](../../../partners/training-partners.md)).

## Estimating the training cost

```text
Course              Participants   Days   Sessions   Trainer days   Trainer rate   Participant days   Internal rate
Editors 101         <n>            <d>    <s>        <d*s>          <rate>         <n*d>              <rate>
Site administration <n>            <d>    <s>        <d*s>          <rate>         <n*d>              <rate>
...
Lab:                <machine or hosted lab, set up once per course>
Onboarding per year: <new staff per year> x <course days>
```

Use the course lengths above as the starting point and adjust with your trainer. The lab is described in
[Building the lab](../../../trainers/train-the-trainer/module-02-building-the-lab/README.md).

## Ways to lower the cost

- Train in-house trainers once with Train the trainer, then run courses internally.
- Use the shorter schedules each course overview lists.
- Combine departments into one session when their tasks are the same.
- Use the help in the administration for self-study between sessions.

## Pitfalls

- Budgeting the launch only, with no onboarding for new staff.
- Training on a different version or design than production uses.
- Using copies of the real site with personal data as a training lab; use invented content.
