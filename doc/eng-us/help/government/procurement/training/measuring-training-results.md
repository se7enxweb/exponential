# Measuring training results

This page is for the training coordinator and the contract manager who need to know whether training delivered what
was paid for. It shows the assessment material that already exists in this help and how to turn it into measurable
outcomes. The assessment material comes from [Assessments and practical exercises](../../../trainers/assessments/README.md),
which states itself that it is not an official certification.

## Three levels of results

| Level | Question | Measured by |
|---|---|---|
| Reaction | Did participants find the course useful? | a feedback slip |
| Learning | Can they do the tasks? | a written test and a practical task with a rubric |
| Work | Do they do it right on the live site? | review of their first publications, support requests |

The first is the easiest to collect and the least telling. Aim for the second and third.

## Tests and tasks that exist

The assessments course has written tests with sourced answer keys and practical tasks with rubrics for eleven course
areas ([Assessments](../../../trainers/assessments/README.md)). For a public body the most used are:

| Course | Written test | Practical task |
|---|---|---|
| Editors | [Editors written test](../../../trainers/assessments/module-02-editorial-courses/editors-written-test.md) | [Editors practical task](../../../trainers/assessments/module-02-editorial-courses/editors-practical-task.md) |
| Layouts | [Layouts written test](../../../trainers/assessments/module-02-editorial-courses/layouts-written-test.md) | [Layouts practical task](../../../trainers/assessments/module-02-editorial-courses/layouts-practical-task.md) |

## Feedback from participants

Collect feedback at the end of every course with a short slip ([Trainer toolkit](../../../trainers/trainer-toolkit.md))
and follow up a few weeks later ([After the course](../../../trainers/train-the-trainer/module-06-assessment-and-follow-up/after-the-course.md)).
Ask what participants could not do yet on the live site; that tells you what to add to the next course.

## Signs in the site

Some results show in the system itself. These are indicators you can watch, not proof:

| Indicator | Where to look |
|---|---|
| Drafts left unfinished | Dashboard > My drafts per editor ([My drafts and my pending items](../../../../guides/drafts-and-pending.md)) |
| Items waiting long for approval | My pending items, and the workflow processes ([Workflows](../../../../guides/workflows.md)) |
| Broken links and relations | Setup > Link management and Broken relations ([Content checks](../../../../features/6.0/content-checks.md)) |
| Images without alternative text | a review of published content; nothing in the software forces editors to fill the field ([Accessibility standards](../../../../standards/accessibility.md#known-gaps)) |
| Repeated questions to the service desk | your ticket system |

## Outcomes in a contract

Write outcomes that can be checked, not "participants are trained":

```text
Training outcome - <course>
- <n> of <m> participants pass the practical task of <course> with the rubric of the assessments course
  (or a rubric agreed in writing), assessed by <role> within <n> days after the course.
- Participants who do not pass receive <one follow-up session> at no extra cost.
- The supplier hands over the exercises, the lab reset procedure and the material in an editable format.
```

Choose the pass threshold and the number of follow-up sessions yourself; nothing in this help prescribes them.

## Pitfalls

- **Measuring only satisfaction.** A pleasant course is not proof of skill.
- **Tests that do not match the version.** Answers and screens change with upgrades; rehearse the test.
- **Personal data in assessment records.** Record the name, course, date and result; nothing else is needed.
- **Calling a completion certificate a certification.** The assessments course asks for honest wording without
  claims of official status ([Completion certificates you issue](../../../trainers/assessments/module-01-designing-assessments/completion-certificates-you-issue.md)).
