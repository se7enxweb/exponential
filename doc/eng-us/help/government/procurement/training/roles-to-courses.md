# Roles to courses

This page is for the training coordinator who has to say, for each person in a public body, which training they need.
It maps the usual roles to the trainer courses of this help and to the modules within them. The courses, modules and
lengths come from the [Course catalogue](../../../trainers/course-catalogue.md) and the course overviews it links.

## How to read the matrix

- **Must** means the person should not work on the live site without it.
- **Should** means within the first months.
- **Optional** means when the person's work needs it.

A "role" here is a job in your organization. Exponential also has roles, which are sets of permissions
([Roles, policies and unactivated users](../../../../guides/roles-and-policies.md)); the last section shows how the
two meet.

## Matching roles to permissions

Training and permissions should agree: a person is given a role in Exponential only after the training it needs.

| Job role | Typical Exponential role or setup | Read |
|---|---|---|
| Editor | a role limited to a section or subtree | [Sections](../../../../guides/sections.md), [Roles and policies](../../../../guides/roles-and-policies.md) |
| Approver | a role allowed to approve in a workflow | [Workflows](../../../../guides/workflows.md) |
| Site administrator | an administrator role | [Security and audit](../../../../guides/security-and-audit.md) |

Write the rule into the onboarding procedure: "the role is assigned after the course is completed". The quarterly
access review in the government checklists section checks that it held.

## Keeping the matrix current

- Review it when the site gains a feature (a newsletter, layouts, a new language).
- Review it when the course catalogue changes; course overviews state their modules and lengths.
- Keep it next to the training plan (see "A training plan for a public body" in this folder).
