# Accessible and inclusive training

This page is for the training coordinator of a public body and the trainers who teach Exponential to its staff.
Public employers have duties toward staff with disabilities, and editors who use assistive technology must be able to
learn the system they will use. The guidance comes from
[Module 5 of Train the trainer: accessibility and inclusion](../../../trainers/train-the-trainer/module-05-accessibility-and-inclusion/README.md)
and the accessibility evidence in [Accessibility standards](../../../../standards/accessibility.md).

> **Note** This page describes features and common practice; it is not legal advice. Check the law that applies to
> your organization with your legal counsel.

## Teach on the most accessible administration

Exponential has several administration designs. The accessibility work concentrates on **admin4** and its layout
variant **admin4l**: skip link, landmarks, keyboard support for tabs and menus, labeled form controls, error
summaries, contrast-checked colors in light and dark mode, and targets of at least 24 by 24 pixels. Its status
against WCAG 2.2 AA is **Partly complies**; the classic administration is not covered by the same tests
([Accessibility standards](../../../../standards/accessibility.md)).

For a participant who uses a screen reader, the keyboard only, high zoom or dark mode, teach on admin4:

- show the [Keyboard shortcuts](../../../keyboard-shortcuts.md);
- show the light, dark and system modes, which follow the user's preference
  ([The admin4 design](../../../../features/6.0/admin4-design.md));
- check exercises at 200% zoom, as the project does for its own pages.

The online editor (TinyMCE) was **not audited** for WCAG; plan extra time and a fallback for participants who find
it hard to use with assistive technology ([Accessibility standards](../../../../standards/accessibility.md#known-gaps)).
[Teaching with the accessible administration](../../../trainers/train-the-trainer/module-05-accessibility-and-inclusion/teaching-with-the-accessible-administration.md)
has the lesson.

## Accessible course material

- Handouts with real headings, alternative text for screenshots and readable contrast
  ([Accessible course materials](../../../trainers/train-the-trainer/module-05-accessibility-and-inclusion/accessible-course-materials.md)).
- The help itself follows the same rules: one heading per page, named navigation, labeled search
  ([Help system](../../../../features/6.0/help-system.md#languages-and-accessibility)), so participants can use it
  with assistive technology.
- Captions for recorded sessions ([Captions and accessible video](../../../press/captions-and-accessible-video.md)
  gives practice for video).
- Send pre-work and the lab sheet before the course so participants can set up their tools.

## Languages and right-to-left

The administration is available in several interface languages, and right-to-left languages set the page direction
([Languages](../../../languages.md), [Right-to-left languages](../../../../features/6.0/right-to-left.md)). If your
staff work in a language other than English, check which administration translations are complete before the course
and plan for terms that stay in English. [Languages and right to left in class](../../../trainers/train-the-trainer/module-05-accessibility-and-inclusion/languages-and-right-to-left-in-class.md)
covers teaching in mixed-language groups.

## Accommodations in class and in assessment

| Need | In class | In assessment |
|---|---|---|
| Screen reader user | admin4, keyboard-first demonstrations, describe every click as a path | extra time; oral or practical instead of a timed written test |
| Low vision | zoom, dark mode, large-print handouts | extra time |
| Deaf or hard of hearing | captions, written instructions for every exercise | written instructions |
| Learning differences | shorter blocks, written steps | extra time, a quiet room |

Ask participants privately before the course what they need. Details:
[Accommodations and inclusive practice](../../../trainers/train-the-trainer/module-05-accessibility-and-inclusion/accommodations-and-inclusive-practice.md)
and [Accommodations in assessment](../../../trainers/assessments/module-01-designing-assessments/accommodations-in-assessment.md).

## What to require from a supplier

- Courses taught on admin4 when a participant needs it.
- Accessible handouts and captioned recordings.
- A process to ask for and provide accommodations before each course.
- Accessibility of published content as a topic in editor courses
  ([Module 8 of Editors 201](../../../trainers/editors-201/module-08-quality-and-accessibility/README.md)).
