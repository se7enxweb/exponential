# Onboarding new staff to Exponential

This page is for the training coordinator and the site administrator who receive a new colleague after the site has
gone live. The launch team was trained in a project; the people who come later depend on a routine. This page gives
that routine, using the help pages and trainer courses that ship with Exponential.

## The principle

Give access in steps: a new person first reads, then practices on a training installation, then publishes under
supervision, and only then gets the full role. Exponential supports this with roles limited to sections or subtrees
([Roles, policies and unactivated users](../../../../guides/roles-and-policies.md)) and with workflows that hold a
version for approval ([Workflows](../../../../guides/workflows.md)).

## Week 1: account and self-study

1. **Create the account** in the right user group. Use the person's work e-mail; never share an account between
   people, so the audit trail can tell who did what ([Audit trail](../../../../features/6.0/audit-trail.md)).
2. **Assign a limited role first**, or none on the live site; give access to the training installation.
3. **Ask the person to sign in and change the initial password** ([Accounts and sign-in](../../../accounts-and-sign-in.md)).
   If your site uses two-step sign-in, set it up now ([Two-step sign-in](../../../../guides/two-factor-login.md)).
4. **Self-study**, about two hours:
   - [Logging into the editor](../../../logging-into-the-editor.md) or [Logging into the admin](../../../logging-into-the-admin.md);
   - [Admin tour](../../../admin-tour.md);
   - [Editing content](../../../editing-content.md);
   - [Publishing and versions](../../../publishing-and-versions.md);
   - [Media and uploads](../../../media-and-uploads.md);
   - your organization's own editorial guide.
5. Show the person the help: the "?" button opens help about the page they are on
   ([Help system](../../../../features/6.0/help-system.md)).

## Weeks 2 to 4: course and supervised work

| Step | What | Material |
|---|---|---|
| Course | Editors 101, in a group or as self-paced lessons with a mentor | [Editors 101](../../../trainers/editors-101/README.md) |
| Practice | the capstone article of Editors 101 on the training installation | [Module 9: the Editor siteaccess](../../../trainers/editors-101/module-09-the-editor-siteaccess/README.md) |
| First live work | edit existing pages in the person's own section; a senior editor reviews before publishing | [My drafts and my pending items](../../../../guides/drafts-and-pending.md) |
| Accessibility basics | alternative text, headings, link text | [Accessibility for site builders](../../../creators/website-creators/accessibility-for-site-builders.md) |

If your organization does not run Editors 101 often enough for new arrivals, a mentor can walk the person through
the lessons one module at a time; each lesson is a page with its own exercise.

## Sign-off and the live role

Sign-off is a short practical check, not a ceremony. A senior editor checks one article the person made alone,
against a list such as the Editors 101 capstone list or the editorial quality checklist in the government checklists
section. Then:

1. assign the full editorial role for the person's section;
2. record the date and the course in your training record;
3. tell the person who to ask for help (the service desk, the senior editor) and where the help is.

## Onboarding an administrator

An administrator gets more power and needs more care:

- start with Editors 101, modules 1 to 3, then [Site administration](../../../trainers/site-administrators/README.md);
- give the administrator role only after the security module
  ([Module 8: security](../../../trainers/site-administrators/module-08-security/README.md));
- require two-step sign-in for administrators where your site uses it
  ([Two-step sign-in](../../../../guides/two-factor-login.md));
- walk through the operating routine together: [Operating a site](../../../../guides/operating-a-site.md).

## What to record

| Record | Why |
|---|---|
| course and date per person | to show the training rule was followed in the access review |
| role assigned and date | to match training with permissions |
| mentor or reviewer | to know who signed off |

Keep personal data in the record to the minimum: the name, the course, the date. The same rule applies to
certificates you issue ([Completion certificates you issue](../../../trainers/assessments/module-01-designing-assessments/completion-certificates-you-issue.md)).
