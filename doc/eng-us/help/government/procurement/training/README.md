# Training in procurement

This part of the procurement section is for the people in a public body who are responsible for training: the
project manager who puts it in the plan, the procurement officer who buys it, and the training coordinator who keeps it
going after go-live. It builds on the trainer courses of this help ([Course catalogue](../../../trainers/course-catalogue.md),
[Trainers: start here](../../../trainers/trainers-start-here.md)), which are free documentation and can be used by
your own staff or by a supplier.

## The pages

| Page | It answers |
|---|---|
| [A training plan for a public body](training-plan-for-a-public-body.md) | Which groups need which course, at which moment of the project, and who delivers it |
| [Roles to courses](roles-to-courses.md) | For each job role, the courses and modules that are a must, a should or optional |
| [Onboarding new staff](onboarding-new-staff.md) | The first weeks of a new editor or administrator, from account to sign-off |
| [Budgeting training effort](budgeting-training-effort.md) | A worksheet built on the published course lengths and your own rates |
| [Buying training from a partner](buying-training-from-a-partner.md) | What to ask a supplier for, how to compare offers, rights in the material |
| [Building an in-house training team](building-an-in-house-training-team.md) | Train your own trainers, build the lab, keep the material current |
| [Measuring training results](measuring-training-results.md) | Tests, practical tasks, feedback, signs in the site, outcomes in a contract |
| [Accessible and inclusive training](accessible-and-inclusive-training.md) | Teaching every member of staff, with accommodations, on the accessible administration |

## Reading order

1. [A training plan for a public body](training-plan-for-a-public-body.md) and [Roles to courses](roles-to-courses.md)
   give the shape of the program.
2. [Budgeting training effort](budgeting-training-effort.md) turns it into a budget line.
3. Choose who delivers it: [Buying training from a partner](buying-training-from-a-partner.md),
   [Building an in-house training team](building-an-in-house-training-team.md), or both.
4. Make it last: [Onboarding new staff](onboarding-new-staff.md) and
   [Measuring training results](measuring-training-results.md).
5. Check every step against [Accessible and inclusive training](accessible-and-inclusive-training.md).
