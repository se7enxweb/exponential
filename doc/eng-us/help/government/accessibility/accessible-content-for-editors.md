# Accessible content for editors

This page is for editors and the trainers who teach them. A template can be built once to be accessible; content is
written every day, and most accessibility problems on a live site come from it. The project's statement says the
same: alternative text, heading order and link text in published content depend on editors
([Accessibility standards: Known gaps](../../../standards/accessibility.md#known-gaps)). Each habit below links to a
page in this section with more detail.

## Seven habits

| Habit | In short | Detail page in this section |
|---|---|---|
| Describe images | fill the alternative text with what the image says in its context | "Alternative text for images" |
| Use real headings | headings from the editor's heading formats, in order, never bold text instead | "Headings and structure" |
| Write meaningful links | the link text says where it leads; never "click here" | "Link text" |
| Keep tables for data | header row, a caption, no layout tables | "Tables in content" |
| Prefer pages to documents | publish as a page; when a document is needed, make it accessible | "Documents and PDFs" |
| Write plainly | short sentences, common words, one idea per paragraph | "The understandable principle" |
| Mark the language | content in another language goes in its translation or is marked | "Language of the page and its parts" |

For plain language in public communication, the US federal guidance at <https://www.plainlanguage.gov/> is a free
reference.

## The tools Exponential gives you

- **Alternative image text** field on every image field of the edit form. An administrator can make it required for a
  class ("Alternative text is required"); the image then cannot be stored without it.
- **Structured rich text**: the online editor writes headings, lists, tables with header cells and captions, links and
  embeds as structured XML rather than free HTML.
- **Versions**: every save is a version; you can compare versions and go back
  ([Versions of an object](../../../guides/content-history.md)).
- **Preview** before publishing, in the language and siteaccess you choose.
- **Content checks** find embeds and links that point to removed or hidden content
  ([The content checks](../../../features/6.0/content-checks.md)).
- **Optional**: where the Exponential Intelligence extension is activated, a **Write alt text** button suggests an
  alternative text for you to review, and a report lists images without one
  ([Exponential Intelligence](../../../features/6.0/extensions/expai.md)). The image is sent to an AI provider under the
  site's data policy; your organization decides whether that is allowed.

## A checklist before you publish

- [ ] Every informative image has an alternative text; decorative images are handled as your design guide says.
- [ ] The page has headings in order, the first under the title being level 2.
- [ ] Every link text makes sense on its own.
- [ ] Tables have a header row and a caption, and are not used for layout.
- [ ] Linked documents are accessible, or the same information is on the page.
- [ ] Text is plain; abbreviations are written out at first use.
- [ ] Videos have captions; audio has a transcript.
- [ ] Color is never the only way information is given.
