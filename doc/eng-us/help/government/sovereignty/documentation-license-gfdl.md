# The documentation license (GFDL)

This page is for anyone in a public body who wants to reuse the Exponential documentation: in internal manuals,
training courses, translations into official languages, or procurement documents. The documentation of the 6.x line
is under the [GNU Free Documentation License 1.3](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/fdl-1.3.html) or any later version,
with no Invariant Sections, no Front-Cover Texts and no Back-Cover Texts ([The license of this documentation](../../../README-LICENSE.md)).

## What you may do

Copy, distribute and modify the documentation, commercially or not, in any language, as the plain-words page
[Free documentation](../../free-documentation.md) explains: read, copy, share, translate, adapt and improve.

## The conditions

- Modified versions are released under the same license.
- Keep the copyright and license notices; add your own copyright for your changes.
- Give the source of a modified version in a transparent (editable) form when you distribute many copies; the
  license text has the details.

## Older pages with other terms

Some files in `doc/` were inherited from the documentation of the original software and keep their own terms (the
GPL v2 of the repository); the list is in [The license of this documentation](../../../README-LICENSE.md#older-pages-with-their-own-terms).
The help shows a different notice on those pages.

## Practical uses in a public body

| Use | How |
|---|---|
| An internal editors' manual | copy the pages you need, adapt them to your site's classes and menus, keep the notice |
| A translation into an official language | translate, mark it as a translation, keep the notice; consider contributing it back |
| Training material | adapt the course pages ([Course catalogue](../../trainers/course-catalogue.md)) |
| Procurement documents | quote pages to describe features, with the source |

Contributing improvements back helps every other user ([Contributing docs and translations](../../partners/contributing-docs-and-translations.md)).
