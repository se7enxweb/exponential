# Choosing a support provider

This page is for project managers and procurement staff who decide who will support the site. Because the software is
GPL, any qualified company may support it; the project's partner program and its published support levels are
described in the partners section ([Partners start here](../../partners/partners-start-here.md)).

## The options

| Option | What you get | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Community support | issue trackers, documentation, community channels; no guaranteed response | [Support levels and response times](../../partners/support-levels-and-response-times.md) |
| A partner | contracted services by a company in the partner program | [What is an Exponential partner](../../partners/what-is-an-exponential-partner.md) |
| 7x | the company that leads development; paid support | [Working with 7x](../../partners/working-with-7x.md) |
| In-house | your own staff, trained with the free material | [Course catalogue](../../trainers/course-catalogue.md) |

## Questions to ask a provider

- Which response times do you commit to, for which severity, at which hours and in which language?
- How do you handle security updates: who watches releases, how fast are they applied?
- Which Exponential sites do you run, and may we speak to a reference?
- What have you contributed to the project (extensions, fixes, translations)?
- Where are your staff and your systems, and who can access ours?
- How do you hand over to another provider at the end?
- Will all custom code be in our repository, under our control?

## Combining providers

Hosting, development and support can come from different companies, as long as one of them, or your own staff,
coordinates. Write down who does what for updates, incidents, backups and changes, so nothing falls between them.
