# Government FAQ

These pages answer the questions that public bodies ask about Exponential, in procurement, in evaluation and in daily
work. Every answer links the page of the documentation that backs it, and the answers say "no" where the product does
not do something: no VPAT, no cookie banner, no published SLA, no built-in records schedules, no GIS. Those honest
answers are as useful in a procurement as the positive ones.

## The pages

| Page | Short answer to the main question |
|---|---|
| [Accessibility](accessibility-faq.md) | admin4 partly complies with WCAG 2.2 AA; EN 301 549 and Section 508 unverified; no VPAT; public designs are the builder's responsibility |
| [Security](security-faq.md) | mitigations for the whole OWASP Top 10, tamper-evident audit trail; no ASVS assessment; lockout and 2FA are your decisions |
| [Privacy and data protection](privacy-faq.md) | consent records, erasure and log masking are provided; the organization stays the controller; no cookie banner |
| [Hosting and data location](hosting-and-data-location-faq.md) | install it wherever you want: your data center, your country, any hosting model |
| [Licensing and open source](licensing-and-open-source-faq.md) | GPL version 2 or later, no license fee, source public |
| [Cost](cost-faq.md) | no license or per-user cost; services, hosting, training and upgrades cost |
| [Support](support-faq.md) | free community support, paid support from 7x or partners; the project publishes no SLA |
| [Languages and right-to-left](languages-faq.md) | multilingual content and right-to-left in content and administration |

| [Search](search-faq.md) | built-in search, or Solr through ezfind |
| [Forms and newsletters](forms-and-newsletters-faq.md) | information collection forms and cjw_newsletter with double opt-in |
| [Many sites and many departments](multisite-faq.md) | several sites on one installation, permissions per department |
| [Performance and Velocity](performance-and-velocity-faq.md) | caches and Velocity; measure your own site with `exp:benchmark` |
