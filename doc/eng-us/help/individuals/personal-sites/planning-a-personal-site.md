# Planning a personal site

This page is for individuals who are about to make a personal site and want to decide what goes on it before they
install anything. An hour of planning saves a weekend of rebuilding. Write your answers in the
[site plan worksheet](../checklists/site-plan-worksheet.md).

## Purpose in one sentence

Finish this sentence: "This site exists so that ___ can ___." For example: "so that people who meet me at
conferences can find my talks and contact me", or "so that clients can see my illustration work". If you need two
sentences, you may need two sections of the site; if you need five, start with the most important one.

## Readers

| Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Who reads it first? | Decides what the home page says |
| What do they want to do? | Read, look at pictures, contact you, download a CV |
| On what device? | Most readers use a phone; check every page at phone width |
| In which language? | One language is easier; a second one is possible from the start ([Multilingual sites](../../creators/website-creators/multilingual-sites.md)) |
| Who must *not* see something? | Leads to a private area ([Private areas](../family-sites/private-areas-with-roles-and-sections.md)) |

## The page list

Keep the first version small. Five to eight pages is plenty:

1. Home
2. About
3. Work (or Projects, or Talks)
4. Writing (later a blog)
5. Contact
6. Legal notice and privacy, where required

Draw them as a tree: each page is a node, and the menu follows the tree
([Structuring the content tree](../../creators/website-creators/structuring-the-content-tree.md),
[Site map and content inventory](../../creators/website-creators/site-map-and-content-inventory.md)).

## What you publish about yourself

A personal site is about a real person: you. Decide deliberately:

- **Mail address**: use a contact form instead of printing it; forms keep it away from address harvesters
  ([About page and contact form](about-page-and-contact-form.md)).
- **Home address and phone**: only if your law requires them for a site of your kind, and then check what the minimum is.
- **Photos**: of you, yes if you like; of other people, only with their consent.
- **Employer, family, children, daily routines**: think about who could combine them.
- **Old content**: a page can be hidden or moved to the trash later; search engines and archives may keep copies.

Your country's rules on legal notices and privacy apply to you even on a hobby site. This help explains features,
not law; see [GDPR basics](../../businesses/security/gdpr-basics.md) for an overview.

## Choosing content classes

The default site package already has the classes most personal sites need:

| Page | Class | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Home | *Frontpage* or *Folder* | Lists what is below it |
| About, legal | *Article* | Title, intro, body, image |
| Work | *Folder* holding *Article* or *Gallery* | One item per project |
| Writing | *Blog* holding *Blog post* | Dates, tags, feeds |
| Contact | *Feedback form* | Stores and mails messages |

You only need new classes if your work has fields these do not have (a year, a medium, a client). Then read
[Designing your content model](../../creators/website-creators/designing-your-content-model.md).

## A rhythm you can keep

| How often | Do |
|---|---|
| When something happens | Add the talk, the project, the post |
| Monthly | The [monthly upkeep checklist](../checklists/monthly-upkeep-checklist.md): updates, backup, broken links |
| Yearly | Reread About and Contact; renew the domain |

A site that is small and current is better than a big one that is stale.

## A worked example

An invented example. Purpose: "so that people who heard one of my talks can find the slides and contact me".

| Page | Class | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Home | *Folder* | One-sentence introduction, the three newest talks |
| Talks | *Folder* | One *Article* per talk with a *File* for the slides |
| About | *Article* | Short biography, a photo |
| Contact | *Feedback form* | Messages go to a private mailbox |
| Privacy | *Article* | What the contact form stores and for how long |

Five pages, no new classes, an evening of writing. The blog can come later.
