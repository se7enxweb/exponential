# Personal sites

This section is for individuals who want a home on the web of their own: a page that says who you are, what you do,
what you have written or made and how people can reach you. It may later grow a blog, a portfolio or a private
area; every one of those starts from the same small site.

## What a personal site usually holds

| Part | Purpose | Typical content class |
|---|---|---|
| Home page | The first impression: one sentence about you, a photo, links to the rest | *Frontpage* or *Folder* |
| About | Your story, what you do, what you are looking for | *Article* |
| Work or projects | What you have made, with pictures | *Folder* with *Article* or *Gallery* items |
| Writing | Notes, essays or a blog | *Blog* with *Blog post* items |
| Contact | A form, so you do not publish your mail address | *Feedback form* |
| Legal | Who runs the site, privacy notice where your law requires one | *Article* |

The class names are those of the default site package; see
[Content classes for websites](../../creators/website-creators/content-classes-for-websites.md).

## What carries it in Exponential

- **Folders and articles** make the pages; the menu is built from the content tree
  ([Menus and navigation](../../creators/website-creators/menus-and-navigation.md)).
- **The *Feedback form* class** is a ready contact form whose answers are stored and mailed to a recipient
  ([Building a contact form](../../creators/website-creators/building-a-contact-form.md)).
- **Readable addresses** come from the page names ([URL aliases and nice URLs](../../creators/website-creators/url-aliases-and-nice-urls.md)).
- **Search engine basics**: titles, descriptions and a sitemap ([SEO basics](../../creators/website-creators/seo-basics.md)).
- **A theme**: the default `sevenx_themes_simple` design ([Choosing a site package and theme](../setup/choosing-a-site-package-and-theme.md)).
