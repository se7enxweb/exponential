# Is Exponential right for you?

This page is for individuals who are deciding whether Exponential is the right tool for a site of their own. It
does not try to sell you anything. Exponential is a full content management system; that is a strength for some
sites and more than you need for others.

## Where it fits well

Exponential's most notable feature, in the words of its `README.md`, is its "fully customizable and extendable
content model". For one person that matters when your content has a shape:

| Your site | Why Exponential fits |
|---|---|
| A portfolio with many works, each with a title, year, medium, caption and several images | Classes describe exactly those fields; galleries, image variations and an image editor ship |
| A blog you intend to keep for years | Drafts, versions, scheduled publishing, tags, feeds and readable addresses are built in |
| A family site with a private area | Sections and roles keep a part of the site, or all of it, for signed-in family members |
| A club with members who sign up, read news, see events and get a newsletter | Registration, member areas, *Event* classes and `cjw_newsletter` ship |
| A site in two or more languages | Multilingual content is part of the core ([Multilingual sites](../creators/website-creators/multilingual-sites.md)) |
| A site you want to own completely | Free software, your own server, your own data, no account with a platform |

## Where something simpler may be better

Be honest with yourself. A simpler tool may suit you better when:

- **You want a single page** and never expect to grow it. A static HTML page is less to look after.
- **You never want to think about a server, updates or backups.** A hosted service does that for you; with
  Exponential you, your host or a partner must ([Updates for one person](running-it/updates-for-one-person.md)).
- **You need a feature that is not here** and cannot pay for it to be built: ticketing, membership fees, a
  ready-made comment system with threads and likes, a one-click import from another platform
  ([What exists and what does not](what-exists-and-what-does-not.md)).
- **You need it live tonight** and have never run a website. Plan a weekend at least
  ([A personal site in a weekend](personal-sites/a-personal-site-in-a-weekend.md)).

None of these is a reason to stay away forever. Many people start small elsewhere and move once their content
outgrows it ([Moving from another blog platform](blogs/moving-from-another-blog-platform.md)).

## Ten questions

Answer yes or no. Mostly yes means Exponential is a good fit; mostly no means look at the alternatives above first.

1. Does my content have repeating kinds (posts, works, events, members) with their own fields?
2. Will I keep the site for more than a year?
3. Do I want to own the data and the software rather than rent an account?
4. Do I need a private area, or members who sign in?
5. Do I need more than one language, or might I later?
6. Can I spend an evening a month on upkeep, or pay someone who can?
7. Can I take and test a backup?
8. Is there a host I trust that offers PHP 8, or can I run a small server?
9. Am I comfortable reading documentation like this page?
10. Would I like to learn how a full content management system works?

## If you are unsure

- Install it on your own computer with SQLite and try it for an evening; nothing is lost if you decide against it
  ([Choosing an install method](setup/choosing-an-install-method.md)).
- Look at the demonstration installation at <https://alpha.se7enx.com>.
- Ask in the community ([Community channels](../advocates/community-channels.md)).
- If you are a student or teacher, consider the Student partner level
  ([Learning with the Student level](running-it/learning-with-the-student-level.md)).
