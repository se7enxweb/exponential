# Checklists for individuals

This section is for individuals who like to work from a list. Each checklist is short enough to print on one or two
pages, and every line links to the page that explains it.

## Contents

1. [The checklists](#the-checklists)
2. [When to use which](#when-to-use-which)
3. [Making them your own](#making-them-your-own)

## The checklists

| Checklist | Use it |
|---|---|
| [Site plan worksheet](site-plan-worksheet.md) | Before you install: purpose, readers, pages, privacy, budget |
| [Launch checklist for individuals](launch-checklist-for-individuals.md) | The day you go live |
| [Monthly upkeep checklist](monthly-upkeep-checklist.md) | Once a month, about an hour |
| [Family privacy checklist](family-privacy-checklist.md) | Before a family site goes live, and once a year after |

## When to use which

| Stage | Checklist |
|---|---|
| An idea | Site plan worksheet |
| Built on your computer | Launch checklist, sections "Before moving" |
| Online, before telling anyone | Launch checklist, the rest |
| Every month after | Monthly upkeep checklist |
| A family site | Family privacy checklist as well |

## Making them your own

Copy a checklist into a private article on your site, or into your notes, and change it: remove lines that do not
apply, add your own (the renewal date of your domain, the name of your deputy). The documentation is free to copy and
adapt ([Free documentation](../../free-documentation.md)).

Other checklists in this help that may fit: [Launch checklist (website creators)](../../creators/website-creators/launch-checklist.md),
[Security checklist for owners](../../customers/healthy/security-checklist-for-owners.md),
[Small group checklist](../../businesses/associations/small-group-checklist.md),
[Member site checklist](../../businesses/members/member-site-checklist.md).
