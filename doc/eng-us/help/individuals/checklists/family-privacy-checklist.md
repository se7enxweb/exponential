# Family privacy checklist

This page is for individuals who run a family site. It turns
[Privacy for your family](../family-sites/privacy-for-your-family.md) into a list. Do it before launch and once a
year after. It is general information, not legal advice.

1. [Agreements](#agreements)
2. [Content](#content)
3. [Accounts](#accounts)
4. [The private area](#the-private-area)
5. [Data that leaves the site](#data-that-leaves-the-site)
6. [Requests and mistakes](#requests-and-mistakes)

## Agreements

- [ ] House rules written and published inside the site ([Planning a family site](../family-sites/planning-a-family-site.md#house-rules)).
- [ ] Parents agreed on what may be shown of their children.
- [ ] Older children asked themselves.
- [ ] Everyone knows they can ask for removal.

## Content

- [ ] No addresses, phone numbers, school names, travel dates, health details, identity documents.
- [ ] Dates of birth only as day and month, and only inside the private area ([Birthdays and family dates](../family-sites/birthdays-and-family-dates.md)).
- [ ] Location data removed from photos before upload ([Uploading many images](../portfolios/uploading-many-images.md)).
- [ ] Backgrounds checked: house numbers, car plates, school logos.
- [ ] Faces or name tags pixelated where needed ([Editing images with ezie](../portfolios/editing-images-with-ezie.md)).
- [ ] No photo uploaded that would do harm if its address leaked (image files are served directly).

## Accounts

- [ ] Each person has a personal account; no shared family login ([Inviting family members](../family-sites/inviting-family-members.md)).
- [ ] Readers have reading rights only; contributors only what they need.
- [ ] Two-step sign-in for administrators and contributors ([Two-factor sign-in](../running-it/two-factor-sign-in.md)).
- [ ] Registration closed, or lands in a group without access.
- [ ] Accounts of people who left the family circle disabled.

## The private area

- [ ] Tested in a private browser window: asked to log in ([Private areas with roles and sections](../family-sites/private-areas-with-roles-and-sections.md#testing-as-a-stranger)).
- [ ] Site search shows no private content to strangers.
- [ ] No RSS export covers private content.
- [ ] Private content not in the sitemap.
- [ ] Fully private site, if chosen: `RequireUserLogin=true` works on every page.

## Data that leaves the site

- [ ] Backups encrypted or kept where only you and your deputy reach them ([Backups for one person](../running-it/backups-for-one-person.md)).
- [ ] Old backup generations deleted.
- [ ] Outside services listed: analytics, maps, videos, CAPTCHA, AI provider; each one needed?
- [ ] The writing assistant not used on private family content, or limited to a local model ([The writing assistant](../running-it/writing-assistant-expai.md)).
- [ ] Exports (CSV) of family data not left on shared drives.

## Requests and mistakes

- [ ] You know how to remove an object completely, including old versions and the trash ([Publishing and versions](../../publishing-and-versions.md)).
- [ ] You know how to disable an account and reset passwords.
- [ ] A deputy can do both if you are away.
