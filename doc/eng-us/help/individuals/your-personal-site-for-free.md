# Your personal site for free

You want a blog, a site for your club or family, or a page about your hobby, and you do not want to pay for hosting
or look after a server. Static publishing gives you that: you write in Exponential, and a static copy of your site is
hosted for free on GitHub Pages.

## What you'll have at the end

- Your site online at `https://<your-name>.github.io/<repository>/`, or at your own domain.
- Writing and editing in Exponential, with its editor, images and pages, as before.
- Every publish kept: you can always go back to an earlier one.

## How it works for you

1. Exponential can run on your own computer or a small server; only the copy is public.
2. When you are happy with a change, you **build** the site, look at what changed, and **publish** it.
3. GitHub Pages serves the copy to your visitors. It is fast and stays up.

Your computer does not need to be on for the site to be online.

## What a static site cannot do

- **Comments and guest books:** visitors cannot post to a static copy. You can hide them or link to the live page.
- **Member areas:** a page only signed-in members can see is never copied. See
  [Keep private content private](../static-publishing/keep-private-content-private.md).
- **Search:** works, with a small search page that runs in the visitor's browser.

More in [What static publishing is](../static-publishing/what-static-publishing-is.md).

## Costs

- GitHub Pages: free for public repositories.
- A domain of your own: optional, a few dollars a year. See [Use your own domain](../static-publishing/use-your-own-domain.md).
