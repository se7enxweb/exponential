# When to hire help

This page is for individuals who run their site alone and wonder when paying someone is the better choice. Doing
everything yourself is possible; doing everything yourself well, for years, is a lot. A few hours of paid help at the
right moment can save weekends.

## What one person usually does well

Writing and publishing, images and galleries, menus and pages, comments and accounts, a newsletter edition, the
monthly upkeep. None of these needs a professional.

## Warning signs

Get help when:

- you have postponed a security update for weeks because you are unsure how;
- you have no backup you have ever restored;
- mail from the site stopped arriving and you do not know why;
- you are copying commands you do not understand onto a live server;
- the site is for a club or family and you are the only one who can fix it.

## Describing a small job

A clear brief gets a clear price. Write:

1. **What you want**, in one sentence ("A gallery view that shows images up to 1600 pixels wide").
2. **Your setup**: Exponential version (see `/ezinfo/about`), hosting type, database, web server.
3. **What exists**: classes, design extension, any changes you made.
4. **What done means**: how you will check it works.
5. **Access**: a test copy if possible, not your live site.
6. **Budget and date.**

The project brief template of the businesses section works for small jobs too
([Project brief template](../../businesses/templates/project-brief-template.md)).

## Questions to ask

- Have you worked with Exponential 6 (or the older releases it continues)?
- Will you work on a copy first?
- Will the change live in my own extension, so updates do not overwrite it?
- Will you write down what you changed?
- What happens if it breaks after you finish?

## Keeping ownership

- Create a separate account for the helper; disable it when the job is done.
- Keep the domain, hosting and administrator passwords in your name.
- Ask for the changed files, or for the change in a Git repository you can read.
- You may ask that improvements of general use are contributed back to the project; it helps everyone
  ([Contribute extensions back](../../contribute-extensions-back.md)).
