# Pointing to the right help page

This page is for advocates who answer questions with links. Find the question on the left, send the page on the right.
In the administration, the help can also be searched: press `/` in the Help center, or use the "?" menu
([The help system, Finding a page](../../features/6.0/help-system.md#finding-a-page)).
