# Claims you must not make

This page is for every advocate, and it is the one page you should read before you say anything in public. One
unsupported claim costs more trust than ten correct ones earn. Each claim below has no source in the documentation;
the right column says what you can say instead.

## The rule

> If you cannot link the source, do not make the claim. If the source says "not documented", say "not documented".

## Numbers

| Do not say | Why | Say instead |
|---|---|---|
| "Thousands of sites use Exponential." | The sources do not count installations or users | "I do not know how many sites use it; the sources do not count them." |
| "There are thousands of Exponential developers." | No count exists ([Skills](../business/objection-handling.md#skills-can-we-find-people-who-know-it)) | "The stack is plain PHP, and the learning material is large and free." |
| "It is N times faster than X." | No published comparison exists | "Here is how it is made fast; measure your own site" ([Talking points: speed](talking-points-speed-and-velocity.md)) |
| "Market share", "most popular", "number one" | No source | Leave it out |
| Any figure you remember but cannot find | Figures change with releases | Look it up on [Where the facts live](where-the-facts-live.md) |

The figures that **are** documented (such as 368 locale files, 15 6.0.x releases, 66 required packages) carry a
date; numbers change, so cite the page, not your memory ([Who is 7x?, 7x in numbers](../who-is-7x.md#7x-in-numbers)).

## Security and compliance

| Do not say | Say instead |
|---|---|
| "It is secure", "unhackable", "bank-grade" | "Here is the security process and the hardening guide" ([Talking points: security](talking-points-security.md)) |
| "It has never had a vulnerability." | "Vulnerabilities are reported privately and fixed in the next release." |
| "It is GDPR compliant", "HIPAA compliant", or any legal compliance | "Compliance depends on how a site is built and run; here are the checklists" ([Accessibility, security and compliance checklists](../leadership/accessibility-security-and-compliance-checklists.md)) |
| "It is WCAG compliant." | "The administration follows WCAG rules that tests check" ([Talking points: accessibility](talking-points-accessibility.md)) |
| "Support answers within N hours." | "The only documented time is the first answer to a security report, within one business day." |

## People, companies and endorsements

| Do not say | Say instead |
|---|---|
| "Big company X uses it." | Only with that organization's written consent ([Writing a community case study](writing-a-community-case-study.md)) |
| "Endorsed by eZ Systems or Ibexa", "official successor" | "Not associated with eZ Systems or Ibexa" ([Who is 7x?, In short](../who-is-7x.md#in-short)) |
| "7x owns Exponential." | "7x leads it; the Exponential Foundation and 7x hold the copyright; the license is GPL." |
| "The Foundation's board is ..." | "The Foundation's governance is not documented in the sources." |
| "We" (as if you were 7x or the Foundation) | "I", unless they asked you to speak for them ([Disclosure](disclosure-and-conflicts-of-interest.md)) |

## The future

| Do not say | Say instead |
|---|---|
| "6.0.15 will be released on date X." | "6.0.15 is in development; releases are announced when they are tagged." |
| "Feature Y is coming." | "I have not seen it documented. Check the changelog and the issue tracker." |
| "It will be supported for N years." | "Here is the support commitment as documented" ([The support commitment](../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#the-support-commitment)) |
| "It will never go away." | "The license lets anyone keep and maintain the code." |

## Features

| Do not say | Say instead |
|---|---|
| "The AI publishes content for you." | "It writes drafts only" ([Talking points: Exponential Intelligence](talking-points-exponential-intelligence.md)) |
| "DXP has the layouts editor." | "Nexus ships the layouts editor; DXP does not" ([Knowing the product family](knowing-the-product-family.md#common-mix-ups)) |
| "Every language is fully translated." | "Ten administration translations are complete" ([Languages](talking-points-languages-and-right-to-left.md)) |
| "It runs on any hosting." | "Operating systems are documented in four tiers; shared hosting has limits." |
| "It does everything X does." | Describe what it does; let people compare ([Objection: "Why not another CMS?"](objection-why-not-another-cms.md)) |

## Events and programs

| Do not say | Say instead |
|---|---|
| "The official Exponential conference is ..." | "No events are documented. I am organizing a meetup myself." ([Proposing a meetup](proposing-a-meetup.md)) |
| "Join the official ambassador program." | "There is no documented program. You can start helping today." |
| "You get a certificate." | No certification is documented. |
| "There is a Discord server at ..." | "Discord and Slack are named on exponential.earth/about/project"; do not invent addresses ([The community channels](community-channels.md)) |

## When you got it wrong

Everyone makes mistakes. Correct them in the open:

1. Edit or reply to your post: "Correction: I wrote X. The source says Y (link)."
2. Do not delete silently; people may have quoted you.
3. If someone else repeated your mistake, tell them politely with the source.
4. Learn the source so it does not happen again.

```text
Correction: In my post above I said "<wrong claim>". That is not right. The documentation says
"<correct statement>" (<link>). Sorry for the confusion.
```
