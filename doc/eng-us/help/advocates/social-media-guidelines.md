# Social media guidelines

This page is for advocates who post about Exponential on any social network. Posts travel fast and far, out of
context. These guidelines keep your posts useful, accurate and safe. They are advice for volunteers, not rules of the
project; where the project has rules (names, security, licenses), the pages linked below state them.

## Ten guidelines

1. **Speak as yourself.** Your profile says who you are. You do not speak for 7x or the Foundation unless asked
   ([Disclosure and conflicts of interest](disclosure-and-conflicts-of-interest.md)).
2. **Link a source** for every fact ([Where the facts live](where-the-facts-live.md)).
3. **One post, one point.** Add the link where people can read more.
4. **Use the right names** ([Naming rules quick card](naming-rules-quick-card.md)).
5. **Be kind**, also to critics and to other projects ([Responding in public](responding-in-public.md)).
6. **Protect people**: no personal data, no screenshots with names, no customer names without consent
   ([Protecting people's privacy](protecting-peoples-privacy.md)).
7. **Keep security private**: never post vulnerability details; point to `SECURITY.md`
   ([Security reports stay private](security-reports-stay-private.md)).
8. **Disclose** when you are paid or work for a provider.
9. **Correct mistakes openly** ([Claims you must not make](claims-you-must-not-make.md#when-you-got-it-wrong)).
10. **Send questions to the channels** where others can find the answer: the forums and Telegram
    ([The community channels](community-channels.md)).

## Do and don't

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "Exponential 6.0.14 was released on 7 June 2026 (link)" | "Huge release, everyone is switching!" |
| Share a help page that solved your problem | Paste whole help pages as images |
| Credit others: "thanks to <name> for the fix" (if public) | Take credit for others' work |
| Ask "what do you use for X?" with curiosity | Reply to every mention of another CMS with Exponential |
| Answer a question with a link | Argue in other communities' threads |
| Post screenshots of your demo installation | Post screenshots of real sites' administration |
| Use the project's addresses as written | Invent accounts, groups or hashtags "of the project" |

## Names and links

- Exponential, Exponential 6, Exponential Velocity, Exponential Intelligence, Exponential Layouts, 7x, the Exponential
  Foundation. Old name only in the past tense.
- Link addresses the documentation uses: `exponential.earth`, `share.exponential.earth/forums`,
  `t.me/exponentialcms`, `github.com/se7enxweb/exponential`, the sponsor pages.
- The documentation does not name official social media accounts or hashtags. Do not present any account or hashtag as
  official. If you use a hashtag, it is your choice; `#Exponential` alone is ambiguous, so add context in the text.
- Discord and Slack are only "named among the support channels" on exponential.earth/about/project; the sources give
  no addresses here, so do not invent them ([Community places](../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#community-places)).

## Accessibility of posts

- Add alternative text to every image.
- Put the essential text in the post, not only in an image.
- Write hashtags in CamelCase (`#OpenSource`) so screen readers read them as words.
- Caption videos ([Recording a screencast](recording-a-screencast.md)).
- Avoid emoji walls; they are read aloud one by one.

## The pre-post checklist

```text
[ ] Is it true, and is the source linked?
[ ] Are the names right?
[ ] Does it contain anyone's personal data, a secret, a server address, a customer name? (then no)
[ ] Does it criticize another project or person? (then rewrite)
[ ] Do I need a disclosure?
[ ] Do images have alt text?
[ ] Would I be happy to see it quoted out of context?
```
