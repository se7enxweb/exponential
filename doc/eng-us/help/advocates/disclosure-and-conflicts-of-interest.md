# Disclosure and conflicts of interest

This page is for every advocate. People trust advocates because they believe you speak from your own experience. If
anyone pays you, employs you or gives you something related to what you recommend, readers deserve to know. Disclosure
does not weaken your message; hiding it destroys it when it comes out.

## When to disclose

| Your situation | Disclose? |
|---|---|
| A volunteer user with no financial link | Optional, but "I'm a volunteer" helps |
| You work for 7x | Yes, always |
| You work for any company that sells Exponential services, hosting or extensions | Yes, always |
| You were paid for this post, talk or video | Yes, clearly, at the start |
| You received something free (hosting, a license, travel) | Yes |
| You sell your own extension or theme and recommend it | Yes |
| You are a sponsor of 7x or the Foundation | Optional; it is not a conflict, but openness is welcome |
| A client asked you to write about their project | Yes, and get their approval ([Writing a community case study](writing-a-community-case-study.md)) |

## Disclosure lines to copy

```text
VOLUNTEER
I'm a volunteer Exponential advocate; nobody pays me for this.

EMPLOYEE OF A PROVIDER
I work for <company>, which offers Exponential services. Views are my own.

EMPLOYEE OF 7x
I work for 7x, the company that leads Exponential's development.

PAID CONTENT
This <post | video | talk> was paid for by <who>. They <did | did not> review it before publication.

OWN PRODUCT
I'm the author of <extension>, which I mention here.

SPEAKING OFFICIALLY (only when asked)
I'm speaking on behalf of <7x | the Exponential Foundation> at their request.
```

Put the line where people see it before they decide: at the top of a post, on the first slide, in the first seconds of
a video.

## Speaking for an organization

- 7x and the Exponential Foundation are two organizations
  ([7x and the Foundation for advocates](7x-and-the-foundation-for-advocates.md)).
- Unless one of them asked you, you speak for yourself. Say "I", not "we".
- Never announce releases, prices, events, partnerships or decisions for them.
- If someone treats you as an official voice, correct it kindly: "I'm a volunteer; for an official answer, ask
  <organization> through its website."

## Conflicts of interest

A conflict exists when your own gain could bend your advice. Handle it:

1. **Disclose** it.
2. **Separate** advice from offers: answer the question fully before mentioning your services, and only if asked.
3. **Point to alternatives**: the GPL means any provider can help; say so.
4. **Step back** when the conflict is strong, for example in a tender where you compete: let others answer.

| Situation | Better approach |
|---|---|
| A forum question you could turn into a client | Answer it publicly and fully; offer paid help only if they ask |
| Recommending your own extension | Mention alternatives too, and disclose |
| Reviewing a competitor's contribution | Review it on its merits, or ask someone else to |

## Platform rules and laws

Many platforms and countries have rules about marking paid or sponsored content. They differ by place and change over
time; follow those that apply to you. This page is not legal advice.
