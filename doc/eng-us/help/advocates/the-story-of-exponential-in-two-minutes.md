# The story of Exponential in two minutes

This page is for advocates who are asked "what is Exponential, and where does it come from?" It gives the story in
three lengths, every fact with its source, and the rules for talking about the old name. Learn the 30-second version
by heart; read the others from a card until you know them.

## The 30-second version

> "Exponential is a free, open source PHP content management system and application framework. Its code has been
> developed since 1999; when its original vendor discontinued it, 7x kept it alive, and since January 2024 has
> released Exponential 6. A nonprofit, the Exponential Foundation, now operates the project. It runs on PHP 8, on
> six databases including SQLite, and it is yours under the GNU GPL."

## The two-minute version

> "Exponential is a professional PHP application framework with advanced CMS functionality. Its most notable feature
> is a fully customizable content model: you define your own content types without code. It has a shop, multisite,
> many languages including right to left, approval workflows, a REST API and a visual page builder.
>
> The code base is old in a good way. Its copyright notice starts in 1998 and 1999. It was developed for many years
> under the name eZ Publish, a product its creator discontinued after almost eighteen years. 7x, a North American
> company that worked with this software since its first versions, took over leadership of the project in 2023, and the
> product was renamed Exponential in August 2025.
>
> Since 1 January 2024 there have been fifteen 6.0.x releases, and 6.0.15 brings Velocity, a PHP web server, an
> audit trail, the layouts editor, an AI writing assistant that only writes drafts, and the help you see inside the
> administration. The Exponential Foundation, a nonprofit founded in March 2026, operates the project. Everything is
> free software: the code under the GPL, the documentation under the GNU FDL."

## Words to use and words to avoid

| Say | Do not say |
|---|---|
| "Exponential continues the code base of eZ Publish" | "Exponential is the new eZ Publish" |
| "developed for many years under the name eZ Publish" | "eZ Publish, also called Exponential" |
| "7x leads the project; the Foundation operates it" | "7x owns Exponential" |
| "fifteen 6.0.x releases since January 2024" | "hundreds of releases", "updated every week" |
| "not associated with eZ Systems or Ibexa" | "the official successor" |

More in [Writing about the history correctly](writing-about-the-history-correctly.md) and the
[Brand and naming rules](../business/brand-and-naming-rules.md#the-old-name-ez-publish-only-as-history).

2. Tell the two-minute version to a friend who does not know the product. Ask what they remember.
3. Write down one question they asked, and find its source on [Where the facts live](where-the-facts-live.md).
