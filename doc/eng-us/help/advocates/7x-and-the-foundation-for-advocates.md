# 7x and the Exponential Foundation, explained for advocates

This page is for advocates who need to explain who stands behind Exponential. There are **two organizations** with
one project. Getting them right builds trust; mixing them up confuses people. Every fact here comes from
[Who is 7x?](../who-is-7x.md) and [Who is the Exponential Foundation?](../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md).

## Side by side

| | 7x | Exponential Foundation |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | A company: "a responsive North American web design, support, development and website hosting company" | A nonprofit: "a 501(c)(3) Non-Profit Corporation founded in Seattle, Washington, USA" on 19 March 2026 |
| Role | Leads development, support, hosting and services; "the current leader in contributions" | Operates the project; its mission is "Funding, Adoption, Support, Direction, Education, Development, Promotion and Documentation" |
| Sells services? | Yes: design, development, training, migration, support, hosting | No; it funds and directs the open source project |
| Sponsor page | [github.com/sponsors/se7enxweb](https://github.com/sponsors/se7enxweb) | [github.com/sponsors/exponentialcms](https://github.com/sponsors/exponentialcms) |
| Website | [se7enx.com](https://se7enx.com/) | [foundation.exponential.earth](https://foundation.exponential.earth/), [exponential.earth](https://exponential.earth/) |
| Names in code | `se7enxweb` (GitHub and Composer vendor) | |

Sources: [The Foundation and 7x](../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#the-foundation-and-7x),
[Who is 7x?, In short](../who-is-7x.md#in-short).

The copyright is shared: "Copyright (C) 1998 - 2026 Exponential Foundation and 7x. All rights reserved."
([COPYRIGHT.md](https://github.com/se7enxweb/exponential/blob/main/COPYRIGHT.md)).

## Sentences you can say

- "7x is the company that leads Exponential's development; the Exponential Foundation is the nonprofit that operates
  and funds the open source project."
- "If you want to hire someone for a site, 7x is one option; any provider can work with Exponential because the code
  is GPL."
- "If you want to support the open source project itself, sponsor the Foundation; if you want to support the company
  that does most of the work, sponsor 7x. Both have GitHub Sponsors pages."
- "7x used to be called Brookins Consulting, an eZ Publish partner in the USA."

## Mistakes to avoid

| Wrong | Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| "The 7x Foundation" | "the Exponential Foundation" | Two organizations, two names |
| "The Foundation sells support" | "7x and other providers sell services" | The Foundation does not sell services ([Brand and naming rules](../business/brand-and-naming-rules.md#the-nonprofit-the-exponential-foundation)) |
| "7x owns Exponential" | "7x leads Exponential" | The copyright is shared and the license is GPL |
| "Exponential Inc." | "7x" or "the Exponential Foundation" | No such company is named in the sources |
| "Sponsor us" (when you are a volunteer) | "You can sponsor the Foundation or 7x" | You do not speak for either ([Disclosure](disclosure-and-conflicts-of-interest.md)) |

## Questions people ask

| Question | Answer | Source |
|---|---|---|
| "Who pays for the development?" | "The Exponential Open Source Project is funded by donations of time and resources by 7x (and Exponential Sponsors)." | [The Foundation and 7x](../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#the-foundation-and-7x) |
| "What does a Foundation sponsorship pay for?" | Core library maintenance, documentation, integration layers, community infrastructure, security work on Zeta Components | [What the Foundation funds](../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#what-the-foundation-funds) |
| "Are donations tax deductible?" | The Foundation says so for the United States; check with your own tax adviser | [How to support or join](../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#how-to-support-or-join) |
| "Is it related to eZ Systems or Ibexa?" | No: "not associated with the original eZ Publish software or its original developer, eZ Systems or Ibexa" | [Who is 7x?, In short](../who-is-7x.md#in-short) |
| "Can I hire someone else than 7x?" | Yes. The code is GPL; anyone may host, change and support it | [Talking points: open source and licensing](talking-points-open-source-and-licensing.md) |

## What the sources do not say

The sources do not say who sits on the Foundation's board, how it is governed, whether it has members besides its
supporters, or what its budget is ([What the sources do not say](../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#what-the-sources-do-not-say)).
If someone asks, say: "That is not documented. You can ask the Foundation through its website." Never guess.
