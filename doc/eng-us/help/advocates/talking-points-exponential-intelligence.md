# Talking points: Exponential Intelligence

This page is for advocates who are asked about AI in Exponential. People have strong feelings about AI: some want it
everywhere, some want none. Your job is to describe precisely what Exponential Intelligence does and does not do, so
each person can decide.

## What it is, in one sentence

"A writing assistant built into the administration interface" that "writes drafts, as the signed-in editor and with
that editor's own permissions", and never publishes on its own
([Exponential Intelligence](../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#exponential-intelligence)).

## Points for the skeptical

| Concern | Answer | Source |
|---|---|---|
| "AI will publish nonsense." | Drafts only; a human publishes; workflows still apply | [expai, For editors](../../features/6.0/extensions/expai.md#for-editors) |
| "Our data will leave the building." | Nothing is sent until a provider key is added; a local model is possible | [Exponential Intelligence](../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#exponential-intelligence) |
| "We do not want AI at all." | Administrators can switch it off; read the feature page first | [expai, For administrators](../../features/6.0/extensions/expai.md#for-administrators) |
| "Who is responsible for the text?" | The editor who publishes it, as with any text | Your organization's policy |

## Do not say

- "The AI writes your site." It drafts; people decide.
- "It is GDPR compliant" or any legal statement. That depends on the provider, the region and the organization's own
  assessment; it is not for an advocate to declare.
- "It is free." The software is; a hosted provider may charge for use.
- "Exponential AI" or "ExpAI": the name is **Exponential Intelligence**, or `expai` in technical text
  ([Naming rules quick card](naming-rules-quick-card.md)).
- Anything about which provider is "best".

## Before you demo it

- Only demo it on an installation you control that has a provider key, and never show the key
  ([Live demo safety](live-demo-safety.md)).
- Use invented sample content, never real customer text.
- Mention the cost line in the edit form so nobody is surprised.
