# Writing a blog post

This page is for advocates who want to write about Exponential on their own blog, on Share or on a company site. A good
post helps one kind of reader do or understand one thing, and every fact in it has a link. The steps below take you
from idea to published post.

## Pick one reader and one point

Write one sentence before anything else:

```text
This post helps <reader> to <do or understand something> by <how>.
Example: This post helps editors to build a landing page without a developer by walking through the layouts editor.
```

If you cannot write it, the post is not ready yet.

## Structure

1. **Title**: what the reader gets. "Build a landing page in the layouts editor", not "Exponential rocks".
2. **First paragraph**: who it is for and what they will be able to do.
3. **Body**: steps or sections with headings; one idea per paragraph; screenshots with alternative text.
4. **Version line**: "Written for Exponential 6.0.x, admin4 design, checked on <date>."
5. **Next steps**: the help pages and the community channels.
6. **Disclosure** if relevant.

## The post template

```text
# <Title: what the reader gets>

<Who this is for, and what you'll be able to do after reading. 2 sentences.>

Written for Exponential <6.0.x>, checked on <date>.

## <Step or section 1>
<Text. Link the help page you followed: (link).>

## <Step or section 2>
...

## What's next
- The help inside your administration: the "?" button on every page.
- <Related help page>
- Questions: share.exponential.earth/forums or t.me/exponentialcms

<Disclosure: I'm a volunteer Exponential advocate. / I work for <company>, which <relation>.>
<License of this post, if you choose one.>
```

## Sources, names and disclosure

- **Link every fact.** Use [Where the facts live](where-the-facts-live.md); cite the page, not your memory.
- **Quote exactly** and mark quotes with quotation marks.
- **Names**: Exponential, 7x, the Exponential Foundation; the old name only as past history
  ([Naming rules quick card](naming-rules-quick-card.md)).
- **Disclosure**: say if you work for a company that sells Exponential services, or if anyone paid for the post
  ([Disclosure and conflicts of interest](disclosure-and-conflicts-of-interest.md)).
- **Licenses**: if you copy text from the help, respect the GNU FDL; quoting short parts with a link is simplest
  ([Citing sources and licensing your writing](citing-sources-and-licensing-your-writing.md)).

## Review and publish

1. Read it aloud; cut every sentence that does not help the reader.
2. Follow your own steps on a fresh installation.
3. Ask another advocate to read it; offer to read theirs.
4. Publish; share it in the forums, on Share and on social media ([Social post templates](social-post-templates.md)).
5. Answer comments; correct mistakes openly.

## Checklist

```text
[ ] One reader, one point (the sentence is written)
[ ] Every fact linked; quotes exact
[ ] Steps tested on a fresh installation; version line present
[ ] Names right; American English if writing in English for the project
[ ] No personal data, customer names without consent, secrets or server details
[ ] Images with alternative text
[ ] Disclosure if relevant; license stated if you chose one
[ ] Next steps link the help and the community channels
```
