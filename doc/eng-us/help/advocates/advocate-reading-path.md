# Advocate reading path

This page is for advocates who want to read the whole advocate section in a sensible order. Read a stage, then do
something with it before the next one: answer a question, write a post, give a demo. Every page of the section is
listed below, in the order of its place in the section.

## Stages

| Stage | Pages (order) | Reading time, roughly |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Orientation | 1 to 9 | 1 hour |
| 2. What to say | 10 to 25 | 2 hours |
| 3. Answering doubts | 30 to 37 | 1 hour |
| 4. Showing and writing | 40 to 58 | 2 to 3 hours |
| 5. Social media and channels | 60 to 74 | 1 to 2 hours |
| 6. Events and newcomers | 80 to 94 | 1 to 2 hours |
| 7. Contributing, values, ethics and tools | 100 to 145 | 2 to 3 hours |

The reading times are estimates for planning.
