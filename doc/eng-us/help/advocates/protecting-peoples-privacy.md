# Protecting people's privacy

This page is for every advocate. You will see other people's names, messages, sites and sometimes data: in forum
threads, in a friend's administration, at a meetup. Protecting them is part of being trustworthy. This page is not
legal advice; data protection laws differ by country and apply to you as they apply to anyone.

## The principles

1. **Collect nothing you do not need.**
2. **Publish nothing about a person without consent.**
3. **Use invented data** in demos, tutorials and screenshots.
4. **Remove what slipped through**, quickly.

The project follows the same line: bug reports must never contain "the personal data of customers or users"
([When something goes wrong](../when-something-goes-wrong.md#a-good-bug-description)), and the security policy asks to
leave out personal data ([SECURITY.md](https://github.com/se7enxweb/exponential/blob/main/SECURITY.md)).

## Where personal data leaks

| Place | Risk | Prevent it |
|---|---|---|
| Screenshots of an administration | Names, e-mails, orders in lists | A demo installation with invented content ([Live demo safety](live-demo-safety.md)) |
| Log excerpts in issues or forums | IP addresses, user names, e-mails | Remove or mask them before posting |
| Case studies | Staff names, customer numbers | Consent and approval ([Writing a community case study](writing-a-community-case-study.md)) |
| Chat screenshots | Other members' names and messages | Do not repost without consent |
| Recordings of meetups | Faces, voices, questions | Tell people before recording; let them opt out |
| Your contribution log | Names of people you helped | Log links, not personal details ([Tracking your contributions](tracking-your-contributions.md)) |

## Consent

- **Ask in writing**, explain where and how long it will be published, and keep the answer.
- **People can change their mind**: remove the content when asked.
- **Public handles in public places** (a GitHub user name on a public pull request) may be mentioned in that context;
  do not combine them with other information about the person.
- **Never reveal** a person's employer, location or real name from other sources.

## Events and sign-ups

When you organize a meetup ([Proposing a meetup](proposing-a-meetup.md)):

- Ask only for what you need (often a name or nickname and a contact).
- Say who sees the list and when you delete it; delete it after the event.
- Do not share attendee lists with sponsors or companies.
- Ask before taking photos of people; offer a "no photo" sign.

## Checklist

```text
[ ] My screenshots and demos use invented people and content
[ ] Logs and messages I post are free of IPs, e-mails, names and keys
[ ] Everyone named in my post agreed, or is named only by a public handle in its public context
[ ] I did not repost chat or forum content of others without consent
[ ] Event sign-ups ask the minimum and are deleted after the event
[ ] I know how to take something down fast when asked
```
