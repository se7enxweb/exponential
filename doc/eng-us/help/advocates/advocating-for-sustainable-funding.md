# Advocating for sustainable funding

This page is for advocates who want Exponential to have the money it needs: for new features, documentation,
applications, extensions, themes, product lines and the community. Free software is free to use; it is not free to
make. Encouraging people and organizations who rely on it to fund it is one of the most useful things an advocate can
do.

## The two sponsor pages

There are two organizations with two missions ([7x and the Foundation for advocates](7x-and-the-foundation-for-advocates.md)):

| Sponsor | Page | Funds |
|---|---|---|
| The Exponential Foundation | [github.com/sponsors/exponentialcms](https://github.com/sponsors/exponentialcms), or through [foundation.exponential.earth](https://foundation.exponential.earth/) | "the open-source project itself" |
| 7x | [github.com/sponsors/se7enxweb](https://github.com/sponsors/se7enxweb); also sponsor.se7enx.com and PayPal, per the README | "the company that keeps the lights on" |

Sources: [How to support or join](../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#how-to-support-or-join),
[The Foundation and 7x](../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#the-foundation-and-7x). The Foundation says donations
to it are tax deductible in the United States; everyone must check this with their own adviser.

## What the money pays for

From the se7enxweb profile README, as quoted in the help:

- **The Foundation**: core library maintenance, API and core documentation, integration layers (GraphQL, REST, Solr),
  community infrastructure, Zeta Components security hardening
  ([What the Foundation funds](../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#what-the-foundation-funds)).
- **7x**: monthly releases, Zeta Components patches, the LegacyBridge, the Symfony fork, the database translator, the
  training book mirror ([The Foundation and 7x](../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#the-foundation-and-7x)).

## Making the case

| Argument | Say |
|---|---|
| Reliance | "If your work depends on Exponential, a small monthly sponsorship keeps it maintained." |
| Value | "Security fixes, PHP support and documentation are work someone has to pay for." |
| Freedom | "Sponsoring keeps the software free for everyone, including you." |
| Choice | "You choose the mission: the open source project (the Foundation) or the company leading development (7x), or both." |
| Proportion | "A fraction of what a commercial license would cost." (Only as a general idea; no figures.) |

Also remind people that contributing time counts too: docs, translations, bug reports, fixes
([The advocacy ladder](the-advocacy-ladder.md)).

## To an employer or client

```text
Subject: Supporting the open source software we use: Exponential

We run <n> sites on Exponential, which is free software (GNU GPL). Its maintenance is funded by
sponsors. I suggest we sponsor it monthly:

- the Exponential Foundation (the open source project): github.com/sponsors/exponentialcms
- and/or 7x (the company that leads development):       github.com/sponsors/se7enxweb

Why: we benefit from security fixes, PHP support and documentation without license fees. A sustaining
sponsorship is a small, predictable cost that protects our investment.
Proposed amount: <amount>, reviewed yearly.
```

[Growing the community](../business/growing-the-community.md#every-year) lists a yearly decision on sponsorship as a
routine for organizations.

## Do not say

- That a sponsorship buys support, features or priority, unless the sponsor page says so.
- Amounts others give, or how much the organizations need: the sources do not state budgets
  ([What the sources do not say](../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#what-the-sources-do-not-say)).
- That you collect money: send people to the official sponsor pages only.
- Tax advice.
