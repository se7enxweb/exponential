# Respecting other projects and companies

This page is for every advocate. Exponential stands on the work of many others, and its users also use other software.
Speaking well of others is not only polite; it is how the project itself behaves, and it makes you credible.

## Why it matters

- Exponential's code base carries the work of eZ Systems and hundreds of contributors
  ([Contributors and friends of the project](../who-is-7x.md#contributors-and-friends-of-the-project)).
- Its features build on other projects: Netgen Layouts, Mugo Web's LoveStack, CJW Network's newsletter, Zeta
  Components, Symfony, PHP itself ([The PHP stack it stands on](../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#the-php-stack-it-stands-on-and-gives-back-to)).
- People you insult today may be contributors tomorrow.
- A reader who sees an attack on another product trusts every other claim less.

## How the project credits others

| Whom | How the sources say it |
|---|---|
| Netgen | Exponential Layouts brings "the Netgen Layouts concept to the Exponential kernel" |
| Mugo Web | LoveStack improvements rebuilt "with full credit to Mugo Web" |
| CJW Network | `cjw_newsletter`; request-shield is "a project of CJW Network ... not of the Foundation" |
| eZ Systems | Its copyright 1999 to 2014 stays in the code |
| eZ Systems and Ibexa | "This project is not associated with" them; spoken of factually |

Sources: [Brand and naming rules, Other companies and products](../business/brand-and-naming-rules.md#other-companies-and-products),
[Who is 7x?](../who-is-7x.md#contributors-and-friends-of-the-project).

## Rules for talking about others

1. **Never describe another product's weaknesses.** Describe what Exponential does; let people check the other
   product in its own documentation ([Comparing Exponential factually](../business/comparing-exponential-factually.md#the-rules-of-a-fair-comparison)).
2. **Credit work you build on**, by name.
3. **No speculation** about other companies' motives or futures.
4. **No association claims**: never suggest partnerships, endorsements or continuity that the sources do not state.
5. **Respect users' choices.** "If it works for you, great."
6. **Use their names correctly**, as they write them.

## In other communities' spaces

- Do not advertise Exponential in threads about other products unless someone asks for alternatives.
- If asked, answer briefly with facts and a link, then leave.
- Follow each community's rules.
- Help with general questions (PHP, accessibility, hosting) without mentioning Exponential at all: that builds goodwill.

## Examples

| Instead of | Say |
|---|---|
| "X is bloated." | "Exponential's install is one command on SQLite." |
| "Ibexa abandoned you." | "If you are on eZ Platform or Ibexa, there is a documented migration path" ([chapter 16](../../install/16-migrating-from-ez-platform-and-ibexa.md)) |
| "Our layouts editor is better than Netgen's." | "Exponential Layouts brings the Netgen Layouts concept to Exponential." |
| "Nobody serious uses X." | Say nothing. |
