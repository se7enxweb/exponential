# Non-technical people: start here

This section is for people who use or look after an Exponential website but do not want to become technical: the
office manager who updates the opening times, the volunteer who posts club news, the owner of a small business who
pays for the site, the family member who keeps a photo page, the board member who must sign a hosting contract.

You will not find a command line here. Every page says what you do in your web browser, what somebody else does for
you (a hosting company, a developer or a partner), and what to ask them for.

> **Note**
> The section starts with words, not with steps. Computers are confusing mostly because of the vocabulary. Read the
> [glossary](non-technical-people-glossary.md) first, or keep it open in a second tab: every other page uses the same
> words in the same meaning.

## Exponential in plain words

Exponential is the program that runs a website and lets people change it from a web browser. The things on the site,
such as a page, a news article, a picture or a document, are called **content items**. They are arranged in a
**tree**, like folders on a computer. You change them in the **administration** (some sites also have a simpler
**Editor**), and visitors see them on the **site** ([Getting started](../getting-started.md#what-exponential-is)).

Three facts make life easier for a non-technical person:

- **Nothing goes live by accident.** You always work on a **draft**. The site keeps showing the old version until you
  press **Send for publishing** ([Publishing, versions and the trash](../publishing-and-versions.md#drafts-and-versions)).
- **Mistakes can be undone.** Earlier versions are kept, and a removed item goes to the **trash** first, from where it
  can be put back ([Undoing a mistake](first-steps/undoing-a-mistake.md)).
- **The software costs nothing to license.** Exponential is free software under the GNU General Public License. You pay
  for hosting and for people's time, never for a license ([Licensing for buyers](../business/licensing-for-buyers.md)).

The longer version is [What Exponential is, in plain words](what-exponential-is-in-plain-words.md).

## What you do, and what you never have to do

| You do this, in the browser | Somebody technical does this for you |
|---|---|
| Sign in, change text, add pictures and documents | Install Exponential on a server |
| Publish, hide, move and remove pages | Install updates and security fixes |
| Arrange a page with blocks, if your site uses Layouts | Make backups and test that they restore |
| Translate a page, if your site has more languages | Set up the domain name, e-mail and HTTPS |
| Decide who in your team may do what (if you are the administrator) | Change the design templates and add extensions |

Installing and updating Exponential normally uses a command line and a tool called Composer, on a server that meets
the requirements ([Requirements at a glance](../hosting/basics/requirements-at-a-glance.md)); a setup wizard in the
browser finishes the installation once the files are there. That is why this section says plainly
that installation is a job for a hosting company, a developer or a partner, and helps you pick one. Details are on
[What you never have to do](what-you-never-have-to-do.md).

## Your first hour

| Minutes | Do | Read |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 10 | Read the glossary once, quickly | [Glossary](non-technical-people-glossary.md) |
| 10 to 20 | Sign in and look around without changing anything | [Signing in](first-steps/signing-in-for-the-first-time.md), [Finding your way around](first-steps/finding-your-way-around.md) |
