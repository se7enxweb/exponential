# What to ask before you sign

You do not need to understand the technology to judge an offer. You need clear answers to the right questions. Ask
them in writing, and compare the answers side by side.

## Updates and security

| Ask | A good answer sounds like |
|---|---|
| Who installs new Exponential releases and security fixes? | "We do, within N days of a security release, and we tell you what changed." |
| How do I know an update worked? | "We send you the new version number and confirm the site and the administration work." ([How updates work](../../customers/healthy/how-updates-work.md#what-tells-you-an-update-worked)) |
| Will editors have two-step sign-in? | "Yes, it is available and we can require it." ([Your password and two-step sign-in](../first-steps/your-password-and-two-step-sign-in.md)) |

Exponential fixes problems only in new releases; a published version is never corrected afterwards. So "staying
current is the security plan" ([How updates work](../../customers/healthy/how-updates-work.md#why-you-update-at-all)).

## Backups

| Ask | A good answer sounds like |
|---|---|
| What is backed up? | "The database, the uploaded files and the settings." |
| How often, and where? | "Daily, to a different place from the server." |
| When was a backup last restored as a test? | A date, and what was restored. |
| Can I get a copy? | "Yes, on request." |

([Backups](../../customers/healthy/backups.md))

## Ownership

| Ask | A good answer sounds like |
|---|---|
| In whose name is the domain registered? | "In yours." |
| Who owns the content and the design work? | "You do." (Exponential itself is free software under the GPL; nobody can sell you a license to it.) |
| Do I get an administrator account? | "Yes." Even if you never use it. |

([What you own](../../customers/what-you-own.md), [Licensing for buyers](../../business/licensing-for-buyers.md))

## Support

| Ask | A good answer sounds like |
|---|---|
| How do I report a problem? | One clear channel: an e-mail address, a phone number or a ticket form. |
| When are you available, and how quickly do you answer? | Concrete hours and times, in the agreement. |
| What happens when the site is down at night? | "Our monitoring alerts us, and ..." |
| Is editor help included? | A clear yes or no, and the price if no. |

The [SLA template](../../customers/partners-and-hosts/sla-template.md) shows how such promises are written down.

## Data protection

| Ask | A good answer sounds like |
|---|---|
| Where are the servers and the backups? | A country, and the name of the data center company if you need it. |
| Will you sign a data processing agreement? | "Yes." (If your site collects personal data through forms, a shop or members.) |
| Who at your company can see our data? | "Only the people who need to, and they are bound to confidentiality." |

## Leaving

| Ask | A good answer sounds like |
|---|---|
| If we leave, what do we get? | "A complete export: database, files, settings." |
| How quickly, and at what cost? | A number of days, and a price or "included". |
| What happens to our data afterwards? | "We delete it and confirm in writing." |

([Moving to a new host](moving-to-a-new-host.md))

## Warning signs

Be careful when you hear:

- "You don't need updates, it's stable." Every site needs updates.
- "The domain is easier in our name." It is not easier for you when you want to leave.
- "We don't do restore tests, the backups are fine." Nobody knows until they test.
- "That's a license fee for Exponential." Exponential has no license fee; ask what the money is really for.
- Answers you do not understand after asking twice.
