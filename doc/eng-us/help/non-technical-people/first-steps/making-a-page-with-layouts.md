# Making a page with Layouts

**Exponential Layouts** is a visual page builder. Instead of one long text, a page is made of **blocks** (a title, a
text, a picture, a list of news, a gallery) placed in **zones** (areas such as left, right, header, main). It is used
for front pages and campaign pages ([The layouts editor](../../layouts-editor.md)).

> **Note**
> Not every site uses Layouts, and not every role may change them. The **Layouts** tab is shown only to roles that
> may work with layouts. If you do not see it, ask your administrator.

## The parts of the editor

- **At the top**: the layout's name, **Discard**, **Cancel** and **Publish layout**.
- **On the left**: the plus button, **Add a block**.
- **In the middle**: the zones and their blocks, roughly as the page will look.
- **On the right**: the settings of the block you clicked.

You always work on a **draft** of the layout; the site keeps showing the published one until you publish, exactly as
with articles ([Drafts, versions and publishing explained](../words/drafts-versions-and-publishing-explained.md)).

## Steps: add a block

1. Open **Layouts**, then **Layouts**, and press **Open** next to the layout of your page.
2. Press **Add a block** (the plus). The block types appear in groups such as **Basic** (Title, Text, Button / Link,
   Rich text, Image), **Listing** and **Gallery**.
3. Drag a block type into a zone, to the place where it should go.
4. Click the new block. On the right, the **Content** tab holds what it shows (the text of a title, which items a
   list shows and how many); the **Design** tab holds how it looks.
5. Check the middle area: it shows your change.

([Add a block](../../layouts-editor.md#add-a-block))

## Change, move or delete a block

- **Change**: click it and edit its settings on the right.
- **Move**: drag it by its handle to another place or zone.
- **Copy or delete**: the three-dot menu on the block has **Duplicate block** and **Delete block**.

## Publish or keep for later

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Publish layout** | Publishes and closes the editor. The page shows the change at once |
| **Publish and continue editing** (three-dot menu at the top) | Publishes and keeps the editor open |
| **Save draft and close** (same menu) | Keeps your draft for later; the page is unchanged |
| **Discard** | Throws your draft away after you confirm |

If the editor asks whether to clear caches after publishing, choose **Clear caches** so visitors see the change.

## What to leave to a site builder

- **Rules** (mappings) decide which pages use which layout. Changing them can change whole parts of the site
  ([Which pages use a layout](../../layouts-editor.md#which-pages-use-a-layout)).
- **Shared layouts** hold the header and footer of many pages; a change there changes them all.
- New layouts with a new **layout type**.

When in doubt, change blocks, not rules.
