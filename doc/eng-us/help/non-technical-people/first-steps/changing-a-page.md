# Changing a page

The most common job of all: something on the site is out of date, and you fix it.

## Contents

1. [Before you start](#before-you-start)
2. [Steps](#steps)
3. [Working in the text box](#working-in-the-text-box)

5. [If it does not work](#if-it-does-not-work)

## Before you start

Have the new text ready, ideally written in a plain document first. Copying from a word processor works, but keep
formatting simple: headings, bold, lists and links.

## Steps

1. **Find the page.** Open **Content structure** and click through the tree, or type a word from the page into
   **Search...** ([Finding something](finding-something.md)). Click the page's name.
2. **Press Edit.** If the page exists in several languages, choose the language in the list next to **Edit** first.
   The edit form opens with a **draft**: a copy only you can see
   ([Drafts, versions and publishing explained](../words/drafts-versions-and-publishing-explained.md)).
3. **Find the field.** The bar at the top of the form has one button per field (*Title*, *Intro*, *Body* ...); a click
   jumps to that field ([The edit form](../../editing-content.md#the-edit-form)).
4. **Make your change.**
5. **Look before you publish.** **Preview** in the left column shows the draft as it will look.
6. **Press Send for publishing.** On most sites the change is on the site at once. If your site uses approvals, it
   waits under **My pending items** until someone approves it.
7. **Check the site.** Open the page on the public site. If you still see the old text, see
   [Caches explained](../words/caches-explained.md#when-you-see-an-old-page).

## Working in the text box

Long text fields (rich text) have a small toolbar: paragraph styles, bold, italic, lists, links, tables, pictures and
**Fullscreen** ([Rich text](../../editing-content.md#rich-text)). Good habits:

- Use the **paragraph styles** for headings instead of making text big and bold. Screen readers and search engines
  understand real headings.
- Keep paragraphs short.
- Write link text that says where it goes ("our opening times"), never "click here".

Pressing Enter in a one-line field (such as *Title*) is the same as pressing **Send for publishing**. In a long text
field, Enter makes a new paragraph as usual.

## Not finished yet?

- **Store draft and exit** keeps your draft. Continue later from **My drafts** on the dashboard.
- **Discard draft** throws your change away; the published page stays exactly as it was.
- The draft is saved automatically every few minutes while you type, so a closed browser tab costs little.

## If it does not work

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| The form comes back with a red message | A required field (red star) is empty, or a value is wrong. The message at the top and at the field says which. |
| *Possible edit conflict* | You or a colleague already has a draft. Continue **your** draft, or start a **New draft** ([When someone else is editing](../../editing-content.md#when-someone-else-is-editing)). |
| **Edit** is grayed out | Your role may not change this page. Ask your administrator. |
| The change is not on the site | Was it published, or is it pending? Then a cache ([Caches explained](../words/caches-explained.md)). |
| You published something wrong | Go back to the previous version ([Undoing a mistake](undoing-a-mistake.md)). |
