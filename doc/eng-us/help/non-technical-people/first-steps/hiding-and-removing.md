## Hide or remove?

| You want | Use | Because |
|---|---|---|
| A seasonal page (summer menu, holiday hours) off the site until next year | **Hide** | It stays in the administration, ready to unhide |
| A page that is not ready yet, but already created | **Hide**, or keep it as a draft | Visitors do not see it |
| Something harmful off the site right now | **Hide** first, then fix or remove | Hiding is instant and fully reversible |
| An old page nobody needs | **Remove** | It goes to the trash and can still be restored |

A hidden item stays in the administration but is not shown on the site, **and neither is anything below it**
([Hide an item](../../publishing-and-versions.md#hide-an-item)).

## Hide an item

1. In the tree on the left, click the icon in front of the item's name to open its menu.
2. Choose **Advanced** > **Hide / unhide**.

The item is marked as hidden in the tree and in lists, and the badge on its page shows its visibility. Do the same
again to unhide it.

## Remove an item

1. Open the item and press **Remove**.
2. Read the confirmation: it says what the removal touches and which other items use this one.
3. Confirm. The item, with everything below it, goes to the **trash**.

Items in the trash are not on the site, and can be restored ([Undoing a mistake](undoing-a-mistake.md#you-removed-something)).
When an item has more than one location, removing one location keeps it in its other places
([Remove and restore](../../publishing-and-versions.md#remove-and-restore)).

## Several items at once

In the **Sub items** list of a folder, tick the items, open **More actions** and choose **Hide selected**, **Unhide
selected** or **Remove selected** ([Many items at once](../../editing-content.md#many-items-at-once)).

## Before you remove: think of links

Other pages, newsletters, search engines and bookmarks may point at the page you remove. Visitors following them land
on a "not found" page. Before removing an important page:

- check the confirmation for other items that use it;
- ask your administrator whether the old address should **redirect** to a new page
  ([How do I redirect old addresses?](../../manual/how-do-i-redirect-old-addresses.md));
- for a page that might come back, prefer **Hide**.
