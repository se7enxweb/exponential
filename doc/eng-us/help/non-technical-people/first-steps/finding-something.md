## Four ways to find

| You know | Use |
|---|---|
| A word or two from the page | **Search...** at the top |
| The kind of item, roughly when, roughly where | **Advanced search** |
| The address of the page on the public site | **Content finder** in the left column |
| Where it lives in the menu | Click through the tree |

([How do I find content?](../../manual/how-do-i-find-content.md))

## Search by words

1. Type a word or two into **Search...** in the top bar and press Enter.
2. The results list matching items, best first. Click one to open it.

Put words in quotation marks for an exact phrase: `"summer fair"`.

## Search with filters

1. Open **Advanced search** (from the search results, or on the dashboard).
2. Choose a content type (for example *Article*), a section, when it was published, and below which place.
3. Press **Search**.

This helps when a word appears on too many pages.

## Find by the address of the page

The quickest way when you are looking at the page on the public site:

1. Copy the address from the browser's address bar, for example `https://www.example.com/news/summer-fair`.
2. In the administration, type it (or just `/news/summer-fair`) into **Content finder** in the left column.
3. Press **Find**. The item opens, and **Edit** is one click away.

## If you cannot find it

| Reason | What to do |
|---|---|
| It was published a moment ago | The search index updates a few minutes after publishing. Use the Content finder or the tree meanwhile |
| It is in the trash | Look in **Trash** at the bottom of the tree |
| Your role may not see it | Ask your administrator |
| It exists in another language only | Search in that language, or ask which languages the site has |
| Too many results | Use quotation marks or **Advanced search** |

The help has its own search too: open **Help** and type your question
([Searching](../../searching.md)).
