# Using the writing assistant

**Exponential Intelligence** (the extension `expai`) is an optional writing assistant. It suggests texts,
rewrites, translations and alternative texts. It **never publishes on its own**: what it writes becomes a suggestion
or a draft, and you decide ([How do I use the AI assistant in the editor?](../../manual/how-do-i-use-the-ai-assistant-in-the-editor.md)).

## Does my site have it?

If it is switched on and your role may use it, each text field in the edit form shows **Exponential Intelligence**
with buttons such as **Write**, **Rewrite**, **Translate**, **Suggest** and **Improve**
([Exponential Intelligence](../../editing-content.md#exponential-intelligence)). If you see none of these, either
your role lacks the permission or no AI provider is set up. Ask your administrator; setting it up needs an account
with an AI provider, which your site's owner chooses and pays for.

## Steps

1. Edit an item.
2. In the field you want help with, press one of the assistant's buttons, for example **Improve**.
3. Read the suggestion.
4. Accept it if it is good, change it, or ignore it. Nothing in the field changes until you accept.
5. Store the draft or publish as usual.

The full user manual of the assistant is in the help center under **Help** > **Extensions** > **Exponential
Intelligence** > **User manual**.

## What the buttons do

| Button | Use it to |
|---|---|
| **Write** | Get a first draft from a short instruction |
| **Rewrite** | Say the same thing differently, for example shorter or friendlier |
| **Translate** | Get a draft in another language, to check and correct |
| **Suggest** | Get ideas, for example for a title |
| **Improve** | Fix spelling, grammar and clarity |

## Checking what it wrote

You are the author. An AI can be fluent and wrong at the same time. Before you accept:

- Check **every fact**: names, dates, prices, opening hours, numbers.
- Check that it says what **you** mean, in your organization's voice.
- Check **translations** with someone who speaks the language, at least for important pages.
- Remove anything you cannot stand behind.

[Using AI drafts responsibly](../../customers/growth/using-ai-drafts-responsibly.md) has a fuller checklist.

## Cost and privacy

- The box at the top of the edit form says which service the assistant uses and what a click costs. Many providers
  charge per use, so your organization pays for each request.
- What you send to the assistant goes to that outside provider. Do not paste personal data, passwords or
  confidential information into it.
