## Two kinds of links

| Link to | How | Why |
|---|---|---|
| A page of **your own site** | Choose it with **Browse** | The link follows the page if it is moved or renamed |
| **Another website** | Type its full address, starting with `https://` | The other site decides whether its address stays valid |

([How do I add a link in the online editor?](../../manual/how-do-i-add-a-link-in-the-online-editor.md))

## Steps

1. Edit the page and select the words that become the link.
2. Press **Insert/edit link** in the toolbar.
3. For a page of your site, press **Browse** and choose the item. For another site, type or paste its address.
4. If you like, choose to open the link in a new window, and give it a title.
5. Press **Insert**.
6. Press **Send for publishing**.

Typing the address of one of your own pages also works, but it breaks when that page moves. Use **Browse**.

## Writing good link text

The words you make into a link should say where the link goes, even when read on their own. Screen reader users
often jump from link to link, hearing only the link text.

| Weak | Better |
|---|---|
| For our opening times, **click here** | See our **opening times** |
| **More** | **More about the summer fair** |
| **https://www.example.org/forms/2025/membership.pdf** | **Membership form (PDF)** |

Open other websites in the same window unless there is a good reason not to; visitors know how to go back.

## Removing a link

Put the cursor inside the link and press **Unlink** in the toolbar, then publish.

## Broken links

Other websites change their addresses, and a link that worked last year may lead nowhere now. If your role allows it,
**Setup > Link management** lists links that fail
([How do I find and fix broken links?](../../manual/how-do-i-find-and-fix-broken-links.md)). Otherwise, ask your
administrator to look once a month; it is on the
[Monthly checklist for site owners](../checklists/monthly-checklist-for-site-owners.md).
