## The three places you use every day

1. **The dashboard**, to see your unfinished work.
2. **Content structure**, to find the page you want to change.
3. **The edit form**, to change it.

Everything else you can ignore at first.

## The dashboard

After you sign in you see the **Dashboard**: a greeting, quick links, a few numbers (your drafts, your pending items,
content published lately) and lists of recent content ([Getting started](../../getting-started.md#2-look-around)).
The two lists to look at every time:

- **My drafts**: changes you started and did not publish.
- **My pending items**: changes waiting for someone's approval.

## The tabs at the top

| Tab | What is there |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Your starting page |
| **Content structure** | The pages of the site, as a tree |
| **Media** | The media library: pictures, files, videos |
| **Users**, **Store**, **Layouts**, **Setup** and more | Only when your role allows them |

At the right of the header are the **Search...** field, the **Help**, your user menu and the sign-out button. If you
use the simpler **Editor**, you see fewer tabs; that is normal
([The site and the administration](../words/the-site-and-the-administration.md)).

## The tree on the left

The left column shows the **tree**: the items of the site arranged like folders. Click the small triangle in front of
a name to open a branch, and click a name to open the item. The icon in front of a name opens a menu with actions such
as **Create here** and **Edit in** ([Content items and the tree](../words/content-items-and-the-tree.md)).

At the bottom of the tree is the **Trash**, where removed items wait
([Undoing a mistake](undoing-a-mistake.md)).

## An item's page

When you open an item, its page shows:

- its **title**, its **type** (Article, Folder ...) and a badge that says whether it is visible;
- its **content**, as it is stored;
- the **buttons**: **Edit**, **Move**, **Copy**, **Remove**, and the round **Manage versions** button with a clock;
- under **Sub items**, the items inside it, with **Create new** and **Upload**.

A button you may not use is grayed out; point at it to read why
([Creating and editing content](../../editing-content.md#move-copy-and-remove)).

## A ten-minute exercise

Do this once, changing nothing:

1. On the dashboard, find **My drafts**. Is anything there?
2. Click **Content structure** and open two folders in the tree.
3. Open an article. Find **Edit**, **Manage versions** and **Sub items**.
4. Click **Media** and open the **Images** folder, if your site has one.
5. Type a word from your site into **Search...** and press Enter.
6. Open **Help** and type "publish" into its search.

You now know where everything you need lives. [A tour of the administration](../../admin-tour.md) explains the rest
of the screen when you are curious.
