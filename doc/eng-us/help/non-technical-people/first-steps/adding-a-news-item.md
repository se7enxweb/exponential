## Before you start

- Know **where** news lives on your site: usually a folder called *News*, *Blog* or *Updates*. You always create a new
  item *inside* the place it belongs ([Content items and the tree](../words/content-items-and-the-tree.md)).
- Have the text and, if you like, one picture ready.

## Steps

1. Open **Content structure** and click the news folder.
2. Under **Sub items**, press **Create new**. A list of the content types you may create here opens.
3. Choose **Article** (your site may call it *News*, *Blog post* or similar). An empty form opens
   ([Create a new item](../../editing-content.md#create-a-new-item)).
4. Fill in the **Title**. Keep it short and clear; it often becomes the web address of the page too.
5. Fill in the **Intro**: one or two sentences that make people want to read on. Lists of news often show only this.
6. Fill in the **Body**: the full text.
7. Add a picture, if the form has an *Image* field: press **Upload new** to take one from your computer, or **Add
   existing** to pick one from the media library ([Adding a picture](adding-a-picture.md)).
8. Press **Preview** to check, then **Send for publishing**.

The new item now appears in the news folder and, on most sites, on the news list and the front page.

## Writing a good news item

| Part | Tip |
|---|---|
| Title | Says what happened, in under ten words |
| Intro | Answers who, what and when in one or two sentences |
| Body | The most important information first; short paragraphs; headings for longer texts |
| Picture | Your own or properly licensed, with an alternative text |
| Links | To the page where readers can act: sign up, buy, read more |

## Publishing later

- **Store draft and exit** keeps it as a draft, visible only to you, under **My drafts**.
- Some sites can publish at a set date and time. Whether yours can, and how, depends on how it was set up
  ([Scheduled publishing](../../customers/day-to-day/scheduled-publishing.md)).
- If your site uses approvals, **Send for publishing** sends it to the approver first.

## If it does not work

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Create new** is missing | You may not create items here. Check you are in the right folder, or ask your administrator. |
| *Article* is not in the list | This folder accepts other types only. Use the news folder. |
| The form comes back red | A required field is empty; the message says which. |
| The item is not on the front page | The front page may show only some news, or a cache is showing the old page ([Caches explained](../words/caches-explained.md)). |
| You created it in the wrong folder | Use **Move** on the item's page ([Move, copy and remove](../../editing-content.md#move-copy-and-remove)). |
