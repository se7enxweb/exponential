# Undoing a mistake

Mistakes happen to everyone. Exponential keeps enough history that almost every mistake can be undone by you, in the
browser, in a minute.

## Contents

1. [Which mistake?](#which-mistake)
2. [You have not published yet](#you-have-not-published-yet)

## Which mistake?

| Mistake | Go to |
|---|---|
| I am still in the edit form and messed it up | [You have not published yet](#you-have-not-published-yet) |
| I published a wrong text or picture | [You published something wrong](#you-published-something-wrong) |
| I removed a page that should still be there | [You removed something](#you-removed-something) |
| I moved a page to the wrong place | Use **Move** again and choose the right place ([Move, copy and remove](../../editing-content.md#move-copy-and-remove)) |

## You have not published yet

Press **Discard draft** and confirm. Your draft is thrown away and the page stays exactly as it was. Then start again
with **Edit**. Nothing reached the site.

## You published something wrong

The previous version is still there, in the drawer.

1. Open the item.
2. Press **Manage versions**: the round button with a clock.
3. Find the version before your change. To be sure, use **Compare**: choose two versions and press **Show
   differences**; changes are marked field by field.
4. Under **New draft from this version, in**, choose the language and press **Make a new draft**.
5. Check the new draft and press **Send for publishing**.

The good version is back. Your wrong version stays in the history, which is fine
([Go back to an earlier version](../../publishing-and-versions.md#go-back-to-an-earlier-version)).

If the wrong content is harmful (personal data, something offensive), first **hide** the item so it disappears from
the site at once ([Hiding and removing](hiding-and-removing.md)), then fix it calmly.

## You removed something

Removed items go to the **trash**, at the bottom of the tree in the left column.

1. Open **Trash**.
2. Find the item; the filters narrow the list by who removed it, its type and the date.
3. Select it and press **Restore**.

It goes back to its old place if that still exists. If its parent folder is in the trash too, restore the parent
first ([Remove and restore](../../publishing-and-versions.md#remove-and-restore)). Your role decides whether you may
restore; if you may not, ask your administrator quickly.

## What cannot be undone

Be careful with these, and leave them to your administrator when in doubt:

- **Empty trash** and **Remove selected** inside the trash delete for good.
- **Remove selected versions** deletes old versions for good.
- Very old versions are removed automatically when an item has reached the site's version limit.
- A **newsletter** that was sent cannot be unsent.
- An e-mail notification that went out to followers cannot be called back.

For anything beyond this, the last resort is your host's **backup** ([Backups](../../customers/healthy/backups.md)).

## When to call for help

Call your administrator or host when the trash is empty and something is gone, when a whole part of the site looks
wrong, or when you are not sure what happened. Note what you did, the time and the address of the page
([How to ask for help](../when-stuck/how-to-ask-for-help.md)).
