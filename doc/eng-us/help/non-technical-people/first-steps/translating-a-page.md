# Translating a page

On a site with more than one language, each page can exist in each language. A translation is edited and published
separately, so the English page can be ready while the Spanish one is still a draft
([Languages](../../languages.md)).

## Contents

1. [Before you start](#before-you-start)
2. [Steps](#steps)
3. [Tips for translators](#tips-for-translators)
4. [If it does not work](#if-it-does-not-work)

## Before you start

- Your site must already have the language set up. Adding a whole new language to a site is a job for your
  administrator ([How do I add a content language?](../../manual/how-do-i-add-a-content-language.md)).
- Your role must allow translating into that language.

## Steps

1. Open the page in **Content structure** and press **Edit**. On a multilingual site, a page with the languages opens.
2. Under **New translation**, choose the language to add.
3. Under **Translate based on**, choose the language to copy from (so the fields start filled in), or **None** to
   start empty.
4. Press **Edit**. The form opens in the new language.
5. Translate **every** field, including the title: it usually becomes the web address in that language.
6. Press **Send for publishing**.
7. Check the item's **Translations** window: the new language is listed.

([How do I translate content?](../../manual/how-do-i-translate-content.md))

## Tips for translators

- Translate **meaning**, not word by word. A local reader should not notice it is a translation.
- Check **dates, prices, phone numbers and units**; they often differ between countries.
- **Pictures** are shared, but their alternative text can be translated: check it.
- The writing assistant can give you a first draft with **Translate**, if your site has it; always check it
  ([Using the writing assistant](using-the-writing-assistant.md)).
- When the original page changes, its translations do not change by themselves. Ask your administrator how your
  team keeps track ([How do I find what is not translated?](../../manual/how-do-i-find-what-is-not-translated.md)).

## If it does not work

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| *You do not have permission to create a translation in another language* | Your role is limited to other languages. Ask your administrator |
| The language is not offered | It is not a language of the site yet, or the page already has it (then choose it under **Existing translations**) |
| Visitors do not see the translation | No part of the site shows that language yet. Ask your administrator |
