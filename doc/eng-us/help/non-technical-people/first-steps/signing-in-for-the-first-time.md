## What you need

- **The address** of the administration or the Editor. Your administrator gives it to you. It often looks like
  `https://www.example.com/admin`, `https://www.example.com/editor` or `https://edit.example.com`
  ([The site and the administration](../words/the-site-and-the-administration.md)).
- **Your username** (many sites also accept your e-mail address) and **your password**.
- For some sites, **your phone** with an authenticator app, or access to your e-mail, for two-step sign-in.

## Steps

1. Open the address in your browser. Bookmark it now, so you never have to look for it again.
2. Type your **Username** (or your e-mail address).
3. Type your **Password**. The eye button in the field shows what you typed; press it again to hide it.
4. Press **Sign in**, or Enter.
5. You see the **Dashboard**, with a greeting and your name. You are in
   ([Logging into the Editor](../../logging-into-the-editor.md#after-signing-in)).

If you received a temporary password, change it now: open **Change password** in the user menu or under **My
account** ([Your password and two-step sign-in](your-password-and-two-step-sign-in.md)).

## If the site asks for a code

Some sites use **two-step sign-in**. After your password, a page called **Confirm it is you** asks for a 6-digit code:

- from the **authenticator app** on your phone, or
- from an **e-mail** the site just sent you (the code is also in the subject line).

Type it and press **Verify and sign in**. Spaces in the code are fine. Each code works once. If the site requires
two-step sign-in for everyone and you have not set it up yet, it walks you through the setup right after your
password ([Two-step sign-in](../../accounts-and-sign-in.md#two-step-sign-in)).

## If it does not work

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *The system could not log you in ...* | Username or password does not match | Check spelling and the Caps Lock key; try your e-mail address instead of the username |
| *... exceeded allowable number of failed login attempts* | Too many wrong passwords; the account is locked | Ask your administrator to unlock it ([How do I disable or unlock a user account?](../../manual/how-do-i-disable-or-unlock-a-user-account.md)) |
| *Access denied!* | Your account is fine, but your role may not use this door | Ask your administrator; maybe you should use the Editor address |
| A small browser window asks for a name and password before the page appears | An extra password on a test server; not your Exponential account | Ask whoever gave you the address |
| You land on the sign-in form again after signing in | Your browser blocks cookies | Allow cookies for the site, or try a private window |
| A yellow **Demo mode** banner | A public trial site | Fine for trying; never enter anything private ([Demo mode](../../demo-mode.md)) |

**Forgot your password?** Choose **Forgot your password?** under the button, enter your e-mail address and follow the
link in the mail. The link works once and, by default, for one hour. No mail? Check the spam folder
([Forgot your password?](../../logging-into-the-editor.md#forgot-your-password)).

## Signing out

Press the last button at the right end of the header: an arrow leaving a box (**Logout**). It is grayed out while you
edit; store or discard your draft first. On a shared computer, always sign out
([Getting started](../../getting-started.md#5-sign-out)).
