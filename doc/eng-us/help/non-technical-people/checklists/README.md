# Checklists for non-technical people

Checklists turn good habits into routine. Each one fits on a printed page. Copy them into your own document, adapt them
to your site, and tick them off.

## The checklists

| Checklist | When | Who |
|---|---|---|
| [First day checklist](first-day-checklist.md) | Your first day with a new account or a new site | Everyone |
| [Before you publish checklist](before-you-publish-checklist.md) | Every time, before **Send for publishing** | Everyone who publishes |
| [Monthly checklist for site owners](monthly-checklist-for-site-owners.md) | Once a month, about 30 minutes | The owner or manager |
| [Handover checklist when someone leaves](handover-checklist-when-someone-leaves.md) | When a person leaves the team | The owner and the administrator |
| [Accessibility checklist in plain words](accessibility-checklist-in-plain-words.md) | For every new page, and once a quarter for the site | Editors and the owner |
