# Before you publish checklist

Two minutes before **Send for publishing** save an hour of corrections afterwards.

## The text

- [ ] The **title** is short and says what the page is about.
- [ ] The **intro** makes sense on its own (lists often show only the intro).
- [ ] **Facts** are right: dates, times, prices, phone numbers, names.
- [ ] **Spelling** checked; ideally someone else read it.
- [ ] **Headings** use the paragraph styles, not just bold text
      ([Changing a page](../first-steps/changing-a-page.md#working-in-the-text-box)).
- [ ] If the writing assistant helped, I checked every sentence it wrote
      ([Using the writing assistant](../first-steps/using-the-writing-assistant.md#checking-what-it-wrote)).

## Links, pictures and files

- [ ] Every **link** works and its text says where it goes ([Adding a link](../first-steps/adding-a-link.md)).
- [ ] Links to our own pages were chosen with **Browse**.
- [ ] Every **picture** has an alternative text, and we are allowed to use it
      ([Adding a picture](../first-steps/adding-a-picture.md)).
- [ ] **Documents** have a clear title, and say type and size in the link text
      ([Adding a document for download](../first-steps/adding-a-document-for-download.md)).

## Privacy and place

- [ ] No **personal data** that should not be public: no private phone numbers, addresses or form entries.
- [ ] People in photos agreed to be shown.
- [ ] The item is in the **right place** in the tree, and in the **right language**.
- [ ] **Preview** looks right.

## After publishing

- [ ] I opened the page on the **public site** and it looks right.
- [ ] If it is not there yet: draft, pending, or cache? ([Caches explained](../words/caches-explained.md#when-you-see-an-old-page))
- [ ] If the page replaces an old one, I asked about a redirect for the old address.
