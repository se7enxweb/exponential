# Handover checklist when someone leaves

People leave: staff change jobs, volunteers move on, a family member hands the site to someone else. Done well, this
takes an hour. Done badly, a former member keeps access for years, or nobody knows how to renew the domain.

## Before they leave

- [ ] Ask them to **finish or remove their drafts** (**My drafts**), and to list anything half done.
- [ ] Ask them to write down **what they did regularly**: weekly news, answering forms, the newsletter, orders.
- [ ] Find who takes over each of those jobs.
- [ ] Ask which **outside logins** they hold: the domain registrar, the host's customer area, a payment provider, a
      map or AI provider, social media. Each needs a new holder.

## On their last day

- [ ] The administrator **disables** their account. Disabling keeps their name on the versions they made, so the
      history stays readable; deleting an account is rarely needed
      ([How do I disable or unlock a user account?](../../manual/how-do-i-disable-or-unlock-a-user-account.md)).
- [ ] If they had the **Administrator** role, make sure at least one other person has it first.
- [ ] Change any **shared passwords** they knew (there should be none in the site itself; outside services may have
      some).

## After they left

- [ ] Update [Who to call](../when-stuck/who-to-call.md).
- [ ] Check that their regular jobs happen under the new person (the first newsletter, the first form answers).
- [ ] Tell your host or partner if they were a contact person there.

The fuller version for organizations is [Offboarding staff](../../customers/day-to-day/offboarding-staff.md).
