# Monthly checklist for site owners

About thirty minutes once a month. Nothing here needs technical skills: where something technical must happen, you ask
for a result.

## Content

- [ ] The front page is current: no expired events, offers or announcements.
- [ ] Opening hours, contact details and prices are right.
- [ ] Old drafts: ask your team to finish or remove them (**My drafts**).
- [ ] Pending items do not wait for weeks (**My pending items**).
- [ ] Seasonal pages are hidden or unhidden as needed ([Hiding and removing](../first-steps/hiding-and-removing.md)).

## Visitors

- [ ] Form submissions were read and answered
      ([How do I see form submissions?](../../manual/how-do-i-see-form-submissions.md)).
- [ ] If you have a shop: orders are processed and nothing is stuck
      ([Daily store routine](../../customers/commerce/daily-store-routine.md)).
- [ ] Broken links were checked, or someone was asked to check them
      ([How do I find and fix broken links?](../../manual/how-do-i-find-and-fix-broken-links.md)).

## People

- [ ] Everyone with an account still needs it; people who left are disabled
      ([Handover checklist when someone leaves](handover-checklist-when-someone-leaves.md)).
- [ ] Nobody shares an account.
- [ ] Two-step sign-in is on for everyone who can publish.

## Ask your provider for

- [ ] **Updates**: the current Exponential version, and whether a newer release or a security fix is waiting
      ([How updates work](../../customers/healthy/how-updates-work.md)).
- [ ] **Backups**: that they ran this month, and the date of the last restore test
      ([Backups](../../customers/healthy/backups.md)).
- [ ] **Incidents**: any outages, errors or security events this month.

Write the answers down. Three months of notes tell you more about your provider than any brochure.

## Once a year (pick a fixed month)

- [ ] Review [Who to call](../when-stuck/who-to-call.md).
- [ ] Check the domain renewal date ([Domain name and e-mail](../hosting-choices/domain-name-and-email.md)).
- [ ] Review your provider: did they keep their promises? ([Reviewing your provider each year](../../customers/partners-and-hosts/reviewing-your-provider-each-year.md))
- [ ] Run the [accessibility checklist](accessibility-checklist-in-plain-words.md) on the main pages.
- [ ] Remove content nobody needs any more ([Content audits](../../customers/day-to-day/content-audits.md)).
