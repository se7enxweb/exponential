# First day checklist

About one hour. Do it in order.

## Accounts and addresses

- [ ] I have the address of the **public site**, bookmarked.
- [ ] I have the address of the **administration** or the **Editor**, bookmarked
      ([The site and the administration](../words/the-site-and-the-administration.md)).
- [ ] I signed in with my **own** account, not a shared one
      ([Signing in for the first time](../first-steps/signing-in-for-the-first-time.md)).
- [ ] I changed the temporary password to my own, stored in a password manager.
- [ ] I turned on **two-step sign-in**, or I know my site does not offer it
      ([Your password and two-step sign-in](../first-steps/your-password-and-two-step-sign-in.md)).

## A look around

- [ ] I found **My drafts** and **My pending items** on the dashboard.
- [ ] I opened **Content structure** and found the part of the site I will work on.
- [ ] I opened **Media** and found where pictures and files go.
- [ ] I found **Help** and searched for "publish"
      ([Finding your way around](../first-steps/finding-your-way-around.md)).

## Practice (on a test page, if possible)

- [ ] I asked where I may practice.
- [ ] I created a test article, published it and looked at it on the site
      ([Adding a news item](../first-steps/adding-a-news-item.md)).
- [ ] I changed it and published again ([Changing a page](../first-steps/changing-a-page.md)).
- [ ] I went back to the first version ([Undoing a mistake](../first-steps/undoing-a-mistake.md)).
- [ ] I removed it, found it in the trash, and asked whether to restore or leave it.

## Words and people

- [ ] I read the [glossary](../non-technical-people-glossary.md) once.
- [ ] I know who my **administrator** is, and how to reach them.
- [ ] I know whom to tell when the site is down ([Who to call](../when-stuck/who-to-call.md)).
- [ ] I know whether my changes need **approval**, and who approves.

## Before I go home

- [ ] I stored or discarded every draft I started.
- [ ] I signed out (on a shared computer, always).
