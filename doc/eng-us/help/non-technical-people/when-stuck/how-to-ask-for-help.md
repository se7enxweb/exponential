## Who to ask first

Your **own** administrator, developer, partner or host, in that order for most questions. They know your site.
Keep their contacts on [Who to call](who-to-call.md).

## A message that gets answered

Copy this and fill it in:

```
Subject: [Site name] Problem with <what>, <short description>

What I wanted to do:
What I did, step by step:
1.
2.
3.
What I expected:
What happened instead (exact message, reference ERR-... if shown):
When: <date and time>
Page address: <copied from the address bar>
My browser and device: <for example Firefox on a laptop>
Did it work before? Does it happen every time?
How urgent: <for example: visitors cannot send the contact form>
```

The page [Reading an error message](reading-an-error-message.md#what-to-note) explains each line.

## Never send these

- **Passwords**, also your own. Nobody needs it; an administrator can reset it.
- **Two-step sign-in codes.**
- **Personal data** of visitors, members or customers, for example form entries or orders, unless the person you send
  it to is allowed to see it and the channel is private.
- Screenshots that show any of the above. Blur or crop them first.

## Free help from the community

For "how do I ...?" questions, the Exponential community helps for free: the
[community forums](https://share.exponential.earth/forums), the [Telegram chat](https://t.me/exponentialcms), the
[Discord server](https://discord.gg/zXzGMgfzej) and the [Slack workspace](https://exponentialcms.slack.com/)
([Asking for help](../../when-something-goes-wrong.md#asking-for-help)). Free help depends on the time people give,
so nobody promises an answer time. Be friendly, be precise, and never post private data in a public place.

## Paid support

When you cannot wait, the project's support page recommends paid support from 7x, the company that maintains
Exponential, and partners offer support too ([Getting support](../../project/getting-support.md),
[Partners: start here](../../partners/partners-start-here.md)).

## When the problem is in the software itself

If your administrator finds that the problem is in Exponential itself rather than in your site's setup, they report it
publicly so it is fixed for everyone. The **Report an error** page of the help prepares such a report
([Filing a bug in Exponential](../../when-something-goes-wrong.md#filing-a-bug-in-exponential)). You need a free
GitHub account to report it yourself.

A **security problem** is never reported publicly: send it privately to the address in
[Security problems](../../when-something-goes-wrong.md#security-problems).
