# Who to call

Fill this page in **now**, on a quiet day. In an emergency, nobody remembers which company runs the e-mail.

## Contents

1. [Which problem goes to whom](#which-problem-goes-to-whom)
2. [Your contact sheet](#your-contact-sheet)
3. [Keeping it current](#keeping-it-current)

## Which problem goes to whom

| Problem | Call first | Then |
|---|---|---|
| "How do I ...?" in the administration | This help, then your administrator | The community ([How to ask for help](how-to-ask-for-help.md#free-help-from-the-community)) |
| I cannot sign in | Your administrator | |
| A page shows the wrong thing | Whoever edited it, or your administrator | |
| The design looks broken | Your developer or partner | |
| The whole site is down | Your host | Your developer or partner |
| The padlock is missing, or a certificate warning | Your host | |
| E-mail does not arrive | Your mail provider or host | |
| The domain stopped working | Your registrar (check the renewal) | Your host |
| Something was deleted and is not in the trash | Your host (backups) | Your developer |
| A security problem | Your administrator and host, privately | The project's private security address ([Security problems](../../when-something-goes-wrong.md#security-problems)) |

The owners' version, with contracts in mind, is [When to call whom](../../customers/partners-and-hosts/when-to-call-whom.md).

## Keeping it current

- Check it once a year, and whenever someone leaves or a contract changes.
- Put the review on the [Monthly checklist for site owners](../checklists/monthly-checklist-for-site-owners.md) once
  a year.
- When someone leaves, follow the [Handover checklist](../checklists/handover-checklist-when-someone-leaves.md).
