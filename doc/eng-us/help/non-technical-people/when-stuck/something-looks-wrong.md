# Something looks wrong

Find your situation in the tables below. Most have an easy explanation you can check yourself in a minute.

## Contents

1. [On the public site](#on-the-public-site)
2. [In the administration](#in-the-administration)

## On the public site

| What you see | Likely cause | What you can do |
|---|---|---|
| My change is not there | Not published (still a draft), waiting for approval, or a cache | Check **My drafts** and **My pending items**; then reload with Shift held ([Caches explained](../words/caches-explained.md#when-you-see-an-old-page)) |
| A page has disappeared | Hidden, removed, or moved | Look for it in the administration; check the trash ([Undoing a mistake](../first-steps/undoing-a-mistake.md)) |
| "Page not found" for an old address | The page was moved or removed | Ask your administrator for a redirect ([How do I redirect old addresses?](../../manual/how-do-i-redirect-old-addresses.md)) |
| A picture is missing | Removed, hidden, or never published | Open the picture item in **Media** and check it |
| The menu is in the wrong order | The order of sub items | [How do I change the order of sub items?](../../manual/how-do-i-change-the-order-of-sub-items.md) |
| A translation does not show | That language is not shown on the site yet | Ask your administrator ([Translating a page](../first-steps/translating-a-page.md#if-it-does-not-work)) |
| The design looks broken everywhere | A design or server problem | Not yours to fix: tell your developer or host |

## In the administration

| What you see | Likely cause | What you can do |
|---|---|---|
| A button is grayed out | You may not use it here or now | Point at it to read why ([Roles and permissions explained](../words/roles-and-permissions-explained.md#how-you-notice-your-role)) |
| A tab my colleague has is missing | Your role is different | Ask your administrator, saying what you need to do |
| *Possible edit conflict* | A draft of this item already exists | Continue your draft, or make a new one ([When someone else is editing](../../editing-content.md#when-someone-else-is-editing)) |
| The form will not publish | A required field is empty or wrong | Read the red message at the top and at the field |
| The sign-out button is gray | You are editing | Store or discard your draft first |
| Search does not find a new item | The search index updates a few minutes later | Use the Content finder meanwhile ([Finding something](../first-steps/finding-something.md#if-you-cannot-find-it)) |
| A yellow **Demo mode** banner on our real site | A setting on the server that must be removed | Tell your host at once ([Demo mode](../../demo-mode.md)) |

## The whole site is down

1. **Check from another device or network**, for example your phone without Wi-Fi. If it works there, the problem is
   your own connection.
2. **Check the address**: is it typed correctly, with `https://`?
3. **Did something just change?** An update, a move, the domain renewal date?
4. **Call your host** with the time you noticed and what you see. The order of calls is on
   [Who to call](who-to-call.md), and the owner's playbook is [When the site is down](../../customers/healthy/when-the-site-is-down.md).

Do not try to fix a server yourself; you could make it harder to repair.

## Still stuck?

Note what you see, then [ask for help](how-to-ask-for-help.md). The more precise your notes, the faster the fix.
