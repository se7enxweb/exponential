# What Exponential is, in plain words

If somebody told you "your site runs on Exponential" and you nodded without knowing what it meant, this page is for
you. It takes about five minutes to read.

## The short answer

Exponential is a **content management system**: a program that runs on a server, shows your website to visitors, and
lets you and your team change that website from a web browser without touching any code. The project describes itself
as "a professional PHP application framework with advanced CMS (content management system) functionality" (the file
`README.md` in the installation). You do not need to know what PHP is: it is the programming language Exponential is
written in, and your host takes care of it.

## What it does for you

| You want to | Exponential gives you |
|---|---|
| Change text and pictures yourself | An edit form in the browser for every page ([Changing a page](first-steps/changing-a-page.md)) |
| Avoid publishing half-finished work | Drafts that only you see until you publish ([Drafts, versions and publishing explained](words/drafts-versions-and-publishing-explained.md)) |
| Undo a mistake | Earlier versions and a trash ([Undoing a mistake](first-steps/undoing-a-mistake.md)) |
| Let several people help, safely | Accounts, roles and approvals ([Roles and permissions explained](words/roles-and-permissions-explained.md)) |
| Arrange a page visually | Exponential Layouts, a page builder with blocks, where your site uses it ([Making a page with Layouts](first-steps/making-a-page-with-layouts.md)) |
| Offer the site in more languages | Translations of every page ([Translating a page](first-steps/translating-a-page.md)) |
| Run a small shop | A built-in shop with products, a basket and orders ([Online store start here](../customers/types/online-store-start-here.md)) |
| Send a newsletter | The newsletter extension, where it is installed ([Newsletters](../customers/growth/newsletters.md)) |
| Get help writing | The optional writing assistant, Exponential Intelligence, which writes drafts only ([Using the writing assistant](first-steps/using-the-writing-assistant.md)) |

Which of these your own site has depends on how it was set up. If you do not see a feature described here, ask the
person who looks after your site; many features are add-ons (**extensions**) that can be switched on
([Extensions explained](words/extensions-explained.md)).

## What it does not do

Being honest about this saves you money and disappointment:

- **It does not register your domain name or run your e-mail.** You buy a domain from a registrar, and e-mail usually
  comes from your host or a mail provider ([Domain name and e-mail](hosting-choices/domain-name-and-email.md)).
- **It does not host itself.** It needs a server. A hosting company provides one ([Three ways to get a site](hosting-choices/three-ways-to-get-a-site.md)).
- **It does not install itself from a button.** Installation normally uses a command line and the tool
  Composer; the setup wizard in the browser finishes an installation, but getting the files onto a suitable server
  comes first ([Requirements at a glance](../hosting/basics/requirements-at-a-glance.md)). A host, developer or
  partner does it.
- **It has no appointment booking, table reservation or ticketing module.** Such things come from an outside service
  that your site links to or embeds. The same goes for a dedicated donation module
  ([Non-profits: donations](../businesses/nonprofits/donations-and-fundraising-pages.md)).
- **It does not design your site for you.** It ships with ready-made designs (themes) that a site builder chooses and
  adapts ([Choosing a design or theme](../creators/website-creators/choosing-a-design-or-theme.md)).

## Who makes it

Exponential has a long history: its copyright notice runs from 1998 to today. It was once known under another name,
was discontinued by its original maker, and has been carried on since then by the company **7x**, with the
**Exponential Foundation**, a nonprofit, operating the project ([Who is 7x?](../who-is-7x.md),
[Who is the Exponential Foundation?](../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md)). A community of developers, designers
and hosting companies works with it worldwide.

## What it costs

The software is **free software**: "Using Exponential under the terms of the GNU GPL is free of charge" (`README.md`,
"License"). There is no license fee, per user, per site or per year. What you do pay for:

| Cost | Paid to | Typical form |
|---|---|---|
| Hosting | A hosting company | A monthly or yearly fee |
| Domain name | A domain registrar | A yearly fee |
| Building or adapting the site | A developer, designer or partner | A project price or hourly rate |
| Looking after it: updates, backups, help | Your host, a developer or a partner | A maintenance agreement |
| Your own time | You | Hours per month |

This help names no prices: they depend on who you hire. [Questions about cost](hosting-choices/questions-about-cost.md)
helps you compare offers.

## What you need around it

1. **A host** that runs the site ([What a host does for you](hosting-choices/what-a-host-does-for-you.md)).
2. **Somebody technical you can call**: the host, a developer or a partner ([Who to call](when-stuck/who-to-call.md)).
3. **An account for each person** who changes the site, with two-step sign-in
   ([Your password and two-step sign-in](first-steps/your-password-and-two-step-sign-in.md)).
4. **A short routine** to keep it healthy ([Monthly checklist for site owners](checklists/monthly-checklist-for-site-owners.md)).
