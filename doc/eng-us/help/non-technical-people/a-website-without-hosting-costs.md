# A website without hosting costs

Normally a website lives on a rented computer, called a server, that runs all day and costs money every month.
Exponential can do it differently: it makes a copy of your website as simple pages and puts that copy on GitHub
Pages, a service that shows such pages to the world for free.

## What you'll have at the end

- Your website online, with an address people can visit.
- No monthly bill for showing it.
- The same way of writing and changing pages as before.

## How it works, in three steps

1. **You write** your pages in Exponential, as usual.
2. **You press Build**: Exponential makes a copy of the website as simple pages.
3. **You press Publish**: the copy goes to GitHub Pages, and visitors see it.

If you change your mind, you can go back to an earlier copy with one click.

## What someone technical sets up once

- A free account on GitHub, the company that hosts the copy.
- A "target" in Exponential that says which website goes where. A wizard asks the questions.
- If you want your own address like `www.my-name.example`, a setting at the place where you bought the name.

After that, you do not need them for daily work.

## What such a site cannot do

- Take payments or run a shop.
- Have pages only members can see.
- Let visitors leave comments.

A contact form can still send you messages in some setups. If you need these things, ask whether
[static is right for your site](../static-publishing/is-static-right-for-my-site.md).

## Words you may hear

- **Static site**: a website made only of ready pages, nothing is computed when someone visits.
- **GitHub Pages**: the free service that shows the pages.
- **Publish**: send the newest copy online.

More: [What static publishing is](../static-publishing/what-static-publishing-is.md) and the
