# Reading path for non-technical people

Start with the shared part, then follow your track. You do not have to read everything: stop when you know enough
for your job.

## Contents

1. [Everybody: the words](#everybody-the-words)
2. [Track A: you edit the site](#track-a-you-edit-the-site)
3. [Track B: you own or pay for the site](#track-b-you-own-or-pay-for-the-site)
4. [Track C: you do not have a site yet](#track-c-you-do-not-have-a-site-yet)
5. [Whenever you are stuck](#whenever-you-are-stuck)

## Everybody: the words

About 45 minutes.

1. [Non-technical people: start here](non-technical-people-start-here.md)
2. [Glossary for non-technical people](non-technical-people-glossary.md)
3. [What Exponential is, in plain words](what-exponential-is-in-plain-words.md)
4. [Content items and the tree](words/content-items-and-the-tree.md)
5. [Drafts, versions and publishing explained](words/drafts-versions-and-publishing-explained.md)
6. [The site and the administration](words/the-site-and-the-administration.md)

## Track A: you edit the site

About two hours, spread over a week.

1. [Signing in for the first time](first-steps/signing-in-for-the-first-time.md) and the [First day checklist](checklists/first-day-checklist.md)
2. [Finding your way around](first-steps/finding-your-way-around.md)
3. [Changing a page](first-steps/changing-a-page.md)
4. [Undoing a mistake](first-steps/undoing-a-mistake.md)
5. [Adding a news item](first-steps/adding-a-news-item.md)
6. [Adding a picture](first-steps/adding-a-picture.md) and [Adding a document for download](first-steps/adding-a-document-for-download.md)
7. [Adding a link](first-steps/adding-a-link.md)
8. [Finding something](first-steps/finding-something.md)
9. [Hiding and removing](first-steps/hiding-and-removing.md)
10. [Your password and two-step sign-in](first-steps/your-password-and-two-step-sign-in.md)
11. [Before you publish checklist](checklists/before-you-publish-checklist.md) and [Accessibility checklist in plain words](checklists/accessibility-checklist-in-plain-words.md)
12. When your site has them: [Making a page with Layouts](first-steps/making-a-page-with-layouts.md),
    [Translating a page](first-steps/translating-a-page.md), [Using the writing assistant](first-steps/using-the-writing-assistant.md)
13. [Roles and permissions explained](words/roles-and-permissions-explained.md) and [Caches explained](words/caches-explained.md)

Then continue with the [User manual](../manual/README.md) when a new question comes up.

## Track B: you own or pay for the site

About ninety minutes.

1. [What you never have to do](what-you-never-have-to-do.md)
2. [Hosting, domains and HTTPS explained](words/hosting-domains-and-https-explained.md)
3. [Extensions explained](words/extensions-explained.md)
4. [Roles and permissions explained](words/roles-and-permissions-explained.md)
5. [What a host does for you](hosting-choices/what-a-host-does-for-you.md)
6. [Domain name and e-mail](hosting-choices/domain-name-and-email.md)
7. [Who to call](when-stuck/who-to-call.md): fill it in
8. [Monthly checklist for site owners](checklists/monthly-checklist-for-site-owners.md)
9. [Handover checklist when someone leaves](checklists/handover-checklist-when-someone-leaves.md)

Then continue with the [Customers](../customers/customers-start-here.md) section for owners.

## Track C: you do not have a site yet

About an hour, then conversations with providers.

1. [What Exponential is, in plain words](what-exponential-is-in-plain-words.md)
2. [Three ways to get a site](hosting-choices/three-ways-to-get-a-site.md)
3. [Questions about cost](hosting-choices/questions-about-cost.md)
4. [What to ask before you sign](hosting-choices/what-to-ask-before-you-sign.md)
5. [Domain name and e-mail](hosting-choices/domain-name-and-email.md)
6. Then the section for your kind of site: [Individuals](../individuals/individuals-start-here.md),
   [Local businesses](../local-businesses/local-businesses-start-here.md) or [Non-profits](../non-profits/non-profits-start-here.md).

Moving an existing site? Read [Moving to a new host](hosting-choices/moving-to-a-new-host.md).
