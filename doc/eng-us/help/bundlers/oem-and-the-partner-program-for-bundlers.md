# OEM and the partner program for bundlers

This page is for bundlers who sell their bundle as a product and want to know what the Exponential Partner Program
offers them. Every fact is taken from the partner pages; where they are silent, this page says "not published; ask
the partner team".

## OEM resale

"OEM options are also available to resell your own products based on Exponential", and "OEM Resale Opportunities" is
a benefit at every level (<https://exponential.earth/partner/benefits>, read 18 October 2026, quoted on
[OEM and reselling](../partners/oem-and-reselling.md)). The OEM benefit "is a program promise; its details are not
published". Ask the partner team.

The product shapes the partner pages name are a packaged solution, a hosted product, an appliance or image, and an
extension bundle; for an appliance or image "Distribution: GPL obligations apply".

## The Distribution profile

"Distribution Partners: Extend the Exponential ecosystem by distributing and reselling Exponential software or
certifications (e.g., distributors, resellers, and training centers)" ([Distribution partners and resellers](../partners/distribution-partners.md)).
Most bundlers who distribute their bundle to customers fit this profile; agencies shipping starter kits to their own
projects may fit the IT profile ([Partner profiles](../partners/partner-profiles.md)).

## Levels and prices

The levels are Student, Now, Starter, Basic, Professional, Silver, Gold and Platinum. Prices per month, tickets,
response times, revenue requirements and discounts are on [Partner plans and prices](../partners/partner-plans-and-prices.md),
taken from <https://exponential.earth/partner/benefits>. Two examples quoted from that page: Student $25 and
Platinum $2,600 per month; Professional and above have a revenue requirement per quarter (Professional $1,800,
Silver $3,000, Gold $4,500, Platinum $7,500). The store and the live pages are authoritative.

## Leads and commission

"The program pays a lead referral commission on the total contract value": **10%** for a cold lead, **20%** for a
hot lead, "customer ready to purchase 7x" ([Referrals and leads](../partners/referrals-and-leads.md#leads-from-partners-to-7x)).
A bundler who meets customers that need more than the bundle (development, hosting, support from 7x) can refer them.

## Joining

1. Apply at <https://exponential.earth/partner/apply>.
2. **Pay first**: buy the partner subscription in the 7x store, <https://my.se7enx.com/store/exponential-partners>.
3. **Then sign**: sign the partnership agreement and send it to <partners@exponential.earth>.
4. Activation follows.

Source: [How to apply](../partners/how-to-apply.md#the-steps). A partner website and dashboard are planned for
mid-2027 ([Partner plans and prices](../partners/partner-plans-and-prices.md#the-partner-website-and-dashboard)).

## Questions to ask about your bundle

Write to <partners@exponential.earth>, or use <https://exponential.earth/partner/contact-us>:

- [ ] May the bundle carry "Exponential" in its name or description, and how?
- [ ] Does 7x list or promote OEM products and bundles?
- [ ] Do bundle sales count as revenue for your level?
- [ ] Who supports the Exponential core inside your bundle, and through which channel?
- [ ] Which logos may the bundle's listing and packaging use?

None of these answers is published; ask the partner team.
