# Naming and branding a bundle

This page is for bundlers who write the name, listing text and documentation of their bundle. The partner's rule set
is [Naming and brand rules for partners](../partners/naming-and-brand-for-partners.md); the sales team's is
[Brand and naming rules](../business/brand-and-naming-rules.md). This page applies them to bundles.

## The names to use

| Write | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Exponential** | the product, the CMS and framework |
| **Exponential 6**, **Exponential 6.0.x** | the current major line |
| **7x** | the company that leads development, support and hosting |
| **the Exponential Foundation** | the nonprofit that operates the project |
| **Exponential Platform**, **Legacy**, **Nexus**, **DXP**, **Basic** | the product family |
| **Exponential Velocity** | the web and application server |

Source: [Naming and brand rules for partners](../partners/naming-and-brand-for-partners.md#the-names).

## Naming your bundle

| Do | Do not |
|---|---|
| Give the bundle a name of your own and say it is "based on Exponential" | call the bundle "Exponential" or "Exponential 6" as if it were the project's release |
| "Example Hosting's Exponential image", "Example Intranet Kit, based on Exponential 6" | "Official Exponential image", "Exponential Enterprise" (an invented product name) |
| Name the Exponential version inside | hide which version is inside |
| "Exponential Partner (Silver)" if you are one | "Certified" without a certification; "Exponential reseller" unless you have the Distribution profile |

Whether a product may carry "Exponential" in its name, and how, is a question to agree with 7x before you publish:
"Whether the product may carry 'Exponential' in its name or description, and how" is the first item on the list in
[OEM and reselling](../partners/oem-and-reselling.md#what-to-agree-with-7x). Ask the partner team:
<partners@exponential.earth>.

Logos: see [Using partner logos and the certified mark](../partners/partner-logos-and-marks.md). Where a published
rule is missing, write "not published; ask the partner team" in your own notes and ask.

## Naming your code

"`sevenx` is 7x's official public PHP code prefix ... `exp` is used with intent to assign an extension or solution to
the Exponential project and the Exponential Foundation"; partners must not use `exp` on their own extensions
([Naming prefixes](../../standards/naming-prefixes-sevenx-and-exp.md)). Give your extensions, classes and site
packages a prefix of your own company.

## The old name

The product was renamed on 11 August 2025. Use the old name only for history, in the past tense, never to sell the
bundle. The project states that it is not associated with the original software or its original developer, or with
Ibexa; do not suggest otherwise (<https://exponential.earth/about/project>, quoted on
[Naming and brand rules for partners](../partners/naming-and-brand-for-partners.md#the-old-name)).
Class names such as `eZContentObject` are identifiers in the code and stay as they are.

## Credits on bundled sites

A bundle may suggest a "Powered by Exponential" credit, but the site belongs to the recipient: "Only with the
customer's consent", "Honest wording", "Link to the project, <https://exponential.earth/>"
([Showing "Powered by Exponential"](../partners/powered-by-exponential.md)). Make it easy to remove; never hide it in
a way the recipient cannot change.

## Ready-to-use phrases

```text
<Bundle> is based on Exponential <version>, the open source content management system led by 7x and
operated by the Exponential Foundation. Exponential is free software under the GNU General Public
License, version 2 or later. <Bundle> is made by <your company>, which is responsible for its support.
```

```text
<Bundle> is not an official release of the Exponential project. Exponential releases are published at
https://github.com/se7enxweb/exponential.
```
