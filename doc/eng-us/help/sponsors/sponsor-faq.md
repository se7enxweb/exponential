# Sponsor FAQ

Values as listed on 18 October 2026; the live pages are authoritative.

**Where do I sponsor?**
7x: <https://github.com/sponsors/se7enxweb>. The Exponential Foundation: <https://github.com/sponsors/exponentialcms>.
[Two organizations, two sponsor pages](two-organizations-two-sponsor-pages.md)

**What is the smallest amount?**
The lowest listed tiers are $2 a month and $5 one time (7x) and $5 a month (the Foundation).
[Sponsoring 7x](sponsoring-7x.md), [Sponsoring the Foundation](sponsoring-the-foundation.md)

**Monthly or one time?**
Monthly, if you can: it lets the organizations plan maintenance. One-time tiers suit a specific need (consulting,
pair programming, a bounty).

**Can I sponsor without being listed?**
Yes: "You can choose whether to display your sponsorship publicly" (GitHub Docs). [How sponsors are recognized](sponsor-recognition.md)

**Are there fees?**
GitHub charges no fee on sponsorships from personal accounts and "up to 6%" on sponsorships from organization
accounts (GitHub Docs). [Billing, receipts and tax](billing-receipts-and-tax.md)

**Can my company pay by invoice?**
"enterprises and organizations may opt to be billed by recurring invoice" (GitHub Docs). [Sponsoring as a company](sponsoring-as-a-company.md)

**Is it tax deductible?**
The Foundation states that donations to it are tax deductible in the United States. Ask your adviser; 7x is a company.
[Billing, receipts and tax](billing-receipts-and-tax.md)

**Can I change or cancel?**
Yes, through GitHub's sponsorship settings; "If the sponsored account retires your tier, the tier will remain in place
for you until you choose a different tier or cancel your subscription" (GitHub Docs).

**Does sponsoring guarantee a feature?**
No. Tiers raise priority; guaranteed work needs a written agreement.
[Feature requests and sponsored features](../partners/feature-requests-and-sponsored-features.md)

**I do not use GitHub.**
[Other ways to give money](other-ways-to-give.md)

**Who already sponsors?**
7x is the first sponsor ([7x, the first sponsor](7x-the-first-sponsor.md)). This help names no other sponsors.
