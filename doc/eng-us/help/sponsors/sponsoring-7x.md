# Sponsoring 7x on GitHub

This page is for people and organizations who want to sponsor 7x, the company that leads the development of
Exponential. The page is <https://github.com/sponsors/se7enxweb>. Everything below was read through the GitHub API on
18 October 2026; tiers can change, and the live page is authoritative.

## What 7x offers sponsors

The listing's section "What we offer to sponsors like you" names: mentions of sponsors on X.com, Instagram, the
community portal and LinkedIn; installation support; general maintenance; troubleshooting support; advanced
development support; joining "the product backlog team and get your ideas turned into real software"; and help
releasing and supporting your own custom solutions.

> **Note**
> Some tier texts on GitHub still use the product's former name. This page describes them with the current name,
> Exponential.

## The tiers

### Monthly

| Tier | What the listing says it includes (summarized) |
|---|---|
| $2 a month | A Sponsor badge on your GitHub profile |
| $25 a month | A Sponsor badge on your profile |
| $30 a month | Join the support chat on Telegram; your feature ideas added to 7x's product backlog "to be considered for development with greater priority and schedule"; access to chat, videos, screencasts and tutorials |
| $99 a month | Sponsor the next release: your organization's information and marketing material in the official release announcement and related documentation |
| $100 a month | Your bug reports given special attention and priority |
| $125 a month | "Best Package Offering": the rewards of the $25, $30 and $100 tiers |
| $250 a month | Your logo or name in the project README; a community chat space |
| $750 a month | A bounty for the development of a feature tailored to your needs |
| $1,000 a month | 7x joins your company chat for direct help and support |
| $2,600 a month | A premium maintenance and support package: prioritized maintenance, updates, security patches, optimizations, a dedicated support team |

### One time

| Tier | Includes |
|---|---|
| $5 one time | Support of any size ("Lowball support") |
| $10 one time | A shoutout on social media |
| $200 one time | One hour of pair programming |
| $350 one time | One hour of consulting or mentorship |
| $1,000 one time | One bug or a medium-sized bounty |
| $5,000 one time | A large contract project, on contact |

## Choosing a tier

| You are | A fitting start |
|---|---|
| An individual who uses Exponential | $2 to $25 a month |
| A freelancer or small agency | $30 or $125 a month (backlog, chat, bug priority) |
| A company that relies on Exponential | $250 to $1,000 a month, or $2,600 for a maintenance package |
| Someone with one specific need | A one-time tier: pair programming, consulting or a bounty |

## What tiers promise and do not promise

Backlog and bug tiers raise priority; the listing says features are "considered" with greater priority. A guaranteed
delivery needs a written agreement with 7x ([Feature requests and sponsored features](../partners/feature-requests-and-sponsored-features.md)).
"GitHub is not responsible for how developers represent themselves nor does GitHub endorse any sponsored open source
projects" (GitHub Docs); the tiers are 7x's own offer.
