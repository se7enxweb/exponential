# 7x, the first sponsor

This page is for sponsors who want to know who has carried Exponential so far, and why more sponsors are needed.

## The first sponsor

7x is the first sponsor of the Exponential Project and of the Exponential Foundation (7x's account, given by the
owner of 7x on 18 October 2026, for this help). The public sources say the same in their own words:

| Statement | Source (read 18 October 2026) |
|---|---|
| "The Exponential Open Source Project is funded by donations of time and resources by 7x (and Exponential Sponsors) which sponsors the Project for the foreseeable future." | <https://exponential.earth/about/project> |
| 7x is "the current leader in contributions both financial, code, extension development, new features, infrastructure, maintenance and much much more back to the project and to the end users of the Exponential Community, greater Exponential Ecosystem, PHP Community and Symfony Community." | <https://foundation.exponential.earth/supporters> |
| "7x builds their core products on top of Zeta Components and contributes to the project financially." | <https://exponential.earth/about/project> |
| "7x took over leadership of the project and it's development, support, adoption and community growth in 2023" | README |

## What 7x has carried

The story is told in full on [Who is 7x?](../who-is-7x.md). In short: 7x, formerly Brookins Consulting, kept the code
base alive after its original vendor ended it, published the 6.0.x releases from 1 January 2024, renamed the product
Exponential in August 2025, and helped found the Exponential Foundation in March 2026
([Who is the Exponential Foundation?](../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#timeline)).

## Why more sponsors are needed

One company carrying a project is a risk for everyone who uses it. More sponsors mean:

- maintenance that does not depend on one organization's finances;
- more features, documentation, extensions and themes than one company can fund;
- a Foundation that can plan.

7x's own sponsor page puts it plainly: "We work very hard for our free software projects that do not generate direct
income" (<https://sponsor.se7enx.com/>).
