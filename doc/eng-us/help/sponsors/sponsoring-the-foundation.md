# Sponsoring the Exponential Foundation

This page is for people and organizations who want to fund the nonprofit that operates the Exponential project. Its
GitHub Sponsors page is <https://github.com/sponsors/exponentialcms> (the GitHub organization "Exponential", website
<https://exponential.earth>). Everything below was read through the GitHub API on 18 October 2026; the live page is
authoritative.

## What the Foundation focuses on

"Support the Exponential Foundation's open source work to grow the Exponential (CMS and Family of CMSs) Projects. We
are focused on the development of Features, Documentation, Tutorials, How-To's, FAQs, Source Code,
Plugins/Extensions/Bundles." (GitHub Sponsors listing)

Its mission: "dedicated directly to the continued Funding, Adoption, Support, Direction, Education, Development,
Promotion and Documentation for the Exponential Enterprise Open Source Project" (<https://foundation.exponential.earth/>).

## The tiers

### Monthly

| Tier | Includes (as listed) |
|---|---|
| $5 a month | A Sponsor badge on your profile |
| $10 a month | Weekly newsletter updates |
| $25 a month | Logo or name in the project README; join the community chat space |
| $30 a month | Access to videos, screencasts and tutorials |
| $100 a month | Logo or name on the project website; bug reports prioritized |
| $1,000 a month | The Foundation joins your company chat for help and support |
| $8,000 a month | Joins your company project for help and support |
| $12,000 a month | Develops and supports a new product for your company |

### One time

| Tier | Includes (as listed) |
|---|---|
| $10 one time | A shoutout on social media |
| $50 one time | A mention in the release notes |
| $200 one time | One hour of pair programming |
| $350 one time | One hour of consulting or mentorship |
| $500 one time | A workshop for your team |
| $1,000 one time | One bug or a medium-sized bounty |
| $2,000 one time | A talk at your conference |
| $5,000 one time | A small contract project, on contact |
| $10,000 one time | A medium contract project, on contact |

## Other ways to give to the Foundation

"Support the Exponential Foundation with a sustaining subscription or one time donation. Funds donated are tax free
and tax deductible on the donner's taxes in the United States." (<https://foundation.exponential.earth/>). The page
links to GitHub Sponsors and to PayPal. Check with your own tax adviser how the statement applies to you
([Billing, receipts and tax](billing-receipts-and-tax.md)).

## Choosing a tier

| You are | A fitting start |
|---|---|
| An individual | $5 to $30 a month |
| A company that wants to be seen supporting the project | $25 (README) or $100 a month (website) |
| A team that wants hands-on help | One-time workshop, consulting or pair programming |
