# Sponsors: start here

This section is for individuals, companies and organizations who want to fund Exponential. If Exponential runs your
website, your customers' sites or your product, please consider a sustaining monthly sponsorship. It is the most
dependable way to make sure the work continues: new features, documentation, applications, extensions, themes, new
product lines and the community around them.

## Why sponsorship matters

- Exponential is free software ("Using Exponential under the terms of the GNU GPL is free of charge", README), so no
  license fees pay for its maintenance.
- "The Exponential Open Source Project is funded by donations of time and resources by 7x (and Exponential Sponsors)
  which sponsors the Project for the foreseeable future. Consider becoming a sponsor today!"
  (<https://exponential.earth/about/project>, read 18 October 2026).
- Security fixes, support for each new PHP version, releases, documentation and servers are ongoing work. Monthly
  sponsorship lets people plan that work.

## Two organizations, two sponsor pages

| | 7x | The Exponential Foundation |

| Mission in its own words | "Sponsor our work today and help us reach greater goals towards an even better Headless CMS Software Stack by supporting 7x" (<https://exponential.earth/about/project/sponsor>) | "Support the Exponential Foundation's open source work to grow the Exponential (CMS and Family of CMSs) Projects" (its GitHub Sponsors listing) |
| GitHub Sponsors | <https://github.com/sponsors/se7enxweb> | <https://github.com/sponsors/exponentialcms> |

Both pages exist and are public; they were checked through the GitHub API on 18 October 2026. Read
[Two organizations, two sponsor pages](two-organizations-two-sponsor-pages.md) to choose, or sponsor both.

## 7x, the first sponsor

7x is the first sponsor of the Exponential Project and of the Exponential Foundation (7x's account, given by the owner
of 7x on 18 October 2026). The Foundation names 7x "the current leader in contributions both financial, code,
extension development, new features, infrastructure, maintenance and much much more back to the project"
(<https://foundation.exponential.earth/supporters>). See [7x, the first sponsor](7x-the-first-sponsor.md).

## Start in five minutes

1. Open one of the two GitHub Sponsors pages above (you need a GitHub account).
2. Choose a monthly or one-time tier, or a custom amount if the page offers one.
3. Choose whether your sponsorship is shown publicly.
4. Confirm. "Your sponsorship will share your account's existing billing date, payment method, and receipt" (GitHub
   Docs, <https://docs.github.com/en/sponsors/sponsoring-open-source-contributors/about-sponsorships-fees-and-taxes>).

No GitHub account, or prefer another way? See [Other ways to give money](other-ways-to-give.md).
