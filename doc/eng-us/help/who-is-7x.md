# Who is 7x?

**7x** is the North American company that maintains, develops, supports and hosts **Exponential**, the open source
content management system you are using right now. This page tells who 7x is, where the software came from, what
7x builds around it today and how you can take part.

The page is long on purpose: it covers a history of nearly thirty years. Every statement names its source, either a
file in this repository (open it in the help or on GitHub) or a public web page. Where two sources disagree, the page
says so instead of choosing one. Where something could not be checked, it is left out.

> **Note**
> 7x's sister organization, the nonprofit that funds and directs the open source project, has its own page:
> [Who is the Exponential Foundation?](who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md)

## In short

- **7x is a company.** It describes itself as "a responsive North American web design, support, development and
  website hosting company" and as "enterprise content management integrators, implementing the Exponential
  enterprise content management system" (<https://se7enx.com/about>).
- **7x leads Exponential.** "7x took over leadership of the project and it's development, support, adoption and
  community growth in 2023" ([README.md](https://github.com/se7enxweb/exponential/blob/main/README.md), section
  "Who is 7x").
- **7x used to be Brookins Consulting.** Brookins Consulting was an eZ Publish partner in the USA
  (<https://se7enx.com/about/exponential-hosting-usa>). 7x grew from it in three stages: the idea in 2014, the start
  of the project in 2022 and its launch in December 2023 ([From Brookins Consulting to 7x](#from-brookins-consulting-to-7x-in-three-stages)).
- **Nearly thirty years.** The copyright notice of Exponential reads "Copyright (C) 1998 - 2026 7x & Exponential
  Foundation" next to "Copyright (C) 1999 - 2014 eZ Systems AS"
  ([COPYRIGHT.md](https://github.com/se7enxweb/exponential/blob/main/COPYRIGHT.md)).
- **One code base, kept alive.** "Exponential has made it beyond it's end of life in 2021 and survived" (README,
  "Exponential Project Status"). Since December 2023 7x has published the 6.0.x releases, from 6.0.0 on
  1 January 2024 to 6.0.14 on 7 June 2026, with 6.0.15 in development ([release overview](../release-overview)).
- **Not eZ Systems, not Ibexa.** "This project is not associated with the original eZ Publish software or its
  original developer, eZ Systems or Ibexa" (README, "Exponential Project Notice : 2025.08.12";
  <https://exponential.earth/about/project>).

### 7x and the Exponential Foundation

The copyright holder named in every file of the kernel is "7x & Exponential Foundation" (or "7x / Exponential
Foundation"): see [COPYRIGHT.md](https://github.com/se7enxweb/exponential/blob/main/COPYRIGHT.md),
[LICENSE](https://github.com/se7enxweb/exponential/blob/main/LICENSE) and the header of almost every PHP file, for
example `autoload.php`. The documentation carries "Copyright (C) 1998 - 2026 7x & Exponential Foundation and the
Exponential contributors" ([The license of this documentation](../README-LICENSE.md)).

The two are not the same organization. 7x is the company; the Exponential Foundation is a nonprofit corporation,
founded on 19 March 2026, which names 7x "the current leader in contributions both financial, code, extension
development, new features, infrastructure, maintenance and much much more back to the project"
(<https://foundation.exponential.earth/>). The Foundation's own page explains the rest:
[Who is the Exponential Foundation?](who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md)

### From Brookins Consulting to 7x, in three stages

Brookins Consulting was founded in 2001 (<https://graham.se7enx.com/7x>). 7x did not appear on one day: according to
7x's own account (the owner's statement of 18 October 2026, for this page), it grew in three stages.

| Stage | Year | What happened | Sources that mark it |

| The idea | 2014 | The initial idea, started quietly: the se7enx.com domain was registered and the fork of the entire ecosystem, every resource of it, was prepared. | 7x's account; "I founded 7x in 2001 as Brookins Consulting (rebranded in 2014)" (<https://graham.se7enx.com/7x>) |
| The start | 2022 | The project started. | 7x's account; "Previously before 2022, 7x was called Brookins Consulting" ([README.md](https://github.com/se7enxweb/exponential/blob/main/README.md)) |

Each of the three public sources names the stage it describes, which is why they give three different years. Through
all three, Brookins Consulting and 7x are the same company, and it has worked with this software since its first
versions.

### Why the product is called Exponential

"At the specific request of Ibexa we are changing this projects name to 'Exponential' or 'Exponential (CMS)'
effective as of August, 11th 2025" (<https://exponential.earth/about/project>). In the repository the rename landed
the next day: the README, `composer.json` and `lib/version.php` changed on 12 August 2025, and one commit renamed the
product in `bin/`, `doc/`, `design/` and the translations (332 files). Class names such as `eZContentObject`, paths
and database tables did not change ([August 2025](../history/2025/2025-08.md)). That is why you still see `eZ` in
the code: it is the prefix of real identifiers, not the product name.

## The eZ Publish years, 1999 to 2021

Exponential did not start in 2023. Its code was first the code of **eZ Publish**, the content management system that
the Norwegian company eZ Systems developed from 1999 (COPYRIGHT.md; <https://se7enx.com/about/exponential-hosting-usa>).
eZ Publish was a product that its creator discontinued after almost eighteen years of service to the world (7x's
account, owner, 18 October 2026); its last releases are listed until 2021 in the release overview. The name belongs
to that past: the software you use today is Exponential.
The history of this repository goes back to 2 September 2002, when the 3.0 line was in development, and contains
every commit since: 27,242 commits on `main` on 18 October 2026 (`git rev-list --count origin/main`).

What the history shows, generation by generation:

- **eZ Publish 1 and 2** (2000 to 2002): the first generation, with eZ Trade for shops. 7x's founder started with it
  in 2000 (<https://graham.se7enx.com/7x>). Brookins Consulting released "eZ Region" as free software for eZ Publish
  2.2, sold eZ Publish 2.2 support to businesses and government agencies, and created eZCommunity.net and the
  eZCommunity2 repositories "for the continued development and improvement of eZ Publish 2.2 + PHP5 support"
  (<https://exponential.earth/about/about-exponential/features-added-by-7x/7x-core-contributions-to-ez-publish-cms-2000-2015>).
  That line lives on as [Exponential Basic](#exponential-basic).
- **eZ Publish 3** (2002 to 2007): introduced the content model you still use in Exponential, with classes, objects, versions, siteaccesses
  and the template language. The [changelogs from 2.9 to 3.10](../../changelogs/README.md) are in the help.
- **eZ Publish 4** (2007 to 2012): the PHP 5 generation, with ezjscore, the online editor and the 4.x
  administration. [Changelogs 4.0 to 4.7](../../changelogs/README.md).
- **eZ Publish 5 and the community builds** (2012 to 2021): the legacy kernel next to the new Symfony stack, the
  monthly community builds, then the 2017.08 to 2019.03 releases until September 2021 (release overview).

During those years, according to 7x's own list, Brookins Consulting gave "Over 18 Years of steady forum support to eZ
Publish Version 3 & 4 & 5 Community Forum Users", released eZ Publish compatible extensions, and mirrored the eZ
Publish repositories, the project forge extensions and the documentation of doc.ez.no for preservation (same source
as above). Twenty of its pull requests were merged into the code base between 2014 and 2023
(`git log --grep="from brookinsconsulting/"`).

The successors of eZ Publish took other roads. "eZ Platform was eZ Publish's technological successor before being
abandoned by Ibexa", and Ibexa DXP OSS is "the rebranded and evolved successor of eZ Platform" (README, "What was
eZ Platform?" and "What is Ibexa DXP OSS?").

## Stewardship: from a dormant code base to Exponential 6

The [history of Exponential](../history/README.md) begins: "This is where the story of Exponential 6 begins: the
month a dormant code base changed hands" ([December 2023](../history/2023/2023-12.md)). In that month the
maintainers:

- merged the first outside change (pull request #40, 11 December 2023);
- rewrote the README to say what the software is, which PHP versions it supports and where to ask for help;
- added `SECURITY.md`, `CONTRIBUTING.md` and funding metadata;
- pointed `composer.json` at the se7enxweb packages, so the software installs with Composer under its new name;
- made the setup wizard work on PHP 8 and stop sending users to the servers of the former vendor;
- published the database update scripts from 5.4 to 6.0 for MySQL and PostgreSQL;
- set 6.0.0beta1 (25 December) and beta2 (31 December).

7x's description of the same moment: it "forked the recently no longer functional core of eZ Publish Project Software
Stack and quickly released a working stable version as eZ Publish 6.0.0 in 2024/01"
(<https://se7enx.com/about/exponential-hosting-usa>).

The [ecosystem overview](../history/ecosystem.md) counts the work since: 6,556 ledger changes in 53 repositories
since December 2023, 1,037 of them made by the se7enxweb team, the rest upstream history that the forks carry along.
Every one of them is listed in [the ledgers](../history/ledger/README.md), and [the coverage page](../history/coverage.md)
shows that none is missing.

## The product: what Exponential is

"Exponential is a professional PHP application framework with advanced CMS (content management system)
functionality. As a CMS its most notable feature is its fully customizable and extendable content model. It is also
suitable as a platform for general PHP development" (README). The project site calls it "an award-winning,
enterprise-grade Open Source Content Management System and development framework with functionality for web
publishing, media portals, intranets, e-commerce and extranets" (<https://exponential.earth/about/project>).

## The platform line: Exponential Platform, Legacy, Nexus and DXP

Next to Exponential 6, 7x maintains the Symfony based line that descends from eZ Platform and Ibexa OSS. "In short
Exponential Platform is the direct continuation of the software stack known as eZ Platform created by eZ Systems and
discontinued by Ibexa" (README, "What is Exponential Platform?"). The descriptions below come from each distribution's
own `composer.json` and repository description (read with `gh api repos/se7enxweb/<name>`), not from the upstream
feature lists.

| Distribution | What it is | Includes Exponential 6? |
|---|---|---|
| **Exponential Platform 3.2.9** (`exponential-platform`) | "CMS / DXP based on the last free versions of eZ Platform. Symfony / New Stack Only." | No |
| **Exponential Platform Legacy** (`exponential-platform-legacy`) | "eZ Platform (+Legacy) distribution": the Symfony platform plus the legacy bridge and `se7enxweb/exponential` | Yes |
| **Exponential Platform Nexus** (`exponential-platform-nexus`) | "Platform v2/v3/v4/v5 + Exponential v6.x (Legacy) + Netgen Media Site + NG Layouts", with a demo site; requires `netgen/layouts-ui` and `se7enxweb/exponential` | Yes |
| **Exponential Platform DXP** (`exponential-platform-dxp`) | "A meta package for installing 7x Exponential Platform DXP": Symfony only, with the admin UI, content forms, REST, GraphQL and GraphiQL, HTTP cache, Solr and core search, rich text, matrix and query field types, notifications, messenger, cron and JWT; of the layouts packages it requires only `se7enxweb/layouts-core` | No |
| **Nexus starter** (`exponential-platform-nexus-starter`) | A ready-to-run Nexus installation built on the DXP package, with Netgen Layouts and the media site data | No |

The DXP does not ship a layouts editor, an approval workflow, a scheduler or the legacy kernel: its requirements
contain none of them. When an existing Exponential site has to move forward step by step, the dual-kernel
distributions Legacy and Nexus run Exponential 6 next to the platform (README, "What Exponential Platform DXP
adds"). The platform repositories, their forks of Symfony, Twig and Doctrine pieces and their histories are mapped on
[The Exponential platform ecosystem](../history/ecosystem.md). Learn more at <https://platform.exponential.earth>.

## Exponential Velocity

Exponential Velocity is the PHP web server that Exponential recommends "for every stage from development to
production" ([Velocity chronicle](../history/velocity/README.md)). "Velocity is a PHP web server for real production
work. It began as a fork of the Qbix web server engine, and the two projects have since drifted far apart" (the
package's README, `se7enxweb/exponential-velocity`). The repository is a fork of `Qbix/webserver` (GitHub API) and
the package is MIT licensed (its `composer.json`).

Its first commits are from 20 July 2026, its first release `v0.0.1` from September 2026, and it became part of
Exponential on 22 September 2026 ([September 2026](../history/2026/2026-09b.md)). What it measures, and how to run
it, is in the README section "Exponential Velocity — the fast option" and on
[The persistent-worker server](../features/6.0/velocity-persistent-worker-server.md).

## Principles you can see in the code

These are not slogans; each is something you can check in this repository.

- **Git first.** "Building websites with Exponential? The recommended way is to get the kernel and every se7enxweb
  extension as Git clones with their full history" (README, "Installation"; [Develop with Git](develop-with-git.md)).
  The full history back to 2002 is kept, and every change since December 2023 is explained in
  [the history](../history/README.md).
- **Published versions never change.** A fix always arrives as the next version (SECURITY.md).
- **Security by default.** Private reporting to security@se7enx.com with a first answer "within one business day"
  (SECURITY.md); a Content Security Policy in the administration; a hardening guide written "secure by default in
  the OpenBSD sense" ([Security hardening](security-hardening.md)).
- **Accessible and readable.** Light and dark themes, right-to-left layouts, keyboard shortcuts, and the rule that no
  administration template carries inline styles ([CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/se7enxweb/exponential/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md);
  [Keyboard shortcuts](keyboard-shortcuts.md)).
- **Current PHP.** "Current releases are primarily aimed at easing the requirements to support current versions of
  the PHP language like PHP 8.2, 8.3, 8.4, 8.5, 8.6 and beyond php9" (README, "Exponential Project Status").
- **Open source, completely.** "Everything we do is GPL-licensed and freely available" (se7enxweb GitHub profile
  README); the documentation is free documentation.
- **Honest numbers.** Performance figures in the README are "reproducible with scripts that ship with the project".
