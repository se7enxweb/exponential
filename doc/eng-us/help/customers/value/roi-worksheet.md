# ROI worksheet

This page is a template for the owner who checks once a year what the site returned. The businesses section has a
worksheet for deciding on a project before it starts
([ROI worksheet template](../../businesses/templates/roi-worksheet-template.md)). This one is for after: it puts what
you planned next to what really happened, so the next year's plan is better. The measures are chosen on
[Measuring ROI](measuring-roi.md), and a filled example is on [ROI example assumptions](roi-example-assumptions.md).

> **Note**
> Template. The worksheet holds no figures of its own: every amount you enter must come from an invoice, a statement,
> the site's own figures or a written judgment, and the source column says which.

## How to use it

1. Copy the worksheet below into your own document or spreadsheet.
2. Fill the **Planned** column from last year's budget or business case.
3. Fill the **Actual** column from the sources named on [Measuring ROI](measuring-roi.md).
4. Do the arithmetic in section D.
5. Write the explanations and decisions in sections E and F.
6. Bring it to the [annual value review](annual-value-review.md).

```text
A4 Updates and upgrades                    ______     ______     partner invoices / own hours
A5 Own staff time on the site (h x rate)   ______     ______     time records
A6 Third-party services (payment fees,     ______     ______     statements

B3 Staff time saved (h x rate x realiz.)   ______     ______     survey / time sample
B4 Costs no longer paid                    ______     ______     canceled contracts
B5 Other measurable benefit: ______        ______     ______     ______

C. MEASURES (no money, but tracked)        Planned    Actual     Source

D. RESULT                                  Planned    Actual
D1 Net benefit = B - A                     ______     ______

E. WHY ACTUAL DIFFERS FROM PLANNED (one line per large difference)
E1 ____________________________________________________________

F1 Keep: ______________________________________________________

Checked by (someone who redid the arithmetic): ______    Date: ______
```

## Where the actual figures come from

| Line | Source | Read |
|---|---|---|
| A2 to A4, A7 | Invoices of host and partner | [Cost of ownership for owners](cost-of-ownership-for-owners.md) |
| A5, B3 | Your time records, or a two-week time sample | [Measuring ROI](measuring-roi.md) |
| A6 | Payment provider and service statements; the Exponential Intelligence dashboard for AI costs (`README.md`) | [Cost of ownership for owners](cost-of-ownership-for-owners.md) |
| B1 | Bookkeeping, with the store's product statistics | [Store reports](../commerce/store-reports.md) |
| B2 | **Setup > Collected information**, newsletter administration | [Collected information](../../../guides/collected-information.md) |

## Checking the worksheet

- [ ] Every Actual figure has a source in the last column.
- [ ] Judgments (new share of sales, realization of time saved) are the same as last year, or the change is explained.
- [ ] Test orders and test submissions are not counted.
- [ ] Someone outside the project redid the arithmetic in section D.
- [ ] Section F has at least one concrete decision with an owner.
