# Checklists for owners

This folder holds short checklists for the people who own and run a site on Exponential. They are written for the
owner's side: what to look at, what to ask, who on the team does it. The technical work behind many items belongs to
your administrator, host or partner; each checklist links to the pages that explain it.

## The checklists

| Checklist | Time it takes | Usually done by |
|---|---|---|
| [Daily](daily.md) | 10 minutes | The person who looks after the site that day |
| [Weekly](weekly.md) | 30 minutes | Site manager or senior editor |
| [Monthly](monthly.md) | 1 to 2 hours | Owner, with the host or partner |
| [Quarterly](quarterly.md) | Half a day | Owner, with the host or partner |
| [Yearly](yearly.md) | A day, spread over a few weeks | Owner and management |
| [Before a campaign](before-a-campaign.md) | Spread over the weeks before | Marketing and owner |
| [After an incident](after-an-incident.md) | 1 to 2 hours, within a week | Owner, with the host or partner |

The times are suggestions for a small team; measure your own in the first months.

## Using them well

- **Copy and keep.** Each list is text you can paste into your own document or ticket system. Keep the filled
  copies; they show trends and are evidence that the work was done.
- **One name per list.** A checklist that "the team" owns is done by nobody.
- **Shorten them.** Strike items that do not apply to your site, for example the store lines if you do not sell.
- **Ask for the technical parts.** Items such as backups and cron jobs are checked by your host; the list tells you
  what to ask and how to recognize a good answer.

> **Tip**
> Put the daily and weekly lists where the work happens: pinned in the team chat, or printed next to the screen.
