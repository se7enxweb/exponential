# Annual value review

This page is for the owner who wants one clear moment a year to ask: what did the site give us, what did it cost,
and what do we do next? It gives a meeting plan with inputs, an agenda and the decisions to record. The operational
side of the year (updates, backups, security, data protection) has its own template, the
[Annual review checklist](../../businesses/templates/annual-review-checklist.md); hold both on the same day, or the
operational one first.

## Who attends

| Person | Brings |
|---|---|
| Owner (chairs and decides) | The goals of the site, the budget |
| The people who use the site daily (editor in chief, store manager) | What works, what costs time |
| Whoever keeps the books | The cost register and income figures |
| Partner, for part of the meeting | Proposals, the state of the installation |
| Host, optionally | Usage, incidents, capacity |

## What to prepare

- [ ] The [ROI worksheet](roi-worksheet.md), filled with the year's actual figures.
- [ ] The [cost register](cost-of-ownership-for-owners.md) for the year.
- [ ] The [feature tour](feature-tour.md), each feature marked: used, not needed, worth trying.
- [ ] The store's monthly reports, if you sell ([Store reports](../commerce/store-reports.md)).
- [ ] Incidents of the year and what was learned ([After an incident](checklists/after-an-incident.md)).
- [ ] The operational annual review, done or scheduled.
- [ ] The release line: is the site on the newest 6.0.x release? Exponential 6.0 is the maintained line
  (`SECURITY.md`).

## A two-hour agenda

| Time | Topic | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 15 min | Goals: are the reasons we have the site still the same? | Goals for next year |
| 25 min | Results: the ROI worksheet, planned against actual | Agreed result, with its explanations |
| 20 min | Costs: the register, costs that grew, invoices that surprised | Cost actions |
| 20 min | The team's view: what costs time, what is missing | A list of pain points |
| 20 min | The feature tour: what we have and do not use | Up to three features to try |
| 20 min | Proposals from the partner, and next year's budget | Approved projects and budget |

## Decisions to record

```text
ANNUAL VALUE REVIEW - [site] - [year]           Date: ______
Attended: ______________________________________________

Result of the year (from the ROI worksheet): ____________________
Goals for next year:
1. ________________________  2. ________________________

KEEP     (what works and stays as it is):
CHANGE   (what we do differently, with owner and date):
STOP     (what we no longer do or pay for):
TRY      (features from the tour, with owner and date):
INVEST   (projects approved, with budget):

Budget for next year agreed: ______     Next review: ______
Signed: ______________________
```

## Questions that help

- Which task does the team do by hand every week that the software could do? Look at
  [Features customers often miss](features-customers-often-miss.md).
- Which feature did we pay for in the build and do not use?
- Which cost grew without a decision?
- If the site were down for a day, what would it cost us
  ([Cost of downtime worksheet](../../businesses/costs/cost-of-downtime-worksheet.md))?
- Are we still on a supported release, and is next year's update work in the budget
  ([Budgeting for upgrades and maintenance](../../businesses/costs/budgeting-for-upgrades-and-maintenance.md))?

> **Tip**
> Keep the filled review with last year's. Three years side by side show more than any single year.

## After the meeting

- [ ] Decisions sent to everyone who attended, and to the partner and host.
- [ ] Each change and project has an owner and a date in the team calendar.
- [ ] The quarterly checklist picks up the follow-ups ([Quarterly checklist](checklists/quarterly.md)).
