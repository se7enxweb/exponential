# Newsroom reading path

This is the order in which a news desk gets to know its site. It assumes the site is already running and that
someone (your administrator, host or partner) has set up the accounts. Each step names who should read the page.
Tick the steps off as a team; most take less than an hour.

| # | Read | Who | Why |
|---|---|---|---|

| 3 | [Accounts, passwords and two-factor sign-in](../day-to-day/accounts-passwords-and-two-factor.md) | Everyone | Keep the accounts that can publish safe |
| 4 | [Editing content](../../editing-content.md) | Reporters, editors | Create and change stories |

| 7 | [Roles for your team](../day-to-day/roles-for-your-team.md) | Managing editor | Who may publish and who may only write |
| 8 | [When something goes wrong](../../when-something-goes-wrong.md) | Managing editor | The first steps when the site misbehaves |
