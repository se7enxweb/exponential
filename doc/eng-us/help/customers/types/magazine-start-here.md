# Magazines: start here

A magazine publishes less often than a newsroom but with more care per piece: long reads, photo stories, themed
issues, and pages that are designed rather than listed. Its site is also a brand: readers notice when a feature
page looks like every other page. What a magazine needs is an editorial process with clear stages, pages its
editors can lay out themselves, and a calendar that keeps the issues coming.

This page is for the editor, the art director and the person who owns the site for the publisher.

## Organizing an issue

Most magazines find a simple structure works best:

1. **One folder or category per issue or theme**, so the issue has its own address and its own page.
2. **A landing page for the issue**, laid out with Layouts, that presents its pieces in the order the editors want.
3. **Each piece as its own item**, related to the issue page and to earlier pieces on the same subject.
4. **A publication date for the issue**, with the pieces scheduled to appear together.

Your builder or partner decides how this maps onto classes and the content tree when the site is built
([Structuring the content tree](../../creators/website-creators/structuring-the-content-tree.md)). If the structure
does not fit the way you work any more, that is a change request for your partner
([Change requests](../partners-and-hosts/change-requests.md)), not something to work around in every issue.

## Roles in a magazine

- **Writers** create drafts and submit them.
- **Editors** edit, approve and publish, and decide the order of an issue.
- **The art director or layout editor** arranges the landing pages with Layouts and checks the images.
- **The site owner** keeps the calendar, the accounts and the contact with the host or partner.

See [Roles for your team](../day-to-day/roles-for-your-team.md) for how roles and permissions are set up.

> **Tip**
> Layout editors change what readers see first. Give the right to publish layouts to the people who own the look of
> the magazine, and let others prepare drafts of a layout for them to check.

## Rhythm

- **Per issue**: the issue plan in the [content calendar](../day-to-day/content-calendar.md), the landing page
  draft, the approval of every piece, a final look in preview, the scheduled date.
- **Every week**: the [weekly owner routine](../day-to-day/weekly-owner-routine.md).
- **Every quarter**: a [content audit](../day-to-day/content-audits.md) of older issues: are the links still good,
  are the images still allowed to be shown?
