# Reading path for owners of several sites

The order in which the owner and the site managers of a group of sites learn their installation. Follow it next to
the reading path of each site's type. Each step names who should read the page.

## The first week: who owns what

| # | Read | Who | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Owners of several sites: start here](multisite-owner-start-here.md) | Owner, site managers | What is shared and what is per site |
| 2 | [One installation, many sites](../../businesses/multisite/one-installation-many-sites.md) | Owner | How the sites relate |
| 3 | [Roles for your team](../day-to-day/roles-for-your-team.md) | Owner | Managers and editors per site |
| 4 | [Roles and policies](../../../guides/roles-and-policies.md) | Administrator | Limiting a role to one site's part |
| 5 | [Who does what](../partners-and-hosts/who-does-what.md) | Owner | Partner, host and your team |

## The first month: shared content and settings

| # | Read | Who | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 | [Shared content across brands](../../businesses/multisite/shared-content-across-brands.md) | Site managers | Who may change shared items |
| 7 | [Cross-site links](../../businesses/multisite/cross-site-links.md) | Site managers | Links that open in the right site |
| 8 | [Per-brand designs](../../businesses/multisite/per-brand-designs.md) | Owner | Each site its own look |
| 9 | [Per-site settings and logs](../../businesses/multisite/per-site-settings-and-logs.md) | Administrator | Settings and logs per site |
| 10 | [Content calendar](../day-to-day/content-calendar.md) | Site managers | One calendar or one per site |
| 11 | [Weekly owner routine](../day-to-day/weekly-owner-routine.md) | Site managers | The same checks on every site |
