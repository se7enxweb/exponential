# Membership site reading path

The order in which a membership team learns its site. Each step names who should read the page.

## The first week: members and access

| # | Read | Who | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Membership sites: start here](membership-site-start-here.md) | Everyone | How a member site runs |
| 2 | [Logging into the admin](../../logging-into-the-admin.md) | Membership manager | Where to sign in |
| 3 | [Accounts, passwords and two-factor sign-in](../day-to-day/accounts-passwords-and-two-factor.md) | Everyone with access | Member data needs safe accounts |
| 4 | [Registration admin links](../../../features/6.0/registration-admin-links.md) | Membership manager | Approve or reject new members |
| 5 | [Members-only content](../../businesses/members/members-only-content.md) | Membership manager | What the public sees and what members see |
| 6 | [Roles for your team](../day-to-day/roles-for-your-team.md) | Board member who owns the site | One role per task |
| 7 | [Editing content](../../editing-content.md) and [Publishing and versions](../../publishing-and-versions.md) | News editors | Member news |

| # | Read | Who | Why |
