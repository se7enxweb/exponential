# Online store reading path

The order in which a store team learns its shop. Orders come first, because they cannot wait; the catalog, prices
and VAT next; growth and upkeep last. Each step names who should read the page.

| 3 | [Accounts, passwords and two-factor sign-in](../day-to-day/accounts-passwords-and-two-factor.md) | Store team | Accounts that see customer data must be safe |
