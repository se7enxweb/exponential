# Magazine reading path

The order in which a magazine team learns its site. Each step names who should read the page; most take less than
an hour. It assumes the site runs and the accounts exist.

## The first week: work safely

| # | Read | Who | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Magazines: start here](magazine-start-here.md) | Everyone | How an issue is organized |
| 2 | [Logging into the editor](../../logging-into-the-editor.md) | Everyone | Where to sign in |
| 3 | [Editing content](../../editing-content.md) | Writers, editors | Create and change pieces |
| 4 | [Publishing and versions](../../publishing-and-versions.md) | Writers, editors | Drafts, publishing and going back |
| 5 | [Media and uploads](../../media-and-uploads.md) | Art director, editors | Images, galleries and files |
| 6 | [Roles for your team](../day-to-day/roles-for-your-team.md) | Site owner | Who may publish and who may lay out |
| 7 | [Accounts, passwords and two-factor sign-in](../day-to-day/accounts-passwords-and-two-factor.md) | Everyone | Safe accounts |
