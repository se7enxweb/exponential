# Customers reading path

Every kind of site has its own reading path (see [Customer types](types/README.md)). This one is the part they share:
the pages a site owner and the core team should read whatever the site does. Follow it together with the path of
your type; where both list the same page, read it once.

## Keep going

After the first quarter, the [checklists](value/checklists/README.md) carry the routine: daily, weekly, monthly,
quarterly and yearly. Once a year, review your providers
([Reviewing your provider each year](partners-and-hosts/reviewing-your-provider-each-year.md)) and the value of the
site ([Annual value review](value/annual-value-review.md)).
