# Customers: start here

This section is for the organizations and people who already run a site on Exponential for their own needs:
publishing news, a magazine, a blog or documentation; selling in an online store; serving members or staff; reaching
readers in several languages or with several sites. It is written for the **site owner and the team around the
site**: the person who answers for it, the managers and editors who work in it every day, and whoever talks to the
hosting company or the implementation partner.

You do not need to be technical to use it. Each page says what to do, how often and who on your team usually does
it, and links to the step-by-step help for editors and administrators and to the technical pages for your provider.

> **Note**
> Every fact here comes from the Exponential repository, from a demonstration installation
> or from <https://exponential.earth>, and the source is named on the page. The section
> names no customers and quotes no figures of anyone's business. Where a page calculates, the inputs are labeled as
> example assumptions to replace with your own. Pages about VAT, contracts and personal data describe the software
> and what to check; they are not legal advice.

## Your first 30 days

If you have just taken over a site (a new job, a new owner, a new partner), [Your first 30 days as a site
owner](first-30-days-as-owner.md) gives you a week-by-week plan. Before anything else, make sure you know:

1. **who can sign in with full rights**, and that each of those accounts belongs to a named person
   ([Admin accounts and access](healthy/admin-accounts-and-access.md));
2. **where the backups are and when one was last restored** ([Backups](healthy/backups.md),
   [Restore test](healthy/restore-test.md));
3. **who to call** for content, for the code and for the server ([When to call whom](partners-and-hosts/when-to-call-whom.md));
4. **what you own**: the content, the data, the code written for you, and the license of the software
   ([What you own](what-you-own.md)).
