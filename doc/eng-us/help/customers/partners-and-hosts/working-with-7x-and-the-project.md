# Working with 7x and the project

Your host and partner are your first contacts. Behind them stand the people who make Exponential: 7x, the company
that leads the project, and the Exponential Foundation, the nonprofit that operates it. This page tells an owner
what each offers and how to reach them. The longer version for businesses is
[Working with 7x and the project](../../businesses/choosing/working-with-7x-and-the-project.md).

## Who is who

| | 7x | Exponential Foundation |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | A company: web design, support, development and hosting | A nonprofit corporation |
| Role | "7x took over leadership of the project and it's development, support, adoption and community growth in 2023" (`README.md`) | Operates the project |
| More | [Who is 7x?](../../who-is-7x.md) | [Who is the Exponential Foundation?](../../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md) |

Source: [Working with 7x and the project](../../businesses/choosing/working-with-7x-and-the-project.md).

## What you get for free

| What | Where |
|---|---|
| The software and its updates | Under the GPL; no license fee ([Licensing explained for buyers](../../business/licensing-for-buyers.md)) |
| This help, in your administration | The help dashboard, `/exphelp/dashboard` |
| Community help | Forums and chats ([Getting support](../../project/getting-support.md)) |
| Public issue trackers | [GitHub issues](../healthy/github-issues.md) |
| Private security reports | security@se7enx.com ([Private security reports](../healthy/private-security-reports.md)) |

Free help comes without a promise: the project's support page says its developers "are not obligated to provide
free support" ([Support levels and response times](../../partners/support-levels-and-response-times.md)).

## What you can buy

7x sells services around the free software: web design and development, e-commerce and payment integration,
training, migration to Exponential 6, support and maintenance contracts, and managed hosting. "Prices and terms are
on the 7x website, not here" ([Who is 7x?](../../who-is-7x.md)). When you cannot wait, the project's support page
recommends paid support from 7x ([Getting support](../../project/getting-support.md)). Other companies may offer the
same services; the GPL names no required supplier
([Choosing a partner](../../businesses/choosing/choosing-a-partner.md)).

## How the project affects your site

| What the project does | What it means for you |
|---|---|
| Fixes on the maintained 6.0 line, published as the next release | Your provider must apply releases ([Release line and support](../healthy/release-line-and-support.md)) |
| Never changes a published version | What was tested is what is deployed |
| Answers private security reports, first answer within one business day | The only response promise in the project's files (`SECURITY.md`) |
| Publishes changelogs and behavior change notes | Your provider reads them before each upgrade |

## Giving back

An organization that relies on Exponential has an interest in the project staying healthy. Voluntary ways to help
([Working with 7x and the project](../../businesses/choosing/working-with-7x-and-the-project.md)):

- report bugs well, with version, steps and expected result;
- let your partner contribute general fixes back ([Contributing code back](../../partners/contributing-code-back.md));
- improve the documentation: this help is free documentation under the GNU FDL;
- sponsor 7x or the Foundation ([Give and donate](../../project/give-and-donate.md));
- share your story as a case study, with your approval
  ([Case study template](../../businesses/templates/case-study-template.md)).
