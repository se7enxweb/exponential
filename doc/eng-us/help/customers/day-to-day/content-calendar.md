# Content calendar

This page is for the owner, the editor-in-chief and the marketing lead who plan what appears on the site. Exponential
has no calendar view of planned content of its own; it has the tools a plan needs: drafts that only their creator sees,
approvals, publishing at a date by a workflow, layouts that switch on and off by date, and newsletters with scheduled
and recurring sends. Keep the plan in a shared calendar or sheet (start from the
[Content calendar template](content-calendar-template.md)) and link each line to the item in the site.

## What goes in the calendar

| Column | Why |
|---|---|
| Publication date and time | when visitors should see it |
| Title and type (article, landing page, newsletter edition, product) | what is made |
| Where in the tree | so roles and approvals apply as expected |
| Owner (a job, not only a name) | who writes it |
| Approver | who signs it off, if your site uses approvals |
| Languages | which translations must be ready on the same day |
| Status | idea, drafting, in review, scheduled, published |
| Link | the item's address or node number in the administration |

Use the node number or address for the link. Anyone on the team can open it with the search
([Open an item by its number or address](../../searching.md)).

## How each line becomes real in the site

| Calendar status | In Exponential | Help |
|---|---|---|
| Drafting | a **draft**; only its creator sees it, on *My drafts* | [My drafts and my pending items](../../../guides/drafts-and-pending.md) |
| In review | a **pending** version held by an approval | [Approvals and review](approvals-and-review.md) |
| Scheduled | a version held until its date by a *Wait until date* workflow, if your site has one | [Scheduled publishing](scheduled-publishing.md) |
| Published | the published version | [Publishing, versions and the trash](../../publishing-and-versions.md) |
| Landing page for a period | a layout mapped by a rule with a `time` condition | [Landing pages with layouts](../growth/landing-pages-with-layouts.md) |
| Newsletter | an edition with a scheduled or recurring send | [Newsletter routine](../growth/newsletter-routine.md) |

> **Note**
> Drafts are personal. A colleague cannot open someone else's draft to finish it; they make a new draft from a
> version on the versions page ([Versions of an object](../../../guides/content-history.md)). Plan hand-overs before
> holidays.

## A weekly planning rhythm

| When | What | Who |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | look at next two weeks; every line has an owner and an approver | editor-in-chief |
| Daily | update statuses; move lines whose draft is late | each owner |
| Before a publication day | check *My pending items* of the approvers: nothing waits that should go out | approvers |
| Friday | compare the calendar with what was published this week (the dashboard's recent content) | editor-in-chief |
| Monthly | read what visitors searched for and did not find; add topics ([Site search and what visitors look for](site-search-and-what-visitors-look-for.md)) | owner |

## Campaign periods

A campaign often means a landing page, a newsletter, a few articles and perhaps translations, all on one day:

1. Put every part in the calendar with the same date.
2. Build the landing page as a layout and map it with a rule whose `time` condition has `from` and `to`, at a higher
   priority than the page's normal rule ([Layout rules](../../creators/website-creators/layout-rules.md)).
3. Schedule the newsletter edition, and send it to a test group first
   ([Writing and sending editions](../../businesses/newsletters/writing-and-sending-editions.md)).
4. Use the [Campaign landing page checklist](../growth/campaign-landing-page-checklist.md) the day before.

## Keeping it honest

- A calendar that nobody updates is worse than none. Keep it to the columns above.
- Put the approver in the line, not only the writer: approvals are where dates slip.
- Note who will be away. Drafts cannot be taken over silently.
- After a campaign, write one line on what worked; read it before the next one.
