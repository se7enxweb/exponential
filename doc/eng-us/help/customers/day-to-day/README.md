# Running your site day to day

This section is for the people who already run a site on Exponential: the owner, the manager of the editorial team,
the editors and the person who talks to the host or the partner. It does not repeat the step-by-step editor pages.
Each page gives the owner's view (what to do, how often, who on the team does it and what to check) and links to the
help page with the clicks.

## What day-to-day ownership means

A site stays good when a few routines are kept: the right people have the right accounts, content is planned and
reviewed before it goes out, files and pages are tidied regularly, and somebody looks at what visitors search for and
cannot find. Exponential supports each routine with built-in tools: roles and policies
([Roles and policies](../../../guides/roles-and-policies.md)), approval workflows
([Workflows](../../../guides/workflows.md)), versions and the trash
([Publishing, versions and the trash](../../publishing-and-versions.md)), the content checks
([The content checks](../../../features/6.0/content-checks.md)) and the account review page
([Check user accounts](../../../features/6.0/user-hygiene.md)).
