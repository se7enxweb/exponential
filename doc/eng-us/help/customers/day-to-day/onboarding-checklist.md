# Onboarding checklist

This page is a **template** to copy for every new person who works on the site: employees, freelancers and agency
staff alike. The reasons behind each step are on [Onboarding new staff](onboarding-new-staff.md). Keep the completed
checklist with your team records; it is the evidence that access was granted on purpose.

## Contents

1. [Before the start date](#before-the-start-date)
2. [Day one](#day-one)
3. [First week](#first-week)
4. [After one month](#after-one-month)
5. [Sign-off](#sign-off)

## Before the start date

- [ ] The job on the site is written down (author, editor, approver, translator, form processor, store staff).
- [ ] The role, group and limits are chosen ([Choosing roles for new staff](choosing-roles-for-new-staff.md)).
- [ ] The site owner approved the access request.
- [ ] One personal account is created, with a real e-mail address of the person (password reset and e-mail codes
      go there).
- [ ] The account is in the right user group; the role is assigned to the group, not to the person.
- [ ] If the person is temporary: the end date is noted for [Offboarding staff](offboarding-staff.md).
- [ ] A safe first task is prepared (a draft, or a hidden page).

## Day one

- [ ] The person signs in ([Logging into the admin](../../logging-into-the-admin.md) or
      [Logging into the Editor](../../logging-into-the-editor.md)).
- [ ] The password is changed to one only they know ([Your account and signing in](../../accounts-and-sign-in.md)).
- [ ] Two-step sign-in is set up under *My account* if your site offers it
      ([Two-factor login](../../../guides/two-factor-login.md)).
- [ ] Notification and e-mail preferences are set
      ([Notifications: the user's guide](../../../features/6.0/notifications.md)).
- [ ] The tour is done with the site's own content ([A tour of the administration](../../admin-tour.md)).
- [ ] A first draft is made, previewed and then published or discarded.

## First week

- [ ] Creating and editing, relations and rich text ([Creating and editing content](../../editing-content.md)).
- [ ] Uploading files and writing alternative texts ([Images, media and uploads](../../media-and-uploads.md)).
- [ ] Searching and opening an item by its number ([Searching](../../searching.md)).
- [ ] Versions: comparing and going back ([Drafts, versions and undo](drafts-versions-and-undo.md)).
- [ ] If behind an approval: one version sent and approved ([Approvals and review](approvals-and-review.md)).
- [ ] If editing landing pages: one layout draft made and discarded ([The layouts editor](../../layouts-editor.md)).
- [ ] If translating: one translation made ([Languages](../../languages.md)).
- [ ] The house rules read: titles, images, link texts, headings
      ([Accessibility checklist for editors](accessibility-checklist-for-editors.md)).
- [ ] The person knows how to report a problem ([When something goes wrong](../../when-something-goes-wrong.md)).
- [ ] The person knows who to ask (a role name, not a person who may leave).

## After one month

- [ ] Questions from the first month are added to the site's editor guide.
- [ ] The role still fits; extra access was asked for only for real tasks.
- [ ] The [Team roles worksheet](team-roles-worksheet.md) is updated.
