# SEO checks

This page is for the owner and the marketing lead who want the site to be found. Most of what search engines need is
built in or one extension away; the rest is good writing by editors. The builder's checklist is
[SEO basics for Exponential sites](../../creators/website-creators/seo-basics.md); this page is the owner's view: what
to check, who fixes what, and how often. The monthly routine is the [SEO monthly checklist](seo-monthly-checklist.md).

## What the software already does

From [SEO basics for Exponential sites](../../creators/website-creators/seo-basics.md):

| Need | How |
|---|---|
| Readable addresses from content names | URL aliases, built in |
| Old addresses keep working after a rename or move | history aliases redirect, built in |
| Your own short addresses and patterns | global aliases and wildcards, built in |
| `robots.txt` | shipped at the web root |
| Feeds | RSS exports, built in ([RSS and podcast feeds](../growth/rss-and-podcast-feeds.md)) |
| Fast pages | caches and the Velocity response cache |

## What depends on how the site was built

Ask your site builder or partner once, and write the answers down:

| Question | Why | Read |
|---|---|---|
| Does every page have its own title and description? Which field becomes the description? | the snippet in search results | [Page metadata and social sharing](../../creators/website-creators/metadata-and-social-sharing.md) |
| Is there an XML sitemap, regenerated by cron, listed in `robots.txt`? | helps search engines find every page | [XML sitemaps](../../creators/website-creators/sitemaps.md) |
| Do content items with several locations print one canonical address? | one address per page | [SEO basics](../../creators/website-creators/seo-basics.md) |
| Is there one host name, with HTTPS, and the others redirected? | no duplicate sites | [Going live: hosts, HTTPS and siteaccesses](../../creators/website-creators/going-live-hosts-https.md) |
| Does each language have its own address? | search engines show the right language | [Language switcher and language URLs](../../creators/website-creators/language-switcher-and-language-urls.md) |

## What editors control

Editors write what search engines read: titles, intros, alternative texts and link texts
([SEO basics](../../creators/website-creators/seo-basics.md)).

- [ ] **Titles** short and clear. The name of an item becomes its title and its address.
- [ ] **Intro or description** filled in: it becomes the description when no metadata field is filled.
- [ ] **Headings** in order, one main heading per page.
- [ ] **Link texts** that say where they lead ("Opening hours", not "click here").
- [ ] **Alternative texts** on every image ([Image and file guidelines](image-and-file-guidelines.md)).
- [ ] **Rename and move freely**; old addresses redirect. **Removing** is different: decide where the visitors go
      ([URLs, redirects and moved pages](urls-redirects-and-moved-pages.md)).

| Look at | It tells you | Source |

## Who fixes what

| Problem | Who |
|---|---|
| Weak titles, missing descriptions or alternative texts | editors |
| A removed page without a redirect | an editor with access to URL aliases, or the administrator |
| No sitemap, no canonical link, several host names | site builder or partner |
| Slow pages | host or partner ([Performance and caching](../../creators/website-creators/performance-and-caching.md)) |
