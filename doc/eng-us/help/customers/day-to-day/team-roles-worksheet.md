# Team roles worksheet

This page is a **template** for the owner or manager. Fill it in once with your administrator, keep it next to your
site's other records, and update it when someone joins, changes job or leaves. It translates "who does what" into the
roles and groups Exponential uses ([Roles and policies](../../../guides/roles-and-policies.md)), so the
administrator can check that the site matches it.

## How to use it

1. Fill in Part 1 with the jobs on your team, not with names.
2. Ask the administrator to write next to each job the role, group and limits that exist on the site today. Open
   **Users > Roles and policies** together: each role page lists its policies as sentences such as *May read
   content, in section Standard, of the classes Article and Folder*.
3. Fill in Part 2 from the staff list. Compare with **Users > Check user accounts**
   ([Check user accounts](../../../features/6.0/user-hygiene.md)).
4. Mark every difference and agree who fixes it.
5. Review the sheet once a year ([Access reviews](../../businesses/security/access-reviews.md)).

> **Note**
> Use roles and job names in shared documents, not personal details you do not need. A shared sheet with only
> "Editor, Marketing" in it ages better than one with names, phone numbers and private addresses.
