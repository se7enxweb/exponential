# Writing the adoption decision

A written decision prevents the most common reason adoptions stall: nobody knows who decided what.

## Contents

1. [Why write it down](#why-write-it-down)
2. [The template](#the-template)
3. [After the decision](#after-the-decision)

## Why write it down

- It names an owner who can make the next decisions.
- It records the evidence, so the decision can be explained later.
- It lists the risks you accepted, so nobody is surprised by them.

## The template

```text
ADOPTION DECISION

Date:               <date>
Decided by:         <name and role>
Owner of the site:  <name and role>

What we adopt:      Exponential <version line, e.g. 6.0.x>, served by <engine>, on <database>
For:                <the sites and purposes>
Why:                <three reasons, with the scorecard total>
Evidence:           <proof of concept write-up, scorecard, answers to questions>

Who runs it:        <ourselves / hosting company / partner / 7x>
Budget:             one-time <...>, per year <...> (from the budget worksheet)
Timeline:           <launch target, measured from rehearsals>

Accepted risks:     <risk - how we reduce it>
Not included:       <what we decided not to do now>
Revisit if:         <conditions that would reopen the decision>
```

## After the decision

- Share it with everyone who will work on the adoption.
- Move to [Planning](../planning/README.md).
- Review it at the first annual review ([Annual value review](../../customers/value/annual-value-review.md)).
