## Support options

| Option | What you get | Page |
|---|---|---|
| The help in the administration | Documentation for your version | [Getting help step by step](../../community/joining/getting-help-step-by-step.md) |
| The community | Free answers, no guaranteed answer time | [Where people meet](../../community/joining/where-people-meet.md) |

## Matching support to your needs

| If | Then |
|---|---|
| The site earns money or serves the public daily | A written agreement with response times ([SLA template](../../customers/partners-and-hosts/sla-template.md)) |
| One person runs a personal site | The community and the help are usually enough |
| You have developers on staff | Community plus a small support agreement for emergencies |
