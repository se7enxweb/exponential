# Licensing for adopters

This is a summary, not legal advice. The license texts are binding.

## Contents

1. [The license in short](#the-license-in-short)

## The license in short

- "Exponential is GNU GPLv2 licensed" and "Using Exponential under the terms of the GNU GPL is free of charge"
  (`README.md`, "License").
- "The Professional License is no longer available. This effectively makes Exponential GNU GPLv2-or-later ONLY"
  (same).
- The documentation is under the GNU FDL 1.3 or later ([Free documentation](../../free-documentation.md)).
- Velocity is MIT licensed ([Exponential Velocity](../../ecosystem/products/exponential-velocity.md)).

## What it means for you

| Situation | In short |
|---|---|
| You run a site for your organization | No license fee; no obligation to publish your own templates or extensions |
| A provider builds custom code for you | Agree in the contract who owns it; you receive the GPL freedoms for GPL code |
| You distribute Exponential or code based on it to others | The GPL's conditions apply: the source goes with it |
| You stop working with a provider | The software stays usable; nobody can revoke it |

The buyers' page goes deeper: [Licensing for buyers](../../business/licensing-for-buyers.md). Questions:
[Licensing FAQ](../../project/licensing-faq.md).

## What to clarify with providers

- Who owns custom extensions, designs and site packages written for you.
- That you receive the source and the Git history of your project.
- Which third-party services (payment, AI providers, analytics) have their own terms.
- That nobody presents free software to you as their proprietary product (`CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`, "Partners and
  commercial conduct").
