# What adoption means

Installing Exponential takes minutes ([Install in one command](../../features/6.0/install-in-one-command.md)). Adopting
it takes longer, because an adoption changes how people work.

## Contents

1. [Five things an adoption changes](#five-things-an-adoption-changes)
2. [What success looks like](#what-success-looks-like)
3. [Why adoptions stall](#why-adoptions-stall)

## Five things an adoption changes

| Area | What changes | Where this section helps |
|---|---|---|
| Software | A new system, its hosting, its updates | [Hosting and database choices](planning/hosting-and-database-choices.md), [Keeping current](after-adoption/keeping-current.md) |
| Content | Content moves in and is structured in a content model | [Content modeling first steps](planning/content-modeling-first-steps.md), [Migrating](migrating/README.md) |
| People | Editors and administrators learn new screens and habits | [Skills and training](planning/skills-and-training.md), [Rolling out to editors](launching/rolling-out-to-editors.md) |
| Processes | Approval, publishing, translation, roles | [Roles and permissions for the rollout](launching/roles-and-permissions-for-rollout.md), [Governance after launch](after-adoption/governance-after-launch.md) |
| Providers | Who hosts, builds and supports it | [Choosing who runs it](deciding/choosing-who-runs-it.md) |

## What success looks like

- Editors publish without help a few weeks after launch.
- Updates are applied on a schedule, not in a panic.
- Someone owns the site and its decisions.
- The organization knows what the site costs and what it delivers
  ([Measuring adoption success](after-adoption/measuring-adoption-success.md)).

## Why adoptions stall

| Cause | Remedy |
|---|---|
| Nobody owns the decision | Name an owner in the decision record ([Writing the decision](deciding/writing-the-decision.md)) |
| The content model copies the old system's quirks | Model content for its purpose ([Content modeling first steps](planning/content-modeling-first-steps.md)) |
| Editors are trained once, too early | Train close to launch, then again after a month ([Rolling out to editors](launching/rolling-out-to-editors.md)) |
| Updates are postponed until they become a project | A routine from the first month ([Keeping current](after-adoption/keeping-current.md)) |
| Everything is migrated at once | Adopt in stages ([Adopting in stages](planning/adopting-in-stages.md)) |
