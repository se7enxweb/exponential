## The stages

| Stage | Outcome | Usually owned by | Pages |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Evaluate | A filled scorecard and a proof of concept | A project lead with a developer | [Evaluating](evaluating/README.md) |
| 2. Decide | A written decision with owner, budget and provider | The decision maker | [Deciding](deciding/README.md) |
| 3. Plan | A team, a timeline, a content model, a hosting choice | The project manager | [Planning](planning/README.md) |
| 4. Migrate | Content in Exponential, rehearsed and verified | Developer or partner | [Migrating](migrating/README.md) |
| 5. Launch | The site live, editors working, old site retired | Project manager and site owner | [Launching](launching/README.md) |
| 6. Live with it | Routines for updates, security, governance, growth | The site owner | [After adoption](after-adoption/README.md) |

## Gates between stages

| Before | Check |
|---|---|
| Deciding | The proof of concept answered the hardest requirement |
| Planning | The decision is written, with an owner and a budget |
| Migrating | The content model and hosting are agreed |
| Launching | A full rehearsal ran, was measured and verified; a rollback plan exists |
| Living with it | The launch checklist is complete; editors can publish alone |

Project managers use the same idea as quality gates: [Quality gates and acceptance](../leadership/quality-gates-and-acceptance.md).

## Durations

The project gives no hour or price figures for an adoption or a migration. "Measure the duration in a rehearsal and
plan from the measurement, as the installation book advises" ([Switching to Exponential](../businesses/choosing/switching-to-exponential.md)).
[Timeline](planning/timeline.md) shows how to build a schedule from your own measurements.
