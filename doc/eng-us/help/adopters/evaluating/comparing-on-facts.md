## Rules for a fair comparison

1. **The same requirements** for every system, weighted before you look at any of them.
2. **The same kind of evidence**: a proof of concept for each finalist, not a demo for one and a brochure for another.
3. **Documented facts only.** For Exponential, every fact has a source in the repository or this help; ask the same
   of the others.
4. **The same time horizon** for cost: several years, not the first invoice.

The sales section uses the same discipline: [Comparing Exponential factually](../../business/comparing-exponential-factually.md).

## Traps

- **Feature lists.** A checkmark says nothing about how well a feature fits your work. Test the ones that matter.
- **Numbers without dates.** Ask when and how a number was measured.
- **License fees as the only cost.** Work, hosting and upgrades usually cost more ([What you do not pay for](../../businesses/costs/what-you-do-not-pay-for.md)).
- **Comparing a product line with one product.** Exponential has a family; compare the product that fits
  ([Choosing a product](../../ecosystem/products/choosing-a-product.md)).
