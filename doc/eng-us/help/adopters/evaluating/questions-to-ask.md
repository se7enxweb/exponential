## Questions the documentation already answers

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| What does it cost in licenses? | Nothing; GPL v2 or later (`README.md`) |
| Which versions get security fixes? | The 6.0 line; fixes in the next 6.0.x release (`SECURITY.md`) |
| Which PHP versions and databases? | PHP 7.4 to 8.5; SQLite, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, MariaDB, Oracle, MySQL ([Database support](../../database-support.md)) |
| Who leads the project? | 7x leads development; the Exponential Foundation operates the project ([Who decides](../../community/how-it-works/who-decides.md)) |
| How are security problems reported? | Privately to security@se7enx.com, first answer within one business day (`SECURITY.md`) |

## Questions for a partner or 7x

- Which Exponential sites like ours have you built, and may we speak with the owner?
- Who on your team has worked with the kernel, and what have they contributed?
- How do you keep customers on current releases? ([Keeping customers current](../../partners/keeping-customers-current.md))
- What is in your support agreement: response times, hours, escalation?
- What will we own at the end: code, content, accounts? ([What you own](../../customers/what-you-own.md))
- Which partner level do you hold? ([Partner levels](../../partners/partner-levels.md))

More: [Questions to ask a partner](../../businesses/choosing/questions-to-ask-a-partner.md).

## Questions for a hosting company

- Which PHP version, database and server will the site run on?
- Can we run Velocity, or only Apache or Nginx with PHP-FPM?
- How are backups made and tested?
- Who applies updates, and how quickly after a security release?

More: [What to ask your host](../../customers/healthy/what-to-ask-your-host.md).

## Questions for the community

Ask in the forums ([Asking a good question](../../community/joining/asking-a-good-question.md)):

- Has anyone built something like our hardest requirement?
- Which extension do people use for X?

## Questions for yourself

- Who will own the site after launch?
- How many editors, how often do they publish, in how many languages?
- What does an hour of downtime cost us? ([Cost of downtime worksheet](../../businesses/costs/cost-of-downtime-worksheet.md))
- What must never be lost? (content, orders, member data)
