## The phased plan

From [Switching to Exponential](../../businesses/choosing/switching-to-exponential.md#a-phased-plan):

1. Inventory: content types, languages, extensions, integrations, traffic.
2. Choose the path and a partner if needed.
3. Rehearse on a staging copy and measure every step.
4. Verify content, URLs, forms, the store and sign-ins on the copy.
5. Plan the cut-over: a content freeze, a maintenance window with maintenance mode, the final run.
6. Keep a rollback plan until the new site is accepted.
