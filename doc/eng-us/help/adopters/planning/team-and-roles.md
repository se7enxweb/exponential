# Team and roles for the adoption

In a small adoption one person holds several roles. What matters is that every role has a name.

## Contents

1. [The roles](#the-roles)
2. [A worksheet](#a-worksheet)
3. [Roles in the software](#roles-in-the-software)

## The roles

| Role | During the adoption | After launch |
|---|---|---|
| Owner | Decides, approves the budget, accepts the launch | Owns the site and its decisions |
| Project lead | Plans, tracks, removes obstacles | Hands over to the owner |
| Content lead | Models content, plans the migration of content, writes guidelines | Leads the editors |
| Developer (internal or partner) | Builds templates and extensions, migrates | Maintains custom code |
| Administrator (internal, host or partner) | Sets up servers, environments, backups | Updates, monitoring, security |
| Editors | Test, give feedback, learn | Publish |
| Provider contacts | Deliver what the contract says | Support and maintenance |
