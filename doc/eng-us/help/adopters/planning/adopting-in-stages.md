# Adopting in stages

An adoption in stages starts with something small that matters, learns from it, and grows. It is the safest way to
adopt in a larger organization.

## Contents

1. [Choosing the pilot](#choosing-the-pilot)
2. [Typical stages](#typical-stages)
3. [Learning from the pilot](#learning-from-the-pilot)
4. [When to move on](#when-to-move-on)

## Choosing the pilot

| Good pilot | Poor pilot |
|---|---|
| Real users and real content | A test site nobody visits |
| Small enough to finish in weeks | The whole organization at once |
| Representative content types | Something unlike everything else |
| An owner who wants it | A team forced into it |

## Typical stages

| Stage | Example |
|---|---|
| 1 | One site or one section, one language, a few editors |
| 2 | More content types; a second language ([Adding a language](../../customers/growth/adding-a-language.md)) |
| 3 | A second site on the same installation ([Adding a site](../../customers/growth/adding-a-site.md)) |
| 4 | Features such as a store, newsletters, member areas ([Growth](../../customers/growth/README.md)) |
| 5 | Integrations and apps through the REST API ([Mobile and apps](../../customers/growth/mobile-and-apps.md)) |

Exponential's multisite and multilingual support makes this growth possible on one installation (`README.md`).

## Learning from the pilot

After the pilot, write down:

- what editors found hard;
- which parts of the content model had to change;
- how long the migration of the pilot took per item;
- which tasks the provider and the internal team actually did.

Use these to replan the next stage ([Timeline](timeline.md)).

## When to move on

- Editors publish without help.
- The first update after launch was applied without problems ([Keeping current](../after-adoption/keeping-current.md)).
- The measurements from the pilot are in the plan.
