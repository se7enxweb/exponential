## When to train

| When | Who | What |
|---|---|---|
| During the evaluation | One future editor | Enough to judge the proof of concept |
| Before the build | Developers, designers | The tools they will use |
| One or two weeks before launch | All editors | Their daily tasks, on the new site with real content |
| A month after launch | All editors | Questions from real work; shortcuts |

Training too early is forgotten; training after launch is too late. See
[Rolling out to editors](../launching/rolling-out-to-editors.md).
