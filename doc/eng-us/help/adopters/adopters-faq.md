## Cost and license

**Is there a license fee?**
No. "Using Exponential under the terms of the GNU GPL is free of charge" (`README.md`). You pay for work, hosting and
support you choose ([Licensing for adopters](deciding/licensing-for-adopters.md)).

**What will the adoption cost?**
That depends on your providers and your organization; this help quotes no prices. Fill in the
[budget worksheet](deciding/budget-worksheet.md) with your own quotes.

## Time and effort

**How long does an adoption take?**
The project gives no figures. Measure a rehearsal and plan from it ([Timeline](planning/timeline.md)).

**Can we adopt step by step?**
Yes: a pilot first, then more sites, languages and features ([Adopting in stages](planning/adopting-in-stages.md)).

## Technical

**What does it run on?**
PHP 8.0 to 8.5, almost any operating system, SQLite or one of five database servers, Velocity or a traditional web
server ([Hosting and database choices](planning/hosting-and-database-choices.md)).

**Do we need a database server?**
No, if one machine serves the site: SQLite is the default ([Database support](../database-support.md)).

**Can we move our existing site?**
Yes, from older Exponential releases, eZ Platform or Ibexa, and other systems ([Migrating](migrating/README.md)).

**Can our apps use the content?**
Yes, through "A headless CRUD REST API" (`README.md`).

## Support and future

**Who supports us?**
The community for free, 7x, partners and hosting companies for pay ([Longevity and support](deciding/longevity-and-support.md)).

**Will Exponential still be maintained?**
The 6.0 line is maintained, a company and a nonprofit stand behind it, and the code is yours under the GPL whatever
happens ([Risks and how to weigh them](evaluating/risks-and-how-to-weigh-them.md)).

**How do we report a security problem?**
Privately to security@se7enx.com (`SECURITY.md`).

**Can our staff learn it?**
Yes: the help, course material and training partners ([Skills and training](planning/skills-and-training.md)).
