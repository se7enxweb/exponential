## Who decides what

| Decision | Usually decided by |
|---|---|
| What is published and how it is written | Content lead and editors, within guidelines |
| New content types or features | The owner, with the content lead and the developer |
| When updates are applied | The administrator or provider, on the agreed schedule |
| Who has which access | The owner, implemented by the administrator |
| Budget and providers | The owner |

Write this down in a short page that everyone can find. Keep the decision record from the adoption with it
([Writing the decision](../deciding/writing-the-decision.md)).

## Change requests

Requests for new features or changes go through one channel to the owner, who decides and passes them to the
provider: [Change requests](../../customers/partners-and-hosts/change-requests.md).
