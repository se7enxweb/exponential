## Giving back

An organization that relies on Exponential has a stake in its future. Ways to give back, from small to large:

- report bugs you find and confirm others' ([Filing a bug](../../community/contributing/filing-a-bug.md));
- let your developers contribute fixes ([Contributing code](../../community/contributing/contributing-code.md));
- share an anonymized case study ([Writing a community case study](../../advocates/writing-a-community-case-study.md));
- sponsor 7x or the Foundation ([Funding the community](../../community/how-it-works/funding-the-community.md));
- become a partner, if you sell services ([From community to partner](../../community/paths/from-community-to-partner.md)).
