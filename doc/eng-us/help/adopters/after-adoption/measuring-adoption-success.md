## Start from the goals

Measure against what the decision record said the adoption should achieve
([Writing the decision](../deciding/writing-the-decision.md)), not against whatever is easy to count.

## Measures

| Area | Example measures |
|---|---|
| Editors | Time to publish a typical item; share of items published without help; questions per week |
| Visitors | Page speed; errors; search use; the goals your site exists for |
| Operations | Availability; time from a security release to its installation; restore test done |
| Cost | Actual against the budget worksheet ([Budget worksheet](../deciding/budget-worksheet.md)) |
