# Adopters: start here

This section is for anyone who is adopting Exponential: an organization choosing its next content management system,
a team moving a site from another system or from an older release, or one person who wants to run their own site on
it. It follows an adoption from the first question ("does it fit?") to the months after launch.

Adoption is more than installation. It is a decision, a plan, a migration, people learning new tools, and habits that
keep the site healthy for years. Each page here says what to do at one step and links to the detailed pages for
whoever does the work.

> **Note**
> Facts on these pages come from the repository and from other pages of this help, and the source is named. No page
> quotes prices of providers or invents durations: where a page helps you budget or plan, every number is labeled as
> an example assumption for you to replace.

## Exponential in one minute for adopters

- **What it is.** "a professional PHP application framework with advanced CMS (content management system)
  functionality", whose most notable feature "is its fully customizable and extendable content model" (`README.md`).
- **What it costs in licenses.** Nothing: "Using Exponential under the terms of the GNU GPL is free of charge"
  (`README.md`). You pay for work and hosting ([Licensing for adopters](deciding/licensing-for-adopters.md)).
- **What it runs on.** PHP 8.0 or later on almost any operating system; SQLite needs no database server
  ([Hosting and database choices](planning/hosting-and-database-choices.md)).
- **Who stands behind it.** 7x leads development; the nonprofit Exponential Foundation operates the project
  ([Longevity and support](deciding/longevity-and-support.md)).
- **How it is kept current.** The 6.0 line is maintained; security fixes come in the next 6.0.x release (`SECURITY.md`).
