# How do I tag content?

Tags are keywords from a shared tree. Content with the same tag is grouped on the site, for example in a tag cloud or a
list of related articles.

## Who can do this

Editors who may edit the object. Adding a tag that is not in the tree yet needs `tags/add`.

## Steps

1. Edit the object.
2. Find the tags field (its name depends on the class, for example **Tags**).
3. Type part of a tag under **Find a tag to add**, then click a match. Or click one of the **Suggested tags**, or
   **Choose a tag** to pick from the tree.
   The tag appears under **Selected tags**.
4. To take a tag off, click **Remove tag** next to it.
5. Click **Send for publishing**.

## What can go wrong

- **The tag you need does not exist and cannot be added.** The field only allows tags from the tree, or your role lacks
  `tags/add`. Ask whoever keeps the tag tree.
- **Two tags mean the same.** Use the main one. Whoever keeps the tree can make the other a synonym or merge them: see
  [How do I keep the tag tree tidy?](how-do-i-keep-the-tag-tree-tidy.md)
