# How do I subscribe to notifications for a part of the site?

Follow an item, such as a news folder, to get a mail when it or its sub items change.

## Who can do this

Users whose role has `notification/use`.

## Steps

1. In **Content structure**, find the item to follow.
2. Open its context menu (the icon in front of its name, or a right click) and choose **Add to my notifications**.
3. Confirm.
   The item is listed under **Items I follow** in **My notification settings**.
4. Alternatively, open **Dashboard** > **My notification settings**, click **Add items** and choose the items in the
   browser.
5. Choose how often the mail comes: see
   [How do I choose which notifications I get?](how-do-i-choose-which-notifications-i-get.md)

## What can go wrong

- **"You already follow this item."** It is in your list. To stop, open the same page and click **Stop notifications
  for this item**.
- **No mail for a change.** The author used **Publish without notification**, or the change was in a draft that is not
  published yet.
