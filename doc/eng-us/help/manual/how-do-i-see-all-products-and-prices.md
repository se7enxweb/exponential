# How do I see all products and their prices?

The products overview lists every product of one class with its price, so you can check prices without opening each
product.

## Who can do this

Users whose role has `shop/administrate`.

## Steps

1. Click **Store**, then **Products overview**.
2. Choose the product class and click **Show products**.
3. Under **Sorting**, choose **Name** or **Price** and **Ascending** or **Descending**, then click **Sort products**.
4. Click a product's name to open it, and edit it to change the price.
5. For what sells, click **Product statistics** in the left menu.

## What can go wrong

- **"The product list is empty."** No published object of that class has a price field, or another class is chosen.
- **A product is missing.** It is a draft, hidden or in the trash. Search for it in **Content structure**.
