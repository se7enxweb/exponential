# How do I publish an RSS feed of my content?

An RSS export publishes the newest items of a folder as a feed that readers and other sites can subscribe to.

## Who can do this

Users whose role has `rss/edit`.

## Steps

1. Open **Setup** > **RSS**.
   The page **RSS feeds** lists **RSS exports** and **RSS imports**.
2. Under **RSS exports**, click **New export**.
3. Enter the **Name** (the title of the feed), a **Description** and the **Site URL**.
4. Under **Source**, click **Browse** and choose the folder whose items the feed lists. Choose the **Class** of the
   items and click **Set**, then choose which field is the title and which the description.
5. Choose the **Feed format**, the **Number of objects**, and the **Access URL** (the last part of the feed's
   address).
6. Tick **Active** and click **OK**.
7. Click **Open feed** in the list to check it.

![RSS feeds with the lists RSS exports and RSS imports and the buttons New export and New import](images/rss-feeds.webp)

## What can go wrong

- **The feed is empty.** The class or the folder is wrong, or the items are not readable for visitors.
- **Readers see the wrong text.** Choose other fields for title and description, then **OK**.
