# How do I preview a draft before I publish it?

The preview shows a version as a visitor would see it, without publishing it. You choose the site design, the
language and the place in the tree it is shown in, and the screen size.

## Who can do this

Editors who may read versions of the item (`content/versionread`); the draft's creator always may.

## Steps

1. In the edit form, click **Preview** in the left column, under the current draft.
   What you typed is stored in the draft, and the page **Preview of the version** opens.
2. Under **View control**, choose:
   - **Siteaccess**: the site whose design is used, for example the public site rather than the administration;
   - **Translation**: the language shown;
   - **Location**: which of the item's locations it is shown in (for a new item, the place chosen for it);
   - **View mode**: usually the full page.
3. Click **Update view**.
4. Choose a **Preview size** (**Desktop**, **Large Desktop**, **iPad**, **iPhone** or **Landscape**) to see the page at
   that width. **Open the preview in a new window** shows it full size.
5. Click **Back to edit** to change the draft, or **Publish** to publish it from here.

An older version can be previewed the same way: open **Manage versions** and click **View** in its row.

## What can go wrong

- **The preview looks unstyled or like the administration.** The administration siteaccess is chosen. Choose the
  public siteaccess and click **Update view**.
- **The form comes back with a message instead of the preview.** A field is not valid (for example a malformed
  address). Correct it and click **Preview** again.
- **Lists and menus look old.** The preview shows this version of this item only; lists on other pages still show
  the published versions until you publish.
- **Edit is not offered.** The version shown is not a draft (it is published or archived). Make a new draft from it:
  see [How do I go back to an earlier version?](how-do-i-go-back-to-an-earlier-version.md)
