# Where do I see my items that wait for approval?

When the site has an approval workflow, a version you send for publishing waits until an approver accepts it. It is
then listed in **My pending items**.

## Who can do this

Every user who can edit content. You see only your own pending versions.

## Steps

1. Open **Dashboard** in the top menu and choose **My pending items**, or use the **My pending items** block on the
   dashboard.
   The list **Pending versions** shows each version with its name, class and when you sent it.
2. Click a name to see the version as the approver sees it.
3. To read the approver's answer or a message, open **Dashboard** > **Collaboration**.
4. When the approver accepts, the version is published and leaves the list. When they reject it, it goes back to
   **My drafts**, where you can change and send it again.

## What can go wrong

- **An item stays pending for days.** The approver has not handled it, or the workflow cronjob is not running. Ask the
  approver first, then an administrator.
- **You want to change a pending item.** You cannot edit it while it waits. Ask the approver to reject it, then edit
  the draft and send it again.
- **The list is always empty.** The site has no approval workflow for your content: your versions are published at
  once.
