# What do I do about a "Possible edit conflict"?

When you click **Edit**, Exponential first checks for drafts of the item that were never published. If there are
any, the page **Possible edit conflict** opens before the edit form. It says whether the drafts are yours, someone
else's, or both, and lists them under **Current drafts** with their version, creator and dates.

Two drafts in the same language are not merged. Whatever is published last becomes the current version, so two
people working on the same language of an item overwrite each other's work.

## Who can do this

Anyone who may edit the item. Only the creator of a draft can continue it.

## Steps

1. Read the message at the top of the page and look at the drafts in the list.
   Click a draft's version number or language to see what it contains.
2. Choose one way:
   - **Your own draft** holds work you want to keep: select it and click **Edit selected**. You continue where you left
     off.
   - **The drafts are old or unwanted**: click **New draft**. A fresh draft is copied from the published version.
   - **A colleague is working on the item right now**: click **Cancel** and agree with them who goes first.
3. Afterwards, remove drafts you no longer need: open **My drafts** on the dashboard, tick them and click
   **Remove selected**.

## What can go wrong

- **Edit selected is not offered.** None of the drafts are yours. You can only view them; make a new draft or wait.
- **A colleague's changes vanished.** You published a new draft while they were working on theirs, or the other way
  round. Compare the versions and bring back what was lost: see
  [How do I go back to an earlier version?](how-do-i-go-back-to-an-earlier-version.md)
- **The page appears every time.** Old drafts are piling up, often from closing the browser or pressing Back instead of
  **Store draft and exit** or **Discard draft**. Tidy **My drafts** and leave the edit form with its buttons.
