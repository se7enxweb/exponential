# How do I use the AI assistant in the editor?

Exponential Intelligence (the extension expai) adds an AI assistant to the administration. It never publishes on its
own: what it writes becomes a draft or a suggestion, and you decide.

## Who can do this

Users whose role has the Exponential Intelligence policies (for example `expai/write`), on a site where an AI provider
is set up.

## Steps

1. Open the user manual of Exponential Intelligence in the help center: **Help** > **Extensions** >
   **Exponential Intelligence** > **User manual**, at the help address `/exphelp/viewer/ext/expai/manual/README.md`.
2. Choose the question you have, for example how to ask the assistant in the edit form, how to use Vector, or how to
   review an AI draft.
3. Follow the steps there.

## What can go wrong

- **No AI buttons in the editor.** Your role lacks the policies, or no AI provider is set up. Ask an administrator.
- **The AI is wrong.** Always read and correct what it writes before you publish. You are the author.
