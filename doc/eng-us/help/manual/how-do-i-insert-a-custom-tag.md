# How do I insert a custom tag such as a fact box or a quote?

Custom tags are building blocks a site adds to rich text beyond headings, lists and tables. A new installation offers
**factbox** and **quote** (blocks of their own) and **strike**, **sub** and **sup** (inside a line); your site may
have more, and may have hidden some. The site's design decides how each one looks. The button names below are those of
the TinyMCE 8 editor.

## Who can do this

Editors who may edit the item.

## Steps

1. Edit the item. In a rich text field, select the text the tag should wrap, or put the cursor where it should go.
2. Click **Custom tag** in the toolbar.
   The dialog **Insert custom tag** opens.
3. Choose the tag under **Tag**.
   The fields of that tag appear (for example a title or an alignment), and an example image where the site has one.
4. Fill in the fields and click **OK**.
   The tag appears in the text.
5. To change it later, click inside it and choose **Edit custom tag**; **Remove custom tag** takes it out again.
6. Click **Send for publishing**, and check the result on the site with **Preview**.

## What can go wrong

- **The dialog says no custom tags are configured.** Your site offers none. Ask an administrator.
- **The tag you need is not in the list.** It is hidden on this site, or does not exist. Custom tags are set up by
  whoever builds the site.
- **The tag looks plain on the site.** The tag has no template in the site's design yet. Ask whoever looks after the
  design.
- **A tag wraps more or less than you meant.** Inline tags such as **sub** wrap the selected words only; block tags
  such as **factbox** stand as a block of their own. Remove the tag, select again and insert it anew.
