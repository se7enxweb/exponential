# User manual

Each page answers one question about the work in the administration with the **Admin 4** design. Every page has the
same parts: who can do it (the role or policy you need), the steps, what can go wrong, and the pages to read next.
What you see depends on your role: a tab or button you may not use is not shown.

The guides and feature pages explain the background; these pages are for doing the task.

## Exponential Intelligence

Exponential Intelligence (the extension expai) has its own user manual, in the same form, among the documentation of
the extensions: in the help center open **Extensions** > **Exponential Intelligence** > **User manual**, or the help
address `/exphelp/viewer/ext/expai/manual/README.md`. It covers the assistant in the editor, Vector, summaries, alt text
suggestions, chains, the review of AI drafts and the budgets.
