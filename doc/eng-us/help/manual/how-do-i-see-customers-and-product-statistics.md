# How do I see my customers and what sold best?

Two lists of the **Store** tab answer the everyday questions of a shop: who buys, and what they buy. Both are worked
out from the orders in the database.

## Who can do this

Users whose role has `shop/administrate`.

## Steps

### Customers

1. Click **Store**, then **Customers** in the left menu.
   The list shows each customer with the number of orders and the totals with and without VAT.
2. Click a customer's name.
   The page shows the **Customer information**, their **Orders** with time and status, and the **Purchased products**
   with quantities.
3. Click an order to open it.

### Product statistics

1. Click **Store**, then **Product statistics**.
   The list shows each product sold with its **Quantity** and totals, and the sum at the bottom.
2. To look at one period, choose a month and a year (or **All months**, **All years**) and click **Show**.

## What can go wrong

- **A customer appears twice.** Customers are told apart by the e-mail address given at checkout. Someone who
  ordered with two addresses is listed twice.
- **The figures differ from your bookkeeping.** The lists count orders as they were placed, in the shop's currencies.
  Refunds and cancellations made only at the payment provider are not known to them.
- **Archived orders still count.** Both lists include orders moved to the archive; only unfinished checkouts are left
  out. See [How do I archive finished orders?](how-do-i-archive-finished-orders.md)
