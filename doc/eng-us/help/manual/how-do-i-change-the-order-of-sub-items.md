# How do I change the order of sub items?

The parent decides the order of its sub items, for example by name, by date, or by a number you give each one
(priority). The same order is used on the site and in menus.

## Who can do this

Users with `content/edit` on the parent.

## Steps

1. Open the parent (for example a folder) in **Content structure**.
2. Open its **Ordering** tab.
3. Under **Sort by**, choose the field, for example **Published**, **Name** or **Priority**. Under **Sort order**,
   choose **Ascending** or **Descending**.
4. Click **Set**.
5. If you chose **Priority**: in the **Sub items** list, enter a number in the **Priority** column of each item
   (lower first), then click **Update priorities**.

## What can go wrong

- **The site still shows the old order.** The page is cached. It is usually renewed within minutes; ask an
  administrator to clear the content cache if not.
- **The priority fields are missing.** **Sort by** is not **Priority**. Change it first.
- **Set is grayed out.** You may not edit the parent.
