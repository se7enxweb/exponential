# How do I add an anchor and link to it in the Online Editor?

An anchor marks a spot in a text. A link to it jumps straight there, which is useful for a list of contents at the top
of a long page or for pointing to one section of another page. The button names below are those of the TinyMCE 8
editor; another editor your site offers may name them differently.

## Who can do this

Editors who may edit the item.

## Steps

1. Edit the item and put the cursor at the start of the spot to jump to, for example a heading.
2. Click **Anchor** in the toolbar, type a short name without spaces (for example `opening-hours`) and confirm.
   A small anchor mark appears in the text.
3. Select the text that should become the link.
4. Click **Insert link**.
5. For a jump within the same text, choose the anchor under **Anchor in this text**. For a spot on another page, choose
   the page with **Browse** or **Search** and add `#` and the anchor name to the address.
6. Click **OK**, then **Send for publishing**.

## What can go wrong

- **The link opens the page but does not jump.** The anchor name in the link differs from the anchor's name, or the
  anchor was removed. Names must match exactly, including case.
- **Two anchors have the same name.** Only the first is reached. Give every anchor in a text its own name.
- **The jump lands in the wrong place on the site.** A sticky header covers the top of the target. Ask whoever looks
  after the design.
