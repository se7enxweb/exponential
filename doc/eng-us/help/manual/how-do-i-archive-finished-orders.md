# How do I archive finished orders?

Archive the orders you are done with, so that the order list shows only work in progress. Nothing is deleted.

## Who can do this

Users whose role has `shop/administrate`.

## Steps

1. Click **Store**, then **Orders**.
2. Tick the orders that are finished, for example delivered orders older than your returns period.
3. Click **Archive selected**.
   The orders leave the list.
4. To see them, click **Archive** in the left menu. The page is called **Archived orders**.
5. To bring an order back, tick it there and click **Unarchive selected**.

## What can go wrong

- **An order is missing from the order list.** It may have been archived. Look under **Store** > **Archive**.
- **You need to delete an order for data protection.** Archiving does not delete anything. See
  [Data export and erasure](../customers/commerce/data-export-and-erasure.md).
