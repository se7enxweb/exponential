# The Student partner level

The Exponential Partner Program has eight levels, and the first is **Student**, at **$25 a month**. This page
collects what the partner pages of this help say about it, quoted with their sources, so a learner can decide
whether it is worth it. It adds nothing that the program does not publish.

> **Note**
> The partner pages of this help copy the program pages as they read them on 18 October 2026. The live program pages
> and the store are authoritative: "7x reserves full rights to change or administer this program"
> ([Partner plans and prices](../partners/partner-plans-and-prices.md)). Check the live pages before you buy.

## What the Student level is

As published ([Student and education partners](../partners/student-and-education-partners.md#the-student-level-as-published),
[Partner levels from Student to Platinum](../partners/partner-levels.md)):

- "Student – For educational partners exploring foundational skills and knowledge"
  (<https://exponential.earth/partner/program-levels>).
- "Student Partner Plan: For students, universities, or learning institutions. Includes sandbox access, educational
  resources, and training support" (<https://exponential.earth/partner/why>).
- The program groups Student, Now, Starter and Basic in the stage **Learn**, with the value **Open**: "we provide new
  partners with the best educational environment for learning Exponential technologies".

The level guide suggests Student for a "Student, teacher, school", because of the "Lowest price, educational focus"
([Partner levels](../partners/partner-levels.md#choosing-your-starting-level)).

## Price, support and meetings

From [Partner plans and prices](../partners/partner-plans-and-prices.md#the-plans) and
[Support levels and response times](../partners/support-levels-and-response-times.md):

| Item | Student level |
|---|---|
| Price | **$25 a month** (US dollars) |
| GitHub issue tickets | 2 a week |
| Response time | ≤ 36 h (the program page says "Community only") |
| Installation support | No |
| Guidance meetings | 1 a quarter |
| Revenue requirement per quarter | None |

Whether response hours mean business hours or clock hours, and in which time zone, is not stated on the benefits
page.

## Discounts

From [Partner plans and prices](../partners/partner-plans-and-prices.md#discounts-and-lead-priority-by-plan):

| Discount | Student level |
|---|---|
| On 7x services | 0% |
| On subscription products | 4% |
| On knowledge products | 20% + K.O.P. |
| Lead priority | – |

K.O.P. is the Kick-Off Package, which the benefits page describes for the Basic level: it "includes program fee,
book, online training, certification, and 8h support for $3,200 (30% discount)".

## Requirements

[Partner requirements](../partners/partner-requirements.md) lists the way in for Student and Now as "Subscription",
with no minimum revenue and no certifications; the points figure of that row (3,000) is given for Now. The
requirements page adds: "Partnership lasts one year, with renewals and upgrades based on proven activity."

## How to join

The steps of [How to apply](../partners/how-to-apply.md):

1. Fill in the application form at <https://exponential.earth/partner/apply>.
2. Add supporting information.
3. The partner team reviews the application and decides within 10 working days (the page also says "within 5–10
   business days").
4. **Pay first**: buy the partnership in the 7x store, <https://my.se7enx.com/store/exponential-partners>. The Student
   plan has its own store page there (`.../exponential-student-partner-plan`).
5. **Then sign** the agreement and e-mail it to <partners@exponential.earth>.
6. The partnership is activated.

If an application is not approved, you may apply again after 180 days.

## What is not described

The partner pages say plainly what they cannot answer:

- "What 'sandbox access' includes is not described; ask the partner team"
  ([Student and education partners](../partners/student-and-education-partners.md#the-student-level-as-published)).
- Whether response hours are business or clock hours ([Partner plans and prices](../partners/partner-plans-and-prices.md#the-plans)).
- Tax treatment of a subscription ([Partner plans and prices](../partners/partner-plans-and-prices.md#invoices-and-taxes)).

Ask the partner team, <partners@exponential.earth> or <https://exponential.earth/partner/contact-us>, before you
pay if any of these matters to you. The Student level is a purchase of services, not a certification and not a
scholarship.

## Moving up

The education page describes the way from student to professional: learn with the help and a local installation,
contribute, deliver a first project, then "Move to the Now or Starter level when you sell services"
([Student and education partners](../partners/student-and-education-partners.md#growing-from-student-to-professional)).
Tiers are reviewed monthly; "You will always be placed in the highest tier for which you meet the requirements"
([Partner levels](../partners/partner-levels.md#moving-between-levels)).

Compare the level with what is free for everyone before you decide:
[Student level or free community: what to choose](student-level-or-free-community.md).
