# Project 10: capstone, a complete small site

The capstone joins the earlier projects into one site that you plan, build, document and present. It is the
strongest piece of a learner's portfolio. Do it alone or in a team of two to four, with each member owning parts.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Level | Advanced |
| Time | 2 to 4 days of work, spread over two to four weeks |
| Paths | All |
| Prerequisites | At least four of projects 1 to 9, including the ones of your path ([Choosing a learning path](choosing-a-learning-path.md#study-projects-per-path)) |

## Goal

A working site on a fresh study installation that a reader can rebuild from your documentation, with a plan, a
content model, a structure, a look, at least one page built with layouts, a second language, roles for its editors,
a working search, a backup and a short presentation.

## Choose a brief

Pick one, or agree your own with your teacher or group. All content is invented.

| Brief | Content |
|---|---|
| A school newspaper | Articles, authors, sections, an events calendar |
| A club or association | News, events, members' pages, a join page |
| A small museum or library | Collection items, exhibitions, opening hours, two languages |
| A conference | Talks, speakers, a schedule, venue information |

## Milestones

| # | Milestone | Uses | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Plan: audience, pages, content model on paper, roles | All learning paths | A two-page plan |
| 2 | Fresh installation, release noted, backup routine tried | [Resetting and rebuilding your study site](resetting-and-rebuilding-your-study-site.md) | Commands and output |
| 3 | Content classes | [project 1](project-01-a-content-class-of-your-own.md) | Class pages |
| 4 | Structure and content | [project 2](project-02-a-small-site-structure.md) | Site map |
| 5 | Look: an override or a design extension | [project 3](project-03-a-template-override.md) | Files in Git |
| 6 | A landing page with layouts | [project 4](project-04-a-page-with-layouts.md) | Layout and mapping |
| 7 | A second language | [project 5](project-05-a-multilingual-site.md) | `SiteLanguageList` output |
| 8 | Roles for editors | [project 9](project-09-roles-for-a-restricted-editor.md) | Role page, tests |

Write a short entry in your [study journal](keeping-a-study-journal.md) at each milestone.

## Requirements by learning path

Everyone does milestones 1 to 10. In addition, by path:

| Path | Additional requirement |
|---|---|
| Editor | Ten or more published items with images and relations; a style guide for editors of the site |
| Administrator | A written operations note: caches, logs, backups, updates ([Update with Git](../update-with-git.md)); two-step sign-in for the editors |
| Developer | An extension of your own ([project 8](project-08-your-first-extension.md)) or a script that reads the site over the REST API ([project 7](project-07-reading-content-over-the-rest-api.md)) |
| Designer | A design extension with overrides for at least two view modes, checked with the keyboard only and in a narrow window |

## Hand in

1. **The plan** and **the final report** (four to eight pages): what you built, how, what changed from the plan, the
   pages you used, and the three hardest problems.
2. **A rebuild guide**: the commands and clicks that turn a fresh installation into your site. A fellow learner tries
   it; record what they needed to ask.
3. **Your Git repositories**: design or extension, scripts, notes. No `settings/override/`, passwords or keys.
4. **Screenshots** of every milestone, without secrets or personal data.
5. **The release** you built on.

## Presentation

Ten minutes, live or recorded:

1. The brief and the audience (1 minute).
2. A tour of the site as a visitor (3 minutes).
3. A tour as an editor of the site, signed in as the restricted editor (3 minutes).
4. One technical decision and why (2 minutes).
5. What you would do next (1 minute).

Never show passwords or keys on screen. If you present on a shared or public demo installation, remember that its
content may be reset and that others may change it ([Trying the public demo](trying-the-public-demo.md)).

## Rubric

Score the four common criteria of [Grading your own work](grading-your-own-work.md) twice: once for the site and
once for the documentation (0 to 24 in total). Add these checks (10):

1. The plan existed before the build, and the report compares them.
2. The site runs on a fresh installation of a named release.
3. At least one class of the learner's own design is used.
4. No core file was edited; overrides and extensions only.
5. A layout is published and mapped.
6. A second language is shown on a siteaccess.
7. Editors work with a restricted role, not the administrator.
8. The search test plan passes or explains its failures.
9. A fellow learner rebuilt the site from the guide.
10. The path's additional requirement is met.

For bands, double the thresholds of [Grading your own work](grading-your-own-work.md#from-scores-to-a-grade). The
trainers' capstones ([Capstone projects](../trainers/assessments/module-06-capstone-projects/README.md)) are a good
model if your teacher wants a stricter format.
