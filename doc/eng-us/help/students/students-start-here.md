# Students and learners: start here

This section of the help is for **people who are learning Exponential**: students at schools and universities,
apprentices, career changers, self-taught developers and designers, and new team members who want to learn properly.
It gives you learning paths built from the existing help, a study site of your own, graded practice projects, and
honest answers about certificates and the partner program.

Everything you need to learn is free: Exponential is free software under the GNU GPL version 2 or later, and this
help is free documentation under the GNU FDL 1.3. Every fact on these pages comes from the help, the code or the
settings, and the pages say plainly where something is not documented.

> **Note**
> There is no official Exponential certification for learners documented today. The study projects here are graded
> by you, your study group or your teacher, and trainers may issue certificates in their own name; neither is an
> official certification ([Certifications and certificates](certifications-and-certificates.md)).

4. **Look around** (15 minutes): the first stops of [Your first hour in the administration](your-first-hour-in-the-administration.md).

## Rules for learners

1. **Practice on a study site**, never on a live site or one with real data.
2. **Invented data only.** Test accounts use addresses at `example.invalid`; no exercise sends mail to a real person.
3. **No secrets in notes, commits or screenshots**: no passwords, keys, `settings/override/` or `kickstart.ini`.
4. **Use the right names.** Exponential is the product, 7x the company, the Exponential Foundation the nonprofit;
   the old name is history.
5. **Make honest claims.** Never call yourself "Exponential certified"; describe what you did and on which release.

- In the help, filter by the audience **Students and learners** or the category **Learning**, or open **All pages**
