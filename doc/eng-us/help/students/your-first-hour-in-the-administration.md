# Your first hour in the administration

This page is a guided tour for your first hour on your own study site. It follows
[Getting started](../getting-started.md) and [A tour of the administration](../admin-tour.md), and adds a small
observation task to each stop. Write your answers in your [study journal](keeping-a-study-journal.md).

You need a study site ([Your first site: three ways](your-first-site-three-ways.md)) and its administrator sign-in.

## Sign in (5 minutes)

Open the admin login address the installer printed, for example `http://localhost:8087/admin/user/login`, and sign
in as `admin` ([Getting started](../../guides/getting-started.md#5-first-login)). Then change the password through the
**Change password** link in the user menu, if you have not done so yet.

**Observe:** which address did you use, and which siteaccess does it belong to? (`admin`.)

## The header (10 minutes)

From left to right: the logo, the tabs, **Search...**, the globe button that opens the site or another siteaccess,
the sun button for the color mode, the person button with the user menu, and **Logout**
([A tour of the administration](../admin-tour.md#the-header)).

**Observe:**

1. Which tabs do you see? Note them all. The administrator sees everything; an editor sees fewer, because "a person
   only sees the entries their roles allow" ([Getting started](../../guides/getting-started.md#5-first-login)).
2. Press `/` anywhere: where does the cursor go?
3. Switch the color mode twice and back.

## The columns (10 minutes)

- The **left column** holds the tree in **Content structure** and **Media**, the **Trash** at the bottom of the tree,
  and the **Content finder** below it.
- The **right column** holds small tools: **Clear cache**, **Bookmarks**, **Interface**, **Current user**.

Source: [A tour of the administration](../admin-tour.md#the-left-column).

**Observe:** open three folders in the tree. Click the icon in front of an item's name. Which actions does its menu
offer?

## The dashboard (5 minutes)

The dashboard shows a greeting with quick actions, numbers (content items, recent publishing, your drafts, your
pending items), a small publishing chart and short lists of content ([A tour of the administration](../admin-tour.md#the-dashboard)).

**Observe:** how many content items does your study site have? Write the number down; you will compare it after
your first study project.

## An item's page (10 minutes)

Open an article from the tree. Its page shows the path, the name and type, the buttons (**Edit**, **Move**,
**Copy**, **Remove** and more), a line with the **Node** and **Object** numbers, tabs such as **Details**,
**Translations**, **Locations** and **Relations**, and the **Sub items** ([A tour of the administration](../admin-tour.md#an-items-page)).

**Observe:**

1. Note the node number and the object number. Why are there two? (A content object can appear in the tree at
   several places; each place is a node. See [Understanding the content model](understanding-the-content-model.md).)
2. Open the **Details** tab. Which section is the item in?
3. Type the node number into the **Content finder** and press **Find**. Where does it take you?

## A first change (10 minutes)

1. On the article's page press **Edit**.
2. Change the title and press **Send for publishing**.
3. Open the site (the globe button) and find the article. Is the new title there?
4. Back in the administration, open **Manage versions** and look at the versions of the article
   ([Publishing, versions and the trash](../publishing-and-versions.md)).

If the new title does not show in a list on the site, the content cache may still hold the old page; the guide's
remedy is to clear the content cache once ([Getting started](../../guides/getting-started.md#6-first-content),
[Understanding caches](understanding-caches.md)).

**Observe:** how many versions does the article have now, and which one is published?

## The help and signing out (10 minutes)

- Open **Help**. Filter by an audience, then search for a word you met today
  ([Getting started](../getting-started.md#getting-help)).
- Sign out with the last button of the header. While an item is open for editing the button is grayed out: finish
  or leave the edit first ([Getting started](../getting-started.md#5-sign-out)).

**Observe:** write three words you met today that you could not explain. Look them up in the
[Glossary for learners](glossary-for-learners.md).
