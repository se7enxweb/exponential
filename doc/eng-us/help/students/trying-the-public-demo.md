# Trying the public demo

The quickest way to see Exponential is the project's public demo. You need only a browser. This page tells you where
it is, what to try, and why you will soon want a site of your own.

## Where the demo is

| What | Address | Source |
|---|---|---|
| The site | [demo.exponential.earth](https://demo.exponential.earth/) | [Community channels](../project/community-channels.md#try-the-demo) |
| The administration | [edit.demo.exponential.earth](https://edit.demo.exponential.earth/) | same |
| The sign-in details | The website's [community page](https://exponential.earth/about/community) | same |

The project describes it as "a public demo of the latest Exponential 6 with the 7x Simple design and a shop", and
says that everything in the demo is reset every day ([Community channels](../project/community-channels.md#try-the-demo),
[About the project](../project/about-the-project.md#the-public-demo)).

This page does not print the sign-in details. Take them from the community page; the administrator password of a
public demo is published on purpose, as [Demo mode FAQ](../demo-mode-faq.md) explains.

## Demo mode in one minute

A public demo runs in **demo mode**. You will see a yellow **Demo mode** banner in the administration. In demo mode,
administrator passwords cannot be changed or reset, so the published sign-in keeps working for the next visitor.
Everything else still works: you can edit content, create accounts and change settings, which is why the demo is
rebuilt on a schedule ([Demo mode FAQ](../demo-mode-faq.md#what-does-it-not-block),
[Demo mode](../demo-mode.md)).

## What to try in your first hour

1. **Look at the site** as a visitor: the front page, a list, an article, the search.
2. **Sign in** to the administration with the published details. Read the dashboard
   ([Getting started](../getting-started.md#2-look-around)).
3. **Open Content structure** and find the item behind a page you saw on the site.
4. **Edit** that item, change its title and **Send for publishing**. Reload the site.
5. **Look at the versions** of the item, and go back to the earlier one
   ([Publishing, versions and the trash](../publishing-and-versions.md)).
6. **Open the Layouts tab**, if it is shown, and look at a layout in the editor without publishing
   ([The layouts editor](../layouts-editor.md)).
7. **Open the help** in the administration and search for a word you met.

Write what you tried and what surprised you in your [study journal](keeping-a-study-journal.md).

## What the demo cannot teach you

| You cannot | Because | Do it on |
|---|---|---|
| Install, reinstall or update | The demo is installed for you | [Your own study site](installing-a-study-site-in-one-command.md) |
| Use the command line | The demo has no terminal for visitors | Your own study site |
| Change administrator passwords | Demo mode blocks it | Your own study site |
| Keep your work | The demo is reset every day | Your own study site, with your work in Git |
| Work undisturbed | Other visitors use the same demo at the same time | Your own study site |

After one or two sessions on the demo, move on: [Your first site: three ways](your-first-site-three-ways.md).

## Good manners on a shared demo

- Use invented content. Do not enter personal data, yours or anyone else's.
- Do not upload anything you do not have the right to share.
- Do not remove or break things for the sake of it; other learners use the demo too.
- If something on the demo looks broken, report it on the
  [community issue tracker](https://github.com/se7enxweb/exponential-community/issues), which is for "the project,
  the website, the ecosystem" ([When something goes wrong](../when-something-goes-wrong.md#filing-a-bug-in-exponential)).
