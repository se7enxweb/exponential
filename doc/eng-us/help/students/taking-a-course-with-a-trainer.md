# Taking a course with a trainer

The help contains complete courses for trainers, built from modules and lessons, with exercises and quizzes
([Trainers: start here](../trainers/trainers-start-here.md)). If you take one of these courses, this page helps you
get the most out of it. If you study alone, you can still read every lesson.

## How a course is built

```text
Course    a course overview: who it is for, the modules, a reading path, schedules
  Module    a module overview: the lessons, preparing the module, a timing plan
    Lesson    one page: at a glance, objectives, trainer's notes, exercise, expected result, quiz, further reading
```

Source: [How a course is built](../trainers/trainers-start-here.md#how-a-course-is-built) on the trainers' start page.
Each lesson has a duration that includes its exercise, and ends with a quiz and its answers.

## Before the course

- Read the course overview: who it is for, the outcome, the prerequisites.
- Check what the trainer asks you to bring. In Editors 101, for example, learners need "a computer with a current
  browser; no knowledge of Exponential" ([Editors 101](../trainers/editors-101/README.md#prerequisites)).
- Do the matching steps of your learning path to arrive with questions ([Choosing a learning path](choosing-a-learning-path.md)).
- Start a [study journal](keeping-a-study-journal.md) for the course.

## During the course

- **Do every exercise yourself**, on the training installation, in your own folder or on your own site. Trainers use
  training installations, never live sites or real customer data
  ([Trainers: start here](../trainers/trainers-start-here.md#the-rules-every-trainer-keeps)).
- **Compare with the expected result** of each lesson; ask when yours differs.
- **Use test addresses** at `example.invalid`; no exercise should send mail to a real person.
- **Note the lesson page** of each exercise, so you can repeat it later.

## Assessments and certificates

Many courses end with a written test and a practical task, and longer programs with a capstone
([Assessments and practical exercises](../trainers/assessments/README.md)). They are the trainer's own assessment.
If you pass, the trainer may give you a certificate of completion in their own name; if you attended, a certificate
of attendance. Neither is an official certification by 7x or the Exponential Foundation
([Certifications and certificates](certifications-and-certificates.md)).

You have a right to fair treatment: the assessment lessons ask trainers to give accommodations where needed and to
give feedback on results ([Accommodations in assessment](../trainers/assessments/module-01-designing-assessments/accommodations-in-assessment.md),
[Giving feedback on results](../trainers/assessments/module-01-designing-assessments/giving-feedback-on-results.md)).
If you need an accommodation, tell the trainer before the assessment.

## After the course

- Answer each lesson's quiz again a week later ([How to study with the help](how-to-study-with-the-help.md#review-with-quizzes)).
- Do the matching [study projects](study-projects-overview.md) on your own site.
- Take the next course in the suggested order, or follow your learning path.
- Join the community channels the trainer points to ([Asking for help and study groups](asking-for-help-and-study-groups.md)).

## Finding a trainer

The help does not keep a list of trainers. Training centers appear in the partner program's Distribution profile, and
what makes a course "official" is not published: "to be defined by the Foundation (or 7x)"
([Training partners](../partners/training-partners.md#training-in-the-program)). Ask:

- your school, university or employer, who may run courses from this material;
- the community channels ([Community channels](../project/community-channels.md));
- the partner team, for partners who train (<https://exponential.earth/partner/contact-us>).

If you become good at this, you can teach it yourself: [Train the trainer](../trainers/train-the-trainer/README.md).
