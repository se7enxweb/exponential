# Student level or free community: what to choose

Most of what a learner needs to learn Exponential is free. The [Student partner level](the-student-partner-level.md)
adds some things for $25 a month. This page puts both side by side, so you can decide with the facts.

## What is free for everyone

| Resource | What you get | Source |
|---|---|---|
| The software | Free software under the GNU GPL version 2 or later: read, run and change all of it | [Student and education partners](../partners/student-and-education-partners.md#why-exponential-suits-teaching) |
| This documentation | Free documentation under the GNU FDL 1.3: copy it into notes, translate, improve | [Free documentation](../free-documentation.md) |
| The trainers' courses | Complete courses with exercises and quizzes, readable by anyone | [Course catalogue](../trainers/course-catalogue.md) |
| The public demo | A site and administration to try, reset every day | [Trying the public demo](trying-the-public-demo.md) |
| Community help | Forums, Telegram, Discord, Slack; no promised response time | [Getting support](../project/getting-support.md#free-help-from-the-community) |
| Public issue trackers | Bug reports and questions for anyone with a free GitHub account | [When something goes wrong](../when-something-goes-wrong.md#filing-a-bug-in-exponential) |
| The original training books | Mirrored by 7x, under the GNU FDL | [Training partners](../partners/training-partners.md#free-material-to-build-on) |
| Contributing | Pull requests, translations and documentation, with public review | [Contributing as a student](contributing-as-a-student.md) |

The project's support page says plainly that its developers "are not obligated to provide free support"
([Support levels and response times](../partners/support-levels-and-response-times.md)).

## What the Student level adds, as published

From [The Student partner level](the-student-partner-level.md):

- 2 GitHub issue tickets a week, with a response time of ≤ 36 h on the benefits page (the program page says
  "Community only");
- 1 guidance meeting a quarter;
- quarterly partner meetings, a dedicated page on share.exponential.earth, OEM resale opportunities;
- discounts: 4% on subscription products and 20% + K.O.P. on knowledge products (0% on 7x services);
- "sandbox access, educational resources, and training support", whose content is not described.

## Side by side

| Need | Free community | Student level |
|---|---|---|
| Learn the product | Yes: software, docs, courses, demo | Same, plus "educational resources" (not described) |
| A place to practice | Your own study site, the public demo | Plus "sandbox access" (not described) |
| Answers to questions | Community channels, no promised time | Partner tickets with a published response time |
| A conversation with the program | No | 1 guidance meeting a quarter, quarterly partner meetings |
| Visibility | Your own portfolio and public contributions | A dedicated page on share.exponential.earth |
| Discounts on books, training and certifications | No | 20% + K.O.P. on knowledge products |
| An official certification | No | No; official certifications are planned and not available yet ([Certifications and certificates](certifications-and-certificates.md)) |

## Questions to ask yourself

1. Will I use two tickets a week, or is the community enough while I learn?
2. Do I want a guidance meeting each quarter, and what would I ask in it?
3. Do I plan to buy knowledge products (books, training) where the discount matters?
4. Do I want a page on share.exponential.earth now, or after my capstone?
5. Can I commit to a year? The requirements page says "Partnership lasts one year"
   ([Partner requirements](../partners/partner-requirements.md)).

If most answers are "no", start free and revisit after your first projects. If you study at a school or university,
ask whether it is, or wants to become, an education partner ([Student and education partners](../partners/student-and-education-partners.md)).

## Questions to ask the partner team

Write to <partners@exponential.earth> or use <https://exponential.earth/partner/contact-us>:

- What does "sandbox access" include for a Student partner?
- What do "educational resources" and "training support" include?
- Are the response hours business hours or clock hours, and in which time zone?
- Can an individual student apply, or only an institution?
- Is a one-year commitment required, and how are renewals handled?

Keep the answers in writing, and in your [study journal](keeping-a-study-journal.md).
