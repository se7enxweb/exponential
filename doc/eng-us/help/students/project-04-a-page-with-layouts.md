# Project 4: a page with layouts

Exponential Layouts lets editors and site builders arrange pages from blocks in zones. In this project you build the
landing page of your student club from [project 2](project-02-a-small-site-structure.md) with the layouts editor,
publish it and map it to one page.

| Prerequisites | A study site with the **Layouts** tab (the default site package uses the `explayouts` extensions, [Site packages for learners](site-packages-for-learners.md)); [project 2](project-02-a-small-site-structure.md) |

If you do not see the **Layouts** tab, your role may not work with layouts, or the site was installed without the
layouts extensions ([The layouts editor](../layouts-editor.md)).

## Goal

A published layout for the "Student club" folder with at least two zones and five blocks, including a list of your
news articles, mapped so that only that page uses it.

## Before you start

Read [The layouts editor](../layouts-editor.md) and [How do I build a page with layouts?](../manual/how-do-i-build-a-page-with-layouts.md).
Learn the words: **layout**, **layout type**, **zone**, **block**, **mapping** (also called a rule) and **shared
layout**.

## Tasks

1. **Open the dashboard.** The **Layouts** tab opens the **Layouts Dashboard** with the numbers of layouts, zones,
   blocks and mappings. Note them.
2. **Create a layout.** Press **New layout**, choose a layout type with at least two zones (for example *2 columns*
   or *Sidebar left*), name it "Student club landing" and press **Create layout**.
3. **Add blocks.** Press **Add a block** and drag at least five blocks into the zones: a **Title**, a **Rich text**,
   an **Image**, and a block from the **Listing** group that shows your news articles.
4. **Configure them.** Select each block; fill its **Content** tab (the text, or which items the list shows and how
   many) and look at its **Design** tab.
5. **Save a draft.** Use **Save draft and close**. Open the layout again: your work is there, and the site is
   unchanged, because you always work on a draft.
6. **Publish** with **Publish layout**.
7. **Map it.** In **Rules** (the mappings), add a mapping with **New mapping** whose target is the "Student club"
   folder only. Check that it is **Enabled**. The first enabled mapping by priority whose targets and conditions
   match a page wins.
8. **Check on the site.** Open the folder on the public site: the page shows your layout. Open another page: it does
   not. If the editor asks whether to clear caches after publishing, choose **Clear caches**.
9. **Look at the page's Layouts tab** in the administration: it shows the layout the page resolves to and the mappings
   applied.

All steps follow [The layouts editor](../layouts-editor.md).

## Hand in

- Screenshots: the layout in the editor with its zones and blocks; the mapping with its target; the public page; the
  page's **Layouts** tab.
- The layout's name, type, and the numbers on the dashboard before and after.
- A short report: what each block shows, why you chose the layout type, and how the mapping decides where the
  layout is used.

## Rubric

Score the four common criteria of [Grading your own work](grading-your-own-work.md), and these checks (7):

1. The layout has at least two zones in use and five blocks.
2. A listing block shows the learner's own articles.
3. A draft was saved and reopened before publishing.
4. The layout is published.
5. One enabled mapping targets only the club folder.
6. Another page is shown to be unaffected.
7. The report uses the words layout, zone, block and mapping correctly.

## Stretch goals

- Link a zone of your layout to a zone of a **shared layout** (the button at the bottom of the left side of the
  editor), and explain why a header built once can serve many pages
  ([Shared layouts and linked zones](../../bc/6.0/LAYOUTS.md#shared-layouts-and-linked-zones)).
- Add a mapping with a condition (for example a content type) and explain which mapping wins.
- Follow the trainers' [Exponential Layouts](../trainers/layouts/README.md) course, module 7, on mapping rules.
