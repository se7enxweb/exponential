# For teachers: assigning these pages

This page is for teachers, lecturers and course leaders who want to use Exponential in a class. The student pages
are written for learners; the trainers' section is written for people who teach
([Trainers: start here](../trainers/trainers-start-here.md)). This page shows how to combine them.

## A semester plan

An example for a 14-week web development course with about four hours a week:

| Weeks | Topic | Pages |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Study habits, a first site | [How to study with the help](how-to-study-with-the-help.md), [Installing a study site in one command](installing-a-study-site-in-one-command.md) |
| 2 to 3 | Editing and the content model | [Learning path: editor](learning-path-editor.md), [project 2](project-02-a-small-site-structure.md) |
| 4 | Content classes | [project 1](project-01-a-content-class-of-your-own.md) |
| 5 to 6 | Templates and overrides | [Learning the template language](learning-the-template-language.md), [project 3](project-03-a-template-override.md) |
| 7 | Layouts | [project 4](project-04-a-page-with-layouts.md) |
| 8 | Languages and roles | [project 5](project-05-a-multilingual-site.md), [project 9](project-09-roles-for-a-restricted-editor.md) |
| 9 to 10 | Extensions and the REST API | [project 8](project-08-your-first-extension.md), [project 7](project-07-reading-content-over-the-rest-api.md) |
| 11 | Git and a first contribution | [Using Git as a student](using-git-as-a-student.md), [Your first contribution, step by step](your-first-contribution-step-by-step.md) |
| 12 to 14 | Capstone and presentations | [project 10](project-10-capstone-a-complete-small-site.md) |

For design or editorial programs, swap weeks 9 to 11 for the designer or editor path.

## The lab

- Prefer one installation per learner: "nobody can break a neighbour's exercise, and every learner can be an
  administrator" ([One installation per learner](../trainers/train-the-trainer/module-02-building-the-lab/one-install-per-learner.md)).
- Shared installations need accounts per learner and, if all are administrators, demo mode
  ([Sharing one study site with a class](sharing-one-study-site-with-a-class.md)).
- No exercise may send mail to a real person; test accounts use `example.invalid`.
- The trainers' [Module 2: Building the lab](../trainers/train-the-trainer/module-02-building-the-lab/README.md) covers
  requirements, getting the code for a class, serving and resetting.

## Grading

- Each study project has a rubric with four common criteria and project checks
  ([Grading your own work](grading-your-own-work.md)). You may change the bands.
- Ask for the release in every hand-in; features change between releases.
- A 0 in "It is safe" should mean "not yet": secrets in a repository or unescaped output must be fixed first.
- Peer review works well before your own grading ([Grading your own work](grading-your-own-work.md#peer-review)).

## Copying and adapting the pages

The help is free documentation under the GNU FDL 1.3 or later. You may copy it into course notes, translate and adapt
it, if you keep the notices and license your material under the same license
([Free documentation](../free-documentation.md), [Training partners](../partners/training-partners.md#free-material-to-build-on)).
Improvements you make are welcome back as pull requests ([Improving the help as a student](improving-the-help-as-a-student.md)).

## Certificates and claims

You may give your learners a certificate of attendance or completion in your own or your institution's name, with a
statement that it is not an official certification by 7x or the Exponential Foundation
([Lesson 1.7: Completion certificates you issue in your own name](../trainers/assessments/module-01-designing-assessments/completion-certificates-you-issue.md)).
Do not describe your course as "official" training: what makes a course official is not published
([Training partners](../partners/training-partners.md#training-in-the-program),
[Certifications and certificates](certifications-and-certificates.md)).

## Your institution and the partner program

The partner program has a Student level "For students, universities, or learning institutions", at $25 a month
([The Student partner level](the-student-partner-level.md)). The education page suggests that teachers use the help as
reading assignments, plan exercises that end in a real contribution reviewed in public, and tell the community about
their course ([Student and education partners](../partners/student-and-education-partners.md#for-teachers)).
