# Certifications and certificates

Many learners ask: "Can I get certified in Exponential?" This page answers it plainly, with the sources.

> **In short**
> There is **no official Exponential certification for students or individual learners** documented today. The
> Exponential Partner Program names certifications for partner teams, but their syllabus, exam, price and examiners are
> not published. A trainer may give you a certificate of attendance or completion **in the trainer's own name**; it is
> not an official certification.

## What the partner program publishes

From [Certifications](../partners/certifications.md#what-is-published), quoting the program pages (read 18 October
2026):

- "For Silver tier and above, partners must hold valid certifications."
- Profile certifications: "Exponential Developer Certification (for technical teams)", "Exponential Design
  Certification (for design/ux teams)", "Exponential Solution Certification (for content-focused teams)".
- "Certifications: Required for Silver and higher. Types: Developer, WCMS, Solution, Sales. ... All certifications
  can be earned in 1 day."
- "Silver, Gold and Platinum partners are certified and can use the 'Exponential certified' logo on their website and
  in their marketing material."

These are certifications for **partner companies** at Silver level and above. The Student partner level has none
([Partner requirements](../partners/partner-requirements.md)).

## What is not published

[Certifications](../partners/certifications.md#what-is-not-published) lists what is "to be defined by the Foundation
(or by 7x, which runs the program)":

- the syllabus and learning objectives of each certification;
- the exam format, the pass mark and the languages;
- the price, outside the Kick-Off Package for Basic;
- the dates and places;
- how long a certification stays valid;
- who may examine, and whether the Foundation endorses the certificates.

So you cannot book an exam today from what is documented. The partner team's contact form is
<https://exponential.earth/partner/contact-us>.

## Certificates from trainers

The trainers' material includes assessments: written tests with answer keys, practical tasks with rubrics, and
capstones ([Assessments and practical exercises](../trainers/assessments/README.md)). The help is explicit about what
they are:

> "Everything in this course is the trainer's own assessment, not an official certification; the partner program's
> certifications are separate and their details are not published."

A trainer may give you:

| Certificate | Means | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Certificate of attendance | You were there | [Certificates of attendance in your own name](../trainers/train-the-trainer/module-06-assessment-and-follow-up/certificates-of-attendance.md) |
| Certificate of completion | You passed the trainer's assessment | [Lesson 1.7: Completion certificates you issue in your own name](../trainers/assessments/module-01-designing-assessments/completion-certificates-you-issue.md) |

An honest certificate of completion names the issuer (the trainer or the organization), your name and nothing else
about you, the course and its hours, the parts passed, the date, the release taught, and this kind of statement:
"This certificate is issued by `<issuer>`. It is not an official certification by 7x or the Exponential Foundation."
It does not show scores.

If a certificate you receive claims more, for example "Official" or "Certified by 7x", ask the issuer to correct it.

| Evidence | Why it convinces | Page |

| Graded study projects and a capstone | Real work on a named release | [Study projects: overview](study-projects-overview.md) |

| A portfolio that links all of this | One place for an employer to look | [Building a portfolio](building-a-portfolio.md) |

## Words you must not use

About yourself, on a CV, a profile or a portfolio, unless an official certification is one day published and you
have passed it:

- "Exponential Certified" or "Certified Exponential Developer";
- "Certified by 7x", "Exponential Foundation certified", "official certification";
- logos of 7x, the Foundation or the partner program.

Instead, write what is true: "Completed *Editors 101: editing with Exponential* (trainer's assessment, passed,
release 6.0.15)" or "Built and documented a multilingual site with Exponential 6.0.15 (capstone project)". The same
care is asked of everyone who speaks about Exponential ([Claims you must not make](../advocates/claims-you-must-not-make.md)).
