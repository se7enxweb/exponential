# Asking for help and study groups

Nobody learns alone. This page shows where learners ask, how to ask well, and how to study in a group.

## Look it up first

From [Getting support](../project/getting-support.md#first-look-it-up), in this order:

1. **This help**, with its search.
2. **The documentation website**, [exponential.doc.exponential.earth](https://exponential.doc.exponential.earth/).
3. **eZpedia**, [ezpedia.exponential.earth](https://ezpedia.exponential.earth/), the community wiki.
4. **The articles on Share Exponential**, [share.exponential.earth/learn](https://share.exponential.earth/learn).

Also search the issue trackers for the exact error message.

## Where to ask

The free channels the project's support page names ([Getting support](../project/getting-support.md#free-help-from-the-community)):

| Channel | Good for | Address |
|---|---|---|
| Share Exponential forums | Questions that need a longer answer, that others will search for later | <https://share.exponential.earth/forums> |
| Telegram | Quick questions in real time | <https://t.me/exponentialcms> |
| Discord | Chat with the community, development talk | <https://discord.gg/zXzGMgfzej> |
| Slack | Chat, especially about development | <https://exponentialcms.slack.com/> |

Community help has no promised response time: the developers "are not obligated to provide free support"
([Support levels and response times](../partners/support-levels-and-response-times.md)). Be patient and kind. More
places: [Community channels](../project/community-channels.md).

## How to ask

From [Getting support](../project/getting-support.md#how-to-ask-so-you-get-an-answer):

- **Say what you run**: Exponential version, PHP version, web server, database. **Setup > System information**
  gives a summary to copy, without passwords, keys or server paths.
- **Say what you did, what you expected and what happened**, with the exact message.
- **Show what you tried** and what you already read.
- **Report back** when you solved it, so the next person finds the answer.

A template:

```text
I am learning Exponential (<release>, PHP <x>, <web server>, SQLite).
I am doing <page or project>, step <n>.
I ran / clicked: <...>
I expected: <...>
I got: <exact message>
I already tried: <...> and read <page>.
```

## What never to post

Passwords, keys, session cookies, database addresses, or personal data of anyone. Security problems go privately to
the address in `SECURITY.md`, never into a channel ([Bugs you find while learning](bugs-you-find-while-learning.md#security-problems-stay-private)).

## Study groups

A group of three to six learners works well:

| Role | Rotates weekly |
|---|---|
| Host | Picks the page or project, keeps time |
| Driver | Shares the screen and types; others guide |
| Scribe | Writes the group's notes and open questions |
| Checker | Compares each result with the page's "Expected" |

A 90-minute session:

1. 10 minutes: each person says what they did since last time.
2. 50 minutes: one project or learning path step, together.
3. 20 minutes: open questions; decide which to ask in the community, and who asks.
4. 10 minutes: plan the next session.

Every member works on their own study site; a shared site needs care ([Sharing one study site with a class](sharing-one-study-site-with-a-class.md)).
Peer review of study projects is described in [Grading your own work](grading-your-own-work.md#peer-review).

## Giving back

When you know the answer to someone else's question, answer it, with a link to the help page. Answering is one of the
ways to support the project ([Helping others in the community](../supporters/helping-in-the-community.md)), and it
shows you what you have really understood.
