# Theme designers: start here

This page is for **theme designers**: visual, interface and product designers who design the look and behavior of
sites built with Exponential, whether or not they write code. It explains what this section adds, and gives a
**task-to-page index**: for each task a theme designer has, the page to read.

> **Note**
> The [designer help](../designers/designers-start-here.md) already covers the technical side of themes in depth:
> designs and the cascade, templates, stylesheets and the packer, theme extensions, distribution, selling and
> maintenance. This section does not repeat it. It covers the **design work** around it (content, foundations,
> components, page types, process and quality) and links to the designer help wherever the build starts.

## Rules every theme design follows

These are the project's rules for designs ([Designers: start here](../designers/designers-start-here.md#rules-every-design-follows)):

- Your design lives in your own extension; shipped designs are never edited, only overridden.
- No `<style>` blocks, no `style=""` attributes, no inline scripts or `on*=` handlers.
- Colors, spacing, radius and fonts from tokens, with a dark value for every color.
- Every printed value washed, every interface text translatable.
- Tested in light and dark, at 1440 px, at 960 px with 200% zoom and at 375 px, with the keyboard.
- No `?v=` version queries on asset links.
