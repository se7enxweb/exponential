# Project brief template

This page is for owners and managers who start a project on Exponential, alone or with a partner. A good brief saves
weeks: every partner answers the same question, and your own team agrees on the goal before work starts. The brief
is short on purpose; the details go in the [Requirements checklist](requirements-checklist.md). For a fuller project
charter and a RACI matrix, see [Project templates](../../leadership/project-templates.md).

## When to use it

- At the start of a new site, a relaunch or a migration.
- To request proposals from two or three partners ([Choosing a partner](../choosing/choosing-a-partner.md)).
- To align management, marketing and IT on one page.

## The template

```text
PROJECT BRIEF - <project name>          Version <x>, <date>        Owner: <name, role>

1. ABOUT US
   <Organization, what it does, size, markets, languages.>

2. WHY THIS PROJECT
   Problem today: <what does not work>
   Goal: <what must be true when the project is done, measurable where possible>
   How we will measure it: <figure, system, period>

3. AUDIENCES
   <Customers | members | staff | partners | donors>: what each needs from the site.

4. SCOPE
   Type: <public website | online store | intranet | extranet | member site | newsletter | API for an app>
   Sites and languages: <number of sites, languages, countries>
   In scope: <...>
   Out of scope: <...>
   Existing site to migrate: <none | system and version, size>

5. CONTENT
   Main content types: <products, articles, events, documents, people ...>
   Volume: <approximate number of items, media size>
   Who writes and approves: <roles, approval steps>

6. FEATURES (details in the requirements checklist)
   Must have: <...>
   Should have: <...>
   Could have later: <...>

7. HOSTING AND OPERATIONS
   Preference: <none | managed | our own server | existing host>
   Who will run updates and backups: <...>
   Availability expectation: <business hours | always; cost of downtime per hour if known>

8. SECURITY AND DATA
   Personal data collected: <accounts, orders, forms, newsletter ...>
   Sign-in requirements: <two-step sign-in for staff | directory sign-in>
   Legal or contractual rules to meet: <...> (ask your adviser)
   Accessibility requirements: <...>

9. BUDGET AND TIMELINE
   Budget range: <one-time> and <yearly running>
   Key dates: <decision>, <start>, <launch>, <fixed events>

10. ROLES
   Decision maker: <name>   Day-to-day contact: <name>   Content owner: <name>
   Technical contact: <name>   Data protection contact: <name>

11. WHAT WE ASK FROM PROPOSALS
   - Approach and phases, team and experience with Exponential
   - One-time, recurring and third-party costs, separately
   - Hosting proposal, update and security policy, support response times
   - Ownership of work and exit terms
   - Two references we may contact
   Proposals due: <date>   Questions to: <contact>
   We will judge proposals by: <criteria and weights, see the vendor evaluation scorecard>
```

## Tips for each part

| Part | Tip | Read |
|---|---|---|
| 2 Goal | One sentence anyone can check after launch | [Return on investment of stability](../costs/roi-of-stability.md) |
| 4 Scope | Say what is out of scope; it prevents most disputes | [Planning an Exponential project](../../leadership/planning-an-exponential-project.md) |
| 5 Content | Content work is usually the largest effort; plan it early | [Content modeling workshops](../../leadership/content-modeling-workshops.md) |
| 6 Features | Check what is built in before asking for custom work | [Estimating and scoping with extensions](../../leadership/estimating-and-scoping-with-extensions.md) |
| 7 Hosting | Fill in the cost of downtime first | [Cost of downtime worksheet](../costs/cost-of-downtime-worksheet.md) |
| 8 Security | List the personal data now, not after launch | [Personal data in Exponential](../security/personal-data-in-exponential.md) |
| 9 Budget | A range gets better proposals than no figure | [Total cost of ownership](../costs/total-cost-of-ownership.md) |
| 11 Proposals | Publish your scoring criteria | [Vendor evaluation scorecard](vendor-evaluation-scorecard.md) |

## Before you send it

- [ ] Every section filled or marked "not applicable".
- [ ] Management has read and agreed the goal and the budget range.
- [ ] No confidential personal data in the brief.
- [ ] The same version sent to every candidate, on the same day.
- [ ] A date set for questions and answers, shared with all candidates.
