# Financial documents

This page is for treasurers and directors. Openness about money is the strongest argument a non-profit has with
donors. Exponential is a publishing tool here: it does not do accounting and calculates no figures. You publish what
your accounts and your auditor have produced.

> **Note**
> What you must publish depends on your country, your legal form and your funders. This page lists common practice,
> not legal duties. Ask your accountant.

## What non-profits commonly publish

| Document | Typical form |
|---|---|
| Annual financial statements (audited or reviewed) | PDF, per year |
| Annual regulatory filing | PDF, per year |
| Where the money goes | A short HTML page with a table or chart |
| Budget for the current year | PDF or HTML |
| Fundraising costs | A figure in the annual review or a page |
| Grants received and from whom | A list, if funders allow it to be public |
| Pay of the highest paid, where your law requires it | As your filing states it |

## Three ways to publish

| Way | Fits | How in Exponential |
|---|---|---|
| Documents | The official statements | File objects by year ([Annual publications as downloads](annual-publications-as-downloads.md)) |
| A summary page | Donors and the public | An article with a table and a link to the statement |
| A register kept as content | Many similar entries (grants received, grants given) | A small class of your own with one object per entry ([Spending and contract publishing](../../government/transparency-and-foi/spending-and-contract-publishing.md)) |

A register kept as content can be exported for others to use; see [Open data, RSS and REST](open-data-rss-and-rest.md).

## An HTML summary that people read

Most donors will not open a forty-page PDF. Write a one-screen summary:

- Total income and where it came from (donations, grants, other), as a small table.
- Total spending and on what (programs, fundraising, administration).
- One sentence of context per figure.
- A link to the full statement, with format and size.

Tables need header cells and should be simple ([Tables in content](../../government/accessibility/tables-in-content.md)).
If you show a chart as an image, give the figures as text too.

## Grant spending

Funders sometimes ask you to publish how their grant was spent. Use one page per grant or a register entry with:
funder, amount, period, purpose, outcome summary, and a link to the outcome document
([Grant outcomes for funders](../grants/grant-outcomes-for-funders.md)). Check the grant agreement for what you may
publish.

## Check before publishing

| Check | Why |
|---|---|
| No personal data of donors, beneficiaries or staff beyond what the law requires | Privacy ([Privacy checks before publishing data](../../government/open-data/privacy-checks-before-publishing-data.md)) |
| No commercially confidential terms of contracts | Agreements with suppliers |
| Signatures and bank details removed or redacted | Fraud risk |
| The version matches the approved, final document | Avoid publishing drafts |

Redaction is a manual step done before upload ([Redaction is a manual step](../../government/transparency-and-foi/redaction-is-a-manual-step.md)).

## Approval and timing

- Keep financial pages in a section only staff may edit ([Volunteers as editors](../volunteers/volunteers-as-editors.md#pages-only-staff-may-change)).
- Use an approval workflow so the treasurer approves before publication
  ([Approval before publishing](../../government/transparency-and-foi/approval-before-publishing.md)).
- Publish on the date the board approves, with scheduled publication if useful
  ([Scheduled publishing](../../customers/day-to-day/scheduled-publishing.md)).
- Corrections are visible, never silent ([Annual publications as downloads](annual-publications-as-downloads.md#corrections)).

## Checklist

- [ ] List of documents to publish agreed with the accountant.
- [ ] Statements as files by year; summary page in HTML.
- [ ] Personal and confidential data checked and removed.
- [ ] Treasurer approved; published on the agreed date.
- [ ] Giving page links to the summary.
- [ ] Reviewed every year ([Annual checklist](../checklists/annual-checklist.md)).
