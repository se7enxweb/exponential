# Working with a partner

This page is for directors and project leads. A partner is a company in the Exponential Partner Program that builds,
hosts, supports or trains. Many non-profits need one for the technical work; the trick is to get the work you need
while keeping ownership and control of your site.

## Partner levels as a signal

The program publishes eight levels, from Student to Platinum. The "Suits" column of the published levels gives a
sense of experience: for example Basic for "small to mid-size firms with project experience", Gold for "teams
delivering complex or large-scale Exponential projects" ([Partner levels](../../partners/partner-levels.md)).
A small non-profit site rarely needs a top-level partner; ask for relevant references instead.

## Is there non-profit pricing?

**No.** The partner program publishes one set of plans and prices, which partners pay to the program, and names no
non-profit discount ([Partner plans and prices](../../partners/partner-plans-and-prices.md),
[Partner benefits](../../partners/partner-benefits.md)). The Student level is for "students, universities, or
learning institutions" ([Partner levels](../../partners/partner-levels.md)), not for non-profits in general.

What a partner charges you is the partner's own business. Some partners may offer reduced rates or pro bono work to
non-profits; ask them directly, and get any such offer in writing. The software itself has no license fee in any case
(`README.md`, "License").

## Choosing a partner

| Ask | Why |
|---|---|
| Which Exponential sites like ours have you built or run? | Relevant experience |
| Who will do the work, and who replaces them? | Continuity |
| How do you handle updates and security releases? | Long-term health |
| Do you know accessibility testing? Data protection? | Non-profit needs |
| What will a year of running cost, after the build? | Sustainability ([Budget lines](../grants/budget-lines-for-hosting-and-maintenance.md)) |
| Will everything be in our name: domain, hosting, code, content? | Ownership |

## Keep ownership

- Domain, hosting account and donation platform in the organization's name.
- At least one administrator account held by your organization.
- Custom code under a license that lets you take it elsewhere; the software itself is GPL
  ([What you own](../../customers/what-you-own.md)).
- A handover checklist at the end of every project ([Handover checklist](../../customers/partners-and-hosts/handover-checklist.md)).

## Contributing back

Non-profits can help the project too, without money: report problems you find
([GitHub issues](../../customers/healthy/github-issues.md)), improve or translate documentation
([Contributing docs and translations](../../partners/contributing-docs-and-translations.md)), and share a case study
when you are ready. The project is operated by the nonprofit Exponential Foundation
([Who is the Exponential Foundation?](../../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md)).

## Checklist

- [ ] Partner chosen on references and continuity, not level alone.
- [ ] Any non-profit rate in writing, from the partner.
- [ ] Support, maintenance and data processing agreements signed.
- [ ] Everything registered in the organization's name.
- [ ] Yearly review in the calendar.
