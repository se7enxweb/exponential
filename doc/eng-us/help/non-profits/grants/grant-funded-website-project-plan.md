# Grant-funded website project plan

This page is for the person who writes the proposal and later runs the project. It gives a plan you can adapt: goals,
phases, deliverables, roles, a timeline, risks and the part funders care about most, what happens after the money is
spent. It names no prices and no durations as facts; the durations in the example are assumptions for you to replace.

## Start from the mission

Funders fund outcomes, not websites. Write two or three goals that link the site to your mission, each with a figure
you can actually measure:

| Goal | Output (what you build) | Outcome (what changes) | Measured by |
|---|---|---|---|
| More people find our services | Service pages in two languages | More requests through the contact form | Collected information count |
| More volunteers | A volunteer sign-up form | More applications per month | Collected information count |
| Supporters stay informed | A monthly newsletter | Confirmed subscribers | `cjw_newsletter` lists |

What Exponential can count and what needs an outside service is listed in
[Measuring outcomes with site data](measuring-outcomes-with-site-data.md).

## Phases and deliverables

| Phase | Deliverables a funder can verify | Exponential pages |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Discovery | Goals, audiences, content inventory, requirements | [Non-profit site planning](../../businesses/nonprofits/nonprofit-site-planning.md), [Site map and content inventory](../../creators/website-creators/site-map-and-content-inventory.md) |
| 2. Setup | A test installation, hosting chosen, accounts and roles | [Installing Exponential in one command](../../../features/6.0/install-in-one-command.md), [Low-budget hosting](../operations/low-budget-hosting.md) |
| 3. Build | Content model, design, forms, newsletter, donation route | [Planning your website](../../creators/website-creators/planning-your-website.md), [Donations](../donations/README.md) |
| 4. Content | Pages written, translated, images with alternative text | [Accessibility basics](../accessibility/accessibility-basics-for-non-profits.md) |
| 5. Testing | Accessibility test, form test, restore test | [Testing accessibility](../../creators/website-creators/testing-accessibility.md), [Backups and restores](../../creators/website-creators/backups-and-restores.md) |
| 6. Launch | Site live over HTTPS, launch checklist signed | [Launch checklist](../checklists/launch-checklist.md) |
| 7. Handover | Editors trained, routine documented, owner named | [Handing over to editors](../../creators/website-creators/handing-over-to-editors.md) |

Each deliverable should be something a person outside the project can check: a live address, a signed checklist, a
training attendance list, a restore test log.

## Roles

| Role | Responsible for | Often filled by |
|---|---|---|
| Project owner | Goals, budget, funder contact | Director or program manager |
| Content lead | Writing, approval, accessibility of content | Communications staff |
| Technical lead | Installation, hosting, updates, backups | A partner or a technical volunteer |
| Editors | Day-to-day content | Staff and volunteers |
| Data protection contact | Forms, newsletter, retention | A named staff member |

Write names into the [Roles and permissions worksheet](../templates/roles-and-permissions-worksheet.md). A role
nobody holds is the most common cause of a site that decays after launch.

## An example timeline

The durations are **example assumptions**, not a promise for your project:

| Months | Phases |
|---|---|
| 1 | Discovery, setup |
| 2 to 3 | Build |
| 3 to 4 | Content, testing |
| 4 | Launch |
| 5 | Handover and first monthly routine |
| 6 to end of grant | Running, measuring, reporting |

## Risks and how to reduce them

| Risk | Reduce it by |
|---|---|
| Key volunteer leaves mid-project | Documented setup, more than one administrator, written routine ([When volunteers leave](../operations/when-volunteers-leave.md)) |
| Content is late | Content plan from month 1 ([Content calendar](../../customers/day-to-day/content-calendar.md)) |
| No money left for maintenance | Maintenance lines in the budget ([Budget lines](budget-lines-for-hosting-and-maintenance.md)) |
| Personal data mishandled | Data protection plan before the forms go live ([Donor and volunteer data protection](../operations/donor-and-volunteer-data-protection.md)) |
| Nothing to report at the end | Measurements switched on before launch ([Measuring outcomes](measuring-outcomes-with-site-data.md)) |

## After the grant

Answer these in the proposal, because funders ask:

- Who owns the site, the domain and the hosting contract after the grant? (Your organization, not a contractor.)
- Who applies updates? Exponential publishes maintenance releases; see
  [Release line and support](../../customers/healthy/release-line-and-support.md).
- What does a year of running cost, and from which budget? See
  [Budgeting for upgrades and maintenance](../../businesses/costs/budgeting-for-upgrades-and-maintenance.md).
- What happens to the content if the organization closes or merges? (Export, archive, hand to a successor.)

## Plan checklist

- [ ] Two or three goals, each with a measurable outcome.
- [ ] Deliverables listed per phase, each verifiable from outside.
- [ ] Every role has a name.
- [ ] Budget includes the years after the grant.
- [ ] Measurements switched on before launch.
- [ ] Ownership of domain, hosting and content written down.
