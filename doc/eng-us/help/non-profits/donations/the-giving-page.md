# The giving page

This page is for the fundraiser or editor who writes the Donate page. It works for both donation routes
([Donations](README.md)). Exponential gives you an ordinary page, a button block and file downloads; what convinces a
donor is the content.

## An outline that works

| Part | What it says | Example wording (replace) |
|---|---|---|
| Headline | What a gift does, in one line | "Your gift keeps the help line open at night" |
| Example | One real, specific outcome | "A gift of [amount] pays for [one concrete thing]" |
| Button | Donate, above the fold | "Donate now" |
| Who we are | Legal name, registration details your law requires, a contact | |
| Where the money goes | Two or three lines, with a link to the latest annual publication | "Read our annual review (PDF)" |
| Other ways to give | Bank transfer, in kind, volunteering, legacy | |
| Privacy | Who processes the payment, a link to the privacy notice | |
| Button again | At the end | "Donate now" |

## Writing tips

- **Lead with the people you help, not with your organization.**
- **One concrete example beats three statistics.** Do not invent figures; use your own, with their source.
- **Short paragraphs, plain words.** See [Plain language](../../government/languages/plain-language.md) and
  [Writing for readability](../../government/languages/writing-for-readability.md).
- **One clear action.** Two buttons, both "Donate", not five different links.
- **Honest about fees and process.** Say where donors continue and what they will receive.
- **Exponential Intelligence can draft**, but a person checks and publishes; it writes drafts only
  ([Using AI drafts responsibly](../../customers/growth/using-ai-drafts-responsibly.md)).

## Other ways to give

Some donors prefer not to give online. List the alternatives with what they need:

| Way | What to show |
|---|---|
| Bank transfer | Account details as your bank provides them, the reference to use |
| In-kind gifts | What you accept, a contact form ([Forms with the information collector](../../creators/website-creators/forms-with-the-information-collector.md)) |
| Volunteering | A link to the volunteer form ([Volunteers](../volunteers/README.md)) |
| Regular giving | The external platform's monthly option, if you use one |
| Gifts in a will | A contact person, not a form |

## Accessibility

- The button is a real link or button with a clear text, not an image of text
  ([Link text](../../government/accessibility/link-text.md)).
- Colors meet contrast requirements ([Color and contrast](../../government/accessibility/color-and-contrast.md)).
- The PDF of the annual publication is accessible, or an HTML version exists
  ([Documents and PDFs](../../government/accessibility/documents-and-pdfs.md)).
- The whole path works with the keyboard ([Accessibility basics for non-profits](../accessibility/accessibility-basics-for-non-profits.md)).

## Giving page checklist

- [ ] Headline says what a gift does.
- [ ] One real example, no invented figures.
- [ ] Donate button above the fold and at the end.
- [ ] Legal name and registration details shown as required.
- [ ] Link to the latest annual publication.
- [ ] Other ways to give listed.
- [ ] Privacy note names the payment provider.
- [ ] Tested on a phone and with the keyboard.
