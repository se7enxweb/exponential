# Training volunteer editors

This page is for the staff member or experienced volunteer who trains new editors. You do not need a training
background. "A training session following the admin tour with the site's own content takes most editors from zero to
working" ([Handing the site over to editors, Training](../../creators/website-creators/handing-over-to-editors.md#training)).

## Before the session

- [ ] Create the volunteer's account in the right group ([Volunteers as editors](volunteers-as-editors.md)).
- [ ] Send the sign-in address and ask them to set up two-step sign-in ([Two-factor login](../../../guides/two-factor-login.md)).
- [ ] Pick one real page they will look after, and a practice folder they can play in.
- [ ] Print or share the house rules and the cheat sheet below.

## A first session of about 90 minutes

The timing is a suggestion, not a measured figure.

| Minutes | Topic | Help page |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | Why the site matters to the mission; who does what | [Non-profits: start here](../non-profits-start-here.md) |
| 15 | Signing in, the dashboard, finding content | [Logging into the editor](../../logging-into-the-editor.md), [Admin tour](../../admin-tour.md) |
| 20 | Editing a page, saving a draft, publishing | [Editing content](../../editing-content.md) |
| 10 | Images with alternative text, links with clear text | [Accessible content for editors](../../government/accessibility/accessible-content-for-editors.md) |
| 10 | Versions: compare and undo; the trash | [Publishing and versions](../../publishing-and-versions.md) |
| 10 | Approval: what "pending" means, who approves | [Approvals and review](../../customers/day-to-day/approvals-and-review.md) |
| 15 | Practice on their own page, with you watching | |

End with one real task they finish alone in the next week.

## House rules

From [Shared editing with volunteers, House rules](../../businesses/associations/shared-editing-with-volunteers.md#house-rules),
adapted for a non-profit:

- [ ] Titles short and clear; they become addresses.
- [ ] Every image with an alternative text.
- [ ] Link texts that say where they lead.
- [ ] Headings in order; no bold paragraphs as headings.
- [ ] No personal data of volunteers, donors or the people you serve on public pages.
- [ ] Photos of people only with their permission.
- [ ] Ask before changing a page another volunteer owns.
- [ ] Never change the Donate page, privacy notice or financial documents; ask staff.
- [ ] If something goes wrong, tell the coordinator; most mistakes are one click from undone.

## A one-page cheat sheet

```text
Sign in ............................ (your administration address)
My pages ........................... (folder or section)
Save without publishing ............ Store draft
Publish ............................ Send for publishing
Undo a change ...................... Versions > compare > new draft from old version
Restore something removed .......... Trash > Restore
Who approves my changes ............ (names)
Help ............................... (coordinator's name and contact)
```

The exact button names depend on the administration design; check them on your site before you print.

## The first weeks

| Week | What happens |
|---|---|
| 1 | Their first changes go through approval; the approver gives friendly feedback |
| 2 to 4 | More tasks; they follow the pages they look after to get notified of changes ([Notifications](../../../features/6.0/notifications.md)) |
| After a month | Review: keep approval, or let them publish directly |

Writing help: Exponential Intelligence can draft text in the administration, and writes drafts only; a person checks
and publishes ([Using AI drafts responsibly](../../customers/growth/using-ai-drafts-responsibly.md)).
