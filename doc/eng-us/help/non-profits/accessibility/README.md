# Accessibility for non-profits

This folder is for directors, communications staff, editors and the website creator. Many non-profits exist to serve
people who are older, disabled, ill, poor or not fluent in the main language; a site they cannot use fails the mission
itself. In many countries accessibility is also a legal duty or a funder's condition.

The compact page in the Businesses section is [Accessibility for non-profits](../../businesses/nonprofits/accessibility-for-nonprofits.md);
the full treatment for public bodies, most of which applies to non-profits too, is the
[Government accessibility section](../../government/accessibility/README.md).

## What Exponential provides

From [What Exponential provides for accessibility](../../government/accessibility/what-exponential-provides.md) and
[Accessibility for site builders](../../creators/website-creators/accessibility-for-site-builders.md):

| Part | Status |
|---|---|
| The admin4 and admin4l administration designs | Designed and regression-tested against WCAG 2.2 AA; the project rates them **Partly complies** and makes no conformance claim |
| Guidance for template authors | Language, landmarks, skip link, headings, focus, contrast, zoom |
| Image fields | Can require alternative text |
| Online editor | Writes structured rich text (headings, lists, tables) rather than free HTML |
| Right-to-left content | Marked with `lang` and `dir` |
| Content checks | Find problems in content ([Content checks](../../../features/6.0/content-checks.md)) |
| Conformance reports | **None**: there is no VPAT, no Accessibility Conformance Report and no WCAG-EM report for Exponential |

## What stays with you

| Part | Who answers for it |
|---|---|
| The public design of your site | You, or the person who builds it |
| Content: alternative texts, headings, link texts, documents | Your editors |
| PDFs and other documents | Whoever produces them |
| Embedded services (donation forms, videos, maps, CAPTCHAs) | You choose them; test them |
| An accessibility statement | You publish it |
