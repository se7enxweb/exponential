# Grant budget worksheet

Copy this worksheet, fill in your own quotes and estimates, and attach it to the proposal or your yearly budget.
Every line is explained in [Budget lines for hosting and maintenance](../grants/budget-lines-for-hosting-and-maintenance.md).
The worksheet contains **no prices**: none are published for your situation, and the help does not invent any.

## Notes for the treasurer

- Keep quotes with the worksheet. A figure without a source is a guess.
- Revisit Part B every year; hosting and rates change.
- If a partner offers a discount, record it as theirs: the partner program publishes no non-profit pricing
  ([Working with a partner](../operations/working-with-a-partner.md)).
