# Non-profit templates and worksheets

This folder holds worksheets to copy and fill in. Each one is plain text and tables, so it works in any word
processor or spreadsheet. Nothing in them is a price or a fact about your organization; you supply those.

## Contents

1. [The worksheets](#the-worksheets)

3. [More templates elsewhere in the help](#more-templates-elsewhere-in-the-help)

## How to use them

1. Copy the worksheet into a document your team shares.
2. Fill it in with the people who will do the work, not alone.
3. Keep the filled-in copy with your project files; it is the record of what was decided and why.
4. Review it once a year with the [Annual checklist](../checklists/annual-checklist.md).

> **Tip**
> A filled-in worksheet is good evidence for a funder that the project was planned. Attach it to a proposal when the
> funder asks for a project plan.
