# Non-profit annual checklist

Do this once a year, ideally before the budget for the next year is set. It is a review for the director or board,
prepared by the person responsible for the site. The general version is the
[Annual review checklist](../../businesses/templates/annual-review-checklist.md).

## Contents

1. [Money and funding](#money-and-funding)
2. [Providers](#providers)
3. [Technology](#technology)

5. [Data protection](#data-protection)
6. [Accessibility](#accessibility)
7. [Transparency](#transparency)
8. [Content and goals](#content-and-goals)
9. [Sign-off](#sign-off)

## Money and funding

- [ ] Actual costs of the year compared with the budget ([Grant budget worksheet](../templates/grant-budget-worksheet.md)).
- [ ] Next year's recurring lines budgeted: hosting, domain, updates, backups, a responsible person ([Budget lines](../grants/budget-lines-for-hosting-and-maintenance.md)).
- [ ] Grant reports due this year planned, figures available ([Grant outcomes for funders](../grants/grant-outcomes-for-funders.md)).
- [ ] Donation route still the right one; provider fees reviewed ([Donation options at a glance](../donations/donation-options-at-a-glance.md)).

## Providers

- [ ] Host reviewed: uptime, support, backups, price ([Reviewing your provider each year](../../customers/partners-and-hosts/reviewing-your-provider-each-year.md)).
- [ ] Partner reviewed, agreements renewed or changed ([Working with a partner](../operations/working-with-a-partner.md)).
- [ ] Domain renewal date known; registered to the organization.

## Technology

- [ ] The site runs a maintained release; PHP version still supported ([Release line and support](../../customers/healthy/release-line-and-support.md)).
- [ ] A full restore tested this year ([Restore test](../../customers/healthy/restore-test.md)).
- [ ] `exp:audit verify` run; audit keys backed up ([Versions and the audit trail](../transparency/versions-and-the-audit-trail.md)).
- [ ] Database still fits the editing load (SQLite: one writer at a time) ([SQLite and Velocity](../operations/sqlite-and-velocity-for-small-teams.md)).

## People and access

- [ ] Every account checked against the [Roles and permissions worksheet](../templates/roles-and-permissions-worksheet.md).
- [ ] **Check user accounts** run; leavers deactivated ([When volunteers leave](../operations/when-volunteers-leave.md)).
- [ ] At least two administrators; all with two-factor login.
- [ ] Editors offered a refresher ([Training volunteer editors](../volunteers/training-volunteer-editors.md)).

## Data protection

- [ ] [Data protection checklist](data-protection-checklist.md) worked through.
- [ ] Retention plan followed; old submissions, exports and accounts removed.
- [ ] Privacy notice current: every processor named.

## Accessibility

- [ ] Key pages tested with the keyboard and an automated checker ([Accessibility basics](../accessibility/accessibility-basics-for-non-profits.md)).
- [ ] Accessibility statement reviewed and dated ([Accessibility statement](../accessibility/accessibility-statement-for-non-profits.md)).
- [ ] Feedback received during the year answered and fixed.

## Transparency

- [ ] New annual publication and financial statements online ([Annual publications as downloads](../transparency/annual-publications-as-downloads.md)).
- [ ] Board page and governance documents current ([Board and minutes pages](../transparency/board-and-minutes-pages.md)).
- [ ] Approved minutes of the year published, if you publish them.

## Content and goals

- [ ] Content audit: outdated pages updated, archived or removed with redirects ([Content audits](../../customers/day-to-day/content-audits.md)).
- [ ] Figures of the year summarized for the board ([Measuring outcomes](../grants/measuring-outcomes-with-site-data.md)).
- [ ] Two or three goals for next year, each with a measure.

## Sign-off

```text
Prepared by .............................. date ________
Reviewed by (director or board) .......... date ________
Decisions for next year .................. ______________________
```
