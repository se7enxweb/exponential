# Non-profit checklists

This folder holds the checklists of the non-profits section. Each one links to the pages that explain its items. Copy
a checklist into your own document, put names and dates next to the items, and keep the signed copies; they are your
record that the work was done, and good evidence for a funder or board.

## Contents

1. [The checklists](#the-checklists)
2. [How to use them](#how-to-use-them)
3. [Related checklists elsewhere](#related-checklists-elsewhere)

## The checklists

| Checklist | When | Signed by |
|---|---|---|
| [Launch checklist](launch-checklist.md) | Before the site, or a relaunch, goes live | Project owner and technical lead |
| [Monthly checklist](monthly-checklist.md) | The first week of each month | The person responsible for the site |
| [Annual checklist](annual-checklist.md) | Once a year, before the budget is set | Director or board |
| [Data protection checklist](data-protection-checklist.md) | At launch, then yearly, and whenever a new form or service is added | Data protection contact |

## How to use them

1. Copy the checklist; add a column "Who" and a column "Done on".
2. Put a name next to every line before you start. A line without a name will not be done.
3. Where a line does not apply, strike it out and write why.
4. Keep the signed copy with your project files.
5. Change the checklist when you learn something; it is yours.

> **Tip**
> If you only do one thing from this folder, do the monthly checklist. Most expensive problems on small sites are
> missed backups, missed updates and nobody looking ([Backups and updates routine](../operations/backups-and-updates-routine.md)).
