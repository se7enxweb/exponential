# Non-profit monthly checklist

Do this in the first week of every month. It takes about an hour for a small site once you are used to it (an
estimate, not a measured figure). Put a name next to each section; several people can share it.

## Contents

1. [Technical health](#technical-health)
2. [Money](#money)
3. [Forms and people](#forms-and-people)
4. [Communications](#communications)
5. [Content](#content)
6. [Figures](#figures)
7. [Note of the month](#note-of-the-month)

## Technical health

- [ ] The latest backup exists and is recent ([Backups and updates routine](../operations/backups-and-updates-routine.md)).
- [ ] New Exponential or extension releases checked; updates planned or applied ([How updates work](../../customers/healthy/how-updates-work.md)).
- [ ] Cron ran: scheduled pages appeared, the newsletter sent, the audit trail rotates ([Cronjobs](../../../guides/cronjobs.md)).
- [ ] Logs glanced at for repeated errors ([Log viewer](../../../features/6.0/log-viewer.md)).
- [ ] HTTPS certificate valid for more than a month.

## Money

- [ ] The Donate link goes where it should (open it logged out).
- [ ] Gifts on the platform, or paid shop orders, match the provider's statement ([Donor trust and thank-you](../donations/donor-trust-and-thank-you.md)).
- [ ] Orders stuck in "payment failed", if you use the shop, looked at.

## Forms and people

- [ ] Volunteer applications and contact requests answered ([Processing volunteer applications](../volunteers/processing-volunteer-applications.md)).
- [ ] Spam removed in bulk.
- [ ] Submissions older than your retention period removed.
- [ ] CSV exports made this month deleted.
- [ ] Accounts of people who left disabled ([When volunteers leave](../operations/when-volunteers-leave.md)).

## Communications

- [ ] Newsletter edition sent; bounces looked at ([Supporter newsletter](../communications/supporter-newsletter-with-cjw-newsletter.md)).
- [ ] Upcoming events online; past events tidied ([Events and calendars](../communications/events-and-calendars-for-non-profits.md)).
- [ ] Campaign layouts that should have ended, ended ([Campaigns and appeals](../communications/campaigns-and-appeals.md)).

## Content

- [ ] Home page current: nothing older than you would accept.
- [ ] One key page tested with the keyboard ([Accessibility basics](../accessibility/accessibility-basics-for-non-profits.md)).
- [ ] Broken links checked ([Link and content checks](../../creators/website-creators/link-and-content-checks.md)).
- [ ] Translations of priority pages in step ([Multilingual non-profit sites](../communications/multilingual-non-profit-sites.md)).
