# Non-profit data protection checklist

Work through this at launch, once a year, and whenever you add a form, a list or an outside service. Each line links to
the page that explains it. The explanation is in [Donor and volunteer data protection](../operations/donor-and-volunteer-data-protection.md).

> **Note**
> Basics, not legal advice. Your data protection officer or lawyer decides what your law requires.

## Inventory

- [ ] Every place personal data lives is listed: submissions, accounts, orders, subscriptions, logs, audit trail,
      search phrases, exports, backups ([Personal data in Exponential](../../businesses/security/personal-data-in-exponential.md)).
- [ ] Each has an owner in the organization.
- [ ] Records of processing kept, if your law requires them ([Records of processing](../../government/privacy/records-of-processing.md)).

## Collect less

- [ ] Every form field has a purpose ([Volunteer form field worksheet](../templates/volunteer-form-field-worksheet.md)).
- [ ] No sensitive data (health, religion, immigration status) collected through the website without a decision by
      the data protection contact.
- [ ] Search statistics only on if used ([Measuring outcomes](../grants/measuring-outcomes-with-site-data.md)).

## Access

- [ ] `infocollector/read` only for coordinators.
- [ ] Shop and newsletter administration only for named people.
- [ ] `audit/read` only for administrators.
- [ ] Two-factor login for everyone with access to personal data ([Two-factor login](../../../guides/two-factor-login.md)).
- [ ] Accounts of leavers disabled ([When volunteers leave](../operations/when-volunteers-leave.md)).

## Retention and deletion

- [ ] A written retention period per kind of data ([Data retention and deletion](../../businesses/security/data-retention-and-deletion.md)).
- [ ] Old submissions removed on schedule ([Processing volunteer applications](../volunteers/processing-volunteer-applications.md#a-retention-plan)).
- [ ] Event sign-ups deleted after the event.
- [ ] Backup rotation known, so deleted data also leaves backups in time.

## Consent

- [ ] Newsletter by double opt-in only; donors not added without their own subscription ([Opt-in and consent](../../businesses/newsletters/opt-in-and-consent.md)).
- [ ] Unsubscribe link in every edition ([One-click unsubscribe](../../government/privacy/one-click-unsubscribe.md)).
- [ ] Consent wording next to checkboxes on forms ([Consent and forms](../../businesses/security/consent-and-forms.md)).
- [ ] Cookies and analytics: consent handled where required ([Cookie banners and consent](../../government/privacy/cookie-banners-and-consent.md)).
- [ ] Photos of people published only with permission.

## Processors and the privacy notice

- [ ] Privacy notice names: host, payment provider or donation platform, mail delivery, CAPTCHA provider, analytics,
      maps and video, AI provider if used ([Privacy notice outline](../../government/privacy/privacy-notice-outline.md)).
- [ ] Data processing agreements in place where required.
- [ ] Where data is stored (country) known for each processor.

## Requests from people

- [ ] A named person handles access, correction and erasure requests ([Right of access](../../government/privacy/right-of-access.md),
      [Right to erasure](../../government/privacy/right-to-erasure.md)).
- [ ] Requests and answers logged ([GDPR request log template](../../customers/commerce/gdpr-request-log-template.md)).

## Exports, backups and incidents

- [ ] CSV exports deleted after use; the audit trail records each export with its row count
      ([Collected information](../../../guides/collected-information.md)).
- [ ] Backups stored securely, with restricted access.
- [ ] A plan for a data breach: who decides, who informs whom, within which time
      ([Incident notes template](../../customers/healthy/incident-notes-template.md)).
