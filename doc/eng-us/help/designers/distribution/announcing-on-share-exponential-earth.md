# Announcing a theme on share.exponential.earth

This page is for designers who want the community to know about their theme. What follows describes the site as it was
on 18 October 2026.

## What the site offers

[share.exponential.earth](https://share.exponential.earth/) is the community portal of Exponential. Its menus include
**Get Involved** (Develop, Exchange, Learn & Teach, Localize, Spread the word), **Project** (downloads, extensions,
issue tracker, roadmap, social channels), **Blogs**, **Forums** and **Learn** (tutorials, documentation, books, code
standards). The forums include **General**, **Extensions**, **Developer**, **Install & configuration**, **Setup &
design**, **Discussions** and **Translation**. Registered users can post in the forums and submit tutorials ("Write a
tutorial!").

Its footer says "Copyright © 1998 - 2026 7x. All rights reserved except where otherwise noted": what you post is
published there under the site's terms; keep a copy of anything you want to publish elsewhere under your own license.

## Step by step: announce a release

1. Register at `https://share.exponential.earth/user/register` and log in.
2. Choose the forum: **Extensions** for a new theme extension, **Setup & design** for design questions and tips.
3. Write the post:
   - title: "<Theme name> <version>: <one line of what it is>";
   - what it is and for whom, two or three sentences;
   - one screenshot and a link to the demo;
   - install command and links to the repository, Packagist and the project page;
   - the license;
   - where to report problems.
4. Answer questions in the thread, and point bug reports to your issue tracker.
5. For a deeper piece (how you built a feature), write a tutorial instead of a forum post.

## Etiquette

- One announcement per notable release, not per fix.
- Disclose that it is your work and, if you sell services around it, say so.
- No customer names or private details in examples.
