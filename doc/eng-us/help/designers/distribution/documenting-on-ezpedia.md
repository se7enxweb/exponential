# Documenting a theme on eZpedia

This page is for designers who want to share knowledge beyond their own README. What follows describes the site as it
was on 18 October 2026.

## What eZpedia is

[eZpedia](https://ezpedia.exponential.earth/) calls itself the free encyclopedia of the product line "that anyone can
edit", with articles collected since 2006. **All text is available under the terms of the GNU Free Documentation
License.** It is organized in namespaces such as Learning, Solution, Snippet, Project and Platform.

Articles a designer will find useful:

| Article | Address |
|---|---|
| Learning: How to create a design extension | `/en/learning/learning_how_to_create_a_design_extension` |
| Design structure | `/en/learning/design_structure` |
| Extensions and projects | `/en/project/extensions`, `/en/project/` |

Many of these articles were written for earlier versions of the product. The settings they describe
(`ActiveExtensions`, `ActiveAccessExtensions`, design extensions) still exist; check details against this help.

## Step by step: add a page about your theme

1. Read a few existing project articles to follow their form.
2. Write the page: what the theme is, requirements, install, links to the repository, Packagist, the project page and
   the demo.
3. Keep anything you add compatible with the GFDL: your own text, or text under the GFDL. Do not paste text you
   cannot license that way.
4. Link it from your README's "More documentation" section.

## Using eZpedia text in your own docs

eZpedia's text is under the GFDL. If your documentation is under the GFDL too
([The GFDL for theme documentation](gfdl-for-theme-documentation.md)), you may copy and adapt it, keeping the
license and attribution. Under any other license, link to the article instead of copying it.
