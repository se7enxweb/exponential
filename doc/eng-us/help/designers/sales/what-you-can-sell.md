# What you can sell around a theme

This page is for designers looking for ways to be paid. The list is of options; most studios combine two or three.

## The options

| Offer | The buyer gets | It takes from you | Recurs? |
|---|---|---|---|
| A copy of a ready theme | The files, now | Building it once, then support questions | No, unless updates are sold |
| Custom theme design | A theme made for them | Design and build time per client | No |
| Setup and installation | A working site with the theme | A few hours per site | No |
| Customization | Their colors, logo, layout changes | Time per change | Sometimes |
| Support | Answers and fixes within agreed times | Being available | Yes |
| Maintenance | The theme kept compatible and secure | Upgrade testing per release | Yes |
| Hosting | A site that runs | Servers, monitoring, backups | Yes |
| Training | Editors and developers who can work alone | Preparing and giving sessions | Sometimes |
| Assets | Photos, illustrations, icon sets | Creating them | No |

## How to choose

1. List what you are good at and what you enjoy.
2. Mark the offers that recur: they make income steadier but bind your time.
3. Check which offers fit the GPL without any special terms (all of the above do, see
   [The GPL and selling](gpl-and-selling.md)).
4. Start with one product and one service; add more when the first runs smoothly.

## Things you cannot sell

- Exclusive rights to GPL code you did not write.
- A promise that buyers may not share the GPL code they received.
- The name "Exponential" as if it were your product ([Names and trademarks](names-and-trademarks.md)).
