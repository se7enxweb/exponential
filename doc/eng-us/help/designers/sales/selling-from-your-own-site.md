# Selling from your own site

This page is for designers who want a place they control to sell from. Your site is the one channel with payments
([Where Exponential themes are found today](where-themes-are-found.md)).

## What the site needs

| Part | Page |
|---|---|
| A product page per theme or service | [Writing a theme's product page](product-page.md) |
| A live demo | [Demos that sell](demos-that-sell.md) |
| Terms of sale and support | [Contracts and terms](contracts-and-terms.md) |
| A refund policy | [Refund policies](refund-policies.md) |
| A privacy notice | [Customer data and privacy](customer-data-and-privacy.md) |
| A contact and support page | [Support offerings](support-offerings.md) |
| Invoices that meet your country's rules | [Taxes and invoices](taxes-and-invoices.md) |

## Build it with Exponential

Your own site is the best showcase: build it with Exponential and your own theme. Exponential includes a shop (basket,
orders, VAT and shipping handlers); payment gateways are added by extensions, for example the Stripe extension
`se7enxweb/sevenx_payment_stripe` listed in the `se7enxweb` organization. Check the current documentation of any
gateway before relying on it.

## Payment options, as choices

| Option | Fits |
|---|---|
| Invoices and bank transfer | Services, companies as buyers |
| A payment gateway in your shop | Theme sales and subscriptions |
| A hosted checkout page of a payment provider | Few products, little setup |

## Step by step

1. Write the pages of the table.
2. Choose a payment option and test the whole purchase with a test payment.
3. Set up delivery: an email with access instructions, or a download ([Delivering updates](delivering-updates-to-customers.md)).
4. Link the site from your README and project listing.
