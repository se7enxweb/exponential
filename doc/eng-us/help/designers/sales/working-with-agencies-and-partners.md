# Working with agencies and partners

This page is for designers who want others to bring their themes to more sites.

## Kinds of partners

| Partner | They bring | You bring |
|---|---|---|
| An agency | Clients and projects | Themes, customization, support behind them |
| A hosting company | Customers who want a ready site | A theme that installs cleanly, documentation |
| Another designer or developer | Skills you lack | Skills they lack |

## What to agree in writing

1. **Roles**: who talks to the end client, who designs, who builds, who supports.
2. **Support path**: how a bug travels from the end client to you, and the times involved.
3. **Money**: fixed fee, revenue share or referral fee, as options; when and how it is paid.
4. **Names and credits**: how the partner names the theme ([Customization and white-label work](customization-and-white-label.md))
   and that license notices stay.
5. **Data**: what client data you will see and how you protect it ([Customer data and privacy](customer-data-and-privacy.md)).
6. **Ending**: what happens to ongoing support when the partnership ends.

## Step by step

1. Start with one project and a short written agreement.
2. Review after the project: what worked, what did not.
3. Turn the lessons into a standard agreement (with your lawyer).
