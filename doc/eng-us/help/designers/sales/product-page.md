# Writing a theme's product page

This page is for designers who write the page that sells a theme on their own site. Its structure mirrors the README,
with the buyer in mind ([A README that explains and sells](../distribution/readme-that-explains-and-sells.md)).

## Sections

1. **Headline**: what it is and for whom, one line.
2. **Screenshots and the demo link**: light, dark, phone ([Screenshots for a theme](../distribution/screenshots-for-a-theme.md)).
3. **Who it is for**: the kind of site (magazine, association, shop), and who it is not for.
4. **Proven qualities**: each with how it was checked (accessibility level and tool, CSP without inline code, widths).
5. **What is included**: files, updates, support ([What buyers receive](what-buyers-receive.md)).
6. **Compatibility**: Exponential and PHP versions you tested.
7. **License**: the code license and any asset license, in one or two sentences.
8. **Price and how to buy**, with the support tiers.
9. **Questions**: the five questions buyers really ask, answered.

## Claims to avoid

| Avoid | Because |
|---|---|
| "Official Exponential theme" | Only the project can call something official |
| "Certified" without a named certification | No such program exists unless you can name it |
| Figures you have not measured | Buyers will measure |
| Named customers without their consent | Their consent first |

See also [Brand and naming rules](../../business/brand-and-naming-rules.md).

## Step by step

1. Draft the nine sections.
2. Replace every adjective with a fact you can show.
3. Have someone outside your team read it and say what they would buy and why.
4. Keep it in step with each release.
