# Training and workshops

This page is for designers who teach. Training is a service clients value after a launch, and it spreads knowledge of
Exponential.

## Formats

| Format | Audience | Outline |
|---|---|---|
| Editor session | The client's editors | Creating and publishing content, images, the layouts editor, where help is |
| Template workshop | Front-end developers | Templates, overrides, view modes, the design cascade |
| Theme-building course | Designers | From the design extension wizard to a released theme |
| Maintenance workshop | Site owners | Updates, upgrade testing, accessibility checks |

## Materials you may use

The Exponential documentation is free documentation under the GNU FDL 1.3: you may copy, adapt and hand it out,
keeping the license and attribution ([Free documentation](../../free-documentation.md)). Good starting points:

- for editors: [Getting started](../../getting-started.md), [Editing content](../../editing-content.md),
  [The layouts editor](../../layouts-editor.md);
- for designers: this help's designer pages ([Theme extensions: start here](../extensions/theme-extensions-start-here.md)).

## Step by step

1. Agree on the audience, goals and length with the client.
2. Prepare a training site with demo content, never the client's live site ([Demo content for a theme](../distribution/demo-content.md)).
3. Prepare exercises with expected results.
4. Hand out the material with its license notice.
5. Collect feedback and improve the material.
