# Demos that sell

This page is for designers presenting a theme to a buyer, live or on a call. The general demo scripts for Exponential
are in [Demo scripts](../../business/demo-scripts.md); this page adds what is specific to a theme.

## A guided path (about 20 minutes)

1. **The front page** at full width: the look, the navigation.
2. **Resize to a phone width**, then zoom to 200%: the layout holds.
3. **Switch to dark mode** (if the theme supports it).
4. **Open an article**: typography, images, related content.
5. **Edit it**: show an editor changing a title and an image, and publishing; the theme shows the change.
6. **The layouts editor** (if the theme styles layout blocks): add a block, publish, view.
7. **Keyboard only**: tab through the page; focus is visible.
8. **Questions**.

## Prepare

- A demo site with good demo content ([Running a demo site](../distribution/demo-sites.md),
  [Demo content](../distribution/demo-content.md)).
- A demo editor account that can only edit content.
- A backup: screenshots, in case the network fails.

## Follow up

Within a day, send the demo link, the product page, what you showed and the answers you promised. Keep notes of the
questions; they belong on the product page.
