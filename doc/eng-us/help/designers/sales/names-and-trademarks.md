# Names and trademarks

This page is for designers naming a theme, a studio offer or a domain. The project's naming rules are in
[Brand and naming rules](../../business/brand-and-naming-rules.md); this page applies them to theme sellers. It is not
legal advice.

## Rules of thumb

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "Harbor, a theme for Exponential" | "Exponential Harbor" (looks like part of the product) |
| "Built for Exponential 6" | "Official Exponential theme" (unless the project says so) |
| Name the product **Exponential** | Use the old product name for your product; it appears only as history (it was discontinued by its creator after almost 18 years) |
| Your own studio name for services | Names of other companies or their products for yours |

## Step by step: check a name

1. Search the name with "Exponential" and "theme" on GitHub, Packagist and projects.exponential.earth.
2. Search a trademark register of your main market for the name in software and design classes.
3. Check that the domain and the Packagist vendor name are free.
4. If anything is close, choose another name now; renaming later costs every user work
   ([Naming your theme extension](../extensions/naming-your-theme-extension.md)).

## In code

Do not rename kernel identifiers such as `eZINI` or `eZTemplate` in your theme to fit a brand; they are code, not
names. New names of your own use your prefix (and `exp` where they name an Exponential concept).
