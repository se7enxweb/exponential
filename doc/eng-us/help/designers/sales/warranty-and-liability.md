# Warranty and liability

This page is for designers who sell themes or services. **It is not legal advice.**

## What the GPL says

The GPL version 2 states that the program is provided without warranty, to the extent permitted by law, and limits
liability. That covers the free license. It does not stop you from offering a warranty of your own, for a fee, to a
customer: the GPL explicitly allows you to offer warranty protection in exchange for a fee.

## Options for what you promise

| Option | Example |
|---|---|
| No promise beyond the license | "Provided as is under the GPL" |
| A limited fix promise | "Defects reported within 90 days are fixed free of charge" |
| A compatibility promise | "Works with the Exponential and PHP versions listed; updates for new versions for 12 months" |
| A service level | Response and fix times ([Response times and commitments](response-times-and-commitments.md)) |

Only promise what you can deliver, and define "defect" (behavior differing from the documentation, not a wish).

## Questions for your lawyer and insurer

- Which limits of liability are valid where you and your customers are?
- Do consumer protection rules apply to your buyers?
- Does your professional insurance cover software and design work?

## Step by step

1. Decide your promise from the table.
2. Write it into your terms ([Contracts and terms](contracts-and-terms.md)).
3. Make sure your internal process can keep it (testing, records).
