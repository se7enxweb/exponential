# Move content to another place in the tree

Recipes move below Healthy eating without breaking layouts.

*Life cycle: Migrate, example 17 of 34. Worked out on the Fit & Healthy site of Exponential 6.0.15+.*

## Contents

1. [In the Layouts editor](#in-the-layouts-editor)
2. [In the content tree](#in-the-content-tree)
3. [Which to choose](#which-to-choose)
4. [Check](#check)

## In the Layouts editor

1. Check mappings with **Path** or **Path prefix** targets and **Subtree** targets whose subtree changed.

## In the content tree

1. Move in **Content structure** (**Move**); `MainMenuID[]` entries keep working.

## Which to choose

Prefer Location and Subtree targets for content that may move.

## Check

- The moved pages keep their layouts.
