# Move blocks to another zone

The newsletter band moves from `main` to `pre_footer`.

*Life cycle: Redesign, example 10 of 34. Worked out on the Fit & Healthy site of Exponential 6.0.15+.*

## Contents

1. [In the Layouts editor](#in-the-layouts-editor)
2. [With templates](#with-templates)
3. [Which to choose](#which-to-choose)

## In the Layouts editor

1. In the editor, drag the block by its handle into the other zone, **Publish layout**.

## With templates

1. In templates, move the include.

## Which to choose

Layouts editor.

## Check

- The block shows in its new place.
