# Show the Prefooter on more page types

The "Most popular articles" band also on topic pages.

*Life cycle: Redesign, example 12 of 34. Worked out on the Fit & Healthy site of Exponential 6.0.15+.*

## Contents

1. [In the Layouts editor](#in-the-layouts-editor)
2. [With templates](#with-templates)
3. [Which to choose](#which-to-choose)
4. [Check](#check)

## In the Layouts editor

1. Open Full / Topic; linking button > **Link zone** on PRE_FOOTER > *Prefooter* > **Select zone** on MAIN; **Publish layout**.

## With templates

1. Nothing in templates.

## Which to choose

Layouts editor.

## Check

- A topic page shows the band; `exp:layouts list` lists the Prefooter under Full / Topic's *Links to*.
