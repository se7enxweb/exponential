# Give a section a background color

The newsletter band in the primary color.

*Life cycle: Maintain, example 19 of 35. Worked out on the Fit & Healthy site of Exponential 6.0.15+.*

## Contents

1. [In the Layouts editor](#in-the-layouts-editor)
2. [With templates](#with-templates)
3. [Which to choose](#which-to-choose)
4. [Check](#check)

## In the Layouts editor

1. Open **Layouts > Layouts** (or **Shared layouts**) and *the layout*.
2. Select the block; on the Design tab tick **Set background color** and choose the color.
3. **Publish layout**.

## With templates

1. The colors are the design's (`primary` on the header column, `secondary` on the Prefooter column); a new one is CSS for `.bg-color-<name>` in your stylesheet.

## Which to choose

Design option for existing colors; CSS for new ones.

## Check

- The wrapper has `bg-color-<color>`.
