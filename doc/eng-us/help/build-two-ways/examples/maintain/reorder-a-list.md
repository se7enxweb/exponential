# Reorder a hand-picked list

Put the most read article first.

*Life cycle: Maintain, example 2 of 35. Worked out on the Fit & Healthy site of Exponential 6.0.15+.*

## In the Layouts editor

1. Select the List, drag the items by their handles in **Items**, **Publish layout**.

Before explayouts_ui_api 1.3.29, one addition could leave two items with position 0; on such a version, if the site order differs from the editor, drag once more ([Known editor behaviors](../../known-editor-behaviors.md)).

## With templates

1. In a template the order is the order of the IDs in the code.

## Which to choose

Layouts editor.

## Check

- The site shows the editor's order.
