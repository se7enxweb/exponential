# Retire a campaign page

The spring campaign ends without losing its work.

*Life cycle: Maintain, example 27 of 35. Worked out on the Fit & Healthy site of Exponential 6.0.15+.*

## Contents

1. [In the Layouts editor](#in-the-layouts-editor)
2. [With templates](#with-templates)
3. [Which to choose](#which-to-choose)
4. [Check](#check)

## In the Layouts editor

1. Untick **Enabled** on its mapping; keep the layout (or export it).

## With templates

1. Hide the campaign page in the content tree, or move it to an archive folder.

## Which to choose

Export the layout before deleting anything.

## Check

- The campaign address no longer shows the campaign (or answers as you decided).
