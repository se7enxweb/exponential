# Swap one article in a hand-picked list

This week's "Most popular articles" gets a new third article.

*Life cycle: Maintain, example 1 of 35. Worked out on the Fit & Healthy site of Exponential 6.0.15+.*

## Contents

1. [In the Layouts editor](#in-the-layouts-editor)
2. [With templates](#with-templates)
3. [Which to choose](#which-to-choose)
4. [Check](#check)

## In the Layouts editor

1. **Layouts > Shared layouts > Prefooter**, select the List.
2. On the Content tab, remove the old item from **Items**, **+ Add items**, pick the new article, **Add selected**, drag it to position 3.
3. **Publish layout**.

## With templates

1. A template with fixed node IDs needs an edit and a deploy for the same change.

## Which to choose

Layouts editor: a two-minute editor task.

## Check

- The band on an article page shows the new article third.
