# Replace the hero image

A new campaign picture in the hero.

*Life cycle: Maintain, example 5 of 35. Worked out on the Fit & Healthy site of Exponential 6.0.15+.*

## Contents

1. [In the Layouts editor](#in-the-layouts-editor)
2. [With templates](#with-templates)
3. [Which to choose](#which-to-choose)
4. [Check](#check)

## In the Layouts editor

1. Open **Layouts > Layouts** (or **Shared layouts**) and *the landing page layout*.
2. Select the block; on the Design tab change **Image URL** and **Image alt text**.
3. **Publish layout**.

## With templates

1. If the hero reads a content item, edit that item and upload the new image; no layout change.

## Which to choose

Typed image addresses (block) break when the file moves; images from content follow the content.

## Check

- The new image shows with its alternative text.
