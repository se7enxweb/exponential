# Preview a layout before it goes live

Review a layout without publishing a mapping.

*Life cycle: Launch, example 30 of 33. Worked out on the Fit & Healthy site of Exponential 6.0.15+.*

## Contents

1. [In the Layouts editor](#in-the-layouts-editor)
2. [With templates](#with-templates)
3. [Which to choose](#which-to-choose)
4. [Check](#check)

## In the Layouts editor

1. In **Layouts > Layouts** (or **Shared layouts**), **Preview** on the layout's row.
2. Or open it in the editor and look at the middle area.

## With templates

1. Template changes are previewed on a test copy.

## Which to choose

Preview for reviews; a test mapping (Path to a test page) to see it in the real page layout.

## Check

- The reviewer has seen every block.
