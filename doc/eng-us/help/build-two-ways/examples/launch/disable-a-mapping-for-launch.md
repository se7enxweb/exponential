# Prepare a mapping and switch it on at launch

The new layouts are ready and take over with one click.

*Life cycle: Launch, example 31 of 33. Worked out on the Fit & Healthy site of Exponential 6.0.15+.*

## Contents

1. [In the Layouts editor](#in-the-layouts-editor)
2. [With templates](#with-templates)
3. [Which to choose](#which-to-choose)
4. [Check](#check)

## In the Layouts editor

1. **New mapping** with **Enabled** unticked; at launch open it and tick **Enabled**.

## With templates

1. The template way is a deploy at go-live.

## Which to choose

Prepared mappings make go-live a click.

## Check

- After enabling, the page shows the new layout (the resolver may remember the old answer for up to `[ResolverSettings] CacheTTL`).
