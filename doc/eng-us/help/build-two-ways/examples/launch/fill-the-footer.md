# Fill both footer lines for launch

The footer links to the legal pages, contact and the main sections.

*Life cycle: Launch, example 8 of 33. Worked out on the Fit & Healthy site of Exponential 6.0.15+.*

## Contents

1. [In the Layouts editor](#in-the-layouts-editor)
2. [With settings](#with-settings)
3. [Which to choose](#which-to-choose)
4. [Check](#check)

## In the Layouts editor

1. Second line: in **Media > Configuration > Footer links**, one **Menu item** per link (Title, Item URL), ordered by priority.

## With settings

1. First line: `FooterMenuID[]` in `menu.ini` of the siteaccess, then **Template & content**.

## Which to choose

Settings for the fixed sections, content for links editors adjust.

## Check

- Both lines show; every link opens a page.
