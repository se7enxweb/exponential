# Launch the section landing pages

Each section front page has its own composition.

*Life cycle: Launch, example 26 of 33. Worked out on the Fit & Healthy site of Exponential 6.0.15+.*

## Contents

1. [In the Layouts editor](#in-the-layouts-editor)

## In the Layouts editor

1. For each section: a layout (Landing Page / Fitness ...) and a mapping with **Location** to the section node (92, 130, 163, 164, 192).

## The template way

1. The template way would be a full view per node; with five sections that is five templates to maintain.

## Which to choose

Layouts: section fronts are where editors change things most.
