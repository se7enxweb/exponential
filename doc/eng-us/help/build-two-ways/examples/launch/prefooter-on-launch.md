# Fill the prefooter for launch

The "Most popular articles" band shows real launch content.

*Life cycle: Launch, example 10 of 33. Worked out on the Fit & Healthy site of Exponential 6.0.15+.*

## Contents

1. [In the Layouts editor](#in-the-layouts-editor)
2. [With templates](#with-templates)
3. [Which to choose](#which-to-choose)
4. [Check](#check)

## In the Layouts editor

1. **Layouts > Shared layouts > Prefooter**, select the List, **+ Add items**, remove placeholders, order, **Publish layout**.

## With templates

1. A fixed list in a template would need a deploy for each change; keep it in Layouts.

## Which to choose

Layouts editor.

## Check

- An article and a recipe page show the band with the chosen articles.
