# Old addresses after a relaunch

Links to the old site still lead somewhere.

*Life cycle: Launch, example 27 of 33. Worked out on the Fit & Healthy site of Exponential 6.0.15+.*

## Contents

1. [In the Layouts editor](#in-the-layouts-editor)
2. [In the administration](#in-the-administration)
3. [Which to choose](#which-to-choose)
4. [Check](#check)

## In the Layouts editor

1. Mappings by **Path** follow the address: check them after renaming pages.

## In the administration

1. **Setup > URL wildcards** and **URL translator** manage redirects and aliases; a moved page keeps its node ID, so menus and mappings by Location still work.

## Which to choose

Prefer Location targets for pages whose address may change.

## Check

- The old addresses answer with the new pages.
