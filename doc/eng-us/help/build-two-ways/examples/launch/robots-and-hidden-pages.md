# Keep test pages out of the launch

No test page is reachable at launch.

*Life cycle: Launch, example 22 of 33. Worked out on the Fit & Healthy site of Exponential 6.0.15+.*

## Contents

1. [In the Layouts editor](#in-the-layouts-editor)
2. [In the content tree](#in-the-content-tree)
3. [Which to choose](#which-to-choose)
4. [Check](#check)

## In the Layouts editor

1. In **Layout mappings**, disable or remove mappings of test layouts (each mapping has **Enabled**).
2. Delete test layouts in **Layouts > Layouts** (**Delete**).

## In the content tree

1. Hide or delete test content in **Content structure**.

## Which to choose

Disable first, delete after launch when sure.

## Check

- The test addresses no longer show the test layout.
