# A numbered top-five list

"Top 5 recipes this month", numbered.

*Life cycle: Build, example 22 of 35. Worked out on the Fit & Healthy site of Exponential 6.0.15+.*

## Contents

1. [In the Layouts editor](#in-the-layouts-editor)

## In the Layouts editor

1. Open **Layouts > Layouts** and the layout *Landing Page / Home*; the editor opens it in a draft.
2. Open the block palette (**+** at the top left) and drag **List** (group *Listing*) into the zone `main`.
3. Fill the collection with five recipes.
4. Select the block, open the **Design** tab and fill **View type** `list_numbered`.
5. **Publish layout**. The pages that use the layout show the block on their next view.

Changing only the view type does not save until another field changes ([Known editor behaviors](../../known-editor-behaviors.md)).

## With templates

1. In a template, an `<ol>` around the fetched items does the same.

## Which to choose

The view type is one choice in the editor.

## Check

- The numbers start at 1 and follow the collection order.
