# A horizontally scrolling strip of images

A strip of recipe photos that scrolls sideways.

*Life cycle: Build, example 28 of 35. Worked out on the Fit & Healthy site of Exponential 6.0.15+.*

## Contents

1. [In the Layouts editor](#in-the-layouts-editor)
2. [With templates](#with-templates)
3. [Which to choose](#which-to-choose)
4. [Check](#check)

## In the Layouts editor

1. Open **Layouts > Layouts** and the layout *Landing Page / Home*; the editor opens it in a draft.
2. Open the block palette (**+** at the top left) and drag **Sushi bar** (group *Gallery*) into the zone `main`.
3. Select the block, open the **Design** tab and fill the image source fields.
4. **Publish layout**. The pages that use the layout show the block on their next view.

## With templates

1. Override `explayouts/block/gallery/sushi_bar.tpl` (media design).

## Which to choose

Good for many small images where a grid would be too tall.

## Check

- The strip can be scrolled with keyboard and touch.
