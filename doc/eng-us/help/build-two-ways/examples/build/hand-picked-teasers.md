# Hand-picked teasers

Three articles an editor chooses for this week.

*Life cycle: Build, example 19 of 35. Worked out on the Fit & Healthy site of Exponential 6.0.15+.*

## In the Layouts editor

1. Open **Layouts > Layouts** and the layout *Landing Page / Home*; the editor opens it in a draft.
2. Open the block palette (**+** at the top left) and drag **List** (group *Listing*) into the zone `main`.
3. On the **Content** tab press **+ Add items**, open the branches, tick the articles, **Add selected**.
4. Drag the items into order; on the **Design** tab choose view type and item view type.
5. **Publish layout**. The pages that use the layout show the block on their next view.

The Prefooter was changed this way: [Change the prefooter in the Layouts editor](../../prefooter-in-the-layouts-editor.md).
