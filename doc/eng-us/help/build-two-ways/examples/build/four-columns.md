# Four equal columns

Four short service descriptions in a row.

*Life cycle: Build, example 31 of 35. Worked out on the Fit & Healthy site of Exponential 6.0.15+.*

## Contents

1. [In the Layouts editor](#in-the-layouts-editor)

## In the Layouts editor

1. Open **Layouts > Layouts** and the layout *Landing Page / Home*; the editor opens it in a draft.
2. Open the block palette (**+** at the top left) and drag **Four columns** (group *Containers*) into the zone `main`.
3. Drag a block into each placeholder, `col_1` to `col_4`.
4. **Publish layout**. The pages that use the layout show the block on their next view.

## With templates

1. Or one List block with view `grid` and **Number of columns** 4 when the four are content items.

## Which to choose

Different block types per column: Four columns. Four content items of one kind: a grid.

## Check

- Each column holds its block.
