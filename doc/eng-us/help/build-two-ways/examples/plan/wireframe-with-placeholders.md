# Wireframe a page with titles and spacers

Agree the order of the sections ("Hero", "Latest", "Recipes", "Newsletter") first.

*Life cycle: Plan, example 6 of 35. Worked out on the Fit & Healthy site of Exponential 6.0.15+.*

## Contents

1. [In the Layouts editor](#in-the-layouts-editor)
2. [With templates](#with-templates)
3. [Which to choose](#which-to-choose)
4. [Check](#check)

## In the Layouts editor

1. Create the layout; add a **Title** per planned section with the section's name.
2. Add **Spacer** blocks where large blocks will come.
3. Publish to a test page (a mapping with a Path target), or keep it as a draft.

## With templates

1. Sketch the sections as comments in the full view template.

## Which to choose

The wireframe in the editor becomes the real page by replacing the placeholders.

## Check

- Everyone involved has seen the order.
