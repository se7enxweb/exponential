# Prototype a page in a draft

Show a page idea with real blocks before deciding.

*Life cycle: Plan, example 5 of 35. Worked out on the Fit & Healthy site of Exponential 6.0.15+.*

## Contents

1. [In the Layouts editor](#in-the-layouts-editor)
2. [With templates](#with-templates)
3. [Which to choose](#which-to-choose)
4. [Check](#check)

## In the Layouts editor

1. Open a layout; the editor works in a draft.
2. Add and arrange blocks; the middle of the editor previews them.
3. **Discard** (it asks: *Are you sure you want to discard your changes to the layout draft?*) to throw it away, or **Publish layout** to keep it.

## With templates

1. A template prototype is a branch of your design extension on a test installation.

## Which to choose

The draft is the fastest prototype: no deploy, no code.

## Check

- `./console exp:layouts list --drafts` shows no forgotten draft afterwards.
