# Decide who changes what

Every kind of change has an owner and a tool.

*Life cycle: Plan, example 4 of 35. Worked out on the Fit & Healthy site of Exponential 6.0.15+.*

## Contents

1. [In the Layouts editor](#in-the-layouts-editor)

## In the Layouts editor

1. Editors: content in the tree, blocks and collections in the Layouts editor, footer links (Menu items in Footer links).

## Developers

1. Developers: the design extension (templates, stylesheets), new block and view types, settings like the header menu list in `menu.ini`.

## Administrators

1. Settings in **Setup > Ini settings**, layout mappings, caches, export and import of layouts.

## Which to choose

Write the split down; [Which way: the decision table](../../decision-table.md) is a starting point.

## Check

- For each item of the header, footer and home page, you can name who changes it and where.
