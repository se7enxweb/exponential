# Story: Exponential Platform Nexus and Legacy

This page is for journalists who cover migration and long-lived sites. Legacy and Nexus run Exponential 6 next to a
Symfony platform, so a site can adopt the newer stack without starting over.

## Contents

1. [The story in one paragraph](#the-story-in-one-paragraph)
2. [Facts you can print](#facts-you-can-print)
3. [What not to claim](#what-not-to-claim)
4. [Angles and headline ideas](#angles-and-headline-ideas)

## The story in one paragraph

**Exponential Platform Legacy** "provides a clean distribution of Exponential Platform ... with Exponential (Legacy)
Included (now with SQLite support)": the Symfony platform and the legacy bridge with Exponential 6, a "dual kernel".
**Exponential Platform Nexus** adds Netgen Media Site and Exponential Layouts with demo content: "Platform v2/v3/v4/v5 +
Exponential v6.x (Legacy) + Netgen Media Site + NG Layouts" ([Exponential Platform Legacy](../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#exponential-platform-legacy),
[Exponential Platform Nexus](../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#exponential-platform-nexus)). They are meant for
sites that keep Exponential 6, including sites built in the eZ Publish years, and want to move forward step by step.

## What not to claim

| Do not say | Say instead |
|---|---|
| "Legacy means outdated" | "Legacy" names Exponential 6 running beside Symfony |
| "Nexus is Netgen's product" | A 7x distribution built on Netgen's open source packages, credited ([Crediting Netgen, Mugo Web and CJW Network](crediting-other-projects.md)) |
| "The starter is stable" | Its description says public beta |
| "Every Nexus includes Exponential 6" | The 1.3.0.x line and the v5 starter do not ([Covering Exponential Platform releases](covering-platform-releases.md)) |

## Angles and headline ideas

- "Keep the old site running while you adopt Symfony."
- "A complete demo site with layouts in minutes."
