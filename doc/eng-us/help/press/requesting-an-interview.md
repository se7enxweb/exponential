# Requesting an interview

This page is for journalists, podcasters and creators who want to talk to the people behind Exponential, or who need
a written statement. It lists only contacts that are published, says what each is for, and gives you a request you
can adapt. The sources do not describe a press office or a press response time, so plan for that.

## Whom to ask

Two organizations stand behind the project, and they answer different questions
([Who is the Exponential Foundation?, The Foundation and 7x](../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#the-foundation-and-7x)):

| Ask 7x about | Ask the Exponential Foundation about |
|---|---|
| Development, releases, the product family, services and hosting | Its mission, funding and sponsorship, its plans for the project |
| The history of the company (Brookins Consulting, the three stages 2014, 2022, 2023) | Its founding (19 March 2026, Seattle) and its nonprofit status |
| Velocity, Exponential Layouts, Exponential Intelligence and other features | Governance and board, which the sources do not describe |

Do not address an individual by name unless they have agreed to speak to you; ask the organization and let it choose
who answers. Question ideas: [Interview question bank](interview-question-bank.md).

Community channels such as the forums and Telegram ([The community channels](../advocates/community-channels.md))
are for users helping users. You may observe them for a story, but a request for an official comment belongs in one
of the contacts above.

## What to put in a request

| Element | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| Your name, outlet and role | So they know who is asking |
| The topic and the angle in two sentences | So the right person can answer |
| The format: live interview, recorded call, video, written questions | Written questions are the easiest to answer exactly |
| Your deadline, in a time zone | 7x describes itself as North American; say your zone plainly |
| Whether you will quote, and how | So they can give you a statement in writing ([Quotes policy](quotes-policy.md)) |
| Any embargo or publication date | See [Embargoes and timing](embargoes-and-timing.md) |
| Your disclosure, if any | For example, if you are a sponsor or contributor |

## A request template

```text
Subject: Press request: <topic> - <outlet>, deadline <date>

Hello,

I am <name>, <role> at <outlet>. I am preparing <a story / an episode / a video> about <topic>,
planned for <date>. I would like to <interview someone from 7x / the Exponential Foundation / receive
a written statement> about:

1. <question>
2. <question>
3. <question>

Format: <written answers / a 20-minute call / on camera>. I will quote only what you give me in
writing or say on the record, and I will link the sources I use from the Exponential documentation.

Deadline: <date and time, time zone>. Contact: <e-mail>.
<Disclosure line, if any.>
```

## What to expect

- The sources state no response time for press requests. The only documented response time is for security reports
  ("within one business day", SECURITY.md); do not apply it to press requests.
- If you get no answer by your deadline, write "7x did not respond to a request for comment by publication time" or
  "The Exponential Foundation did not respond ...", with the date you asked.
- Never fill the silence with a quote from memory, a forum post presented as an official answer, or a paraphrase
  presented as a statement ([Attributing statements](attributing-statements.md)).
