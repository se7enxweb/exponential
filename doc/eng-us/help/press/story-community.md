## The story in one paragraph

Exponential's community is older than its name. eZpedia, "The free eZ Publish encyclopedia that anyone can edit", has
run since 2006; the forums at share.exponential.earth and the Telegram chat carry today's questions; Discord and Slack
are listed on the project website; and the GitHub trackers take bugs and ideas
([Community places](../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#community-places)). Before 2016, 194 different commit
authors appear in the history; since December 2023 pull requests have come from 7x and from other companies and
developers, and "Every pull request is merged" ([How the project grows](../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#how-the-project-grows)).
The README: "The Exponential Project and the Exponential Foundation support the open source community directly ...
and put the support needs of community users first."
