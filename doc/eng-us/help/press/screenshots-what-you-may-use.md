# Screenshots: what you may use

This page is for picture editors, reporters and creators who need images of Exponential. There are four sources of
screenshots, each with different terms. When in doubt, take your own ([Taking your own screenshots](taking-your-own-screenshots.md)).

## The four sources

| Source | Terms | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| The images of this help (`doc/eng-us/help/images/`) | Part of the documentation in `doc/`, published with it under the GNU FDL 1.3 or later ([The license of this documentation](../../README-LICENSE.md)) | Articles and videos that can carry a license notice and credit |
| Galleries on exponential.earth | No license stated on the page | Looking; ask before reuse |
| The press kit's `screenshots/` folder | Covered by the kit's guidelines ("editorial, review, and partnership coverage"); in v1.0.0 the folder lists planned files only | Editorial use once the files are there |
| Your own screenshots of an installation you run | The interface is GPL software; the content on screen is yours or your demo's | Anything you write, if the screen holds no one else's data |

## The images in this help

The help pages use screenshots of a demonstration installation, for example the admin dashboard, the edit form,
search, the layouts editor and the two-step sign-in setup. They are WebP files under `doc/eng-us/help/images/` in the
repository (<https://github.com/se7enxweb/exponential/tree/main/doc/eng-us/help/images>), shown on pages such as
[The admin tour](../admin-tour.md) and [The layouts editor](../layouts-editor.md).

The GNU FDL lets you copy and redistribute them, including commercially, if you keep the license notice, credit the
copyright holders and license your modified versions under the same terms ([Free documentation](../free-documentation.md)).
For a news article, a credit line and a link to the license are the practical way to do that (see below).

## The galleries on exponential.earth

<https://exponential.earth/about/about-exponential/screenshots> shows eight galleries (admin login, dashboard, content
structure, edit front page, edit folder, store order list, media, cache admin) and describes itself as "Coming
soon! A visual walk through of Exponential". It states no license and invites people to send their own screenshots
to hi [at] exponential [dot] earth. Ask the project before you reuse these images ([Press contacts](press-contacts.md)).

```text
  Image: Exponential press kit

For your own:
  Screenshot: <outlet or your name>, Exponential <version> on a demo installation
```

## Before you publish any screenshot

- [ ] No names, e-mail addresses or other personal data of real people on screen.
- [ ] No passwords, keys, tokens or one-time codes; no address bar with a private host name.
- [ ] The version shown matches the version you name in the text.
- [ ] The design is named correctly (admin4, the classic admin, AdminUI) if it matters to the story.
- [ ] Alt text describes what the screenshot shows ([Making your coverage accessible](making-your-coverage-accessible.md)).
