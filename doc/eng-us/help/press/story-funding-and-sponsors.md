## The story in one paragraph

"The Exponential Open Source Project is funded by donations of time and resources by 7x (and Exponential Sponsors)"
(<https://exponential.earth/about/project>). In March 2026 a nonprofit was added: the Exponential Foundation, whose
mission includes the "continued Funding" of the project. There are two sponsor pages with two missions: sponsoring
7x funds "the company that keeps the lights on", sponsoring the Foundation funds "the open-source project itself"
(se7enxweb GitHub profile). 7x is "the first sponsor" in the help's own words
([7x, the first sponsor](../sponsors/7x-the-first-sponsor.md)).

## What not to claim

| Do not say | Say instead |
|---|---|
| "Funded by big tech" | Sponsors as the pages show them, with consent |
| "Struggling" or "well funded" | No budget is published |
| "Donations to 7x are tax deductible" | The Foundation says its donations are; 7x is a company |
