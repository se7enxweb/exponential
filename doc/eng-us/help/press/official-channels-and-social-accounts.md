# Official channels and social accounts

This page is for reporters and creators who want to cite, tag or follow the project. It lists the channels that
the project's own pages name. They were read on **18 October 2026** from the footer of <https://exponential.earth/>,
<https://exponential.earth/about/project> and the repository's README. If an account is not on this list, do not
treat it as official.

## Tagging and citing

- Tag the product account for product news and the 7x account for company news; tag the Foundation for its own
  announcements ([Two organizations, one project](two-organizations-one-project.md)).
- A post on these accounts is a published statement of the account's owner; cite it with its link and date
  ([Attributing statements](attributing-statements.md)).
- No official hashtag is documented. Use the product name in plain text.
- Creators: tagging an account is not an endorsement by it, and must not be presented as one
  ([Disclosure and sponsorship ethics](disclosure-and-sponsorship-ethics.md)).
