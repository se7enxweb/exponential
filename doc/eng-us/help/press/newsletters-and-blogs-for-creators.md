# Newsletters and blogs for creators

This page is for newsletter writers and bloggers. Written posts last longer than videos and are searched for years,
so accuracy and dates matter even more. The advocates' writing guides cover much of the craft; this page adds what a
creator with an audience needs.

## Sourcing and linking

- Link the primary source for each fact: the tag, the changelog, the feature page ([Where the facts live, for the press](where-the-facts-live-for-press.md)).
- Put the version and the date at the top of tutorials: "Tested with Exponential 6.0.14 on <date>".
- Link to the help pages for steps rather than copying long procedures; they are updated with the software.

## Reusing the documentation

The documentation is free under the GNU FDL 1.3: you may quote it, translate it and adapt it, including in a paid
newsletter, if you keep the notices and license your adapted text the same way
([Quoting and reusing the documentation](quoting-and-reusing-the-documentation.md)).

```text
Adapted from the Exponential documentation, "<page>", (C) 1998-2026 7x & Exponential Foundation and the
Exponential contributors, GNU FDL 1.3 or later.
```

## Disclosure

Say at the top of the post if the post is sponsored, if you received anything, or if you work with 7x, the Foundation
or a partner ([Disclosure and sponsorship ethics](disclosure-and-sponsorship-ethics.md),
[Paid, gifted and affiliate content](paid-gifted-and-affiliate-content.md)).

## Keeping posts accurate

- When a version described as "coming" is released, add an update note ([Corrections and updates](corrections-and-updates.md)).
- When a reader reports an error, fix it openly ([Correcting a video or post](correcting-a-video-or-post.md)).
- Once a year, re-check your most-read tutorials against the current release.
