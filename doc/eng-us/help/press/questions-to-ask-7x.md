# Questions to ask 7x

This page is for journalists and podcasters preparing an interview with 7x. Read the public answers first
([7x for reporters](7x-for-reporters.md), [Who is 7x?](../who-is-7x.md)) and spend the interview on what they do not
cover. Each group notes what is already documented.

## The company

Already documented: the self-description, Brookins Consulting, the three stages ([7x in three stages](7x-in-three-stages.md)).

- Who should we name and quote for 7x, and with which role?
- How many people work on Exponential at 7x? (not published)
- What was the decisive moment between the idea in 2014 and the start in 2022?

## The stewardship

Already documented: December 2023, the first changes, 6.0.0 on 1 January 2024 ([December 2023](../../history/2023/2023-12.md)).

- Why take over a code base whose original developer had stopped?
- What was hardest in the first month?
- How do you decide what to modernize and what to keep for existing sites?

## Releases and plans

Already documented: the release list, the 6.0.15 changelog. Not documented: a release date for 6.0.15, a long-term
roadmap with dates ([What the sources do not say](what-the-sources-do-not-say.md)).

- When do you expect 6.0.15, and what decides that it is ready?
- How long will the 6.0 line be maintained?
- Is a 6.1 or 7 planned?

## Products

Already documented: what Velocity, Exponential Intelligence and Exponential Layouts do ([Feature stories: index](feature-stories-index.md)).

- Why write a web server in PHP rather than tune existing servers?
- Why does Exponential Intelligence never publish on its own? Was that a debate?
- Who is the Symfony line (Exponential Platform) for, compared with Exponential 6?

## Business and funding

Already documented: 7x sells services; the software is free; sponsor pages exist ([Writing about funding and sponsors](writing-about-funding-and-sponsors.md)).

- What share of the work is funded by services, and what by sponsorship?
- What would more sponsorship change?
- How does 7x avoid conflicts between its business and the Foundation's project?

## Security and trust

Already documented: private reporting, first answer within one business day, published versions never change
([Story: security](story-security.md)).

- How many security reports have you received since 2023? (not published)
- Who reviews the code before it reaches the main branch?

## Community

Already documented: the channels, every pull request merged ([Story: the community](story-community.md)).

- Who outside 7x contributes most, and how can a newcomer start?
- Are events or meetups planned?

## Before and after the interview

- Send the topics in advance if your outlet allows it ([Preparing a briefing](preparing-a-briefing.md)).
- Record or take notes that let you quote word for word ([Quotes policy](quotes-policy.md)).
- Check every number given in the interview against a source, or attribute it clearly ([Verifying numbers](verifying-numbers.md)).
