# Paid, gifted and affiliate content

This page is for creators who are offered money, a gift or a commission for content about Exponential, or who earn
from links in their content. It sorts the cases, says which arrangements the sources actually document, and gives a
checklist to agree on before you publish. How to word a disclosure is on
[Disclosure and sponsorship ethics](disclosure-and-sponsorship-ethics.md).

> **Important**
> This is ethical guidance, not legal advice. Rules about advertising, sponsored content and affiliate links differ by
> country and platform and change over time. Check the rules where you live and where you publish. For a contract,
> ask a professional in your country.

## What exists and what does not

| Arrangement | Documented? | Source |
|---|---|---|
| A creator, influencer or ambassador program of 7x or the Foundation | No | No source names one ([Claims you must not make](../advocates/claims-you-must-not-make.md#events-and-programs)) |
| An affiliate program with links that pay creators | No | No source names one |
| The Exponential Partner Program for companies, with lead referral commissions | Yes | [Referrals and leads](../partners/referrals-and-leads.md) |
| Sponsorship of 7x and of the Foundation on GitHub (money goes to them) | Yes | [Sponsors: start here](../sponsors/sponsors-start-here.md) |
| Paid services and hosting from 7x | Yes, as services | [Who is 7x?, Services and hosting](../who-is-7x.md#services-and-hosting) |
| Free review copies | Not applicable | The software is free under the GNU GPL; anyone can install it |

Because the software and its documentation are free, the usual "review unit" does not exist. What a company might
give a creator is something around the software: hosting, support time, a commercial extension, a fee.

## The four cases

### 1. Paid content

Someone pays you to make or publish content. The payer may be a hosting company, an agency, an extension vendor or
anyone else. Label it as paid, say who paid, and say whether they saw it first.

### 2. Gifted products and services

You receive something free: a hosting account, a support contract, a paid extension, a ticket. Label it as a gift and
say what it was. If you keep using it after the video, say that too.

### 3. Referral, affiliate and commission links

A link earns you or your company money when viewers buy. The only documented commissions are those of the Partner
Program for partner companies. If a third party (a hosting company, for example) gives you an affiliate link, the
arrangement is theirs, not the project's: never call it "the Exponential affiliate link".

### 4. Your own business

You sell services, themes, extensions or courses around Exponential. Your content is then also advertising for you.
Say so, and show the product fairly before you mention your offer.

## Before you accept: the agreement checklist

```text
[ ] Who pays or gives, and what exactly (amount, product, duration)?
[ ] Is it in writing, with the payer's real name or company?
[ ] Does the payer see the content before publication? May they ask for changes? Which kind?
[ ] May I say what I really think, including criticism?
[ ] Which facts must be included? (Check each against the help before you agree.)
[ ] How must it be labeled, and does that meet the rules where I publish?
[ ] Does it ask me to claim an endorsement, a program, a number or a date no source supports? (then no)
[ ] Does it ask me to show a real site or real data? (then only with written consent)
[ ] Who owns the recording, and may the payer reuse it? Where, for how long?
[ ] What happens if I find an error after publication? (I correct it; see the correction page)
```

## Labeling each case

| Case | Spoken or on screen | Description or post |
|---|---|---|
| Paid | "This video is paid for by <who>." | "Paid partnership with <who>. They <did / did not> review it." |
| Gift | "<Who> gave me <what> for free." | "Gift: <what>, from <who>. No other payment." |
| Commission link | "Links below may earn me a commission." | "Commission links are marked <how>. You pay the same either way." |
| Own business | "I sell <what>." | "Disclosure: I am the author of <what> and sell <service>." |

Templates with full sentences are on [Disclosure and sponsorship ethics](disclosure-and-sponsorship-ethics.md#disclosure-lines-to-copy).

## Offers to be careful with

| Offer | Why | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| "Join the official Exponential creator program" | No such program is documented | Ask for it in writing and check it with the contacts on exponential.earth |
| "Say it is N times faster than X" | No published comparison supports a multiple | Decline; show how the speed is measured ([Demo script: Velocity](demo-script-velocity.md)) |
| "Say it is endorsed by eZ Systems or Ibexa" | Exponential is not associated with them | Decline ([Brand and naming rules](../business/brand-and-naming-rules.md#other-companies-and-products)) |
| "Say 6.0.15 ships on date X" | It is in development and has no date | Decline |
| "Do not mention you were paid" | It misleads your audience | Decline |
| "Use our customer's site in the demo" | Real data on camera | Only with written consent; prefer a demo installation |
