# Questions to ask the Foundation

This page is for journalists preparing questions for the Exponential Foundation. Its public self-description is short,
so many reasonable questions are open. Read [The Exponential Foundation for reporters](the-foundation-for-reporters.md)
first, then use the interview for what it does not answer.

## Mission

Already published: the mission statement and the groups the Foundation serves (<https://foundation.exponential.earth/>).

- What does "Direction" in the mission mean in practice: who decides what the project does next?
- Which of the products it names (Exponential, Exponential Platform, Exponential Basic) gets most of its attention?

## Governance

Not published: board, governance, members ([What the sources do not say](what-the-sources-do-not-say.md)).

- Who serves on the board, and how are members chosen?
- Can contributors or sponsors become members, and with which rights?
- Will the Foundation publish annual reports?

## Money

Already published: what sponsorship funds; the sponsor page <https://github.com/sponsors/exponentialcms>
([What the Foundation funds](../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#what-the-foundation-funds)). Not published: budget, donors.

- How large is the budget, and how much comes from 7x and how much from other sponsors?
- How are funds allocated between code, documentation and community infrastructure?
- Are donations tax deductible, and where? (The Foundation says they are in the United States; ask for details.)

## The relationship with 7x

Already published: 7x is "the current leader in contributions"; the copyright is shared
([Two organizations, one project](two-organizations-one-project.md)).

- How are conflicts of interest between 7x's business and the Foundation's project handled?
- What would happen to the project if 7x stopped contributing?
- Does the Foundation pay 7x for work?

## Goals for the project

Already published: the website mentions "a long detailed list of growth goals for the Exponential Project in 2026"
without listing them.

- Can you share the goals for 2026?
- How will you measure whether they are met?

## Documentation, education and community

Already published: the documentation is free under the GNU FDL; the help system is part of 6.0.15
([Story: free documentation](story-free-documentation.md)).

- What does the Foundation plan for training and education?
- Are events or meetups planned?
- How can a newcomer contribute?

## Handling the answers

- Quote answers word for word and attribute them to the person and role given ([Quotes policy](quotes-policy.md)).
- If a question is not answered, say so plainly; do not speculate ([Attributing statements](attributing-statements.md)).
- Tell readers which facts come from the Foundation itself and which from independent records.
