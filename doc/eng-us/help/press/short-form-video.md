# Short-form video

This page is for creators who make short vertical videos: clips of 15 to 60 seconds for platforms that show them in a
feed. Short videos travel far without context, so each one must be true on its own, without the description. One
clip, one idea, one source.

## The rules for a short clip

1. **One idea.** "Every edit is a draft" is a clip. "Everything about editing" is not.
2. **True without the caption.** Viewers may never see your description; the spoken words and on-screen text must
   carry the facts.
3. **Disclose inside the clip** if anyone paid or gave you something: on screen at the start, and in the caption
   ([Disclosure and sponsorship ethics](disclosure-and-sponsorship-ethics.md#where-to-put-the-disclosure)).
4. **Name the version** on screen when you show a feature of the 6.0.15 development line.
5. **Captions burned in or added**, because most short videos are watched without sound
   ([Captions and accessible video](captions-and-accessible-video.md)).
6. **Link the help page** in the caption or your profile.

## Framing a desktop interface for a phone

- Zoom the browser so one part of the screen fills a vertical frame: a form, a block, a button.
- Hide the left or right column with the tabs at the window edges when you need the space
  ([Hiding and resizing the columns](../admin-tour.md#hiding-and-resizing-the-columns)).
- Or use a narrow window: on a narrow screen the tabs and search move into the menu behind the button with three
  lines, which looks natural in a vertical clip.
- Leave room at the top and bottom for the platform's buttons and your captions.
- Never crop a screenshot so that it changes its meaning, for example hiding "draft" next to a button.

## A clip script to copy

```text
CLIP - <idea>                     Length: <30> s     Version on screen: Exponential <version>
0-2 s    Hook text on screen: "<one short claim from the table>"
          (disclosure on screen here if needed)
2-20 s   The action, with a voice line and burned-in captions
20-25 s  The result on screen
25-30 s  Text: "Source: <help page title>"  +  "Link in caption"
CAPTION  <one sentence> Source: <link>. <disclosure>. #OpenSource
```

## Clipping from your long videos and streams

- Clip only parts that are true on their own; do not cut "it does not" down to "it does".
- Keep the disclosure: if the long video was sponsored, the clip is too.
- Check every frame for secrets again; a clip may show a frame the long edit hid behind a transition
  ([Keeping secrets off screen](keeping-secrets-off-screen.md)).
- Label old clips when a later release changes the screen.
