# Story: the extension ecosystem

This page is for journalists who cover platforms and their ecosystems. Exponential is extended by packages called
extensions; many are installed with every site and many are switched off until needed.

## Contents

1. [The story in one paragraph](#the-story-in-one-paragraph)
2. [Facts you can print](#facts-you-can-print)
3. [What not to claim](#what-not-to-claim)
4. [Angles and headline ideas](#angles-and-headline-ideas)

## The story in one paragraph

On 18 October 2026 the kernel's `composer.json` required 66 `se7enxweb/*` packages: themes, the page builder, the writing assistant, tags,
newsletters, two-factor sign-in, the Oracle driver, the REST API, the job queue and more; "Many are inactive until you
switch them on" ([The extension ecosystem](../who-is-7x.md#the-extension-ecosystem)). Each has its own repository,
releases and changelog, and states its version in its own files. On Packagist, the vendor se7enxweb has 219 packages.
Some extensions come from other companies' work, such as CJW Network's newsletter, and are credited.

The README's "thousands" refers to extensions available over the code base's history; this documentation does not
count them. Attribute it to the README if you use it.

## What not to claim

| Do not say | Say instead |
|---|---|
| "66 extensions all active" | Required by the kernel; many inactive until switched on |
| "An app store" | Packages on GitHub and Packagist; no store is documented |
| "Thousands of extensions" (as a fact of your own) | Attribute the README, or use the counted figures |
