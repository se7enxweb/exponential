# Third-party music, fonts and assets

This page is for video creators, designers and bloggers. A video about free software can still break someone else's
copyright through its background music or a font. This page lists what to check. It is practical guidance, not legal
advice; rules differ by country and platform.

## Material from the Exponential project

| Material | Terms | Page |
|---|---|---|
| The software's interface (shown in your screenshots) | GPL software | [Licenses explained for reporters](licenses-explained-for-reporters.md) |
| Text and images of this documentation | GNU FDL 1.3 or later | [Quoting and reusing the documentation](quoting-and-reusing-the-documentation.md), [Screenshots: what you may use](screenshots-what-you-may-use.md) |
| Logos and brand assets from the press kit | The press kit's guidelines: editorial, review and partnership coverage | [Logos and brand assets](logos-and-brand-assets.md) |
| Bundled third-party libraries (jQuery, TinyMCE, Bootstrap Icons and others) | Their own licenses | The library's license file in the repository |

Showing the interface in a screenshot is showing the software; reusing a bundled icon set in your own designs is using
that icon set under its own license.

## Third-party material

| Material | Check |
|---|---|
| Music | A license that covers your platform and monetization; platform audio libraries state their terms |
| Fonts | The font license allows use in video or graphics; many free fonts do |
| Stock images and footage | The license covers your use and does not forbid editorial or commercial use as needed |
| Icons and illustrations | License and attribution requirements |
| Sound effects | Same as music |

When in doubt, use material you made yourself or material under a license that clearly fits.

## Other people's screenshots and sites

- Do not show another person's site or administration without permission ([Privacy on camera](privacy-on-camera.md)).
- The screenshot galleries on exponential.earth state no license; ask before reuse ([Screenshots: what you may use](screenshots-what-you-may-use.md)).
- Other creators' videos and images belong to them; link, do not re-upload.

## Attribution

```text
Music: "<title>" by <artist>, <license>, <link>
Font: <name>, <license>
Screenshots: Exponential documentation, GNU FDL 1.3 / own screenshots of a demo installation
Logo: Exponential project
```

Put attribution in the description or the credits, as the license asks.

## Keeping a record

Keep a folder per video with the license or receipt of every third-party asset. If a claim reaches your video later,
you can answer it in minutes.
