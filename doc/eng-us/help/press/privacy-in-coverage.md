# Privacy in your coverage

This page is for reporters and editors. Stories about software touch people: site owners, editors, contributors,
people who reported bugs. This documentation follows a simple rule for itself, and asks the press to follow it too:
**no personal data, and no names of private people or customers, without their consent.**

## Customers and site owners

- No customer list of 7x or users list of Exponential is published. If you learn that an organization uses
  Exponential, ask that organization before naming it as a user ([What the sources do not say](what-the-sources-do-not-say.md)).
- A public site that shows "Powered by Exponential" says something about itself; still ask before you describe its
  internals or quote its staff.
- Partners publish customer stories only with written permission ([Writing a case study](../partners/writing-a-case-study.md));
  ask for that permission before you repeat details.

## Contributors and community members

- Contributors appear in the public Git history under the names or accounts they chose. Use those, and do not dig
  further into their private lives.
- Forum and chat posts are public, but a person posting a question is not a public figure. Ask before you quote them
  by name, or quote without the name.
- Advocates speak for themselves ([Disclosure and conflicts of interest](../advocates/disclosure-and-conflicts-of-interest.md)).

## People who report problems

- Someone who reported a vulnerability privately may not want to be named; respect their choice
  ([Covering security fixes](covering-security-fixes.md)).
- Bug reports in the public trackers may contain personal data by mistake; do not reproduce it.

## Images and recordings

- Screenshots and videos must not show personal data of real people ([Privacy on camera](privacy-on-camera.md)).
- Blur names, e-mail addresses, IP addresses and order details; better, use a demo installation
  ([Taking your own screenshots](taking-your-own-screenshots.md)).

## What this documentation does

- It describes people by their role ("the founder of 7x", "contributors") and names accounts only as they appear in
  the public history.
- It does not repeat telephone numbers or postal addresses, even where the organizations publish them
  ([Press contacts](press-contacts.md)).
- It contains no customer names and no personal data.

Data protection laws differ by country; your outlet's legal advisers know the rules that apply to you.
