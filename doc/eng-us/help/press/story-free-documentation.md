# Story: free documentation

This page is for journalists who write about open knowledge and documentation. Many open source projects license their
code freely but not their manuals. Exponential's documentation is free documentation: anyone may copy, translate,
adapt and redistribute it.

## The story in one paragraph

The documentation in the repository's `doc/` directory, including the help in the administration, is
"Copyright (C) 1998 - 2026 Exponential Foundation and 7x. All rights reserved." and licensed under the GNU Free
Documentation License, Version 1.3 or later, with no invariant sections and no cover texts
([The license of this documentation](../../README-LICENSE.md)). A plain-words page explains what that lets readers do:
read, copy, share, translate, adapt and cite it ([Free documentation](../free-documentation.md)). Older pages inherited
from eZ Systems keep their original terms. Around the documentation, the community keeps the eZpedia wiki under the
GFDL since 2006, and free training books are mirrored on GitHub.

| Training books, "eZ Publish Training Books (100% Compatible With Exponential)", as free PDFs | same |

## What not to claim

| Do not say | Say instead |
|---|---|
| "Creative Commons documentation" | GNU FDL 1.3 or later |
| "All documentation is GFDL" | The project's own pages are; inherited older pages keep their terms |
| "Public domain" | Copyrighted and freely licensed |
