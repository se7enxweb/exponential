# Writing about the eZ Publish years

This page is for journalists who need to mention the software's past. Exponential continues a code base that was
developed as **eZ Publish**. That past is real and worth telling; the rule is only that it stays the past.

## The rule

> eZ Publish appears only in the past tense, as the name the software had while eZ Systems developed it, a product
> that its creator discontinued after almost 18 years of service. The software today is Exponential.

Sources: [Brand and naming rules](../business/brand-and-naming-rules.md#the-old-name-ez-publish-only-as-history),
[Who is 7x?](../who-is-7x.md#the-ez-publish-years-1999-to-2021).

## What the eZ Publish years were

| Generation | Years | What it brought | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| eZ Publish 1 and 2 | 2000 to 2002 | The first generation, with eZ Trade for shops; lives on as Exponential Basic | [The eZ Publish years](../who-is-7x.md#the-ez-publish-years-1999-to-2021) |
| eZ Publish 3 | 2002 to 2007 | The content model still used today: classes, objects, versions, siteaccesses, the template language | same |
| eZ Publish 4 | 2007 to 2012 | The PHP 5 generation, ezjscore, the online editor | same |
| eZ Publish 5 and community builds | 2012 to 2021 | The legacy kernel beside a Symfony stack; releases until September 2021 | same; [release overview](../../release-overview) |

The successors of eZ Publish took other roads: "eZ Platform was eZ Publish's technological successor before being
abandoned by Ibexa", and Ibexa DXP OSS is "the rebranded and evolved successor of eZ Platform" (README).

## Sentences that work

```text
"Exponential continues a code base first developed from 1999 as eZ Publish."
"The software was called eZ Publish until 2025, when it was renamed Exponential."
"eZ Publish, a product its creator discontinued after almost 18 years, ..."
"The 6.0 line was announced in January 2024, while the software was still called eZ Publish."
"Sites built in the eZ Publish years can be moved onto Exponential 6."
```

## Sentences that do not

| Avoid | Why | Instead |
|---|---|---|
| "eZ Publish 6 is out." | Present tense, current name | "Exponential 6.0.14 is out." |
| "Exponential, also known as eZ Publish" | A second current name | "Exponential, called eZ Publish until 2025" |
| "the new eZ Publish" | Implies continuity of the product brand and its owner | "Exponential continues the code base" |
| "eZ Publish by 7x" | Mixes the old brand with the new steward | "Exponential, led by 7x" |
| "eZ Systems' Exponential" | No association exists | "Exponential (not associated with eZ Systems or Ibexa)" |

## Historic titles and quotes

Quote historic titles exactly; do not rename them:

- eZpedia, "The free eZ Publish encyclopedia that anyone can edit" (<https://ezpedia.exponential.earth/>).
- The book "Learning eZ Publish", now released under the GNU FDL ([The community](../who-is-7x.md#the-community)).
- Press releases from 2024 and 2025 whose titles use the former name ([Earlier press releases and coverage](earlier-press-releases-and-coverage.md)).

## Sites built in those years

When a story is about migration, follow 7x's own wording in substance: moving a site built while the software was
still called eZ Publish onto the current Exponential 6 ([Services and hosting](../who-is-7x.md#services-and-hosting);
[Migrating from 4.x](../../install/14-migrating-from-4x.md)).
