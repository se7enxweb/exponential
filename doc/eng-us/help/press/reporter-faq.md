## The product

**What is Exponential?**
A free, open source PHP content management system and application framework with a fully customizable content model,
multisite, many languages and a built-in shop ([Exponential in brief](exponential-in-brief.md)).

**What is the newest version?**
6.0.14, released on 7 June 2026. 6.0.15 is in development ([The 6.0.x releases at a glance](the-6-0-x-releases-at-a-glance.md)).

**What is new?**
The 6.0.15 line adds Exponential Velocity, Exponential Layouts, Exponential Intelligence, the admin4 design, an audit
trail and a help system ([Feature stories: index](feature-stories-index.md)).

**Does it use AI?**
Exponential Intelligence is a writing assistant that writes drafts, as the signed-in editor, and never publishes on its
own; it sends nothing anywhere until a provider is configured ([Story: Exponential Intelligence](story-exponential-intelligence.md)).

## The organizations

**Who owns Exponential?**
Nobody owns it in the sense of a proprietary product. 7x and the Exponential Foundation hold the copyright together; the
software is licensed under the GNU GPL ([Licenses explained for reporters](licenses-explained-for-reporters.md)).

**Who is 7x?**
The North American company that leads development, support and hosting; formerly Brookins Consulting ([7x for reporters](7x-for-reporters.md)).

**Who is the Exponential Foundation?**
A 501(c)(3) nonprofit founded on 19 March 2026 in Seattle that operates and funds the project ([The Exponential Foundation for reporters](the-foundation-for-reporters.md)).

## History

**Is Exponential the same as eZ Publish?**
It continues the same code base. The software was called eZ Publish while eZ Systems developed it; that product was
discontinued by its creator after almost 18 years. 7x took over the code base in December 2023 and renamed the product
Exponential in August 2025 ([Writing about the eZ Publish years](writing-about-the-ez-publish-years.md)).

**Is it related to Ibexa?**
No. "This project is not associated with the original eZ Publish software or its original developer, eZ Systems or
Ibexa" (README). The rename was made at Ibexa's request ([Why it is called Exponential](why-it-is-called-exponential.md)).

**When did 7x start?**
Founded in 2001 as Brookins Consulting; the idea of 7x in 2014, the project in 2022, the launch in December 2023
([7x in three stages: 2014, 2022, 2023](7x-in-three-stages.md)).

## Money

**What does it cost?**
The software is free of charge under the GPL; "The Professional License is no longer available" (README). 7x sells
services such as development, support and hosting.

**How is it funded?**
By 7x's contributions and by sponsors, through GitHub Sponsors pages for 7x and for the Foundation
([Writing about funding and sponsors](writing-about-funding-and-sponsors.md)).

## Use and adoption

**How many sites use it?**
No source counts installations, users or customers ([What the sources do not say](what-the-sources-do-not-say.md)).

**Who uses it?**
No customer list is published. Name an organization as a user only with its consent ([Privacy in your coverage](privacy-in-coverage.md)).

**Where does it run?**
PHP 8.0 to 8.5; SQLite, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, MariaDB, Oracle or MySQL; Linux, BSD, macOS and Windows in documented tiers
([Fact sheet for journalists](fact-sheet-for-journalists.md)).

## Security

**Is it secure?**
Do not print "secure" as an absolute. Print the process: private reports to security@se7enx.com, a first answer within
one business day, fixes in the next release, published versions never changed, a Content Security Policy in the
administration, an audit trail and a hardening guide ([Story: security](story-security.md)).

**I found a vulnerability. What do I do?**
Report it privately; do not publish it before the fix ([If you find a vulnerability](if-you-find-a-vulnerability.md)).
