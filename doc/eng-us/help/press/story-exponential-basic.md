# Story: Exponential Basic

This page is for journalists who write about small sites, education or software history. Exponential Basic is the
smallest member of the family and has the longest thread of history behind 7x.

## Contents

1. [The story in one paragraph](#the-story-in-one-paragraph)

3. [What not to claim](#what-not-to-claim)

## The story in one paragraph

"Exponential Basic is the simple to us to use CMS Framework that is for everyone, educational purposes, personal use
and worldwide general" (repository description, quoted as published). It descends from the second generation of the
code base: its repository is a fork of `brookinsconsulting/ezcommunity2`, the eZ Publish 2.2 line that Brookins
Consulting kept alive with PHP 5 support long after that generation ended ([Exponential Basic](../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#exponential-basic),
[Who is 7x?, Exponential Basic](../who-is-7x.md#exponential-basic)).

## What not to claim

| Do not say | Say instead |
|---|---|
| "A light version of Exponential 6" | A separate line, descending from the 2.x generation |
| "eZ Publish 2 is back" | Exponential Basic continues that line under its own name |

## Angles and headline ideas

- "The smallest CMS in the family, with the longest memory."
- "A CMS for classrooms, kept on current PHP."
