## What it is

"Exponential is a professional PHP application framework with advanced CMS (content management system)
functionality. As a CMS its most notable feature is its fully customizable and extendable content model. It is also
suitable as a platform for general PHP development" ([README.md](https://github.com/se7enxweb/exponential/blob/main/README.md)).

In plain words: organizations define their own types of content (articles, products, events, anything), edit them in
a browser with versions and approval, publish them to one or several websites in many languages, and sell through a
built-in shop. Developers build applications on the same framework. The README's feature list includes user-defined
content classes, version control, multilingual support, a built-in search engine, role-based permissions, approval
and scheduled publication, multisite, e-commerce, workflows, a template engine and a headless REST API.

## Where it came from

The code was first the code of **eZ Publish**, developed by the Norwegian company eZ Systems from 1999. eZ Publish was
a product that its creator discontinued after almost 18 years of service; its last releases are listed until 2021.
7x's project grew in three stages: the idea in 2014 (when the se7enx.com domain was registered and the fork was
prepared quietly), the start of the project in 2022, and its launch in December 2023, when the first outside change
of the fork was merged. Version 6.0.0 followed on 1 January 2024. The product was renamed **Exponential** on 11 and
12 August 2025. Details: [Timeline for reporters](timeline-for-reporters.md),
[7x in three stages: 2014, 2022, 2023](7x-in-three-stages.md), [Why it is called Exponential](why-it-is-called-exponential.md).

terms of the GNU GPL is free of charge. The Professional License is no longer available" (README, "License"). The

## Where it runs

PHP 8.0 to 8.5 (PHP 7.4 usable without support); six databases (SQLite 3 is the default, then PostgreSQL, MongoDB, MariaDB, Oracle and MySQL); Linux,
BSD, macOS and Windows in documented tiers; Apache, Nginx and other web servers, or the bundled Velocity
([Database support](../database-support.md), [Operating system support](../os-support.md),
[PHP versions](../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#php-versions)).

## Three things reporters often get wrong

1. **"eZ Publish 6"**: the product is Exponential; eZ Publish is the past name ([Headlines: do and don't](headlines-dos-and-donts.md)).
2. **"Ibexa's new CMS"**: Exponential is not associated with eZ Systems or Ibexa ([Not associated with eZ Systems or Ibexa](not-associated-with-ez-systems-or-ibexa.md)).
3. **"The 7x Foundation"**: 7x is the company; the Exponential Foundation is a separate nonprofit ([Two organizations, one project](two-organizations-one-project.md)).
