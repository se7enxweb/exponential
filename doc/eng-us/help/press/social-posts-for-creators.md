# Social posts for creators

This page is for creators who post about Exponential. Short posts lose nuance first; these templates keep the facts
that matter. Replace the angle brackets, keep the sources.

## Contents

1. [Rules for short posts](#rules-for-short-posts)
2. [Templates](#templates)
3. [Accounts to tag](#accounts-to-tag)
4. [Sponsored and gifted posts](#sponsored-and-gifted-posts)

## Rules for short posts

- One claim per post, with a link that proves it.
- The product is Exponential; the old name only as history ([Naming quick reference for news editors](naming-quick-reference-for-editors.md)).
- "Released" only for a tagged version.
- No superlatives without a published comparison ([Headlines: do and don't](headlines-dos-and-donts.md)).
- No screenshots with personal data or secrets ([Keeping secrets off screen](keeping-secrets-off-screen.md)).

## Templates

```text
RELEASE
Exponential <version> is out. <One change in plain words>. Changelog: <link>

IN DEVELOPMENT
Coming in the next Exponential release (6.0.15, in development): <feature>. From the changelog: <link>

FEATURE
Exponential Intelligence, the writing assistant in Exponential, writes drafts and never publishes on its
own. How it works: <link to your video or post>

TUTORIAL
New tutorial: <task> in Exponential <version>, step by step. <link>

VIDEO OR STREAM
Live at <time, time zone>: installing Exponential from zero on SQLite, in one command. <link>

HISTORY
The oldest commit in Exponential's repository is from September 2002. The code base has been developed since
1999; the name Exponential since August 2025. <link>

CORRECTION
Correction to my post above: <right statement> (source: <link>).
```

Advocates keep more templates: [Social post templates](../advocates/social-post-templates.md).

## Accounts to tag

Use the accounts listed on the project website footer ([Official channels and social accounts](official-channels-and-social-accounts.md)):
the Exponential account for product news, the 7x account for company news. Tagging an account is not an endorsement
by it. No official hashtag is documented.

## Sponsored and gifted posts

Put the disclosure at the start, where it is seen before "read more":

```text
[Sponsored by <who>] <post>
[Ad] <post>
[Gifted: <what>, no obligation to post] <post>
```

Use the labels your platform provides as well. Details: [Disclosure and sponsorship ethics](disclosure-and-sponsorship-ethics.md).
