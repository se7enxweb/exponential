# Story: a code base kept alive

This page is for journalists who write the general-interest story: what happens to software when its creator moves
on. Exponential is a documented answer.

## Contents

1. [The story in one paragraph](#the-story-in-one-paragraph)

## The story in one paragraph

The code was developed from 1999 by eZ Systems as eZ Publish, a product that its creator discontinued after almost 18
years; its last releases are listed until September 2021. "Exponential has made it beyond it's end of life in 2021 and
survived" (README). 7x, which as Brookins Consulting had worked with the software since its first versions, launched
its project in December 2023, released 6.0.0 on 1 January 2024 and fourteen more releases by June 2026, moved the code
to PHP 8.0 to 8.5 and renamed it Exponential in August 2025; in March 2026 a nonprofit, the Exponential Foundation, was founded for it
([The history for long-form stories](the-history-for-long-form-stories.md)).

## What not to claim

| Do not say | Say instead |
|---|---|
| "Rescued from eZ Systems" | Continued under the GPL; no dealings with eZ Systems are documented |
| "Never stopped" | The original line ended in 2021; 7x launched its project in December 2023 |
| "Thousands of legacy sites saved" | No usage numbers exist |

## Angles and headline ideas
