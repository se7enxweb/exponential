# Story: Exponential Platform DXP

This page is for journalists who cover Symfony and enterprise content platforms. Exponential Platform DXP is the
Symfony-only member of the family, separate from the Exponential 6 kernel.

## The story in one paragraph

"Exponential Platform DXP is a free, open-source Digital Experience Platform built on the Symfony framework, the
community continuation of the eZ Platform and Ibexa OSS line maintained by 7x" (README). Its meta package describes
itself as "Stable; Open Source", licensed "GPL-2.0-or-later or proprietary". It ships the admin UI, content forms,
REST, GraphQL and GraphiQL, HTTP cache, core and Solr search, several field types, notifications, messenger, cron and
JWT authentication. It does **not** ship a layouts editor, an approval workflow, a scheduler or the legacy kernel
([Exponential Platform DXP](../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#exponential-platform-dxp)).

## What not to claim

| Do not say | Say instead |
|---|---|
| "Ibexa DXP for free" or "by Ibexa" | A community continuation by 7x; not associated with Ibexa |
| "Includes the page builder" | It does not ship a layouts editor; Nexus does ([Story: Exponential Platform Nexus and Legacy](story-nexus-and-legacy.md)) |
| "Runs Exponential 6" | It does not include the legacy kernel |

## Angles and headline ideas

- "A free Symfony DXP, continued by the community."
- "Two lines, one family: PHP-native Exponential and Symfony-based Platform."
