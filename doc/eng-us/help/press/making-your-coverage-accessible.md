# Making your coverage accessible

This page is for writers, picture editors and producers. Exponential's administration is tested for contrast and
target sizes ([Story: accessibility](story-accessibility.md)); coverage of it should be readable by the same people:
readers who use screen readers, magnification, captions or keyboard only.

## Screenshots and alt text

Describe what the screenshot shows and why it is there, not every pixel.

```text
Weak:   "Screenshot"
Weak:   "Exponential admin"
Good:   "The admin4 dashboard of Exponential in dark mode, with the content tree on the left and a list of
         recently modified articles."
Good:   "The layouts editor: a block called 'Title' is being dragged into the main zone of a page layout."
```

If the screenshot shows text that matters to the story (an error message, a setting), put that text in the article too.

## Code, commands and tables

- Put commands in text, never only in an image: readers need to copy them, and screen readers need to read them.
- Use real tables with header rows for comparisons, not images of tables.
- Keep code blocks short and introduce them with a sentence that says what they do.

## Links

- Link text says where it goes: "the project's security policy", not "click here".
- Mark links to downloads and PDFs as such: "the press kit (ZIP, 43 KB)".

## Charts and figures

- Give the numbers in the text or a table next to the chart.
- Do not rely on color alone; label lines and bars.
- Keep the conditions of performance figures beside the chart ([Verifying numbers](verifying-numbers.md)).

## Video and audio

- Captions for every video, checked by a person, especially for product names and commands
  ([Captions and accessible video](captions-and-accessible-video.md)).
- Say aloud what you click: "I open Setup, then Extensions" rather than "I click here".
- Transcripts for podcasts, with the commands and links written out.

## Plain language

- Explain terms at first use: content management system, content class, siteaccess
  ([Glossary for journalists](glossary-for-journalists.md)).
- Short sentences for steps; one action per step.
- Expand abbreviations once: Content Security Policy (CSP).
