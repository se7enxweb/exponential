# A reseller's first 90 days

This page is for owners starting a reseller line around Exponential. It turns the reseller pages into a plan for the
first three months. Adjust the pace to your team; do not skip the steps.

## Contents

1. [Month 1: decide](#month-1-decide)
2. [Month 2: build](#month-2-build)
3. [Month 3: sell and deliver](#month-3-sell-and-deliver)
4. [After 90 days](#after-90-days)

## Month 1: decide

| Week | Do | Page |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Learn what you may sell and what you owe when you distribute | [What reselling means for free software](../basics/what-reselling-means-for-free-software.md), [What you can and cannot sell](../basics/what-you-can-and-cannot-sell.md) |
| 1 | Learn who is behind the product | [Working with 7x and the Foundation](../basics/working-with-7x-and-the-foundation.md) |
| 2 | Choose your reseller models | [Reseller models](../basics/reseller-models.md) |
| 3 | Fill in the cost model | [Margin and cost worksheet](../offers/margin-and-cost-worksheet.md) |
| 4 | Choose a partner level; apply, pay, then sign | [Choosing a partner level](../basics/choosing-a-partner-level.md), [How to apply](../../partners/how-to-apply.md) |

- [ ] Models chosen and written down.
- [ ] Break-even number of customers known.
- [ ] Partner level chosen; application sent.

## Month 2: build

| Week | Do | Page |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Tiers and prices | [Packaging offers in tiers](../offers/packaging-offers-in-tiers.md), [Pricing worksheet](../offers/pricing-worksheet.md) |
| 6 | Service levels from your real team | [Service level design](../offers/service-level-design.md), [Support tiers you offer](../offers/support-tiers-you-offer.md) |
| 6 | Servers or supplier checked | [Hosting requirements for your offers](../offers/hosting-requirements-for-offers.md) |
| 7 | Contract template reviewed by a lawyer | [Contracts with customers](../customers/contracts-with-customers.md) |
| 7 | Security and data protection procedures | [Security responsibilities](../responsibilities/security-responsibilities.md), [Data protection](../responsibilities/data-protection.md) |
| 8 | Demo site and marketing kit | [Demo sites](../offers/demo-sites.md), [Marketing kit](../sales/marketing-kit.md) |

- [ ] Tier sheets with prices and exclusions.
- [ ] Service level statement your staff can keep.
- [ ] Contract template and data processing terms.
- [ ] Backup, restore test, monitoring and update procedure working on a test site.
- [ ] Demo site live; marketing material reviewed for correct names.

| Week | Do | Page |
|---|---|---|

- [ ] First customer live, with a tested restore.
- [ ] Monthly routine documented.

## After 90 days

- Review the cost model with real hours.
- Ask the first customers what they value and what they miss.
- Plan your first case study, with consent ([Case studies for resellers](../sales/case-studies.md)).
- Put the yearly review on the calendar ([Yearly checklist](yearly-checklist.md)).
