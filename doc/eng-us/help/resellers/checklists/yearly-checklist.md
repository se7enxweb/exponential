# Yearly checklist for resellers

This page is for owners of reseller businesses. Once a year, step back from the monthly routine and review the whole
line: whether it makes money, whether your promises still hold, and whether the sites are ready for the year ahead.

## Contents

1. [Partnership](#partnership)
2. [Business](#business)
3. [Offers and contracts](#offers-and-contracts)
4. [Customer sites](#customer-sites)
5. [Security and data protection](#security-and-data-protection)
6. [Marketing and giving back](#marketing-and-giving-back)

## Partnership

- [ ] "Partnership lasts one year": plan your renewal ([Renewals, upgrades and downgrades](../../partners/renewals-upgrades-and-downgrades.md)).
- [ ] Is your level still right? Count tickets and meetings really used, and benefits you need
      ([Choosing a partner level](../basics/choosing-a-partner-level.md)).
- [ ] From Professional on: did you meet the revenue requirement each quarter
      (Professional $1,800, Silver $3,000, Gold $4,500, Platinum $7,500)?
- [ ] Points and certifications for the next level ([The points system](../../partners/the-points-system.md)).
- [ ] Logos and partner status on your website match your current level.

## Business

- [ ] Recompute the cost model with the year's real hours ([Margin and cost worksheet](../offers/margin-and-cost-worksheet.md)).
- [ ] Margin per tier and per model; drop or reprice what loses money.
- [ ] Price changes announced with the notice your contracts require ([Pricing your offer](../offers/pricing-your-offer.md#reviewing-prices)).
- [ ] Referral income and commission records reconciled ([Lead handoffs](../sales/lead-handoffs.md#commission-records)).
- [ ] Churn: who left and why ([Renewals and churn](../customers/renewals-and-churn.md)).

## Offers and contracts

- [ ] Service levels compared with measured performance; adjust promises or staff
      ([Service level design](../offers/service-level-design.md)).
- [ ] Tier sheets current; features checked against the current release.
- [ ] Contract template reviewed by your lawyer (law changes, new subprocessors).
- [ ] White-label and other supplier agreements renewed in writing.

## Customer sites

- [ ] Every site on the newest 6.0.x release; plans for any that are not ([Keeping customers current](../customers/keeping-customers-current.md)).
- [ ] PHP versions: which sites run a branch whose security support ends within the year? **Setup > System information**
      warns about ended support ([System information](../../../guides/system-information.md)).
- [ ] Extensions: is every extension still maintained? Plan replacements.
- [ ] Content languages per installation against the limit of 62 on 64-bit PHP ([Multilingual offers](../offers/multilingual-offers.md)).
- [ ] Yearly review meeting held with each customer ([Renewals and churn](../customers/renewals-and-churn.md#the-yearly-review)).

## Security and data protection

- [ ] Security review across all sites ([Security review checklist](../../hosting/checklists/security-review.md)).
- [ ] Staff access reviewed; leavers removed everywhere.
- [ ] Incident plan rehearsed once ([Security responsibilities of resellers](../responsibilities/security-responsibilities.md#incidents)).
- [ ] Subprocessor list and data processing terms current ([Data protection for resellers](../responsibilities/data-protection.md)).
- [ ] Demo sites: rebuilt on schedule, no real data, demo mode not on any production process
      ([Demo sites for resellers](../offers/demo-sites.md)).

## Marketing and giving back

- [ ] Marketing kit reviewed and dated ([Marketing kit for resellers](../sales/marketing-kit.md)).
- [ ] At least one new case study or reference, with consent ([Case studies for resellers](../sales/case-studies.md)).
- [ ] Customer manual updated for screens that changed ([Documentation you can give your customers](../customers/documentation-for-your-customers.md)).
- [ ] Contributions back to the project this year: bug reports, fixes, documentation
      ([Contributing code back](../../partners/contributing-code-back.md),
      [Contributing documentation and translations](../../partners/contributing-docs-and-translations.md)).
- [ ] Support for the Foundation considered ([Working with the Exponential Foundation](../../partners/working-with-the-foundation.md)).
