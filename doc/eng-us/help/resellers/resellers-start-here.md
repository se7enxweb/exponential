# Resellers: start here

This section of the help is for **companies and freelancers who sell offers built on Exponential** to their own
customers: hosting companies that resell plans, agencies that sell managed sites and support contracts, partners who
deliver 7x services under their own name, makers of OEM products and extension bundles, trainers who resell courses,
and anyone who only passes leads to 7x for a commission.

It answers four questions: what you may sell when the software itself is free, which reseller model suits your
business, how to build, price and sell an offer that you can actually deliver, and what you owe your customers after
the contract is signed (updates, security, data protection, a fair exit).

> **Note**
> Every statement about the partner program on these pages is quoted from, or linked to, the partner pages of this
> help, which cite the published program pages with the date they were read. Every technical statement names the
> file, setting or command it comes from. Pages about licenses, contracts and taxes describe common practice; they
> are **not legal or tax advice**. Examples use placeholders such as "your company" and `example.com`.

## Reselling Exponential in one minute

- **The software costs nothing; your work is what you sell.** Exponential is licensed under the GNU General Public
  License, version 2 or any later version (`composer.json`: `"license": "GPL-2.0-or-later"`). "Using Exponential
  under the terms of the GNU GPL is free of charge" (`README.md`). You may charge for hosting, support, development,
  training, packaging and distribution, but there is no "Exponential license" to sell
  ([What reselling means for free software](basics/what-reselling-means-for-free-software.md)).
- **Distributing brings duties.** If you hand over copies (an image, a download, a product), the recipients get the
  GPL, the copyright notices and the source code ([Distribution obligations checklist](responsibilities/distribution-obligations-checklist.md)).
- **Pick a model.** Hosting reseller, managed service, white label, OEM product, extension and theme bundles,
  support contracts, training, or referral only ([Reseller models](basics/reseller-models.md)).
- **The partner program is the formal route.** Levels Student, Now, Starter, Basic, Professional, Silver, Gold and
  Platinum, from $25 to $2,600 a month; "OEM Resale Opportunities" at every level, "White-Label 7x Services" from
  Professional ([Choosing a partner level for reselling](basics/choosing-a-partner-level.md)).
- **Referrals pay too.** The program pays **10%** of the total contract value for a cold lead and **20%** for a hot
  lead (a customer ready to purchase from 7x) ([Referral-only reselling](basics/referral-only-reselling.md)).
- **Promise only what you deliver.** The response times of the partner plans are what 7x promises **you**, not what
  you can promise your customers ([Service level design](offers/service-level-design.md)).
- **Name things correctly.** The product is Exponential; your status is "Exponential Partner (Level)"
  ([Naming and brand for resellers](sales/naming-and-brand.md)).

## What sets reselling free software apart

| In proprietary reselling | With Exponential |
|---|---|
| You buy licenses at a discount and sell them on | There are no licenses to buy; you sell services, products and your own work |
| The vendor can stop the customer using the software | Nobody can: the GPL gives every recipient the same rights |
| Your margin is the license discount | Your margin is the value of your work, plus partner discounts on 7x services |
| The customer is locked to the channel | The customer may move to another host or partner at any time ([Licensing for hosts](../hosting/business/licensing-for-hosts.md)) |
| Documentation belongs to the vendor | The help is free documentation you may copy, adapt and rebrand under the GNU FDL 1.3 ([Documentation you can give your customers](customers/documentation-for-your-customers.md)) |

That last row is a strength to sell: a customer who knows they can leave is more willing to sign.
