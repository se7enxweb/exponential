# White-label reseller

This page is for partners who want to sell services that 7x delivers, under their own brand. "White-Label 7x
Services" is a benefit from the **Professional** level on (<https://exponential.earth/partner/benefits>, read
18 October 2026; [White-label 7x services](../../partners/white-label-services.md)).

Which of these are available white-label, at what price and under which terms is **not published**. Ask the partner
team, <partners@exponential.earth>, and get it in writing.

## Where your margin comes from

| Level | Discount on 7x services | Discount on subscription products |
|---|---|---|
| Professional | 5% | 4% |
| Silver | 10% | 15% |
| Gold | 15% | 20% |
| Platinum | 20% | 25% |

Source: [Partner benefits in detail](../../partners/partner-benefits.md#discounts-by-level). Below Professional the
white-label benefit does not apply.

The discount alone is rarely enough margin. Most white-label resellers add their own work on top: the first line of
support, account management, reporting, editor training or design. Model it on the
[Margin and cost worksheet](../offers/margin-and-cost-worksheet.md).

```text
White-label margin per customer per month
  Price to the customer .......................... $________
  7x price for the service ....................... $________
  minus your discount (___ %) .................... $________
  = your cost of the 7x service .................. $________
  Your own work (hours x rate) ................... $________
  Margin = price - cost - own work ............... $________
```

## Settle these in writing

- [ ] Which services, with which service levels on the 7x side.
- [ ] Prices, your discount and how often prices may change.
- [ ] Who talks to the end customer when something breaks, and how you escalate to 7x.
- [ ] Where data is hosted and who processes personal data ([Data protection for resellers](../responsibilities/data-protection.md)).
- [ ] What happens to the end customer's site if your partnership or the white-label agreement ends.
- [ ] Whether the white-label revenue counts toward your level's revenue requirement.

## Honest white labeling

White label means your brand on the service; it does not mean hiding facts the customer needs.

- Your contract names **you** as responsible to the customer. Under the partner contract "Partners are fully
  responsible for their subcontractors" (<https://exponential.earth/partner/program>), and the same logic applies when
  7x is your subcontractor.
- Data protection law may require you to name subprocessors to the customer; check yours.
- Never claim to have built the software. Exponential is GPL software by 7x, the Exponential Foundation and its
  contributors ([Licensing for partners](../../partners/licensing-for-partners.md)).
- Do not promise the customer a response time shorter than the one you have in writing from 7x, minus the time you
  need yourself ([Service level design](../offers/service-level-design.md)).

## A white-label service sheet

```text
Service name (your brand): ______________________
Delivered by: 7x, under agreement dated ________ (internal; disclose as required by law)
What the customer gets: ______________________
Your first-line support: hours ______  channel ______
Escalation to 7x: channel ______  agreed response ______
Data location and processors: ______________________
End of service: data export within ____ days; handover as in our offboarding procedure
```
