# Training reseller

This page is for training companies, agencies and freelancers who sell courses about Exponential, to their own
customers' editors or on the open market. The trainers' section of the help has the course modules themselves
([Trainers: start here](../../trainers/trainers-start-here.md)); this page is about selling them.

## Material you may build on

The help and the documentation in `doc/` are under the GNU FDL 1.3 or later. You may copy them into course
material, translate them and adapt them, for free or for a fee, if your material:

- keeps the copyright notices and the license notice, and includes the license text;
- is released under the same license when you change it, and says what you changed and by whom;
- has a title of its own, unless the original publisher agrees that you keep it.

Details: [Free documentation](../../free-documentation.md) and
[Documentation you can give your customers](../customers/documentation-for-your-customers.md). Your own slides,
exercises and recordings that do not copy the help are your own work.

## Describing your course honestly

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "Exponential for editors, a one-day course by <Your company>" | "Official Exponential training" (unless the program defines and grants it) |
| "Based on the free Exponential documentation" | Remove the license notices from copied pages |
| Name the version taught: "Exponential 6.0.x" | Teach screens from an old version without saying so |
| "Certificate of attendance from <Your company>" | "Exponential certification" ([Certifications](../../partners/certifications.md)) |

## Course offer sheet

```text
Course: ______________________   Audience: ______________________
Version taught: Exponential 6.0.__   Format: on site / online / recorded
Length: ____ hours   Group size: max ____   Prerequisites: ______________
Outcomes (three): 1. ______________ 2. ______________ 3. ______________
Material: our slides + adapted help pages (GNU FDL 1.3, notices kept)
Lab: a training installation per group, rebuilt after each course
Price: per seat $____ / per day $____   Includes: material, certificate of attendance
```

For a training installation that visitors may sign in to, see [Demo sites](../offers/demo-sites.md).
