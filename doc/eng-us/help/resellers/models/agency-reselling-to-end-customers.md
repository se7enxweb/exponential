# Agencies reselling to end customers

This page is for web agencies and freelancers who build Exponential sites for clients and want to sell everything
around the site as well: hosting, a care plan, support, training. It brings together the reseller models from an
agency's point of view.

## The agency's offer stack

| Layer | One-off or recurring | Page |
|---|---|---|
| Discovery and content modeling | One-off | [Pre-sales questions and answers](../sales/pre-sales-questions-and-answers.md) |
| Build: design, templates, extensions | One-off | [Implementation partners](../../partners/implementation-partners.md) |
| Hosting | Recurring | [Hosting reseller](hosting-reseller.md) |
| Care plan (updates, backups, monitoring) | Recurring | [Managed service reseller](managed-service-reseller.md) |
| Support contract | Recurring | [Support contract reseller](support-contract-reseller.md) |
| Editor training | One-off, repeated for new staff | [Training reseller](training-reseller.md) |
| Small changes | Hours from a bank | [Packaging offers in tiers](../offers/packaging-offers-in-tiers.md) |

The recurring layers are what keep an agency stable between projects.

## Build or buy in

| Layer | Do it yourself when ... | Buy in when ... | From whom |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hosting | You already run servers well | You do not have operations staff | A hosting partner, or white-label 7x hosting from Professional ([White-label reseller](white-label-reseller.md)) |
| Care plan | You have a developer who owns updates | Your developers are always on projects | A managed host, or 7x |
| Out-of-hours support | You can staff an on-call rota | You cannot | A support provider or paid 7x support |
| Large projects | Your team can carry them | The project is bigger than your team | Refer it: 10% cold / 20% hot commission ([Referral-only reselling](../basics/referral-only-reselling.md)) |

Whatever you buy in, the client's contract is with you, and you are responsible for your subcontractors.

## Who owns what

Clear ownership prevents most disputes at the end of a relationship. Put this in the contract:

| Item | Usual owner | Note |
|---|---|---|
| The content and the database | The client | Exportable at any time |
| The domain name | The client, registered in their name | Not in the agency's account |
| Exponential and project extensions | Nobody exclusively: GPL | The client has the same rights as you |
| Custom extensions and templates you wrote | Agreed in the contract | If you hand them over, the GPL duties apply ([Distribution obligations checklist](../responsibilities/distribution-obligations-checklist.md)) |
| Design files, fonts, images | Agreed; check their licenses | Stock images often cannot be transferred |
| Administrator accounts | The client has one of their own | Never only the agency's |

The client's view of the same questions is on [What you own](../../customers/what-you-own.md).

## Pricing the stack

1. Price the project as a project.
2. Price each recurring layer from its real cost ([Margin and cost worksheet](../offers/margin-and-cost-worksheet.md)).
3. Offer the recurring layers as tiers, not as a list of extras ([Packaging offers in tiers](../offers/packaging-offers-in-tiers.md)).
4. Include the first year of the care plan in the project offer, so the client never runs an unmaintained site.

## The agency's reselling checklist

- [ ] Every recurring layer has a service description and a price.
- [ ] Every layer you buy in is covered by a written agreement with its supplier.
- [ ] Your service levels can be met with your own staff ([Service level design](../offers/service-level-design.md)).
- [ ] The contract states ownership as in the table above ([Contracts with customers](../customers/contracts-with-customers.md)).
- [ ] The client has their own administrator account and the domain in their name.
- [ ] A handover procedure exists before the first site goes live ([Handing over](../customers/handing-over.md)).
- [ ] Editor training is in the launch plan ([Launch checklist](../checklists/launch-checklist.md)).
