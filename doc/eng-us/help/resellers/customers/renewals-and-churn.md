# Renewals and churn

This page is for owners and account managers at resellers. Under the GPL a customer can take their site to another
provider at any time ([What reselling means for free software](../basics/what-reselling-means-for-free-software.md)).
That is not a threat to manage away with contract tricks; it means your customers renew only because your service
is worth it.

## Why customers leave

| Reason | Early sign | What helps |
|---|---|---|
| They do not see what you do | No questions, no contact | A short monthly or quarterly report ([Monthly checklist](../checklists/monthly-checklist.md)) |
| Slow or unclear support | Repeated "any news?" messages | Measured service levels, honest status updates |
| A bad incident | One outage handled badly | A written incident report and what changed afterwards |
| Price | Questions about the invoice | Show what the price pays for ([Pricing your offer](../offers/pricing-your-offer.md#explaining-the-price)) |
| The site feels old | Comparisons with competitors' sites | A yearly roadmap with small improvements |
| A new decision maker | New names in e-mails | A meeting to introduce yourself and your service |

## The renewal cycle

```text
-90 days   Review the account: tickets, incidents, updates, open wishes
-60 days   Yearly review meeting with the customer
-45 days   Send the renewal offer (with any price change, as the contract requires)
-30 days   Follow up; answer questions
  0        Renewal, or a planned exit
+30 days   After renewal: confirm next year's plan in writing
```

Set the dates from your contract's notice periods ([Contracts with customers](contracts-with-customers.md)).

## The yearly review

Bring to the meeting:

- [ ] versions kept current: the list of updates applied and when ([Keeping customers current](keeping-customers-current.md));
- [ ] support: tickets by severity, measured response times;
- [ ] backups and restore tests, with dates;
- [ ] incidents and what was done;
- [ ] what is coming: PHP version changes, extensions to replace, new Exponential features worth using;
- [ ] the customer's plans for next year: languages, sites, shop, campaigns.

The customer's own list of questions for this meeting is on
[Reviewing your provider each year](../../customers/partners-and-hosts/reviewing-your-provider-each-year.md); read it so
you are ready.

## Price changes

- Announce them as the contract requires, with the reason (costs, scope).
- Offer a choice where possible: the same price with less, or the new price with the same.
- Never raise prices because leaving is hard; it is not, and the customer knows.

## When a customer leaves

1. Thank them and confirm the exit date.
2. Do the handover properly ([Handing over](handing-over.md)).
3. Ask why, and record the answer.
4. Delete their data on schedule.
5. Leave the door open: many customers return.

## Your own partner renewal

"Partnership lasts one year" (partner requirements page), and partner tiers are reviewed monthly. Review your level
before your own renewal with the [Margin and cost worksheet](../offers/margin-and-cost-worksheet.md) and
[Renewals, upgrades and downgrades](../../partners/renewals-upgrades-and-downgrades.md). After a downgrade, update your
use of the partner logo within 30 days.
