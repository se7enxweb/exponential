# Documentation you can give your customers

This page is for resellers who want to give customers a manual: for editors, store staff or site owners. You do not
have to write it from nothing. This help is **free documentation** under the GNU Free Documentation License 1.3 or
later ([Free documentation](../../free-documentation.md)), and that lets you copy, adapt, translate and rebrand it, on
conditions. This page is a summary, not legal advice: the license text decides.

## Three ways to use the help

| Way | Effort | Conditions |
|---|---|---|
| Link to pages | None | "Linking to the pages needs no conditions at all" ([Licensing for hosts](../../hosting/business/licensing-for-hosts.md#copying-this-documentation)) |
| Copy pages unchanged | Low | Keep the notices and include the license |
| Adapt and rebrand into your own manual | Medium | The conditions for modified versions, below |

## What you may do

From [Free documentation](../../free-documentation.md):

- Copy pages, or the whole documentation, and give the copies to anyone, "for free or for a fee, in any medium".
- Translate them; a translation is a modified version.
- Change pages: correct, shorten, extend, rewrite them for your customers, or take sections into your own manual.
- Put them next to your own, independent documents in a collection; the license does not extend to your own
  independent works.

## What you must do

**With every copy:**

- [ ] keep the copyright notices of the pages;
- [ ] keep the license notice (the paragraph that says the GNU FDL applies);
- [ ] include a copy of the license (`doc/eng-us/LICENSE-GFDL-1.3.txt`);
- [ ] add no conditions of your own and no copy protection.

**For a changed version, additionally:**

- [ ] publish it under the same license, the GNU FDL;
- [ ] add your own copyright notice for your changes;
- [ ] say that it was changed and by whom (the license asks for the authors in the title page and a "History" section
      that lists the versions);
- [ ] give it a title of its own, unless the original publisher agrees that you keep its title.

**When you give out many copies:** more than 100 copies in a format that is hard to edit (a PDF without its source)
need an editable copy as well, or a note where to get one; more than 100 printed copies need a cover with the full
title and you as publisher (section 3 of the license).

The copyright notice of the documentation is "Copyright (C) 1998 - 2026 Exponential Foundation and 7x. All rights reserved."
(portions by the Exponential contributors), with no Invariant Sections, no Front-Cover Texts and no Back-Cover Texts
([The license of this documentation](../../../README-LICENSE.md)).

## Rebranding, done right

| Allowed | Not allowed |
|---|---|
| Your own title: "Editing your site: a guide by <Your company>" | Presenting the manual as the original Exponential documentation |
| Your layout, logo and colors | Removing the original copyright notices or the license |
| Screenshots of your customer's design | Claiming you wrote the parts you copied |
| Your own chapters next to adapted pages | Releasing the parts derived from the help under any license other than the GNU FDL |
| Removing pages that do not apply to the customer | Adding terms that stop the customer from copying the manual further |

Your own independent chapters, placed next to the adapted pages, are not covered by the license of the help; whether
a chapter is independent or derived is a question for counsel when in doubt.

A short notice in the manual covers most of it:

```text
This manual is based on the Exponential documentation, Copyright (C) 1998 - 2026 Exponential Foundation and 7x. All rights reserved.
and the Exponential contributors, used under the GNU Free Documentation License 1.3 or later.
Changes: Copyright (C) <year> <Your company>. History: <version>, <date>, <what changed>, <by whom>.
The license is included at the end of this manual.
```

## Pages with other terms

A few older files in `doc/`, inherited from the documentation of the original software (the feature, upgrade,
specification and changelog documents of versions before 6.0, `doc/eng-us/shop/` and a few others), keep their own
terms, the GPL v2 of the repository; the help shows a different notice on those pages
([The license of this documentation](../../../README-LICENSE.md#older-pages-with-their-own-terms)). Do not mix them
into a GNU FDL manual without checking.

## A customer manual plan

```text
Manual: ______________________   For: editors / store staff / owners
Based on (help pages, with versions/dates): ______________________
Our own chapters: ______________________
Title (our own): ______________________
Notices kept: yes   License included: yes   History section: yes
Format: web / PDF + editable source / print (<100 or more copies?)
Review: every Exponential update that changes the screens
```

Give improvements back: a fix to a help page helps every reader ([Contributing documentation and translations](../../partners/contributing-docs-and-translations.md)).
