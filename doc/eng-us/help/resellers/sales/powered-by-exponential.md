# Showing Powered by Exponential as a reseller

This page is for resellers who deliver sites, plans and products running Exponential. A small credit on a live site
shows that real organizations use Exponential, and it earns points in the partner program. The partners' version of
the rules is [Showing "Powered by Exponential"](../../partners/powered-by-exponential.md).

## In the program

| Activity | Points | Source |
|---|---|---|
| "'Powered by Exponential' site badge" | 500 | <https://exponential.earth/partner/requirements>, read 18 October 2026 |
| "Customer site running Exponential" | 1,000 | same |

Where an official badge graphic is published is not stated; ask the partner team. A text credit works everywhere.
Points and how to log them: [The points system](../../partners/the-points-system.md),
[Logging your partner activity](../../partners/logging-partner-activity.md).

## Rules

1. **Only on sites that run Exponential.**
2. **Only with the customer's consent.** The site belongs to the customer; agree it in the contract.
3. **Honest wording.** "Powered by Exponential" or "Built with Exponential"; not "Certified by Exponential".
4. **Link to the project**, <https://exponential.earth/>.
5. **Accessible.** A text link, or an image with the alt text "Powered by Exponential", readable contrast, reachable
   by keyboard.

## Where resellers place it

| Place | Example |
|---|---|
| Customer site footer, next to the copyright | `Powered by Exponential` linked to the project, then `Site by <Your company>` |
| Your hosting plan pages | "Plans for Exponential sites" with a link to the project |
| Your product's About page | "<Product> is based on Exponential" |
| Your demo site | A visible credit and a page about your services ([Demo sites for resellers](../offers/demo-sites.md)) |

```text
Powered by Exponential — https://exponential.earth/
Site by <Your company> — <your URL>
```

## White-label and OEM cases

| Case | What to do |
|---|---|
| White-label 7x services | The credit names the software (Exponential), not the service provider; your brand stays on the service |
| OEM product | State "based on Exponential" in the product's About page and documentation, even if the customer removes the footer credit |
| Customer refuses a credit | Respect it; you can still log the project as a customer site running Exponential if the customer agrees to be named privately to the partner team |

Whatever the credit, the copyright notices in the code and the license files stay where they are; that is a GPL
condition when you distribute, not a matter of branding
([Distribution obligations checklist](../responsibilities/distribution-obligations-checklist.md)).

## A contract clause to copy

```text
Credit. The Customer agrees that the site may show the text "Powered by Exponential", linked to
https://exponential.earth/, and "Site by <Company>" in the footer. The Customer may withdraw this agreement
at any time by written notice; <Company> will remove the credit within <n> business days.
Reference. <Company> may / may not name the Customer as a reference (see the reference consent form).
```
