# Lead handoffs for resellers

This page is for sales staff who pass leads on or receive them. A reseller sits between customers, 7x and other
partners, so leads move in every direction. The program publishes the commission, not the procedure; the procedure
below follows the partner pages' recommended practice ([Handing leads over and back](../../partners/lead-handoffs.md)).

> **Note**
> This is a recommended practice, not a published program rule. Agree its details with 7x and with the partners you
> work with.

## The directions a lead can travel

| From | To | Why | Terms |
|---|---|---|---|
| You | 7x | The customer wants to buy from 7x, or the project is too large | 10% of the total contract value (cold lead), 20% (hot lead) ([Referral-only reselling](../basics/referral-only-reselling.md)) |
| 7x | You | Customer project referrals from Professional; lead priority by level | [Referrals and leads](../../partners/referrals-and-leads.md) |
| You | Another partner | Wrong region, skills or size | Agree with that partner; not set by the program |
| Another partner | You | Same | Same |

## Principles

1. **The customer chooses.** A lead is a person or organization with a need, not property.
2. **One owner at a time.** Whoever has the lead says so, and answers the customer.
3. **Fast or not at all.** A useful default: acknowledge within one business day, contact the customer within two.
4. **Written.** Every hand-off has a note and a date.
5. **Consent first.** Share contact details only with the person's consent, and only what is needed
   ([Data protection for resellers](../responsibilities/data-protection.md)).

## The hand-off note

```text
Lead hand-off
From: <company/role>   To: <company/role>   Date: <date>
Customer: <organization> (contact details shared with consent: yes/no, date)
Need: <one paragraph>
What was said or promised so far: <...>
Cold or hot (as agreed): <...>
Commission / referral terms: <reference to written agreement>
Your role after the hand-off: <none / hosting / training / first-line support>
Next step and by when: <...>
```

## Conflicts

- If two parties already talk to the same customer, the one with the earlier documented contact continues, unless the
  customer decides otherwise.
- Never disparage another partner to win a customer ([Partner code of conduct](../../partners/partner-code-of-conduct.md)).
- Unresolved conflicts go to the partner team at 7x, <partners@exponential.earth>.

## Commission records

Commission disputes are settled by records. Keep, for every lead you pass to 7x:

```text
| Date sent | Organization | Cold/hot | Consent date | Sent to | Outcome | Contract value | Commission % | Invoiced | Paid |
|-----------|--------------|----------|--------------|---------|---------|----------------|--------------|----------|------|
```

Before the first referral, settle in writing how a lead is registered, who classifies it as cold or hot, and when the
commission is paid ([Referral-only reselling](../basics/referral-only-reselling.md#settle-these-before-the-first-lead)).

## Closing the loop

Tell the referring party the outcome (won, lost, no decision) without sharing confidential details. Returning a lead
you cannot serve is good conduct: the program's value of **Sharing** is to "work together as a team, both
internally and with customers, partners and the Exponential community" (<https://exponential.earth/partner/program>).
