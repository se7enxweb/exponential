# Case studies for resellers

This page is for marketing and sales staff at resellers. A case study proves that your offer works for a real
customer. The step-by-step method is on [Writing a case study](../../partners/writing-a-case-study.md); this page adds
what is particular to reselling.

## Why it matters

The program rewards it: "Case study published on exponential.earth" earns 1,000 points and "Customer reference
published" 500 (<https://exponential.earth/partner/requirements>, read 18 October 2026, as documented on
[Writing a case study](../../partners/writing-a-case-study.md)). Buyers trust a story from a customer like them more
than any brochure.

## What a reseller case study shows

A project case study shows the build. A reseller's case study also shows the service over time:

| Section | Content for a reseller |
|---|---|
| The customer | Sector, size, what the site does (only what the customer approves) |
| The situation | Why they needed a provider: no staff, an old site, many brands, a store |
| The offer | Which tier, which services, which model (hosting, managed, support, product) |
| The result after months | Updates applied, incidents handled, restore tests, editors trained, measured response times |
| Exponential used | Version, features (languages, multi-site, shop, layouts) |
| A quote | In the customer's words, approved |

Facts must be checkable: versions, dates, numbers the customer confirms. Use the
[Case study template](../../partners/case-study-template.md).

## Consent and approval

1. Ask for consent in writing **before** you write anything.
2. Interview the customer; do not put words in their mouth.
3. Remove personal data beyond the names and roles the customer approved
   ([Data protection for resellers](../responsibilities/data-protection.md)).
4. Get written approval of the final text.
5. Update or withdraw the case study if facts change or the customer asks.

Levels of permission, from "may be named privately" to "full case study", are on
[Customer references](../../partners/customer-references.md).

## White-label and OEM cases

| Case | What to write |
|---|---|
| You resell white-label 7x services | Describe your service and your work; do not claim to have built the software. Whether to mention 7x as your supplier follows your agreement and the law on subprocessors. |
| You sell an OEM product | Name the product and that it is "based on Exponential" |
| You only referred the lead | The case study belongs to whoever delivered; you may ask to be credited as the referrer |

## Where to publish

| Place | Note |
|---|---|
| Your own website | Always possible with consent |
| The program | Submit it as described on [Writing a case study](../../partners/writing-a-case-study.md) |
| Your partner page on share.exponential.earth | [Your partner page](../../partners/your-partner-page.md) |
| Joint campaigns with 7x | Silver and higher ([Co-marketing with 7x and the project](../../partners/co-marketing.md)) |

If you want others to reuse your case study, publish it under a free license and say so; the program asks for
"FLOSS-licensed" articles to earn article points.
