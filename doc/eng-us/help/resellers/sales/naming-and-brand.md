# Naming and brand for resellers

This page is for anyone at a reseller who writes about Exponential: on your website, in offers, contracts, product
names and invoices. The partners' rules are on [Naming and brand rules for partners](../../partners/naming-and-brand-for-partners.md)
and the full set on [Brand and naming rules](../../business/brand-and-naming-rules.md); this page applies them to
reselling.

## The names

| Write | Meaning | Never write |
|---|---|---|
| **Exponential** | The product, the CMS and framework | The old product name for the product today |
| **Exponential 6**, **Exponential 6.0.x** | The current major line | The old product name with "6" |
| **7x** | The company that leads development, support and hosting | "7X", "SevenX" in running text |
| **the Exponential Foundation** | The nonprofit that operates the project | "the Exponential company" |

The product was renamed on 11 August 2025; its code base comes from a product its creator discontinued after
almost 18 years. Use the old name only for that history, never in an offer. The project states that it is not
associated with the original software or its original developer, eZ Systems or Ibexa
(<https://exponential.earth/about/project>); do not suggest otherwise.

## Your own status

| Your situation | Write | Source |
|---|---|---|
| You are a partner | "Exponential Partner (<Level>)", for example "Exponential Partner (Basic)" | [Naming and brand rules for partners](../../partners/naming-and-brand-for-partners.md) |
| You are a partner with the Distribution profile | You may describe yourself as a reseller: the profile is for "distributors, resellers, and training centers" | [Distribution partners and resellers](../../partners/distribution-partners.md) |
| You are a partner with another profile | Describe what you do: "managed hosting for Exponential sites", "Exponential support" | The partner naming table lists "Exponential reseller" under "never write" unless you have the Distribution profile |
| You are not a partner | Describe what you do; never suggest a status | |
| You are certified | Only certifications you actually hold ([Certifications](../../partners/certifications.md)) | |

## Your brand next to Exponential

| Offer | Naming rule |
|---|---|
| Hosting plans, care plans, support | Your own plan names; say the plan is "for Exponential sites" |
| White-label 7x services | Your own brand on the service; the contract still names you as responsible ([White-label reseller](../models/white-label-reseller.md)) |
| An OEM product | Its own name, "based on Exponential"; whether "Exponential" may be part of the product name is not published, ask the partner team ([OEM product reseller](../models/oem-product-reseller.md)) |
| Training | "Exponential for editors, a course by <Your company>"; not "official training" ([Training reseller](../models/training-reseller.md)) |
| Documentation you hand out | Your own title for adapted pages, with the original notices kept ([Documentation you can give your customers](../customers/documentation-for-your-customers.md)) |
| Logos | Only the marks your level grants ([Using partner logos and the certified mark](../../partners/partner-logos-and-marks.md)) |

In code, `sevenx` and `exp` are the project's prefixes; do not use `exp` for your own extensions
([Naming prefixes](../../../standards/naming-prefixes-sevenx-and-exp.md)).

## Ready-to-use phrases

```text
<Company> provides <hosting / managed sites / support> for Exponential, the open source content management
system led by 7x and operated by the Exponential Foundation.

Exponential is free software under the GNU General Public License, version 2 or later. You pay us for our
services, not for a license.

<Company> is an Exponential Partner (<Level>).
```

## Wrong and right

| Wrong | Right |
|---|---|
| "Exponential license, 12 months" on an invoice | "Managed hosting for Exponential, 12 months" |
| "We are the Exponential company in <country>." | "We are an Exponential Partner in <country>." |
| "Official Exponential reseller" (without the Distribution profile) | "Exponential Partner (<Level>)", or a description of your services |
| The old product name plus "6 hosting" | "Exponential 6 hosting" |
| "Exponential, by eZ Systems" | "Exponential, led by 7x and operated by the Exponential Foundation" |
| "Certified Exponential support" (without certification) | "Exponential support by <Company>" |
| "The Foundation endorses our services." | Only with a written statement of the Foundation |
