# Factual comparisons for resellers

This page is for sales staff who are asked "how does this compare with X?". Resellers face two comparisons: the
software against other systems, and your offer against other providers of the same software. Both must be factual.
The full method is on [Comparing Exponential factually](../../business/comparing-exponential-factually.md).

## Rules for any comparison

1. **Describe Exponential, not others.** State what is true for Exponential and let the buyer ask the same question
   of the alternatives.
2. **Only verifiable properties.** Each with a source the buyer can check without you.
3. **No rankings.** "Better", "fastest", "most secure" cannot be proven.
4. **Say where it is not the right fit** ([Handling objections as a reseller](handling-objections.md#when-the-honest-answer-is-no)).
5. **Never disparage other providers or partners** ([Partner code of conduct](../../partners/partner-code-of-conduct.md)).

## Comparing your offer with other providers

Other resellers, hosts and partners may offer the same software. Compare what differs between providers, never the
software:

| Compare | Your evidence |
|---|---|
| Measured response times | Your ticket statistics, not a target |
| Update rhythm | How many days after a release your customers are current |
| Backup and restore | The date and result of your last restore test |
| Staff skills | Years with this code base, certifications you actually hold |
| Region, language, hours | Where your staff are and when they work |
| Exit terms | What a customer receives when leaving, and in how many days |
| Partner status | "Exponential Partner (<Level>)", or none |

If a competitor offers something you do not, say what you offer instead; do not claim they cannot deliver.

## Phrases to use and avoid

| Avoid | Use instead |
|---|---|
| "We are better than X." | "Here is what Exponential does, and how you can check it." |
| "X locks you in." | "With Exponential you keep the code under the GPL and your content in a standard database." |
| "We are the official Exponential provider." | "We are an Exponential Partner (<Level>)." |
| "It is the old product with a new name." | "Exponential continues a code base whose original product its creator discontinued after almost 18 years." |
| "We guarantee support from the developers." | "We support your site; security reports to the project get a first answer within one business day." |
