# Sales process for resellers

This page is for sales staff and owners who sell a reseller's offers. It describes a process in seven stages that
works for hosting plans, managed sites, support contracts and projects alike. Adjust the names to your own CRM.

## Contents

1. [The seven stages](#the-seven-stages)
2. [Stage by stage](#stage-by-stage)
3. [When to involve 7x](#when-to-involve-7x)
4. [Pipeline record](#pipeline-record)

## The seven stages

| Stage | Goal | Exit when |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Lead | Know who and what | Contact and need written down, consent recorded |
| 2. Qualification | Decide whether to pursue | Budget, decision maker, timeline and fit known |
| 3. Discovery | Understand the customer's situation | Requirements written and confirmed by the customer |
| 4. Demonstration | Show that it works for them | The customer has seen their own use case |
| 5. Proposal | A written offer they can sign | Offer sent, with scope, price, service levels, terms |
| 6. Negotiation | Agree the details | Changes agreed in writing |
| 7. Close and handover | Contract signed, delivery starts | Delivery team has everything ([Onboarding customers](../customers/onboarding-customers.md)) |

## Stage by stage

### 1. Lead

Leads come from your website, referrals, events, 7x (customer project referrals from Professional) and other
partners. Record the source and the person's consent to be contacted
([Data protection for resellers](../responsibilities/data-protection.md)).

### 2. Qualification

Use the qualification sheet on [Referral-only reselling](../basics/referral-only-reselling.md#qualifying-a-lead). If a
lead does not fit your skills, size or region, hand it back or on with a short reason
([Lead handoffs for resellers](lead-handoffs.md)).

### 3. Discovery

Ask the questions on [Pre-sales questions and answers](pre-sales-questions-and-answers.md): kind of site, languages,
number of sites, shop, integrations, hosting, editors, compliance. Write the answers back to the customer.

### 4. Demonstration

Show the customer's own use case on a demo you control ([Demo sites for resellers](../offers/demo-sites.md)), with
the talk tracks in [Demo scripts](../../business/demo-scripts.md).

### 5. Proposal

A proposal contains:

- [ ] scope, in the customer's words;
- [ ] the tier or the offer with its price ([Packaging offers in tiers](../offers/packaging-offers-in-tiers.md));
- [ ] service levels you can keep ([Service level design](../offers/service-level-design.md));
- [ ] a correct license statement: no license fee, GPL v2 or later
      ([What reselling means for free software](../basics/what-reselling-means-for-free-software.md));
- [ ] what is not included;
- [ ] the exit: how the customer leaves with their site and data ([Handing over](../customers/handing-over.md));
- [ ] contract terms or a reference to them ([Contracts with customers](../customers/contracts-with-customers.md)).

### 6. Negotiation

Objections usually concern risk: security, longevity, support, lock-in. Answer from sources
([Handling objections as a reseller](handling-objections.md)). Do not trade away service levels you cannot keep to
win on price.

### 7. Close and handover

Sign, invoice the first period, and hand the deal over to delivery with a written summary: what was promised, by
whom, and every special condition.

## When to involve 7x

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| A large bid needs technical answers you cannot give | Pre-sales support from Professional ([Pre-sales support](../../partners/pre-sales-support.md)) |
| The customer wants to buy from 7x directly | Refer the lead: 10% (cold) or 20% (hot) of the total contract value ([Referral-only reselling](../basics/referral-only-reselling.md)) |
| You resell a 7x service | Confirm price and terms in writing first ([White-label reseller](../models/white-label-reseller.md)) |
| A feature the customer needs is missing | Ask whether it is planned; never promise it ([Feature requests and sponsored features](../../partners/feature-requests-and-sponsored-features.md)) |

## Pipeline record

```text
| Lead | Source | Stage | Next step | Due | Value / month | One-off value | Owner | Notes |
|------|--------|-------|-----------|-----|---------------|---------------|-------|-------|
```

Review the pipeline weekly. Close leads that have not moved in a quarter, with a note why.
