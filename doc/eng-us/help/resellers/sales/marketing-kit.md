# Marketing kit for resellers

This page is for marketing staff at resellers. It lists the material a reseller needs, and where to get or build each
piece without inventing facts. The sales and marketing section has the deeper material
([Sales and marketing: start here](../../business/sales-and-marketing-start-here.md)).

## The kit

| Piece | Build it from | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| Positioning statement | [Positioning and value proposition](../../business/positioning-and-value-proposition.md) | Describe Exponential, not competitors |
| One-page offer per tier | Your tier sheet ([Packaging offers in tiers](../offers/packaging-offers-in-tiers.md)) | Prices, what is included, what is not |
| Feature highlights | [Feature highlights for customers](../../business/feature-highlights-for-customers.md) | Only features in the version you deliver |
| License statement | [What reselling means for free software](../basics/what-reselling-means-for-free-software.md#phrases-to-use-with-customers) | No "license fee", no "license key" |
| About us with partner status | [Naming and brand for resellers](naming-and-brand.md) | Your exact level |
| Logos | The partner team, for the marks your level grants | [Using partner logos and the certified mark](../../partners/partner-logos-and-marks.md) |
| Demo | [Demo sites for resellers](../offers/demo-sites.md) | Never with real customer data |
| Case studies and references | [Case studies for resellers](case-studies.md) | Written consent of the customer |
| Customer documentation | Adapted help pages under the GNU FDL ([Documentation you can give your customers](../customers/documentation-for-your-customers.md)) | Keep the notices |
| Your partner page | [Your partner page](../../partners/your-partner-page.md) | Dedicated page on share.exponential.earth at every level |

## Messages you can use

These statements are sourced and safe in any material:

```text
Exponential is free software under the GNU General Public License, version 2 or later: no license fee.
It runs on PHP 8.0 to 8.5 and on six databases, SQLite 3 by default.
One installation serves many sites and languages.
An online store is built in.
Security reports get a first answer within one business day; fixes come in the next 6.0.x release.
Exponential is led by 7x and operated by the Exponential Foundation.
```

Sources: `README.md`, `SECURITY.md`, [Requirements at a glance](../../hosting/basics/requirements-at-a-glance.md),
[Multi-site offers](../offers/multi-site-offers.md), [E-commerce offers](../offers/e-commerce-offers.md).

Avoid unsourced superlatives ("fastest", "most secure") and user counts the sources do not give
([Factual comparisons for resellers](factual-comparisons.md#phrases-to-use-and-avoid)).

## Images and screenshots

- Take screenshots from your own demo installation, with demo content only.
- Never show a real customer's administration, user names or e-mail addresses
  ([Data protection for resellers](../responsibilities/data-protection.md)).
- Images in the help are part of the documentation; if you reuse them, the GNU FDL conditions apply.
- Show the current administration of the version you sell, and date your material.

| Benefit | Levels (benefits page, read 18 October 2026) |

## Kit checklist

- [ ] Positioning statement approved by the owner.
- [ ] One page per tier, with prices and exclusions.
- [ ] Every feature claim checked against the version you deliver.
- [ ] License statement correct; no "license" on any price list.
- [ ] Partner status and logos match your current level.
- [ ] Demo ready, with demo content only.
- [ ] At least one case study or reference with written consent.
- [ ] Material dated and reviewed each year ([Yearly checklist](../checklists/yearly-checklist.md)).
