# Invoicing and taxes for resellers

This page is for owners and bookkeepers at resellers. It covers how to describe what you sell on an invoice and
which questions to ask your accountant. Tax rules depend on your country and your customer's; this page is **not tax
advice**.

## Describe services, not licenses

Exponential carries no license fee: "Using Exponential under the terms of the GNU GPL is free of charge"
(`README.md`). An invoice line "Exponential license" would describe something that does not exist, and may also be
taxed differently from a service in your country. Describe what you actually deliver
([What you can and cannot sell](../basics/what-you-can-and-cannot-sell.md)).

## Invoice lines that work

| Item | Example wording | Recurring? |
|---|---|---|
| Hosting | "Managed hosting for Exponential site example.com, <month>" | Monthly |
| Care plan | "Care plan Business: updates, backups, monitoring, <month>" | Monthly |
| Support | "Support contract, <period>; extra hours: <n> h at <rate>" | Monthly or yearly |
| Project work | "Design and implementation of example.com, milestone <n>" | One-off |
| Distribution of copies | "Preinstalled server image based on Exponential (GPL v2 or later), delivery" | One-off |
| Training | "Editor training, <date>, <n> participants" | One-off |
| White-label service | Your service name, as sold to the customer | As agreed |

Keep the price of the software at zero in every quote and invoice; your work is the price.

## Commissions and partner costs

| Item | Who invoices whom | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Lead referral commission (10% cold, 20% hot, of the total contract value) | As agreed with the partner team | How and when commission is paid is not published; settle it before the first lead ([Referral-only reselling](../basics/referral-only-reselling.md)) |
| Partner subscription | 7x, through its store, invoices you | "The partner subscription is a purchase of services, not a donation" ([Partner plans and prices](../../partners/partner-plans-and-prices.md#invoices-and-taxes)) |
| White-label 7x services | 7x invoices you; you invoice the customer | Your discount applies by level |
| Donations to the Foundation | Not an invoice for services | Ask your adviser how a donation applies to your company ([Working with the Exponential Foundation](../../partners/working-with-the-foundation.md)) |

## Questions for your accountant

- [ ] How are hosting, support, project work and training taxed in our country, and in our customers' countries?
- [ ] Do cross-border services to business customers fall under reverse charge, or similar rules?
- [ ] How are services bought from 7x (a company in North America, per [Who is 7x?](../../who-is-7x.md)) treated for
      tax in our country?
- [ ] How is referral commission income treated?
- [ ] Does distributing copies of software count as a sale of goods or a service here?
- [ ] Which records must we keep, and how long?

Store customers have their own tax questions; point them to the [VAT checklist](../../customers/commerce/vat-checklist.md)
and their accountant.

## Records to keep

- Contracts, offers and invoices for each customer.
- Records of referred leads with dates and consent ([Lead handoffs for resellers](../sales/lead-handoffs.md#commission-records)).
- Partner subscription invoices and the signed partnership agreement.
- Agreements with subcontractors, including white-label terms.

Keep invoices even after a customer leaves; delete their site data as agreed ([Data protection for resellers](data-protection.md)).
