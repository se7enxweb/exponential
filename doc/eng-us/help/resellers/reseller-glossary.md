# Reseller glossary

This page is for anyone who meets an unfamiliar term in the reseller pages. Terms are in alphabetical order. The
partner program's own terms are also in the [Partner glossary](../partners/partner-glossary.md).

## Contents

1. [A to F](#a-to-f)
2. [G to O](#g-to-o)
3. [P to Z](#p-to-z)

## A to F

| Term | Meaning | Page |
|---|---|---|
| Application support | Help with content classes, templates, extensions and editorial questions, as opposed to hosting support | [Support tiers you offer](offers/support-tiers-you-offer.md) |
| Break-even | The number of customers at which a reseller line covers its fixed costs | [Margin and cost worksheet](offers/margin-and-cost-worksheet.md) |
| Care plan | A monthly service that keeps a site updated, backed up and monitored; a managed service | [Managed service reseller](models/managed-service-reseller.md) |
| Cold lead | A first contact passed to 7x; earns 10% of the total contract value | [Referral-only reselling](basics/referral-only-reselling.md) |
| Controller | Under laws such as the GDPR, the organization that decides why personal data is processed; usually your customer | [Data protection for resellers](responsibilities/data-protection.md) |
| Demo mode | The state switched on by the environment variable `EXP_DEMO_MODE=1`, in which administrator passwords cannot be changed or reset | [Demo sites for resellers](offers/demo-sites.md) |
| Distribution | Giving someone a copy of the software; brings the GPL duties | [Distribution obligations checklist](responsibilities/distribution-obligations-checklist.md) |
| Distribution profile | The partner profile for "distributors, resellers, and training centers" | [Distribution partners and resellers](../partners/distribution-partners.md) |
| Exponential | The product: the content management system and framework, led by 7x and operated by the Exponential Foundation | [Naming and brand for resellers](sales/naming-and-brand.md) |
| Exponential Foundation | The nonprofit that operates the project | [Working with 7x and the Foundation](basics/working-with-7x-and-the-foundation.md) |

## G to O

| Term | Meaning | Page |
|---|---|---|
| GNU FDL | The GNU Free Documentation License 1.3 or later, the license of this help | [Documentation you can give your customers](customers/documentation-for-your-customers.md) |
| GPL | The GNU General Public License, version 2 or any later version, the license of Exponential | [What reselling means for free software](basics/what-reselling-means-for-free-software.md) |
| Handover | Giving a site with its data, settings and documentation to a customer or a new provider | [Handing over](customers/handing-over.md) |
| Hosting support | Help with servers, PHP, the database, certificates, backups and mail | [Support tiers you offer](offers/support-tiers-you-offer.md) |
| Hot lead | A customer ready to purchase from 7x; earns 20% of the total contract value | [Referral-only reselling](basics/referral-only-reselling.md) |
| Kick-Off Package | For Basic: "program fee, book, online training, certification, and 8h support for $3,200 (30% discount)" | [Partner plans and prices](../partners/partner-plans-and-prices.md#the-kick-off-package) |
| Lead priority | The order in which partners receive leads from 7x, 1 (Platinum) to 6 (Starter) | [Choosing a partner level](basics/choosing-a-partner-level.md) |
| Managed service | You run, update and back up a customer's site for a monthly fee | [Managed service reseller](models/managed-service-reseller.md) |
| OEM | Selling your own product based on Exponential; "OEM Resale Opportunities" is a benefit at every level | [OEM product reseller](models/oem-product-reseller.md) |

## P to Z

| Term | Meaning | Page |
|---|---|---|
| Partner level | Student, Now, Starter, Basic, Professional, Silver, Gold or Platinum | [Choosing a partner level](basics/choosing-a-partner-level.md) |
| Processor | Under laws such as the GDPR, an organization that processes personal data for the controller; usually you, when you host the customer's site | [Data protection for resellers](responsibilities/data-protection.md) |
| Release line | The maintained 6.0.x versions; fixes arrive as the next release | [Keeping customers current](customers/keeping-customers-current.md) |
| Revenue requirement | The revenue per quarter a partner must generate from Professional on | [Choosing a partner level](basics/choosing-a-partner-level.md#revenue-requirements) |
| Severity | How urgent a support request is, from 1 (critical) to 4 (low) | [Support contract reseller](models/support-contract-reseller.md#severity-levels) |
| Site package | An Exponential package of type `site` that an installer sets up, such as `sevenx_multisite` | [OEM product reseller](models/oem-product-reseller.md) |
| Siteaccess | One way into an installation: a name, settings in `settings/siteaccess/<name>/`, and a rule for which requests it answers | [Multi-site offers](offers/multi-site-offers.md) |
| SLA | Service level agreement: the promises in your contract on response, availability and fixes | [Service level design](offers/service-level-design.md) |
| Subprocessor | A supplier that processes personal data for you, such as a hosting supplier or 7x for white-label services | [Data protection for resellers](responsibilities/data-protection.md) |
| Tier | One package of an offer, with its own price and promises | [Packaging offers in tiers](offers/packaging-offers-in-tiers.md) |
| Total contract value | The basis of the referral commission; what it includes for long contracts is to be settled with the partner team | [Referral-only reselling](basics/referral-only-reselling.md) |
| White label | Selling 7x services under your own brand; a benefit from Professional | [White-label reseller](models/white-label-reseller.md) |
| 7x | The company that leads development of Exponential and runs the partner program | [Working with 7x and the Foundation](basics/working-with-7x-and-the-foundation.md) |
