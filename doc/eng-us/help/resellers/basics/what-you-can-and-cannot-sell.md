# What you can and cannot sell

This page is for whoever writes your offers, price lists and contracts. It sorts every item a reseller might put on
an invoice into three groups: free to sell, sell only after an agreement, and never sell. It is not legal advice.

## Free to sell

| Item | Why it is yours to sell | Page |
|---|---|---|
| Hosting of Exponential sites | Running the software is not restricted (GPL section 0) | [Hosting reseller](../models/hosting-reseller.md) |
| Setup, installation, configuration | Your work | [Onboarding customers](../customers/onboarding-customers.md) |
| Design, templates, content modeling | Your work | [Agencies reselling to end customers](../models/agency-reselling-to-end-customers.md) |
| Support and maintenance contracts | Your work and availability | [Support contract reseller](../models/support-contract-reseller.md) |
| Updates and upgrades as a service | Your work | [Keeping customers current](../customers/keeping-customers-current.md) |
| A warranty | "you may at your option offer warranty protection in exchange for a fee" (GPL section 1) | [Contracts with customers](../customers/contracts-with-customers.md) |
| Copies of Exponential (an image, a preinstalled server) | "You may charge a fee for the physical act of transferring a copy" (GPL section 1), with the distribution duties | [Distribution obligations checklist](../responsibilities/distribution-obligations-checklist.md) |
| Your own extensions and themes | Your work; license each one so it can be combined with GPL code | [Extension and theme bundles](../models/extension-and-theme-bundles.md) |
| Training you deliver | Your work; course material may build on the GNU FDL help | [Training reseller](../models/training-reseller.md) |
| Printed or adapted documentation | The GNU FDL allows copies "for free or for a fee" | [Documentation you can give your customers](../customers/documentation-for-your-customers.md) |

## Sell only after an agreement with 7x

| Item | What the program publishes | What to settle first |
|---|---|---|
| 7x services under your own name | "White-Label 7x Services" is a benefit from Professional on | Which services, prices, terms, who talks to the end customer ([White-label reseller](../models/white-label-reseller.md)) |
| Your own product based on Exponential, marketed as OEM | "OEM options are also available to resell your own products based on Exponential"; "OEM Resale Opportunities" at every level | Naming, listing, support of the core: details not published, ask the partner team ([OEM product reseller](../models/oem-product-reseller.md)) |
| 7x subscription or knowledge products | Partner discounts on subscription and knowledge products by level | Whether resale (not only your own use) is allowed: not published, ask the partner team |
| Certifications | "Distribution Partners: Extend the Exponential ecosystem by distributing and reselling Exponential software or certifications" | Certifications are still being defined by the program ([Certifications](../../partners/certifications.md)) |
| Use of partner logos | "Use of 7x partner logo" from Professional; "Exponential Certified" logo for certified Silver, Gold and Platinum partners | Use only the mark of your own level ([Partner logos and marks](../../partners/partner-logos-and-marks.md)) |

All values as published on <https://exponential.earth/partner/benefits> and
<https://exponential.earth/partner/program>, read 18 October 2026, and documented on
[Partner benefits in detail](../../partners/partner-benefits.md).

## Never sell or claim

- [ ] An "Exponential license", "license key" or "usage right": none exists ([What reselling means for free software](what-reselling-means-for-free-software.md)).
- [ ] An "official" or "certified" status you do not hold ([Naming and brand for resellers](../sales/naming-and-brand.md)).
- [ ] A product sold under the old product name today, or a suggestion that the project is associated with eZ Systems or Ibexa
      ([Naming and brand rules for partners](../../partners/naming-and-brand-for-partners.md)).
- [ ] Response times that you cannot deliver yourself, copied from a partner plan ([Service level design](../offers/service-level-design.md)).
- [ ] Features that do not exist. Check the feature pages and the code before you write an offer
      ([Pre-sales questions and answers](../sales/pre-sales-questions-and-answers.md)).
- [ ] A restriction on the customer's GPL rights, such as "may not be moved to another host" or "source code
      not included" for a copy you hand over.
- [ ] An endorsement by the Exponential Foundation without a written statement from the Foundation.

## Gray areas to ask about

| Question | Ask |
|---|---|
| Can we keep a customer-specific extension private? | Code you do not distribute is not affected; if you distribute it with Exponential, ask counsel ([Licensing for partners](../../partners/licensing-for-partners.md)) |
| Can we sell third-party extensions? | Check each extension's own license on **Info > About** and in its `extension.xml` |
| Do OEM sales count as revenue for our partner level? | The partner team ([OEM and reselling](../../partners/oem-and-reselling.md)) |
| Can we resell 7x hosting as our own? | The partner team, from Professional ([White-label 7x services](../../partners/white-label-services.md)) |

## An offer review in five questions

```text
Offer: ______________________   Reviewer: __________   Date: ________
1. Does every line describe a service, product or copy, never a license?        yes / no
2. Is every 7x service on it covered by a written agreement?                     yes / no
3. Is every feature named on it present in the version we deliver?               yes / no
4. Are all response times ones our own staff can meet?                           yes / no
5. Does it name the product "Exponential" and our status correctly?              yes / no
```
