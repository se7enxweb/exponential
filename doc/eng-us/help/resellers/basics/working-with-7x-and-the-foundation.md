# Working with 7x and the Foundation as a reseller

This page is for reseller owners and account managers. Your offer depends on a product you do not own alone, so it
pays to know who is behind it and how to work with them.

## Two organizations

| | 7x | The Exponential Foundation |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | A company: web design, support, development and hosting; it leads development of Exponential | A nonprofit "501(c)(3) Non-Profit Corporation founded in Seattle, Washington, USA" that operates the project |
| What it means for you | Runs the partner program; sells services you may resell or white-label; answers your support tickets | Owns the project's direction and funds its open source work |
| Contact | Partner team <partners@exponential.earth>; services <https://se7enx.com/contact> | <https://foundation.exponential.earth/> |
| Read | [Who is 7x?](../../who-is-7x.md) | [Who is the Exponential Foundation?](../../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md) |

The published partner program is run by 7x; the Foundation's role in it is "to be defined by the Foundation"
([Who runs the partner program](../../partners/who-runs-the-partner-program.md)). One sentence that is always
correct: "Exponential, led by 7x and operated by the Exponential Foundation."

## What to expect from the Foundation

The Foundation describes its mission as "the continued Funding, Adoption, Support, Direction, Education, Development,
Promotion and Documentation for the Exponential Enterprise Open Source Project". For a reseller that means: the
project's long-term direction, documentation (such as this help) and community places. It does not sell services and
it is not your support desk.

## Habits that help resellers

1. **Name one owner of the relationship** in your company, and ask 7x who your counterpart is.
2. **Put agreements in writing**: prices, discounts, white-label terms, referral terms.
3. **Report bugs well.** A clear, reproducible report helps every customer you have
   ([Filing bugs as a partner](../../partners/filing-bugs-as-a-partner.md)).
4. **Contribute generic fixes back** so you do not carry a private fork into every update
   ([Contributing code back](../../partners/contributing-code-back.md)).
5. **Bring customer evidence to the roadmap**, not wishes ([Taking part in the roadmap](../../partners/roadmap-participation.md)).
6. **Respect introductions.** Leads 7x passes to you are handled as agreed ([Lead handoffs](../sales/lead-handoffs.md)).
7. **Support the Foundation** if your business depends on the product
   ([Working with the Exponential Foundation](../../partners/working-with-the-foundation.md)).
