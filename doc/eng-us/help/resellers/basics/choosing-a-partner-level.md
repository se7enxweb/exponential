# Choosing a partner level for reselling

This page is for owners who budget the partnership of a reseller business. The levels, prices and benefits are those
of the partner benefits page <https://exponential.earth/partner/benefits>, as documented on
[Partner plans and prices](../../partners/partner-plans-and-prices.md) (read 18 October 2026). The store and the live
pages are authoritative: "7x reserves full rights to change or administer this program".

## The levels and prices

| Level | Price per month (USD) | Revenue requirement per quarter |
|---|---|---|
| Student | $25 | None |
| Now | $45 | None |
| Starter | $175 | None |
| Basic | $300 | None |
| Professional | $500 | $1,800 |
| Silver | $800 | $3,000 |
| Gold | $1,000 | $4,500 |
| Platinum | $2,600 | $7,500 |

Tickets, response times and guidance meetings per level are on
[Partner plans and prices](../../partners/partner-plans-and-prices.md#the-plans). Do not copy those response times
into your own offers: they are what 7x promises you ([Service level design](../offers/service-level-design.md)).

## The benefits that matter for reselling

| Benefit | From level | Use for resellers |
|---|---|---|
| OEM resale opportunities | Every level | Selling your own product based on Exponential ([OEM product reseller](../models/oem-product-reseller.md)) |
| Installation support (basic) | Starter | Help when you set up your first customer sites |
| Pre-sales support | Professional | 7x helps with large bids ([Pre-sales support](../../partners/pre-sales-support.md)) |
| Customer project referrals | Professional | Leads from 7x to you |
| White-label 7x services | Professional | Delivering 7x services under your name ([White-label reseller](../models/white-label-reseller.md)) |
| Use of 7x partner logo | Professional | On your website and offers |
| Joint marketing activities | Silver | Campaigns with 7x |
| Co-marketing budget (up to 5%) | Gold | Paid campaigns |
| Discount on 7x services | 5% Professional, 10% Silver, 15% Gold, 20% Platinum | Your margin on resold 7x services |
| Discount on subscription products | 4% up to Professional, 15% Silver, 20% Gold, 25% Platinum | Your cost of 7x subscriptions |
| Lead priority | 6 Starter, 5 Basic, 4 Professional, 3 Silver, 2 Gold, 1 Platinum (1 = highest) | Who gets a lead first |

Source: [Partner benefits in detail](../../partners/partner-benefits.md). The lead referral commission (10% cold,
20% hot, of the total contract value) is a partner term on [Partner plans and prices](../../partners/partner-plans-and-prices.md#partner-terms).

## Level by reseller model

| Model | Lowest level that fits | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Referral only | Any level; ask the partner team whether referrals without a partnership are paid | Commission is a partner term |
| Training resale | Now or Starter | Knowledge product discount (20% + Kick-Off Package up to Professional) |
| Extension and theme bundles, OEM product | Starter or Basic | OEM resale at every level; installation support from Starter |
| Hosting reseller, managed service, support contracts | Basic | Faster response (≤ 24 h) and three guidance meetings a quarter as your back-up |
| White label | Professional | The benefit starts at Professional; revenue requirement $1,800 per quarter |
| Large bids with 7x | Professional or Silver | Pre-sales support; Silver shares leads, projects and joint customer meetings |

The partner pages give a general table for starting levels in
[Partner levels](../../partners/partner-levels.md#choosing-your-starting-level).

## Revenue requirements

From Professional on, the plan has a revenue requirement per quarter. What counts toward it is described on
[Partner requirements](../../partners/partner-requirements.md); whether resold 7x services or OEM sales count is not
published: ask the partner team before you plan on it.

## Joining

1. Apply at <https://exponential.earth/partner/apply> ([How to apply](../../partners/how-to-apply.md)).
2. **Pay first**: buy the partner subscription in the 7x store.
3. **Then sign** the partnership agreement and send it to <partners@exponential.earth>.
4. Activation follows when the signed agreement and the subscription are confirmed.

A partner website and dashboard are planned for mid-2027; until then keep your own record of activity
([Logging your partner activity](../../partners/logging-partner-activity.md)).
