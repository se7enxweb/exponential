# Reseller models

This page is for owners choosing how to make money around Exponential. Most resellers combine two or three of the
models below; an agency, for example, often sells managed sites, support contracts and a few leads to 7x.

## Contents

1. [The models side by side](#the-models-side-by-side)

## The models side by side

| Model | You sell | Margin comes from | Partner benefit that applies | Page |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hosting reseller | Server space and operations for Exponential sites | Plan price minus infrastructure and staff | Visibility; hosting partners profile | [Hosting reseller](../models/hosting-reseller.md) |
| Managed service | A site that you run, update and back up for a monthly fee | Recurring fee minus hosting and staff time | Support tickets and guidance meetings by level | [Managed service reseller](../models/managed-service-reseller.md) |
| White label | 7x services under your name | Your price minus the 7x price after your partner discount | "White-Label 7x Services" from Professional | [White-label reseller](../models/white-label-reseller.md) |
| OEM product | Your own product based on Exponential | Product price minus development and support | "OEM Resale Opportunities" at every level | [OEM product reseller](../models/oem-product-reseller.md) |
| Extension and theme bundles | Your extensions and themes with installation and support | Bundle price minus maintenance | OEM resale; publishing extensions earns points | [Extension and theme bundles](../models/extension-and-theme-bundles.md) |
| Support contracts | Response, fixes and advice on a schedule | Contract fee minus on-call cost | Support tickets as your back-up | [Support contract reseller](../models/support-contract-reseller.md) |
| Training resale | Courses, workshops, coaching | Seat or day price minus trainer cost | Knowledge product discounts | [Training reseller](../models/training-reseller.md) |
| Referral only | Nothing yourself: you pass a lead to 7x | 10% of the total contract value (cold lead) or 20% (hot lead) | Lead referral commission | [Referral-only reselling](referral-only-reselling.md) |

The benefits are documented on [Partner benefits in detail](../../partners/partner-benefits.md) (benefits page read
18 October 2026). Agencies that sell all of these to their own customers have a page of their own:
[Agencies reselling to end customers](../models/agency-reselling-to-end-customers.md).

## What each model needs

| Model | Technical staff | Sales effort | Capital | Distribution duties (GPL) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hosting reseller | System administrators | Medium | Servers or cloud budget | Only if you hand over images or files the customer owns |
| Managed service | Developers and administrators | Medium | Low | Usually none while you run the site; yes at handover |
| White label | Account manager; little technical | Medium | Low | Depends on the 7x service |
| OEM product | Developers, product manager | High | High | Yes, almost always |
| Extension and theme bundles | Developers | Medium | Medium | Yes when you deliver code |
| Support contracts | Experienced developers on call | Low to medium | Low | None by themselves |
| Training | Trainers | Medium | Low | None for the code; GNU FDL for material built on the help |
| Referral only | None | Low | None | None |

## Typical combinations

| Your business | Common mix |
|---|---|
| Hosting company | Hosting reseller + managed plans + referral of development work to partners or 7x |
| Small agency or freelancer | Managed service + support contracts + referral of large projects |
| Larger agency | Managed service + white-label 7x hosting + support contracts + training for customer editors |
| Software company | OEM product + support contracts + extension bundles |
| Training company | Training + referral |

## Choosing a model

Answer these questions honestly before you pick:

```text
1. Who will answer a customer's call at 02:00 on a Sunday?            ______________
2. Which Exponential skills do we have in-house today?                ______________
3. How much recurring revenue do we need to cover staff?               $____________ / month
4. Will we hand copies of the software to customers?                   yes / no
5. Do we want our own brand on everything, or partly the 7x brand?     own / mixed
6. Which partner level can we afford and fill with real use?           ______________
```

- Few technical staff: start with **referral only** or **white label**, and grow into managed service.
- Strong operations team: **hosting** and **managed service**.
- A repeatable solution for one sector: **OEM product** or **bundles**.
- Then read [Choosing a partner level for reselling](choosing-a-partner-level.md) and fill in the
  [Margin and cost worksheet](../offers/margin-and-cost-worksheet.md).
