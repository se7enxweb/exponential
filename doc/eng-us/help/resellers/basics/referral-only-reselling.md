# Referral-only reselling

This page is for consultants, small agencies, hosting companies and freelancers who meet organizations that need
Exponential work they do not want to deliver themselves. The simplest reseller model is to pass the lead to 7x and
earn the referral commission.

## The commission

| Lead | Commission | As published |
|---|---|---|
| Cold lead | **10%** of the total contract value | a first contact |
| Hot lead | **20%** of the total contract value | "customer ready to purchase 7x" |

Source: the partner terms on [Partner plans and prices](../../partners/partner-plans-and-prices.md#partner-terms) and
[Referrals and leads](../../partners/referrals-and-leads.md). On termination of a partnership, "All commissions
still due" are paid (<https://exponential.earth/partner/program>).

## Settle these before the first lead

The partner pages state the percentages, not the procedure. Ask the partner team, <partners@exponential.earth>, and
keep the answers in writing:

- [ ] How a lead is registered with 7x, and from which date it counts as yours.
- [ ] Who decides whether a lead is cold or hot, and how that is recorded.
- [ ] What "total contract value" includes for a contract that runs for several years or renews.
- [ ] When the commission is paid (on signature, on the customer's payment, by installment) and how you invoice it.
- [ ] Whether a referral earns commission without a partner subscription, or only at a certain level.
- [ ] What happens when the customer also talks to another partner.

## Qualifying a lead

A good lead is one 7x can act on. Fill in this sheet before you pass it on:

```text
Organization: ______________________   Contact role: ______________________
Consent to pass contact details to 7x: yes / no   (date: ________)
Need, in the customer's words: ______________________________________
Kind of site: corporate / intranet / shop / multisite / multilingual / migration / other
Current platform: ______________________
Budget known: yes / no      Decision maker known: yes / no
Timeline: ______________________
Ready to purchase from 7x now (hot)?  yes / no
Your relationship after the hand-off: none / stay involved as ______________
```

## Passing it on

1. Get the customer's consent to share their contact details ([Data protection for resellers](../responsibilities/data-protection.md)).
2. Send the qualification sheet and a short note to the partner team or your contact at 7x
   ([Lead handoffs for resellers](../sales/lead-handoffs.md)).
3. Tell the customer who will contact them and when.
4. Record the date, what was sent and the answer.
5. Ask for the outcome after a reasonable time, and record it.

## What not to do

- Do not promise the customer prices, dates or features on behalf of 7x.
- Do not pass a lead without the person's consent.
- Do not refer the same lead to several companies at once to "see who pays".
- Do not describe yourself as an "Exponential reseller" because you refer leads; your status, if you are a partner,
  is "Exponential Partner (Level)" ([Naming and brand for resellers](../sales/naming-and-brand.md)).

## A referral record

```text
| Date | Organization | Cold/hot | Sent to | Consent | Outcome | Contract value | Commission | Paid on |
|------|--------------|----------|---------|---------|---------|----------------|------------|---------|
|      |              |          |         |         |         |                |            |         |
```

Commission disputes are settled by records; keep this table even for leads that came to nothing.

## When referral grows into more

Many resellers start with referrals and then deliver part of the work themselves (hosting, editor training,
first-line support) while 7x builds the site. Agree the split in writing for each project and see
[Reseller models](reseller-models.md#typical-combinations).
