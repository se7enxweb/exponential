# Pricing your offer

This page is for owners and sales managers who set the prices of a reseller's offers. With Exponential there is no
license cost to mark up: "Using Exponential under the terms of the GNU GPL is free of charge" (`README.md`). Every
dollar you charge pays for work, infrastructure, risk and your availability.

## Three ways to set a price

| Method | How | Use it for | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cost-based | Your cost plus a margin | The floor of every price | Ignores what the customer would pay |
| Value-based | What the outcome is worth to the customer | Care plans, support for business-critical sites | Needs a conversation about the customer's business |
| Market-based | What comparable offers cost | Commodity hosting | Your costs may not fit |

Always calculate the cost-based floor first ([Margin and cost worksheet](margin-and-cost-worksheet.md)), then set the
price between the floor and the value. Never price below the floor to win a deal you cannot serve.

## One-off and recurring prices

| Price | Typical items | Note |
|---|---|---|
| One-off | Setup, migration, design, development, training days | Fixed price for clear scope; hourly for unclear scope |
| Recurring, per month | Hosting, care plan, support contract, white-label services | Price per site, or per installation with several siteaccesses |
| Recurring, per year | Annual review, yearly upgrade window, training refresh | Easier for public bodies with yearly budgets |
| Hours bank | Small changes | Hours per month, unused hours expire or roll over once |

For multi-site installations define what "a site" is before you price per site
([Billing and metering options](../../hosting/business/billing-and-metering-options.md), [Multi-site offers](multi-site-offers.md)).

## What to price separately

- **Upgrades that need code changes.** A routine 6.0.x update is part of a care plan; an upgrade from an old line
  is a project ([Keeping customers current](../customers/keeping-customers-current.md)).
- **Work on third-party code** you did not write.
- **Out-of-hours work** outside the agreed severity.
- **Data exports and handover** beyond what the contract includes ([Handing over](../customers/handing-over.md)).
- **Partner subscription cost**: spread it over your customers in your overhead, not as an invoice line.

## Explaining the price

Customers compare your price with offers that include license fees, and with "free" do-it-yourself hosting. Answer
both:

```text
There is no license fee: Exponential is free software under the GNU GPL v2 or later.
Our price pays for: <servers>, <updates applied every month>, <backups tested every quarter>,
<support from people who know your site>, <a clean handover if you ever leave>.
```

The total cost of ownership pages for business owners help the customer see the whole picture
([Total cost of ownership](../../businesses/costs/total-cost-of-ownership.md),
[What you do not pay for](../../businesses/costs/what-you-do-not-pay-for.md)).

## Reviewing prices

- [ ] Once a year, recompute the cost floor with real hours from your ticket system.
- [ ] When hosting or partner subscription costs change.
- [ ] When the number of sites per staff member changes.
- [ ] Announce price changes in advance, as your contracts require ([Renewals and churn](../customers/renewals-and-churn.md)).
