# Packaging offers in tiers

This page is for owners and product managers who turn their services into offers customers can choose from without
a meeting. Three or four tiers with clear differences sell better than a long list of options.

## Principles

1. **Each tier is a promise you can keep** with the staff you have ([Service level design](service-level-design.md)).
2. **Differences the customer understands**: response time, hours included, number of sites, staging, update rhythm.
   Not technical jargon.
3. **Name tiers after the customer, not after metals.** "Essentials", "Business", "Organization" say more than
   "Bronze, Silver, Gold", and avoid confusion with the partner levels Silver, Gold and Platinum.
4. **The middle tier is the one you want to sell.** Make it the best value.
5. **Software is never the difference.** Every tier runs the same Exponential, with the same rights.

## What differs between tiers

| Dimension | Lower tier | Higher tier | Source of the cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Support hours | Business hours | Extended hours, on call for severity 1 | Your staff |
| First response | Next business day | Hours | Your staff |
| Included work | None | A bank of hours per month | Your staff |
| Updates | On request | On a schedule, with a staging test | Your staff, a staging copy |
| Environments | Production | Production plus staging | Hosting |
| Backups | Daily, kept 14 days | More often, kept longer, quarterly restore test | Storage, staff |
| Sites and languages | One site | Several siteaccesses ([Multi-site offers](multi-site-offers.md)) | Setup, support |
| Reports | None | Monthly report | Staff |
| Training | Self-service help pages | Training sessions per year | Trainer |

## A tier table to copy

```text
                        | Essentials        | Business              | Organization
------------------------|-------------------|-----------------------|---------------------------
For                     | one small site    | a company site        | several sites, languages
Hosting                 | shared, 1 site    | VPS or managed        | managed, staging included
Updates                 | on request        | monthly, tested       | monthly, staging first
Backups                 | daily / 14 days   | daily / 30 days       | twice daily / 90 days, restore test each quarter
Support hours           | business hours    | business hours        | business hours + on call S1
First response          | next business day | same business day     | <your measured time>
Included work           | -                 | 2 h / month           | 8 h / month
Report                  | -                 | quarterly             | monthly
Training                | help pages        | 1 session / year      | 2 sessions / year
Price per month         | $____             | $____                 | $____
```

Fill in times only from measurements of your own team. The hosting section has a plan sheet for the technical side
([Designing hosting plans](../../hosting/business/designing-hosting-plans.md)).

## Add-ons

Things only some customers need belong in add-ons, priced separately:

| Add-on | Note |
|---|---|
| Extra siteaccess or language | Setup and support per site or language |
| Shop operations | Payment gateway, VAT and order support ([E-commerce offers](e-commerce-offers.md)) |
| Extra staging environment | Hosting and update work |
| Additional training day | [Training reseller](../models/training-reseller.md) |
| Accessibility or security review | Specialist work |
| Migration from another system | Project work, priced as a project |

## What never goes into a tier

- A "license" to Exponential ([What you can and cannot sell](../basics/what-you-can-and-cannot-sell.md)).
- A feature that does not exist in the version you deliver.
- Response times copied from your partner plan ([Service level design](service-level-design.md)).
- A lock-in clause that stops the customer from leaving with their data and their site.

## Testing the tiers

- [ ] Can a customer choose without calling you?
- [ ] Is each step up worth its price difference in the customer's eyes?
- [ ] Does each tier make money at the expected usage ([Margin and cost worksheet](margin-and-cost-worksheet.md))?
- [ ] Can your staff deliver every tier at the same time, for all customers you expect in a year?
