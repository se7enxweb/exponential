# Margin and cost worksheet

This page is for owners who decide whether a reseller line makes money. It works on a year, because the partner
subscription, renewals and upgrade work all follow a yearly rhythm. Fill it in before you launch an offer and again
after its first year.

## Fixed costs per year

```text
Partner subscription (monthly price x 12) .............. $______
  Student $25, Now $45, Starter $175, Basic $300, Professional $500,
  Silver $800, Gold $1,000, Platinum $2,600 per month
Kick-Off Package (Basic, if taken: $3,200) ............. $______
Base infrastructure (monitoring, backup target, tools) . $______
Staff that is needed whatever the customer count ....... $______
Sales and marketing (website, demo site, events) ....... $______
Insurance, legal and accounting ........................ $______
= Fixed costs per year (F) ............................. $______
```

Prices from [Partner plans and prices](../../partners/partner-plans-and-prices.md) (benefits page read 18 October
2026). From Professional on, a revenue requirement per quarter also applies (Professional $1,800, Silver $3,000,
Gold $4,500, Platinum $7,500).

## Break-even and margin

```text
Contribution per customer (R - V) ...................... $______
Break-even number of customers = F / (R - V) ........... ______
Customers expected this year (N) ....................... ______
Profit = N x (R - V) - F ............................... $______
Margin = Profit / (N x R) .............................. ____%
```

If the break-even number is higher than the customers you can realistically win and serve, change the offer, not
the forecast.

## Partner discounts and commissions

| Item | Value | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Discount on 7x services | 5% Professional, 10% Silver, 15% Gold, 20% Platinum | Lowers V for resold or bought-in 7x services |
| Discount on subscription products | 4% up to Professional, 15% Silver, 20% Gold, 25% Platinum | Lowers your own subscription costs |
| Lead referral commission | 10% of the total contract value (cold), 20% (hot) | Revenue for leads you do not serve yourself |

Sources: [Partner benefits in detail](../../partners/partner-benefits.md), [Referrals and leads](../../partners/referrals-and-leads.md).

```text
Expected purchases of 7x services per year ............. $______
x your discount ........................................ ____%
= Discount value per year .............................. $______
Expected referred contract value per year .............. $______
x 10% or 20% ........................................... $______
Compare: partner subscription per year ................. $______
```

Do not count on referral income in your first year; the partner pages advise the same
([Partner plans and prices](../../partners/partner-plans-and-prices.md#how-to-budget)).

## Warning signs

- Support hours per customer grow every quarter: the offer promises too much, or editors need training.
- One customer uses most of your on-call time: move them to a higher tier.
- Upgrades are always "free": price them, or the margin disappears every release
  ([Keeping customers current](../customers/keeping-customers-current.md)).
- The partner level is chosen for prestige: count the tickets and meetings you really use
  ([Choosing a partner level](../basics/choosing-a-partner-level.md)).
