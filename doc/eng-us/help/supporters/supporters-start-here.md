# Supporters: start here

This section is for everyone who wants to help Exponential grow: users, developers, designers, translators, writers,
teachers, companies and organizations. You do not need permission and you do not need to be an expert. Every
contribution, from one bug report to a monthly sponsorship, makes the next release better for everyone.

## Why Exponential needs your support

Exponential continues a code base whose original vendor ended it; "Exponential has made it beyond it's end of life in
2021 and survived" (README). It survives because people keep it alive:

- **7x** leads development, support, hosting and community growth ([Who is 7x?](../who-is-7x.md)).
- **The Exponential Foundation**, a nonprofit founded in 2026, operates the project and funds its open source work
  ([Who is the Exponential Foundation?](../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md)).
- **Contributors** send pull requests, translations and documentation; "Every pull request is merged"
  (`doc/eng-us/bc/6.0/ci.md`).
- **Sponsors** pay for the time all of this takes.

"The Exponential Open Source Project is funded by donations of time and resources by 7x (and Exponential Sponsors)"
(<https://exponential.earth/about/project>). Your time and your money are exactly what the project runs on.

## Start now

| You have | Do this |
|---|---|
| 10 minutes | Star <https://github.com/se7enxweb/exponential>; join <https://t.me/exponentialcms>; fix a typo with "Edit on GitHub" on any help page |
| 1 hour | Report one bug well; answer one forum question at <https://share.exponential.earth/forums>; translate a few messages |
| 1 month | Fix an issue with a pull request; write a tutorial; complete a translation section; sponsor monthly |
| A budget | Sponsor 7x and the Foundation: <https://github.com/sponsors/se7enxweb>, <https://github.com/sponsors/exponentialcms> |

## Please consider funding the work

Code, documentation, extensions, themes, applications and new product lines all take paid time. If Exponential helps
you or your organization, a monthly sponsorship of any size is the most dependable support you can give. There are
two sponsor pages for two organizations with different missions: 7x (the company that does most of the development)
and the Exponential Foundation (the nonprofit that operates the project). Read
[Two organizations, two sponsor pages](../sponsors/two-organizations-two-sponsor-pages.md) and choose one, or both.
