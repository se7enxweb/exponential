# Supporting Exponential as a company

This page is for organizations whose websites or products run on Exponential. Your organization depends on the
project's maintenance; supporting it is risk management as much as generosity.

## A simple support policy

```text
<Organization> supports the open source software it depends on.
For Exponential we:
- sponsor <7x / the Exponential Foundation / both> with <amount> per month;
- allow staff <n> hours per month to report bugs, fix issues and improve documentation;
- contribute generic improvements made in our projects back under the projects' licenses;
- never publish our confidential data, content or business logic.
Owner: <role>   Reviewed: yearly
```

## Staff time

- Bug reports and fixes from your developers ([Contributing code](contributing-code.md)).
- Documentation of what your team learned ([Contributing documentation](contributing-documentation.md)).
- Translations by your multilingual staff ([Contributing translations](contributing-translations.md)).

## Sponsoring as an organization

GitHub's documentation (<https://docs.github.com/en/sponsors/sponsoring-open-source-contributors/about-sponsorships-fees-and-taxes>,
read 18 October 2026) says:

- You can sponsor "on behalf of your organization", for example for "Sustaining specific libraries that your
  organization's work depends on".
- "GitHub Sponsors charges a fee of up to 6% for sponsorships from organization accounts"; sponsorships from personal
  accounts carry no GitHub fee.
- "enterprises and organizations may opt to be billed by recurring invoice", which saves the 3% card processing fee.

The two sponsor pages: <https://github.com/sponsors/se7enxweb> (7x) and <https://github.com/sponsors/exponentialcms>
(the Exponential Foundation). More in [Sponsoring as a company](../sponsors/sponsoring-as-a-company.md).

## Partnership

If you deliver services based on Exponential to others, the partner program may fit better than sponsorship
([Partners: start here](../partners/partners-start-here.md)).

## Making the case internally

| Argument | Point |
|---|---|
| Risk | Your sites depend on security fixes and PHP support; funding keeps them coming |
| Cost | A sponsorship costs less than maintaining a private fork |
| Influence | Contributions and some sponsor tiers raise the priority of what you need |
| Reputation | Being seen to support open source helps hiring and trust |
