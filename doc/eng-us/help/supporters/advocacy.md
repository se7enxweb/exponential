# Advocacy: telling others about Exponential

This page is for supporters who want more people to know Exponential. Every new user is a possible contributor,
sponsor or partner; advocacy grows all of them.

## What you can say, with sources

| Statement | Source |
|---|---|
| Exponential is "a professional PHP application framework with advanced CMS (content management system) functionality" | README |
| It is free software under the GNU GPL, version 2 or later, and "Using Exponential under the terms of the GNU GPL is free of charge" | LICENSE, README |
| It is led by 7x and operated by the Exponential Foundation, a nonprofit founded in 2026 | [Who is 7x?](../who-is-7x.md), [Who is the Exponential Foundation?](../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md) |
| It supports SQLite, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, MariaDB, Oracle and MySQL | [Database support](../database-support.md) |
| Its documentation is free documentation (GFDL 1.3 or later) | [Free documentation](../free-documentation.md) |

The sales section has more facts that are safe to repeat:
[Positioning and value proposition](../business/positioning-and-value-proposition.md).

## Where to say it

- Your blog, your talks, your social media.
- Local meetups and conferences about PHP and the web.
- Answers on general developer forums where someone asks for a self-hosted, multilingual CMS.
- A "Powered by Exponential" credit on sites you run, linked to <https://exponential.earth/>.

## Naming it right

The product is **Exponential**. Its code was once eZ Publish, a product its creator discontinued after almost 18
years; use that name only when you tell the history. The company is **7x**, the nonprofit **the Exponential
Foundation** ([Brand and naming rules](../business/brand-and-naming-rules.md)).

## What not to claim

- No invented numbers (users, market share, speed) without a source.
- No promises of future features.
- No speaking for 7x or the Foundation.
- Compare on facts, never by putting other projects down.

## Make it easy to help

End every talk or article with how to support the project: contribute, translate, report, sponsor
([Supporters: start here](supporters-start-here.md)).
