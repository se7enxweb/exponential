# Launch checklist for a local business website

This page is for the owner of a local business and the builder, to work through together in the last week before the
site goes public. Each line links to the page that explains it. The builder's own technical list is
[Launch checklist](../../creators/website-creators/launch-checklist.md).

## Content

- [ ] The five facts on the front page or one tap away: what, where, when, contact, prices
      ([What a small business website needs](../getting-started/what-a-small-business-website-needs.md#job-2-answer-the-basic-questions)).
- [ ] Every page from the page plan exists; no "Lorem ipsum", no test pages
      ([Planning your pages](../getting-started/planning-your-pages.md)).
- [ ] Spelling checked by someone who did not write the text.
- [ ] Photos are your own, with alternative texts and permission from people shown
      ([Photos that sell](../marketing/photos-that-sell.md)).
- [ ] Every language version complete for the priority pages
      ([Multilingual for tourist areas](../marketing/multilingual-for-tourist-areas.md)).

## Hours and location

- [ ] Hours typed once, shown in footer and on the contact page
      ([Opening hours without a special datatype](../maps-and-hours/opening-hours-without-a-special-datatype.md)).
- [ ] Next holiday's notice prepared.
- [ ] Address as text, map or directions link working, marker on the entrance
      ([Showing your location on a map](../maps-and-hours/showing-your-location-on-a-map.md)).
- [ ] Map key (if Google) in the business's account and restricted to the domain.

## Forms

- [ ] Every form tested signed out: error, success, mail arrives, reply works
      ([A contact form for your business](../contact-and-forms/a-contact-form-for-your-business.md#test-it-yourself)).
- [ ] CAPTCHA on every public form ([Stopping spam](../contact-and-forms/stopping-spam.md)).
- [ ] Request forms say they are requests, not bookings
      ([Quote and request forms](../contact-and-forms/quote-and-request-forms.md)).
- [ ] Comments switched off, or protected and moderated ([Ratings and reviews](../contact-and-forms/ratings-and-reviews.md)).

## Getting found

- [ ] Each main page has its own title and description
      ([Titles and descriptions with xrowmetadata](../getting-found/titles-and-descriptions-with-xrowmetadata.md)).
- [ ] Sitemap generated by cron, listed in `robots.txt`, submitted to search engines
      ([Sitemaps and robots.txt](../getting-found/sitemaps-and-robots-txt.md)).
- [ ] Old site's addresses redirect to new pages
      ([Nice URLs and moved pages](../getting-found/nice-urls-and-moved-pages.md#replacing-an-old-website)).
- [ ] Business listings show the new site's address and the same name, address, phone
      ([Business listings outside Exponential](../getting-found/business-listings-outside-exponential.md)).
- [ ] The site is not still blocked for search engines from the test phase (`robots.txt`, password protection).

## Phones and speed

- [ ] The ten-minute phone test passed ([Mobile visitors](../marketing/mobile-visitors.md#a-ten-minute-test-on-your-own-phone)).
- [ ] Phone number is a tappable link.
- [ ] Pages appear quickly on mobile data.

## Privacy and legal pages

- [ ] Privacy note lists the data collected and every outside service: host, mail, map, CAPTCHA, analytics, payment,
      booking ([Handling messages and privacy](../contact-and-forms/handling-messages-and-privacy.md#the-privacy-note)).
- [ ] Consent banner if your adviser says you need one for analytics or embeds.
- [ ] Imprint or legal notice as your country requires (ask your adviser; this is not legal advice).
- [ ] Terms of sale and returns, if you sell.

## Shop

Only if you sell:

- [ ] [Store launch checklist](../../businesses/stores/store-launch-checklist.md) done.
- [ ] Payments tested in test mode and once live ([Payments](../simple-shop/payments-paypal-and-authorize-net.md#testing-before-launch)).
- [ ] VAT confirmed by your accountant; shipping or pickup clear ([VAT, shipping and pickup](../simple-shop/vat-shipping-and-pickup.md)).
- [ ] Someone named to handle orders every day.

## Accounts and safety

- [ ] At least two administrator accounts; each editor with their own account and role
      ([Backups, updates and security](../running-it/backups-updates-and-security.md)).
- [ ] Two-step sign-in required for administrators.
- [ ] Default and test accounts removed or their passwords changed ([Change the admin password](../../change-the-admin-password.md)).
- [ ] HTTPS on every page; one host name.
- [ ] First backup made and its restore tested.
- [ ] Domain in the business's name; list of all accounts kept.

## Launch day and the week after

- [ ] Open the site from a search result on a phone.
- [ ] Send a test message through each form.
- [ ] Tell regular customers (counter, social media, newsletter).
- [ ] After a week: look at form messages, site search terms and errors with the builder.
- [ ] Put the [weekly and monthly routine](../running-it/weekly-and-monthly-routine.md) in the calendar.
