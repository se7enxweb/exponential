# Weekly and monthly routine

This page is for the owner or staff member who looks after a local business website. The routine is short on purpose:
one that is actually done beats a thorough one that is not.

## Every week, fifteen minutes

- [ ] Open the front page on your phone. Is everything current?
- [ ] Opening hours: any exception in the next two weeks? Prepare the notice
      ([Holiday and seasonal hours](../maps-and-hours/holiday-and-seasonal-hours.md)).
- [ ] Specials and events: remove past ones, add the next ([Events and specials](../marketing/events-and-specials.md)).
- [ ] Messages: have all form messages from the week been answered?
- [ ] Reviews and questions on map and search platforms: read and answer
      ([Business listings outside Exponential](../getting-found/business-listings-outside-exponential.md)).

## Every month, one hour

- [ ] Send a test message through each form, signed out; check it arrives
      ([A contact form for your business](../contact-and-forms/a-contact-form-for-your-business.md#keep-it-working)).
- [ ] Look at the site's search statistics: what did visitors search for and not find? Add or rename a page
      ([Site search and what visitors look for](../../customers/day-to-day/site-search-and-what-visitors-look-for.md)).
- [ ] Note this month's numbers: form messages, orders, "how did you find us" tally
      ([Measuring visits](../getting-found/measuring-visits.md)).
- [ ] Check for broken links, or ask your administrator for the link check report
      ([Broken links and content checks](../../creators/website-creators/link-and-content-checks.md)).
- [ ] Replace one tired photo or add one new one.
- [ ] Compare name, address, phone and hours on the platforms with the site.
- [ ] Did the host or partner report an update? Look at the site afterwards.

The general version for any site: [SEO monthly checklist](../../customers/day-to-day/seo-monthly-checklist.md).

## Every year, with your host or partner

Book an hour each January:

- [ ] Ask for proof of a backup restore ([Backups, updates and security](backups-updates-and-security.md)).
- [ ] Which Exponential version runs? Are updates current?
- [ ] Review who has an account; remove those who left.
- [ ] Renew the domain; check it is in the business's name.
- [ ] Reread the privacy note: does it list every outside service (maps, CAPTCHA, analytics, booking)?
- [ ] Delete old form submissions you no longer need
      ([Handling messages and privacy](../contact-and-forms/handling-messages-and-privacy.md)).
- [ ] Review the year's numbers and the costs with the [budget worksheet](../budget/budget-worksheet.md).
- [ ] Plan the year's holidays and seasons ([Seasonal changes](seasonal-changes.md)).
- [ ] Review the agreement with the provider ([Reviewing your provider each year](../../customers/partners-and-hosts/reviewing-your-provider-each-year.md)).
