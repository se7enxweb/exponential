# Running it: a local business website after launch

This group of pages is for the owner or the one person who looks after a local business website once it is live. A
website is not finished at launch: hours change, staff change, software gets updates. A few minutes a week keep it
working.

## Who does what

| Task | Owner or staff | Host or partner |
|---|---|---|
| Hours, specials, events, photos | yes | - |
| Answering messages and orders | yes | - |
| Checking the site works | yes, a quick look | monitoring, if agreed |
| Software updates | - | yes ([Updates and upgrades](../../creators/website-creators/updates-and-upgrades.md)) |
| Backups | ask for proof once a year | yes ([Backups that restore](../../businesses/reliability/backups-that-restore.md)) |
| Accounts for new and leaving staff | yes, or ask the administrator | - |

Write your own version into [Who does what](../../customers/partners-and-hosts/who-does-what.md).

## The three things that go wrong most

1. **Old information**: hours or prices that changed months ago. Fix: the weekly routine.
2. **A broken form or mail**: messages that never arrive. Fix: the monthly test.
3. **Lost access**: the only administrator left, nobody knows the password. Fix: two administrator accounts and a
   written list of accounts ([Backups, updates and security](backups-updates-and-security.md#accounts-when-staff-come-and-go)).
