# Seasonal changes

This page is for local businesses whose year has a rhythm: an ice cream parlor that closes in winter, a garden center
with a spring rush, a ski rental, a bakery at Christmas, a restaurant with a summer terrace. The website should follow
the rhythm without a scramble each time.

## Prepare, then publish

Exponential keeps every edit as a **version** and lets you save a **draft** without publishing
([Drafts, versions and undo](../../customers/day-to-day/drafts-versions-and-undo.md)). Use this:

1. Two weeks before the change, edit the page (menu, hours, front page text) and save it as a draft.
2. On the day, open the draft and publish it.
3. After the season, the previous version is still in the history to restore or copy from.

Your seasonal texts from last year are in the version history too; there is no need to write them from scratch.

## Tools that work on a date

If your administrator has set them up ([Scheduled publishing](../../customers/day-to-day/scheduled-publishing.md)):

| Tool | Use for |
|---|---|
| *Wait until date* workflow | a seasonal page or offer that goes live on a date |
| `hide.php` cron job with a date field | a notice or offer that disappears after a date |
| Layout rule with a time condition | a different front page layout for a period ([Layout rules](../../creators/website-creators/layout-rules.md)) |
| Scheduled newsletter send | announcing the new season ([Newsletters for local businesses](../marketing/newsletters-for-local-businesses.md)) |

Changing the regular opening hours on a date is not automated; publish the prepared draft on the day.

## Typical seasonal changes

| Change | Pages to touch |
|---|---|
| Summer or winter hours | opening hours object (every language), platforms |
| Seasonal menu or range | menu page or product folder; hide out-of-season products ([A small catalog](../simple-shop/a-small-catalog.md#sold-out-and-seasonal-products)) |
| Holiday closures | notice, platforms ([Holiday and seasonal hours](../maps-and-hours/holiday-and-seasonal-hours.md)) |
| Seasonal photos | front page banner |
| Gift vouchers before holidays | voucher product visible and promoted |
| Season's events | event calendar |

## Closing for a season

- Put a clear notice on the front page: closed from when, open again when, how to reach you meanwhile.
- Keep the site online; do not take it down. Pages keep their place in search results.
- If you sell, decide whether the shop stays open (vouchers, shipped goods) or closes; hide products you cannot
  deliver.
- Set the platforms to show the closure.

## The busy season

- Put the most asked question on the front page ("Yes, we are open on Sundays in July").
- Expect more form messages; agree who answers them.
- If you sell, prepare for peaks ([Peak season preparation](../../customers/commerce/peak-season-preparation.md),
  [Store performance at peak times](../../businesses/stores/store-performance-at-peak-times.md)).
