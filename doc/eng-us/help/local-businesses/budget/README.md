# Budget: what a local business website costs

This group of pages helps the owner of a small business put a realistic number on a website. It does not quote prices
of real providers, because they differ by country, by provider and by year. Every number used is labeled an
**example assumption** for you to replace with quotes you received.

## The one fact that is fixed

Exponential is free software under the GNU General Public License, version 2 or later. There is no license fee, and
the extensions this section uses (`xrowmetadata`, `bcgooglesitemaps`, `ezgmaplocation`, `recaptcha`, `hcaptcha`,
`ezstarrating`, `ezpaypal`, `cjw_newsletter`) are free software in the standard `composer.json`
([Licensing for buyers](../../business/licensing-for-buyers.md),
[What you do not pay for](../../businesses/costs/what-you-do-not-pay-for.md)).

What you pay for is **work** (building, updating, support), **hosting** and **outside services** (payment
provider, map or CAPTCHA provider above a free allowance, booking service, mail).

## How to use these pages

1. Read [The cost picture](the-cost-picture.md) to know which lines exist.
2. Look at the [worked example](worked-example-a-local-business-website.md) to see the arithmetic.
3. Ask two or three partners or hosts for quotes, with the [website brief](../templates/website-brief-for-a-local-business.md).
4. Put their numbers into the [budget worksheet](budget-worksheet.md).
5. Compare with what the site should bring in, even roughly.
