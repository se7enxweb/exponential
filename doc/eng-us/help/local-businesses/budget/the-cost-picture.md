# The cost picture

This page is for the owner of a small business who wants to know what a website on Exponential will cost over the
years, not only at launch. It lists the lines and what drives them. It contains **no prices**: get those as quotes,
and enter them in the [budget worksheet](budget-worksheet.md).

## One-time costs

| Line | Includes | Driven by |
|---|---|---|
| Setup | installation, a shipped theme adapted to your colors and logo, content classes (opening hours, locations, forms), extensions switched on | how far the design departs from a shipped theme ([Choosing a design or theme](../../creators/website-creators/choosing-a-design-or-theme.md)) |
| Content | texts, photos, translation | how much you write yourself |
| Shop setup (optional) | product class, VAT, payment, shipping, tests | number of payment and shipping options |
| Moving from an old site (optional) | content transfer, redirects of old addresses | size of the old site ([Nice URLs and moved pages](../getting-found/nice-urls-and-moved-pages.md#replacing-an-old-website)) |
| Training | an hour or two for the people who edit | number of editors |
| License | none | GNU GPL ([Licensing for buyers](../../business/licensing-for-buyers.md)) |

## Yearly costs

| Line | Includes | Driven by |
|---|---|---|
| Domain | the registration | the domain ending |
| Hosting | the server, backups, HTTPS | hosting model ([Hosting cost drivers](../../businesses/costs/hosting-cost-drivers.md)) |
| Updates | applying Exponential and extension updates, checking the site afterwards | frequency; managed host or partner ([Budgeting for upgrades and maintenance](../../businesses/costs/budgeting-for-upgrades-and-maintenance.md)) |
| Support | answering questions, fixing what breaks | response time you agree |
| Small changes | a new form, a new section | how often you ask |

## Outside services

| Service | Needed when | Billing |
|---|---|---|
| Mail sending for a newsletter | larger lists, if the host's mail is not enough | by the provider |
| Map provider (Google) | `ezgmaplocation` maps or the Google variant of the Map block | by Google, above any free allowance; OpenStreetMap in the Map block needs no key |
| CAPTCHA provider | reCAPTCHA or hCaptcha | by the provider; read their terms |
| Analytics | if you add a service | by the provider |
| Payment provider | if you sell | fees per transaction |
| Booking, reservation, ordering platform | if you need them | subscription or commission |

Full list with what is not built in: [What needs an outside service](what-needs-an-outside-service.md).

## Your own time

The largest hidden cost of a small business website is the owner's time. Count it:

| Task | How often |
|---|---|
| Updating hours, specials, events | weekly |
| Answering form messages | daily |
| Photos and new pages | monthly |
| Checking the site still works | monthly ([Weekly and monthly routine](../running-it/weekly-and-monthly-routine.md)) |
| Handling shop orders | daily, if you sell |

## Extra lines for a shop

- Payment provider fees on every sale.
- Packing, shipping or preparing pickups.
- VAT setup checked by your accountant.
- More security attention for payments ([Store security checklist](../../businesses/stores/store-security-checklist.md)).
- Returns and refunds.

The [worked example for a small store](../../businesses/costs/worked-example-small-store.md) calculates these with
example assumptions.

## What makes it cheaper or dearer

| Cheaper | Dearer |
|---|---|
| a shipped theme with your logo and colors | a design made from scratch |
| a few pages, well kept | many pages nobody updates |
| editing hours and specials yourself | asking the partner for every change |
| SQLite on one machine ([Database support](../../database-support.md)) | several servers |
| one language | several languages to keep in step |
| links to outside booking | a custom booking module |
