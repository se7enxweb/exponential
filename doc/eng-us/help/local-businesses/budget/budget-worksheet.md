# Budget worksheet

This page is a worksheet for the owner of a small business. Copy it into a spreadsheet or print it, and fill in the
numbers from the quotes you received. The **Source** column matters: write where each number comes from (a quote,
an invoice, your own estimate), so you can check it later.

The lines are explained in [The cost picture](the-cost-picture.md); a filled-in example is in
[Worked example: a local business website](worked-example-a-local-business-website.md).
