# Getting found: local search for small businesses

This group of pages is for the owner of a local business who wants people nearby to find the business in search
engines. Two halves work together: your **website**, which Exponential helps with, and your **listings on map and
search platforms**, which you manage on those platforms.

## What Exponential does and what it does not

| Exponential does | Source |
|---|---|
| Readable addresses made from page names; old addresses keep working after a rename | [URL aliases and nice URLs](../../creators/website-creators/url-aliases-and-nice-urls.md) |
| Per-page title, description and sharing image with `xrowmetadata` | [Page metadata and social sharing](../../creators/website-creators/metadata-and-social-sharing.md) |
| XML sitemaps with `bcgooglesitemaps` or `xrowmetadata` | [XML sitemaps](../../creators/website-creators/sitemaps.md) |
| A `robots.txt` at the web root | [robots.txt for your site](../../creators/website-creators/robots-txt.md) |
| Fast pages through caching | [Performance and caching](../../creators/website-creators/performance-and-caching.md) |

| Exponential does not | Instead |
|---|---|
| Create or update your profile on map and search platforms | do it on the platform: [Business listings outside Exponential](business-listings-outside-exponential.md) |
| Print `LocalBusiness` structured data with opening hours out of the box | a template change by your builder: [Structured data: what exists](structured-data-what-exists.md) |
| Guarantee a ranking | nobody can; good content and accurate listings help |

## Where to start

1. Make sure your listings on map and search platforms exist and show the same name, address and phone as the site.
2. Read [Local SEO basics](local-seo-basics.md) and work through its checklist.
3. Ask your builder to switch on `xrowmetadata` and a sitemap, and to register the sitemap.
4. Check once a month with the [SEO monthly checklist](../../customers/day-to-day/seo-monthly-checklist.md).
