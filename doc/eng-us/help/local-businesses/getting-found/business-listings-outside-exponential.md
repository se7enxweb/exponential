# Business listings outside Exponential

This page is for the owner of a local business. For many local searches, people see your **listing** on a map or
search platform before they see your website: name, address, hours, photos, reviews and a link to the site. That
listing is **not** part of Exponential. You create it and keep it up to date on the platform itself.

> **Note**
> No extension in `composer.json` or `extension/` connects Exponential to a map or search platform's business profile
> (searched on 20 October 2026). Nothing on this page is automated from the website.

## What lives where

| Information | Website (Exponential) | Listing on platforms |
|---|---|---|
| Name, address, phone | yes, one shared object | yes, typed by you |
| Opening hours, holiday hours | yes | yes, typed by you, separately |
| Photos | yes | yes, uploaded separately |
| Services, menu, prices | full detail | a summary, or a link to the site |
| Reviews | star ratings only, with `ezstarrating` | written reviews by customers |
| Booking or ordering button | a link to your booking service | a link, set on the platform |

## What to put in a listing

- The business name exactly as on your sign and website.
- The same address and phone format as on the website ([Local SEO basics](local-seo-basics.md#same-name-address-and-phone-everywhere)).
- Category: the trade that fits best.
- Regular hours and, ahead of time, holiday hours.
- A link to the website's front page, or to the matching page (menu, booking).
- Real photos: front of the premises (so people recognize it), inside, your work.

## Keeping listings and website in step

The most common mistake is changing the hours on the website and forgetting the listing, or the other way round. Make
it one task:

| When | Change on the website | Change on each platform |
|---|---|---|
| Regular hours change | hours object | regular hours |
| Public holiday | holiday notice ([Holiday and seasonal hours](../maps-and-hours/holiday-and-seasonal-hours.md)) | special hours |
| Phone number changes | contact object | phone |
| Move to new premises | address, map field, directions | address (most platforms ask for a verification) |
| Closing for good | a notice page | mark as closed |

Write the list of platforms you use into the [website brief](../templates/website-brief-for-a-local-business.md).

## Who owns the accounts

The listing speaks for your business. The account should be:

- in the business's name, with a business e-mail address that stays when staff leave;
- shared with a partner as a **manager**, not handed over;
- on the list of accounts you keep ([Working with a local partner or freelancer](../getting-started/working-with-a-local-partner-or-freelancer.md#what-you-keep-yourself)).

## Reviews

Written customer reviews usually live on the platforms. Exponential has star ratings with `ezstarrating`, but no
written review module ([Ratings and reviews](../contact-and-forms/ratings-and-reviews.md)). Answer reviews on the
platform, politely and without personal details of the customer. Do not copy customers' reviews onto your website
without asking them.

## A routine

| How often | Task |
|---|---|
| Every week | read new reviews and questions on the platforms |
| Before each holiday | set special hours on every platform and on the site |
| Every quarter | compare name, address, phone, hours and link on each platform with the site |
| Every year | replace old photos; check who has access to each account |
