# Photos that sell

This page is for the owner or staff member who takes and uploads the photos of a local business. Real photos of your
place, your products and your work build more trust than any stock image.

## What to photograph

| Photo | Why |
|---|---|
| The front of your premises, from the street | people recognize you when they arrive |
| The inside | they know what to expect |
| Your products or dishes, one at a time | for product pages and specials |
| Your work: before and after (trades, salons) | shows the result |
| The team at work, if they agree | a local business is its people |
| Parking, entrance, step-free access | practical questions answered |

## Taking photos with a phone

- Daylight, no flash. Near a window works well for products.
- Hold the phone horizontally for banners and page headers, vertically only for single products.
- Clean the lens.
- A plain background for products.
- Take several and choose the best one.

## Uploading

| Way | For | Read |
|---|---|---|
| In the edit form, an image field | one photo for a page or product | [Media and uploads](../../media-and-uploads.md#uploading-from-the-edit-form) |
| The media library | photos used in several places | [Media and uploads](../../media-and-uploads.md#the-media-library) |
| Multi-upload (`ezmultiupload`) | many photos at once, for a gallery | [ezmultiupload](../../../features/6.0/extensions/ezmultiupload.md) |
| Simple edits (crop, rotate) in the browser | quick fixes | [ezie](../../../features/6.0/extensions/ezie.md) |

Both extensions are in the standard `composer.json`.

The shipped settings let editors upload JPEG, PNG, GIF, WebP, AVIF, TIFF, BMP and SVG, and pages show JPEG, PNG, WebP
and GIF ([Responsive sites](../../businesses/mobile-and-headless/responsive-sites.md)). Photos straight from a phone
are fine.

## Sizes are made for you

You upload one photo; Exponential makes smaller **variations** (aliases) for thumbnails, lists and full pages, as the
design asks for them ([Images and image variations](../../creators/website-creators/images-and-image-variations.md)).
You do not need to resize photos yourself. Very large originals still take disk space; if your phone makes huge files,
a reasonable size before upload saves space.

## Alternative texts

Every image field has an alternative text. Describe what the photo shows, as you would to someone on the phone:

| Weak | Better |
|---|---|
| IMG_2041 | Fresh sourdough loaves on a wooden shelf |
| Photo | Our shop front on Market Street with the green awning |
| (empty) | Bathroom after the renovation: white tiles and a walk-in shower |

It helps visitors who use screen readers and it helps search engines
([Alternative text for images](../../government/accessibility/alternative-text-for-images.md)). A purely decorative
image may have an empty alternative text.

## People in photos

- Ask staff and customers before publishing their photo, and keep a note that they agreed.
- Remove a photo promptly if someone asks.
- Children: ask a parent, and think twice.
- This is not legal advice; rules on images of people differ by country.

## Checklist

- [ ] Front of premises, inside, and main products or work photographed.
- [ ] Every photo has a describing alternative text.
- [ ] Permission noted for every person shown.
- [ ] No stock photo pretends to be your place.
- [ ] Photos replaced when the shop, menu or team changes.
