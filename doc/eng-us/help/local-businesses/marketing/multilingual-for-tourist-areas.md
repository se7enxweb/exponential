# Multilingual for tourist areas

This page is for local businesses where many customers do not speak the local language: a cafe in a holiday town, a
bike rental by the lake, a pharmacy near a station. Exponential has advanced multilingual support built in: each page
can have translations, and each language gets its own addresses
([Multilingual businesses](../../businesses/multilingual/README.md)).

## Translate a few pages well

Visitors from abroad need the basics, not your history. Translate first:

| Priority | Page |
|---|---|
| 1 | front page: what you are, where, when open |
| 1 | opening hours and location, with directions |
| 1 | menu or price list |
| 2 | contact page and form labels |
| 2 | how to book or order (even if it is a link to an outside service) |
| 3 | about us, news |

Choose the languages your visitors actually speak; ask at the counter for a few weeks. One well-kept extra language is
better than three neglected ones.

## How languages work in Exponential

From [Multilingual planning](../../businesses/multilingual/multilingual-planning.md) and
[Multilingual sites](../../creators/website-creators/multilingual-sites.md):

- Your builder adds the languages and a **siteaccess** per language, for example `example.com/en/...` and
  `example.com/de/...`, or a host per language
  ([Language switchers and addresses per language](../../creators/website-creators/language-switcher-and-language-urls.md)).
- A **language switcher** in the header lets visitors change language on the same page.
- Translations of a page are versions of the same object: the photos, prices and map point are shared, the texts are
  translated.
- Each language can have its own sitemap (`googlesitemapsmultilingual`,
  [Sitemaps and robots.txt](../getting-found/sitemaps-and-robots-txt.md)).

Adding a language later is a routine job ([Adding a language](../../customers/growth/adding-a-language.md)).

## Missing translations

What visitors see when a page is not translated depends on the settings: the page in the main language, or nothing
([What visitors see when a translation is missing](../../businesses/multilingual/multilingual-planning.md#what-visitors-see-when-a-translation-is-missing)).
For a small site, showing the main language is friendlier than a missing page. Decide with your builder.

## Who translates

| Option | Note |
|---|---|
| A staff member who speaks the language | best for tone; needs time |
| A professional translator | for the main pages, once |
| Exponential Intelligence | the writing assistant built into the administration writes **drafts** of translations, which a person checks and publishes ([Exponential Intelligence](../../customers/growth/exponential-intelligence.md), [Assisted translation](../../businesses/multilingual/assisted-translation-with-exponential-intelligence.md)) |

Exponential Intelligence uses an outside AI provider chosen by your administrator; it writes drafts only and never
publishes on its own ([Using AI drafts responsibly](../../customers/growth/using-ai-drafts-responsibly.md)). Have
someone who knows the language read the menu and the hours before publishing; a wrong word on a menu or an allergy
note matters.

## Keeping languages in step

The risk of a multilingual site is that the other languages fall behind. In particular:

- **Opening hours and holiday notices**: change them in every language on the same day. A structured hours class
  ([Opening hours without a special datatype](../maps-and-hours/opening-hours-without-a-special-datatype.md#option-2-a-small-opening-hours-class))
  helps, because the times are shared and only the day names and notes are translated.
- **Prices**: kept in one price field, shared by all languages.
- **Specials**: translate them or leave them in the main language only; do not leave a three-month-old special in one
  language.

A routine: [Translation routine](../../customers/day-to-day/translation-routine.md).

## Checklist

- [ ] Languages chosen from real visitor questions.
- [ ] Priority 1 pages translated and checked by a speaker.
- [ ] Language switcher visible on phones.
- [ ] Missing-translation behavior decided.
- [ ] Holiday notices written in every language.
- [ ] Business listings show the right link per language, if the platform allows it.
