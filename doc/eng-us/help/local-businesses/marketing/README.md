# Marketing for local businesses

This group of pages is for the owner or staff member who wants the website to do more than sit there: announce this
week's special, invite regulars to an event, show the work in good photos, welcome visitors from abroad in their
language, and work well on the phones most people use.

## Start small

One special a week, kept up for a year, does more than ten pages written at launch and never touched again. Pick the
one or two things you will really keep up, and put them in your [weekly routine](../running-it/weekly-and-monthly-routine.md).
