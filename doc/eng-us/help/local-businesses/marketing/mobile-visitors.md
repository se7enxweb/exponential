# Mobile visitors

This page is for the owner of a local business. Many of your visitors are standing in the street, in a car park or on
the sofa with a phone, and they want one answer fast: are you open, where are you, how do I call you.

## What phone visitors want

| Question | The answer must be |
|---|---|
| Are you open now? | hours visible on the front page or one tap away |
| Where are you? | address as text and a link that opens their map app |
| Can I call? | a phone number they can tap |
| What do you offer, what does it cost? | a short menu or service list, readable without zooming |
| Can I book or order? | one clear button (even if it goes to an outside service) |

## Small changes with a big effect

| Change | Who |
|---|---|
| Phone number as a `tel:` link in the header or footer | builder once, or editor in rich text |
| "Directions" link under the address | editor |
| Opening hours near the top of the front page | builder or editor |
| Short first paragraph on each page | editor |
| Large, clear buttons for "Call", "Directions", "Book" | builder |
| No PDFs for menus or price lists; text pages instead | editor |
| Forms with few fields and the right keyboard (e-mail, phone) | builder ([Accessible forms](../../government/accessibility/accessible-forms.md)) |

## A ten-minute test on your own phone

Use mobile data, not the shop's Wi-Fi, and a private browser window.

1. Search for your business name and town; open your site from the result.
2. Time how long until the front page is readable.
3. Find the opening hours. How many taps?
4. Tap the phone number. Does the phone offer to call?
5. Tap the directions link. Does the map app open with your address?
6. Open the menu or price list. Can you read it without zooming?
7. Send the contact form. Is the keyboard right for each field? Does the thank-you page appear?
8. If you sell: put something in the basket and go to checkout (do not pay).
9. Switch language, if you have several.
10. Rotate the phone. Does anything break?

Write down what was slow or awkward and send it to your builder. A fuller plan is in
[Responsive sites](../../businesses/mobile-and-headless/responsive-sites.md#a-test-plan-you-can-run-yourself) and the
[mobile checklist](../../businesses/mobile-and-headless/mobile-checklist.md).

## When to call the builder

- Text is cut off or the page scrolls sideways.
- The menu button does not open.
- Pages take long to appear on mobile data.
- A form cannot be sent on a phone.
