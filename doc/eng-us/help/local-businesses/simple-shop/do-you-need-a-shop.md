# Do you need a shop?

This page is for the owner of a local business who is asked "Can I order online?" and wonders whether to say yes. A
shop on Exponential costs no license fee, but it costs setup work, payment fees and daily attention. Answer the
questions below before you ask anyone to build one.

## Contents

1. [Five questions](#five-questions)
2. [The work a shop adds](#the-work-a-shop-adds)
3. [Lighter alternatives](#lighter-alternatives)
4. [Decision table](#decision-table)

## Five questions

1. **Who would buy?** Regular customers who cannot come in, visitors who want a gift, people from further away?
2. **What would they buy?** Things that can be shipped or collected, vouchers, or services that need an appointment
   (which the shop is not made for; see [What needs an outside service](../budget/what-needs-an-outside-service.md))?
3. **How many orders a week do you expect, honestly?** Write a low and a high guess.
4. **Who handles an order** within a day: checks the payment, packs, ships or prepares it for collection, answers
   questions?
5. **What is the margin after payment fees and shipping?** Use your own numbers in the
   [budget worksheet](../budget/budget-worksheet.md).

## The work a shop adds

| Task | How often | Read |
|---|---|---|
| Check new orders and payments | daily | [Daily store routine](../../customers/commerce/daily-store-routine.md) |
| Move orders through their statuses | each order | [Order statuses](../../customers/commerce/order-statuses.md) |
| Keep products, prices and photos current | weekly | [Products and catalog](../../customers/commerce/products-and-catalog.md) |
| Handle returns and refunds | when they happen | [Returns and refunds](../../customers/commerce/returns-and-refunds.md) |
| VAT check | quarterly | [VAT checklist](../../customers/commerce/vat-checklist.md) |
| Answer data requests from customers | when they happen | [GDPR requests](../../customers/commerce/gdpr-requests.md) |

Selling online also brings rules about returns, terms and prices shown with tax that differ by country. Ask your
adviser; this guide is not legal advice.

## Lighter alternatives

| Instead of a shop | How in Exponential | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| A price list or menu page | an article or a matrix table | showing prices without selling |
| An order or enquiry form | an information collector form ([Quote and request forms](../contact-and-forms/quote-and-request-forms.md)) | custom orders, cakes, catering; you confirm and take payment in person |
| Vouchers only | a tiny shop with one product class and PayPal | gifts; little stock to manage |
| A link to a marketplace or delivery platform | a link on your page | restaurants, crafts; outside service |

An enquiry form is often the right first step: you see real demand before paying for a full shop.

## Decision table

| Your answers | Suggestion |
|---|---|
| Few expected orders, custom products | enquiry form |
| Gift demand, especially around holidays | vouchers only, maybe pickup |
| A steady range of shippable goods and someone to handle orders daily | the built-in shop ([A small catalog](a-small-catalog.md)) |
| Hot food to order for delivery | outside ordering service ([Restaurant orders and what is not built in](restaurant-orders-and-what-is-not-built-in.md)) |
| Appointments | booking service, linked from the site |
