# Doing it yourself or hiring help

This page is for the owner of a small business who wonders how much of the website they can handle alone. Exponential
separates two kinds of work clearly: **editing content** in the browser, which anyone can learn, and **setting up and
running the software**, which needs someone comfortable with a web server and a command line.

## Three common arrangements

**1. Owner edits, partner builds and runs.** A local partner or freelancer installs and sets up the site, the host
or partner keeps it updated, and the business edits content. This is the most common arrangement for a business with
no technical staff. See [Working with a local partner or freelancer](working-with-a-local-partner-or-freelancer.md).

**2. Owner edits, partner builds, managed host runs.** The partner builds the site once; a hosting company that runs
Exponential for its customers keeps the software, backups and HTTPS in order
([Hosting models compared](../../businesses/choosing/hosting-models-compared.md)).

**3. Someone in the business does everything.** Realistic when someone on the team already runs servers, or wants to
learn. Exponential runs on SQLite with no database server, on PHP 8.0 or later
([Requirements at a glance](../../hosting/basics/requirements-at-a-glance.md)), so a small installation is not
complicated, but updates and backups remain a duty that does not go away.

## What doing it yourself really costs

Doing it yourself saves money and costs time. Put your own hours on paper before deciding. Example assumptions, to
replace with yours:

| Example assumption | Hours |
|---|---|
| Learning the editor | 4 |
| Installing and setting up a theme, for a first-timer | 20 |
| Writing and photographing the first pages | 15 |
| Monthly upkeep (updates, backups check, content) | 2 a month |

Multiply the hours by what an hour of your time is worth to the business, and compare with a partner's quote.
[The cost picture](../budget/the-cost-picture.md) puts both sides in one table.

## Signs you need help

- The site has not been updated for months because "it is complicated".
- You do not know where the backups are, or whether there are any.
- A payment, a form or the mail stopped working and nobody noticed for a week.
- You are asked for a password nobody in the business has.
- You want something listed in [What needs an outside service](../budget/what-needs-an-outside-service.md).

## Decision table

| Your situation | Suggested arrangement |
|---|---|
| No technical person, little time | 1 or 2 |
| Someone technical in the family or team, small site without shop | 3, with a partner on call for problems |
| Shop with card payments | 1 or 2; payments and security deserve a professional |
| Several languages or locations | 1 |
