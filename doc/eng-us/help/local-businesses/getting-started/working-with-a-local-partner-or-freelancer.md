# Working with a local partner or freelancer

This page is for the owner of a small business who hires someone to build or run the website. A good arrangement
leaves you owning your site, your domain and your content, with someone reliable to call when something breaks.

## Partner or freelancer

| | Freelancer | Partner company |
|---|---|---|
| Typical size | one person | a team |
| Holiday and illness | plan a backup person | usually covered |
| Exponential experience | ask for examples | Exponential Partners are listed in the partner program ([Partners: start here](../../partners/partners-start-here.md)) |
| Hosting | often arranged with a host | sometimes included |

Either can do a good job. What matters is experience with Exponential, a clear written agreement and the handover.

## Questions to ask before hiring

From [Questions to ask a partner](../../businesses/choosing/questions-to-ask-a-partner.md), the ones that matter most
for a small business:

1. Which Exponential sites have you built or run, and may I see one?
2. Who will update the software, and how often? What does an update cost?
3. Where are the backups, how often, and have you ever restored one?
4. Which extensions will you use (for example `xrowmetadata`, `bcgooglesitemaps`, `ezgmaplocation`, `recaptcha`)?
   Are they in the standard `composer.json`, or something custom?
5. Who holds the API keys for maps and CAPTCHA, in whose name?
6. How do I change the opening hours myself? Show me.
7. What happens if you stop working with me?

## What to get in writing

- The price for the build, and what is **not** included.
- The monthly or yearly price for hosting, updates and support, and the hours included.
- Response times: how fast they answer when the site is down, and on which days
  ([SLA template](../../customers/partners-and-hosts/sla-template.md)).
- That the content, the design files made for you and the domain belong to the business.
- That the site uses free software under the GNU GPL, so there is no license fee to pass on
  ([Licensing for buyers](../../business/licensing-for-buyers.md)).
- A handover list at the end of the project ([Handover checklist](../../customers/partners-and-hosts/handover-checklist.md)).

Templates you can adapt: [Maintenance agreement template](../../customers/partners-and-hosts/maintenance-agreement-template.md),
[Support agreement template](../../customers/partners-and-hosts/support-agreement-template.md).

## What you keep yourself

| Keep | Why |
|---|---|
| The domain registration, in the business's name | whoever holds the domain controls the site |
| An administrator account of your own | so you are never locked out ([Accounts, passwords and two-factor](../../customers/day-to-day/accounts-passwords-and-two-factor.md)) |
| The accounts with outside services (payment provider, maps, CAPTCHA, analytics, business listings) | they bill or speak for your business |
| A copy of the latest backup, or the right to ask for one | to move to another partner if needed |
| The list of extensions and settings used | so a new partner can take over ([Taking over a site](../../customers/partners-and-hosts/taking-over-a-site.md)) |

## The handover

At the end of the build, ask for one hour in which the partner shows you, on your live site:

1. signing in and changing the opening hours;
2. adding a special or an event;
3. reading a contact form message;
4. replacing a photo;
5. where to find the order list, if you sell;
6. whom to call, and how, when something is wrong.

Write the answers down; [Escalation contacts template](../../customers/partners-and-hosts/escalation-contacts-template.md)
is a ready form for the last point.

## A request you can send

```text
Subject: Website for <business name>, <town>

We are a <kind of business> with <number> location(s) in <town>.
We would like a website on Exponential that:
- shows our services/menu, opening hours, address with a map and contact details;
- has a contact form with spam protection;
- <optional> sells <products, vouchers> with <PayPal>;
- <optional> is in <languages>.
We want to change texts, hours and photos ourselves.
Please quote separately for: build, hosting, updates and support per year.
Please say which extensions you would use and who holds the API keys.
Our brief is attached.
```

Attach the [website brief](../templates/website-brief-for-a-local-business.md).
