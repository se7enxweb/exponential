# Getting started: local business websites

This group of pages is for the owner of a small business who is about to get a website, or to replace an old one. It
helps you decide what you need before anyone opens an editor, so the money goes where it matters.

## Contents

1. [The pages in this group](#the-pages-in-this-group)

## The order to read them

1. Start with [What a small business website needs](what-a-small-business-website-needs.md). It is short and it
   decides how much of the rest you need.
2. Plan the pages with [Planning your pages](planning-your-pages.md) and write them into the
   [website brief](../templates/website-brief-for-a-local-business.md).
3. Read [Doing it yourself or hiring help](diy-or-hire-help.md) and decide.
4. If you hire, read [Working with a local partner or freelancer](working-with-a-local-partner-or-freelancer.md).
5. Choose hosting last, when you know who will run the site:
   [Choosing hosting for a small business](choosing-hosting-for-a-small-business.md).

## Three questions to answer first

| Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Do you want to sell online, or only be found and contacted? | A shop adds payments, VAT, shipping and daily order work ([Do you need a shop?](../simple-shop/do-you-need-a-shop.md)). |
| Who changes the opening hours on a public holiday? | If the answer is "the partner", every change costs money and time. Plan for someone in the business to do it. |
| Who answers when the site is down on a Saturday? | That decides your hosting and support arrangement ([Choosing hosting for a small business](choosing-hosting-for-a-small-business.md)). |
