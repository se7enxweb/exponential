# Local businesses glossary

This page explains the words used in the local businesses section. The general glossary of the project is
[Businesses glossary](../businesses/businesses-glossary.md).

## Contents

1. [A to D](#a-to-d)
2. [E to M](#e-to-m)
3. [N to S](#n-to-s)
4. [T to Z](#t-to-z)

## A to D

**Administration.** The part of the site where staff sign in to edit content and settings
([Logging into the admin](../logging-into-the-admin.md)).

**Alternative text.** A short description of an image for people who cannot see it and for search engines
([Photos that sell](marketing/photos-that-sell.md#alternative-texts)).

**API key.** A code from an outside provider (maps, CAPTCHA) that lets your site use its service. It belongs to an
account, ideally in the business's name ([Showing your location on a map](maps-and-hours/showing-your-location-on-a-map.md#api-keys-and-privacy)).

**Business listing.** Your profile on a map or search platform, managed on that platform, not in Exponential
([Business listings outside Exponential](getting-found/business-listings-outside-exponential.md)).

**CAPTCHA.** A check that a form is filled in by a person; `recaptcha` and `hcaptcha` add one
([Stopping spam](contact-and-forms/stopping-spam.md)).

**Content class.** The type of a page or item (folder, article, product, feedback form), defining its fields
([Planning your pages](getting-started/planning-your-pages.md)).

**Content object.** One page or item, an object of a content class.

**Cron job.** A task the server runs on a schedule: sitemaps, newsletters, hiding content at a date
([Sitemaps and robots.txt](getting-found/sitemaps-and-robots-txt.md)).

**Datatype.** The kind of a field: text line, text block, date, time, matrix, image, price, map location
([Choosing datatypes](../creators/website-creators/choosing-datatypes.md)).

**Draft.** A saved version not yet published ([Seasonal changes](running-it/seasonal-changes.md#prepare-then-publish)).

## E to M

**Example assumption.** A number used in a calculation to show the method, not a real price; replace it with yours
([Budget](budget/README.md)).

**Exponential Intelligence.** The writing assistant in the administration that writes drafts, never publishes
([Exponential Intelligence](../customers/growth/exponential-intelligence.md)).

**Extension.** An add-on package for Exponential; those in the standard `composer.json` come with the distribution.

**Information collector.** The built-in mechanism behind forms: fields collect input, submissions are stored and
mailed ([Contact and forms](contact-and-forms/README.md)).

**JSON-LD.** A format for structured data in the page head ([Structured data: what exists](getting-found/structured-data-what-exists.md)).

**Layouts editor.** The visual page builder for landing pages with blocks, including a Map block
([Layouts editor](../layouts-editor.md)).

**Matrix.** A datatype that stores a small table, useful for opening hours
([Opening hours without a special datatype](maps-and-hours/opening-hours-without-a-special-datatype.md#option-2-a-small-opening-hours-class)).

## N to S

**Nice URL.** See URL alias.

**Notice.** A short text about an exception to the opening hours ([Opening hours content template](templates/opening-hours-content-template.md#notices)).

**Order status.** Where an order stands, for example Ready for pickup or Shipped
([VAT, shipping and pickup](simple-shop/vat-shipping-and-pickup.md#pickup-in-the-shop)).

**Payment gateway.** The extension that connects checkout to a payment provider, such as `ezpaypal`
([Payments](simple-shop/payments-paypal-and-authorize-net.md)).

**POS.** Point of sale, the till. No POS connection is built in
([What needs an outside service](budget/what-needs-an-outside-service.md)).

**robots.txt.** A file telling search engine crawlers where not to go and where the sitemap is
([Sitemaps and robots.txt](getting-found/sitemaps-and-robots-txt.md#robotstxt)).

**Role.** A set of permissions given to a user, for example editor or administrator
([Backups, updates and security](running-it/backups-updates-and-security.md#accounts-when-staff-come-and-go)).

**Siteaccess.** A way into the same installation, for example one per language or the administration
([Multilingual for tourist areas](marketing/multilingual-for-tourist-areas.md)).

**Sitemap.** A file listing all pages for search engines, made by `bcgooglesitemaps` or `xrowmetadata`.

**Structured data.** Machine-readable facts in a page, in the schema.org vocabulary.

## T to Z

**Theme or design.** The look of the site; the default public design is `sevenx_themes_simple`
([Choosing a design or theme](../creators/website-creators/choosing-a-design-or-theme.md)).

**Two-step sign-in.** A second check at sign-in, from `sevenx_authentication_2fa`.

**URL alias.** The readable address made from a page's name, such as `/Visit-us/Opening-hours`
([Nice URLs and moved pages](getting-found/nice-urls-and-moved-pages.md)).

**VAT type.** A named tax rate used by product prices ([VAT, shipping and pickup](simple-shop/vat-shipping-and-pickup.md)).

**Version.** Each saved state of a content object; old versions can be restored.

**Workflow.** Steps that run on an event, such as payment at checkout or publishing on a date
([Holiday and seasonal hours](maps-and-hours/holiday-and-seasonal-hours.md#preparing-ahead-with-dates)).

**xrowmetadata.** The extension for per-page titles, descriptions and sharing images
([Titles and descriptions with xrowmetadata](getting-found/titles-and-descriptions-with-xrowmetadata.md)).
