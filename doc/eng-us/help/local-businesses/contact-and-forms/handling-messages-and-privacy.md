# Handling messages and privacy

This page is for the owner or staff member who handles the messages a local business receives through its website
forms. A form submission contains personal data: a name, an e-mail address, often a phone number. Treat it with care.

> **Note**
> This page describes what Exponential offers and good practice. It is not legal advice. Data protection rules differ
> by country; ask your adviser. More background: [GDPR basics](../../businesses/security/gdpr-basics.md).

## Where messages are

A submission exists in up to three places:

| Place | Who sees it | Read |
|---|---|---|
| The business mailbox | whoever reads that mailbox | your mail provider |
| **Setup > Collected information** in Exponential | administrators with access | [Form submissions](../../creators/website-creators/form-submissions.md) |
| Backups of the site | the host | [Data retention and deletion](../../businesses/security/data-retention-and-deletion.md) |

Deleting in one place does not delete in the others.

## Answering

- Answer from the business mailbox, not a private one, so a colleague can follow up.
- Do not forward messages to people who do not need them.
- Do not add the sender to a newsletter list because they wrote to you; newsletters need their own consent
  ([Newsletters for local businesses](../marketing/newsletters-for-local-businesses.md)).

## The privacy note

Next to each form, a short sentence and a link to the privacy page:

```text
We use your details only to answer your message and delete them after <period>.
More in our privacy note.
```

The privacy page says, in plain words: what data the forms collect, why, how long it is kept, which outside services
are involved (CAPTCHA provider, mail provider, host, maps, analytics) and how to contact you about it. A starting point
for the content: [Consent and forms](../../businesses/security/consent-and-forms.md).

## Keeping and deleting

Exponential keeps submissions until someone deletes them. Decide a period (an example assumption: twelve months for
general enquiries, longer for quotes that became jobs if your bookkeeping needs it) and delete older ones:

1. **Setup > Collected information** lists forms with their number of collections.
2. Open a form, select old submissions, and remove them.
3. Do the same in the mailbox.

Exporting before deleting is possible
([Reading and exporting form submissions](../../creators/website-creators/form-submissions.md)).
What can be exported and erased in a store is listed in
[Data export and erasure](../../customers/commerce/data-export-and-erasure.md).

## When someone asks about their data

A person may ask what you hold about them, or ask you to delete it.

1. Search the mailbox and Collected information for their e-mail address.
2. If they have an account or orders in the shop, see [GDPR requests](../../customers/commerce/gdpr-requests.md).
3. Answer within the time your law requires, and note what you did. A log template:
   [GDPR request log template](../../customers/commerce/gdpr-request-log-template.md).

## A routine

| How often | Task |
|---|---|
| Daily | read and answer new messages |
| Monthly | send a test message through each form |
| Quarterly | delete submissions older than your period, in Exponential and the mailbox |
| Yearly | reread the privacy note; does it still list every outside service? |
