## How to use them

1. Fill in what you know; leave the rest blank and mark it "to decide".
2. Keep the filled-in templates with your business papers, not only in someone's mailbox.
3. Update them once a year in the [yearly review](../running-it/weekly-and-monthly-routine.md#every-year-with-your-host-or-partner).
