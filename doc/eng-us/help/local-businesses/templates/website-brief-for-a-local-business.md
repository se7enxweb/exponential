# Website brief for a local business

This template is for the owner of a local business. Fill it in before asking for quotes; partners can then quote the
same thing, and you can compare. Leave a line blank if you do not know yet and write "to decide".

## Goals

What should the site achieve in its first year? Tick and add your own:

- [ ] Be found by people nearby ([Local SEO basics](../getting-found/local-seo-basics.md))
- [ ] Answer hours, location and contact questions
- [ ] Receive enquiries or quote requests
- [ ] Sell products or vouchers online
- [ ] Announce events and specials
- [ ] Build a newsletter list
- [ ] Other: ________

How will you know it works? (for example: "five enquiries a month through the form")
