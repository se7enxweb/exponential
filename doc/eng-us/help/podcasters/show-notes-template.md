# Show notes template

This page is for hosts and producers who want a fixed structure for every episode. Copy a template into the rich text
field of the episode and replace the parts in angle brackets. The reasons behind each part are on
[Writing show notes](writing-show-notes.md). In the editor, turn the lines marked as headings into headings with the
paragraph style list.

## Short description for the feed

If your episode class has a separate short field mapped as the feed **Description**, use one or two sentences:

```text
<Guest name>, <role>, talks with <host> about <topic>: <one concrete thing listeners learn>.
```

```text
This week: <topic one>, <topic two> and <topic three>, in <length> minutes.
```

## Interview episode

```text
<One or two sentences: who the guest is and what the conversation is about.>

Heading: In this episode
00:00 Introduction
<MM:SS> <topic>
<MM:SS> <topic>
<MM:SS> Listener questions

Heading: Our guest
<Name>, <role> at <organization>. <Link to the guest's page or site, as the guest agreed.>

Heading: Links mentioned
- <Title of the link>
- <Title of the link>

Heading: Credits
Host: <name>. Production: <name>. Editing: <name>.
Music: <title> by <artist>, <license or permission>.

Heading: Sponsor
<This episode is sponsored by <sponsor>. or: This episode has no sponsor.>

Heading: Transcript
<The transcript is below / on its own page: link.>
```

## Solo or news episode

```text
<One or two sentences: what this episode covers.>

Heading: Topics
<MM:SS> <topic>, with a link to the source
<MM:SS> <topic>, with a link to the source

Heading: Sources
- <Source title, publisher, date>
- <Source title, publisher, date>

Heading: Corrections
<None. or: In episode <number> we said <x>; correct is <y>.>

Heading: Credits
<Host, production, music with license.>
```

A news show should keep corrections visible on the episode page, as the press section recommends for any coverage
([Corrections and updates](../press/corrections-and-updates.md)).

## Live or video episode

```text
<One or two sentences: what happened in the live show or the video.>

Heading: Watch or listen
<Recorded live on <date>. The replay is above.>

Heading: Chapters
00:00 <Start>
<MM:SS> <segment>

Heading: Captions and transcript
<Captions are available in the player / The transcript is below.>

Heading: Links
- <Link>

Heading: Credits
<Host, guests, production, music with license.>
```

For a live stream itself, see [Live streams and premieres](live-streams-and-premieres.md).

## Before you publish

- [ ] All angle brackets replaced, or the line removed.
- [ ] Times match the final edit, not the raw recording.
- [ ] Guests approved how they are named and linked.
- [ ] Music and clips are credited with their license ([Rights and licensing](rights-and-licensing.md)).
- [ ] The first sentence makes sense alone, because apps may show nothing else.
