# Your first week in the community

This plan takes about half an hour a day. Skip any day that does not fit you; the order matters more than the pace.

## Contents

1. [The plan](#the-plan)
2. [Day by day](#day-by-day)
3. [After the first week](#after-the-first-week)

## The plan

| Day | Step | Time | Page |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Read the code of conduct and the two "Who is" pages | 30 min | [The code of conduct](joining/the-code-of-conduct.md) |
| 2 | Visit the places where people meet; join one | 20 min | [Where people meet](joining/where-people-meet.md) |
| 3 | Install a test site or open the demo | 30 min | [Getting started](../getting-started.md) |
| 4 | Introduce yourself, or ask your first question | 15 min | [Introducing yourself](joining/introducing-yourself.md), [Asking a good question](joining/asking-a-good-question.md) |
| 5 | Find one thing that is wrong or unclear | 30 min | [Filing a bug](contributing/filing-a-bug.md) |
| 6 | Make a small contribution | 30 min | [Your first contribution](contributing/your-first-contribution.md) |
| 7 | Choose what you want to do next | 15 min | [Paths](paths/README.md) |

## Day by day

### Day 1: know the ground rules

Read `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md` (the [summary](joining/the-code-of-conduct.md) is enough to start),
[Who is 7x?](../who-is-7x.md#in-short) and [Who is the Exponential Foundation?](../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#in-short).
You now know who leads the project, who operates it, and how people are expected to treat each other.

### Day 2: find the people

Open the [forums](https://share.exponential.earth/forums) and read the newest threads. Join the
[Telegram chat](https://t.me/exponentialcms) if you prefer conversation. Watch the
[se7enxweb/exponential](https://github.com/se7enxweb/exponential) repository on GitHub if you write code.

### Day 3: run it

Nothing replaces using the software. With SQLite a complete site runs from one file and needs no database server
([Getting started](../getting-started.md), [Database support](../database-support.md)). Take the
[admin tour](../admin-tour.md).

### Day 4: say hello

Post a short introduction, or ask the question that came up on day 3. Read
[Asking a good question](joining/asking-a-good-question.md) first; a good question gets a good answer faster.

### Day 5: notice something

You now know the software a little and have fresh eyes, which experienced members no longer have. Write down one
thing that did not work, one step the documentation skipped, or one word that was not translated.

### Day 6: give something back

Turn day 5 into a contribution: a bug report, a corrected help page through "Edit on GitHub", or a translation.
[Your first contribution](contributing/your-first-contribution.md) walks through each.

### Day 7: choose a direction

Look at [Paths](paths/README.md) and pick the one that fits: editor, developer, designer, host, student. Note it
down with one goal for the next month.

## After the first week

- Keep a short record of what you contributed ([Tracking your contributions](../advocates/tracking-your-contributions.md)).
- Help the next newcomer the way you were helped ([Welcoming newcomers](helping-others/welcoming-newcomers.md)).
- When you are ready for more, read [From user to contributor](paths/from-user-to-contributor.md).
