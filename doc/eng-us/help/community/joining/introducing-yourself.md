# Introducing yourself to the community

An introduction is optional. It helps when you plan to stay: people remember who runs which kind of site and can
point you to the right answer or the right person later.

## Contents

1. [What to say](#what-to-say)
2. [What to leave out](#what-to-leave-out)
3. [A template](#a-template)
4. [When someone else introduces themselves](#when-someone-else-introduces-themselves)

## What to say

- **What you do with Exponential**: run a site, edit one, build them for customers, write extensions, translate,
  study it.
- **How long you have used it**, and from which release or which earlier system you come.
- **What you hope to find or give**: answers to a problem, people in your language or region, a project to help with.
- **Your ties**, if you work for a company that sells Exponential services. The code of conduct asks partners and
  everyone who sells services to "Disclose conflicts of interest" (`CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`).

## What to leave out

- Personal data you would not want to stay public for years: a home address, a private phone number.
- Data about your customers or your site's users.
- Server addresses, administrator user names, version details of a production site that is not patched yet.
- A sales pitch. "Use community spaces to help, not to advertise" (`CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`).

## A template

```text
Hello, I'm <first name or handle> from <city or country, optional>.
I <run / build / edit / develop for> <kind of site: a store, an intranet, a magazine ...>
on Exponential <version, if you want> since <year>.
I'm here to <learn X / help with Y / find people working on Z>.
<If it applies: I work for <company>, which offers Exponential services.>
```

Keep it short. A question at the end ("Is anyone else running a multilingual store?") starts a conversation better
than a long biography.

## When someone else introduces themselves

- Welcome them by name and answer any question they asked.
- Point them to one page that fits what they said, not ten ([Pointing to the right help page](../../advocates/pointing-to-the-right-help-page.md)).
- Do not pitch your services in reply to an introduction.

See [Welcoming newcomers](../helping-others/welcoming-newcomers.md) for more.
