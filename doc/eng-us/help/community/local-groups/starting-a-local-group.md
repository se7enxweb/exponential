# Starting a local group

A local group can be three people in a café or a monthly video call. What matters is that it meets regularly and
that people leave knowing more than when they came.

## Finding interest

- Post in the [forums](https://share.exponential.earth/forums) and the chats: "Is anyone in <region> or speaking
  <language> interested in meeting?"
- Ask colleagues, customers and students who use Exponential.
- Two or three interested people are enough to start.

## Choosing a format

| Format | Good for | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| Online call | Spread-out groups, one language | A video tool, a host |
| In person, informal | Cities with several users | A quiet place |
| In person with talks | Larger groups | A room, a projector, a speaker |
| Contribution day | Groups that want to give back | Laptops, test installations ([A contribution day](a-contribution-day.md)) |

The advocates' pages cover the details: [Proposing a meetup](../../advocates/proposing-a-meetup.md),
[Running an online meetup](../../advocates/running-an-online-meetup.md).

## The first meeting

1. Fix a date, a time and a place or link, two to four weeks ahead.
2. Name a contact person for problems, as the code of conduct asks of event organizers
   ([Community safety and privacy](community-safety-and-privacy.md)).
3. Prepare one short talk or demo, and plenty of time to talk.
4. Ask at the end what people want next time.

## A simple agenda

| Time | Part |
|---|---|
| 0:00 | Welcome, introductions, the code of conduct in one sentence |
| 0:10 | One short talk or demo (for example a new feature of the latest release) |
| 0:30 | Questions and problems people bring |
| 1:00 | What we could do together next; next date |

Talk material can be built from the help, which is free documentation
([Twenty-minute talk slide outline](../../advocates/twenty-minute-talk-slide-outline.md),
[Lightning talk script](../../advocates/lightning-talk-script.md)).

## Keeping it going

- Meet on a fixed rhythm, for example the first Tuesday of the month.
- Share the organizing among two or three people.
- Keep a short public note of each meeting, without personal data.
- Invite newcomers explicitly; groups that only talk to themselves shrink.

## Announcing it

Describe it as what it is: a community-run group. Do not use project logos or the word "official" unless the project
has agreed ([Naming and brand](../../partners/naming-and-brand-for-partners.md),
[Claims you must not make](../../advocates/claims-you-must-not-make.md)).
