# Writing tutorials and posts for the community

When you solved something that took you a day, a write-up can save the next person that day.

## Choosing the place

| Your text is | Put it on |
|---|---|
| A short trick or a reference note | [eZpedia](https://ezpedia.exponential.earth/) ([Contributing to eZpedia](../../advocates/contributing-to-ezpedia.md)) |
| A tutorial or an article | [Share](https://share.exponential.earth/) ([Publishing on Share and Projects](../../advocates/publishing-on-share-and-projects.md)) |
| The solution to a forum question | The forum thread itself |
| A correction to the official help | A pull request ([Contributing documentation and help pages](../contributing/contributing-documentation-and-help-pages.md)) |
| Your own opinion or experience | Your own blog, linked from the forums |

## A structure that works

1. **What you will build or solve**, in one or two sentences, with a screenshot of the result.
2. **Who it is for** and what they need to know first.
3. **Requirements**: the Exponential version, extensions, PHP version, setup you tested on.
4. **Steps**, numbered, one action each, with the exact commands or settings.
5. **How to check it worked.**
6. **What can go wrong** and how to fix it.
7. **Sources**: the help pages and files you relied on.

The detailed guide is [Writing a tutorial that works](../../advocates/writing-a-tutorial-that-works.md); for posts,
[Writing a blog post](../../advocates/writing-a-blog-post.md).

## Test every step

Follow your own tutorial on a fresh test installation before you publish it. SQLite makes that quick
([Getting started](../../getting-started.md)). Steps that worked on your long-lived development site often depend on
something you configured months ago.

## Licensing your writing

Say under which license others may reuse your text. If you quote or adapt the help, it is under the GNU FDL 1.3 or
later and your adaptation must keep that license ([Free documentation](../../free-documentation.md),
[Citing sources and licensing your writing](../../advocates/citing-sources-and-licensing-your-writing.md)).

## Naming things correctly

- The product is **Exponential**. Use the old name only for history, in the past tense.
- **7x** is the company; the **Exponential Foundation** is the nonprofit.
- Do not call your tutorial "official" ([Claims you must not make](../../advocates/claims-you-must-not-make.md)).
- Short reference: [Naming rules quick card](../../advocates/naming-rules-quick-card.md).
