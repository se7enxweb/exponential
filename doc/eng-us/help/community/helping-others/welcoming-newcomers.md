# Welcoming newcomers to the community

The first reply someone gets often decides whether they come back. You do not need to solve their problem to welcome
them; you need to make them feel that asking was right.

## Contents

1. [The first reply](#the-first-reply)
2. [Five pages for newcomers](#five-pages-for-newcomers)
3. [Different kinds of newcomers](#different-kinds-of-newcomers)
4. [What makes people leave](#what-makes-people-leave)

## The first reply

- Greet them and thank them for the question or the introduction.
- Answer, or say who might be able to answer.
- Point to one page, not ten.
- Invite them back: "Let us know how it goes."

## Different kinds of newcomers

| Newcomer | What helps |
|---|---|
| Someone who used the product years ago under its old name | Explain that the code base continues as Exponential, led by 7x since 2023 and operated by the Exponential Foundation ([Who is 7x?](../../who-is-7x.md)); point to [Upgrading](../../../install/11-upgrading.md) |
| Someone coming from eZ Platform or Ibexa | [Migrating from eZ Platform and Ibexa](../../hosting/migrations/from-ez-platform-and-ibexa.md) and the platform line on [Who is the Exponential Foundation?](../../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#the-product-family-at-a-glance) |
| A developer new to the kernel | [Develop with Git](../../develop-with-git.md), [Extension development](../../extension-development/README.md) |
| An editor with no technical background | [Getting started](../../getting-started.md), [Editing content](../../editing-content.md); avoid command-line answers |
| A student | Small, safe tasks: a translation, a help page fix ([Community for students and newcomers](../paths/community-for-students-and-newcomers.md)) |
| Someone writing in another language | Answer in their language if you can, or in simple English |

## What makes people leave

- No answer at all. If nobody else has replied after a day, even "I don't know, but try X" helps.
- A sarcastic or impatient answer.
- Being sold to instead of helped.
- Being told to read something huge without a pointer to the right part.

The code of conduct applies to every reply ([The code of conduct](../joining/the-code-of-conduct.md)).
