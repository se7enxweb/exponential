# Helping others in the community

Free help in the forums and chats depends on the time people give ([When something goes wrong](../../when-something-goes-wrong.md)).
Once you know a little, you can give some of that time back. These pages show how to help well.

## Why helping matters

- A newcomer who gets a friendly, correct answer often stays and becomes a contributor.
- An answer in the forums is found by everyone with the same question later.
- Experienced members see which parts of the software and documentation confuse people, and can fix them.

## How much is enough

There is no quota. One good answer a week is a real contribution. Do not answer when you are unsure; say what you
know and what you do not, or leave the question to someone else.
