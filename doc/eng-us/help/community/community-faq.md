## Joining

**How do I join the community?**
By taking part: ask, answer, report, contribute, or simply use Exponential and tell others. There is no sign-up and no
fee ([What the community is](what-the-community-is.md)).

**Where do I ask a question?**
The help first, then the forums; Telegram, Discord or Slack for something quick
([Where people meet](joining/where-people-meet.md)).

**Is anyone obliged to answer me?**
No. Free help has no answer time; paid support from 7x or a partner does
([Getting help step by step](joining/getting-help-step-by-step.md)).

**Is there a mailing list or newsletter for the community?**
None is named in the sources. See [Staying informed](joining/staying-informed.md) for the documented ways.

## Contributing

**Can I contribute without programming?**
Yes: bug reports, confirmations, translations, help pages, testing and answers
([Ways to contribute](contributing/ways-to-contribute.md)).

**Do I have to sign something?**
For code contributed to the kernel repository, yes: the CLA, which assigns the copyright in the contribution to 7x.
Your own extensions and sites are not affected ([The CLA explained](contributing/the-cla-explained.md)).

**Will my pull request be merged?**
Every pull request is merged, once its author has revised, squashed and rebased it as the reviews ask; expect many
rounds ([Reviewing and being reviewed](contributing/reviewing-and-being-reviewed.md)).

**May I use an AI tool for my contribution?**
Yes, on the same terms as any contribution: you are responsible for it, check it, and say when a substantial part was
generated (`CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`, "AI-generated contributions").

**Where do I share my extension?**
In your own repository and on Packagist, listed on Projects ([Sharing extensions and themes](contributing/sharing-extensions-and-themes.md)).

## Conduct and safety

**What if someone treats me badly?**
Report it to community@se7enx.com; the Exponential Foundation decides ([The code of conduct](joining/the-code-of-conduct.md)).

**I found a security problem. Can I post it?**
No. Send it privately to security@se7enx.com ([How security reports are handled](how-it-works/how-security-reports-are-handled.md)).

## The project

**Who leads Exponential?**
7x leads development; the nonprofit Exponential Foundation operates the project ([Who decides](how-it-works/who-decides.md)).

**Are there official events or meetups?**
None are documented. Groups and events that exist are run by community members ([Local groups](local-groups/README.md)).

**Can I call my course, group or tutorial "official"?**
No, unless the project or the partner program has made it so (`CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`).

**What is the difference between the community, partners and the ecosystem?**
Partners fund the project, contribute back and engage directly with 7x and the Foundation; community members use,
contribute to, promote and develop software using Exponential; the ecosystem is the whole Exponential world within
the PHP stack ([Partners, community and ecosystem](partners-community-and-ecosystem.md)).

**How are contributors recognized?**
Through authorship in the Git history and credit in the documentation; sponsors and partners have their own forms
([Recognition and credit](how-it-works/recognition-and-credit.md)).

**Is Exponential the same as the old product?**
It continues the same code base, which began as eZ Publish before its creator discontinued it; Exponential "is not
associated with the original eZ Publish software or its original developer" (`README.md`).
