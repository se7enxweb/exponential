# The community for hosts and agencies

Companies that host or build Exponential sites for others depend on the community and can give a lot back.

## Contents

1. [What companies gain](#what-companies-gain)
2. [Contributions only companies can make](#contributions-only-companies-can-make)
3. [Commercial conduct in community spaces](#commercial-conduct-in-community-spaces)
4. [The step to partnership](#the-step-to-partnership)

## What companies gain

- Answers and fixes from people who run the same software.
- A public record of how problems were solved, for their own staff.
- People to hire: contributors whose work is visible on GitHub.
- A healthier project, which is what their customers' sites run on.

## Contributions only companies can make

| Contribution | Why companies | Page |
|---|---|---|
| Testing releases on many PHP versions, databases and operating systems | They run them | [Testing releases](../contributing/testing-releases.md) |
| Documenting hosting setups and panels | They know them | [Hosting companies: start here](../../hosting/hosting-companies-start-here.md) |
| Giving staff time to contribute | They employ developers | [Supporting Exponential as a company](../../supporters/supporting-as-a-company.md) |
| Sponsoring features customers need | They have budgets | [Sponsoring as a company](../../sponsors/sponsoring-as-a-company.md) |
| Contributing site packages and extensions from client work, where the client agrees | They build them | [Sharing extensions and themes](../contributing/sharing-extensions-and-themes.md) |
| Writing anonymized case studies | They have the projects | [Writing a community case study](../../advocates/writing-a-community-case-study.md) |

If your developers contribute to the kernel for your company, the company usually owns the copyright and signs the
CLA, or confirms permission (`CONTRIBUTING.md`, "FAQ").

## Commercial conduct in community spaces

The code of conduct's "Partners and commercial conduct" applies to "everyone who sells products or services based on
Exponential" (`CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`):

- Tell customers the truth about Exponential and about what you deliver yourself.
- Compete fairly; do not disparage others.
- Disclose conflicts of interest.
- Respect the licenses.
- Protect the personal data of customers and leads.
- "Use community spaces to help, not to advertise."
