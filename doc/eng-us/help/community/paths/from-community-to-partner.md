# From community to partner

Most partners were community members first. Partnership is the step a company takes when it wants to work directly
with 7x and the Exponential Foundation.

## Contents

1. [What changes](#what-changes)
2. [Signs you are ready](#signs-you-are-ready)
3. [The levels](#the-levels)
4. [Community work counts](#community-work-counts)
5. [How to apply](#how-to-apply)

## What changes

| | Community member | Partner |
|---|---|---|
| Definition | Uses, contributes to, promotes and develops software using Exponential | Contributes financially to the sustained future of the project, contributes code and more back, and engages directly with 7x and the Exponential Foundation |
| Agreement | None | The partnership agreement ([The partnership agreement](../../partners/the-partnership-agreement.md)) |
| Cost | None | A monthly subscription by level |
| Contact with 7x | Public channels | Direct: tickets, guidance meetings, at higher levels shared leads and projects |
| Conduct | The code of conduct | The code of conduct, including "Partners and commercial conduct", and the partner code of conduct |

## Signs you are ready

- Your company earns money with Exponential, or plans to.
- Your customers would value a listed partner.
- You want a direct line to 7x for your projects.
- You contribute already and want it recognized.

Not ready yet? Individual support through [Supporters](../../supporters/supporters-start-here.md) or a sponsorship is
a good start ([Why become a partner](../../partners/why-become-a-partner.md)).

## The levels

Eight levels, in the order Student, Now, Starter, Basic, Professional, Silver, Gold and Platinum, at $25 to $2,600 a
month as published on 18 October 2026 ([Partner levels](../../partners/partner-levels.md),
[Partner plans and prices](../../partners/partner-plans-and-prices.md)). Students and teachers start at the Student
level ([Student and education partners](../../partners/student-and-education-partners.md)).

## Community work counts

The partner program has a points system, and community work earns points
([Earning points through community work](../../partners/earning-points-through-community-work.md),
[The points system](../../partners/the-points-system.md)). The contributions in this section, from bug reports to
pull requests, are the same work.

## How to apply

1. Read [Partners: start here](../../partners/partners-start-here.md) and
   [What is an Exponential Partner](../../partners/what-is-an-exponential-partner.md).
2. Choose a level ([Partner requirements](../../partners/partner-requirements.md)).
3. Go through the [Application checklist](../../partners/application-checklist.md).
4. Apply as [How to apply](../../partners/how-to-apply.md) describes.
