# Licenses in the community

This page is a summary for community members, not legal advice. The license texts are binding.

## Contents

1. [The three texts](#the-three-texts)
2. [What you may do](#what-you-may-do)

## The three texts

| Covers | License | Source |
|---|---|---|
| The code of Exponential | GNU General Public License, version 2 or later | `LICENSE`, `README.md` ("License") |
| The documentation, including this help | GNU Free Documentation License 1.3 or later | [The license of this documentation](../../../README-LICENSE.md) |
| Code contributed to the kernel | The GPL, plus a copyright assignment to the Exponential Foundation and 7x through the CLA | `CONTRIBUTING.md` |

"Using Exponential under the terms of the GNU GPL is free of charge" (`README.md`). The former professional license
"is no longer available", so Exponential is "GNU GPLv2-or-later ONLY" (same).

## What you may do

- **Use** Exponential for any purpose, including commercial sites.
- **Study and change** it.
- **Share** it, changed or not, under the same license.
- **Copy, translate and adapt** the documentation under the GNU FDL ([Free documentation](../../free-documentation.md)).

## What the licenses ask of you

- **When you distribute** Exponential or code based on it, the GPL's conditions apply: the source goes with it
  ([Contribute extensions back](../../contribute-extensions-back.md)).
- **Contributions to the project** "are also released under the same GPLv2 or later license" (`CONTRIBUTING.md`).
- **Adapted documentation** stays under the GNU FDL.
- The code of conduct adds: "do not present free software as your own proprietary product or restrict the freedoms
  the licenses give your customers" (`CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`, "Partners and commercial conduct").

## Common situations

| Situation | In short |
|---|---|
| You run a site on Exponential | Free of charge; no obligation to publish your own templates or extensions |
| You build a site for a customer | The customer receives the same freedoms; see [Licensing for buyers](../../business/licensing-for-buyers.md) |
| You publish your own extension | Your copyright; the project recommends GPL 2 or later; no CLA |
| You send a fix to the kernel | GPL, plus the CLA ([The CLA explained](../contributing/the-cla-explained.md)) |
| You translate help pages | GNU FDL; credit the original |
| You quote the help in a talk or course | Allowed under the GNU FDL; cite it ([Citing sources and licensing your writing](../../advocates/citing-sources-and-licensing-your-writing.md)) |

More questions: [Licensing FAQ](../../project/licensing-faq.md).
