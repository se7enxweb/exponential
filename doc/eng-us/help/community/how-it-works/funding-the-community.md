# Funding the community

"The Exponential Open Source Project is funded by donations of time and resources by 7x (and Exponential Sponsors)"
(exponential.earth/about/project, quoted on [Supporters: start here](../../supporters/supporters-start-here.md)).
Community members give time; this page is about money.

## Two organizations to fund

| | 7x | The Exponential Foundation |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | The company that leads development, support and hosting | The 501(c)(3) nonprofit that operates the project, founded 19 March 2026 |
| GitHub Sponsors | <https://github.com/sponsors/se7enxweb> | <https://github.com/sponsors/exponentialcms> |
| Other ways | <https://sponsor.se7enx.com>, <https://www.paypal.com/paypalme/7xweb> (`README.md`) | "a sustaining subscription or one time donation" through <https://foundation.exponential.earth/> |

Source: [Two organizations, two sponsor pages](../../sponsors/two-organizations-two-sponsor-pages.md). The
Foundation says donations are tax deductible in the United States; check with your own tax adviser
([How to support or join](../../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#how-to-support-or-join)).

## For companies: partnership

Partners "contribute financially to the sustained future of the project, contribute code and more back to the
project, and engage directly with 7x and the Exponential Foundation". The partner program has levels from Student to
Platinum with monthly subscriptions "From $25 (Student) to $2,600 (Platinum)"
([Why become a partner](../../partners/why-become-a-partner.md), [Partner levels](../../partners/partner-levels.md)).
See [From community to partner](../paths/from-community-to-partner.md).

## If you have no budget

Time is funding too. A bug report, a translation or a well-answered question saves paid hours that can go to new
work instead ([Ways to contribute](../contributing/ways-to-contribute.md)). Telling your employer or your customers
that the software they rely on accepts sponsorship also helps
([Advocating for sustainable funding](../../advocates/advocating-for-sustainable-funding.md)).
