## Contributors

- **The Git history** keeps the author of every merged commit; `git shortlog -sn` shows everyone who contributed
  ([Recognition for supporters](../../supporters/recognition-for-supporters.md)).
- **Credit is not copyright.** The copyright notice names only the Exponential Foundation and 7x, because every
  contributor assigns the copyright in a contribution to them through the CLA; the credit stays with the people
  ([Copyright and copyleft](../../copyright-and-copyleft.md)).
- **Credit for other projects** appears in the README, for example the LoveStack improvements rebuilt "with full credit
  to Mugo Web" (`README.md`).
- **Contributors by name**: [How the project grows](../../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#how-the-project-grows)
  lists organizations whose pull requests were merged since December 2023, and
  [Who is 7x?](../../who-is-7x.md#contributors-and-friends-of-the-project) the people of earlier years.

## Sponsors

Sponsors on GitHub Sponsors are recognized by tier: badges, mentions, a name or logo in the README or on the
website, depending on the tier and the organization. The table, with the date it was read, is on
[Recognition for supporters](../../supporters/recognition-for-supporters.md); details on
[How sponsors are recognized](../../sponsors/sponsor-recognition.md).

Partners are recognized through their level, the partner listings and points for community work

## What is not defined yet

A general contributors page on the project website, community awards and a supporter badge are not described in the
sources: "to be defined by the Foundation" ([Recognition for supporters](../../supporters/recognition-for-supporters.md)).
Do not promise any of these to people you recruit.

## Giving credit yourself

- Thank people by name in the thread where they helped.
- In a tutorial, name the forum thread or person whose answer you built on.
- In a pull request, credit a co-author or the person who found the bug.
- When you reuse documentation, keep its license and say where it came from.

Ideas for recognizing people in your own group or event: [Recognition ideas](../../advocates/recognition-ideas.md).
