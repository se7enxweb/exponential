# The leadership reading path

This page is for project managers and for sales and marketing people who are new to Exponential and want to become
confident, and then leading, members of an Exponential team. It answers one question: **what should I read, in which
order?**

The path covers every page of the two sections written for you, **Leadership** (`doc/eng-us/help/leadership/`) and
**Business** (`doc/eng-us/help/business/`), and the existing help pages you need next to them. It is arranged per role and
per week. Everything here is in the help of your own installation, at `/exphelp/dashboard`, and free to copy and adapt
([Free documentation](../free-documentation.md)).

## How to use this path

- **Read in order.** Each week builds on the one before. The first week is the same for both roles.
- **Read with the administration open.** Most pages refer to real screens. Use an installation you control, never a
  customer's live site, when you try things out.
- **About four to six hours a week.** The path has six weeks. A fast reader can do it in three; a busy one in eight.
- **Answer the check question** at the end of each week before you move on ([Check questions](#check-questions)).
- **Bring your questions** to the team's weekly meeting ([Working together](working-together.md#meeting-cadences)).

## Check questions

Answer each one from the help, with the page that says so. If you cannot, read that week again.

| Week | Project manager | Sales and marketing |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Who are 7x and the Exponential Foundation, and how do they differ? | Which names may you use for the product today, and how do you mention its past? |
| 2 | Which team role maps to which administration role on your next project? | What is your one-sentence pitch, and which page backs each claim in it? |
| 3 | How many locales and administration translations does Exponential have, and where is that counted? | What may a customer do with the code under the GPL, and with the help under the GFDL? |
| 4 | What happens when a released version has a bug: is the tag changed? | How do you answer "is it still maintained?", with sources? |
| 5 | Which KPIs can the product itself produce, and on which page? | What must a case study have before it may be published? |
| 6 | What are your next three community contributions? | Which documented community places will you promote, and how? |
