A view of the `shop` module, defined in `kernel/shop/module.php` and run by `kernel/shop/orderreceipt.php`.

## Address

```text
/shop/orderreceipt/<Token>/(download)/<Download>
```

Ordered parameters, in this order after the view name: `Token`.

Unordered parameters, in any order as `/(name)/value`: `(download)` sets `Download`.

## Who may open it

Listed in `settings/site.ini` `[RoleSettings] PolicyOmitList[]`: no policy is checked, everyone may open it.
The view names no policy function, so the user needs a policy for the whole module: `shop/*` (every function of the module) in one of the roles assigned to them.
