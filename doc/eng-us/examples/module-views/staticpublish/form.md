## Who may open it

Listed in `settings/site.ini` `[RoleSettings] PolicyOmitList[]`: no policy is checked, everyone may open it.
The view names no policy function, so the user needs a policy for the whole module: `staticpublish/*` (every function of the module) in one of the roles assigned to them.
