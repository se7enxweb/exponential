## Address


Ordered parameters, in this order after the view name: `Format`.

Unordered parameters, in any order as `/(name)/value`: `(token)` sets `Token`.

## Who may open it

Listed in `settings/site.ini` `[RoleSettings] PolicyOmitList[]`: no policy is checked, everyone may open it.
The view names no policy function, so the user needs a policy for the whole module: `mailpreferences/*` (every function of the module) in one of the roles assigned to them.
