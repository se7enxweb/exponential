# Policies of the state module

The policy functions of `state` and their limitations, from `$FunctionList` in `kernel/state/module.php`. In the administration they are chosen under Roles and policies; a policy on `state/*` grants every function.

Some functions also take one limitation per object state group of the site, named `StateGroup_<group identifier>` (`eZContentObjectStateGroup::limitations()`, read from the database, so not listed below).

## administrate

Checked by the views: [edit](../module-views/state/edit.md), [group](../module-views/state/group.md), [group_edit](../module-views/state/group_edit.md), [groups](../module-views/state/groups.md), [view](../module-views/state/view.md).

No limitations: the policy is all or nothing.

```php
// Give a role the policy (eZRole::appendPolicy( $module, $function, $limitations ))
$role = eZRole::fetchByName( 'Editor' );
$role->appendPolicy( 'state', 'administrate' );
```
