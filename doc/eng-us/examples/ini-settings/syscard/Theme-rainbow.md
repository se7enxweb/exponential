# syscard.ini [Theme-rainbow]

The block `[Theme-rainbow]` of `settings/syscard.ini`, with 16 setting(s).

> A theme: a #RRGGBB color per role (empty: the terminal's own color). Logo and LogoShade shade the "e" from the top  
> to the bottom; Accent is the title and the section headings; Gradient\[\] the wordmark and the gradient bar, across  
> the columns or the lines (GradientDirection). <Role>16= names the basic color for a 16-color terminal (black, red,  
> green, yellow, blue, magenta, cyan, white, bright-<name>) when the nearest one would look wrong.  

Never edit `settings/syscard.ini` itself: an update replaces it. Put the settings you change in an override, which wins over the shipped file:

- `settings/override/syscard.ini.append.php` for every siteaccess,
- `settings/siteaccess/<siteaccess>/syscard.ini.append.php` for one siteaccess,
- `extension/<extension>/settings/syscard.ini.append.php` in an extension (read when the extension is active).
