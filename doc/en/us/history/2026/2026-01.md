# January 2026

What changed in Exponential in January 2026, why it matters, and where to read more. Read it when you upgrade a site across this period or want to know where a feature came from; the [history guide](../README.md) explains how the month pages, the ledger and the coverage page fit together.

## In short

- 6.0.12 was in development.
- Templates can ask whether a user has a role or policy.
- Extensions can carry per-site settings and the INI cache can be shared; the view count cronjob was fixed.

Development of 6.0.12 began (`7eb1f5d406`). The month gave template authors and multi-site operators new tools.

## Role and policy checks in templates

On 10 and 11 January `eZRole::hasPolicy()` (`e51905eb28`) and the operators `has_role`, `has_role_id`, `has_any_role`, `has_role_by_user`, `has_role_by_user_id`, `has_policy` and `has_policy_by_user` were added (`2d2271b1d1`, `a1e4334b5d`), renamed from their first, never released `member_of_` names (`24a2c9911a`, `2542972284`). Read [Role and policy checks in templates](../../features/6.0/role-and-policy-template-operators.md).

## Multi-site settings

Extensions that hold a site can carry settings that apply only while their siteaccess runs, and the INI cache can be shared by every project (`124186d096`, `fc1a2f0a91`). See [Per-site settings inside extensions](../../features/6.0/multi-site-ini-overrides.md) and the [specification](../../specifications/6.0/ini-override-placements.md).

## Fixes

The view count cronjob stored all statistics on one node (`ccd8b3857f`).

## Documentation

The README restates the licence (`42e517602e`), explains the Exponential Platform distributions (`3e4ac3f5ac`), repairs links (`7a53c70a1a`) and shows the `composer create-project` usage (`14d91614f2`).
