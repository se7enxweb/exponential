- `settings/override/rad.ini.append.php` for every siteaccess,
- `settings/siteaccess/<siteaccess>/rad.ini.append.php` for one siteaccess,
- `extension/<extension>/settings/rad.ini.append.php` in an extension (read when the extension is active).
