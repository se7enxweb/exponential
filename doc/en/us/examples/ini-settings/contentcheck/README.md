# contentcheck.ini

The shipped settings of `settings/contentcheck.ini`: 7 blocks, 43 settings.

> The content checks: the assets (images, files, media) below a node that nothing uses, and visible content  
> with links, embeds or relations to deleted, trashed, hidden or unpublished content.  
> Guide: doc/features/6.0/content-checks.md  
>
> Do not edit this file: override it in settings/override/contentcheck.ini.append.php or in  
> a siteaccess's contentcheck.ini.append.php.  
>
> Who may use them is decided by role policies, not here: contentcheck/unreferenced opens the  
> list of unreferenced objects, contentcheck/trash is needed as well to move them to the trash  
> (and content/remove on each object), contentcheck/brokenrelations opens the list of broken  
> relations. Every list shows only what content/read lets the user see.
