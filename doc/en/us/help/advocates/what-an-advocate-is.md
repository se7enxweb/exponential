# What an Exponential advocate is

This page is for anyone who wants to help Exponential grow by talking about it, showing it and helping others with it.
It answers: what does an advocate do, what does an advocate not do, and how is the role different from sales or from
speaking for 7x or the Exponential Foundation?

## In one sentence

An Exponential advocate is a person who, on their own initiative, helps other people discover, understand, use and
contribute to Exponential, and who tells the truth with a source for every claim.

| Activity | Example | Page |

## What advocates do not do

- **Speak for 7x or the Foundation** unless one of them asked you to. Say "I", not "we", when you mean yourself.
- **Promise anything**: no dates for releases, no features that are not documented, no support response times. The
  only documented time is the first answer to a security report, "within one business day"
  ([SECURITY.md](https://github.com/se7enxweb/exponential/blob/main/SECURITY.md)).
- **Invent numbers**: users, installations, developers or market share. The sources do not count them
  ([Claims you must not make](claims-you-must-not-make.md)).
- **Attack other projects.** Compare only with facts both sides can check
  ([Respecting other projects and companies](respecting-other-projects-and-companies.md)).
- **Handle security problems in public.** They go privately to the address in `SECURITY.md`
  ([Security reports stay private](security-reports-stay-private.md)).
- **Collect or share personal data** of the people you help ([Protecting people's privacy](protecting-peoples-privacy.md)).

## Advocate, sales, official representative

| | Advocate | Sales and marketing | Official representative |
|---|---|---|---|
| Who | Any user, developer, designer, translator, company | People who sell services or products around Exponential | People 7x or the Foundation asked to speak for them |
| Speaks for | Themselves | Their employer | The organization that asked them |
| Goal | More people helped, more contributors | Customers for their organization | The organization's messages |
| Typical channel | Forums, Telegram, blogs, user groups | Proposals, tenders, demos for buyers | Official announcements, the project websites |
| Pages | This section | [Sales and marketing: start here](../business/sales-and-marketing-start-here.md) | Ask the organization |

One person can be all three at different times. Say which hat you wear in each conversation
([Disclosure and conflicts of interest](disclosure-and-conflicts-of-interest.md)).

## Why the project needs advocates

The project's own sources describe a community-first project:

- "The Exponential Project and the Exponential Foundation support the open source community directly, as part of our
  daily routine and work ethic, and put the support needs of community users first"
  ([Who is the Exponential Foundation?](../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#what-the-foundation-is)).
- The Foundation's mission includes "Adoption", "Support", "Education", "Promotion" and "Documentation"
  ([In short](../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#in-short)). Advocates do exactly these things, as volunteers.
- Every pull request is merged under the contributor's name
  ([How the project grows](../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#how-the-project-grows)): the project wants
  contributions, and advocates bring contributors.

## Kinds of advocacy

| Kind | Time per month | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| **Helper**: answer questions, share links | 1 to 2 hours | Everyone |
| **Writer**: posts, tutorials, eZpedia | 4 to 8 hours | People who like to explain |
| **Speaker**: talks, demos, workshops | A few evenings | People who like to present |
| **Organizer**: meetups, sprints | Ongoing | People who like to bring others together |
| **Builder**: docs, translations, bug reports, fixes | Any | People who like to make things |
| **Supporter**: sponsor, encourage your employer to sponsor | Minutes | Everyone who can |

The time figures are suggestions for planning, not requirements.

## A self-check

```text
[ ] I can say what Exponential is in one sentence, and cite the README
[ ] I know the difference between 7x and the Exponential Foundation
[ ] I know which names to use and which old name belongs only to history
[ ] I know where to send a bug, a question and a security problem
[ ] I say "I" when I speak for myself and disclose who pays me, if anyone
[ ] I have read "Claims you must not make"
```

If any line is not ticked, read [Advocates: start here](advocates-start-here.md#your-first-hour) first.
