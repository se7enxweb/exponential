# Proposing a meetup

This page is for advocates who want to bring Exponential users together in person or online. **No conferences or
meetups are documented in the project's sources** ([Community places](../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#community-places)).
Any meetup you organize is **your** event: present it that way, never as an official one
([Growing the community](../business/growing-the-community.md#the-documented-channels)).

## Decide the purpose

| Purpose | Good format |
|---|---|
| Meet other users nearby | Informal evening, two short talks |
| Learn something | A workshop ([Hands-on workshop outline](hands-on-workshop-outline.md)) |
| Contribute together | A sprint ([Proposing a contribution sprint](proposing-a-contribution-sprint.md)) |
| Reach people far apart | Online ([Running an online meetup](running-an-online-meetup.md)) |

## Choose a format

- **Size**: start small; five people talking is a success.
- **Place**: a library room, a coworking space, a company office that offers it, or online.
- **Time**: two hours, regular if it works (for example every quarter).
- **Cost**: free if at all possible; if not, say what the money is for.

## The proposal

Post it in the forums first to see who is interested:

```text
Title: Informal Exponential meetup in <city | online>? (proposal)

Hi all, I'd like to organize an informal meetup for people who use or build with Exponential.
This is my own initiative, not an official event of 7x or the Exponential Foundation.

Idea: <purpose>, about <2> hours, <format: 2 short talks + discussion | workshop | sprint>.
Where: <place or online>        When: <two date options>
Who: anyone interested, beginners welcome.

Would you come? Would you like to give a short talk? Reply here.
<Your name, your disclosure line>
```

## The announcement

```text
Informal Exponential meetup - <date>, <time>, <place | online link sent to registered people>
Organized by <you> (not an official event).

Agenda
<time>  Welcome and introductions
<time>  Talk 1: <title> (<speaker>)
<time>  Talk 2: <title> (<speaker>)
<time>  Open discussion / help desk

Sign up: <link>  - we only ask for a name or nickname and a contact, deleted after the event.
Accessibility: <step-free access? captions? say what you know>
Ground rules: be kind; no recording without asking; no sales pitches.
```

## Ground rules

- **Conduct**: state simple rules and who to talk to if something goes wrong
  ([Advocate conduct and ethics](advocate-conduct-and-ethics.md)).
- **Privacy**: minimal sign-up data, deleted afterwards; photos only with consent
  ([Protecting people's privacy](protecting-peoples-privacy.md)).
- **No secrets**: speakers follow [Live demo safety](live-demo-safety.md).
- **Disclosure**: speakers say who they work for.
- **Sponsors**: if a company pays for snacks or a room, say so; it gets thanked, not a sales slot.

Checklists for before, during and after: [Event checklists](event-checklists.md).

## After the meetup

1. Post a short summary in the forums: topics, slides links, next date.
2. Answer open questions with sources.
3. Delete the sign-up list.
4. Log it in your contribution log, without attendee names.
