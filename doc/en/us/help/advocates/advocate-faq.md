# Advocate FAQ

This page is for advocates with a quick question about advocacy itself.

| Question | Answer | Page |
|---|---|---|
| Do I need permission to be an advocate? | No. Help people honestly and you are one | [What an advocate is](what-an-advocate-is.md) |
| Is there an official advocate or ambassador program? | None is documented. Do not claim one | [Claims you must not make](claims-you-must-not-make.md#events-and-programs) |
| Can I speak for 7x or the Foundation? | Only if they ask you. Otherwise speak as yourself | [Disclosure and conflicts of interest](disclosure-and-conflicts-of-interest.md) |
| Can I use the logo? | The sources set no logo rules for third parties; ask the owner | [Brand and naming rules](../business/brand-and-naming-rules.md#what-the-sources-do-not-decide) |
| May I be paid for posts or talks? | Yes, if you disclose it clearly | [Disclosure and conflicts of interest](disclosure-and-conflicts-of-interest.md) |
| How do I show "what is this installation" in a demo? | Run `./console exp:about`: the logo, the facts and the project on one screen, no secrets | [The system card in demos](the-system-card-in-demos.md) |
| Can I organize an "Exponential meetup"? | Yes, as your own event, said clearly | [Proposing a meetup](proposing-a-meetup.md) |
| Can I copy help pages into my blog? | Under the GNU FDL's conditions; quoting with a link is easiest | [Citing sources and licensing your writing](citing-sources-and-licensing-your-writing.md) |
| What if I said something wrong? | Correct it openly | [Honesty and accuracy](honesty-and-accuracy.md) |
| How much time does it take? | As much as you have; one answer a week helps | [What an advocate is, Kinds of advocacy](what-an-advocate-is.md#kinds-of-advocacy) |
| Someone told me about a vulnerability | Route it privately | [Security reports stay private](security-reports-stay-private.md) |
| Where do I propose ideas for the project? | The community issue tracker | [Which tracker for which report](which-tracker-for-which-report.md) |
| Is there a code of conduct? | CONTRIBUTING.md links this conduct page as the project's; adopt the advocate's pledge | [Advocate conduct and ethics](advocate-conduct-and-ethics.md) |
| Does a contributor have to sign anything? | For code contributions to the kernel, yes: a Contributor Licensing Agreement, once, before the first merge. By signing and submitting, they assign the copyright in the contribution to 7x and give up control of it: 7x may use, change, relicense and distribute it without asking or paying them, and they cannot withdraw it. Their own extensions need nothing | [Copyright assignment (CLA)](../../../../../CONTRIBUTING.md#copyright-assignment-cla) |
| How do I support the project financially? | The two GitHub Sponsors pages | [Advocating for sustainable funding](advocating-for-sustainable-funding.md) |
| Where do I start? | Your first hour, then your first week | [Advocates: start here](advocates-start-here.md#your-first-hour) |
