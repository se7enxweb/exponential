# Advocate conduct and ethics

This page is for every advocate. Advocates are trusted because they are honest and kind. This page collects the ethics
that the other advocate pages apply, points to the project's code of conduct, and offers a personal pledge.

## Contents

1. [The code of conduct](#the-code-of-conduct)

## The code of conduct

The project's code of conduct is [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](../../../../../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md): the Contributor Covenant 2.1
with Exponential additions, one of them for trainers and advocates. It applies to you in every community space and
whenever you speak for Exponential. [The Exponential code of conduct](../project/code-of-conduct.md) explains it in
short; report a problem to <community@se7enx.com>, and the Exponential Foundation decides.

The principles and the pledge below are how an advocate lives that code day to day. The pledge only binds you; the
code of conduct binds everyone in its scope.

## Six principles

| Principle | In practice | Page |
|---|---|---|
| **Honesty** | Source every claim; say "not documented"; correct mistakes openly | [Honesty and accuracy](honesty-and-accuracy.md) |
| **Respect** | Kindness to newcomers and critics; fairness to other projects | [Respecting other projects and companies](respecting-other-projects-and-companies.md) |
| **Privacy** | No personal data; consent before naming anyone | [Protecting people's privacy](protecting-peoples-privacy.md) |
| **Transparency** | Disclose employers, payments and own products | [Disclosure and conflicts of interest](disclosure-and-conflicts-of-interest.md) |
| **Security** | Vulnerabilities private; no secrets in public | [Security reports stay private](security-reports-stay-private.md) |
| **Inclusion** | Accessible material; patience with language; welcome everyone | [Welcoming newcomers](welcoming-newcomers.md), [Advocating for accessibility](advocating-for-accessibility.md) |

## The advocate's pledge

Copy it, adapt it and publish it with your name if you like. It is a personal commitment, not a document of the
project.

```text
MY PLEDGE AS AN EXPONENTIAL ADVOCATE

1. I tell the truth about Exponential and link my sources. When I do not know, I say so.
2. I correct my mistakes in the open.
3. I say who I am, who I work for and whether anyone pays me.
4. I speak for myself, not for 7x or the Exponential Foundation, unless they ask me to.
5. I treat everyone with respect: newcomers, critics, and the people behind other projects.
6. I protect people's privacy. I publish nothing about anyone without consent.
7. I keep security problems private and send them to the address in SECURITY.md.
8. I make my talks, posts and events accessible.
9. I credit the work of others.
10. I step back gracefully when I can no longer help, and hand over what I know.

<name>                                   <date>
```

## When something goes wrong

| Situation | Do |
|---|---|
| You broke a principle | Apologize, correct, learn |
| Another advocate breaks one | Talk to them privately and kindly first |
| Harassment or threats in a channel | Report to <community@se7enx.com> and to the channel's moderators or the platform; do not engage ([Handling hostile threads](handling-hostile-threads.md)) |
| A problem at your meetup | Act by your announced ground rules; protect the person affected ([Proposing a meetup](proposing-a-meetup.md#ground-rules)) |
| Someone misrepresents the project | Correct the facts once, with a source |
