# Advocates: start here

This section of the help is for **Exponential advocates**: the street team of the project. Advocates are users,
developers, designers, editors, students and companies who like Exponential and help other people find it, try it,
use it well and join the community. Nobody appoints you; you become an advocate the day you help someone else with
Exponential and tell the truth while you do it.

These pages answer three questions: **what can I say** (with the source that proves it), **how do I say it** (talks,
demos, posts, answers), and **where do I take people** (the community channels that really exist). Every fact on
them comes from this documentation or from a public page that the documentation already cites. Where the sources do
not say something, the pages say so, and so should you.

> **Note**
> Advocates speak as themselves. You do not speak for 7x or for the Exponential Foundation unless one of them asked
> you to. Say who you are, what you use Exponential for, and where your facts come from.
> [Disclosure and conflicts of interest](disclosure-and-conflicts-of-interest.md) explains how.

Advocacy is not sales. Sales people have their own pages ([Sales and marketing: start here](../business/sales-and-marketing-start-here.md)),
and project managers theirs ([Project managers: start here](../leadership/project-managers-start-here.md)). An
advocate works peer to peer: one user helping another, in the open.

## The ladder: user, advocate, leader

```text
  USER                     ADVOCATE                         LEADER
  uses Exponential   -->   helps others find and use it -->  helps others become advocates
  asks questions           answers questions                 organizes, mentors, maintains
  reads the help           cites the help, improves it       owns an area of the project
```

Each step has one documented next action. [The advocacy ladder](the-advocacy-ladder.md) describes every rung, and
[Becoming a leader in the Exponential community](../leadership/becoming-a-community-leader.md) has the 30/60/90-day
plan of the top rung.

## Your first hour

1. **Know who stands behind the project** (10 minutes): [Who is 7x?](../who-is-7x.md#in-short) and
   [Who is the Exponential Foundation?](../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#in-short).
2. **Know the product family** (10 minutes): [Knowing the product family](knowing-the-product-family.md).
3. **Learn the core talking points** (15 minutes): [Talking points: the core set](talking-points-core-set.md), and the
   page you must read before you say anything in public: [Claims you must not make](claims-you-must-not-make.md).
4. **Visit the channels** (15 minutes): [The community channels](community-channels.md). Read a few forum threads,
   join the Telegram chat, look at the issue trackers.
5. **Do one helpful thing** (10 minutes): answer one question with a link to a help page, or fix one typo in a help
   page with **Edit on GitHub** ([Improving the help](improving-the-help.md)).

Then follow [Your first week as an advocate](your-first-week-as-an-advocate.md).

## The five rules every advocate keeps

1. **Cite your source.** Every claim gets a link. If you cannot find the source, do not make the claim
   ([Honesty and accuracy](honesty-and-accuracy.md)).
2. **Use the right names.** The product is **Exponential**; 7x is the company; the Exponential Foundation is the
   nonprofit; the old name belongs to the past ([Naming rules quick card](naming-rules-quick-card.md)).
3. **Keep security private.** Vulnerabilities go privately to the address in `SECURITY.md`, never to a forum, a chat
   or a public issue ([Security reports stay private](security-reports-stay-private.md)).
4. **Protect people.** No customer data, no passwords, no screenshots with personal data, no names without consent
   ([Protecting people's privacy](protecting-peoples-privacy.md)).
5. **Be kind and fair.** Respect other projects and the people who ask "beginner" questions
   ([Advocate conduct and ethics](advocate-conduct-and-ethics.md)).

## Finding these pages again

- In the help, search with the audience filter **Advocates**, or open **All pages** and the folder of the advocates.
- Every page has **Edit on GitHub**: if something here is wrong or missing, fix it. That is advocacy too.

**Demo mode.** Public demos run in demo mode (`EXP_DEMO_MODE=1`): a published administrator sign-in that nobody can change. Explain it accurately: it protects only administrator passwords, and it must never be on a real site. [Demo mode FAQ](../demo-mode-faq.md).
