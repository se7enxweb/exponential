# Sales and marketing: start here

This page is for people who sell Exponential, market it, or talk about it in public: account managers, presales
consultants, marketers, agency owners and anyone who writes or speaks about the product. It answers: what is in the
**Business** section of the help, what rules keep your story true, and what you should do in your first week.

You need no technical knowledge. You need an account on an Exponential installation you are allowed to use for
demos, with the policy to read the help (`exphelp/read`), and this help, open at `/exphelp/dashboard`.

## Exponential in one minute

Every sentence below comes from a source you can show a customer.

- **What it is.** "Exponential is a professional PHP application framework with advanced CMS (content management
  system) functionality. As a CMS its most notable feature is its fully customizable and extendable content model"
  (`README.md`). It is a CMS, a framework (CMF) and a built-in shop in one
  ([Who is 7x?](../who-is-7x.md#a-cms-a-cmf-and-a-shop-in-one)).
- **Where it comes from.** Exponential continues the code base of eZ Publish, a product its creator discontinued
  after almost 18 years. The code carries copyright years from 1999 (`COPYRIGHT.md`), and the repository's history
  reaches back to 2002 ([Who is 7x?](../who-is-7x.md#the-ez-publish-years-1999-to-2021)).
- **Who stands behind it.** **7x**, a North American company, leads its development, support and hosting since 2023;
  the **Exponential Foundation**, a nonprofit founded in March 2026, operates the open source project
  ([Who is 7x?](../who-is-7x.md), [Who is the Exponential Foundation?](../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md)).
- **How it is maintained.** Since 1 January 2024 the 6.0.x line has seen releases 6.0.0 to 6.0.14, with 6.0.15 in
  development ([Who is 7x?](../who-is-7x.md#the-60x-releases)).
- **What it costs to license.** Nothing: "Using Exponential under the terms of the GNU GPL is free of charge"
  (`README.md`, "License"). Services such as hosting, development and support are sold separately by 7x and others.

## The ground rules

These rules protect the customer, your company and the project. They are the same rules this help is written by.

1. **Every claim has a source.** If you say it, you can point to the help page, the README or a changelog that says
   it. If no source says it, you do not say it, or you say "the sources do not state that, we will find out".
2. **No invented numbers.** No prices, customer counts, market shares, response times or performance figures that
   are not written in a source. Where a source gives a number (for example 268 locale files in
   [Languages in Exponential](../../languages.md)), quote it with its date and source.
3. **No invented customers or quotes.** A customer story needs the customer's written permission
   ([Writing case studies and content marketing](case-studies-and-content-marketing.md)).
4. **Name things right.** The product is **Exponential** (version line **Exponential 6.0.x**), the company **7x**,
   the nonprofit the **Exponential Foundation**. "eZ Publish" is used only for the past
   ([Brand and naming rules](brand-and-naming-rules.md)).
5. **Compare on facts, never by putting others down.** Exponential's documented strengths are its open license, the
   freedom to host it anywhere, its full Git history, its choice of databases and operating systems, and no vendor
   lock-in. Compare on those ([Comparing Exponential factually](comparing-exponential-factually.md)).
6. **Demo on your own installation.** Never on a customer's live site and never with real personal data
   ([Demo scripts](demo-scripts.md)).
7. **Promises go to the project manager in writing.** Every promise made in a meeting is handed over the same day,
   with the help page it relies on ([Working together](../leadership/working-together.md#the-handoffs)).

## Your first week

Plan four to six hours a day for reading and trying things. Use an installation you control for every exercise.

### Day 1: who and what

| Do | Read | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| Learn the company, the nonprofit and the history | [Who is 7x?](../who-is-7x.md) ("In short", "The product: what Exponential is", "Services and hosting"), [Who is the Exponential Foundation?](../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md) ("In short", "The product family at a glance") | You can explain 7x and the Foundation in two sentences each |
| Learn the names | [Brand and naming rules](brand-and-naming-rules.md) | You can write the product family names correctly from memory |
| Learn the product family | [What Exponential is: the product family](../leadership/what-exponential-is.md) | You know which product fits which kind of customer ([Which product is for you](../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#which-product-is-for-you)) |

### Day 2: the story

| Do | Read | Done when |
|---|---|---|

| Learn the audiences | [Who Exponential is for](who-exponential-is-for.md) | You have filled in the qualification questions for one real prospect |

### Day 3: the screens

| Do | Read | Done when |
|---|---|---|

| Do | Read | Done when |

## A one-page cheat sheet

Copy it, keep it next to you on calls, and replace the placeholders.

```text
EXPONENTIAL CHEAT SHEET                         (sources in brackets)

Product      Exponential 6.0.x: open source CMS, CMF and built-in e-commerce   [README]
Lineage      Continues the eZ Publish code base; copyright from 1999;
             Git history from 2002                                       [COPYRIGHT.md, Who is 7x?]
Stewards     7x (company, leads since 2023);
             Exponential Foundation (nonprofit, founded 19 March 2026)    [Who is 7x?, Foundation page]
License      GPL v2 or later, free of charge; help under GNU FDL 1.3      [README, Free documentation]
Databases    SQLite 3 (default), PostgreSQL, MongoDB, MariaDB, Oracle, MySQL [Database support]
OS           Almost any that runs PHP 8.0 or later                         [Operating system support]
PHP          8.0 to 8.5                                                    [README]
Languages    268 locale files, 40 right to left; 32 admin translations     [Languages in Exponential]
Highlights   Multisite, layouts editor, Exponential Intelligence (drafts only),
             audit trail, Content Security Policy, Velocity server, help system [README, 6.0.15 changelog]
Security     Private reporting per SECURITY.md; first answer within one
             business day; published versions never change                 [SECURITY.md]

My demo installation:   <address you control>
My project manager:     <role contact in your team>
Next pipeline sync:     <date>
```

**Sending prospects to a demo.** The public demo runs in demo mode: prospects sign in with the published administrator password and cannot change it, so every prospect finds it working. Do not present demo mode as a security feature of real sites; it is only for demos. [Demo mode FAQ](../demo-mode-faq.md).
