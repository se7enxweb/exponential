# Supporter FAQ

**Do I need to be a developer to help?**
No. Bug reports, translations, documentation, forum answers and advocacy need no code.
[Ways to support Exponential](ways-to-support-exponential.md)

**What is the quickest useful thing I can do?**
Fix a mistake with "Edit on GitHub" on any help page, or answer one forum question.
[Supporters: start here](supporters-start-here.md)

**Will my pull request be rejected?**
"Every pull request is merged" (`doc/en/us/bc/6.0/ci.md`). While it is open, you squash it and rebase it onto `main`
as the reviews ask.
[Contributing code](contributing-code.md)

**Under which license is my contribution published?**
Code: GPL v2 or later (CONTRIBUTING.md). Documentation: GFDL 1.3 or later.
[Contributing documentation](contributing-documentation.md)

**Do I have to sign anything, and what do I give up?**
For code contributions to the core kernel, yes: a Contributor Licensing Agreement (CLA), once, before the first
merge. By signing and submitting, you assign the copyright in your contribution to 7x, which becomes its owner, and give up control over it: 7x may use, change, relicense
and distribute it in any way, without asking or paying you, and you cannot take it back. Your own extensions are not affected.
[Copyright assignment (CLA)](../../../../../CONTRIBUTING.md#copyright-assignment-cla)

**Where do I report a security problem?**
Privately to security@se7enx.com, never in public. [Testing and reporting bugs](testing-and-reporting-bugs.md)

**Should I give money to 7x or to the Exponential Foundation?**
Both work for the project. 7x is the company that does most of the development; the Foundation is the nonprofit that
operates the project. [Two organizations, two sponsor pages](../sponsors/two-organizations-two-sponsor-pages.md)

**Is my donation tax deductible?**
The Foundation states that donations to it are tax deductible in the United States; check with your tax adviser.
Sponsoring 7x, a company, is not described as a donation. [Billing, receipts and tax](../sponsors/billing-receipts-and-tax.md)

**How small can a contribution be?**
GitHub Sponsors tiers start at $2 a month (7x) and $5 a month (the Foundation), as listed on 18 October 2026; any
amount helps. [Funding the work](funding-the-work.md)

**Will I be credited?**
Your commits keep your name; sponsor tiers list their recognition. [Recognition for supporters](recognition-for-supporters.md)

**My company uses Exponential. What should we do?**
Sponsor, give staff time, and contribute generic work back. [Supporting Exponential as a company](supporting-as-a-company.md)

**What is the difference between a supporter, a sponsor and a partner?**
[What an Exponential Partner is](../partners/what-is-an-exponential-partner.md#partner-supporter-sponsor-contributor-customer)
