# Helping others in the community

This page is for users who know their way around Exponential and want to pass it on. Community help is how most
people get support: "You can post to the telegram channel, discord, slack, share forums, etc within the community to
get support regarding your questions and requests at no cost" (<https://exponential.earth/support>).

## The community places

| Place | What | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Forums <https://share.exponential.earth/forums> | Questions and answers that stay searchable | <https://exponential.earth/about/project> |

## Answering well

1. **Read the whole question.** Ask for the version and the error if they are missing.
2. **Answer the question asked**, then mention the better way if there is one.
3. **Show, don't just tell.** A command with its expected output, a setting with its file.
4. **Link to the source.** A help page, a doc file, an issue.
5. **Be kind.** Many people asking are new; many are not native English speakers.
6. **Close the loop.** When it is solved, say what solved it.

## From answer to documentation

If you answer the same question twice, it belongs in the help. Write it down as a page or improve an existing one
([Contributing documentation](contributing-documentation.md)). The next person finds it without asking.

## Welcoming newcomers

- Point them to [Getting started](../getting-started.md) and the audience pages of the help.
- Suggest a first contribution that fits them: a translation, a bug report, a doc fix.
- Thank people for their first pull request, even when it needs changes.

## What not to do

- Do not ask for or accept passwords or access to someone's site in public channels.
- Do not advertise paid services in answers; help is what builds trust.
