# Check accessibility after a redesign

The new design is as accessible as the old.

*Life cycle: Redesign, example 33 of 34. Worked out on the Fit & Healthy site of Exponential 6.0.15+.*

## Contents

1. [In the Layouts editor](#in-the-layouts-editor)
2. [With templates](#with-templates)
3. [Which to choose](#which-to-choose)
4. [Check](#check)

## In the Layouts editor

1. Check blocks with typed content: Image alt text, Hero image alt text, Title levels.

## With templates

1. Check the overridden templates: one `h1`, headings in order, focus styles, `alt` attributes.

## Which to choose

Both halves need the check.

## Check

- An accessibility checker reports no serious issue on the main page types.
