# Make sure no draft is left open

Launch with every layout in its published state.

*Life cycle: Launch, example 14 of 33. Worked out on the Fit & Healthy site of Exponential 6.0.15+.*

## Contents

1. [In the Layouts editor](#in-the-layouts-editor)
2. [On the command line](#on-the-command-line)
3. [Which to choose](#which-to-choose)
4. [Check](#check)

## In the Layouts editor

1. Open each listed layout and **Publish layout** or **Discard**.

## On the command line

1. `./console exp:layouts list --drafts` lists them with the status `draft`.

## Which to choose

Clean up before launch; later drafts are normal work in progress.

## Check

- The list shows no draft of the site's layouts.
