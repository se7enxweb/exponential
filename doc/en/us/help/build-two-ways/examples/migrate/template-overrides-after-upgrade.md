# Check your template overrides after an upgrade

Your overrides keep up with the theme.

*Life cycle: Migrate, example 20 of 34. Worked out on the Fit & Healthy site of Exponential 6.0.15+.*

## Contents

1. [In the Layouts editor](#in-the-layouts-editor)
2. [With templates](#with-templates)
3. [Which to choose](#which-to-choose)
4. [Check](#check)

## In the Layouts editor

1. Layouts are not affected.

## With templates

1. For each file in `extension/mysite/design/media/templates/`, diff it against the same path in `extension/sevenx_themes_media/design/media/templates/` of the new version and carry over the theme's changes.

## Which to choose

Keep overrides few; prefer view types and CSS.

## Check

- Each override has been compared.
