# Plan a video section

A video index and video pages.

*Life cycle: Plan, example 26 of 35. Worked out on the Fit & Healthy site of Exponential 6.0.15+.*

## Contents

1. [In the Layouts editor](#in-the-layouts-editor)
2. [With templates](#with-templates)
3. [Which to choose](#which-to-choose)
4. [Check](#check)

## In the Layouts editor

1. Video (node 164) has its own landing page layout (Landing Page / Video) by a Location mapping.
2. The External video block is for one-off videos inside other pages.

## With templates

1. Video pages use the full view of NG Video; the theme's stylesheet list includes `exp-youtube-consent.css` for embedded videos.

## Which to choose

Videos as content for anything that should be found and listed.

## Check

- A new video item appears in the video lists.
