# Change a button's text and target

"See the plans" becomes "Start today" and links to the sign-up page.

*Life cycle: Maintain, example 6 of 35. Worked out on the Fit & Healthy site of Exponential 6.0.15+.*

## Contents

1. [In the Layouts editor](#in-the-layouts-editor)
2. [With templates](#with-templates)
3. [Which to choose](#which-to-choose)
4. [Check](#check)

## In the Layouts editor

1. Open **Layouts > Layouts** (or **Shared layouts**) and *the layout*.
2. Select the block; change **Text** and the link (Button / Link) or **Link text** and **Link URL** (Hero).
3. **Publish layout**.

## With templates

1. Change the text in the template (and its translations) and the link; deploy.

## Which to choose

Campaign texts belong in blocks.

## Check

- The button leads to the new page.
