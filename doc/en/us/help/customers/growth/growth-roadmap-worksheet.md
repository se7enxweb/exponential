# Growth roadmap worksheet

This page is a **template** for the owner and the management team. Fill it in once a year, or before a budget round,
with your partner at the table. It helps you pick the few next steps that serve your goals, and see the routine work
each one adds for your team. Numbers you write in are your own estimates; the page gives none.

"Team routine added" matters most and is forgotten most: a newsletter is an issue every month, a language is a
translation of every new page, a members area is a monthly account clean-up. Each owner's page above describes its
routine.
