# How do I find and finish my drafts?

A draft is a version you saved but did not publish. **My drafts** lists all of yours, so nothing you started gets
lost.

## Who can do this

Every user who can edit content. You see only your own drafts.

## Steps

1. Open **Dashboard** in the top menu and choose **My drafts**, or use the **My drafts** block on the dashboard.
   The page **My drafts** opens.
2. To find a draft, type part of its name under **Find a draft**, or narrow the list by **Class**, **Language**,
   **Location** or **Not modified for**, then click **Search**.
3. Click **Edit** next to the draft.
   The edit form opens with your saved changes.
4. Finish the text and click **Send for publishing**.
   The draft becomes the published version and leaves the list.
5. To throw drafts away, tick them and click **Remove selected**. **Remove old drafts** removes every draft older than
   the age you choose.

![My drafts with the search form and the list of drafts](images/my-drafts.webp)

## What can go wrong

- **"Remove for good" removes more than you wanted.** A removed draft cannot be restored. A draft of an object that was
  never published removes that new object too. Check the list before you confirm.
- **Someone else changed the object since your draft.** When you publish, Exponential tells you that a newer version
  exists. Compare the versions before you publish, or you overwrite their change.
- **A draft is missing.** It was published, removed, or it belongs to another user. Drafts of other users are not shown.
