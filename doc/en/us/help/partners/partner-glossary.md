# Partner glossary

This page is for anyone who meets an unfamiliar term in the partner pages.

| Term | Meaning | Page |
|---|---|---|
| **7x** | The company that runs the partner program and leads Exponential; formerly Brookins Consulting | [Who is 7x?](../who-is-7x.md) |
| **Exponential** | The open source CMS and framework; the product partners build on | [Naming and brand rules](naming-and-brand-for-partners.md) |
| **Exponential Foundation** | The nonprofit that operates the project | [Working with the Foundation](working-with-the-foundation.md) |
| **Exponential Partner** | An organization in the official partner program | [What an Exponential Partner is](what-is-an-exponential-partner.md) |
| **Level** (tier) | Student, Now, Starter, Basic, Professional, Silver, Gold, Platinum | [Partner levels](partner-levels.md) |
| **Profile** (class, type) | IT, Creative, Hosting, Distribution (and Development) | [Partner profiles](partner-profiles.md) |
| **Points** | The program's measure of activity; about 1 point per minute of work or per dollar of revenue | [The points system](the-points-system.md) |
| **Revenue requirement** | Revenue a partner must generate per quarter or year for higher levels | [Partner requirements](partner-requirements.md) |
| **Guidance meeting** | A meeting with 7x included per level | [Partner meetings](partner-meetings-and-briefings.md) |
| **K.O.P.** | Kick-Off Package: program fee, book, online training, certification and 8 h support | [Partner plans and prices](partner-plans-and-prices.md) |
| **Certification** | Developer, Design, Solution (WCMS) or Sales; required from Silver | [Certifications](certifications.md) |
| **Exponential Certified** | The logo certified Silver, Gold and Platinum partners may use | [Logos and marks](partner-logos-and-marks.md) |
| **Lead priority** | The order in which partners receive leads (1 = highest) | [Referrals and leads](referrals-and-leads.md) |
| **Cold lead / hot lead** | A first contact / a "customer ready to purchase" | [Referrals and leads](referrals-and-leads.md) |
| **Pre-sales support** | Help from 7x with offers and tenders, from Professional | [Pre-sales support](pre-sales-support.md) |
| **White label** | 7x services delivered under the partner's brand, from Professional | [White-label 7x services](white-label-services.md) |
| **OEM** | Reselling your own product based on Exponential | [OEM and reselling](oem-and-reselling.md) |
| **Co-marketing** | Joint marketing with 7x; a budget for Gold and Platinum | [Co-marketing](co-marketing.md) |
| **Partner Center / Partner Dashboard** | The program's tools for logging activity and seeing points (not online on 18 October 2026) | [Logging your partner activity](logging-partner-activity.md) |
| **Powered by Exponential** | A credit on a site that runs Exponential | [Showing "Powered by Exponential"](powered-by-exponential.md) |
| **GPL** | GNU General Public License, v2 or later: the code's license | [Licensing for partners](licensing-for-partners.md) |
| **GFDL** | GNU Free Documentation License 1.3 or later: the documentation's license | [Free documentation](../free-documentation.md) |
| **CC BY-SA 4.0** | The license of the partner program text | [The partnership agreement](the-partnership-agreement.md) |
| **CLA** | Contributor Licensing Agreement with 7x, signed once, required before a code contribution to the core kernel is merged; by signing and submitting, you give up control over the contribution: 7x may use, change, sublicense, relicense and distribute it in any way, perpetually and irrevocably, without asking or paying you; formally the CLA is a license (§4); your own custom extensions are not affected | [Copyright and control of your contribution (CLA)](../../../../../CONTRIBUTING.md#copyright-and-control-of-your-contribution-cla) |
| **Supporter** | Anyone who helps the project | [Supporters: start here](../supporters/supporters-start-here.md) |
| **Sponsor** | Anyone who funds 7x or the Foundation | [Sponsors: start here](../sponsors/sponsors-start-here.md) |
| **Upstream** | The original project of code you use or changed | [Contributing extensions back upstream](contributing-extensions-back.md) |
| **To be defined by the Foundation** | A question the published sources do not answer yet | [Governance: who decides what](governance-and-decisions.md) |

**Demo mode** - The setting of a public demo, through the environment variable `EXP_DEMO_MODE=1`: its published administrator password cannot be changed or reset. [Demo mode FAQ](../demo-mode-faq.md).
