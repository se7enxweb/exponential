# Partner plans and prices as published

This page is for whoever budgets the partnership. It copies the published plan table and explains it. Prices are in
US dollars per month and were read on 18 October 2026 from <https://exponential.earth/partner/benefits> and
<https://exponential.earth/partner/program>. The store and the live pages are authoritative: "7x reserves full rights
to change or administer this program" (<https://exponential.earth/partner/program>).

## The plans

| Plan | Price per month | GitHub issue tickets | Guidance meetings | Revenue requirement per quarter |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Student | $25 | 2 a week | 1 a quarter | None |
| Now | $45 | 2 a week | 1 a quarter | None |
| Starter | $175 | 15 a month | 1 a quarter | None |
| Basic | $300 | 15 a month | 3 a quarter (1 a month) | None |
| Professional | $500 | 5 a week | 2 a month | $1,800 |
| Silver | $800 | 7 a week | 3 a month | $3,000 |
| Gold | $1,000 | 10 a week | 6 a quarter | $4,500 |
| Platinum | $2,600 | 15 a week | 12 a quarter | $7,500 |

Each plan has its own store page under <https://my.se7enx.com/store/exponential-partners>, for example
`.../exponential-student-partner-plan` and `.../exponential-platinum-partner-plan` (linked from the benefits page).

## Response times

| Plan | Benefits page | Program page |
|---|---|---|
| Student | ≤ 36 h | Community only |
| Now | ≤ 36 h | Community only |
| Starter | ≤ 36 h | ≤ 36 hours |
| Basic | ≤ 24 h | ≤ 24 hours |
| Professional | ≤ 12 h | ≤ 12 hours |
| Silver | ≤ 12 h | ≤ 12 hours |
| Gold | ≤ 4 h | ≤ 4 hours |
| Platinum | ≤ 3 h | ≤ 3 hours |

> **Warning**
> The two pages disagree for Student and Now. Whether hours mean business hours or clock hours, and in which time
> zone, is not stated. Ask for the response time in writing before you promise anything to your own customers
> that depends on it ([Support levels and response times](support-levels-and-response-times.md)).

## How to budget

1. **Pick the level by need, not prestige.** Count the tickets and meetings you will really use in a quarter.
2. **Add the revenue requirement.** From Professional on, you must generate the published revenue per quarter; see
   [Partner requirements](partner-requirements.md) for what counts.
3. **Add certification costs from Silver.** Prices for certifications are not published; see
   [Certifications](certifications.md).
4. **Plan a year.** "Partnership lasts one year" (requirements page); budget twelve months.
5. **Subtract the discounts you will use.** Discounts on 7x services, subscriptions and knowledge products by level
   are on [Partner benefits](partner-benefits.md).

```text
Partnership budget, year 1
  Level ......................  ________
  Monthly price x 12 .........  $________
  Kick-Off Package (if any) ..  $________
  Certifications (if any) ....  $________
  Staff time (hours x rate) ..  $________
  Expected discounts .........- $________
  Expected referral income ...- $________   (do not count on it in year 1)
  Net cost ...................  $________
```

## Invoices and taxes

Invoices for the partner subscription come from the store's seller, 7x. Tax treatment of a business subscription
is not described in the sources; ask your accountant. The partner subscription is a purchase of services, not a
donation; for donations see [Sponsors: start here](../sponsors/sponsors-start-here.md).
