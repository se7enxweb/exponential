# Partner code of conduct

This page is for everyone who works for a partner company. It states the conduct the program expects.

> **Note**
> There is no published, formal code of conduct for partners yet. CONTRIBUTING.md asks contributors to "review our
> Code of Conduct (CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)", but that file does not exist in the repository today. A formal code of
> conduct for partners and contributors is **to be defined by the Foundation**. Until then, the help's conduct page is
> [Advocate conduct and ethics](../advocates/advocate-conduct-and-ethics.md), which is where a link to
> `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md` should lead, and the rules below collect what the program, the contract and the project's own
> documents already require, with their sources.

## 1. Live the program's values

"Open", "Sharing" and "Innovative" are the three values by which 7x "relate[s] to our partners and our world-wide
community" (<https://exponential.earth/partner/program>, read 18 October 2026). For a partner that means: welcome
ideas, share knowledge, solve real problems. See [Open source values in daily partner work](open-source-values-for-partners.md).

## 2. Deliver quality

"Our main criteria is that your company is serious, competent and quality-conscious" (<https://exponential.earth/partner/apply>).

- Promise only what you can deliver.
- Keep customers on supported releases and patched ([Keeping customer sites current](keeping-customers-current.md)).

## 3. Be honest with customers

- Name your level and profile correctly; never claim a certification you do not hold
  ([Using partner logos](partner-logos-and-marks.md)).
- Say who does the work: if 7x delivers white-label services for you, do not misstate it in a contract.
- Use sourced facts about Exponential; no invented figures, customers or quotes
  ([Sales and marketing: start here](../business/sales-and-marketing-start-here.md#the-ground-rules)).

## 4. Respect the licenses

- Code improvements to GPL code are released under the GPL when you distribute them (CONTRIBUTING.md).
- Keep copyright and license notices; never remove them from code or documentation
  ([Licensing for partners](licensing-for-partners.md)).

## 5. Compete fairly

- Compare on facts, never by putting others down ([Comparing Exponential factually](../business/comparing-exponential-factually.md)).
- Leads shared with you are handled as agreed; no poaching of a lead another partner was given
  ([Handing leads over and back](lead-handoffs.md)).
- The contract asks for "No conflict of interest"; disclose one when it arises.

## 6. Behave well in the community

- Answer questions in the forums and chats without advertising.
- Disagree on technical merits, politely, in public channels where others can learn.
- Credit others' work: the project rebuilt Mugo Web's improvements "with full credit to Mugo Web" (README) and
  expects the same of everyone.

## 7. Protect security and data

- Report vulnerabilities privately to security@se7enx.com, never in public issues
  ([SECURITY.md](https://github.com/se7enxweb/exponential/blob/main/SECURITY.md)).
- Never put customer personal data in tickets, forum posts, screenshots or activity logs
  ([Protecting customer data](protecting-customer-data.md)).

## 8. Speak only for yourself

Partners are "Independent companies" under the contract. Do not speak for 7x or the Foundation, and do not present
your own plans as the project's roadmap.

## When something goes wrong

Tell the partner team (<https://exponential.earth/partner/contact-us>) early. Under the contract, "7x may terminate for violations"
(program page); open, early correction is always better.
