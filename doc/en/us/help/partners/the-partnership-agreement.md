# The partnership agreement in plain words

This page is for whoever signs the partnership on behalf of a company. It walks through the contract section by
section as published at the end of <https://exponential.earth/partner/program> (read 18 October 2026).

> **Warning**
> This is a summary, not legal advice and not the contract. Read the version you are asked to sign, and have it
> reviewed by your own counsel. The signed text is what binds you.

## How the contract is used

"This contract must be signed and emailed to 7x" (the address is on the program page) and is "Valid as long as requirements
are met. 7x may terminate for violations" (program page). The parties are 7x and your company.

## Section by section

| Section | What the published text says | In plain words |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Definitions | "Partner(s): includes employees and subcontractors"; "IP: all legal brand/design/trade rights under WIPO 1967" | Your staff and your subcontractors are all "the partner". "IP" means the brands, designs and trade rights. |
| 2. Subcontractors | "Partners are fully responsible for their subcontractors." | If you hire someone, their mistakes are yours. |
| 3. Warranties | "7x does not warranty the products/services delivered by partners." | Your customers' contract is with you; 7x does not guarantee your work. |
| 4. Rights & Information | "No conflict of interest"; "Independent companies"; "IP use requires written consent" | You stay independent; you need written consent to use 7x's or Exponential's brands beyond what the program grants. |
| 5. Completion | "30-day termination notice"; "All commissions still due"; "Material must be returned after breach" | Either side ends with 30 days' notice; earned commissions are still paid; after a breach, return program material. |
| 6. Decreased Partner Tier | "Benefits reflect current tier. Changes to logo/branding must occur within 30 days." | If your level drops, update your logos within 30 days. |
| 7. Other Terms | "Force Majeure: Must notify within 5 working days"; "Adjustments: Only by written agreement"; "Disputes: Settled in 'Seattle, Washington' USA" | Tell 7x within 5 working days if something outside your control stops you; changes only in writing; disputes are handled in Seattle. |

## What the contract does not change

- **The software license.** Exponential's code stays GPL v2 or later and its documentation GFDL 1.3 or later; the
  contract does not give or take any rights under those licenses ([Licensing for partners](licensing-for-partners.md)).
- **Your customer contracts.** You remain responsible for what you promise your customers.

## Questions to ask before signing

- [ ] Which version of the levels, prices and requirements is part of the contract? (The pages disagree in places;
      see [The points system](the-points-system.md).)
- [ ] Which response times apply to our level, and in which hours and time zone?
- [ ] How are commissions calculated, reported and paid (the official terms: <https://exponential.earth/partner/benefits>)?
- [ ] What exactly may we do with the logos at our level?
- [ ] Which law governs the contract, besides the place for disputes?
- [ ] How are personal data handled when leads are exchanged ([Protecting customer data](protecting-customer-data.md))?
- [ ] What is the Foundation's role, if any? (To be defined by the Foundation.)
