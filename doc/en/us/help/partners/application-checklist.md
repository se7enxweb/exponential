# Application checklist

This page is for the person who fills in the application. The form asks for "details about your company, team, and
project experience", and supporting information: "examples of past work, relevant certifications, and a brief
statement on why you want to partner with us" (<https://exponential.earth/partner/apply>, read 18 October 2026).
Prepare all of it before you start.

## Company

- [ ] Legal name, country, website
- [ ] Year founded and size (people working on web projects)
- [ ] Main services, in the words of the partner profiles ([Partner profiles](partner-profiles.md))
- [ ] Role e-mail address for the partnership (not a personal address that may leave with a person)
- [ ] Billing contact for the store subscription

## Team

- [ ] Number of people who have worked with Exponential (or eZ Publish in the past)
- [ ] Their roles: developer, designer, project manager, administrator, sales
- [ ] Certifications held, if any ([Certifications](certifications.md))
- [ ] Who will own the partnership internally

## Experience

- [ ] Projects with Exponential: what, when, which version, which features (multilingual, shop, layouts ...)
- [ ] Projects with other CMSs that show comparable skills
- [ ] Contributions: pull requests, extensions, translations, forum activity (links)
- [ ] Hosting experience, if you choose the Hosting profile

## Examples of past work

- [ ] Two or three public examples, with the customer's permission to name them
- [ ] For each: your role, the problem, the result (no confidential figures)

## Your choice

- [ ] Profile(s): IT, Creative, Hosting, Distribution (Development)
- [ ] Target level and why it fits ([Partner levels](partner-levels.md))
- [ ] Budget approved for a year ([Partner plans and prices](partner-plans-and-prices.md))
- [ ] The agreement read by whoever signs ([The partnership agreement](the-partnership-agreement.md))

## Statement of motivation

```text
Why we want to become an Exponential Partner

We are <company>, a <profile> company in <country> with <n> people.
We have worked with Exponential since <year> on <kinds of projects>.
We want to join the partner program to <two or three concrete goals>.
In the first year we will <plan: projects, contributions, certifications>.
We support the open source values of the program by <what you give back>.
Our partnership contact is <role> at <role e-mail>.
```

Keep it short, concrete and true. Do not claim certifications, customers or figures you cannot show.

## After you send it

- Note the date; the decision is due "within 10 working days" (apply page).
- Prepare the onboarding while you wait ([Onboarding checklist](onboarding-checklist.md)).
