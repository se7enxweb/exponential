# Referrals and leads

This page is for sales staff and owners in partner companies. It explains what the program says about leads in both
directions: leads 7x passes to partners, and leads partners pass to 7x.

## Leads from 7x to partners

| Benefit | Levels | Source (read 18 October 2026) |
|---|---|---|
| Customer project referrals | Professional and higher | <https://exponential.earth/partner/benefits> |
| Lead priority (1 = highest) | Starter 6, Basic 5, Professional 4, Silver 3, Gold 2, Platinum 1 | benefits page |
| Silver: "7x and partners SHARE: leads, projects, joint customer meetings" | Silver | <https://exponential.earth/partner/program> |
| Visibility to customers "looking for an Exponential service provider" | All, by level | program page |

How leads are distributed (by region, profile, priority, rotation) is not published; ask the partner team.

## Leads from partners to 7x

The program pays a lead referral commission for cold leads and for hot leads ("customer ready to purchase 7x").
The program's pages publish different rates for a cold lead; both are listed in
[Values still to be confirmed](partner-plans-and-prices.md#values-still-to-be-confirmed), and the rate is **to be
confirmed** until 7x and the Foundation publish one. Get the rate, the definition of "cold" and "hot", and the payment
terms in writing before you refer a lead. The contract states that on termination "All commissions still due" are paid
(program page).

## Qualifying a lead

Before you pass a lead on or accept one, answer these questions:

```text
Lead: ______________________  Source: ______________________  Date: ________
Need (in the customer's words): ______________________
Budget known?  yes / no      Decision maker known?  yes / no
Timeline: ______________________
Exponential fit: content model / languages / multisite / shop / integration / hosting
Current platform: ______________________
Cold or hot (as defined in your agreement): ______
Consent to share contact details with the other party:  yes / no
```

## Recording leads

- Record the date, source and consent of every lead; disputes about commissions are settled by records.
- Share personal data of a lead only with their consent and only what is needed
  ([Protecting customer data](protecting-customer-data.md)).
