## Who is who

| | 7x | Exponential Foundation |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | a company: "a responsive North American web design, support, development and website hosting company" (<https://se7enx.com/about>) | a nonprofit corporation founded on 19 March 2026 (<https://foundation.exponential.earth/>) |
| Role | "7x took over leadership of the project and it's development, support, adoption and community growth in 2023" (`README.md`) | "This project is operated by the Exponential Foundation" (<https://exponential.earth/about/project>) |
| History | formerly Brookins Consulting; working with this code base since the eZ Publish years ([Who is 7x?](../../who-is-7x.md#in-short)) | names 7x "the current leader in contributions" to the project |
| Copyright | "7x & Exponential Foundation" together (`COPYRIGHT.md`) | |

"This project is not associated with the original eZ Publish software or its original developer, eZ Systems or
Ibexa" (`README.md`, "Exponential Project Notice").

## Getting help

From `README.md`, "Where to get more help":

| Channel | Address | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| Documentation | <https://exponential.doc.exponential.earth/Exponential>, and this help inside the administration | how-to questions |
| Community forums | <https://share.exponential.earth/forums> | questions others may share |
| Telegram community chat | <https://t.me/exponentialcms> | quick questions |
| Project website | <https://exponential.earth> | news, the partner program |
| Extension support website | <https://support.exponential.earth> | extensions |

Community help comes without a promise: "its developers are not obligated to provide free support"
(<https://exponential.earth/support>, quoted in [Support levels and response times](../../partners/support-levels-and-response-times.md)).
If you need a guaranteed response, buy it under a contract (below).

> **Tip**
> Before asking, collect the version (`composer show se7enxweb/exponential`), the PHP version, the engine and the
> database, and any error reference shown on the page ([When something goes wrong](../../when-something-goes-wrong.md)).

## Reporting bugs and requesting features

From `README.md`, "Issue tracker":

- Bugs, improvements and stories for Exponential 6: the Exponential Project Issue Tracker,
  <https://issues.exponential.earth>.
- Anything about the project, community or ecosystem: <https://github.com/se7enxweb/exponential-community/issues>.
- Contributions of features and fixes: see `CONTRIBUTING.md`.

Partners can also sponsor features and take part in roadmap planning
([Feature requests and sponsored features](../../partners/feature-requests-and-sponsored-features.md)).

## Reporting security problems

Never in a public tracker. Mail **security@se7enx.com**; "You can expect a first answer within one business day"
(`SECURITY.md`). Details: [Security updates and disclosure](../security/security-updates-and-disclosure.md#reporting-a-vulnerability).

## Buying services

7x sells services around the free software: web design and development, e-commerce and payment integration,
training, migration "from eZ Publish to Exponential v6", support and maintenance contracts, and managed hosting.
"Prices and terms are on the 7x website, not here" ([Who is 7x?](../../who-is-7x.md#services-and-hosting)).

- Paid support for anyone: "contacting 7x directly for paid support" (<https://exponential.earth/support>).
- A managed service for businesses, the 7x Partner Subscription, is described at <https://se7enx.com/partner>
  ([The partner program at a glance](../../partners/partner-program-at-a-glance.md#two-offers-with-partner-in-the-name)).
- Contact: <https://se7enx.com/contact>.

Other companies may offer the same services; see [Choosing a partner](choosing-a-partner.md).

## Giving back

A business that relies on Exponential has an interest in the project staying healthy. Ways to help, all voluntary:

| Way | How | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Report bugs well | with version, steps and expected result | `README.md`, "Issue tracker" |
| Contribute fixes | pull requests under GPL v2 or later | `CONTRIBUTING.md` |
| Improve documentation | the help is free documentation under the GNU FDL | [Free documentation](../../free-documentation.md) |
| Sponsor or donate | <https://sponsor.se7enx.com>, <https://github.com/sponsors/se7enxweb>, and the Foundation at <https://github.com/sponsors/exponentialcms> | `README.md`, "Donate"; [Who is the Exponential Foundation?](../../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#the-foundation-and-7x) |
| Share your story | a case study, with your approval | [Case study template](../templates/case-study-template.md) |

The two sponsorship streams differ: sponsoring 7x funds "the company that keeps the lights on", sponsoring the
Foundation funds "the open-source project itself"
([Who is the Exponential Foundation?](../../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#the-foundation-and-7x)).

## What the sources do not say

Some questions have no public answer in the sources used here, for example the Foundation's board members and how
its decisions are made ([Who is the Exponential Foundation?](../../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#what-the-sources-do-not-say)),
or response times for paid support outside a contract. Ask 7x or the Foundation directly, and get answers that
matter to you in writing.
