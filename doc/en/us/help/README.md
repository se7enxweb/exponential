# Help for editors and users

These pages explain the everyday work in Exponential: signing in, finding your way around the administration,
writing and publishing content, uploading media, translating, and what to do when something goes wrong. They are
written for editors and for anyone with an account on a site, and need no technical knowledge.

Every page describes what you see in the administration with the **Admin 4** design, the design most installations
use. Other administration designs (Classic, AdminUI) have the same pages and buttons, arranged differently. What you
see also depends on your role: an editor sees fewer tabs and buttons than an administrator, and a button you are not
allowed to use is shown grayed out or not at all.
