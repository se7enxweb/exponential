# What sponsorship funds

This page is for sponsors who want to know where their money goes. It quotes the organizations; it does not
estimate budgets the sources do not publish.

## The Foundation

| Statement | Source |
|---|---|
| "focused on the development of Features, Documentation, Tutorials, How-To's, FAQs, Source Code, Plugins/Extensions/Bundles" | Its GitHub Sponsors listing |
| "The Exponential Foundation funds the open-source project: the core libraries, the documentation, the community tooling, and the long-term survival of the platform as free software." | se7enxweb profile README, cited in [Who is the Exponential Foundation?](../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#what-the-foundation-funds) |
| Core library maintenance; API and core documentation; GraphQL, REST and Solr integration layers; community infrastructure (Slack, mirrors, wikis); Zeta Components security hardening | same, "What the Foundation funds" |

## 7x

| Statement | Source |
|---|---|

## Work you can see

The results of funded work are public: the release history in `doc/en/us/release-overview`, the changelogs in
`doc/changelogs/`, the story of every change since December 2023 in [the history](../../history/README.md), and the
commit history on GitHub. The 6.0.15 line alone added, among much else, Velocity, the admin4 design, Exponential
Layouts, Exponential Intelligence and this help system ([6.0.15 changelog](../../../../changelogs/6.0/6.0.15.md)).

## What is not published

Neither organization publishes a budget or a funding report in the sources used here ("what its budget is" is listed
as not stated in [Who is the Exponential Foundation?](../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#what-the-sources-do-not-say)).
A public funding report is to be defined by the Foundation.
