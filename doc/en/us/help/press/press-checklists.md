## Before writing

```text
[ ] I know which product the story is about          (The product family for reporters)
[ ] I know the current release and its date          (The 6.0.x releases at a glance)
[ ] I have the fact sheet open                        (Fact sheet for journalists)
[ ] I know what the sources do not say                (What the sources do not say)
[ ] I have declared any interest I have               (Disclosure and sponsorship ethics)
```

## Before an interview

```text
[ ] Request sent with topic, outlet, deadline          (Requesting an interview)
[ ] Public answers read, questions focused on gaps     (Questions to ask 7x / the Foundation)
[ ] Agreed how quotes are attributed                   (Quotes policy)
[ ] Recording or verbatim notes ready                  (Verifying quotes)
[ ] Embargo terms in writing, if any                   (Embargoes and timing)
```

Pages: [Requesting an interview](requesting-an-interview.md), [Questions to ask 7x](questions-to-ask-7x.md),

## Before publishing a news story

```text
[ ] Product "Exponential"; old name only in the past tense
[ ] 7x and the Exponential Foundation not confused
[ ] "Released" only for a tagged version; 6.0.15 "in development" until tagged
[ ] Every number with its source and date; no usage numbers
[ ] Every quote real and attributed; documentation not presented as a spokesperson
[ ] No link to eZ Systems or Ibexa implied
[ ] Headline checked against the story
```

Pages: [Common errors in coverage](common-errors-in-coverage.md), [Headlines: do and don't](headlines-dos-and-donts.md),
[Fact-checking checklist](fact-checking-checklist.md).

## Before publishing a feature or review

```text
[ ] Every claim marked and checked at its primary source       (Fact-checking guide)
[ ] Comparisons with the same criteria on both sides            (Comparisons done fairly)
[ ] Performance figures with their conditions                   (Verifying numbers)
[ ] Other companies credited where their work is used           (Crediting Netgen, Mugo Web and CJW Network)
[ ] Images with credit lines and license                        (Screenshots: what you may use)
[ ] Alt text and accessible structure                           (Making your coverage accessible)
```

Pages: [Fact-checking guide](fact-checking-guide.md), [Comparisons done fairly](comparisons-done-fairly.md),
[Verifying numbers](verifying-numbers.md), [Crediting Netgen, Mugo Web and CJW Network](crediting-other-projects.md),
[Screenshots: what you may use](screenshots-what-you-may-use.md), [Making your coverage accessible](making-your-coverage-accessible.md).

## Before publishing a video or stream

```text
[ ] Disclosure in the first seconds and the description
[ ] Recorded on a demo installation with fictional data
[ ] No passwords, keys, tokens, host names or personal data on screen
[ ] Version named and shown
[ ] Captions checked
[ ] Title and thumbnail make a claim the video supports
```

Pages: [Creator checklist](creator-checklist.md), [Keeping secrets off screen](keeping-secrets-off-screen.md),
[Privacy on camera](privacy-on-camera.md), [Captions and accessible video](captions-and-accessible-video.md),
[Titles, thumbnails and descriptions](titles-thumbnails-and-descriptions.md).

## After publishing

```text
[ ] Fact log kept with sources and dates                 (Fact-checking guide)
[ ] Corrections made openly if needed                    (Corrections and updates)
[ ] Errors in the documentation reported to the project  (community issue tracker)
```
