# Titles, thumbnails and descriptions

This page is for creators who publish videos and posts about Exponential. The title, the thumbnail and the first
lines of the description are what most people see; many never watch further. They must be as accurate as the content
itself.

## Titles

A good title names the task or the topic, the product and, for tutorials, the version.

| Kind | Pattern | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Tutorial | <Task> in Exponential <version> | "Build a landing page with the layouts editor in Exponential 6.0.15 (development line)" |
| First look | A first look at <feature> | "A first look at Exponential Intelligence, the assistant that writes drafts only" |
| Install | Install Exponential <version> with one command | "Install Exponential from scratch on SQLite with one command" |
| Review | <Product> review: <angle> | "Exponential review: running a site after the eZ Publish years" |
| Stream | <Topic> live: <what we build> | "Exponential Velocity live: from install to HTTPS" |

Use the names as written in [Brand and naming rules](../business/brand-and-naming-rules.md#the-names-in-one-table):
Exponential, 7x, the Exponential Foundation, Exponential Velocity, Exponential Intelligence, Exponential Layouts.
The old name eZ Publish appears only as history.

## Titles to avoid

| Avoid | Why |
|---|---|
| "The CMS that kills WordPress" | Attacks another project; no source compares them |
| "100x faster than <product>" | No published comparison supports a multiple |
| "eZ Publish 7 is here" | The product is Exponential; there is no such release |
| "Exponential 6.0.15 released!" | 6.0.15 is in development, with no release date |
| "The official Exponential tutorial" | Your content is yours unless 7x or the Foundation asked for it |
| "AI writes your whole site" | Exponential Intelligence writes drafts only |
| "Endorsed by Ibexa" | Exponential is not associated with eZ Systems or Ibexa |

## Thumbnails

- **Show your own screen**: a screenshot of your demo installation with invented content
  ([Keeping secrets off screen](keeping-secrets-off-screen.md), [Privacy on camera](privacy-on-camera.md)).
- **Logos**: the press kit's usage guidelines say, word for word: "Logos and brand assets may be used for editorial,
  review, and partnership coverage." and "Do not alter logo colors, proportions, or add effects." and "Do not use
  assets to imply endorsement without written permission." Version 1.0.0 of the kit contains no logo files yet; its
  folders list planned files ([exponential.earth press kit](https://exponential.earth/about/press-kit)).
- **Colors**: the kit's logo notes list Dark Background `#2b2d42`, Accent/Red `#e63946`, Foreground `#0f0f0f` and Light
  Surface `#f8f8f8`. Using them is your choice; they do not make a thumbnail official.
- **No fake interface**: do not draw buttons, numbers or charts the product does not show.
- **Readable**: large text, high contrast, a few words; repeat the essential words in the title.
- **Alternative text** where the platform allows it.

## Descriptions

Put what matters in the first lines, before "show more":

```text
<One sentence: what the video shows and the promise.>
Shows Exponential <version | 6.0.15 development line as of <date>> on my own test installation, with invented content.
<Disclosure line: paid / gifted / commission / nobody paid for this video.>

SOURCES
- <Help page title>: <link>
- <Help page title>: <link>

CHAPTERS
0:00 Intro and disclosure
0:20 <step>
...

ABOUT
Exponential is free software under the GNU GPL. Project site: https://exponential.earth/
Questions: https://share.exponential.earth/forums - Telegram: https://t.me/exponentialcms
Security problems: report privately as described in SECURITY.md, not in the comments.
License of this video: <your choice>. Help pages quoted are under the GNU FDL 1.3.
```

- Link the help pages behind your claims ([Citing sources and licensing your writing](../advocates/citing-sources-and-licensing-your-writing.md)).
- Link the community channels the documentation names ([The community channels](../advocates/community-channels.md)).
- Never put a password, key, private server address or personal data in a description.

## Tags, chapters and pinned comments

- **Tags**: Exponential, the feature, PHP, CMS, open source. Do not tag another product's name to borrow its audience.
- **Hashtags**: the sources name no official hashtag. `#Exponential` alone is ambiguous; add context in the text
  ([Social media guidelines](../advocates/social-media-guidelines.md#names-and-links)).
- **Chapters**: one per step of your script; they help everyone, including screen reader users.
- **Pinned comment**: use it for corrections ([Correcting a video or post](correcting-a-video-or-post.md)).
