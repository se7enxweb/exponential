# Write the evaluation scoring sheet

**Who:** the project manager with the evaluation team. **When:** before the tender is published; many procurement rules
require the criteria and weights to be published with it. Templates: [Scoring sheet template](../../procurement/evaluation/scoring-sheet-template.md)
and [Weighting criteria](../../procurement/evaluation/weighting-criteria.md).

> **Note** This page describes common practice; it is not legal advice. Follow your organization's procurement rules.

## Before you start

- Requirements final, split into mandatory (pass or fail) and scored ([Requirements](../../procurement/requirements/README.md)).
- Evaluation team named ([Build an evaluation team](build-an-evaluation-team.md)).

## Steps

1. **List mandatory requirements** as pass/fail gates: a bid that fails one is out.
2. **Group scored criteria**, for example: solution fit, accessibility, security, privacy, operations and support,
   training, exit and lock-in, project plan, price over the term.
3. **Set weights** that reflect your priorities; they add up to 100.
4. **Define a score scale with words**, so scorers agree on what a number means:

   | Score | Meaning |
   |---|---|
   | 0 | not addressed |
   | 1 | addressed, no evidence |
   | 2 | partly met, some evidence |
   | 3 | met, with evidence |
   | 4 | met, with strong evidence and a credible plan |

5. **Name the evidence** for each criterion: a demo step, a document, a proof-of-concept task, a reference answer.
   For product facts, the panel can check the bidder's claims against the standards pages
   ([Check a vendor's accessibility claims](check-a-vendors-accessibility-claims.md)).
6. **Price over the term**: score the total cost of ownership model, not the first year
   ([Total cost of ownership](../../procurement/total-cost-of-ownership/README.md)).
7. **Plan moderation**: scorers score alone, then agree in a meeting; record the reasons for each final score.

## Example

```text
Criterion              Weight  Evidence                                    Lead scorer
Solution fit             25    demo script steps 1-8, PoC tasks 1-3         editorial lead
Accessibility            15    test report method, demo with keyboard       accessibility lead
Security                 10    hardening plan, patching commitment          security officer
Privacy                  10    processing plan, third-party list            DPO
Operations and support   10    SLA answers, staffing, upgrade hours         IT specialist
Training                  5    course outline, material rights              training coordinator
Exit and lock-in          5    exit plan, export test offer                 project manager
Price over <n> years     20    TCO model filled in                          project manager
Total                   100
```

The weights are an example; choose your own.

## Result

A sheet published with the tender, which every scorer applies the same way.

## Pitfalls

- Price weighted so high that quality cannot change the result.
- Criteria without evidence: they get scored on the quality of the prose.
- Changing weights after bids are opened.
