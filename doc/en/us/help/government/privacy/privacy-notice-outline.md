# A privacy notice outline for an Exponential site

This page is for data protection officers and the editors who publish the notice. Exponential ships no privacy notice
text. Your organization writes it; the outline below helps you cover what the software does, using facts from the
privacy section of this help. If your authority publishes a model, follow it.

> **Note** This page describes features and common practice; it is not legal advice. Check the law that applies to
> your organization with your legal counsel.

## The outline

| Section | What to say for an Exponential site |
|---|---|
| Who we are | the controller, contact, DPO contact |
| What this notice covers | the site's address(es) and services |
| Visiting the site | technical logs: masked addresses in request logs for security, kept 30 or 90 days; web server logs as your host keeps them |
| Cookies | the session cookie and `is_logged_in` (functional); any others you added (see "Cookies set by Exponential") |
| Forms | which forms, what is collected, who reads it, how long you keep it |
| Accounts | for staff or members: what is stored, sign-in records, two-step sign-in if used |
| E-mail | categories, opt-in and double opt-in, the consent record (time, wording, IP address), unsubscribe in every mail, consent log retention |
| Security and audit | an audit trail of administrative actions, minimized (truncated addresses, hashed sessions), retention |
| Search | whether search terms are recorded, and that they are reset |
| Third parties | each active service (analytics, CAPTCHA, maps, social login, AI provider): what it receives, why |
| Recipients and processors | hosting company, implementation and support partners |
| Retention | per category, from your schedule |
| Your rights | access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, portability, complaint to the authority; how to make a request |
| Changes | date of the notice and how changes are announced |

## Publishing it

1. Create it as a content page in every language siteaccess, with a stable URL alias (for example `/privacy`).
2. Link it from the footer of every page and from every form and mail footer.
3. Write it in plain language, with headings; avoid a PDF-only notice.
4. Keep earlier versions: the object's versions page shows them, within the version history limit.

## Keeping it true

Review it when you activate an extension, add a form or a mail category, change hosting, or change retention settings.
A notice that names services you do not use, or omits ones you do, is a finding in any audit.
