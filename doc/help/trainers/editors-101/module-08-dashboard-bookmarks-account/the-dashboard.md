# Lesson 8.1: The dashboard as a daily start

The dashboard is the first page after signing in. Learners saw it in module 1; now that they have drafts, published
items and maybe pending items, it means something. This lesson turns the dashboard into a daily routine: what is
waiting for me, what did I do, what changed on the site.

## Learning objectives

After this lesson, learners can:

- use the dashboard's quick actions to reach **Content structure**, the **Media library** and **Upload files**;
- read the numbers and open the list behind each;
- read the chart **Publishing, last 14 days**, also as a table;
- use **My drafts**, **My pending items**, **All latest content** and **My latest content** to plan their work.

## Trainer's notes

### How to present it

Source: [The dashboard](../../../admin-tour.md#the-dashboard).

1. **From the top** (6 minutes):
   - the greeting with your name, and quick actions such as **Content structure**, **Media library** and **Upload
     files**;
   - the **numbers**: content items, items published in the last seven days, **My drafts**, **My pending items**, and
     more for administrators; a click on a number opens its list;
   - **Publishing, last 14 days**: a small chart; **Show the numbers** shows it as a table;
   - the lists **My pending items**, **My drafts**, **All latest content** (with **Name**, **Type**, **Published**
     and **Author**) and **My latest content**.
   Some installations add more, such as **Exponential Intelligence: my drafts** where the writing assistant is on.
   Administrators see blocks about the system, updates and security events.
2. **A routine** (4 minutes). Suggest a two-minute start of the day: open **My pending items** (anything waiting for
   me or for my approval?), then **My drafts** (anything unfinished?), then **All latest content** (what did my
   colleagues publish?).

### Common questions

**Can I arrange the dashboard myself?** The blocks are set by the installation and your role. The left column's
links to your own pages are always there.

**Why does my colleague's dashboard have more blocks?** Another role, or extensions that add blocks for some roles.

**Why does All latest content show items I cannot edit?** It shows the latest published content you may read,
whether or not you may edit it.

### Pitfalls

- **Numbers in a fresh training site.** With little content, the chart is nearly empty. It fills as learners publish.
- **Confusing All latest content and My latest content.** Point out the word "My".

## Hands-on exercise

Use the training installation and your Editor training account.

1. Click the **Exponential** logo to open the **Dashboard**.
2. Read the numbers block. Write down your number of drafts and pending items.
3. Click the number of items published in the last seven days and see the list it opens. Go back to the dashboard.
4. Find the chart **Publishing, last 14 days** and press **Show the numbers**. Write down today's number.
5. In **My latest content**, find your articles and images from this course.
6. In **All latest content**, find an article by another learner and write down its author.
7. Press the quick action **Upload files** and check where it opens (do not upload). Go back.
8. Write your own three-step routine for the start of the day on a card, using the dashboard's blocks.

## Expected result

- The learner's notes list their drafts and pending items (usually zero after module 6), today's publishing number
  (several, after this course), and another learner's article with its author.
- **Upload files** opened the upload page with a target location in the media library.
- Each learner has a routine card, which the trainer reads aloud for two or three learners.

## Quiz

1. Name the four lists of content at the bottom of the dashboard.
2. What does **Show the numbers** do?
3. True or false: every role sees the same blocks on the dashboard.
4. Where do you start if you want to know whether something waits for your approval?
   A. **My drafts** B. **My pending items** C. **All latest content** D. The chart
5. What does a click on a number in the numbers block do?

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. **My pending items**, **My drafts**, **All latest content** and **My latest content**
   ([A tour of the administration](../../../admin-tour.md#the-dashboard)).
2. Shows the chart **Publishing, last 14 days** as a table.
3. **False.** Administrators and some extensions add blocks.
4. **B.**
5. It opens the list behind the number.

</details>
