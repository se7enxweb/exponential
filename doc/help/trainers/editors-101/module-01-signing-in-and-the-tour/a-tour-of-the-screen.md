# Lesson 1.3: A tour of the screen

Editors who know where things are on the screen stop getting lost. This lesson walks once around the screen of the
administration in the **Admin 4** design: the header, the tabs, the two columns and the dashboard. Learners finish
by changing two settings that are theirs alone: the interface language and the color mode.

## Learning objectives

After this lesson, learners can:

- name the parts of the header and say what each button does;
- find the tree in the left column and the tools of the right column;
- read the dashboard and open **My drafts** and **My pending items** from it;
- change their **Interface language** and the **Color mode** and say who else is affected (nobody);
- hide, show and resize the side columns.

## Trainer's notes

### How to present it

Show everything on the projector, signed in with an Editor training account rather than the administrator, so
that the screen matches what learners see. Source for the whole lesson:
[A tour of the administration](../../../admin-tour.md).

1. **The header, left to right** (6 minutes):
   - the **Exponential** logo: back to the dashboard;
   - the **tabs**: **Dashboard**, **Content structure**, **Media**, and more depending on the role and the
     extensions; tabs that do not fit move under **More**; a small arrow next to a tab opens its menu
     (**Dashboard menu** ...);
   - **Search...**, with the funnel button **Search scope** (module 7); pressing `/` jumps to the field;
   - the globe button **Open the site or another siteaccess**;
   - the sun button **Color mode**: light, dark or the mode of the computer, one press at a time;
   - the **Help menu** button, which leads to the **Help center**;
   - the person button **User menu**: your name, **Change password** and your e-mail preferences;
   - the arrow leaving a box: **Logout**.
2. **The left column** (4 minutes). On the dashboard it lists your own pages: **My pending items**, **My drafts**,
   **My bookmarks**, **My e-mail preferences**, **My notification settings**, **Help center**, **Change password**,
   **Edit profile**. In **Content structure** and **Media** it holds the tree (module 2) and, below it, the
   **Content finder**.
3. **The right column** (4 minutes): **Bookmarks** with **Add to bookmarks**, **Interface** with the **Interface
   language** list and **Apply**, **Current user** and, for some roles, **User preferences** and **Quick
   settings**. Administrators also see **Clear cache**.
4. **Hiding and resizing** (2 minutes). The tabs on the window edges (**Left column**, **Right column**) hide and
   show the columns; the handle with three dots resizes the left column, and the buttons **Small**, **Medium** and
   **Large** set its width. The choice is remembered.
5. **The dashboard** (4 minutes): the greeting with quick actions such as **Content structure** and **Media
   library**, the numbers, the chart **Publishing, last 14 days**, and the lists **My pending items**, **My drafts**,
   **All latest content** and **My latest content**. Administrators see more blocks (the system, updates, security
   events).

### Common questions

**Does changing the interface language translate the content?** No. It changes the menus and buttons, for you
only, until you sign out. Translating content is a topic of Editors 201.

**Why does my colleague see a Layouts tab and I do not?** Their role allows it. The Editor interface also shows
only the content tabs.

**Can I choose another administration design?** Only when your role has the permission; the **Interface** box then
offers it. These lessons describe **Admin 4**.

**Where is the help?** Under the **Help menu** button in the header: the **Help center** lists every page by
audience and topic and has its own search.

### Pitfalls

- **Different screens in the room.** A learner with a narrow window sees the tabs and the search behind a button
  with three lines. Ask everyone to maximize the browser window, or show both layouts.
- **Lost columns.** A learner who hid the left column by accident cannot find the tree. Point at the **Left column**
  tab on the window edge.
- **Language mix-up.** After the exercise, ask learners to switch back to the class language, or the next lesson's
  button names will not match.

## Hands-on exercise

Use the training installation and your Editor training account.

1. Sign in. On the **Dashboard**, read the greeting and find the numbers block. Write down how many drafts you have.
2. Hover over each button of the header from left to right and write down the name it shows.
3. Press the sun button (**Color mode**) until the screen is dark. Press it again until it is back to the mode you
   like.
4. In the right column, find the **Interface** box. In **Interface language**, choose another language you can
   read (for example **Deutsch (Deutschland)** or **Español (España)** if the list offers them) and press
   **Apply**.
5. Look at the tab names: they are now in that language. Switch back to the class language and press **Apply**.
6. Press the **Left column** tab on the left edge of the window to hide the left column, and press it again to show
   it.
7. Press **Large** at the bottom of the left column, then **Medium**.
8. Click the **Content structure** tab. Notice that the left column now shows the tree.
9. Open the **Help menu** in the header and go to the **Help center**. Type `tour` in its search field and open the
   page **A tour of the administration**.
10. Go back to the **Dashboard** with the **Exponential** logo.

## Expected result

- The learner's list of header buttons matches the table in
  [A tour of the administration](../../../admin-tour.md#the-header) (the exact set depends on the role).
- The tab names changed language in step 5 and changed back.
- The left column was hidden, shown again and resized.
- The Help center found the tour page.
- The trainer checks by asking two learners to point at the **Interface** box and the **Content finder** on the
  projector.

## Quiz

1. Which key jumps to the **Search...** field from anywhere in the administration?
   A. `F1` B. `/` C. `Ctrl+S` D. `Esc`
2. Who else sees the interface language you choose?
3. Name two lists you can open from the left column of the dashboard.
4. Where does the content tree appear?
   A. In the right column on every page B. In the left column in **Content structure** and **Media** C. Only on
   the dashboard D. In the header
5. A learner's tree disappeared. What is the most likely cause, and the fix?

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. **B.** `/` ([Keyboard shortcuts](../../../keyboard-shortcuts.md#everywhere-in-the-administration)).
2. Nobody: the interface language is for you only, until you sign out.
3. Any two of **My pending items**, **My drafts**, **My bookmarks**, **My e-mail preferences**, **My notification
   settings**.
4. **B.** ([A tour of the administration](../../../admin-tour.md#the-left-column)).
5. The left column was hidden; press the **Left column** tab on the window edge to show it again.

</details>
