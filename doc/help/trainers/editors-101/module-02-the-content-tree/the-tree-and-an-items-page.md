# Lesson 2.1: The tree and an item's page

The content tree is where editors find almost everything they work on. This lesson shows how to open folders in the
tree, open an item, and read the page of an item from the top: the path, the name and type, the buttons and the line
with its version and numbers.

## Learning objectives

After this lesson, learners can:

- open and close folders in the tree of **Content structure** and of **Media**;
- open an item from the tree and go back up with the path;
- read an item's name, type, visibility, version and numbers (**Node**, **Object**) from its page;
- name the buttons on an item's page and say which ones change something;
- explain why a button can be missing or grayed out for them.

## Trainer's notes

### How to present it

Source for this lesson: [A tour of the administration](../../../admin-tour.md#the-left-column) and
[An item's page](../../../admin-tour.md#an-items-page).

1. **Two trees** (4 minutes). Click **Content structure**: the left column shows the tree, starting at the top of
   the content (on many installations a folder such as *Home* or *Websites*). Click **Media**: the tree of the media
   library, with folders such as **Images**, **Files** and **Multimedia**. **Trash** is at the bottom of the tree.
2. **Opening folders** (4 minutes). Click the small triangle in front of a name to open a folder in the tree without
   leaving the page; click the name to open the item. Show both, and point out the difference.
3. **An item's page, top to bottom** (8 minutes). Open a folder with a few items below it and point at:
   - the **path** (*You are here:*): click a part to go up;
   - the **name** and the **type** badge (*Folder*, *Article* ...), and whether it is *Visible* or hidden;
   - the language list (for example *English (American)*) and the buttons **Edit**, **Upload**, **Move**, **Copy**,
     **Remove**;
   - the three round buttons **View on site**, **Preview** and **Manage versions**;
   - the line *Modified ... by ...*, *Published ...*, *Version ...*, and the **Node** and **Object** numbers;
   - the **Add to bookmarks** button.
4. **What changes something** (2 minutes). **Edit**, **Move**, **Copy** and **Remove** change content; **View on
   site**, **Preview** and **Manage versions** only show. A button you may not use is grayed out; pointing at it
   says why.
5. **Node and Object** (2 minutes). Keep it simple: the *Object* number belongs to the item itself, the *Node*
   number to its place in the tree. An item can have more than one place (Editors 201 covers locations). The node
   number is what the **Content finder** takes (lesson 2.3).

### Common questions

**Why is the item I am looking for not in the tree?** It may be in a folder you have not opened, in the **Media**
tree instead of **Content structure**, in the trash, or your role may not let you read it. Lesson 7.1 shows search.

**What does *Visible* mean?** The item is shown on the site. A hidden item stays in the administration but is not
shown to visitors, and nor is anything below it ([Publishing, versions and the trash](../../../publishing-and-versions.md#hide-an-item)).

**Why does the tree show some items in gray or with *(Hidden)*?** They are hidden. They are still there for editors.

### Pitfalls

- **Clicking the name instead of the triangle.** Learners lose their place in the tree because the page changes.
  Show the difference twice.
- **Editing the wrong item.** Before any exercise that changes something, ask learners to read the name and the type
  badge aloud. It catches most mistakes.
- **Too deep too early.** Do not explain locations, sections or object states here; they belong to Editors 201.

## Hands-on exercise

Use the training installation and your Editor training account. Nothing in this exercise changes content.

1. Click the **Content structure** tab. In the left column, find the top item of the tree and write down its name.
2. Click the small triangle in front of a folder to open it in the tree. Do not click the name yet.
3. Now click the name of an article (or any item) below that folder. Its page opens.
4. Write down: its name, its type (the badge next to the name), whether it is *Visible*, its *Version*, and its
   **Node** and **Object** numbers.
5. Point at the buttons **Edit**, **Upload**, **Move**, **Copy** and **Remove** without clicking them. Hover over
   any grayed-out button and write down what it says.
6. In the path at the top, click the folder above the item. You are back on the folder's page.
7. Click the **Media** tab. Open the folder **Images** (or the first folder of the media library) in the tree.
8. Open one image and write down its type and its **Node** number.
9. Scroll to the bottom of the tree and find **Trash**. Do not open it yet.
10. Press **View on site** on the image or the article: the item opens as visitors see it. Close that tab and come
    back.

## Expected result

- The learner's notes list the top item of the tree, an item with its type, visibility, version and two numbers,
  and an image with its node number.
- The learner went back up with the path, not with the browser's back button.
- **View on site** opened the public site in the browser.
- The trainer checks by asking one learner to read their notes and opening the same item on the projector.

## Quiz

1. What is the difference between clicking the triangle in front of a folder and clicking its name?
2. Which of these buttons changes content?
   A. **View on site** B. **Preview** C. **Move** D. **Manage versions**
3. A button is grayed out. How do you find out why?
4. Which number does the **Content finder** take to open an item?
   A. The version number B. The node number C. The year of publication D. The number of sub items
5. Where is the trash?

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. The triangle opens the folder in the tree; the name opens the item's page.
2. **C.** **Move** places the item somewhere else; the others only show.
3. Point at it: the tooltip says why ([Creating and editing content](../../../editing-content.md#move-copy-and-remove)).
4. **B.** The node number ([Searching](../../../searching.md#open-an-item-by-its-number-or-address)).
5. At the bottom of the tree in the left column.

</details>
