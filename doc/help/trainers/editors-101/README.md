# Editors 101: course overview

Editors 101 is the course for people who will write and publish content with Exponential and have never used it.
It starts with what a content item is and ends with each learner producing a complete article on their own. Every
exercise happens on a training installation, in a course folder of the learner's own, so nobody changes anybody
else's work and nothing reaches a live site.

## Who it is for

- New editors, authors and communication staff of a site built with Exponential.
- Team members who will use the **Editor**, the simplified administration for editors.
- Administrators who want to know what their editors are taught (they may skip modules 1 and 2).

## Outcome

After the course, learners can sign in, find any item, create and edit folders and articles, format rich text in
the online editor (TinyMCE 8), add images with good alternative texts, upload many files at once, preview and publish,
compare and restore versions, hide items and use the trash, search, keep bookmarks, look after their account, and
work in the Editor.

## Format and length

| | |
|---|---|
| Lessons | 27 lessons in 9 modules |
| Lesson time | 19 hours 30 minutes (presenting and exercises) |
| With breaks | about 21 hours, as in the modules' timing plans |
| Format | Classroom with a projector, or online with screen sharing; every learner at their own computer |
| Group size | Up to about 12 learners per trainer, so the trainer can look at every screen during the exercises |
| Level | Beginner |

## Prerequisites

- Learners: a computer with a current browser; no knowledge of Exponential.
- Trainer: experience as an editor on the version they teach, and the train-the-trainer course or equivalent
  experience ([Train the trainer: course overview](../train-the-trainer/README.md)).

## Lab setup

- One training installation reachable from every learner's computer, for example installed with
  `php bin/php/console exp:install` ([7. The console install](../../../install/07-console-install.md)); with the
  editor siteaccess if you want to teach module 9 hands-on. How to build and reset a lab:
  [Module 2: Building the lab](../train-the-trainer/module-02-building-the-lab/README.md) of Train the trainer.
- One account per learner with the **Editor** role, e-mail addresses at `example.invalid`, and a folder **Training**
  in which that role may create folders, articles and images.
- A few published folders, articles and images on the training site, and neutral sample images (no people) on each
  learner's computer.
- No real mail: the training installation must not send e-mail to real addresses.

## Suggested schedules

Lesson times add up to 19 hours 30 minutes; the schedules add breaks and a short daily review.

### Three full days

| Day | Morning | Afternoon |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Module 1, Module 2 | Module 3 |
| 2 | Module 4, Module 5 | Module 6 |
| 3 | Module 7, Module 8 | Module 9, open questions |

### Six half days (for example one a week)

| Half day | Modules |
|---|---|
| 1 | Module 1, Module 2 (lessons 2.1 and 2.2) |
| 2 | Lesson 2.3, Module 3 |
| 3 | Module 4 |
| 4 | Module 5, Module 7 |
| 5 | Module 6 |
| 6 | Module 8, Module 9 |

Between half days, ask learners to repeat one exercise of the last session on the training installation.

### One day, essentials only

For teams that need a start today and the rest later: lessons 1.1, 1.2, 1.3, 2.1, 3.1, 3.2, 4.1, 5.3, 6.1 and 6.4
(7 hours 45 minutes of lessons, a long day with breaks). Schedule the remaining lessons within a month.

## Assessing learners

- Each lesson's quiz and expected result check understanding during the course.
- Lesson 9.2 (the capstone) has a seven-point checklist for a final practical check.
- The assessments course (Assessments and practical exercises, in this section of the help) has further material.
  All assessments are the trainer's own material, not an official certification ([Certifications](../../partners/certifications.md) says what the partner program publishes).
