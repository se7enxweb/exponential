# Trainers: start here

This section of the help is for **people who teach Exponential**: professional trainers, team leads who onboard new
editors, consultants who hand a site over to a customer, teachers who use Exponential in a class, and community
members who run workshops. It holds complete courses, built from modules and lessons, that you can run as they are,
cut into shorter sessions or adapt for your organization.

Every lesson is written for the trainer and the learners at once: objectives, the trainer's notes, a hands-on
exercise on a real installation, the expected result, a quiz with answers, and links into the rest of the help. Every
fact comes from this documentation, the code or a real installation; when the software changes, the lessons change
with it.

> **Note**
> The assessments in these courses are your material as a trainer. The Exponential Partner Program names
> certifications for partner teams (Developer, Design, Solution, Sales), but their syllabus, exams and examiners are
> not published ([Certifications](../partners/certifications.md)), and these courses are not those certifications.
> Never present a course, an assessment or a certificate of yours as an official certification.

## How a course is built

```text
Course    a course overview: who it is for, the modules, a reading path, schedules
  Module    a module overview: the lessons, preparing the module, a timing plan
    Lesson    one page: at a glance, objectives, trainer's notes, exercise, expected result, quiz, further reading
```

Each lesson has a duration that includes its exercise, so a module's timing plan adds up. The
[first lesson of Train the trainer](train-the-trainer/module-01-preparing-to-teach/how-these-courses-work.md) explains
the structure in detail.

## The rules every trainer keeps

1. **Teach from the real thing.** Rehearse every exercise on the version you teach. When a lesson and the software
   disagree, the software wins, and the lesson gets fixed
   ([Adapting and improving these courses](train-the-trainer/module-03-designing-a-course/adapting-and-improving-these-courses.md)).
2. **Use training installations.** Never a live site, never real customer data, never a shared administrator
   account ([Demonstrating safely](train-the-trainer/module-04-delivering/demonstrating-safely.md)).
3. **Test accounts use `example.invalid`.** No exercise may send mail to a real person.
4. **Use the right names.** The product is Exponential; 7x is the company that leads it; the Exponential Foundation
   is the nonprofit; the old name is history
   ([Names and history in class](train-the-trainer/module-01-preparing-to-teach/names-and-history-in-class.md)).
5. **Keep it accessible and honest.** Accessible materials
   ([Module 5](train-the-trainer/module-05-accessibility-and-inclusion/README.md)), and no claim of official
   certification ([Certificates of attendance](train-the-trainer/module-06-assessment-and-follow-up/certificates-of-attendance.md)).
