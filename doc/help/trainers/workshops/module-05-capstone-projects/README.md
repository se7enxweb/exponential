# Module 5: Capstone projects

A capstone gives learners a brief instead of steps and shows the trainer what they can do alone. Each brief here has
a scoring rubric of six criteria; use them as written or adapt them.

| | |

| Duration | 4 to 8 hours each |
| Level | Beginner to advanced |

## Preparing this module

- One training installation per learner or pair, reset to a known state.
- The brief printed; images under a free license on a shared drive.
- The rubric printed for the trainer.

## Timing plan

| Part | Share of the time |
|---|---|
| Reading the brief and planning | 10 percent |
| Building | 70 percent |
| Self-check and fixes | 10 percent |
| Presentations and feedback | 10 percent |
