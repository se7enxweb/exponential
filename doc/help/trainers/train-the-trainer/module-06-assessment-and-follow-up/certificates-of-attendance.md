# Lesson 6.4: Certificates of attendance in your own name

Learners often want something to show for a course. A certificate of attendance or completion is fine, as long as it
is honest about who issues it. This lesson gives wording that does not claim what does not exist.

> **Note**
> The documentation describes no official Exponential certification program. A certificate you issue is from you or
> your organization, not from 7x or the Exponential Foundation.

## Learning objectives

After this lesson, learners can:

- write a certificate that names the issuer, the course, the topics and the duration;
- avoid wording that suggests official status ("certified by", "official", logos of others);
- use the product name correctly;
- include only the personal data needed (the learner's name).

## Trainer's notes

### How to present it

1. A good certificate (5 minutes): issuer (you or your organization), learner's name, course title, topics or
   modules, duration, date, whether an assessment was passed, signature.
2. Words to avoid (5 minutes): "Official Exponential certification", "certified by 7x", "Exponential Foundation
   certified". Do not use other organizations' logos without permission.

### Common questions

**May I write "Exponential" on it?** Yes, to name the subject: "Course: Editing with Exponential". Follow the
naming rules ([Brand and naming rules](../../../business/brand-and-naming-rules.md)).

**Attendance or completion?** Attendance means they were there; completion means they passed your assessment. Say
which.

### Pitfalls

- **Implied endorsement.** Never suggest that 7x or the Foundation checked the learner.
- **Too much personal data.** No date of birth or address.

## Hands-on exercise

1. Write a certificate text for the Editors 101 course you would run. Use this structure:

   ```text
   CERTIFICATE OF COMPLETION

   <Learner's name>
   completed the course "Editors 101: working with Exponential" (<duration>)
   given by <your name or organization> on <date>, and passed the course's
   practical assessment set by the trainer.

   Topics: <the module titles>

   Issued by <your name or organization>. This certificate is issued by the trainer
   and is not an official certification of 7x or the Exponential Foundation.
   ```

2. Fill in the placeholders with invented values.
3. Check the text against the naming rules and the words to avoid.
4. Exchange with a partner, who looks for any claim of official status.

## Expected result

- A certificate text that names the issuer, course, topics, duration and date, with the disclaimer.
- No "official" or "certified by 7x" wording; the product named correctly.

## Quiz

1. Who issues a certificate for a course you ran?
2. Rewrite: "Official Exponential Certified Editor".
3. What is the difference between attendance and completion?
4. Which personal data does a certificate need?

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. You or your organization.
2. For example: "Completed: Editors 101, working with Exponential (trainer's course)".
3. Attendance: present; completion: passed the trainer's assessment.
4. The learner's name.

</details>
