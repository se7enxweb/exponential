# Train the trainer: course overview

This course is for people who will teach Exponential to others: professional trainers, team leads who onboard new
editors, consultants who hand over sites to customers, and teachers who use Exponential in class. It teaches how to
use the trainer material in this help, how to build a lab that works, how to design and deliver a course, and how to
assess learners honestly.

## Who it is for

- Trainers and teachers who will run any of the courses in the [Course catalogue](../course-catalogue.md).
- Team leads and consultants who onboard editors or administrators.
- Anyone who adapts the courses for their own organization.

**Outcome:** after the course, a trainer can prepare, run and evaluate an Exponential course on a lab they built,
with material that is accurate, accessible and correctly licensed.

## Format and duration

| | |
|---|---|
| Duration | 34 lessons, about 21 hours of lessons (three to four days with breaks) |
| Format | Classroom or online; hands-on |
| Level | Beginner to intermediate |
| Prerequisites | Comfortable with a browser and a terminal; any Exponential experience helps but is not required |
| Lab | One training installation per learner (Module 2 builds it); PHP 8.1 or later, Composer 2 and Git |

| Module | Lessons | Duration | Outcome |

| [1. Preparing to teach Exponential](module-01-preparing-to-teach/README.md) | 5 | 2 h 35 min | Knows the material, the Help center, the learners, the license and the names |

| [3. Designing a course](module-03-designing-a-course/README.md) | 6 | 4 h 5 min | Writes objectives, structures and times a course, writes exercises |
| [4. Delivering a course](module-04-delivering/README.md) | 6 | 4 h 15 min | Runs the first hour, demos safely, troubleshoots, keeps it secure |
| [5. Accessibility and inclusion](module-05-accessibility-and-inclusion/README.md) | 4 | 2 h 30 min | Makes materials and sessions accessible |
| [6. Assessment and follow-up](module-06-assessment-and-follow-up/README.md) | 5 | 2 h 40 min | Checks learning, assesses fairly, collects feedback, follows up |

## Suggested schedules

| Schedule | Plan |
|---|---|
| Four days | Day 1: Module 1 and lessons 2.1 to 2.4. Day 2: lessons 2.5 to 2.8 and Module 3. Day 3: Modules 4 and 5. Day 4 morning: Module 6, then each trainer runs a 20-minute practice lesson |
| Three days, intensive | Day 1: Modules 1 and 2. Day 2: Modules 3 and 4. Day 3: Modules 5 and 6 |
| Six half days | One module per half day, in order |
| Self-study | One module per week, with the exercises done on your own machine |

## Assessing learners

Lesson 6.2 shows how to build an assessment; as a final task, each trainer prepares and runs one lesson from any
course in the catalogue for the group, judged against a rubric of objectives, accuracy, timing, safety and
accessibility. The assessments are the trainer's own material, not an official certification; what the partner
program publishes about its certifications is on [Certifications](../../partners/certifications.md).
