# Public health

This folder is for project managers, administrators and website creators at a health department or public health
agency. Public health websites give advice that people act on: how to protect themselves, where to get vaccinated,
what to do in an outbreak. The advice must be correct, current, understandable by everyone and available in the
languages people speak, and the site must cope when everyone looks at once. The pages answer what public health
bodies publish, how to build it in Exponential, which requirements weigh most, how to run the first 90 days, and how
to publish alerts in many languages and plain language.

Product facts come from the documentation of this repository; descriptions of public health bodies are general
public-sector practice. Health advice itself is the responsibility of your clinical and scientific staff.

## What sets public health apart

| Topic | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Advice people act on | an error can harm; clinical sign-off is part of publishing |
| Change | advice changes as evidence changes; old advice must not linger |
| Everyone must understand | low health literacy, many languages, disabilities |
| Sudden peaks | an outbreak or a scare sends traffic up many times over |
| Trust | the site is the reference others quote and link to |
