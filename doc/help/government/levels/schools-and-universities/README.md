# Schools and universities

This folder is for project managers, administrators and website creators at public schools, school districts,
colleges and universities. Education sites serve prospective students and parents, current students and staff, and
the wider public, often with many departments that each want a site of their own and an intranet for staff and
students. The pages answer what education institutions publish, how to build it in Exponential, which requirements
weigh most, how to run the first 90 days, and how to run intranets and department subsites.

Product facts come from the documentation of this repository; descriptions of education institutions are general
public-sector practice.

## What sets education apart

| Topic | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Many departments | each faculty, school or department wants its own pages, often its own look |
| An academic calendar | admissions deadlines, terms, exams: content follows the year |
| Intranet | staff and students need information that is not public |
| Minors | schools publish about and for children; consent and care for their data and images |
| Turnover | students and staff change every year; accounts must follow |

Exponential serves many sites from one installation ([Multisite setup](../../../creators/website-creators/multisite-setup.md))
and keeps restricted areas behind sections and roles ([Sections](../../../../guides/sections.md)). It does not
replace a learning management system or a student information system; link to them.
