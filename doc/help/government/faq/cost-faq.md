# FAQ: cost

This page answers the cost questions public bodies ask about Exponential. The project publishes no prices for
implementation or hosting, so this page gives no figures; it says what you pay for and where to find the published
terms that exist.

## What does Exponential cost?

Nothing to license: it is free software under the GPL ([Licensing questions](../../project/licensing-faq.md)). The
documentation and the training courses in this help are free too ([Free documentation](../../free-documentation.md)).

## Is there a price per user or per site?

No. There are no seats, editors or sites to license. One installation can serve many sites
([Multisite setup](../../creators/website-creators/multisite-setup.md)) and any number of user accounts.

## What still costs money?

| Cost | Paid to |
|---|---|
| Implementation: content model, design, templates, migration | an agency or partner, or your staff |
| Hosting and operations | a host, or your data center and staff |
| Support with agreed response times | 7x or a partner |
| Training | a training partner or your own trainers ([Budgeting training effort](../procurement/training/budgeting-training-effort.md)) |
| Routine upgrades and security updates | your supplier or staff ([Planning upgrades in the budget](../procurement/lifecycle/planning-upgrades-in-the-budget.md)) |
| Accessibility and security assessments | an independent assessor |
| Third-party services you add (analytics, mail, AI providers) | those services |
| Exit and transition at the end of a contract | the outgoing and incoming suppliers |

The total cost of ownership part of the procurement section builds these into a model ([Procurement](../procurement/README.md)).

## What does support cost?

Community support is free, with no response promise. Paid support from 7x is agreed with 7x
([Getting support](../../project/getting-support.md)). Partner levels and their published terms are on
[Partner plans and prices as published](../../partners/partner-plans-and-prices.md) and
[Support levels and response times](../../partners/support-levels-and-response-times.md); those are the terms for
partners, not a price list for public bodies.

## Are upgrades charged?

New versions themselves are free. The work of applying them is not: someone has to back up, update, test and check.
Its size depends mostly on how much custom code your site has and how far behind it is
([Planning upgrades in the budget](../procurement/lifecycle/planning-upgrades-in-the-budget.md)).

## How do we compare offers?

On the same scope and over the whole contract term, not the first year. Ask bidders for hours per routine update,
hosting per year, support per year, training per session and exit costs, and fill them into one model. Look for costs
that only appear later: undocumented custom code, missing training for new staff, a missing exit plan.
