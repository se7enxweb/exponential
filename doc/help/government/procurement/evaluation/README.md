# Evaluating bids for an Exponential site

This section is for procurement officers, project managers and evaluation panel members who must compare bids for a
content management system, when one or more bids are based on Exponential. It gives templates you can copy, guidance
for running demos and a proof of concept, and evidence guides that tell you what the product documentation proves
and what it does not. Every fact links the repository page it comes from.

## Principles of this section

- **Documented facts only.** The product's status against each standard comes from `doc/standards/`, whose status
  words are explained in [Standards: how to read the status](../../../../standards/README.md#how-to-read-the-status).
- **No invented references, customers or numbers.** Where a weight or score is needed, the template leaves a blank.
- **Software and service are evaluated separately.** The free software has no license fee; the supplier's work,
  hosting and support are where most cost and risk sit.
- **Invented data in demos and tests.** Never real personal data of citizens or staff.
