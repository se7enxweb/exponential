# Total cost of ownership of an Exponential site

This section is for project managers, budget holders and procurement officers who must compare the cost of
content management options over their whole life. Exponential has no license fee under the GNU GPL, so its cost lies
in implementation, hosting, people, support, updates and exit. The pages explain each part and give templates. They
contain no prices: the only figures are in the worked example, and each one there is labeled an example assumption.

## Principles of this section

- **No invented prices.** Use your own quotes, records and measurements.
- **Count internal time.** Staff time does not appear in bids but is part of the cost.
- **Same basis for every option.** Same horizon, rates and assumptions.
- **Include exit.** A model without an exit line favors lock-in.
