# Citizen accounts

This folder is for administrators, website creators and project managers who let members of the public hold accounts
on a public-sector site: to follow content and get notifications, to subscribe to newsletters, to comment, to reach a
members area, or as partners of the organization. It covers the whole life of an account, from registration to
removal, with the settings and pages Exponential provides. Staff accounts use the same mechanisms; where they differ,
the pages say so.

Accounts are optional. Many public sites need none: information is public and transactions happen in other systems.
Offer accounts only for a purpose you can name, because every account is personal data you must look after.

## Decisions before offering accounts

> **Note** This page describes features and common practice; it is not legal advice. Check the law that applies to
> your organization with your legal counsel.

- [ ] What is the purpose of an account, and what does it allow?
- [ ] Which data does registration collect? (Exponential's user class needs a login, an e-mail address and a
  password; everything else is your choice.)
- [ ] Is registration open to everyone, or approved by staff?
- [ ] How long is an unused account kept before it is disabled and removed?
- [ ] Does the public use the same siteaccess as staff? (Staff should sign in to the administration; the public on
  the site.)
