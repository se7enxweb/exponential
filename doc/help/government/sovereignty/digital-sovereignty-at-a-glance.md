# Digital sovereignty at a glance

This page is for managers, architects and administrators in a public body who must show that their web platform
stays under their control: that they can run it where they choose, change it, move away from it and keep their data.
Each row below states one point and what Exponential provides, with the page that backs it.

## Where the work stays with you

Sovereignty is not a product feature alone. These stay with the public body:

- choosing and contracting the hosting, and knowing where the data and the backups are;
- deciding which optional extensions to activate, and reviewing the third parties they involve;
- keeping the installation updated, or contracting someone to do it;
- keeping enough knowledge in-house to direct providers and to change them;
- an exit plan that has been tested.
