# Records and archiving for public bodies

This section is for records managers, archivists, project managers, editors and administrators of public bodies that
publish with Exponential. Exponential is a publishing system, not a records management system: it keeps versions,
records who changed what in a tamper-evident audit trail, keeps URLs stable through renames and moves, and exports
content in several ways. It has no records declaration, disposition schedule or legal hold feature; the pages say how
to build those processes with the tools that exist.

> **Note** This section describes features and common practice; it is not legal advice. Check the law that applies to
> your organization with your legal counsel.
