# Onboard a new editor

**Who:** the site administrator, with the editor's senior editor. **When:** in the editor's first weeks. The full
routine is in [Onboarding new staff](../../procurement/training/onboarding-new-staff.md); this page is the checklist of
clicks for the administrator.

## Contents

1. [Before you start](#before-you-start)
2. [Steps](#steps)
3. [Result](#result)
4. [Pitfalls](#pitfalls)

## Before you start

- The editor's department, section and future role are known.
- The training installation and the next course date are known.

## Steps

1. **Create the account** in the department's user group, with the editor's work e-mail ([Accounts and sign-in](../../../accounts-and-sign-in.md)).
   One account per person, never shared.
2. **Assign a limited role** on the live site, or none yet; give access to the training installation
   ([Roles and policies](../../../../guides/roles-and-policies.md)).
3. **Send the sign-in details** through a channel your security rules allow; ask the editor to change the password at
   first sign-in. Set up two-step sign-in where your site uses it ([Two-step sign-in](../../../../guides/two-factor-login.md)).
4. **Point to self-study**: [Logging into the editor](../../../logging-into-the-editor.md), [Admin tour](../../../admin-tour.md),
   [Editing content](../../../editing-content.md), [Publishing and versions](../../../publishing-and-versions.md).
5. **Book training**: Editors 101 or the department's course ([Plan editor training for a department](plan-editor-training-for-a-department.md)).
6. **Supervise the first publication**: the editor drafts, the senior editor reviews; an approval workflow can enforce it
   ([Workflows](../../../../guides/workflows.md)).
7. **Assign the full role** after sign-off; record course and date.
8. **Check after a month** with **Check user accounts** (`/user/hygiene`) that the account is in use
   ([Check user accounts](../../../../features/6.0/user-hygiene.md)).

## Result

A trained editor with the right permissions from the start, and a record for the next access review.

## Pitfalls

- The full role on day one.
- Sharing one "department" account between several people: the audit trail can then not tell who did what.
