# Correct a published notice and keep the history

This page is for editors who find a mistake in something already published: a wrong date in a notice, a wrong amount,
a wrong address. Public bodies correct openly: the page says it was corrected, and the earlier wording remains
available to staff in the version history. Every save is a version
([Publishing, versions and the trash](../../../publishing-and-versions.md)).

## Contents

1. [Before you start](#before-you-start)

## Before you start

- You can edit the item.
- You know whether the mistake matters to people who acted on it (a wrong deadline does; a typo does not).
- If the notice is a legal or statutory notice, you know whether a correction restarts a notice period (ask the
  responsible officer).

## Steps

1. Open the notice and press **Edit**. A new draft is made from the published version.
2. Correct the text.
3. Add a correction note where readers see it, at the top or the end: "Corrected on <date>: the closing date is
   <date>, not <date>." For a typo, a note is not needed.
4. **Preview** the draft if the change is large.
5. Publish:
   - **Send for publishing** for a correction followers should know about (they are notified at the next run);
   - **Publish without notification**, where your site offers it, for a typo
     ([Publish without notifying subscribers](publish-without-notifying-subscribers.md)).

## Check the result

- [ ] The corrected text shows to signed-out visitors.
- [ ] **Manage versions** lists the previous version as *Archived*; **Compare** shows your change
  ([Compare two versions](compare-two-versions.md)).
- [ ] If the notice is also in an RSS feed, the feed shows the new text when it is next written.

## Pitfalls

| Pitfall | Avoid it |
|---|---|
| Correcting silently a fact people acted on | always add a correction note for facts |
| Removing the old version to hide the mistake | the history is part of accountability; remove versions only for legal reasons such as unlawfully published personal data ([Versions of documents](../../building/documents-and-publications/versions-of-documents.md#removing-a-file-that-must-not-stay)) |
| Editing someone else's open draft | the **Possible edit conflict** page appears; continue your own draft or make a **New draft** |
| The wrong language | choose the language before **Edit**; correct every translation |
