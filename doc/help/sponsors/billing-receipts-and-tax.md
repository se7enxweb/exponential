# Billing, receipts and tax

This page is for sponsors and the people who book their payments. It quotes the sources and adds nothing they do not
say. It is not tax advice.

## Paying through GitHub Sponsors

From GitHub's documentation, "About sponsorships, fees, and taxes"
(<https://docs.github.com/en/sponsors/sponsoring-open-source-contributors/about-sponsorships-fees-and-taxes>, read
18 October 2026):

| Topic | GitHub says |
|---|---|
| Billing and receipts | "Your sponsorship will share your account's existing billing date, payment method, and receipt." |
| Payment methods | "debit or credit cards, and Patreon"; PayPal is not supported for GitHub Sponsors since 23 February 2023 |
| When you are charged | "your balance will be charged effective immediately"; monthly sponsorships are prorated to your billing date |
| Invoices | "enterprises and organizations may opt to be billed by recurring invoice" |
| Fees | No GitHub fee for sponsorships from personal accounts; "a fee of up to 6% for sponsorships from organization accounts" (3% card processing, 3% service), and invoiced billing saves the 3% card fee |
| Information shared | GitHub "may share certain limited tax information with sponsored accounts", such as the transaction date |

The full details are in GitHub's documentation, starting at <https://docs.github.com/en/sponsors>.

## Tax

- **The Exponential Foundation** is "a 501(c)(3) Non-Profit Corporation" and states: "Funds donated are tax free and
  tax deductible on the donner's taxes in the United States" (<https://foundation.exponential.earth/>). Whether a
  GitHub Sponsors payment is treated as a donation to the Foundation for your taxes, and what receipt you need, is for
  you and your adviser to confirm; ask the Foundation for a donation receipt if you need one.
- **7x** is a company. The sources do not describe sponsorship of 7x as a donation; several of its tiers include
  services (support, consulting, pair programming). Treat it as your adviser tells you.
- Outside the United States, your own country's rules apply.

## Invoices for services

Partner subscriptions and services bought from 7x are purchases with invoices from 7x's store
([Partner plans and prices](../partners/partner-plans-and-prices.md#invoices-and-taxes)).
