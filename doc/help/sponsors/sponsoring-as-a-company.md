# Sponsoring as a company

This page is for organizations that want to fund Exponential. Companies that depend on Exponential carry the most
weight: a few monthly organization sponsorships pay for steady maintenance.

## Why companies sponsor

GitHub lists reasons organizations sponsor, including "Sustaining specific libraries that your organization's work
depends on", "Investing in the ecosystem you rely on" and "Developing brand awareness as an organization that values
open source" (GitHub Docs, "About sponsorships, fees, and taxes").

For Exponential in particular: your sites need security fixes and support for each new PHP version
(SECURITY.md, README). Funding the people who make them is cheaper than maintaining a private fork.

## How to sponsor from an organization

1. Sponsor from your GitHub organization account, not a personal one, so the sponsorship belongs to the company.
2. Pick the page: <https://github.com/sponsors/se7enxweb> (7x) or <https://github.com/sponsors/exponentialcms>
   (the Foundation), or both ([Two organizations, two sponsor pages](two-organizations-two-sponsor-pages.md)).
3. Consider invoiced billing: "enterprises and organizations may opt to be billed by recurring invoice", which saves
   the 3% card processing part of the "up to 6%" fee on organization sponsorships (GitHub Docs).
4. Owners can see and export sponsorships in the organization's sponsorship dashboard (GitHub Docs).

## Tiers for business needs

| Need | 7x tier (as listed) | Foundation tier (as listed) |
|---|---|---|
| Visibility | $250 a month: logo or name in README | $25 a month README; $100 a month website |
| Priority for your issues | $100 a month bug priority; $125 a month package | $100 a month bug reports prioritized |
| A feature | $750 a month feature bounty; $1,000 one time bounty | $1,000 one time bounty |
| Help in your team | $1,000 a month in your company chat; $2,600 a month maintenance package | $1,000 a month in your chat; $8,000 a month in your project |
| Training | $350 one time consulting | $500 one time workshop |

Details: [Sponsoring 7x on GitHub](sponsoring-7x.md), [Sponsoring the Exponential Foundation](sponsoring-the-foundation.md).

## Internal approval

```text
Request: sponsorship of <7x / the Exponential Foundation> — <amount> per month
Why: our <sites/products> run on Exponential; maintenance (security fixes, PHP support, releases) depends on it.
What we get: <tier benefits>
Billing: GitHub organization account, <card / invoice>; fees as GitHub documents them
Review: yearly, owner <role>
```
