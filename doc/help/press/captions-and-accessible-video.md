# Captions and accessible video

This page is for creators who want their videos, streams and episodes to work for everyone: people who are deaf or
hard of hearing, people who are blind or have low vision, people who watch without sound on a train, and people who
follow in a second language. Exponential's administration is built and tested for accessibility
([Talking points: accessibility](../advocates/talking-points-accessibility.md)); content about it should meet the same
bar. The general rules for slides and images are on [Accessible slides and visuals](../advocates/accessible-slides-and-visuals.md).

## Captions

- **Correct captions, not only automatic ones.** Automatic captions mishear product names and commands. Edit them.
- **Every spoken word and every meaningful sound**: "[keyboard typing]", "[error sound]" where it matters.
- **Commands exactly as typed**: `exp:install`, not "exp install".
- **Short lines, in sync**: two lines at most on screen at a time.
- **Speaker names** when more than one person speaks.
- **Live streams**: use live captions if your platform offers them; correct the recording afterwards.
- **Translations**: if you add captions in other languages, have a speaker of the language review them.

## A caption glossary

Give this list to whoever corrects your captions:

| Write | Not |
|---|---|
| Exponential (or "Exponential CMS" at a first mention) | "exponential" in lower case, "the Exponential", "Exponent" |
| 7x | "seven x", "se7en x" (se7enx and se7enxweb are domain and account names) |
| the Exponential Foundation | "the Exponential foundation" |
| Exponential Velocity | "velocity" alone at first mention |
| Exponential Intelligence | "the AI", "Exponential AI" |
| Exponential Layouts | "layouts plugin" |
| eZ Publish (only as history) | "EZ Publish", "Ezpublish" |
| SQLite, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, MariaDB, MySQL | "sequel lite", "Postgres SQL" |
| `exp:install`, `exp:velocity`, `composer install` | Spoken forms |

The full naming rules: [Brand and naming rules](../business/brand-and-naming-rules.md#the-names-in-one-table).

## Transcripts

- Publish a transcript with every video and podcast episode: in the description, on your site, or as a file.
- Put commands and links in the transcript so people can copy them; nobody can copy from a video frame.
- For a podcast, the transcript is the accessible version of the episode. Put the show notes and sources at its end
  (Podcast episode outlines).

## Say what you do

- Name the button and the place: "I press **Send for publishing** at the top of the form", not "I click here".
- Read out what appears: "A dialog asks whether to discard the changes."
- Never let information exist only on screen or only in color: "the zone with the red dashed border is linked" should
  also be "this zone is linked".
- For a demonstration that is mostly visual, such as dragging blocks in the layouts editor, describe the result:
  "The Title block is now in the main zone, above the full view block."

## A readable picture

- Record at a zoom that is readable on a phone; enlarge the part you talk about.
- Light color mode is usually clearer in video; if you show dark mode, say so.
- Keep the pointer slow and visible; consider a highlight around it.
- **No flashing** content and no fast zooms or cuts.
- Keep important content away from the bottom of the frame, where captions appear.

## Showing accessibility features honestly

Exponential has keyboard features you can demonstrate, all documented in
[Keyboard shortcuts](../keyboard-shortcuts.md):

| Show | Say |
|---|---|
| Reload a page, press `Tab` once | "**Skip to main content** appears." |
| Press `/` | "The focus jumps to search." |
| Focus the content tree, use the arrow keys | "The tree works with the keyboard." |
| Switch the interface to Arabic or Hebrew | "The administration turns around for right-to-left languages." |

Say what the sources say, no more: "The admin4 design follows WCAG rules that tests check: color contrast in light and
dark mode and targets of at least 24 by 24 pixels" ([Who is the Exponential Foundation?, Languages and accessibility](../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#languages-and-accessibility)).
Do not say "fully accessible" or "WCAG compliant" ([Claims you must not make](../advocates/claims-you-must-not-make.md#security-and-compliance)).

## Checklist

```text
[ ] Captions corrected by a person; product names and commands spelled right
[ ] Transcript published, with commands and links
[ ] Actions said aloud; nothing only on screen or only in color
[ ] Readable zoom; no flashing; captions do not cover important content
[ ] Thumbnails and images have alternative text where the platform allows it
[ ] Accessibility claims limited to what the sources say
```
