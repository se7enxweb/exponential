# Preparing a briefing

This page is for journalists, podcast hosts and creators who have a briefing or interview about Exponential coming
up. Preparation lets you spend the conversation on what only the people can tell you, such as plans, reasons and
opinions, instead of on facts that are already documented.

## Reading before the briefing

About 30 minutes covers the basics:

| Minutes | Read | Gives you |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | [Who is 7x?, In short](../who-is-7x.md#in-short) | The company, the name history, the releases |
| 5 | [Who is the Exponential Foundation?, In short](../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#in-short) | The nonprofit, its mission, its relation to 7x |
| 5 | [Who is the Exponential Foundation?, What the sources do not say](../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#what-the-sources-do-not-say) | The open questions worth asking |
| 5 | [The history, The story so far](../../history/README.md#the-story-so-far) | The main events since December 2023 |
| 5 | The changelog of the newest release, for example [6.0.14](../../changelogs/6.0/6.0.14.md) | What just shipped |

## The briefing sheet

Fill this in before the call. Every "fact" line needs a source; the "open" lines become your questions.

```text
BRIEFING SHEET                                         date: ________   with: 7x / the Foundation

FORMAT        [ ] on the record   [ ] background only (agree before you start)   [ ] recorded (consent asked)
DEADLINE      ____________________   time zone: ______

FACTS I WILL NOT ASK ABOUT (with source)
- Newest release: ____________  (changelog link)
- Development line: __________  (6.0.15 changelog)
- Foundation founded: 19 March 2026, Seattle (foundation.exponential.earth)
- License: GNU GPL v2 or later; documentation GNU FDL 1.3 (README-LICENSE.md)
- __________________________________________________________

OPEN QUESTIONS (the sources do not say)
- ____________________________________________________________
- ____________________________________________________________

CLAIMS I NEED CONFIRMED OR DENIED
- ____________________________________________________________

NAMES TO SPELL RIGHT
Exponential - 7x - the Exponential Foundation - Exponential Velocity - Exponential Layouts -
Exponential Intelligence - Exponential Platform (Legacy, Nexus, DXP)
```

## Planning your questions

- **Ask for reasons and plans, not documented facts.** "Why did 7x keep the code base alive?" is a good question;
  "When was 6.0.0 released?" is in the [release overview](../../release-overview).
- **Use the gaps.** The Foundation page lists questions "left open because no public source used here answers them":
  the board, governance, members and budget. These are legitimate interview questions.
- **Ask about dates carefully.** If you ask when 6.0.15 will be released, record the answer as an answer, with the
  speaker, and print it only as what they said, never as a fact ([Attributing statements](attributing-statements.md)).
- Pick five to eight questions from the [Interview question bank](interview-question-bank.md) and order them from easy
  to hard.

## During the briefing

1. Confirm the ground rules at the start: on the record or background, recorded or not.
2. Ask for consent before recording; say where the recording will be kept.
3. Note exact words for anything you might quote, and mark them in your notes with quotation marks.
4. When a number is mentioned, ask for its source or date: "Where can readers check that?"
5. At the end, ask: "Is there anything you would like to give me in writing?" Written statements are the safest quotes
   ([Quotes policy](quotes-policy.md)).

## After the briefing

| Step | Why |
|---|---|
| Transcribe or note quotes the same day | Memory changes words |
| Check every fact you heard against the documentation | A spoken number may be from memory; the source wins, or you report both |
| Mark which statements are quotes, which are paraphrase, which are background | So nothing background ends up attributed |
| Send a follow-up for anything unclear | Better than guessing ([Verifying quotes](verifying-quotes.md)) |
| Delete recordings when you no longer need them, if you promised to | Privacy ([Protecting people's privacy](../advocates/protecting-peoples-privacy.md)) |
