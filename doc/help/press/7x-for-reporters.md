## In one paragraph

7x is a North American web design, development, support and hosting company. It was founded in 2001 as Brookins
Consulting, which worked with the software that became Exponential since its first versions. 7x prepared its project
from 2014, started it in 2022, and launched it in December 2023, when it took over the code base. It has led the
development of Exponential since then, publishing the 6.0.x releases from 1 January 2024. It is the main contributor
to the project that the Exponential Foundation operates. Sources: [Who is 7x?](../who-is-7x.md#in-short),
[7x in three stages](7x-in-three-stages.md).

## What it sells, and what stays free

7x sells services: web design and development, e-commerce integration, training, migrations of sites built while the
software was still called eZ Publish, support and maintenance contracts, and managed hosting
([Services and hosting](../who-is-7x.md#services-and-hosting)). The software itself stays free under the GPL. Prices
are on 7x's website, not in this documentation. Write "7x sells services around free software", never "7x sells
Exponential".

## Names and spelling

| se7enx.com (the domain), se7enxweb (GitHub, Composer) | se7enx as the company name in prose |
| 7x, formerly Brookins Consulting | Brookins Consulting as today's name |
| 7x leads Exponential | 7x owns Exponential (copyright is shared; the license is GPL) |

## What the sources do not say

The public sources used by this help do not give 7x's revenue, number of employees, customers or market share. Do
not estimate them. The founder is named on a public page of 7x; this help refers to the person as "the founder of
7x" and does not publish personal details. If your story needs a person, ask 7x whom to name and quote.
