# Press, media and influencers: start here

This section of the help is for people who write, film or talk about Exponential for an audience: journalists and
news editors, trade and technical press, bloggers, newsletter writers, podcasters, video creators, streamers and
other influencers. It gives you the facts with their sources, the names spelled right, the contacts that really
exist, and practical guides for covering a release, preparing an interview, checking your facts and making a fair
video.

Every fact on these pages comes from the Exponential repository and its documentation, from this help, or from a
public page that is named and linked. Where the sources do not answer a question, the pages say so instead of
guessing. If you print a fact from here, link or name the source we name.

> **Note**
> These pages are documentation, not statements from a press office. Nothing here is a quote from 7x or the
> Exponential Foundation. For a quote, ask them ([Press contacts](press-contacts.md), [Quotes policy](quotes-policy.md)).

## Exponential in one paragraph

**Exponential** is a free and open source PHP content management system and application framework. Its README
describes it as "a professional PHP application framework with advanced CMS (content management system)
functionality", whose most notable feature is "its fully customizable and extendable content model". It includes a
shop, multisite, many languages including right-to-left scripts, workflows, a REST API and a visual page builder.
The code is licensed under the GNU GPL, version 2 or later. The company **7x** leads its development; the
**Exponential Foundation**, a nonprofit founded in 2026, operates and funds the project. The code base has been
developed since 1999, first under the name eZ Publish, a product that its creator discontinued after almost 18 years;
7x's project kept it alive and launched it in December 2023, and the product has been called Exponential since
August 2025. Sources: [Who is 7x?](../who-is-7x.md#in-short),
[Who is the Exponential Foundation?](../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#in-short).

Longer and shorter versions you may copy are on [Boilerplate descriptions you may copy](boilerplate-descriptions.md).

## Five rules for every story

1. **Cite the source.** Numbers and dates change with releases. Link the page or file you took them from, and write
   the date you checked them ([Fact-checking guide](fact-checking-guide.md)).
2. **Use the right names.** The product is **Exponential**, the company **7x**, the nonprofit **the Exponential
   Foundation**. The old name is history only ([Brand and naming for media](brand-and-naming-for-media.md)).
3. **Never invent a quote.** Quote published text word for word with its link, or ask for a quote in writing
   ([Quotes policy](quotes-policy.md)).
4. **Keep vulnerabilities private.** If you learn of an unfixed security problem, it goes privately to the address
   in `SECURITY.md`, not into a story ([If you find a vulnerability](if-you-find-a-vulnerability.md)).
5. **Protect people.** No customer names without their consent, no personal data, no passwords on screen
   (Privacy in your coverage).
