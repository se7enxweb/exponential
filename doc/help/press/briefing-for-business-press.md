# Briefing for business press

This page is for business journalists, trade magazines and management newsletters. It covers the organizations, the
money and the licensing around Exponential: what is documented, and what is not. Business stories need numbers, and
most business numbers about Exponential are not published. Saying so plainly is part of an accurate story.

## The business model as documented

- **The software is free.** "Using Exponential under the terms of the GNU GPL is free of charge. The Professional
  License is no longer available" (README, in [Who is 7x?, Open source licenses](../who-is-7x.md#open-source-licenses)).
- **7x sells services around it**: web design and development, e-commerce integration, training, migration of sites
  built while the software was still called eZ Publish, support contracts and managed hosting. "Prices and terms are
  on the 7x website, not here" ([Services and hosting](../who-is-7x.md#services-and-hosting)).
- **The project is funded by donations and sponsorship.** "The Exponential Open Source Project is funded by donations
  of time and resources by 7x (and Exponential Sponsors)"; there are two sponsor pages,
  [github.com/sponsors/se7enxweb](https://github.com/sponsors/se7enxweb) for 7x and
  [github.com/sponsors/exponentialcms](https://github.com/sponsors/exponentialcms) for the Foundation
  ([The Foundation and 7x](../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#the-foundation-and-7x)).
- **The Foundation says donations are tax deductible in the United States**; the help adds "check with your own tax
  adviser" ([How to support or join](../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#how-to-support-or-join)).

## The longevity story

- The code base dates back to the oldest commit of 2 September 2002; the copyright notice runs from 1998
  ([7x in numbers](../who-is-7x.md#7x-in-numbers)).
- Its creator discontinued it as eZ Publish after almost 18 years; "The platform originally known as eZ Publish
  reached end-of-life in 2021. We kept going." (se7enxweb profile README, in the
  [Foundation timeline](../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#timeline)).
- Fifteen 6.0.x releases from 1 January 2024 to 7 June 2026, and a development line in progress
  ([The 6.0.x releases](../who-is-7x.md#the-60x-releases)).
