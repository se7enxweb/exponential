# Creators and influencers: start here

This part of the press help is for people who make content about Exponential for their own audience: video creators,
live streamers, podcasters, bloggers, newsletter writers and people who post on social networks. It tells you how to
show the product on an installation you control, which demo scripts match the real screens, how to stay honest with
your audience about money and gifts, and how to keep secrets and personal data off camera.

These pages are documentation, not a contract and not legal advice. Every product fact on them comes from the
Exponential repository and its help; where the sources do not say something, the pages say so. Nobody at 7x or the
Exponential Foundation reviews your content before you publish it, and these pages do not speak for them
([Press, media and influencers: start here](press-start-here.md)).

> **Note**
> No creator, influencer, ambassador or affiliate program for Exponential is documented in the sources. Nobody is
> paid through such a program, and no page here promises payment, free hosting or early access. If someone offers you
> one in the project's name, ask for it in writing and check it with the contacts the documentation names.

## The five rules for creators

1. **Disclose.** If anyone paid you, gave you something, employs you or will earn from your viewers, say so at the
   start ([Disclosure and sponsorship ethics](disclosure-and-sponsorship-ethics.md)).
2. **Show your own installation.** Never record a customer's, an employer's or a shared site
   ([Setting up a demo installation](setting-up-a-demo-installation.md)).
3. **Say which version you show.** Much of what the help describes belongs to the 6.0.15 line, which is in
   development and has no release date. Name the version or the development line in the video and the description.
4. **Keep secrets and people off screen.** No passwords, keys, server addresses or personal data
   ([Keeping secrets off screen](keeping-secrets-off-screen.md), [Privacy on camera](privacy-on-camera.md)).
5. **Cite the help, and correct in the open.** Link the help page behind every claim; when you get something wrong,
   say so where your audience sees it ([Correcting a video or post](correcting-a-video-or-post.md)).

## What the sources do not offer creators

- No creator, influencer or affiliate program, no payments, no review units, no free accounts.
- No official hashtag; no rules that make your channel "official".
- No license for the screenshots on exponential.earth; its screenshots page invites people to send their own.
- No logo files in version 1.0.0 of the press kit; its folders list planned files only.

Where a source exists, the pages above link it. Where it does not, say "the sources do not say".
