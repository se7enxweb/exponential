# Podcast and radio guests

This page is for podcast hosts and radio producers. Audio has its own risks: a wrong claim said live cannot be edited,
and listeners cannot see a screenshot. This page helps you prepare an episode about Exponential that stays accurate.

## Booking a guest

- For 7x or the Foundation, write to the addresses on [Press contacts](press-contacts.md) and ask who can speak and
  in which role ([Requesting an interview](requesting-an-interview.md)).
- For a community guest, ask in the forums or the chat; note how they relate to the project and disclose it on air
  ([Attributing statements](attributing-statements.md)).
- Tell the guest whether the show is live or recorded, how long, and where it will be published.

## The pre-interview

A short call or e-mail before recording:

```text
1. How should I introduce you (name, role, organization)?
2. Which two or three topics matter most to you?
3. Is there anything you cannot talk about (unreleased dates, security issues)?
4. Which numbers might come up, and where are they documented?
```

Read [Exponential in brief](exponential-in-brief.md) and prepare questions from
[Interview question bank](interview-question-bank.md).

## Names and pronunciation

| Name | Note |
|---|---|
| Exponential | Pronounced like the English word |
| 7x | "seven x"; written with the digit |
| se7enx.com | Say "seven x dot com"; spell it in the show notes |
| the Exponential Foundation | Use the full name at first mention |
| Velocity, Exponential Intelligence, Exponential Layouts | Full product names at first mention |
| eZ Publish | Only for history, in the past tense ([Writing about the eZ Publish years](writing-about-the-ez-publish-years.md)) |

Ask the guest how to pronounce their own name.

## Explaining software for the ear

- Describe what people do, not what the screen looks like: "editors drag blocks into a page" rather than "this panel
  on the left".
- One number at a time, with its context: "a cached page in about a third of a millisecond, measured by the project".
- Say web addresses slowly and put them in the show notes.

## Claims during the show

- If a guest gives a number you cannot check live, attribute it on air: "according to you".
- Do not let unverifiable claims stand as fact: usage numbers, rankings, "the most secure" ([What the sources do not say](what-the-sources-do-not-say.md)).
- If a guest mentions a vulnerability that is not yet fixed, steer away and cut it from the recording
  ([Covering security fixes](covering-security-fixes.md)).
- If the episode is sponsored, say so at the start ([Disclosure and sponsorship ethics](disclosure-and-sponsorship-ethics.md)).

## Show notes

```text
Guest: <name>, <role> at <organization>
Topics: <...>
Links:
  Exponential           https://exponential.earth/
  The code              https://github.com/se7enxweb/exponential
  The Foundation        https://foundation.exponential.earth/
  Pages mentioned       <links to the help pages you cited>
Facts checked on <date>. Corrections: <link to this section if any>
Disclosure: <sponsorship or relationship, or "none">
```

## After the episode

- Publish a transcript ([Making your coverage accessible](making-your-coverage-accessible.md)).
- Check the claims made on air; correct in the show notes and, if needed, at the start of the next episode
  ([Correcting a video or post](correcting-a-video-or-post.md)).
