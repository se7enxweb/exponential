# Earlier press releases and coverage

This page is for reporters who want to know what was published before. The project collects press releases and
outside coverage on its page **In the News**, <https://exponential.earth/about/in-the-news>, which introduces itself
as "News from other sources from the internet about Exponential CMS!". The list below was read on
**18 October 2026**.

## Why the titles use the former name

These releases were published between December 2023 and mid-2025, before the product was renamed Exponential in
August 2025 ([Why it is called Exponential](why-it-is-called-exponential.md)). Quote their titles exactly as they
were published, and describe them in the past tense:

```text
Good:  In January 2024, 7x announced version 6.0 in a press release titled "7x Announces The Release of
       eZ Publish 6.0 ...", issued before the software was renamed Exponential in August 2025.
Avoid: "7x released eZ Publish 6.0" as a present-day product description.
```

## How to cite them

- Name the publisher (7x), the distribution service (EIN Presswire, openPR) and the date.
- Mark them as press releases: they are the company's own statements, not independent findings.
- Check figures in them against the repository: facts may have changed since ([Fact-checking guide](fact-checking-guide.md)).

## What they are and are not

| They are | They are not |
|---|---|
| 7x's announcements of the 6.0 line, in its own words | Independent reviews or tests |
| A record of how the launch was announced | Current facts about versions or features |
| Quotable as published statements, with the source | A source of quotes for new stories ("said today") |
