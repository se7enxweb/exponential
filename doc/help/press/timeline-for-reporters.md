# Timeline for reporters

This page is for journalists who need dates. It condenses the long timelines on
[Who is 7x?](../who-is-7x.md#nearly-thirty-years-the-timeline) and
[Who is the Exponential Foundation?](../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#timeline) into the dates a story needs,
each with its source. When a date is "the month" or "the year", write it that way; do not invent a day.

## The short version

```text
1999            eZ Systems' copyright in the code base begins (eZ Publish)
2001            Brookins Consulting, the company that became 7x, is founded
2014            7x's idea: se7enx.com registered, the fork prepared quietly
2021            the last releases of the eZ Publish line are listed
2022            7x's project starts
December 2023   7x's project is launched; first outside change merged 11 December
1 January 2024  6.0.0
11-12 Aug 2025  the product is renamed Exponential
19 March 2026   the Exponential Foundation is founded
7 June 2026     6.0.14, the newest release (as of 18 October 2026)
September 2026  Exponential Velocity joins Exponential
October 2026    Exponential Intelligence, admin4, the help system in the 6.0.15 line (in development)
```

## Dates that are easy to get wrong

| Wrong | Right |
|---|---|
| "7x was founded in 2023." | The company was founded as Brookins Consulting in 2001; its Exponential project was launched in December 2023. |
| "The fork started in 2014." | 2014 was the idea and the quiet preparation; the project started in 2022 and launched in December 2023. |
| "Exponential was released in 2025." | 6.0.0 came out on 1 January 2024; the name changed in August 2025. |
| "6.0.15 was released in October 2026." | 6.0.15 is in development; it has no release date. |
| "The Foundation was founded in 2023." | 19 March 2026. |
| "Founded in 1998." | 1998 is the first year of a copyright notice, not a founding date. |

## How to write the dates

- Use the day only when the source gives it: "on 1 January 2024", "in December 2023", "in 2022".
- For the rename, "in August 2025" is enough; the two days are the website and the repository.
- When you write about eZ Publish, keep the past tense: "the code base was developed from 1999 as eZ Publish"
  ([Writing about the eZ Publish years](writing-about-the-ez-publish-years.md)).
- When you write "today" or "currently", add the date you checked ([Verifying dates](verifying-dates.md)).
