# Press contacts

This page is for journalists, editors and creators who need to reach 7x or the Exponential Foundation. It lists only
addresses that are published on the project's own websites or in its repository. Each was checked on
**18 October 2026** on the page named in the last column. The project does not publish a separate press office
address; use the general channels below and say in the subject that you are writing for publication.

## Which contact for which request

| You want | Use |
|---|---|
| An interview, a comment or a quote | info@se7enx.com or the contact form; see [Requesting an interview](requesting-an-interview.md) |
| Logo files, screenshots or videos from the press kit | support@exponential.earth |
| A statement from the Foundation | The Foundation's website; copy info@se7enx.com if you get no answer |
| To check a fact on a help page | The page's **Edit on GitHub**, or the [community issue tracker](https://github.com/se7enxweb/exponential-community/issues) |
| To report a software bug you found while testing | The [Exponential issue tracker](https://github.com/se7enxweb/exponential/issues), without personal data |
| To report a vulnerability | security@se7enx.com, never in public |

## What to put in a press request

```text
Subject: Press request - <outlet> - <topic> - deadline <date, time zone>

Hello,
I am <name>, <role> at <outlet> (<link>). I am preparing <article / video / podcast> about <topic>,
to be published on <date>.

I would like to <ask N questions by e-mail / have a 20-minute call / get a written statement> about:
1. <question>
2. <question>

Quotes will be attributed to <the person or organization you name>. I will <send quotes for a factual check /
not send the piece before publication> (my outlet's rule: <rule>).

Thank you,
<name, phone or e-mail for replies>
```

Give a realistic deadline. No response time for press requests is published; the only documented response time is
the first answer to a security report, "within one business day" (SECURITY.md).

## Security reports are different

If you, a source or a reader found a vulnerability, it goes privately to security@se7enx.com or through GitHub's
private vulnerability reporting, as [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/se7enxweb/exponential/blob/main/SECURITY.md)
describes. Do not publish details before a fix is released. Read [If you find a vulnerability](if-you-find-a-vulnerability.md)
and [Covering security fixes](covering-security-fixes.md).

## What is not published

- A dedicated press office e-mail or press phone line.
- A named press spokesperson. Ask whom to quote and attribute accordingly ([Attributing statements](attributing-statements.md)).
- An embargo policy ([Embargoes and timing](embargoes-and-timing.md)).
