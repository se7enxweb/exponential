# Handling comments and questions

This page is for creators whose videos, streams and posts attract questions. Your comment section becomes a small
support forum; this page helps you answer well without speaking for people you do not represent.

## Answer with a source

Link the help page or the file that answers the question. If you do not know, say so and point to a place that can
help ([Answering questions well](../advocates/answering-questions-well.md)).

## Where to send questions

| Question | Send to |
|---|---|
| "How do I ...?" | The help in the administration, then the [forums](https://share.exponential.earth/forums) or [Telegram](https://t.me/exponentialcms) |
| A software bug | The [Exponential issue tracker](https://github.com/se7enxweb/exponential/issues), without personal data |
| An idea for the project or website | The [community issue tracker](https://github.com/se7enxweb/exponential-community/issues) |
| Services, hosting, a quote for a project | 7x through <https://se7enx.com/> |
| The Foundation | <https://foundation.exponential.earth/> |

The full map is on [The community channels](../advocates/community-channels.md).

## Bug and security reports from viewers

- **Bugs**: thank the viewer and send them to the issue tracker; do not ask them to post logs with personal data in
  your comments.
- **Security problems**: if a viewer describes a possible vulnerability in a public comment, hide or remove the
  details if your platform allows it, and ask them to report privately to security@se7enx.com as
  [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/se7enxweb/exponential/blob/main/SECURITY.md) describes ([If you find a vulnerability](if-you-find-a-vulnerability.md)).

## Criticism and hostile comments

- Fair criticism deserves a fair answer; agree where the commenter is right.
- Correct factual errors with a source, once, calmly.
- Do not argue with hostility; the advocates' guide applies to creators too
  ([Handling hostile threads](../advocates/handling-hostile-threads.md)).
- Moderate according to your platform's rules: remove personal attacks and personal data.

## What not to promise

- Release dates, features or fixes: you do not decide them ([What the sources do not say](what-the-sources-do-not-say.md)).
- Support from 7x or the Foundation.
- That a bug "will be fixed soon".

## Reply templates

```text
Good question. The help explains it here: <link>. If that does not solve it, the forum is the best place:
https://share.exponential.earth/forums

That sounds like a bug. Could you report it at https://github.com/se7enxweb/exponential/issues with the steps
to reproduce (no passwords or personal data, please)?

Please do not post security details here. Report them privately as described in
https://github.com/se7enxweb/exponential/blob/main/SECURITY.md - thank you!

You are right, I got that wrong. The correct version: <statement> (<link>). I have added a correction.
```
