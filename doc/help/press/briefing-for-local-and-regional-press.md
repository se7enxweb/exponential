# Briefing for local and regional press

This page is for local newspapers, regional radio, city magazines and community newsletters. Local stories need a
place and people. The documentation gives a few places and very few people, so this page shows what you can say,
where to find a genuine local angle, and which details must not be assumed.

## The documented places

| Fact | Source |
|---|---|
| The Exponential Foundation is "a 501(c)(3) Non-Profit Corporation founded in Seattle, Washington, USA", founded on 19 March 2026 | [Who is the Exponential Foundation?, In short](../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#in-short) |
| 7x describes itself as "a responsive North American web design, support, development and website hosting company" | [Who is 7x?, In short](../who-is-7x.md#in-short) |
| The se7enxweb GitHub account bio gives its location as Seattle, Washington, USA | [Who is 7x?, In its own words](../who-is-7x.md#in-its-own-words) |
| Brookins Consulting, the earlier name of 7x, was "an eZ Publish partner in the USA" | [Who is 7x?, In short](../who-is-7x.md#in-short) |

The Foundation's postal address and registration numbers are published on its own websites; this help does not
repeat them ([What the sources do not say](../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#what-the-sources-do-not-say)).
If you print an address, take it from the Foundation's site and check it is meant for the public.

## Finding a genuine local angle

| Possible angle | How to make it real |
|---|---|
| A Seattle-founded nonprofit for an open source project | Documented; ask the Foundation for comment ([Requesting an interview](requesting-an-interview.md)) |
| A local organization that runs its site on Exponential | Find it yourself and get its consent to be named; the sources name no customers |
| A local developer or agency that contributes | Ask them directly; contributions are public in the Git history, but a person's name and location are theirs to share |
| A local meetup or class | The sources document no events; report one only if you can confirm it with its organizer ([Proposing a meetup](../advocates/proposing-a-meetup.md)) |
| A student project | See [Briefing for education and student media](briefing-for-education-and-student-media.md) |

## Explaining Exponential to a general audience

A version for readers who have never heard of a content management system:

```text
Exponential is free software that organizations use to build and run their websites: editors write and
publish pages in it without programming. It is open source, meaning anyone may use, study and change it.
A company, 7x, leads its development, and the Exponential Foundation, a nonprofit founded in Seattle in
March 2026, operates and funds the project. The software has been developed since 1999; it was first
called eZ Publish, a product its creator discontinued after almost 18 years, and it has been called
Exponential since August 2025.
```

Every fact in that text is sourced on [Who is 7x?](../who-is-7x.md#in-short) and
[Who is the Exponential Foundation?](../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#in-short); "developed since 1999" is the
line "Code base developed since 1999" of the [One-page fact sheet](../advocates/one-page-fact-sheet.md#the-fact-sheet),
which rests on the copyright years "1999 - 2014 eZ Systems AS" in [COPYRIGHT.md](https://github.com/se7enxweb/exponential/blob/main/COPYRIGHT.md).

## People, users and events

- **Do not name individuals** connected to 7x or the Foundation unless they agree; refer to "the founder of 7x" or
  to the organization ([Attributing statements](attributing-statements.md)).
- **Local users**: name an organization only with its consent, and only for what it confirms.
- **Events**: no conferences or meetups are documented ([Community places](../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#community-places)).
  If you cover a local event, say who organizes it; do not call it "official".
- **Photos**: ask before photographing people at events; see [Protecting people's privacy](../advocates/protecting-peoples-privacy.md).

## Local facts not to assume

| Do not assume | Why |
|---|---|
| That 7x has an office in your city | The sources give "North American" and the GitHub bio location only |
| That a local web agency is a partner | The sources name no partners in your area |
| That the Foundation employs local staff | Not documented |
| That a site "uses Exponential" because its code looks like it | Ask the site owner |
