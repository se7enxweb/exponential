# Story: AdminUI

This page is for journalists who cover editor experience. Exponential lets an installation choose among
administration designs; AdminUI is the one that comes from Netgen's work.

## Contents

1. [The story in one paragraph](#the-story-in-one-paragraph)

3. [What not to claim](#what-not-to-claim)

## The story in one paragraph

AdminUI is "A feature complete port of the legendary Netgen AdminUI Administration UI. This solution depends on
Exponential 6.0.15* or greater" (repository description of `se7enxweb/exp_adminui`). It is one of the administration
designs; admin4 is the one most installations use ([AdminUI](../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#adminui)). The
kernel requires the package since the 6.0.15 line ([The product family at a glance](../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#the-product-family-at-a-glance)).

## What not to claim

| Do not say | Say instead |
|---|---|
| "Netgen's new admin for Exponential" | A port by 7x of the Netgen AdminUI, credited ([Crediting Netgen, Mugo Web and CJW Network](crediting-other-projects.md)) |
| "Replaces admin4" | An alternative; admin4 remains the common one |
| "Works with 6.0.14" | It needs 6.0.15 or greater |
