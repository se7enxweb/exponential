# Logos and brand assets

This page is for picture editors, designers and creators who want to show the Exponential logo or other brand
assets. The only published rules are the four guidelines of the press kit. This page applies them and tells you
where the files are, and are not, available.

## The published guidelines

From the press kit v1.0.0, `README.md`, "Usage Guidelines" ([The press kit](the-press-kit.md#the-usage-guidelines-word-for-word)):

> - Logos and brand assets may be used for editorial, review, and partnership coverage.
> - Do not alter logo colors, proportions, or add effects.
> - Do not use assets to imply endorsement without written permission.
> - For commercial licensing inquiries, contact: support@exponential.earth

The documentation of the project sets no other logo rules: no clear space, minimum sizes or trademark policy
([Brand and naming rules](../business/brand-and-naming-rules.md#what-the-sources-do-not-decide)).

## Where the files are

| Source | State on 18 October 2026 |
|---|---|
| Press kit ZIP, folder `logos/` | Lists the planned files (vector logo in color, dark and white, app icons 64 to 512 pixels, a wordmark) but contains only a README |
| The project website | Shows the logo in its header; the press kit page offers the ZIP |
| The repository | The administration designs carry their own interface images; they are part of the GPL software, not a press logo set |

Until the logo files are in the kit, ask support@exponential.earth for them ([The press kit](the-press-kit.md#asking-for-material-that-is-missing)).
Do not lift a logo from a third-party site, and do not redraw one.

## Brand colors

The kit's `logos/LOGOS_README.txt` lists:

| Name in the kit | Value |
|---|---|
| Dark Background | `#2b2d42` |
| Accent / Red | `#e63946` |
| Foreground | `#0f0f0f` |
| Light Surface | `#f8f8f8` |

Use them for a graphic that accompanies coverage if you like; they do not make your graphic official.

## Do and don't

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Use the logo next to a story, review or video about Exponential | Use it on a product or service that is not about Exponential |
| Keep the files as received | Recolor, stretch, crop into a new shape, add shadows or effects |
| Credit: "Logo: Exponential project" where your outlet credits images | Present your thumbnail or graphic as made by the project |
| Make clear whose opinion a review is | Place the logo so that it suggests the project endorses your content |
| Show it beside other products' logos in a fair comparison | Make it smaller or worse-looking on purpose in a comparison |

## Partner marks are different

The partner program names its own marks (the Exponential Partner logo, the 7x partner logo, "Exponential Certified",
"Powered by Exponential") and rules for them ([Using partner logos and the certified mark](../partners/partner-logos-and-marks.md)).
A journalist may show a partner's mark when reporting on that partner; a creator may not use them to look like a partner.

## Uses that need permission

- Any commercial use beyond coverage: merchandise, ads for your own products, course covers sold for money.
- Co-branding: your logo and the Exponential logo combined.
- Anything that could imply endorsement, sponsorship or partnership.

Ask support@exponential.earth in writing and keep the answer ([Press contacts](press-contacts.md)).
