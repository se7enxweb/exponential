## The story in one paragraph

admin4 is "a complete administration design in the look of the setup wizard, with a light and a dark mode"
([The admin4 design](../../features/6.0/admin4-design.md)). It brings every template the administration needs into
one design, so it works without the older ones. Users switch between light, dark and "system", which follows the
operating system; the choice follows the user from browser to browser. Printed pages show only the content, in light
colors. It uses system fonts only, downloads none, and its styles live in shared stylesheets with color tokens that
tests check for contrast in light and dark mode.

## What not to claim

| Do not say | Say instead |
|---|---|
| "WCAG certified" | "Follows WCAG rules that tests check" |
| "The only admin" | One of several designs; others remain (classic admin, admin3, AdminUI) |
| "Released" | In the 6.0.15 line, in development |
