# Funding the work

This page is for supporters who can help with money. It explains why it matters and where it goes. The details of
each sponsor page are in the [Sponsors](../sponsors/sponsors-start-here.md) section.

## Why money matters

Exponential is free software, but its maintenance is work: security fixes, support for new PHP versions, releases,
documentation, translations, servers, extensions, themes and new product lines. The project says it plainly: "The
Exponential Open Source Project is funded by donations of time and resources by 7x (and Exponential Sponsors)"
(<https://exponential.earth/about/project>), and 7x writes: "We work very hard for our free software projects that do
not generate direct income" (<https://sponsor.se7enx.com/>).

A monthly sponsorship, even a small one, is the most useful form: it lets people plan.

## Two organizations

| | 7x | The Exponential Foundation |
|---|---|---|
| What | The company that leads development, support and hosting | The nonprofit that operates the project |
| GitHub Sponsors | <https://github.com/sponsors/se7enxweb> | <https://github.com/sponsors/exponentialcms> |
| Other ways | <https://sponsor.se7enx.com/>, PayPal, Patreon, Venmo, Cash App | "a sustaining subscription or one time donation" through <https://foundation.exponential.earth/> |

Both GitHub Sponsors pages were checked through the GitHub API on 18 October 2026. Which one to choose:
[Two organizations, two sponsor pages](../sponsors/two-organizations-two-sponsor-pages.md).

## What funding pays for

- The Foundation's sponsor listing: "We are focused on the development of Features, Documentation, Tutorials, How-To's,
  FAQs, Source Code, Plugins/Extensions/Bundles."
- 7x: "Every little contribution is very valuable and helps us develop more of features to give back away to the
  community for zero cost under a open source license" (<https://sponsor.se7enx.com/>).

More: [What sponsorship funds](../sponsors/what-sponsorship-funds.md).
