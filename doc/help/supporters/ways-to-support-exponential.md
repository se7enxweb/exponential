# Ways to support Exponential

This page is for anyone choosing how to help. Pick what fits your skills and time; all of it counts.

## How to choose

- **You use Exponential daily:** report bugs, answer questions, fix the docs you stumble over.
- **You speak another language:** translate; few contributions reach as many people.
- **You build with Exponential:** send your fixes back as pull requests.
- **You have less time than money:** sponsor monthly.
- **Your company depends on it:** combine sponsorship with a few hours a month of staff time
  ([Supporting Exponential as a company](supporting-as-a-company.md)).

## A note on money

Volunteer work is valuable, but maintenance (security fixes, PHP upgrades, releases, infrastructure) needs paid,
dependable time. If you can, combine your contribution with a sponsorship of 7x, the Exponential Foundation, or both
([Two organizations, two sponsor pages](../sponsors/two-organizations-two-sponsor-pages.md)).
