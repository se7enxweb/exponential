# Roll out a redesign step by step

No big-bang release.

*Life cycle: Redesign, example 34 of 34. Worked out on the Fit & Healthy site of Exponential 6.0.15+.*

## Contents

1. [In the Layouts editor](#in-the-layouts-editor)
2. [With templates](#with-templates)
3. [Finish](#finish)
4. [Which to choose](#which-to-choose)
5. [Check](#check)

## In the Layouts editor

1. Switch blocks to the new view types layout by layout; publish each.

## With templates

1. Ship new view types and overrides that only apply when chosen.

## Finish

1. When no block uses an old look, retire it ([Retire a view type after a redesign](retire-a-view-type.md)).

## Which to choose

Additive steps keep every step reversible.

## Check

- At every step the site is consistent.
