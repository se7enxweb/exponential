# The template part of a launch checklist

Nothing on the template side is forgotten.

*Life cycle: Launch, example 21 of 33. Worked out on the Fit & Healthy site of Exponential 6.0.15+.*

## Contents

1. [With templates](#with-templates)
2. [Which to choose](#which-to-choose)
3. [Check](#check)

## With templates

1. The design extension is first in `ActiveExtensions[]` and its design comes after the theme in `DesignExtensions[]`.
2. Its stylesheet is in the packed CSS.
3. Header and footer menu lists are final.
4. **Template & content** cleared after the last change.
5. No `<style>` or `style=""` in your templates.

## Which to choose

Run it after the last deploy.

## Check

- All five ticked.
