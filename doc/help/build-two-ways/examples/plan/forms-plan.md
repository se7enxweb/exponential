# Plan the forms

Know which forms exist and where they appear.

*Life cycle: Plan, example 22 of 35. Worked out on the Fit & Healthy site of Exponential 6.0.15+.*

## Contents

1. [In the Layouts editor](#in-the-layouts-editor)
2. [With templates](#with-templates)
3. [Which to choose](#which-to-choose)

## In the Layouts editor

1. A form page (Contact, node 185) gets its layout through a mapping; blocks such as a Map can surround it.

## With templates

1. The form itself is the class *Contact form* (`ng_contact_form`, fields such as `sender_email`, `sender_message`, `recipient`, `success_text`) and its full view template.

## Which to choose

Forms are content plus template; layouts only arrange around them.

## Check

- Each planned form has its class and its page.
