# Change the number of columns of a grid

Recipes in four columns instead of three.

*Life cycle: Maintain, example 17 of 35. Worked out on the Fit & Healthy site of Exponential 6.0.15+.*

## Contents

1. [In the Layouts editor](#in-the-layouts-editor)
2. [With templates](#with-templates)
3. [Which to choose](#which-to-choose)
4. [Check](#check)

## In the Layouts editor

1. Open **Layouts > Layouts** (or **Shared layouts**) and *the layout*.
2. Select the block; on the Design tab change **Number of columns**.
3. **Publish layout**.

## With templates

1. The column templates are `explayouts/block/list/grid/<n>_columns.tpl` of the media design.

## Which to choose

Design option.

## Check

- Four per row on a wide screen.
