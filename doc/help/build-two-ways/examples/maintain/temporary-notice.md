# Put up a temporary notice

"Closed for maintenance on Saturday" on the home page.

*Life cycle: Maintain, example 15 of 35. Worked out on the Fit & Healthy site of Exponential 6.0.15+.*

## Contents

1. [In the Layouts editor](#in-the-layouts-editor)
2. [With templates](#with-templates)
3. [Which to choose](#which-to-choose)
4. [Check](#check)

## In the Layouts editor

1. Open the home layout, drag **Alert** (group *Basic*) into `post_header`, fill **Message** and **Type**, **Publish layout**.
2. Afterwards select it, remove it with its `...` menu, **Publish layout**.

## With templates

1. A template block reading a "Notice" content item lets editors switch it by publishing or hiding that item.

## Which to choose

The Alert block for a one-off.

## Check

- The notice shows only during the event.
