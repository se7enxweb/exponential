# Rotate the featured item of a grid

A different lead story each week.

*Life cycle: Maintain, example 16 of 35. Worked out on the Fit & Healthy site of Exponential 6.0.15+.*

## Contents

1. [In the Layouts editor](#in-the-layouts-editor)
2. [With templates](#with-templates)
3. [Which to choose](#which-to-choose)
4. [Check](#check)

## In the Layouts editor

1. Select the List (view `grid_featured`), drag the new lead item to position 1, **Publish layout**.

## With templates

1. In a template the first fetched item is the lead; sort by priority and change priorities in the tree.

## Which to choose

Layouts editor.

## Check

- The new lead is large.
