# Show more items in a list

The latest-articles list shows eight instead of five.

*Life cycle: Maintain, example 4 of 35. Worked out on the Fit & Healthy site of Exponential 6.0.15+.*

## Contents

1. [In the Layouts editor](#in-the-layouts-editor)
2. [With templates](#with-templates)
3. [Which to choose](#which-to-choose)
4. [Check](#check)

## In the Layouts editor

1. Dynamic list: Content tab, **Offset & number of items**, set the number.
2. For many items, on the Design tab tick **Enable pagination**.
3. **Publish layout**.

## With templates

1. Change `limit` in the fetch; paging in templates uses `offset` from the view parameters.

## Which to choose

Editor settings for both.

## Check

- The list shows the new number; with pagination, the page navigation appears.
