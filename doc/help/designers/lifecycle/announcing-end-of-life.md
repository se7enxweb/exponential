# Announcing end of life

This page is for designers ending maintenance ([End of life](end-of-life.md)).

## What the announcement says

1. The theme and the date maintenance ends.
2. What stops: fixes, compatibility updates, support.
3. What stays: the repository (read-only), every release, the Packagist package, the GPL rights to fork.
4. The way forward: a successor, a new maintainer, or "fork it".
5. For paying customers: what happens to their contracts.

## A text to adapt

```markdown
## End of life: My Theme

My Theme will no longer be maintained after 31 March 2027. Release 1.4.9 is the last one.

All releases stay installable from Packagist and GitHub, and the theme remains free software under the GPL:
anyone may fork and continue it. Its successor is Harbor (https://github.com/yourvendor/harbor), which
covers the same content classes; see UPGRADE-TO-HARBOR.md.

Customers with a support contract: we will contact you about the remaining term.
```

## Where to post it

- The README (top) and a GitHub release or a pinned issue.
- The project page news on projects.exponential.earth.
- Your announcement thread on share.exponential.earth.
- Directly to paying customers, before the public announcement.
