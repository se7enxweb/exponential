# Taxes and invoices

This page is for designers who start selling. Tax rules differ by country and change; **this page gives no tax
advice**. It lists questions to settle with an accountant.

## Questions for your accountant

1. Do I need to register for sales tax or VAT, and from which turnover?
2. Is a theme download a digital good, and is support a service, in my country and my buyers' countries?
3. Which rate applies for buyers in other countries, and for companies versus consumers?
4. What must an invoice contain here (numbering, tax numbers, addresses, dates)?
5. How long must I keep invoices and records?
6. How are subscriptions and refunds booked?

## In your shop

Exponential's shop calculates VAT through a VAT handler and shipping through a shipping handler, both replaceable by
extensions; the RAD tools can generate handler extensions for both ([RAD tools](../../../features/6.0/rad-tools.md)).
Configure them to what your accountant tells you, and test with real cases before going live.

## Step by step

1. Ask the questions above before the first sale.
2. Configure your shop or invoicing tool accordingly.
3. Keep every invoice and refund in one place.
4. Review once a year, or when you start selling to a new country.
