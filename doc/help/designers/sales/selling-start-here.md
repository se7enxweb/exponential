# Selling themes and services: start here

This section is for designers and small studios who want to be paid for their work on Exponential themes. It describes
**options**: no price, model or channel here is a recommendation, and nothing here is legal or tax advice. For
contracts, taxes and licensing decisions, ask a lawyer and an accountant in your country.

## Three facts to start from

1. **Exponential is free software** under the GPL (version 2 or later), and so are the themes 7x publishes. Under
   the GPL you may charge money; you may not take away the freedoms of the people who receive the code
   ([The GPL and selling](gpl-and-selling.md)).
2. **Most money around GPL software is made with services**: custom design, setup, support, maintenance, training,
   hosting ([What you can sell around a theme](what-you-can-sell.md)).
3. **There is no official Exponential theme marketplace** at the time of writing. Themes are found on GitHub,
   Packagist, projects.exponential.earth, the community portal and the authors' own sites
   ([Where Exponential themes are found today](where-themes-are-found.md)).
