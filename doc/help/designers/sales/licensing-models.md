# Licensing models, as options

This page is for designers deciding under which terms to sell. Each model is an option; none is recommended here,
and the choice deserves a lawyer's review.

## The models

| Model | Code | Assets | Money comes from | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| All GPL | GPL | GPL or Creative Commons | Services, convenience, trust | Others may resell the same files |
| GPL code, separate asset license | GPL | Your own terms (for example: use in the buyer's sites, no redistribution) | Copies with assets, services | The asset license must be clear and the theme must work without them |
| GPL plus a service agreement | GPL | Any of the above | Support, updates delivery, maintenance | Your time is the product |
| Proprietary | Your own terms | Your own terms | Copies | Templates and PHP that run inside a GPL program may be considered a derivative work; get legal advice first |

The package and RAD wizards can record each choice: GPL family, Creative Commons, and `LicenseRef-Proprietary`
([Choosing a license in the package wizard](../extensions/package-licenses-in-the-wizard.md)).

## Questions that decide

1. Do I want others to improve and share my theme? (GPL)
2. Is my value in the files or in my time? (files: assets license or proprietary; time: services)
3. Can I enforce a restriction at all, and is it worth it?
4. What do my buyers' lawyers accept? (Many organizations prefer well-known licenses.)

## Step by step

1. Answer the four questions.
2. Pick a model and write it as one sentence for the product page.
3. Have a lawyer check the sentence and your terms.
4. Put the license files into the theme ([Licensing choices](../distribution/licensing-choices-overview.md)).
