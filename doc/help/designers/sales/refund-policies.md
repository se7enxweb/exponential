# Refund policies, as options

This page is for designers selling themes or subscriptions. Consumer law in many countries sets minimum rights for
online purchases, including digital content; check yours. **This is not legal advice.**

## Options

| Policy | Effect | Note |
|---|---|---|
| No refunds after download | Simple | May be invalid where consumer law grants a withdrawal right |
| Refund within a period, no questions | Builds trust | With GPL code the buyer keeps the files; accept that |
| Refund if a reported defect cannot be fixed | Fair to both | Define "defect" |
| Pro-rata refund for unused subscription time | Fair for long subscriptions | More bookkeeping |
| No refund for services already delivered | Common | Say so before the work starts |

## Step by step

1. Check the minimum rules for your buyers' countries (or ask your lawyer).
2. Choose one policy per product and service.
3. Publish it before the purchase, linked from checkout.
4. Record each refund and why; patterns show where the product misleads.
