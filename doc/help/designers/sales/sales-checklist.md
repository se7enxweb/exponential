# Checklist: ready to sell

This page is for designers about to start selling. Tick each item; the linked page explains it.

## Product

- [ ] The theme passes [Checklist: the theme extension is ready](../extensions/checklist-theme-extension-ready.md).
- [ ] It is published or deliverable ([Checklist: before you publish](../distribution/distribution-checklist.md)).
- [ ] A demo and screenshots exist ([Demos that sell](demos-that-sell.md)).

## Offer and license

- [ ] What buyers receive is written down ([What buyers receive](what-buyers-receive.md)).
- [ ] The licensing model is chosen and stated ([Licensing models](licensing-models.md)).
- [ ] Every asset may be sold or shared ([Images and fonts under separate licenses](assets-under-separate-licenses.md)).
- [ ] The name is checked ([Names and trademarks](names-and-trademarks.md)).

## Prices and paperwork

- [ ] The pricing model is chosen ([Pricing models](pricing-models.md)).
- [ ] Terms reviewed by a lawyer ([Contracts and terms](contracts-and-terms.md)).
- [ ] Warranty and refund policy published ([Warranty and liability](warranty-and-liability.md), [Refund policies](refund-policies.md)).
- [ ] Tax questions answered by an accountant ([Taxes and invoices](taxes-and-invoices.md)).

## Delivery and support

- [ ] Delivery tested end to end ([Delivering updates](delivering-updates-to-customers.md)).
- [ ] Support defined, with times you can keep ([Support offerings](support-offerings.md)).
- [ ] A security contact published ([A security policy for a theme](../distribution/security-policy-for-a-theme.md)).

## Privacy

- [ ] A privacy notice and data handling routine ([Customer data and privacy](customer-data-and-privacy.md)).
