# Online stores and small shops

This section is for business owners, shop managers and decision makers who want to sell products online with
Exponential. It explains, in plain language, what the shop built into Exponential does, what you need to decide before
you open a store, how orders, payments and taxes work, and how to keep the store running day after day. Every page
links to the technical pages for whoever sets the store up.

## What this section covers

Exponential ships a shop in its kernel. The project README lists "Advanced e-Commerce Stores (Built in)" among the
main features (README.md). Products are ordinary content with a price field; visitors add them
to a basket, check out, pay through a payment gateway, and the shop staff handle the orders in the **Store** tab of
the administration, which opens the Store dashboard
([Store dashboard and order statuses](../../../features/6.0/store-dashboard.md)).

The pages of this section cover:

- planning a store and deciding what to sell, where and to whom;
- products, prices, currencies, VAT and the countries you sell to;
- shipping, the basket, checkout and customer accounts;
- orders, order statuses, receipts and confirmation e-mail;
- payments: PayPal, card payments and what card security means for a small business;
- discounts, wishlists and digital products;
- being found, promoting the store, staying fast at peak times and staying secure;
- launching, running the store every day, and common questions.

## What you will not find here

These pages explain and summarize; they do not repeat the configuration steps. Settings, templates and code are on
the technical pages they link to, mostly under
[website creators](../../creators/website-creators/website-creators-start-here.md) and the feature pages in
`doc/features/6.0/`. Tax and legal questions are for your tax adviser and lawyer; these pages only describe what the
software does.
