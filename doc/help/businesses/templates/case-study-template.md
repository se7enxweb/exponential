# Case study template

This page is for businesses that want to tell the story of their own Exponential project, and for partners or
agencies who write one about a customer. It gives a template with placeholders, a fact sheet and an approval record.
The full process (choosing a candidate, consent, interview, checking, approval, publishing and withdrawal) is on
[Case studies and content marketing](../../business/case-studies-and-content-marketing.md); follow it.

> **Warning**
> Write only real, approved facts. Never invent a customer, a figure, a result or a quote. Name a customer, a
> person, a logo or a screenshot only with the customer's written consent, obtained **before** the interview, and
> publish only after their written approval of the final text. If a fact cannot be checked, leave it out.

## Rules before you start

- [ ] Written consent for the case study, and for each item: name, logo, quotes, screenshots
      ([Step 2: ask for consent](../../business/case-studies-and-content-marketing.md#step-2-ask-for-consent-before-you-write)).
- [ ] People quoted have agreed to be named, or are quoted by role only.
- [ ] Results come only from the customer's own measurements, with source and period.
- [ ] Claims about Exponential match its documentation; comparisons with named products only as
      [Comparing Exponential factually](../../business/comparing-exponential-factually.md) allows.
- [ ] Product name written "Exponential"; the old name only as history, in the past tense
      ([Brand and naming rules](../../business/brand-and-naming-rules.md)).
- [ ] Consent and personal data stored where only the responsible people can read them.

## The template

```text
TITLE: <Organization> <does what> with Exponential
       (example form: "<Organization> runs its <store | intranet | member site> on Exponential")

SUMMARY (2-3 sentences)
<Who the organization is>. <What they needed>. <What they built, and the one verified result>.

AT A GLANCE
Organization:       <name, only with consent | "a <sector> organization in <region>">
Sector:             <sector>
Site or application:<store | intranet | extranet | member site | newsletter | multisite | API backend>
Live since:         <month year>
Built by:           <in-house team | partner name, with consent>
Hosting:            <model, engine, database - e.g. "VPS, Exponential Velocity, SQLite 3">
Main features used: <shop | multisite | languages | workflows | newsletter | REST API | layouts | ...>

THE SITUATION BEFORE
<What did not work, in the customer's words or a faithful summary. No figures without a source.>

WHY EXPONENTIAL
<The customer's reasons. Facts about the product only as stated in its documentation, e.g. GPL license with
no license fee (README "License"), SQLite needs no database server (README "Requirements").>

WHAT WAS BUILT
<Content model, integrations, designs, languages, number of sites - facts the customer approved.>
<Features with a link to the help page that describes each.>

HOW THE PROJECT WENT
<Phases, timeline, what was hard and how it was solved.>

RESULTS
<Result 1>  - source: <system or document>, period: <from-to>
<Result 2>  - source: <system or document>, period: <from-to>
(Leave this section short or out if no measured results exist. Do not estimate.)

IN THEIR WORDS
"<Approved quote>" - <Name, role | role only>, <organization | sector>

WHAT IS NEXT
<Plans the customer is happy to make public.>

ABOUT
<One paragraph about the organization, approved by them.>
<One paragraph about the partner, if any.>

Published <date>. Approved by <name, role> on <date>. Withdrawal: <contact>.
```

## The fact sheet

Keep it with the draft; it is not published.

```text
FACT SHEET - <case study title>
#  | Fact                         | Source (document, system, person)  | Period    | Approved to publish?
---|------------------------------|------------------------------------|-----------|---------------------
1  | <fact>                       | <source>                           | <period>  | yes / no / ask
2  | <fact>                       | <source>                           | <period>  | yes / no / ask
3  | <product fact>               | <help page or README section>      | -         | yes
```

## The approval record

```text
APPROVAL RECORD - <case study title>
Consent to write requested: <date>   Granted by: <name, role>   Form: <e-mail | signed>
Items consented: name [ ] logo [ ] quotes [ ] screenshots [ ] people named [ ]
Draft sent: <date>   Changes requested: <summary>
Final text approved: <date>   By: <name, role>   Stored at: <location, restricted>
Published at: <URL or document>   On: <date>
Review due: <date>   Withdrawn on: <date, if ever>
```

## Good and weak sentences

| Weak | Why | Better |
|---|---|---|
| "Sales doubled thanks to Exponential." | unsourced, causal claim | "Online orders rose from <x> to <y> between <period> and <period>, according to <system>." |
| "The fastest CMS on the market." | unverifiable comparison | "Cached pages are served by Velocity without a worker, as the project documents (README)." |
| "Everyone loves it." | invented sentiment | an approved quote with name or role |
| "Zero downtime." | absolute claim | "<n> hours of planned maintenance in <period>, recorded in <monitoring system>." |

More examples: [Case study template for partners](../../partners/case-study-template.md#good-and-weak-sentences).
