# Costs and return on investment

This section is for business owners, managers and the people who prepare a budget or a business case for a website
application on Exponential. It answers three questions: what does it cost to own and run, what do we not pay for,
and how do we show that the money comes back? Every page explains the method in plain words and links to the
technical pages for whoever does the work.

> **Note**
> The project publishes no prices, and these pages invent none. Where a page shows a calculation, every input is
> marked **Example assumption**, uses round numbers in a neutral "currency unit", and is there only to show the
> arithmetic. Replace each one with your own quotes, salaries and sales figures before you use the result.

## What this section covers

- **Total cost of ownership (TCO)**: every cost over the life of a site, not only the first invoice.
- **What you do not pay for**: the license fee, per-user fees and separate products that Exponential already
  includes.
- **Return on investment (ROI)**: what stability, fewer incidents and lower maintenance are worth, in a form a
  finance team can check.
- **Three worked examples**: a small store, an intranet and a nonprofit site, each step by step.
- **Worksheets**: the cost of downtime, and a budget for upgrades and maintenance.
- **Hosting cost drivers**: which technical choices make hosting cheaper or dearer.

## A suggested order

1. Start with [Total cost of ownership](total-cost-of-ownership.md) to see the full list of cost lines.
2. Read [What you do not pay for](what-you-do-not-pay-for.md) and strike those lines from your list.
3. Fill in the [Cost of downtime worksheet](cost-of-downtime-worksheet.md) with your own figures.
4. Use [Return on investment of stability](roi-of-stability.md) to build the business case.
5. Pick the worked example closest to your organization and copy its structure.
6. Finish with [Hosting cost drivers](hosting-cost-drivers.md) and
   [Budgeting for upgrades and maintenance](budgeting-for-upgrades-and-maintenance.md) for the running budget.

A blank form for the calculation is in the templates section:
[ROI worksheet template](../templates/roi-worksheet-template.md).
