# A 90-day advocate plan

This page is for advocates who finished their [first week](your-first-week-as-an-advocate.md) and want a plan for the
quarter. It answers: what should I aim for in 30, 60 and 90 days, and how do I check that it worked? The plan is a
lighter, advocate-sized version of the 30/60/90-day plan for community leaders in
[Becoming a leader](../leadership/becoming-a-community-leader.md#the-306090-day-plan).

## Principles

- **Small and regular beats big and rare.** One answer a week for 13 weeks helps more people than one long post.
- **Every goal leaves a link.** A forum answer, a pull request, a post. If it has no link, it is hard to count.
- **Goals are yours.** The numbers below are examples; choose what fits your time.
- **Quality first.** One correct answer with a source is better than five guesses
  ([Honesty and accuracy](honesty-and-accuracy.md)).

## Days 1 to 30: learn and help

| Goal | Example target | How |
|---|---|---|
| Know the facts | Read every talking-point page once | [Talking points: the core set](talking-points-core-set.md) |
| Answer questions | 4 answers, one per week | [Answering questions well](answering-questions-well.md) |
| Improve the help | 1 pull request | [Improving the help](improving-the-help.md) |
| Know the channels | Visit each documented channel | [The community channels](community-channels.md) |
| Practice the story | Tell it to two people | [The story in two minutes](the-story-of-exponential-in-two-minutes.md) |

## Days 31 to 60: create

| Goal | Example target | How |
|---|---|---|
| Write | 1 blog post or tutorial | [Writing a blog post](writing-a-blog-post.md), [Writing a tutorial that works](writing-a-tutorial-that-works.md) |
| Show | 1 demo or lightning talk | [Lightning talk script](lightning-talk-script.md) |
| Report | 1 good bug report, or confirm 2 open issues | [Reporting bugs well](reporting-bugs-well.md), [Reproducing and confirming issues](reproducing-and-confirming-issues.md) |
| Translate or review | 50 strings, or one screen reviewed | [Helping with translations](helping-with-translations.md) |
| Keep answering | 4 more answers | as before |

## Days 61 to 90: multiply

| Goal | Example target | How |
|---|---|---|
| Welcome | Greet and guide 2 newcomers | [Welcoming newcomers](welcoming-newcomers.md) |
| Mentor | Guide 1 first contribution | [Mentoring a first contribution](mentoring-a-first-contribution.md) |
| Organize | Propose 1 meetup or sprint | [Proposing a meetup](proposing-a-meetup.md), [Proposing a contribution sprint](proposing-a-contribution-sprint.md) |
| Support | Tell your employer or clients about the sponsor pages | [Advocating for sustainable funding](advocating-for-sustainable-funding.md) |
| Review | Write your 90-day review | [below](#the-review-at-day-90) |

## Choose your track

Not everyone wants every goal. Pick a track and swap goals accordingly.

| Track | Replace | With |
|---|---|---|
| Writer | The demo | A second tutorial and an eZpedia article ([Contributing to eZpedia](contributing-to-ezpedia.md)) |
| Speaker | The blog post | A 20-minute talk ([Twenty-minute talk slide outline](twenty-minute-talk-slide-outline.md)) |
| Translator | The blog post and the demo | 200 strings and a review call for your language |
| Developer | The translation | A fix as a pull request ([Develop with Git](../develop-with-git.md#contribute-a-change-with-a-pull-request)) |
| Designer | The translation | An accessibility review of a theme you use ([Advocating for accessibility](advocating-for-accessibility.md)) |

## The review at day 90

Answer these questions honestly, in writing:

1. What did I do? (List the links from your [contribution log](tracking-your-contributions.md).)
2. What helped people most? (Replies, thanks, questions that stopped repeating.)
3. What did not work, and why?
4. Was every claim I made sourced? Did I correct anything I got wrong?
5. What do I want to do next quarter, and what will I stop?

Share a short version in the forums if you like: it shows newcomers what an advocate does
([Measuring advocacy honestly](measuring-advocacy-honestly.md)).

```text
  [ ] Post or tutorial:                           <link>

NEXT QUARTER
  1. <goal>
  2. <goal>
  3. <goal>
```
