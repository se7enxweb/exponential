# Talking points for editors

This page is for advocates who talk to editors: people who write, translate and publish content every day. Editors
care about safety (can I break something?), speed (how many clicks?) and help (who do I ask?). Every point below has
the screen to show and the source.

## What editors want to know

- "Can I undo a mistake?"
- "Can I see the page before it goes live?"
- "Can I arrange a page without a developer?"
- "Can I work in my language?"
- "Where do I get help?"

## Show it in two minutes

On an installation you control ([Live demo safety](live-demo-safety.md)):

1. Open an item, press **Edit**, change the short name.
2. Press **Preview**, then the phone width.
3. Go back, press **Send for publishing**.
4. Open **Manage versions** and point at the old version.
5. Press the "?" in the header.

The click-by-click version with timings is in [Demo scripts, The 5-minute demo](../business/demo-scripts.md#the-5-minute-demo).

## Questions editors ask

| Question | Answer | Source |
|---|---|---|
| "Does someone have to approve my work?" | Only if your site uses an approval workflow; then the version waits under **My pending items** | [Workflows](../../guides/workflows.md), [My drafts and my pending items](../../guides/drafts-and-pending.md) |
| "Where are my unfinished drafts?" | **Dashboard > My drafts** | [My drafts and my pending items](../../guides/drafts-and-pending.md) |
| "Can I upload many images at once?" | Yes, one file or many | [Images, media and uploads](../media-and-uploads.md) |
| "Why can't I see a button my colleague sees?" | Roles decide what you may do; a button you may not use is grayed out or hidden | [Help for editors and users](../README.md) |
| "Can I turn on two-step sign-in?" | Yes, with an authenticator app or an e-mail code | [Your account and signing in](../accounts-and-sign-in.md) |

## Do not say

- "You cannot break anything." Say: "Every change is a draft first, and older versions are kept."
- "The AI writes your content." Say: "It drafts; you decide and publish."
- "Every language is complete." Say: "Ten administration translations are complete; others are partial."
  ([Languages in Exponential](../../languages.md#2-support-levels))
