# Running an online meetup

This page is for advocates who organize an online meetup: it reaches people everywhere, costs nothing and is easy to
repeat. It is your event, not an official one ([Proposing a meetup](proposing-a-meetup.md)).

## Choose the setup

- **Tool**: any video tool participants can join from a browser; prefer one with captions.
- **Time**: name the time in UTC and two local zones; rotate times across meetups so different regions can join.
- **Length**: 60 to 90 minutes.
- **Link**: send it to registered people, or post it shortly before; this limits uninvited disruption.

## Roles

| Role | Does |
|---|---|
| Host | Welcomes, keeps time, introduces speakers |
| Moderator | Watches the chat, collects questions, removes disruptive participants |
| Speaker(s) | Short talks or demos ([Lightning talk script](lightning-talk-script.md)) |
| Note taker | Writes a summary with links for the forum post |

One person can hold two roles in a small meetup; not host and speaker at once if possible.

## The agenda

```text
ONLINE EXPONENTIAL MEETUP (informal, organized by <you>)      <date> <time UTC>
0:00  Welcome, ground rules (be kind, no recording without consent, questions in chat)
0:05  Round of names and what people use Exponential for (optional, skip if large)
0:15  Talk 1: <title>, 15 min + questions
0:35  Talk 2 or live demo: <title>, 15 min + questions
0:55  Open help desk: bring your questions
1:15  Next meetup, thanks
```

## Accessibility

- Turn on captions; speakers speak clearly and describe what they show.
- Share slides as a file beforehand or afterwards ([Accessible slides and visuals](accessible-slides-and-visuals.md)).
- Read questions from the chat aloud before answering.
- Keep breaks if longer than an hour.

## Recording and privacy

- Announce recording before it starts; let people turn off camera and microphone; record only the talks if possible.
- Do not publish participants' faces, names or chat without consent ([Protecting people's privacy](protecting-peoples-privacy.md)).
- Speakers share one window, not their whole screen ([Live demo safety](live-demo-safety.md)).

## Afterwards

Post a summary with slides and links in the forums, answer open questions with sources, delete registration data,
and fix the date of the next one. Checklists: [Event checklists](event-checklists.md).
