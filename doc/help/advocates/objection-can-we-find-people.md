# Objection: "Can we find people who know it?"

This page is for advocates who hear "nobody knows this system", "we cannot hire for it" or "the community is tiny".
It is the objection to answer most carefully, because **the sources do not count developers, agencies or users**.
Never invent a number. Answer with what is documented, and then do something about it: advocates are how the pool
grows.

## What you must not say

- "There are thousands of Exponential developers."
- "You will find people easily."
- "The community is huge."
- Any number about the job market.

Turn the doubt into a planning step instead: "Put onboarding and training into the project plan"
([Planning an Exponential project](../leadership/planning-an-exponential-project.md)).

## Reply templates

```text
HONEST AND HELPFUL
I can't give you a headcount - nobody publishes one. What I can say: the stack is plain PHP 8 with
Composer, so any PHP developer can learn it, and the learning material is free and large (the help
in the admin, the install book, eZpedia, the training books). There's a step-by-step path from user
to developer here: <link to Develop with Git>. Budget onboarding time and you're fine.

FOR A TEAM LEAD
Two options that work: train a PHP developer you already have (a week with the install book and the
RAD tools goes far), or hire a provider who knows it for the first project and pair your people with them.

COMMUNITY SIZE
It's not the biggest community, and I won't pretend it is. It is an open one: questions get answered
in the forums and on Telegram, and every pull request is merged.
```
