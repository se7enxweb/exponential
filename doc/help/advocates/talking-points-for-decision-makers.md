# Talking points for decision makers

This page is for advocates who talk to people who decide: managers, owners, board members, IT leads. They think in
risk, cost and time. As an advocate you are not selling; you are giving a peer's honest view, with sources. For
buyer-facing material, the sales pages go deeper: [Handling objections](../business/objection-handling.md) and
[Positioning and value proposition](../business/positioning-and-value-proposition.md).

## What decision makers want to know

- "Will it still be here in five or ten years?"
- "Are we locked in to one vendor?"
- "Who supports us when something goes wrong?"
- "Is it secure enough for our organization?"
- "What will it cost?"

## The honest limits

Decision makers notice overstatement. Say these yourself before they ask:

- **Nobody can promise the future.** Say what the record and the structure are, not what will happen.
- **The sources do not count users or developers.** Plan onboarding and training instead of assuming a job market
  ([Skills](../business/objection-handling.md#skills-can-we-find-people-who-know-it)).
- **The Foundation's governance and budget are not documented** in the sources
  ([What the sources do not say](../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#what-the-sources-do-not-say)).
- **There is no documented service level.** Service levels belong in a contract with a provider.
- **Exponential is not the right fit for everything.** See
  [Where Exponential is not the right fit](../business/comparing-exponential-factually.md#where-exponential-is-not-the-right-fit).

## Questions to ask them back

Good questions show you care about their success, not about winning:

1. "What content do you manage today, and in how many languages and sites?"
2. "Who will edit, and how many people?"
3. "Do you have PHP skills in house, or will a provider build it?"
4. "Which database and hosting do you already run?"
5. "What are your requirements for accessibility, security and audit?"

Send their answers to the right pages: [Feature highlights mapped to customer needs](../business/feature-highlights-for-customers.md).

## A three-sentence summary

> "Exponential is a mature, free PHP CMS with a fully customizable content model, multisite and many languages. A
> company and a nonprofit stand behind it, it has had fifteen releases since January 2024, and you are never locked
> in. You will need PHP skills or a provider, and you should budget for onboarding."
