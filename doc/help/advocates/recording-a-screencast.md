# Recording a screencast

This page is for advocates who want to make a video: a two-minute "how to" or a longer walkthrough. Videos reach people
who do not read documentation, but they age quickly and leak secrets easily. Plan short, record clean, caption
everything, and say which version you show.

## Choose the scope

One video, one task. Good topics are tasks the help already describes, so viewers can read on:

| Topic | Length | Help page to link |
|---|---|---|
| Install Exponential in one command | 3 min | [Install in one command](../../features/6.0/install-in-one-command.md) |

## Write a script

```text
SCREENCAST SCRIPT                          Version shown: Exponential <6.0.x>   Design: <admin4>
Title:      <task, as the viewer would search it>
Goal:       After this video you can <task>.
Help page:  <link>

0:00  Intro: "In this video: <task>. I'm <name>, a volunteer." (disclosure)
0:10  Step 1: <click> - say: <sentence>
0:40  Step 2: ...
2:30  Result: show what changed
2:45  Outro: "The help page is linked below. Questions: forums or Telegram."
```

Use the button and menu names exactly as on screen, in **bold** in the transcript
([Brand and naming rules, Language and spelling](../business/brand-and-naming-rules.md#language-and-spelling)).

## Record clean

- Follow [Live demo safety](live-demo-safety.md): a fresh installation, invented content, no keys or passwords on
  screen, notifications off.
- Record one window at a readable zoom; viewers watch on phones.
- Move the mouse slowly; pause on each click.
- Record the voice separately if you can; it is easier to fix.
- Watch the whole recording before publishing, frame by frame where a sign-in or an error page appears.

## Make it accessible

- **Captions**: correct captions, not only automatic ones; product names spelled right.
- **Transcript**: post it with the video; it also makes the video searchable.
- **Describe actions aloud**: "I press **Send for publishing**", not "I click here".
- **No information only in color or only on screen**: say it too.
- **No flashing** content.

More: [Accessible slides and visuals](accessible-slides-and-visuals.md).

## Publish and license

- Title with the task; first line of the description: "Shows Exponential <version>".
- Link the help page and the community channels in the description.
- Choose a license for your video and state it. If you show or read help pages, remember they are under the GNU FDL;
  quoting with a link is the simple way ([Citing sources and licensing your writing](citing-sources-and-licensing-your-writing.md)).
- Disclose if anyone paid for the video ([Disclosure and conflicts of interest](disclosure-and-conflicts-of-interest.md)).
- Share it in the forums and on Share ([Publishing on Share and Projects](publishing-on-share-and-projects.md)).

## Keep it correct

- After each release, check the changelog for changes to the screens you show
  ([6.0.15 changelog](../../changelogs/6.0/6.0.15.md)).
- When the video is wrong, add a pinned comment with the correction, then re-record.
- When it is obsolete, say so in the title or description, or unlist it.
