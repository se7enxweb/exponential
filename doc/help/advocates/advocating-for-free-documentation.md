# Advocating for free documentation

This page is for advocates who believe good documentation should belong to everyone. Exponential's documentation is
free: "Copyright (C) 1998 - 2026 7x & Exponential Foundation and the Exponential contributors", licensed under the GNU
Free Documentation License 1.3 or later ([The license of this documentation](../../README-LICENSE.md)).

## Why it matters

- Knowledge in a chat disappears; knowledge in the documentation stays.
- Free documentation can be translated, printed and taught without asking.
- Improvements are accepted under the same license, so they stay free for everyone
  ([Contributing to the documentation](../../README-LICENSE.md#contributing-to-the-documentation)).

## What readers may do

From [Free documentation](../free-documentation.md#what-you-can-do): read and study it, copy and share it, translate it,
adapt and improve it and share the result, combine it with other works, and cite it, under the license's conditions.
Tell people this; many assume documentation is "all rights reserved".

## Bringing knowledge into the documentation

| Knowledge lives in | Move it to |
|---|---|
| A forum answer given three times | The help page that should have answered it ([Improving the help](improving-the-help.md)) |
| A trick or workaround | eZpedia ([Contributing to eZpedia](contributing-to-ezpedia.md)) |
| A chat explanation | A forum post, then the help |
| Your tutorial | Your blog under the GNU FDL, or a help page |
| A talk | Slides and notes published with a license ([Citing sources and licensing your writing](citing-sources-and-licensing-your-writing.md)) |

## Encouraging translations and teaching

- Invite speakers of other languages to translate help pages ([Helping with translations](helping-with-translations.md)).
- Tell teachers they may use the pages in class ([Presenting at a user group or class](presenting-at-a-user-group-or-class.md)).
- Point to the older free material too: the training books mirror and "Learning eZ Publish" under the GFDL
  ([Community places](../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#community-places)).
