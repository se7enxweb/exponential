# Objection: "Why not another CMS?"

This page is for advocates who are asked "why not just use a more popular CMS?" The fair answer is never "because the
other one is bad". It is "here is what Exponential does, here is how you check it, and here is where it is not the
right fit". The rules come from [Comparing Exponential factually](../business/comparing-exponential-factually.md#the-rules-of-a-fair-comparison).

## The rules

1. **Compare properties, not reputations.** A license file, a release history, a list of databases.
2. **Every claim has a source.**
3. **Never describe another product.** What another product does is for the person to find in that product's own
   documentation.
4. **Date your figures.**
5. **Volunteer the limits.**

## Ask about needs first

The best answer starts with questions:

1. "What content do you manage, and does it have a structure (products, events, people) or is it mostly pages?"
2. "How many sites and languages?"
3. "Who edits, and do they need approvals and versions?"
4. "What skills and hosting do you have?"
5. "How long must the site live?"

Then match the answers to Exponential's documented strengths, or say honestly that it is not the best fit.

## Where it is not the right fit

Say these yourself. Each is documented in
[Where Exponential is not the right fit](../business/comparing-exponential-factually.md#where-exponential-is-not-the-right-fit):

- Microsoft SQL Server is required: no driver in 6.0.x.
- A Symfony-only project without the legacy kernel: then Exponential Platform DXP is the family member.
- Shared hosting that forbids long-running processes: Velocity usually cannot run there.
- More than 62 content languages in one installation.
- A fully translated administration in a language that is not complete today.
- A published SLA document: the project publishes none; contracts come from providers.

## Reply templates

```text
NEUTRAL
Depends what you need. Exponential is strongest when content has structure (many content types),
when you run several sites or languages from one install, and when editors need versions and approvals.
If you mostly need a simple blog, other tools may suit you better. What are you building?

AFTER THEY DESCRIBE THEIR NEEDS
For <their need>, Exponential has <property> (<link>). You can try it in a few minutes on SQLite:
<link to Install in one command>. Compare it with the other options on the same checklist.

WHEN IT IS NOT A FIT
Honestly, for <their need> Exponential isn't the best fit: <documented limit> (<link>). Good luck with
the project - and if your needs change, the forums are open.
```

## Do not say

- Anything negative about another product, its company or its users.
- "Exponential does everything X does."
- "X is insecure / slow / dying."
- Popularity figures about any product.
