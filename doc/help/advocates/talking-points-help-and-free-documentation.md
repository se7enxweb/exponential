# Talking points: the help and free documentation

This page is for advocates who want to show that Exponential takes documentation seriously. The help you are reading
is the documentation of the repository, shown in the administration, and it is free documentation. Both facts are
good stories.

## Show it in one minute

1. Press the "?" in the header of any administration page: the menu offers help for this page.
2. Open the Help center; press `/` and type a word.
3. Open a page and press **Download as PDF**.
4. Point at **Edit on GitHub**.

## Why free documentation matters

- **People can learn without asking permission.** A teacher can print a page for a class; a company can put it on
  its intranet ([Free documentation, What you can do](../free-documentation.md#what-you-can-do)).
- **Translations can happen anywhere.** A community can translate the help into its language and share it.
- **Fixes come back.** A change is accepted under the same license, so the improvement stays free for everyone
  ([Contributing to the documentation](../../README-LICENSE.md#contributing-to-the-documentation)).

## Do not say

- "The documentation is complete." Nobody's is. Say "it is in the repository, and you can improve it".
- "You can do whatever you want with it." Say "under the GNU FDL's conditions: keep the notice and the license".
- "The help is public on the internet." It is shown to signed-in users with the `exphelp/read` policy; the files
  are public in the repository.
