# Growing the community

This page is for people in sales and marketing, and for project managers, who want more people to use, talk about
and contribute to Exponential. It answers: which channels exist, how do people move from first contact to
contributor, what can your organization do each month, and how do you see whether it works? It uses only the
channels the documentation names; it does not invent programs, events or figures.

> **Note**
> A community grows when people get help, see their contributions accepted and find others like them. Your job is to
> lead people to the right place at the right moment, not to build a parallel channel.

## The documented channels

These are the community places that [Who is 7x?](../who-is-7x.md#the-community) and
[Who is the Exponential Foundation?](../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#community-places) list. Do not send
people anywhere else in the project's name.

| Channel | Address | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| Share | [share.exponential.earth](https://share.exponential.earth/) | The community portal: forums, blogs, tutorials and projects (extensions) |
| Forums | [share.exponential.earth/forums](https://share.exponential.earth/forums) | Questions and discussions; a forum for Exponential Platform too |
| Telegram | `t.me/exponentialcms` | "Share Exponential! Telegram Community Support Chat" |
| eZpedia | [ezpedia.exponential.earth](https://ezpedia.exponential.earth/) | An encyclopedia "that anyone can edit", since 2006, under the GNU FDL |
| Documentation | [doc.exponential.earth](https://doc.exponential.earth/) | "The Official Documentation For Exponential 6 CMS" |
| The help in the product | `/exphelp/dashboard` | This help: free documentation that ships with every installation |
| Software issues | [github.com/se7enxweb/exponential/issues](https://github.com/se7enxweb/exponential/issues) | Bugs and improvements of the software |
| Velocity issues | [github.com/se7enxweb/exponential-velocity/issues](https://github.com/se7enxweb/exponential-velocity/issues) | Bugs of the Velocity server |
| Community issues | [github.com/se7enxweb/exponential-community/issues](https://github.com/se7enxweb/exponential-community/issues) | The project, website, ecosystem, anything that is not a software bug |
| Discord and Slack | named on `exponential.earth/about/project` | Named among the support channels |
| Security reports | private e-mail, as described in `SECURITY.md` | Never in public channels |
| Sponsors | `github.com/sponsors/exponentialcms` (Foundation), `github.com/sponsors/se7enxweb` (7x) | Funding |

Sources: the two pages above and [When something goes wrong](../when-something-goes-wrong.md).

No conferences or meetups are documented in the sources. If your organization holds an event about Exponential,
present it as your event.

## The contributor funnel

People move through stages. At each stage there is one documented next step, and something you can do to make it
easier.

| Stage | Who they are | Their next step | How you help | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Aware | Has heard the name | Reads about the project | Link the Foundation and 7x pages in your material | [Who is the Exponential Foundation?](../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md) |
| 2. User | Runs or edits a site | Uses the help, asks in the forums | Point editors to the help dashboard and the audience pages | [The help system](../../features/6.0/help-system.md) |
| 3. Reporter | Meets a problem | Files a good issue | Teach the **Report an error** page and a good bug description | [When something goes wrong](../when-something-goes-wrong.md#a-good-bug-description) |
| 4. Documenter or translator | Knows a part well | Improves a help page with *Edit on GitHub*, completes a translation | Give time for it in projects | [Free documentation](../free-documentation.md#help-others), [Languages](../../languages.md#8-adding-or-completing-an-administration-translation) |
| 5. Contributor | Can change code | Sends a pull request | Install with Git clones from day one | [Develop with Git](../develop-with-git.md), `CONTRIBUTING.md` |
| 6. Reviewer or maintainer | Contributes regularly | Reviews others' work, maintains an extension | Recognize the work publicly | `CONTRIBUTING.md`, "Code Reviews" |

What makes stage 5 attractive, and is documented:

- "Every pull request is merged, and its commits are kept as the contributor wrote them and never rewritten"
  ([Continuous integration](../../bc/6.0/ci.md#the-commit-message-policy)).
- Contributions stay free: "any modifications made to the project are also released under the same GPLv2 or later
  license" (`CONTRIBUTING.md`).
- A fix "reaches every site in the next release" ([When something goes wrong](../when-something-goes-wrong.md#even-better-send-the-fix)).

The leadership side of this ladder, with a 30/60/90-day plan, is on
[Becoming a leader in the Exponential community](../leadership/becoming-a-community-leader.md).

## Contributions that are not code

Marketing and project people can contribute directly. These are documented, concrete openings.

| Contribution | Why it matters | How | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Review a machine-assisted translation | Spanish, French, Ukrainian, Hebrew, Latin and Klingon were machine-assisted; a review is "the quickest win" | Read the administration in the language and report or fix wrong strings | [Languages](../../languages.md#8-adding-or-completing-an-administration-translation) |
| Translate missing strings | About 10,000 strings were added since 6.0; translating them is "the most useful work" for every incomplete language | Qt Linguist or an XML editor, then a pull request | [Languages](../../languages.md#8-adding-or-completing-an-administration-translation) |
| Improve a help page | Better help means fewer support questions | *Edit on GitHub* on the page, then a pull request; accepted under the GNU FDL 1.3 or later | [Free documentation](../free-documentation.md#help-others) |
| Translate a help page | A translated page "helps every reader of that language" | As above | [Free documentation](../free-documentation.md#help-others) |
| Write a tutorial or a blog post | New users find answers | Publish on Share or your own site, link back | [Who is 7x?](../who-is-7x.md#the-community) |
| Edit eZpedia | Keeps the encyclopedia useful | Edit on the site (GFDL) | [Who is 7x?](../who-is-7x.md#the-community) |
| Report clear bugs | Faster fixes for everyone | The **Report an error** page | [When something goes wrong](../when-something-goes-wrong.md) |
| Answer a forum question | The next user gets help | Forums, Telegram | [Who is the Exponential Foundation?](../who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md#community-places) |

## What your organization can do

A plan your organization can adopt and adapt. Every item uses a documented channel.

### Every month

- [ ] Answer at least `<n>` questions in the forums or on Telegram (assign an owner).
- [ ] Publish one useful article from the [content calendar](#a-content-calendar-template).
- [ ] Turn one support question your team answered into a help page improvement (pull request).
- [ ] Check the issue trackers for issues your customers reported, and add what you know.

### Every release

- [ ] Read the changelog of the new release ([changelogs](../../changelogs/README.md)) and the
      [history](../../history/README.md) month pages.
- [ ] Write a short "what is new for editors" post from the changelog, linking the feature pages.
- [ ] Tell your customers which help pages changed.

### Every project

- [ ] Install with Git clones, so fixes can go back as pull requests ([Develop with Git](../develop-with-git.md)).
- [ ] Budget time to send fixes and documentation upstream (see [Estimating and scoping with extensions](../leadership/estimating-and-scoping-with-extensions.md)).
- [ ] Ask the customer whether a case study is possible, with consent
      ([Writing case studies and content marketing](case-studies-and-content-marketing.md)).
- [ ] Introduce the customer's editors to the help and the forums at handover.

### Every year

- [ ] Decide on sponsorship of the Foundation or 7x.
- [ ] Review which extensions your projects use and whether you can help maintain one.

## Bringing customers into the community

Customers are the largest potential community. At handover, give each customer:

```text
WELCOME TO THE EXPONENTIAL COMMUNITY                         <customer>

Your site runs on Exponential <6.0.x>, open source software under the GNU GPL.

Help in your administration     Help (the "?" in the header) or /exphelp/dashboard
Ask questions                   share.exponential.earth/forums   or Telegram: t.me/exponentialcms
Report an error                 Help > Report an error  (never put passwords or personal data in an issue)
Security problems               privately, as described in SECURITY.md
Who is behind Exponential       Help: "Who is 7x?" and "Who is the Exponential Foundation?"
Improve the help                "Edit on GitHub" on every help page
Your contact at <your organization>   <role, contact channel>
```

## A content calendar template

Plan community content a quarter ahead. Tie it to things that really happen: releases, history months, new help
pages, your own projects.

```text
COMMUNITY CONTENT CALENDAR                               Quarter: <Q / year>
Owner: <role>                                            Review: monthly

| Week | Theme                    | Piece                                  | Channel                 | Owner  | Source to cite              | Status |
|------|--------------------------|----------------------------------------|-------------------------|--------|-----------------------------|--------|
| 1    | Getting started          | "Install Exponential in one command"   | blog, forum post        | <role> | help: getting-started.md    | idea   |
| 2    | Help the helpers         | Answer 5 forum questions               | forums                  | <role> | -                           | plan   |
| 3    | Editors                  | "Build a page with the layouts editor" | blog, newsletter        | <role> | help: layouts-editor.md     | draft  |
| 4    | Languages                | Call for translation reviewers         | forum, Telegram         | <role> | doc/languages.md section 8  | plan   |
| 5    | History                  | "This month in Exponential history"    | blog                    | <role> | doc/history/<month>.md      | idea   |
| 6    | Release                  | "What is new in <version>"             | blog, forum, newsletter | <role> | doc/changelogs/6.0/<v>.md   | wait   |
| 8    | Case study (with consent)| <customer story>                       | site, newsletter        | <role> | approved text               | consent|
| 10   | Contribute               | "Your first pull request"              | blog, forum             | <role> | help: develop-with-git.md   | idea   |
| 12   | Quarter review           | Measures, next quarter                 | internal                | <role> | measures below              | -      |

Status values: idea, plan, draft, review, approved, published, wait (for a release), consent (case study)
```

Rules for the calendar:

- Every piece cites its source; product claims link to a help page.
- Release posts wait for the release; never announce a version before its tag exists (a published tag is never
  changed, `SECURITY.md`).
- Case studies follow the consent and approval process.
- Use the product names from [Brand and naming rules](brand-and-naming-rules.md).

## Measuring what you can actually see

The sources do not publish community statistics, and this page does not invent any. Measure what your
organization can observe and count itself:

| Measure | How to count | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Forum answers by your team | Count your team's posts | The forums |
| Issues your team filed or helped close | Filter by your accounts | GitHub issue trackers |
| Pull requests your team sent and got merged | Filter by your accounts; `git log --grep="Merge pull request"` in a clone | GitHub, `git log` |
| Help pages your team improved | Your pull requests touching `doc/` | GitHub |
| Translation strings your team completed | `php bin/php/ezchecktranslation.php <code> --allow-root-user` before and after | [Languages](../../languages.md#8-adding-or-completing-an-administration-translation) |
| Customers introduced to the community | Your handover checklist | Your records |
| Content published from the calendar | Your calendar | Your site |
| Project-wide activity | Commits and merged pull requests, with the commands the source names | [Who is 7x?](../who-is-7x.md#7x-in-numbers) |

Report trends over quarters, not one-off numbers, and always with the date of the count.

## Rules of good conduct for advocates

- **Be accurate.** Use the facts of the help and link them. Say "the sources do not state" when they do not.
- **Be honest about size and gaps.** Do not inflate the community. Invite people to make it bigger.
- **Respect privacy.** Never post customer data, passwords, keys or host names in public channels
  ([When something goes wrong](../when-something-goes-wrong.md#a-good-bug-description)).
- **Keep security private.** Security problems go to the private address of `SECURITY.md`, never to a forum.
- **Credit others.** Name the people and companies whose work you build on ([Who is 7x?](../who-is-7x.md#contributors-and-friends-of-the-project)).
- **Follow the contribution rules.** Read `CONTRIBUTING.md` before you send code: branches, focused pull requests,
  the commit message prefixes and the review. It also says all contributors are expected to adhere to a code of
  conduct; the file it links (`CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`) is not in the repository at the time of writing, so ask the
  maintainers for it if you need the text.
- **Do not speak for the project.** Unless 7x or the Foundation asked you to, speak as yourself or your organization.
