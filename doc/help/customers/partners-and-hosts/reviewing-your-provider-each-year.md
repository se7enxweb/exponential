# Reviewing your provider each year

Once a year, before a contract renews, look at what your host and partner actually delivered. A review based on
evidence is fairer to them and more useful to you than one based on the last incident everyone remembers. This page
gives the evidence to collect, the questions, a simple score and a record.

## Collect the evidence

| Area | Evidence | Page |
|---|---|---|
| Versions | The version history over the year; how soon releases were applied | [Release line and support](../healthy/release-line-and-support.md) |
| Updates | The reports after each upgrade | [Upgrades with your provider](../healthy/upgrades-with-your-provider.md) |
| Availability | The monitor's monthly figures | [Monitoring](../healthy/monitoring.md) |
| Incidents | Your incident notes and their accounts | [Incident notes template](../healthy/incident-notes-template.md) |
| Support | Tickets per severity, response times against the agreement | [Support agreement template](support-agreement-template.md) |
| Backups | Restore test records | [Restore test](../healthy/restore-test.md) |
| Security | Your quarterly security reviews | [Security checklist for owners](../healthy/security-checklist-for-owners.md) |
| Changes | Change requests: estimates against actual, on time or not | [Change requests](change-requests.md) |
| Deploys and maintenance | The audit trail's `system` channel lists deploys and maintenance windows | [The audit trail](../../../features/6.0/audit-trail.md) |

## Ask them

1. What went well this year, and what would you change?
2. Which risks do you see for our site next year: versions, PHP, extensions without maintainers?
3. What will the next upgrade involve?
4. Did any of your staff with access to our site leave? Were their accounts closed?
5. Is anything in our agreement no longer realistic, in either direction?

## A simple score

Score each line 0 (not done), 1 (partly) or 2 (done, with evidence). The idea follows the
[reliability scorecard](../../businesses/reliability/reliability-scorecard.md), which uses the same three levels.

| # | Question | Score |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The site ran a maintained 6.0.x release all year | [0-2] |
| 2 | Security releases were applied within the agreed time | [0-2] |
| 3 | Each upgrade came with the agreed evidence | [0-2] |
| 4 | Availability met the agreement | [0-2] |
| 5 | Support response times met the agreement | [0-2] |
| 6 | Restore tests were done and recorded | [0-2] |
| 7 | Incidents were reported honestly and reviewed | [0-2] |
| 8 | Change requests were estimated in writing and delivered as agreed | [0-2] |
| 9 | Accounts and access were kept tidy | [0-2] |
| 10 | Communication was clear and timely | [0-2] |

## What to do with the result

| Result | Do |
|---|---|
| Mostly 2 | Renew; note what to keep |
| Some 0 or 1 | Agree a plan with dates for each, and review it in a quarter |
| Many 0, or the same 0 as last year | Consider a change ([Choosing a partner](choosing-a-partner.md), [Choosing a host](choosing-a-host.md)) and plan the [handover](handover-checklist.md) |

Also look at your own side: were reports complete, were approvals timely, were contacts current? A provider can only
meet a promise when the customer's part is done too.

## A record to keep

```text
Provider review     year ____     provider ____     reviewed by ____
Score: ____ / 20
Strengths:          ____
Gaps and plan:      ____ (what, who, by when)
Agreement changes:  ____
Decision:           renew / renew with plan / change provider
Next review:        ____
```
