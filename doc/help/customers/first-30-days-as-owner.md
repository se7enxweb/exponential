# Your first 30 days as a site owner

You have just become responsible for a site: a new job, a reorganization, a new partner, or the launch of a site
that was built for you. This plan spreads the work over four weeks so that the important questions are answered
first and nothing is changed before you understand it. It works for any kind of site; the start page of your type
adds the specific parts ([Which type of customer are you?](types/which-type-are-you.md)).

Keep your notes in one document. By the end of the month it is the description of your site that the next owner
will be glad to find.

## Week 1: take stock

Do not change anything yet. Find out:

- [ ] **Who has access**, and with which rights. Ask your administrator for the list of accounts with administrator
      rights and check that each belongs to a named, current person
      ([Admin accounts and access](healthy/admin-accounts-and-access.md)).
- [ ] **Your own access**: an account of your own (never a shared one), with a strong password and, where your site
      offers it, two-factor sign-in ([Accounts, passwords and two-factor sign-in](day-to-day/accounts-passwords-and-two-factor.md)).
- [ ] **Who to call**: the contacts for content questions, for the code and for the server, with their hours and
      how to reach them out of hours ([When to call whom](partners-and-hosts/when-to-call-whom.md),
      [Escalation contacts template](partners-and-hosts/escalation-contacts-template.md)).
- [ ] **The contracts** with your partner and host, and what they promise ([Contracts and SLAs](partners-and-hosts/contracts-and-slas.md)).
- [ ] **The backups**: where they are, how often they run, how long they are kept, and when one was last restored
      ([Backups](healthy/backups.md)).
- [ ] **The version**: which Exponential version runs, and when it was last upgraded
      ([Release line and support](healthy/release-line-and-support.md)).
- [ ] **What you own**: the code, the domain, the third-party accounts ([What you own](what-you-own.md)).

## Week 2: learn the site and the team

- [ ] Take the [tour of the administration](../admin-tour.md) and open the pages in the
      [quick reference](customers-quick-reference.md).
- [ ] Meet each person who works in the site and ask what they do, how often, and what gets in their way.
- [ ] Write down the team's roles as they are, and as they should be
      ([Roles for your team](day-to-day/roles-for-your-team.md), [Team roles worksheet](day-to-day/team-roles-worksheet.md)).
- [ ] Find the routines that exist: the content calendar, approvals, the store's morning round, the newsletter.
- [ ] Read the help's landing page for editors and the start page of your type, so you speak the team's language.

## Week 3: settle upkeep

- [ ] Agree with your provider on the **upgrade calendar**: how often, how you are told, who tests
      ([How updates work](healthy/how-updates-work.md), [Upgrades with your provider](healthy/upgrades-with-your-provider.md)).
- [ ] Ask for a **restore test** if none was done in the last months ([Restore test](healthy/restore-test.md)).
- [ ] Ask how the site is **monitored** and who is told when it is down ([Monitoring](healthy/monitoring.md)).
- [ ] Go through the [security checklist for owners](healthy/security-checklist-for-owners.md) with your administrator.
- [ ] Learn how to **report a problem** well, and tell the team ([Reporting problems](healthy/reporting-problems.md)).

## Week 4: decide what comes next

- [ ] Start the [weekly owner routine](day-to-day/weekly-owner-routine.md) and put the
      [checklists](value/checklists/README.md) in your calendar.
- [ ] Look at what the site already offers and the team does not use ([Features customers often miss](value/features-customers-often-miss.md)).
- [ ] Collect the team's wishes and the problems you found, and sort them: fix now, plan, or drop.
- [ ] Set a baseline for the questions you will be asked: visits, orders or members, the cost of the site
      ([Analytics and what to measure](day-to-day/analytics-and-what-to-measure.md), [Measuring ROI](value/measuring-roi.md)).
- [ ] Agree on the next three changes with your partner, as change requests
      ([Change requests](partners-and-hosts/change-requests.md)).

> **Tip**
> If you took over from a previous partner or owner, also go through [Taking over a site](partners-and-hosts/taking-over-a-site.md)
> and the [handover checklist](partners-and-hosts/handover-checklist.md). Gaps are easier to close while the people
> who know the answers can still be asked.
