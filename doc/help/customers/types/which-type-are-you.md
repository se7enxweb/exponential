# Which type of customer are you?

The customer start pages are written for nine kinds of sites. Most real sites are a mix: a magazine that sells
subscriptions, a store in three languages, an intranet for two companies. Answer the questions below and read every
start page you land on; the reading paths can be followed side by side.

## The questions

| Question | If yes, read |
|---|---|
| Do you publish news several times a day, under time pressure? | [Newsrooms](newsroom-start-here.md) |
| Do you publish features, long reads or themed issues on a schedule? | [Magazines](magazine-start-here.md) |
| Do one or more authors post regularly in a blog, with feeds and perhaps comments? | [Blogs](blog-start-here.md) |
| Is your site a manual, a knowledge base or product documentation? | [Documentation sites](documentation-site-start-here.md) |
| Do customers order and pay on your site? | [Online stores](online-store-start-here.md) |
| Do people register, and see content the public does not? | [Membership sites](membership-site-start-here.md) |
| Is your site used by your own staff, behind a sign-in? | [Intranets](intranet-start-here.md) |
| Is your site in more than one language? | [Multilingual sites](multilingual-site-start-here.md) |
| Do you run more than one site, brand or region from one installation? | [Owners of several sites](multisite-owner-start-here.md) |

If none fits (a campaign site, a portfolio, a catalog without a shop), start with the
[Customers reading path](../customers-reading-path.md), which every type shares, and the
[Feature tour](../value/feature-tour.md).

## Common combinations

| Your site | Read | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|
| A magazine that sells subscriptions | Magazine, Membership, perhaps Online store | Who may see paid content, and how access ends when a subscription ends |
| A store in several countries | Online store, Multilingual | Prices, currencies and VAT per country; translations of product texts |
| A brand group with a shop per brand | Several sites, Online store | Which settings and products are shared, and who handles each brand's orders |
| A company site with a blog and documentation | Blog, Documentation site | One set of roles that keeps the blog's speed and the documentation's review |
| An association with members and events | Membership, Magazine or Blog | Member data, mail preferences and volunteers who change every year |
| An intranet for a group of companies | Intranet, Several sites, Multilingual | Departments and companies that must not see each other's pages |

## What you are not

These pages assume that your site is already running. If you are still choosing a system or a partner, the
[Businesses](../../businesses/businesses-start-here.md) section is written for you. If you are building or changing the
site yourself, the [Website creators](../../creators/website-creators/website-creators-start-here.md) section is.
If you write and publish content and need the steps in the editor, the [Editing content](../../editing-content.md)
page is the place to start.
