# The hosting business: overview

This section is for the people at a hosting company who turn Exponential hosting into a product: product managers,
sales and marketing, and the managers who sign the service terms. It answers: what can an Exponential hosting offer
contain, which commitments can a host reasonably make, how can it be priced and billed, how is it marketed truthfully,
and what does the license allow?

> **Note**
> None of these pages states a price, a response time or a capacity figure. The project's sources do not publish
> any, and every host's costs are different. The pages give you the options, the questions that decide between them,
> and templates with placeholders to fill in from your own measurements.

## The business in one paragraph

Exponential is free software under the GNU GPL v2 or later: "Using Exponential under the terms of the GNU GPL is free
of charge" (`README.md`). A host pays no license fee per site or per server. What a host sells is the work around
the software: a server prepared for it, installation, updates, backups, monitoring, security, support and, if it
wants, development and migration services. 7x itself offers managed hosting ("Exponential Website Hosting" and
"Exponential Platform SSD Hosting", [Who is 7x?](../../who-is-7x.md#services-and-hosting)); other hosts are free to
offer the same.
