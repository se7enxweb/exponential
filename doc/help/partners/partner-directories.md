# Partner directories and listings

This page is for partners who want to be found, and for customers wondering where to find a partner.

## What the program publishes

| Listing | As published (read 18 October 2026) |
|---|---|
| share.exponential.earth | "Listed on the official share.exponential.earth website. Get your own dedicated page to promote your services." (<https://exponential.earth/partner/benefits>) |
| Profile lists | "When applying, partners select the profile that best describes their core competency. They will then be shown in the corresponding profile list on the 7x website. This makes it easy for customers to find the right partner." (<https://exponential.earth/partner/program>) |
| Visibility by level | "A partner's visibility on the site depends on their partner level"; extra visibility on <https://share.exponential.earth/partners> for higher levels (program page) |
| Recognition | "Partners gain visibility on exponential.earth, plus the right to display certification logos" (<https://exponential.earth/partner/benefits>) |

## State of the directory

On 18 October 2026, <https://share.exponential.earth/partners> asked for a sign-in ("Access denied ... You are
currently not logged in"). A public partner directory was therefore not available to anonymous visitors. This help
names no partner companies. Whether a public directory will follow, and whether the Foundation will keep one, is to
be defined by the Foundation.

## Keeping your listing useful

- [ ] Profile(s) and level correct; updated within 30 days after any level change
- [ ] Services described in plain words, matched to customer needs
- [ ] Regions and languages you serve
- [ ] Two or three references with permission ([Customer references](customer-references.md))
- [ ] Contact by role address, answered within a stated time
- [ ] No claims you cannot prove ([Naming and brand rules for partners](naming-and-brand-for-partners.md))

## Other places customers look

- Your own website, with a clear "Exponential" page.
- The community forums, where good answers make you known.
- GitHub, where your contributions show your skill.
