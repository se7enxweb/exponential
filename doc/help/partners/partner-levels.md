# Partner levels from Student to Platinum

This page is for partners choosing a level to start at, and for partners planning their way up. It describes the
levels as the program pages published them on 18 October 2026.

## The levels in one table

| Level | Published meaning | Suits (as published) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Student** | "Educational partners" | "students, universities, or learning institutions"; "sandbox access, educational resources, and training support" | program-levels, why |
| **Now** | "Learning Partners" | "individuals or small teams exploring Exponential"; "educational tools, community access, and early-stage guidance" | why, benefits |
| **Starter** | "Learning partners beginning their journey toward mastery and hands-on experience" | "freelancers, startups, or growing developers" | program-levels, why |
| **Basic** | "Learning partners developing practical expertise with Exponential technologies" | "small to mid-size firms with project experience" | program-levels, why |
| **Professional** | "Qualified partners. These partners have proven their knowledge and competence." | Partners who deliver reliable solutions to clients | program |
| **Silver** | "Qualified partners ... At the Silver level, 7x and partners SHARE: leads, projects, joint customer meetings, etc." | "developers with a track record of reliable delivery" | program, why |
| **Gold** | "Experienced partners. These partners have successfully delivered one or more enterprise-level projects." | "teams delivering complex or large-scale Exponential projects" | program, why |
| **Platinum** | "Expert partners. These highly experienced partners engage in strategic alliances with 7x and their customers." | "Global partners consistently impacting the Exponential ecosystem" | program, why |

Sources, all read 18 October 2026: <https://exponential.earth/partner/program>,
<https://exponential.earth/partner/program-levels>, <https://exponential.earth/partner/why>,
<https://exponential.earth/partner/benefits>.

> **Note**
> The program-levels page lists seven levels (without Now); the benefits, requirements and why pages list eight
> (with Now). The why page also describes "Professional" last, as "Strategic Expert Partners ... Top-tier alliance",
> while every other page places Professional between Basic and Silver. This help follows the order of the price
> table: Student, Now, Starter, Basic, Professional, Silver, Gold, Platinum. Confirm with the partner team which
> description applies to your level.

## The three stages

The program groups its levels by its three values (<https://exponential.earth/partner/program>):

| Stage | Levels | Value | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| Learn | Student, Now, Starter, Basic | **Open** | "we provide new partners with the best educational environment for learning Exponential technologies" |
| Share | Professional, Silver | **Sharing** | 7x and partners share leads, projects and joint customer meetings |
| Innovate | Gold, Platinum | **Innovative** | "an INNOVATIVE collaboration enables us to together meet the highest enterprise project requirements" |

## Moving between levels

- **Reviews.** "Partner tiers are reviewed monthly. You will always be placed in the highest tier for which you meet
  the requirements" (<https://exponential.earth/partner/requirements>).
- **Upgrades.** "If you surpass your current tier during the year, you'll be upgraded immediately"
  (<https://exponential.earth/partner/why>).
- **Downgrades.** "If a partner downgrades (e.g., from Gold to Silver), associated benefits adjust accordingly.
  Partners are required to update their use of the Exponential Partner logo within 30 days"
  (<https://exponential.earth/partner/benefits>).

Details: [Renewals, upgrades and downgrades](renewals-upgrades-and-downgrades.md).

## Choosing your starting level

| Your situation | Start at | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Student, teacher, school | Student | Lowest price, educational focus |
| You are evaluating Exponential | Now | Low cost, community access |
| Freelancer with a first customer | Starter | Basic installation support, 15 tickets a month |
| Agency with a few Exponential projects | Basic | Faster response, three guidance meetings a quarter |
| Agency with steady Exponential revenue | Professional | Referrals, pre-sales support, white label; revenue requirement applies |
| Certified team with a sales pipeline | Silver or higher | Joint marketing, roadmap requests; certifications required |
