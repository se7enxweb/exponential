# Showing "Powered by Exponential"

This page is for partners who build sites for customers. A small credit on a live site is one of the simplest ways to
help the project: it shows that real organizations use Exponential.

## In the program

"'Powered by Exponential' site badge" earns 500 points, and "Customer site running Exponential" earns 1,000
(<https://exponential.earth/partner/requirements>, read 18 October 2026). Where an official badge graphic is
published is not stated; ask the partner team. A text credit works everywhere.

## Rules

1. **Only on sites that run Exponential.** Not on sites built with other systems.
2. **Only with the customer's consent.** The site belongs to the customer; ask, and put the agreement in the contract.
3. **Honest wording.** "Powered by Exponential" or "Built with Exponential". Not "Certified by Exponential".
4. **Link to the project**, <https://exponential.earth/>, not to a page that suggests the customer endorses you.
5. **Accessible.** A text link, or an image with a meaningful alt text ("Powered by Exponential"), readable contrast,
   keyboard reachable.

## Placement

| Placement | Notes |
|---|---|
| Footer, next to the copyright | Most common and least intrusive |
| "About this site" page | A sentence and a link |
| Credits page | Together with your own credit as the builder |

Example text:

```text
Powered by Exponential — https://exponential.earth/
Site by <Partner> — <partner URL>
```

## When a customer says no

Respect it. Many organizations do not want third-party credits on their sites. You can still log the project as a
"customer site running Exponential" if the customer agrees to be named privately to the partner team, and ask for a
reference instead ([Customer references](customer-references.md)).
