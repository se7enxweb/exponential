# White-label 7x services

This page is for partners who want to deliver 7x services under their own name. "White-Label 7x Services" is a
benefit from the Professional level on (<https://exponential.earth/partner/benefits>, read 18 October 2026). The 7x
Partner Subscription page also mentions "agencies who want white-labeled backend infrastructure"
(<https://se7enx.com/partner>).

## Services 7x publishes

| Service | Source |
|---|---|
| Managed Exponential hosting | <https://exponential.earth/partner/hosting-partners> ("7x Premium Hosting") |
| Paid support | <https://exponential.earth/support> |
| Development and design services | <https://se7enx.com/about> |
| Managed hosting, support and development as a subscription | <https://se7enx.com/partner> |

Which of these are available white-label, at what price and under which terms is not published. Ask the partner team
and get it in writing.

## Questions to settle before you sell

- [ ] Which services, exactly, and their service levels
- [ ] Prices and your partner discount (5% to 20% on 7x services by level, benefits page)
- [ ] Who talks to the end customer when something breaks
- [ ] Where data is hosted and who processes personal data ([Protecting customer data](protecting-customer-data.md))
- [ ] What happens to the customer's site if the partnership ends

## Honest white labeling

White label means your brand on the service; it does not mean hiding facts the customer needs.

- Your contract with the customer must name you as responsible; under the partner contract "Partners are fully
  responsible for their subcontractors" (<https://exponential.earth/partner/program>), and the same logic applies when
  7x is your subcontractor.
- Data protection law may require you to name subprocessors; check yours.
- Never claim to have built software you resell; Exponential is GPL software by 7x, the Foundation and its
  contributors ([Licensing for partners](licensing-for-partners.md)).
