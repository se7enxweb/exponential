# Communication channels for partners

This page is for everyone in a partner company. Picking the right channel gets faster answers and keeps knowledge
public where it helps others.

## Which channel for which purpose

| Purpose | Channel | Public? | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Your partnership: application, level, invoices, agreement | The partner team, through <https://exponential.earth/partner/contact-us> | No | <https://exponential.earth/partner/apply>, <https://exponential.earth/partner/program> |
| Technical support under your plan | GitHub issue tickets, per level | Depends on the repository | <https://exponential.earth/partner/benefits> |
| A bug in the software | <https://github.com/se7enxweb/exponential/issues> | Yes | README, [When something goes wrong](../when-something-goes-wrong.md) |
| The project, community or websites | <https://github.com/se7enxweb/exponential-community/issues> | Yes | README |
| A security vulnerability | security@se7enx.com | No, never public | SECURITY.md |
| Questions and answers | Forums <https://share.exponential.earth/forums> | Yes | <https://exponential.earth/about/project> |
| Quick community chat | Telegram <https://t.me/exponentialcms> | Yes | README |
| Chat for developers | Slack <https://exponentialcms.slack.com>, Discord <https://discord.gg/zXzGMgfzej> | Yes | <https://exponential.earth/about/project> |
| Partner updates and briefings | Partner mailing lists | No | <https://exponential.earth/partner/benefits> |

| Services from 7x | <https://se7enx.com/contact> | No | <https://se7enx.com/> |

## Etiquette

- **Search first.** The help, the forums and closed issues often already have the answer.
- **One question, one place.** Do not post the same question in five channels.
- **No customer data in public.** Replace names, e-mail addresses and URLs of customers' private systems with
  placeholders ([Protecting customer data](protecting-customer-data.md)).
- **No advertising in support channels.** Help is what earns trust; a link to your services under every answer does
  not.
- **Follow up.** When a forum thread or issue is solved, say how. Others will find it.
- **Use English in the shared channels** unless a channel says otherwise, so the most people can help.

## Response expectations

Community channels are answered by volunteers and staff when they can: "its developers are not obligated to provide
free support" (<https://exponential.earth/support>). Response times are promised only for partner tickets at your
level ([Support levels and response times](support-levels-and-response-times.md)).
