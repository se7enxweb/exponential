# Partner meetings, mailing lists and briefings

This page is for partnership owners and the people they send to meetings. It lists what the program offers and how
to get value from it.

## What the program offers

| Format | Who | As published (read 18 October 2026) |
|---|---|---|
| Quarterly partner meetings | All levels | "Invitations to quarterly regional and international partner meetings" (<https://exponential.earth/partner/benefits>) |
| Guidance meetings | By level: 1 a quarter (Student, Now, Starter), 3 a quarter (Basic), 2 a month (Professional), 3 a month (Silver), 6 a quarter (Gold), 12 a quarter (Platinum) | benefits page |
| Monthly briefings | Professional and higher | benefits page |
| Mailing lists, updates, strategic briefings | All levels | "Access to mailing lists, updates, and strategic briefings" (benefits page) |
| Events | Point value for "Full event attendance (conference + meetings)" | <https://exponential.earth/partner/requirements> |

How meetings are scheduled and announced, whether they are online or in person, and in which regions, is not
published: ask the partner team after activation.

## Guidance meetings: getting value

A guidance meeting is time with 7x for your plans. Prepare it like a customer meeting.

```text
Guidance meeting — <Company> — <date>

Goal of the meeting (one sentence): __________________________
Since last meeting: projects ____, points ____, tickets ____, contributions ____
Questions (max 5, most important first):
  1. ______________________________
  2. ______________________________
Decisions we need: ______________________________
Help we offer (code, docs, events): __________________
Follow-up owner on our side: ____________
```

## Quarterly partner meetings

- Send someone who can decide, not only listen.
- Bring one lesson learned from a project to share; the program's value "Sharing" works in both directions.
- Write a one-page summary for your team within two days.

## Briefings and mailing lists

- Route them to a shared role address, not one person's inbox.
- Turn each briefing into actions: releases to test, customers to inform, campaigns to join.

## Points from meetings and events

Event attendance earns points ("Full event attendance (conference + meetings)" 5,000; "Partner event participation
(per person)" 1,000 on the program page). Log them ([Logging your partner activity](logging-partner-activity.md)).
