# Pre-sales support

This page is for partners preparing offers, tenders and demos. "Pre-Sales Support" is a benefit from the
Professional level on (<https://exponential.earth/partner/benefits>, read 18 October 2026); at Silver, 7x and
partners share "joint customer meetings" (<https://exponential.earth/partner/program>). What the benefit includes in
hours or formats is not published; ask the partner team.

## When to involve 7x

| Situation | Involve 7x? |
|---|---|
| Large or enterprise tender with technical questions about the platform's future | Yes, early |
| The customer asks about security processes, releases, PHP support | Often answered by the help; involve 7x if the tender requires a vendor statement |
| A feature that does not exist yet | Yes, before you promise anything ([Feature requests and sponsored features](feature-requests-and-sponsored-features.md)) |
| A small site with standard features | Usually not |

## Preparing a request

```text
Pre-sales request
Partner: <company> (<level>)     Deadline: <date>
Customer / sector: <with permission or anonymized>
Scope: <sites, languages, users, integrations, hosting>
Questions (numbered, exact wording of the tender where possible):
  1. ...
What we already answered ourselves (with sources): ...
What we need from 7x: answers / a call / a joint meeting / a statement
```

## Never promise

- Features that are not released.
- Response times shorter than your own staff can deliver ([Support levels and response times](support-levels-and-response-times.md)).
- Statements in the name of 7x or the Foundation that they did not give you in writing.
- Certifications you do not hold.
