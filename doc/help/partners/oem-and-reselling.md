# OEM and reselling

This page is for partners who want to sell their own product built on Exponential. "OEM options are also available to
resell your own products based on Exponential" and "OEM Resale Opportunities" is a benefit at every level
(<https://exponential.earth/partner/benefits>, read 18 October 2026). The program page puts it as: "Partners can also
resell their own products based on Exponential (OEM options)".

## Product shapes

| Shape | Example | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Packaged solution | An intranet, a school site or a newsroom on Exponential with your extensions and setup | Sold as a project or subscription |
| Hosted product (SaaS) | Your solution run by you for many customers | You run the software; customers use it |
| Appliance or image | A preinstalled server image or container | Distribution: GPL obligations apply |
| Extension bundle | Your extensions with installation and support | See [Extension and theme partners](extension-and-theme-partners.md) |

## The GPL and your product

Exponential is GPL v2 or later. You may charge for it, for your work and for services. When you distribute GPL
software, recipients receive the same freedoms: the license, the source code and the right to share it on. The
practical list is on [Distribution partners and resellers](distribution-partners.md#obligations-of-a-distributor);
the licenses themselves are on [Licensing for partners](licensing-for-partners.md). This is not legal advice; ask
counsel for your product.

## What to agree with 7x

The OEM benefit is a program promise; its details are not published. Ask:

- [ ] Whether the product may carry "Exponential" in its name or description, and how
      ([Naming and brand rules for partners](naming-and-brand-for-partners.md))
- [ ] Whether 7x lists or promotes OEM products
- [ ] Whether OEM sales count as revenue for your level
- [ ] Support: who supports the Exponential core inside your product

## Good practice

- State plainly that the product is based on Exponential, free software under the GPL.
- Keep your product on supported Exponential releases ([Keeping customer sites current](keeping-customers-current.md)).
- Contribute generic fixes upstream so your product does not carry a private fork forever
  ([Contributing code back](contributing-code-back.md)).
