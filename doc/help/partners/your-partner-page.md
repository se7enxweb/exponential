# Your partner page

This page is for whoever writes the partner company's page. Every level includes a "Dedicated Page on
share.exponential.earth" (<https://exponential.earth/partner/benefits>, read 18 October 2026), called a
"Self-managed page" on the program page. How the page is created and edited is explained to you at activation.

## What a good partner page answers

1. Who are you, where are you, which languages do you work in?
2. What do you do with Exponential (your profile, in plain words)?
3. For whom (sectors, sizes)?
4. What proof is there (references, contributions, certifications)?
5. How do I contact you, and how fast will you answer?

## Template

```text
<Company>
Exponential Partner (<Level>) · <Profile(s)> · <Country/region> · <Languages>

What we do
<Two or three sentences in customer language: "We build multilingual websites and intranets on
Exponential for public bodies and associations.">

Services
- <service> — <one line>
- <service> — <one line>

Experience with Exponential
Since <year>. <Number> projects on Exponential 6 (if you can prove it). Certifications: <list or none>.

References (with permission)
- <Customer> — <one line> — <link to case study>

Contributions to Exponential
<Links to pull requests, extensions, translations, tutorials.>

Contact
<role e-mail> · <phone> · we answer within <time>.
<Website>
```

## Wording

- Facts, not superlatives. "Since 2015" says more than "leading".
- Correct names ([Naming and brand rules for partners](naming-and-brand-for-partners.md)).
- Only the logo and level you currently hold ([Using partner logos](partner-logos-and-marks.md)).

## Accessibility

- Images with alt texts; text, not images of text.
- Links that say where they go ("our case study for [customer]"), not "click here".

## Upkeep

- Review every quarter.
- Update within 30 days of a level change.
- Remove references that no longer hold.
