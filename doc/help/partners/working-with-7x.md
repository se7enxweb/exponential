# Working with 7x

This page is for partner staff who deal with 7x: owners, project managers, developers and sales. 7x runs the
partner program and leads the development of Exponential ([Who runs the partner program](who-runs-the-partner-program.md)).

## Who 7x is, in one paragraph

7x describes itself as "a responsive North American web design, support, development and website hosting company"
and as "enterprise content management integrators, implementing the Exponential enterprise content management
system" (<https://se7enx.com/about>). It was formerly Brookins Consulting and has worked with this code base since
its early years. The full story: [Who is 7x?](../who-is-7x.md).

## Habits that make the cooperation work

1. **One owner on each side.** Name your partnership owner, and ask who your counterpart at 7x is.
2. **Write it down.** Every agreement on prices, response times, referrals or co-marketing in writing.
3. **Come prepared to guidance meetings.** An agenda, numbers, the decision you need.
4. **Use public channels for public questions.** Technical questions others may have belong in the forums or the
   issue trackers, not in private e-mail.
5. **Give before you ask.** A partner who files good bug reports and sends fixes gets faster answers in practice,
   because the problem is already understood.
6. **Respect 7x's customers.** Leads and customers introduced to you are handled as agreed
   ([Handing leads over and back](lead-handoffs.md)).
