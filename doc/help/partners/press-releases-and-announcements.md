# Press releases and announcements

This page is for marketing staff writing announcements about their Exponential work.

## What needs approval

| Announcement | Approval |
|---|---|
| "We became an Exponential Partner ([Level])" on your own channels | Not stated; a courtesy note to the partner team is wise |
| A press release that quotes or names 7x or the Foundation | Their written approval of the quote and the wording |
| A press release meant to earn points | "Approved press release" earns 500 points (<https://exponential.earth/partner/requirements>); approval by the program |
| A customer project | The customer's written permission |
| A new extension or theme | None from others; follow the naming rules |

## Writing rules

- One news item per release; the headline says it.
- Facts with sources; no market shares or customer counts you cannot prove.
- Name the product **Exponential**, the company **7x**, the nonprofit **the Exponential Foundation**
  ([Naming and brand rules for partners](naming-and-brand-for-partners.md)).
- Do not invent quotes. A quote from 7x or the Foundation must come from them in writing.
- Do not announce features that are not released ([Governance: who decides what](governance-and-decisions.md)).

## Template

```text
FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

<Company> becomes an Exponential Partner

<City>, <date> — <Company>, a <profile> company that <one line about the company>, has joined the
Exponential Partner Program at the <Level> level.

Exponential is an open source content management system and application framework, free software under the
GNU General Public License. It is led by 7x and operated by the Exponential Foundation, a nonprofit founded
in 2026.

<Two or three sentences: what the company will do with Exponential, for whom.>

"<Quote from your own company's representative>," said <role> of <Company>.

About <Company>: <boilerplate>
About Exponential: https://exponential.earth/
Contact: <role e-mail>
```

## Social media

```text
We are now an Exponential Partner (<Level>). We build <what> with Exponential, the open source CMS
led by 7x and operated by the Exponential Foundation. https://exponential.earth/partner
```

## After publishing

- Send the link to the partner team and log it ([Logging your partner activity](logging-partner-activity.md)).
- Keep a copy with the approvals.
