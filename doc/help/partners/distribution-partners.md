# Distribution partners and resellers

This page is for partners with the Distribution profile. "Distribution Partners: Extend the Exponential ecosystem by
distributing and reselling Exponential software or certifications (e.g., distributors, resellers, and training
centers)" (<https://exponential.earth/partner/program>, read 18 October 2026).

## What you can distribute

| What | Can you resell it? | Conditions | Page |
|---|---|---|---|
| Exponential itself | You may distribute it, and charge for distribution and services | GPL v2 or later: pass on the license and the source; recipients get the same freedoms | [Licensing for partners](licensing-for-partners.md) |
| Your own extensions based on Exponential | Yes ("OEM options are also available to resell your own products based on Exponential", benefits page) | If they are distributed with or as part of GPL code, they must be GPL compatible; ask your counsel | [OEM and reselling](oem-and-reselling.md) |
| 7x services and subscriptions | As agreed with 7x; white label from Professional | Partner agreement | [White-label 7x services](white-label-services.md) |
| Training and certifications | Training: yes. Certifications: as the program defines them | Certifications are still to be defined | [Training partners](training-partners.md) |
| Documentation | Yes, under the GFDL 1.3 or later | Keep notices, include the license | [Free documentation](../free-documentation.md) |

## Packaging offers honestly

- Separate what costs money (services, support, hosting, training, your own work) from what is free (the software
  and its documentation). Customers trust a reseller who says so.
- Name the versions you deliver and how long you will maintain them.
- Do not call the software "licensed to" the customer as if it were proprietary; describe the GPL correctly
  ([Licensing explained for buyers](../business/licensing-for-buyers.md)).

## Obligations of a distributor

When you distribute Exponential (for example as a download, an image, an appliance or a preinstalled server):

- [ ] Include the license texts (`LICENSE`, `COPYRIGHT.md`) and keep every copyright notice.
- [ ] Provide the corresponding source code, including your changes, as the GPL requires.
- [ ] Do not add restrictions that the GPL forbids.
- [ ] Keep the documentation's GFDL notices and include the license text with it.
- [ ] Name the product correctly ([Naming and brand rules for partners](naming-and-brand-for-partners.md)).

This list is a reminder, not legal advice; read the licenses and ask counsel for your packaging.

## Points and benefits

Distribution partners earn points through revenue ("1 point per $1 USD earned by exponential.earth via your direct
sales, project licensing, or subscriptions", requirements page) and through training and certification activities
([The points system](the-points-system.md)).
