# The partner program at a glance

This page is for readers who want the whole program on one screen before going into the details. All values were
read on 18 October 2026 from the pages under <https://exponential.earth/partner>; the live pages are authoritative.

## One table

| Topic | Summary | Detail page |
|---|---|---|
| Purpose | For "companies who deliver professional products and services based on the Exponential open source Content Management System" | [What an Exponential Partner is](what-is-an-exponential-partner.md) |
| Run by | 7x; the Foundation's role is to be defined by the Foundation | [Who runs the partner program](who-runs-the-partner-program.md) |
| Values | Open, Sharing, Innovative | [Open source values](open-source-values-for-partners.md) |

## The path through the program

```text
  Interest ──► Apply (form) ──► Review (up to 10 working days) ──► Sign agreement
                                       │                                 │
                                       └─ not approved: reapply          ▼
                                          after 180 days         Buy subscription (7x store)
                                                                         │
                                                                         ▼
  Renew yearly ◄── Level reviewed (points, revenue, certifications) ◄── Active partner
```

## Two offers with "partner" in the name

| | Exponential Partner Program | 7x Partner Subscription |
|---|---|---|
| Where | <https://exponential.earth/partner> | <https://se7enx.com/partner> |
| For | Companies that deliver services based on Exponential to their own customers | Businesses that want 7x to look after their own website |
| What you get | Levels, profiles, benefits, referrals, listing | "managed hosting, support, and CMS setup", development hours, "uptime SLAs, and roadmap planning" by tier |
| Prices as published | $25 to $2,600 a month by level | "Starter Partner – $45/month", "Pro Partner – $125/month", "Enterprise Partner – $450/month" |
| Store | <https://my.se7enx.com/store/exponential-partners> | <https://my.se7enx.com/store/7x-partners> |

The 7x page ends with "Become a 7x Partner and an Exponential Partner Today", so the two can be combined. This
section of the help covers the Exponential Partner Program; for the managed service ask 7x.

## What is not decided yet

Several parts are not described in the published sources: the Foundation's role, a formal code of conduct, exam
dates for certifications, how partner meetings are scheduled. See
