# Support levels and response times

This page is for partners who promise support to their own customers and need to know what support they can count
on themselves. All values as published on 18 October 2026.

## Three kinds of support

| Kind | Who | Response promise | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Community support | Everyone | None: "its developers are not obligated to provide free support. We do, however, love to see people use our product and try to go the extra mile where possible" | <https://exponential.earth/support> |
| Partner support | Partners, by level | Response times by level (below) | <https://exponential.earth/partner/benefits> |
| Paid support from 7x | Anyone | Agreed with 7x | <https://exponential.earth/support> ("Hire 7x!") |

## Partner support by level

| Level | GitHub issue tickets | Response time | Installation support | Guidance meetings |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Student | 2 a week | ≤ 36 h (program page: "Community only") | No | 1 a quarter |
| Now | 2 a week | ≤ 36 h (program page: "Community only") | No | 1 a quarter |
| Starter | 15 a month | ≤ 36 h | Basic | 1 a quarter |
| Basic | 15 a month | ≤ 24 h | Basic | 3 a quarter |
| Professional | 5 a week | ≤ 12 h | Basic | 2 a month |
| Silver | 7 a week | ≤ 12 h | Basic | 3 a month |
| Gold | 10 a week | ≤ 4 h | Basic | 6 a quarter |
| Platinum | 15 a week | ≤ 3 h | Basic | 12 a quarter |

Not published: whether response time means first answer or resolution, business or clock hours, the time zone, and
what "Basic" installation support covers. Get these in writing ([The partnership agreement](the-partnership-agreement.md)).

## Aligning your own SLAs

Never promise a customer a shorter response than you can deliver with your own staff, whatever your partner level.

| Your promise to customers | Needs |
|---|---|
| Next business day | Your own on-call developer; partner tickets as back-up |
| Same day for critical issues | Your own staff; a partner level whose response time leaves room (Gold, Platinum); a tested escalation path |
| Security fixes within days of release | A process to test and deploy releases ([Testing releases](testing-releases.md)) |

```text
Support statement for <Customer>
First response: <time> in <hours>/<time zone>
Critical (site down, security): <time>, phone <role number>
Normal: <time>
Who fixes what: <Company> for the site and its extensions; upstream fixes through the Exponential project.
```

## Using tickets well

- One problem per ticket, with the information in [Opening a partner support ticket](opening-a-partner-support-ticket.md).
- Use your ticket budget for problems you cannot solve, not for questions the documentation answers.
- When the answer is generally useful, help turn it into documentation.
