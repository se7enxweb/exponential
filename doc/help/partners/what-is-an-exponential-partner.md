# What an Exponential Partner is

This page is for anyone who wants a clear picture of the word "partner" around Exponential before reading the details
of the program. It answers: who is a partner, who the program is for, and how a partner differs from the other
roles people play in the Exponential community.

## The short definition

An **Exponential Partner** is an organization or professional that delivers products or services based on
Exponential and has joined the official partner program. The program describes itself in one sentence:

> "The official partner program is designed for companies who deliver professional products and services based on
> the Exponential open source Content Management System." (<https://exponential.earth/partner/apply>, read
> 18 October 2026)

In the words this help uses for the whole audience: partners are organizations, companies and users who support
Exponential directly, as **sustaining partners** in the combined success of the community on the internet. A partner
earns from Exponential, and in return helps keep it alive: with a subscription, with work given back, and with
customers who are well served.

## Who the program is for

The apply page names who it is looking for: "agencies, development firms, and digital solution providers who:
are committed to open-source excellence; have experience working with or a strong interest in the Exponential
platform; share our vision for high-quality digital experiences and customer success"
(<https://exponential.earth/partner/apply>).

The levels reach further than agencies. The lowest levels are for learners:

| Kind of partner | Typical level | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Students, universities, learning institutions | Student | <https://exponential.earth/partner/why> |
| Individuals or small teams exploring Exponential | Now | <https://exponential.earth/partner/why> |
| Freelancers, startups, growing developers | Starter | <https://exponential.earth/partner/why> |
| Small to mid-size firms with project experience | Basic | <https://exponential.earth/partner/why> |
| Partners who have proven their knowledge and competence | Professional, Silver | <https://exponential.earth/partner/program> |
| Partners who delivered one or more enterprise-level projects | Gold | <https://exponential.earth/partner/program> |
| Strategic alliances with 7x and their customers | Platinum | <https://exponential.earth/partner/program> |

See [Partner levels](partner-levels.md) for the full list.

## Partner, supporter, sponsor, contributor, customer

The same organization can play several of these roles at once. The table keeps them apart.

| Role | What it means | Paid? | Where to read more |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Partner** | Delivers products or services based on Exponential within the partner program; has a level, a profile and an agreement | Yes, a partner subscription, or qualification through points | This section |
| **Supporter** | Helps the project in any way: code, documentation, translations, testing, advocacy, money | Not necessarily | [Supporters: start here](../supporters/supporters-start-here.md) |
| **Sponsor** | Gives money to 7x or to the Exponential Foundation, for example through GitHub Sponsors | Yes | [Sponsors: start here](../sponsors/sponsors-start-here.md) |
| **Contributor** | Sends pull requests, issues, translations or documentation | No | [Develop with Git](../develop-with-git.md) |
| **Customer** | Buys services or subscriptions from 7x or from a partner | Yes | [Who is 7x?](../who-is-7x.md) |

A partner is usually also a contributor and often a sponsor. The program rewards that: many of the activities that
earn points are community contributions ([Earning points through community work](earning-points-through-community-work.md)).

## What a partner commits to

The published program asks for:

- **Values.** "Exponential partners must align with open-source values and a commitment to quality"
  (<https://exponential.earth/partner/requirements>).
- **Seriousness.** "Our main criteria is that your company is serious, competent and quality-conscious ... Being an
  Exponential Partner is a serious commitment, both on the part of the partner and on the part of Exponential"
  (<https://exponential.earth/partner/apply>).
- **A contract.** "Approved partners sign a contract, gain access to resources, and join the global network"
  (<https://exponential.earth/partner/requirements>); see [The partnership agreement](the-partnership-agreement.md).
- **A yearly cycle.** "Partnership lasts one year, with renewals and upgrades based on proven activity" (same page).

## What being a partner does not mean

- **It does not change the license.** Exponential stays GPL v2 or later for everyone; a partner gets no private
  license to the code ([Licensing for partners](licensing-for-partners.md)).
- **It does not make you 7x or the Foundation.** Partners are "independent companies" under the agreement
  (<https://exponential.earth/partner/program>, contract section 4). Speak for your own company only.
- **It is not required to work with Exponential.** Anyone may build, host and sell services around Exponential under
  the GPL. The program adds recognition, support and business opportunities on top.
