# Open questions for the Foundation

This page is for partners, and for the Exponential Foundation and 7x. It lists what the partner pages of this help
mark as "to be defined by the Foundation" or "not stated", based on the sources read on 18 October 2026. When an
answer is published, the page that raised the question should be updated with the source, and the question removed
here.

## How to raise or answer a question

Partners: ask the partner team (partner@exponential.earth) or open an issue in the community tracker
<https://github.com/se7enxweb/exponential-community/issues>. The Foundation and 7x: publish the answer and update the
page with its source; this help is free documentation and anyone may send the change.
