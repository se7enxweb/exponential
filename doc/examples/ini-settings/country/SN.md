The block `[SN]` of `settings/country.ini`, with 4 setting(s).

Never edit `settings/country.ini` itself: an update replaces it. Put the settings you change in an override, which wins over the shipped file:

- `settings/override/country.ini.append.php` for every siteaccess,
- `settings/siteaccess/<siteaccess>/country.ini.append.php` for one siteaccess,
