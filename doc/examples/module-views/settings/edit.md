## Address

Ordered parameters, in this order after the view name: `SiteAccess`, `INIFile`, `Block`, `Setting`, `Placement`.

## Who may open it

The view names no policy function, so the user needs a policy for the whole module: `settings/*` (every function of the module) in one of the roles assigned to them.
