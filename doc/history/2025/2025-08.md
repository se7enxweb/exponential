# August 2025

**The product is renamed to Exponential.** After eight months as a fork, the product takes its own name. The README, `composer.json` and `lib/version.php` changed on 12 August (`8284ae20f5`, `149d88dc`, `56c3ec01`, `3570896ffd`), then one commit renamed the product in `bin/`, `doc/`, `design/` and the translations (`837c7b835d`, 332 files), followed by clean-up of settings and package server URLs (`5d3f294bee`), templates (`2775315493`) and the setup wizard (`c26d609a3c`). New logos arrived for the admin login and the logo sprite (`e3367a7392`, `30e033d7ab`, `c5d87c4116`). Identifiers stayed: `eZ` classes, paths and tables are unchanged. Read [The product is called Exponential](../../features/6.0/rebranding-to-exponential.md) for what to check on your own site.

Also in this month:

- `sevenx_themes_simple` was added to the requirements so the default theme is always installed (`a88d0acd39`) and its footer template was restructured so the "Powered by" line is easy to remove (`fbf356e91d`).
- A debug output improvement was finished (`85d8697fda`) and the documentation links in templates were replaced (`4e2891e4fb`).
- Three commits tested the platform installer package (`200c3b0c0a`, `3776188c55`, `a6f01e98`); the stand-alone installation setting was fixed (`1887ab8a0a`, tag 6.0.10 on 28 August, [Changelog 6.0.10](../../changelogs/6.0/6.0.10.md)).
