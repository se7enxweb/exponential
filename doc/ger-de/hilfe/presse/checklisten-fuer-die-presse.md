## Vor dem Schreiben

```text
[ ] I know which product the story is about          (The product family for reporters)
[ ] I know the current release and its date          (The 6.0.x releases at a glance)
[ ] I have the fact sheet open                        (Fact sheet for journalists)
[ ] I know what the sources do not say                (What the sources do not say)
[ ] I have declared any interest I have               (Disclosure and sponsorship ethics)
```

## Vor der Veröffentlichung einer Nachricht

```text
[ ] Product "Exponential"; old name only in the past tense
[ ] 7x and the Exponential Foundation not confused
[ ] "Released" only for a tagged version; 6.0.15 "in development" until tagged
[ ] Every number with its source and date; no usage numbers
[ ] Every quote real and attributed; documentation not presented as a spokesperson
[ ] No link to eZ Systems or Ibexa implied
[ ] Headline checked against the story
```

Seiten: [Häufige Fehler in der Berichterstattung](haeufige-fehler-in-berichten.md), [Überschriften: was geht und was nicht](ueberschriften-was-geht-und-was-nicht.md),
[Checkliste zum Faktencheck](checkliste-faktenpruefung.md).

## Vor der Veröffentlichung eines Features oder einer Rezension

```text
[ ] Every claim marked and checked at its primary source       (Fact-checking guide)
[ ] Comparisons with the same criteria on both sides            (Comparisons done fairly)
[ ] Performance figures with their conditions                   (Verifying numbers)
[ ] Other companies credited where their work is used           (Crediting Netgen, Mugo Web and CJW Network)
[ ] Images with credit lines and license                        (Screenshots: what you may use)
[ ] Alt text and accessible structure                           (Making your coverage accessible)
```

## Vor der Veröffentlichung eines Videos oder Streams

```text
[ ] Disclosure in the first seconds and the description
[ ] Recorded on a demo installation with fictional data
[ ] No passwords, keys, tokens, host names or personal data on screen
[ ] Version named and shown
[ ] Captions checked
[ ] Title and thumbnail make a claim the video supports
```

## Nach der Veröffentlichung

