> The log viewer (Setup > Log files, setup/logs): the log files of the installation, followed live,  
> searched, cross-referenced and, when switched on here, downloaded, rotated, emptied or removed.  
> Guide: doc/eng-us/features/6.0/log-viewer.md  
>
> Do not edit this file: override it in settings/override/logviewer.ini.append.php or in the  
> administration siteaccess's logviewer.ini.append.php.  
>
> Who may use it is decided by role policies, not here:  
>   setup/logs        open the viewer, follow and search the logs, download a range (read only)  
>   setup/managelogs  rotate, empty and remove archived rotations (each action also switched on below)  
>   setup/unmasklogs  show e-mail addresses, IP addresses, tokens and session ids unmasked (a toggle per viewer)  
> No role but Administrator (which holds every policy) has them in a new installation.
