# Spec Delta

## Purpose

A form submission is passed on to a newsletter list, a welcome series or a webhook, without the visitor ever
losing the submission or seeing an error caused by what happens afterwards.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Destinations per form
An administrator SHALL be able to switch on destinations for a form, per content class or per form object, each
independently: newsletter subscription, welcome series, webhook. A destination set on a form object SHALL take
precedence over the same destination set on its class.

#### Scenario: Subscription for one form only
- **WHEN** the class "Event registration" has no destination and the form "Autumn conference" subscribes to the list "Events"
- **THEN** only submissions of "Autumn conference" are subscribed to "Events"

### Requirement: Field mapping
Each destination SHALL map form fields to the values it needs (address, first name, last name) or, for the
webhook, to the fields it sends. A destination that needs an address SHALL NOT be switchable on without a mapped
address field.

#### Scenario: Address field missing
- **WHEN** an administrator switches on the newsletter destination for a form without a mapped address field
- **THEN** the page refuses and names the missing mapping

### Requirement: Newsletter subscription with double opt-in
The newsletter and series destinations SHALL subscribe the submitted address through the list's double opt-in;
the address SHALL NOT receive any edition or series step before the list sends to it.

#### Scenario: Sign-up through a form
- **WHEN** a visitor submits the form with the address of a new subscriber
- **THEN** the subscriber receives the confirmation mail of the list and no edition

### Requirement: Webhook destination
The webhook destination SHALL deliver the event "form submitted" to the chosen webhook, with the form, the
submission time and the mapped fields, signed and retried like every webhook delivery, and visible in that
webhook's delivery log.

#### Scenario: Sign-up passed to an event tool
- **WHEN** a visitor submits "Autumn conference" with the webhook destination "Event tool" switched on
- **THEN** "Event tool" receives one signed "form submitted" request with the mapped fields, and the delivery log shows it

### Requirement: The submission is never lost
The submission SHALL be stored before any destination runs. Destinations SHALL run in the background. A failing
destination SHALL NOT remove the submission, SHALL NOT change what the visitor sees, and SHALL be listed with
its error on the form's admin page.

#### Scenario: Webhook target down
- **WHEN** a visitor submits a form whose webhook target does not answer
- **THEN** the visitor sees the normal confirmation, the submission is stored, and the admin page lists the failed delivery

### Requirement: Once per submission
Each destination SHALL run at most once per submission, also when the background job is retried.

#### Scenario: Retried job
- **WHEN** the job of a submission is retried after a worker crash
- **THEN** the webhook target receives the submission once

### Requirement: Share link with QR code
An administrator SHALL be able to show a form's public link and a QR code for it, generated by the site without
an external service.

#### Scenario: QR code for a poster
- **WHEN** an administrator opens the share dialog of "Autumn conference"
- **THEN** the dialog shows the form's link and a QR code that can be downloaded as an image
