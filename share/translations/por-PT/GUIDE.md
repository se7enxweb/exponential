# European Portuguese (por-PT): translation guide

The rules common to every language are in [TRANSLATING.md](../TRANSLATING.md). The catalogues were translated and cross-checked in October 2026; review by native speakers is welcome.

## Rules

EUROPEAN Portuguese (Portugal), never Brazilian. Avoid 'você'; use impersonal style. Buttons: infinitive (Guardar, Cancelar, Eliminar, Editar). Vocabulary of Portugal: ficheiro (not arquivo), ecrã (not tela), utilizador (not usuário), transferir (download), guardar (not salvar), definições (settings), palavra-passe (password), registo, equipa, contacto, facto, ação/ativo per current spelling (AO1990). Quotes «».

Keep every placeholder (%name, %1, %name%), HTML tag, entity and line break exactly. Do not translate code, file names, product names.
