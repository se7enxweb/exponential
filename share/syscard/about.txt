@ about | What Exponential is | from README.md, doc/eng-us/help/who-is-7x.md
Exponential is a professional PHP application framework with advanced CMS (content management system)
functionality. As a CMS its most notable feature is its fully customizable and extendable content model. It is also
suitable as a platform for general PHP development. Everything on a site, from a page or a news article to an image,
a file or a user account, is a content item, arranged in a tree and published from drafts.

@ about | The Exponential Foundation | from doc/eng-us/help/who-is-the-exponential-foundation.md
The Exponential Foundation is the nonprofit organization behind the Exponential open source project: "a 501(c)(3)
Non-Profit Corporation founded in Seattle, Washington, USA", dedicated to the continued funding, adoption, support,
direction, education, development, promotion and documentation of the project.

@ about | 7x | from README.md, doc/eng-us/help/who-is-7x.md
7x is the North American company that maintains, develops, supports and hosts Exponential. 7x has been supporting
Exponential website customers and projects for over 24 years, and took over leadership of the project, its
development, support, adoption and community growth in 2023. Before 2022, 7x was called Brookins Consulting.

@ about | Exponential Velocity | from README.md, doc/eng-us/help/who-is-7x.md
Since 6.0.15 Exponential also ships a web server of its own, Exponential Velocity, the recommended engine for every
stage from development to production. Velocity is a PHP web server for real production work that keeps the
application loaded between requests. It began as a fork of the Qbix web server engine and is MIT licensed.
