# Exponential Code of Conduct

This is the code of conduct of the Exponential project. It is the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1, with additions for the Exponential
community: partners and commercial conduct, AI-generated contributions, the contributor agreement (CLA), and trainers
and advocates. The Contributor Covenant text is kept as published, except for the contact address and the parts this
project fills in; the Exponential additions are marked as such.

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy
community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our community include:

* Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
* Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
* Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
* Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from the experience
* Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the overall community

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

* The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
* Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
* Public or private harassment
* Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission
* Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Exponential additions

The following sections are added by the Exponential project. They apply the standards above to the ways people take
part in Exponential, and they bind everyone in the [scope](#scope) of this code.

### Partners and commercial conduct

Exponential is free software that companies build businesses on. Commercial interests are welcome; they do not
outrank the standards above. Partners of the Exponential Partner Program, and everyone who sells products or
services based on Exponential:

* Tell customers the truth about Exponential, about their own partner level and certifications, and about what they
  deliver themselves and what others deliver. Claim only the level and the logos you hold.
* Compete fairly. Do not disparage other partners, other companies or other projects; compare on facts you can
  source.
* Disclose conflicts of interest: a referral commission, a partnership, an employer or an own product, when it could
  affect what you recommend.
* Respect the licenses. Exponential is licensed under the GNU GPL version 2 or later and its documentation under the
  GNU FDL 1.3 or later; do not present free software as your own proprietary product or restrict the freedoms the
  licenses give your customers.
* Protect the personal data of customers and leads. Share it only with consent and only what is needed.
* Use community spaces to help, not to advertise. Answer the question that was asked; offers belong in the places
  meant for them.

The partner agreement adds its own terms; this code does not replace them. Under the agreement, 7x may end a
partnership for violations.

### AI-generated contributions

Contributions made with the help of AI tools (code, documentation, translations, issues, reviews and answers in
community spaces) are welcome on the same terms as any other contribution:

* You are responsible for everything you submit, whoever or whatever wrote the first draft. Read it, understand it,
  test it and be ready to explain it in review.
* Say when a substantial part of a contribution was generated by an AI tool, in the pull request, the issue or the
  post.
* Submit only what you have the right to submit. Do not submit generated material that copies code or text under a
  license that is not compatible with the project's licenses.
* Do not flood maintainers or community spaces with generated pull requests, issues, reports or answers that nobody
  has checked. Unverified security reports go nowhere public; verify them and send them as `SECURITY.md` describes.
* Do not post generated answers as facts. Check them against the software and its documentation first, or say that
  they are unchecked.

### The contributor agreement (CLA)

Contributions to the core kernel of Exponential need a signed Contributor Licensing Agreement (CLA) before they can
be merged. By signing the CLA and submitting a contribution to the core kernel, you assign (transfer) the copyright in
that contribution to the Exponential Foundation and 7x; your own custom extensions are not affected. The details are in
[CONTRIBUTING.md, Copyright assignment (CLA)](CONTRIBUTING.md#copyright-assignment-cla).

* Be honest about authorship. Sign the CLA only for contributions you have the right to assign, and do not submit
  someone else's work as your own.
* Respect the decisions of maintainers about what is merged; disagree on technical merits, in public, and politely.

### Trainers and advocates

Trainers who teach Exponential and advocates who speak for it represent the community to people who meet it for the
first time:

* Teach and present accurately. Source your claims, say "not documented" when it is not, and correct mistakes openly.
* Do not call a course, a certificate or an event "official" unless the project or the partner program has made it
  so.
* Disclose your ties: an employer, a payment, a partnership or an own product.
* Make your events and material welcoming and accessible, and name a person participants can turn to if something
  goes wrong. This code applies at every event about Exponential you organize.
* Protect the privacy of participants: no photos, recordings or names published without consent.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take
appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening,
offensive, or harmful.

Community leaders have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits,
issues, and other contributions that are not aligned to this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for
moderation decisions when appropriate.

For Exponential, the community leaders are the maintainers of the project and the moderators of its community
spaces. The **Exponential Foundation** decides on reports and on the consequences of a violation.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces, and also applies when an individual is officially
representing the community in public spaces. Examples of representing our community include using an official e-mail
address, posting via an official social media account, or acting as an appointed representative at an online or
offline event.

For Exponential, it applies to:

* **contributors and maintainers**: everyone who takes part in the repositories of the project and its extensions
  (code, documentation, translations, issues, pull requests and reviews);
* **partners**: the companies of the Exponential Partner Program and the people who work for them, when they act in
  connection with Exponential;
* **community spaces**: the forums, the chat channels, the issue trackers, the project's websites and its social
  media, and events about Exponential;
* **trainers and advocates**: everyone who teaches, presents or speaks for Exponential.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the community leaders
responsible for enforcement at **community@se7enx.com**. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly
and fairly. The Exponential Foundation decides.

All community leaders are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.

Security vulnerabilities are not conduct reports: send them as [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) describes.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Community leaders will follow these Community Impact Guidelines in determining the consequences for any action they
deem in violation of this Code of Conduct:

### 1. Correction

**Community Impact**: Use of inappropriate language or other behavior deemed unprofessional or unwelcome in the
community.

**Consequence**: A private, written warning from community leaders, providing clarity around the nature of the
violation and an explanation of why the behavior was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.

### 2. Warning

**Community Impact**: A violation through a single incident or series of actions.

**Consequence**: A warning with consequences for continued behavior. No interaction with the people involved,
including unsolicited interaction with those enforcing the Code of Conduct, for a specified period of time. This
includes avoiding interactions in community spaces as well as external channels like social media. Violating these
terms may lead to a temporary or permanent ban.

### 3. Temporary Ban

**Community Impact**: A serious violation of community standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior.

**Consequence**: A temporary ban from any sort of interaction or public communication with the community for a
specified period of time. No public or private interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited
interaction with those enforcing the Code of Conduct, is allowed during this period. Violating these terms may lead
to a permanent ban.

### 4. Permanent Ban

**Community Impact**: Demonstrating a pattern of violation of community standards, including sustained inappropriate
behavior, harassment of an individual, or aggression toward or disparagement of classes of individuals.

**Consequence**: A permanent ban from any sort of public interaction within the community.
